# Reference verification

Every citation in the manuscript gets a row. A row is done when someone other
than the person who added the citation has opened the source and confirmed that
the sentence citing it is true of the source.

Claim a row by putting your name in **Checked by** and opening a PR.

Verdicts: `ok`, `wrong-claim` (source does not support the sentence),
`wrong-source` (citation points at the wrong paper), `cannot-access`.
