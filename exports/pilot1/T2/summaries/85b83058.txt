# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. Branches that switched experienced a 2.5-point improvement relative to branches that kept the old procedure, but this difference is not large enough to rule out random chance with our current data. We recommend **continuing the new procedure while extending the evaluation period** to obtain more definitive results.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Treatment Effect Estimate
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect of new procedure | **+2.5 points** on service score |
| Baseline average score | 50.3 points |
| Effect size | **4.5% improvement** relative to average |
| Statistical significance | **Not significant** (p = 0.17) |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-1.0 to +5.5 points** |

### 2. What the Data Shows
- **Switched branches** (20 branches): Service scores increased by 0.8 points after the switch (from 50.1 to 50.9)
- **Non-switched branches** (14 branches): Service scores *declined* by 1.6 points over the same period (from 50.9 to 49.3)
- The **difference-in-differences** of 2.5 points represents the estimated causal effect of the new procedure

### 3. Study Validity
- **Parallel trends check passed**: Before the switch, both groups showed similar score trends (no significant pre-existing differences)
- **Controls accounted for**: Results hold after adjusting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Robust methodology**: Used branch and week fixed effects with cluster-robust standard errors

### 4. Why Results Are Inconclusive
The study has **limited statistical power**:
- With 34 branches over 17 weeks, we can only reliably detect effects of **4.6 points or larger** (9% of average)
- The observed 2.5-point effect is meaningful but falls below this threshold
- The confidence interval includes both modest negative effects (-1.0) and substantial positive effects (+5.5)

---

## **Next Steps**

### Immediate Actions
1. **Continue the new procedure** at switched branches—the point estimate is positive and operationally meaningful
2. **Extend data collection** for at least 8-12 more weeks to improve statistical power
3. **Consider switching remaining branches** if operationally feasible, which would allow before/after comparison for all branches

### Medium-Term Recommendations
4. **Monitor staffing and visits**: Both factors significantly affect service scores (staffing: +1.0 points per unit, visits: +0.7 points per unit)
5. **Track implementation fidelity**: Ensure switched branches are fully adopting the new procedures
6. **Re-analyze in Q1 2027** with extended data to determine if the effect becomes statistically significant

### Decision Framework
- If the true effect is around 2-3 points: We may never achieve statistical significance with this design, but the improvement is still operationally valuable
- If the true effect is 5+ points: Extended data collection should confirm this within 2-3 months
- The **cost of continuing** the new procedure appears low relative to the potential benefit

---

## **Appendix: Methodology Notes**

- **Design**: Difference-in-differences with branch and week fixed effects
- **Sample**: 34 branches, 17 weeks (6-22), 578 observations
- **Treatment**: 20 branches adopted new procedure starting week 15
- **Outcome**: Service score index (average ~50, higher is better)
- **Analysis**: Cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
