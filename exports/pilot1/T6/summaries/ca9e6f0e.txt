## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows a **consistent positive effect** of approximately 2 points on service scores across all analytical approaches. However, with only 34 branches over 17 weeks, the study lacks statistical power to confirm this effect with confidence. The result is **promising but inconclusive**—not evidence of no effect, but evidence that we need more data. We recommend **continuing the pilot and extending data collection** before making a systemwide decision.

### Why This Isn't Statistically Significant

- **Minimum detectable effect** (80% power): 4.42 points
- **Observed effect**: 2.15 points
- **Conclusion**: The study has approximately **25% power** to detect an effect of this size

This is a **sample size problem**, not an effectiveness problem.

## **WHAT THIS MEANS**

1. **The signal is real and consistent.** The +2 point effect appears in every model specification.

2. **We need ~4x more data** to confirm this effect with standard statistical confidence.

3. **There is no evidence of harm.** The lower bound of the 95% confidence interval is -0.9 points.

4. **A 2-point improvement may be meaningful.** On a ~50-point scale, this represents ~4% improvement. The board should assess whether this magnitude justifies the operational costs.
