# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure: Impact on Service Scores**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Impact Assessment of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Service scores improved more at branches that adopted the new procedure, but we cannot fully attribute this to the procedure change.** Branches that switched showed a 1.8-point greater improvement than non-switching branches. However, analysis reveals the switching branches were already improving faster *before* the change, which means the true effect of the new procedure is **uncertain** and likely smaller than 1.8 points.

**Recommendation:** Continue monitoring and gather more data before making a system-wide rollout decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Shows**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Raw difference-in-differences** | +1.82 service score points |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Post-treatment gap (Weeks 15-22)** | Treatment group averaged 1.78 points higher |

### **Critical Limitation: Pre-Existing Trends**

| Issue | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Parallel trends violated** | Treatment branches were improving **0.28 points/week faster** than control branches *before* the switch (p < 0.001) |
| **Implication** | The 1.82-point estimate likely **overstates** the true procedure effect |
| **Adjusted estimate** | Could be as low as **0 to 0.5 points** after accounting for pre-trends |

### **Pre-Treatment Differences (Treatment − Control)**

| Week | Difference |
|------|------------|
| 6 | −0.84 |
| 7 | −1.23 |
| 8 | −0.86 |
| 9 | +0.11 |
| 10 | +0.07 |
| 11 | −0.19 |
| 12 | +0.53 |
| 13 | +1.12 |
| 14 | +0.93 |

*The treatment group went from scoring lower to scoring higher even before the procedure change.*

### **What We Can Confidently Say**

1. **Switching branches did improve more** than non-switching branches after Week 15.
2. **The gap widened over time** (from 0.9 points in Week 15 to 2.6 points by Week 21).
3. **However, we cannot isolate** how much of this is due to the new procedure versus pre-existing differences between the branches that chose to switch versus those that did not.

---

## **Next Steps**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Pause system-wide rollout** | Evidence is insufficient to justify the cost of full implementation |
| **2** | **Extend observation period** | Collect 10+ more weeks of data to see if the gap continues widening |
| **3** | **Investigate branch selection** | Understand why certain branches switched — were they already higher-performing or more motivated? |
| **4** | **Consider randomized rollout** | For future changes, randomly assign branches to treatment/control to ensure valid causal inference |
| **5** | **Gather qualitative feedback** | Interview staff at switching branches to understand perceived benefits and challenges |

## **Appendix: Technical Notes**

- **Analysis method:** Difference-in-Differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
- **Key limitation:** Parallel trends assumption violated (pre-treatment slope difference p < 0.001)
- **Control variables:** Staffing index, visits index
- **Observations:** 12,053 weekly records from 709 branches
