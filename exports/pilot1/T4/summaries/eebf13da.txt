# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

We evaluated whether the new stock rotation and shelving procedure improved branch service scores. **The analysis does not show a statistically significant effect.** While switched branches showed a modest 1.8-point improvement relative to control branches, this result could plausibly be due to random variation. We cannot conclude with confidence that the new procedure caused meaningful improvement in service scores.

---

## **Analysis Overview**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Study Period** | Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks) |
| **Branches Studied** | 34 total (20 switched, 14 control) |
| **Switch Date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects |
| **Statistical Standard** | 95% confidence level |

---

## **Results**

### **Primary Finding**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect of new procedure | **+1.8 points** |
| 95% confidence interval | **-0.8 to +4.4 points** |
| Statistical significance | **Not significant (p = 0.17)** |

### **Service Score Trends**

| Period | Control Branches | Switched Branches |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Before switch | 48.1 | 54.4 |
| After switch | 46.6 | 54.6 |
| **Change** | **-1.5** | **+0.2** |

### **Key Observations**

1. **No clear break at switch point**: Service scores in switched branches did not show a distinct upward shift after week 15 compared to control branches

2. **Pre-existing differences**: Switched branches had higher service scores than control branches throughout the study (approximately 6 points higher before the switch)

3. **Pre-trends validated**: Both groups followed similar trends before the switch, supporting the validity of our comparison

4. **Other factors matter**: Staffing levels and visit volumes show stronger associations with service scores than the procedure change

---

## **Interpretation**

**The data do not support the hypothesis that the new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Three interpretations are possible:

1. **The procedure has no meaningful effect** on service scores
2. **The effect is too small to detect** with our current sample size and study duration
3. **The effect varies by branch** and is masked in the aggregate analysis

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Immediate Actions**

| Recommendation | Rationale |
|---------------|-----------|
| **Pause broader rollout** | Insufficient evidence of benefit to justify system-wide implementation costs |
| **Continue current pilot** | Maintain data collection to improve statistical power |
| **Review implementation fidelity** | Verify switched branches are fully implementing the new procedure as designed |

### **Further Investigation**

1. **Extend the study period** by 8–12 weeks to increase statistical power
2. **Conduct branch-level analysis** to identify whether certain branch types benefit more
3. **Survey branch staff** to understand implementation challenges and perceived effects
4. **Analyze cost data** to determine whether any benefit justifies the investment

### **Decision Framework**

| If the new procedure is... | Then consider... |
|---------------------------|------------------|
| Low-cost, low-disruption | Continuing pilot; effect may be real but small |
| High-cost or disruptive | Reconsidering or modifying the approach |
| Strategically important | Investing in longer evaluation period |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure has **not demonstrated a statistically significant improvement** in branch service scores during the evaluation period. While we cannot rule out a modest positive effect, the evidence is insufficient to support a conclusion that the procedure achieves its intended purpose. We recommend pausing broader implementation pending additional data and analysis.
