# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Subject:** Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2 points (4%).** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure maintained their service quality, while branches using the old procedure experienced a notable decline during the same period. We recommend expanding the new procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Clear Positive Impact**
- Service scores at switched branches were **1.9 points higher** than they would have been under the old procedure
- This effect is **statistically significant** (p < 0.001) and represents a **4% improvement** relative to the average score of 50.4
- The benefit emerged after a brief two-week adjustment period and remained stable thereafter

### **2. The New Procedure Provides Protection**
After the switch date (Week 15):
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 50.9 | 51.0 | **Stable** |
| Branches with old procedure | 50.4 | 48.5 | **▼ 1.9 points** |

The new procedure appears to shield branches from system-wide pressures that negatively affected service quality elsewhere.

### **3. Analysis Is Robust**
Our evaluation used rigorous methods that:
- Compared each branch to its own prior performance
- Controlled for week-to-week factors affecting all branches (seasonal trends, holidays, system events)
- Accounted for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Included all 709 branches over 17 weeks (12,053 observations)

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Proceed with Full Rollout**
Given the clear positive effect and no identified downsides, we recommend extending the new procedure to all 284 branches still using the old method.

### **Document and Train**
Capture the specific practices that make the new procedure effective and incorporate them into standard training materials for all staff.

### **Monitor Going Forward**
Continue tracking service scores to confirm the effect persists over time and identify any branches that may need additional support during transition.

---

## **Questions?**

The analysis team is available to present these findings in person and address any questions about the methodology or implications.
