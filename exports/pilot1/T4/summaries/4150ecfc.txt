# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

---

## **Subject: Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores**

**Date:** Analysis of weeks 6–22  
**Data:** 709 branches, 12,053 weekly observations

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 1.8 points.** This represents a meaningful 3–4% gain in service quality. The improvement is statistically robust and grew stronger over the eight weeks following implementation. We recommend expanding the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Overview**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total branches analyzed | 709 |
| Branches using new procedure | 425 (60%) |
| Branches using old procedure | 284 (40%) |
| Observation period | Weeks 6–22 |
| Switch implemented | Week 15 |

### **2. Service Score Impact**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| New procedure branches | 50.14 | 54.68 | +4.54 |
| Old procedure branches | 50.18 | 52.91 | +2.72 |
| **Effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.82** |

### **3. Statistical Confidence**
- **Effect size:** +1.85 points
- **95% confidence interval:** +1.31 to +2.38 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Conclusion:** We can be highly confident the improvement is real and attributable to the new procedure, not random variation.

### **4. Effect Strengthened Over Time**

| Weeks After Switch | Treatment Advantage |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| 1–2 | +0.5 to +0.9 points |
| 3–4 | +1.3 to +2.2 points |
| 5–8 | +2.1 to +2.6 points |

Branches continued to improve relative to controls throughout the observation period, suggesting a learning curve or cumulative benefits.

### **5. Validity Checks Passed**
- Pre-switch trends were similar between groups (parallel trends assumption satisfied)
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- No branches changed group assignment during the study

---

## **RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **1** | Approve systemwide rollout of new procedure to remaining 284 branches | Next quarter |
| **2** | Develop transition support package for switching branches (training, troubleshooting) | Immediate |
| **3** | Continue monitoring service scores for 6–12 months to assess long-term effects | Ongoing |
| **4** | Conduct cost-benefit analysis comparing implementation costs to service improvements | 60 days |
| **5** | Document best practices from high-performing switched branches for knowledge sharing | 90 days |

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The new rotation and shelving procedure has demonstrably improved service quality across the cooperative. The effect is statistically significant, practically meaningful, and appears to grow over time. Expanding this procedure to all branches is a data-driven decision that should enhance patron satisfaction across our network.
