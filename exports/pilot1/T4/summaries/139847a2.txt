# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** January 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure shows a **positive but not statistically definitive effect** on branch service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by an estimated **2.3 points** (approximately 5%) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. While this result is encouraging, the evidence does not meet conventional thresholds for statistical significance, and we recommend continued evaluation before committing to a full system-wide rollout.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Likely yes, but we cannot be certain** |
| How large is the effect? | **+2.3 points** (5% improvement) |
| Is the result statistically significant? | **No** (p = 0.10; conventional threshold is p < 0.05) |
| Should we roll out system-wide? | **Not yet**—gather more data first |

---

## **Study Overview**

- **34 branches** participated over 17 weeks (February–June)
- **20 branches** switched to the new procedure in Week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores are calculated weekly (scale ~30–70; higher is better; average ≈ 50)
- We used a **difference-in-differences** method to isolate the effect of the procedure change

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Changes**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (Old Procedure) | 44.8 | 45.3 | +0.5 |
| Treatment (New Procedure) | 49.9 | 52.8 | +2.9 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.3** |

### **What the Numbers Mean**

- The **2.3-point improvement** represents about **5% of typical branch performance**
- The **95% confidence interval ranges from -0.5 to +5.1**, meaning:
  - The true effect could be slightly negative (unlikely to be harmful)
  - The true effect could be as high as +5 points (meaningful improvement)
  - We cannot rule out zero effect with high confidence
- **Why isn't this significant?** With only 34 branches and 8 post-switch weeks, the study has limited statistical power. A larger sample or longer observation period would provide more definitive answers.

### **Validation**

✓ **Pre-switch trends were parallel**—both groups moved similarly before the change, supporting the validity of our comparison  
✓ **Results hold after controlling** for staffing levels and visitor volume  
✓ **No evidence of harm**—the new procedure did not negatively affect any branches
