# **LIBRARY COOPERATIVE BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a 2.5-point improvement relative to branches that kept the old procedure. However, this difference is not large enough to rule out random variation with our current data. We recommend **continuing the new procedure while extending the evaluation period** to obtain more definitive results.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Treatment Effect Summary

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect of new procedure | **+2.5 points** on service score |
| Baseline average score | 50.3 points |
| Effect size | **4.5% improvement** |
| Statistical significance | Not significant (p = 0.17) |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-1.0 to +5.5 points** |

### What Changed After the Switch

| Group | Before (Weeks 6-14) | After (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched branches (20) | 50.1 | 50.9 | +0.8 |
| Non-switched branches (14) | 50.9 | 49.3 | -1.6 |
| **Net effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.5** |

### Study Validity

✓ **Parallel trends verified**: Both groups showed similar score patterns before the switch  
✓ **Controls applied**: Results account for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume  
✓ **Robust methods**: Branch and week fixed effects with cluster-robust standard errors  

### Why Results Are Inconclusive

The study has **limited statistical power** due to sample size:
- 34 branches over 17 weeks provides 578 observations
- Minimum detectable effect: **4.6 points** (9% of average)
- Observed effect of 2.5 points is meaningful but below detection threshold
- The true effect could range from slightly negative (-1.0) to substantially positive (+5.5)

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

### Immediate Actions (Next 30 Days)

1. **Continue the new procedure** at all switched branches—the point estimate is positive and operationally meaningful

2. **Extend data collection** for at least 8-12 more weeks to improve statistical power and reach a definitive conclusion

3. **Consider expanding adoption** to remaining branches if operationally feasible, enabling before/after comparison for all locations

### Medium-Term Recommendations (Next Quarter)

4. **Monitor key drivers**: Staffing levels and visitor volume both significantly affect service scores and should be tracked alongside procedure changes

5. **Verify implementation fidelity**: Ensure all switched branches are fully adopting the new procedures as designed

6. **Re-analyze in Q1 2027**: With extended data, determine whether the effect becomes statistically significant

### Decision Framework

| If True Effect Is... | Implication |
|---------------------|-------------|
| 2-3 points | May never reach statistical significance, but still operationally valuable |
| 5+ points | Extended data collection should confirm within 2-3 months |
| Near zero | No harm done; procedure can be maintained or adjusted |

**Bottom line**: The cost of continuing the new procedure appears low relative to the potential benefit of a 4-5% service score improvement.

---

## **METHODOLOGY SUMMARY**

- **Design**: Difference-in-differences with branch and week fixed effects
- **Sample**: 34 branches, 17 weeks (weeks 6-22), 578 observations
- **Treatment**: 20 branches adopted new procedure starting week 15; 14 branches maintained old procedure
- **Outcome**: Service score index (average ~50, higher is better)
- **Analysis**: Cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
