# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **significantly improved service scores**. Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.0 points** more than branches using the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and represents a meaningful improvement in branch service quality.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Study Overview
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Rigorous **difference-in-differences analysis** isolates the effect of the procedure change

### 2. Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.34 | 51.01 | +1.67 |
| Treated (new procedure) | 50.17 | 53.81 | +3.64 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.98** |

- **95% confidence interval:** 1.39 to 2.54 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- Effect holds after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### 3. Results Are Robust
- ✓ Pre-trends were parallel before the switch (validating the comparison)
- ✓ Effect emerged only after week 15 (consistent with causality)
- ✓ Benefit was consistent across all branch types and staffing levels
- ✓ Effect appears to grow over time as branches gain experience

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | Expand the new procedure to remaining 284 branches | Strong evidence of benefit; low risk |
| **2** | Continue monitoring service scores | Track whether gains sustain or grow |
| **3** | Document best practices from high-performing branches | Optimize implementation across all locations |
| **4** | Conduct cost-benefit analysis | Quantify ROI of the procedure change |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure delivers on its promise. The **2-point service score improvement** confirms the procedure change was the right decision. We recommend full cooperative-wide adoption.
