# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **works**. Branches that adopted it achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than would have been expected under the old procedure. This is a statistically significant improvement that grew stronger over the eight weeks following implementation.

---

## **What We Did**

We analyzed weekly service scores from **709 branches** over **17 weeks**. Starting in week 15, **425 branches** switched to the new procedure while **284 branches** continued with the old approach. This natural experiment allows us to isolate the effect of the new procedure from other factors affecting all branches.

---

## **What We Found**

### **Service Score Impact**

| | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Net Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branches with new procedure | 50.1 | 54.7 | +4.5 |
| Branches with old procedure | 50.2 | 52.9 | +2.7 |
| **Effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.8** |

### **Key Points**

- **The effect is real:** Statistical testing confirms this result is not due to random chance (p < 0.001).
- **The effect is meaningful:** A 1.8-point gain represents approximately a **4% improvement** on the service score index.
- **The effect grows over time:** Benefits were modest in the first week (+0.9 points) but reached +2.3 points by week 22, suggesting branches improve with experience.
- **Results are robust:** The finding holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.

---

## **Recommendations**

1. **Proceed with full rollout** to remaining branches using the old procedure.

2. **Allow a 4–6 week ramp-up period** for transitioning branches, as full benefits take time to materialize.

3. **Continue monitoring** service scores quarterly to confirm sustained improvement.

4. **Document best practices** from early-adopter branches to accelerate learning at remaining locations.
