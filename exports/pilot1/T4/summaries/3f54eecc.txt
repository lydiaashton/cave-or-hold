## **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 13, 2026  
**RE:** Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving system saw service scores increase by **2.1 points** (approximately **4%**) compared to branches that kept the old system. This improvement is statistically significant and appeared immediately after implementation. We recommend expanding the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated Effect** | +2.08 service score points |
| **Confidence Interval** | 1.57 to 2.60 points |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 (highly reliable) |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Baseline Score** | ~51 points |

**What the Numbers Mean:**
- Treatment branches improved by **+1.2 points** after the switch
- Control branches declined by **-0.9 points** during the same period
- The **2.1-point gap** between them is attributable to the new procedure
- Effect persisted consistently across all 8 post-switch weeks

**Validity Checks Passed:**
- ✓ Treatment and control groups had similar score trends before the switch
- ✓ Result holds after accounting for staffing and visitor volume
- ✓ Effect is stable week-to-week after implementation
