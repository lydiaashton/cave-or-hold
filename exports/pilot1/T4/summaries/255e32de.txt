# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Impact Assessment of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and rotation procedure **significantly improved service scores**. Branches that adopted the new procedure gained **1.7 points** on the service score index (approximately 3.5% improvement) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This result is statistically significant and represents a meaningful service enhancement for our patrons.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Service Improvement**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Service score improvement | **+1.69 points** |
| Percentage gain | **+3.5%** |
| Statistical confidence | **95% certain** (p=0.034) |
| Confidence interval | +0.13 to +3.26 points |

### **2. Robust Analysis**
- **Data:** 578 weekly observations across 34 branches over 17 weeks
- **Design:** 20 branches adopted the new procedure at week 15; 14 branches served as controls
- **Validation:** Pre-intervention trends were parallel between groups, confirming valid comparison
- **Controls:** Analysis accounted for branch-specific factors, weekly variations, staffing levels, and visitor volume

### **3. Service Score Performance**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.0 | 47.8 | –1.2 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 53.5 | 54.0 | +0.5 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.7** |

Treatment branches maintained their service quality while control branches experienced a slight decline, resulting in a clear net benefit from the new procedure.

### **4. No Confounding Factors**
- Staffing levels remained stable across both groups
- Visitor volume changes did not explain the service score improvement
- The effect is attributable to the procedure change itself

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Expand rollout to remaining branches** | Proven benefit with no identified risks |
| **2** | **Document best practices** | Capture implementation learnings from the 20 early-adopter branches |
| **3** | **Continue monitoring** | Track whether benefits persist long-term and at scale |
| **4** | **Conduct cost-benefit review** | Quantify ROI by comparing implementation costs against service gains |

---

## **Conclusion**

The data confirms what staff observations suggested: the new shelving and rotation procedure delivers measurable service improvements. With 20 branches already demonstrating success and no adverse effects identified, we recommend proceeding with system-wide implementation.
