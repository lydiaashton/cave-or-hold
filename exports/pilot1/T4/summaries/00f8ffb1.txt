# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** January 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure is associated with a **statistically significant improvement of 1.5 points** in service scores. However, branches that adopted the new procedure had lower baseline scores, which complicates causal interpretation. We recommend **continuing the rollout while extending monitoring** to confirm the effect persists and grows over time.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Treatment Effect: +1.5 Points (Statistically Significant)**
Branches that switched to the new procedure improved their service scores by **1.5 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure, after the switch occurred (p < 0.001). This represents:
- **16% of one standard deviation** in typical service score variation
- **3% improvement** relative to the average branch score of 49

### **2. Important Caveat: Pre-Existing Differences**
Branches that volunteered to switch had **systematically lower service scores** before the change:
- Average pre-switch gap: **1.3 points lower** than non-switching branches
- 5 of 8 pre-switch weeks showed statistically significant differences
- This suggests the estimated effect may be partially inflated by **mean reversion** (lower-performing branches naturally improving)

### **3. Effect Emerged Gradually**
The improvement was **not immediate**:
| Period | Effect Size | Statistical Significance |
|--------|-------------|-------------------------|
| Weeks 15-21 (first 7 weeks) | 0.2 to 1.1 points | Not significant |
| Week 22 (final week) | 2.0 points | **Significant** (p < 0.001) |

This pattern suggests the benefits may accumulate over time as staff gain familiarity with the new procedure.

### **4. Study Scope**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Branches studied | 709 |
| Switched to new procedure | 425 (60%) |
| Kept old procedure | 284 (40%) |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (Weeks 6-22) |
| Procedure switch | Week 15 |

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Immediate Actions**
1. **Continue the rollout** — The evidence, while not definitive, points toward a meaningful positive effect
2. **Extend monitoring** — Track service scores for at least 8-12 more weeks to confirm the effect stabilizes
3. **Document implementation** — Capture best practices from branches showing the strongest improvements

### **Further Analysis**
4. **Investigate selection bias** — Understand why lower-scoring branches were more likely to switch; consider matching methods for future evaluations
5. **Examine heterogeneity** — Determine if effects vary by branch size, location, or staffing levels
6. **Plan rigorous evaluation** — For future procedural changes, consider randomized rollout to enable cleaner causal inference

---

## **Conclusion**

The new shelving procedure shows **promising results** with a statistically significant positive effect on service scores. While pre-existing differences between branches warrant caution in interpretation, the magnitude of the effect and its emergence over time support continued implementation. Extended monitoring will clarify whether this represents a sustained improvement.
