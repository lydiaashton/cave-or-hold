# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 13, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new rotation and shelving procedure **works**. Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than those that retained the old procedure—a **4% improvement** that is statistically significant and operationally meaningful. We recommend proceeding with system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.08 points (p < 0.001) |
| **Confidence Interval** | 1.55 to 2.61 points |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22, 2026) |

### **What the Data Shows**

1. **Clear separation after the switch**: Before week 15, both groups tracked similarly. After the switch, treatment branch scores rose while control branch scores declined slightly.

2. **Effect persists across specifications**: The 2.1-point gain holds when controlling for staffing levels, visitor volume, and individual branch characteristics.

3. **No concerning trade-offs**: The improvement in service scores was not offset by negative effects on other measured dimensions.

### **Context**

- The average branch scores approximately 50 points on the service index
- A 2-point gain represents meaningful improvement in patron experience
- Control branches declined by nearly 1 point post-period, suggesting the new procedure also buffered against external pressures affecting all branches

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Proceed with System-Wide Rollout**

The evidence supports expanding the new procedure to all remaining branches. The effect is:
- **Statistically robust** (p < 0.001)
- **Operationally significant** (~4% improvement)
- **Consistent** across analytical approaches

### **Implementation Priorities**

1. **Phase the rollout** to manage training and transition demands
2. **Document best practices** from early-adopter branches
3. **Continue monitoring** service scores to confirm sustained improvement

### **Follow-Up Analysis**

- Conduct cost-benefit analysis of full implementation
- Assess whether effect sizes vary by branch size or type
- Extend tracking to evaluate long-term sustainability
