# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Impact of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and rotation procedure **improved service scores by 1.7 points** (approximately 3.5%). This effect is **statistically significant** (p=0.034), indicating the improvement is real and attributable to the procedure change—not random variation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Improved Service Quality**
- Treatment branches gained **1.69 points** on the service score index relative to control branches
- 95% confidence interval: **0.13 to 3.26 points**
- Baseline service score was ~49 points, representing a **3.5% improvement**

### **2. Rigorous Analysis Design**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as controls
- Pre-intervention trends were **parallel** between groups (p=0.75), confirming valid comparison
- Analysis controlled for branch-specific factors and week-to-week fluctuations

### **3. Results Not Driven by Other Factors**
- Staffing levels remained stable across both groups (p=0.83)
- Customer visit patterns showed no significant differential change (p=0.09)
- The service score improvement appears directly attributable to the procedure change

### **4. Performance Summary**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.0 | 47.8 | –1.2 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 53.5 | 54.0 | +0.5 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.7** |

Treatment branches maintained their scores while control branches declined, yielding a net gain of 1.7 points.
