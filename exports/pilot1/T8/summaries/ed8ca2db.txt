## **Final Verdict**

**The new procedure cannot be credited with improving service scores based on this evidence.** 

The apparent 2-point benefit reflects that non-adopting branches declined while adopting branches held steady—not that the new procedure generated gains. Since the two groups were already on diverging trajectories before the switch (treatment branches rising, control branches falling), we cannot isolate the procedure's effect from pre-existing differences.

**However, the procedure shows no negative effects and may offer resilience.** There is no evidence it harms service quality, and it may help branches maintain performance during system-wide challenges.
