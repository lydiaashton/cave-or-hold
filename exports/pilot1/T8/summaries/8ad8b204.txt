# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a positive effect on service scores, but the evidence is not definitive.** Switched branches improved by an estimated **1.8 points** relative to control branches (p = 0.06). This is **marginally significant** – suggestive of a real benefit, but not conclusive at the conventional 95% confidence level. The data are consistent with either a modest positive effect or no effect at all.

---

## **Analysis Overview**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Study Period** | Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks) |
| **Branches Studied** | 34 total (20 switched, 14 control) |
| **Switch Date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects |

---

## **Results**

### **Primary Finding**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect of new procedure | **+1.8 points** |
| Standard error | 0.96 |
| t-statistic | 1.87 |
| Two-sided p-value | **0.06** |
| One-sided p-value | **0.03** |
| 90% confidence interval | **0.2 to 3.4 points** |
| 95% confidence interval | −0.1 to 3.7 points |

### **Service Score Trends**

| Period | Control Branches | Switched Branches | Gap |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|-----|
| Before switch | 48.1 | 54.4 | 6.2 |
| After switch | 46.6 | 54.6 | 8.0 |
| **Change** | **-1.5** | **+0.2** | **+1.8** |

---

## **Interpretation**

### **What the Data Show**

✓ The effect is **positive** (in the expected direction)  
✓ The effect is **nearly statistically significant** (p = 0.06)  
✓ At 90% confidence, the effect is **significantly positive** (CI: 0.2 to 3.4)  
✓ Pre-trends were parallel, supporting the validity of the analysis  

⚠️ At 95% confidence, we **cannot rule out zero effect**  
⚠️ The study may be **underpowered** to detect effects smaller than ~2.5 points  
⚠️ Switched branches started with higher scores, though trends were parallel  

### **What This Means**

The evidence suggests the new procedure **likely provides a modest benefit**, but we lack the statistical precision to state this with 95% confidence. The true effect could plausibly range from essentially zero to approximately 3–4 points (a 6–8% improvement).

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with cautious expansion.**

The weight of evidence favors the conclusion that the new procedure provides a **real but modest benefit**. While not definitive at the 95% confidence level, three factors support moving forward:

1. **The effect is in the expected direction** – no evidence of harm
2. **Statistical significance at 90% confidence** – appropriate for operational decisions where reversal is possible
3. **The study is likely underpowered** – the observed effect (1.8 points) is close to the minimum detectable effect (~2.5 points), suggesting a real effect may exist but our sample is too small to confirm it with high precision

**Recommended action:** Continue implementation with switched branches and expand to 5–10 additional branches while collecting 8–12 more weeks of data. This will provide a definitive answer within one quarter while capturing the likely benefits already indicated by the data.

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure demonstrates **suggestive positive evidence** of improved service scores. The marginally significant result (p = 0.06), combined with the expected direction of effect and the study's limited statistical power, supports **cautious expansion** rather than withdrawal or full-scale implementation. Additional data collection will provide the definitive evidence needed for a final rollout decision.
