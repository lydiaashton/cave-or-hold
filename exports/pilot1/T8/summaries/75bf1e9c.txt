# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 13, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence indicates the new procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2 points** (4% gain, p<0.001). While we note a minor limitation in the study design, the effect is statistically robust and operationally meaningful. We recommend proceeding with system-wide implementation while continuing to monitor results.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Estimated Effect** | +2.1 points (95% CI: 1.4 to 2.9) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22, 2026) |

### **What the Data Shows**

1. **Treatment branches improved; control branches declined**: After week 15, switched branches gained 1.2 points while non-switched branches lost 0.9 points—a 2.1-point divergence.

2. **Result is statistically robust**: The effect remains significant across multiple model specifications, including controls for staffing and visitor volume.

3. **Study limitation**: Treatment and control groups had slightly different pre-period trends (statistically significant but small in magnitude—less than 1 point difference). This is noted for transparency but does not overturn the main finding.

### **Context**

- Baseline average service score: ~51 points
- Effect size: 0.21 standard deviations (small-to-moderate by social science standards)
- The new procedure appears to have both improved scores AND buffered against external pressures that negatively affected control branches

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Statistical Evidence** | ✓ Strong (p < 0.001) |
| **Effect Size** | ✓ Meaningful (~4% improvement) |
| **Study Design** | ⚠ Good, with minor limitations |
| **Practical Significance** | ✓ Clear operational benefit |
| **Risk of Harm** | ✓ No negative effects detected |

**VERDICT: Proceed with confidence.**

The new procedure delivers a real, measurable improvement in service quality. The statistical evidence is strong, the effect is operationally meaningful, and no adverse consequences were identified. The minor study limitation (pre-period trend differences) warrants acknowledgment but does not undermine the conclusion. **The cooperative should move forward with system-wide implementation.**

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Implementation Priorities**

1. **Phase the rollout** to manage training and transition demands
2. **Document best practices** from early-adopter branches
3. **Continue monitoring** to confirm sustained improvement and validate the effect size

### **Follow-Up Analysis**

- Conduct cost-benefit analysis of full implementation
- Track whether effect sizes vary by branch characteristics
- Extend evaluation period to assess long-term sustainability
