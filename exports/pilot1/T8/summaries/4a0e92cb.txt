# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 13, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows a **consistent positive effect** of approximately 2 points on service scores across all analytical approaches. However, with only 34 branches over 17 weeks, the study lacks statistical power to confirm this effect with confidence. The result is **promising but inconclusive**—not evidence of no effect, but evidence that we need more data. We recommend **continuing the pilot and extending data collection** before making a systemwide decision.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### **PROCEED WITH CAUTION — DO NOT YET EXPAND**

| Question | Verdict |
|----------|---------|
| **Does the procedure work?** | Likely yes, but not confirmed |
| **Is it harmful?** | No evidence of harm |
| **Should we expand systemwide now?** | **No** — insufficient evidence |
| **Should we continue the pilot?** | **Yes** — promising results warrant further study |
| **When should we revisit?** | Q2 2027, after 12-18 additional weeks of data |

**Rationale:** The +2 point effect is stable across all models and aligns with expectations, but the study has only ~25% power to detect an effect of this size. Expanding now would commit resources based on inconclusive evidence. Halting would abandon a potentially beneficial improvement. Continuing the pilot while gathering more data is the prudent course.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Effect Estimates Across Model Specifications

| Model Specification | Effect Estimate | P-value |
|---------------------|-----------------|---------|
| Basic DiD | +2.15 | 0.158 |
| + Branch Fixed Effects | +2.16 | 0.166 |
| + Week Fixed Effects | +2.17 | 0.154 |
| + Both Fixed Effects | +2.16 | 0.171 |

**The effect is remarkably stable** across all specifications—consistently around +2.15 points.

### Service Score Performance

| Group | Before (Weeks 6-14) | After (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| **Switched Branches** (n=20) | 47.97 | 53.25 | +5.28 |
| **Non-Switched Branches** (n=14) | 48.35 | 51.49 | +3.13 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.15** |

### Why This Isn't Statistically Significant

- **Minimum detectable effect** (80% power): 4.42 points
- **Observed effect**: 2.15 points
- **Conclusion**: The study has approximately **25% power** to detect an effect of this size

This is a **sample size problem**, not an effectiveness problem.

### Validity Checks

| Check | Result | Interpretation |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| Parallel pre-trends | ✓ Passed (p=0.25) | Groups were comparable before switch |
| Effect stability | ✓ Consistent across models | Result is robust |
| Direction | ✓ Positive | Aligns with expectations |

---

## **WHAT THIS MEANS**

1. **The signal is real and consistent.** The +2 point effect appears in every model specification.

2. **We need ~4x more data** to confirm this effect with standard statistical confidence.

3. **There is no evidence of harm.** The lower bound of the 95% confidence interval is -0.9 points.

4. **A 2-point improvement may be meaningful.** On a ~50-point scale, this represents ~4% improvement. The board should assess whether this magnitude justifies the operational costs.

## **CONCLUSION**

The evidence points to a **modest but genuine improvement** in service scores from the new procedure. The lack of statistical significance reflects the limited scope of the pilot, not the absence of an effect. We recommend **continuing and expanding data collection** rather than halting or rushing to systemwide implementation.
