## **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**RE:** Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure is associated with higher service scores, but we cannot definitively attribute the full effect to the procedure itself.** Branches that switched showed a 2-point advantage after implementation. However, these branches were already trending slightly better before the switch, which means the true causal effect is likely **smaller than 2 points** but still positive. We recommend proceeding with expansion while monitoring results closely.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Observed Effect** | +2.08 service score points |
| **Confidence Interval** | 1.57 to 2.60 points |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Likely True Effect** | Positive, but < 2 points (see verdict below) |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

**What Changed After Week 15:**
- Treatment branches: **+1.2 points** (from 51.6 to 52.8)
- Control branches: **-0.9 points** (from 51.0 to 50.0)
- Observed gap: **2.1 points**

**Important Caveat:**
Treatment branches showed a slightly stronger upward trend than control branches even before the switch (p = 0.0002). This suggests the 2.1-point estimate may overstate the true causal effect.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did service scores improve at switched branches?** | ✓ Yes, clearly |
| **Is the improvement statistically significant?** | ✓ Yes (p < 0.001) |
| **Can we claim the full 2-point effect is causal?** | ✗ No—pre-existing trends inflate the estimate |
| **Is there still a real positive effect?** | ✓ Very likely yes |
| **Should we expand the procedure?** | ✓ Yes, with monitoring |

**Reasoning:** The direction and significance of the effect are clear. The pre-trend issue means we should treat the 2-point estimate as an **upper bound**, not a precise measure. Even if half the observed effect is causal (~1 point), the procedure still delivers meaningful improvement at low cost.
