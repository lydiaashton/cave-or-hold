# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 13, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **suggestive evidence of a positive effect** on service scores. Switched branches experienced an estimated **1.8-point increase** (approximately 3.5% improvement) relative to non-switched branches. While this does not meet conventional statistical significance thresholds (p = 0.17), there is a **91% probability the true effect is positive**, and the confidence interval ranges from -0.8 to +4.4 points.

**Recommendation:** The evidence leans favorable. Consider expanding the pilot to additional branches while continuing to collect data for a more definitive evaluation in 2–3 months.

---

| | |
|---|---|
| **Effect Direction** | ✓ Positive |
| **Statistical Certainty** | ⚠ Inconclusive |
| **Practical Significance** | ✓ Potentially Meaningful |
| **Implementation Risk** | ✓ Low (pilot proven safe) |

**Rationale:** The balance of evidence favors the new procedure. The effect is positive in direction, the pre-trend analysis validates our comparison, and there is no evidence of harm. The primary limitation is sample size, not methodology. Expanding the pilot now captures potential benefits while generating the data needed for a definitive systemwide decision.
