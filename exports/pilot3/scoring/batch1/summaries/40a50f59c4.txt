# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Assessment of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure is associated with an improvement in service scores of approximately **2.5 points** (about 5% relative to baseline). However, this result is **not statistically significant** at conventional levels, meaning we cannot rule out that the observed improvement occurred by chance. The evidence is suggestive but not conclusive. We recommend **continuing the evaluation while gathering more data** before committing to a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 34 total (20 switched to new procedure, 14 kept old procedure) |
| **Time period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22 of the year) |
| **Switch point** | Week 15 |
| **Service score scale** | Average branch scores ~50 (higher is better) |

### Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 50.84 | 51.63 | +0.78 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 52.85 | 56.09 | +3.23 |
| **Estimated Treatment Effect** | | | **+2.45** |

### Statistical Summary
- **Point estimate:** +2.45 points improvement
- **95% Confidence Interval:** -0.62 to +5.52 points
- **p-value:** 0.13 (not significant at the 0.05 level)
- **Effect size:** Medium (Cohen's d = 0.57)

### Interpretation

1. **Positive direction, consistent with expectations.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed greater improvement than those that did not.

2. **Result is not statistically conclusive.** The confidence interval includes zero, meaning the true effect could range from slightly negative to substantially positive. With our current sample size, we have approximately 87% confidence that the effect is positive—but not the 95% typically required for definitive conclusions.

3. **Study design is sound.** Pre-switch trends were similar between groups, supporting the validity of our comparison method.

4. **Potential practical significance.** If the effect is real, a 2.5-point improvement on a ~50-point scale represents meaningful progress in service quality.

### Why the Uncertainty?
- **Limited sample size:** Only 34 branches total
- **Short post-switch window:** Just 8 weeks of data after implementation
- **Natural week-to-week variability:** Service scores fluctuate due to many factors beyond the procedure change

---

## **Next Steps**

### Recommended Actions

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | Extend the pilot by 8–12 weeks | Additional data will narrow the confidence interval and provide greater certainty |
| **2** | Monitor implementation fidelity | Ensure switched branches are fully adopting the new procedure as intended |
| **3** | Track cost and resource implications | Assess whether the procedure requires additional staffing or resources |
| **4** | Prepare for phased rollout | If results remain positive, consider expanding in waves to allow continued evaluation |

### Decision Framework

| Outcome After Extended Monitoring | Recommendation |
|-----------------------------------|----------------|
| Effect becomes statistically significant (p < 0.05) with positive direction | Proceed with system-wide rollout |
| Effect remains positive but non-significant | Consider rollout with continued monitoring |
| Effect diminishes or becomes negative | Re-evaluate the procedure before any expansion |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure shows promise. Branches that adopted it experienced greater service score improvements than those that maintained the old procedure. However, the evidence is not yet strong enough to justify immediate system-wide implementation with high confidence.

**We recommend extending the evaluation period** to gather more data. This modest investment in additional monitoring will provide the board with greater certainty for this important operational decision while avoiding the risk of rolling out a procedure that may not deliver meaningful benefits.

---

*Analysis based on weekly service scores from 34 branches over 17 weeks. Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). Contact the Data Analysis Team with questions.*
