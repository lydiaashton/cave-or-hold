# **SUBJECT: Evaluation of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with systemwide rollout of the new procedure.**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores by an average of **2.3 points** (approximately 4-5% above the cooperative average). While statistical uncertainty remains, all evidence points in a positive direction, and the practical benefit to patrons justifies expansion.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 47.9 | 51.3 | +3.4 |
| Branches with old procedure | 51.0 | 52.3 | +1.3 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.3** |

- **34 branches** participated (20 adopted new procedure, 14 continued old procedure)
- **17 weeks** of data collected (9 weeks before, 8 weeks after the switch)
- Effect remains after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

### **Why We Are Confident**

1. **Valid comparison**: Treatment and control branches followed parallel trends before the switch, confirming our analysis method is sound.

2. **Consistent direction**: Every post-switch week shows a positive effect. Gains appear to grow over time, suggesting branches improve with experience.

3. **Meaningful magnitude**: A 2.3-point gain on a ~50-point scale represents real improvement in patron experience.

### **Uncertainty to Acknowledge**

The 95% confidence interval ranges from **-0.5 to +5.1 points**. This means:
- We cannot completely rule out no effect
- The true benefit could be as high as 5 points
- More data would narrow this range
