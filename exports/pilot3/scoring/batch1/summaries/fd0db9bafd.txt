# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** Analysis of Weeks 6–22 Service Score Data  
**Recommendation:** Proceed with System-Wide Rollout

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure saw statistically significant gains of approximately **2.3 points** on the service score index (a 4.6% improvement). We recommend approving immediate rollout to all branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.29 points (p < 0.001) |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | 1.69 to 2.89 points |
| **Branches in Pilot** | 425 (new procedure) / 284 (control) |
| **Observation Period** | 17 weeks (9 before, 8 after switch) |

**What Changed:**
- Switched branches: Scores rose from 50.9 to 52.9 (+2.0 points)
- Control branches: Scores declined from 48.9 to 48.6 (−0.3 points)
- The effect is specific to service quality; no concurrent changes in staffing or visitor traffic explain the improvement

**Why We Are Confident:**
- Large sample size (12,053 branch-week observations)
- Statistically significant at the 1% level
- Effect persists across multiple post-switch weeks
- Control branches provide a valid comparison for underlying trends

**Important Note:**
Branches selected for the pilot had slightly higher baseline scores, which may mean our estimate is somewhat conservative. The true effect could vary, but the positive direction is clear and consistent.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve full rollout of the new procedure to all 709 branches.**

The evidence indicates the new procedure delivers meaningful service improvements without requiring additional staffing or resources. The expected system-wide impact is an average service score increase of approximately 2 points across all branches.
