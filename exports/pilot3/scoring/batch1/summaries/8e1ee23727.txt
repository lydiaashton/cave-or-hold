# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

**The new stock rotation procedure is associated with a meaningful improvement in service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.0 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure. This represents about a **4% improvement** on our service index. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores

Our analysis compared 425 branches that switched to the new procedure (week 15) against 284 branches that continued with the old procedure.

| Group | Before (Weeks 6-14) | After (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 49.97 | 53.92 | +3.95 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 48.43 | 50.36 | +1.92 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.02** |

The **2.02-point net improvement** is statistically significant (p < 0.001), with a 95% confidence interval of 1.46 to 2.59 points.

### 2. Effect Is Consistent Over Time

The improvement persisted throughout the 8-week post-switch period. The gap between switched and non-switched branches widened from an average of 1.5 points before the switch to 3.6 points after, with no evidence of the effect diminishing over time.

### 3. Important Context

Branches selected for early adoption showed slightly stronger improvement trends even before the switch. This suggests the early-adopter branches may have had characteristics (e.g., staff engagement, management quality) that contributed to better outcomes. While our analysis accounts for this, the true effect of the procedure alone may be somewhat smaller than estimated.

---

## Recommendation

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.** The evidence indicates a positive, statistically significant effect on service scores that is unlikely to be due to chance.
