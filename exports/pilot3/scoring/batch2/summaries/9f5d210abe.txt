# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence is **inconclusive but suggestive**. The best estimate is a **2-point improvement** in service scores, but statistical uncertainty means the true effect could range from slightly negative to +4.5 points. There is approximately **95% probability the effect is positive**, but we cannot confirm this with conventional statistical certainty. Given the absence of negative consequences and the potential for meaningful gains, we recommend a **careful expansion with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide rollout.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recommendation** | ❌ Do NOT approve system-wide rollout at this time |
| **Alternative** | ✅ Approve expanded pilot (10-15 additional branches) |
| **Confidence in Finding** | ⚠️ Moderate—evidence suggests benefit but is not definitive |
| **Risk of Full Rollout** | Medium—could commit resources to ineffective change |

**Rationale:** The data leans positive, but not strongly enough to justify committing all 34 branches to a procedural change. An expanded pilot generates better evidence at modest cost.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Estimated Effect Has Substantial Uncertainty**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Point estimate | +2.15 points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.35 to +4.65 points |
| 90% Confidence Interval | +0.05 to +4.25 points |
| Statistical significance | Not significant at 5% level (p=0.10) |
| Probability effect is positive | ~95% |
| Probability effect exceeds 2 points | ~55% |

**Interpretation:** The data leans toward a positive effect, but we cannot rule out no effect or a trivially small effect.

### **2. Both Groups Improved—Question Is Why**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches | 49.67 | 52.97 | +3.31 |
| Non-switched branches | 51.02 | 52.18 | +1.16 |

The control group also improved (+1.16 points), suggesting system-wide factors (seasonal patterns, other initiatives) affected all branches. The new procedure's *additional* contribution is the 2.15-point difference.

### **3. Effects Appear to Strengthen Over Time**

| Period After Switch | Estimated Effect |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Week 1 | -1.0 (not significant) |
| Week 2 | +3.3 (significant) |
| Weeks 3-6 | Variable (0 to +2) |
| Weeks 7-8 | +6.0 to +6.9 (significant) |

This pattern is consistent with a learning curve but could also reflect random variation in a small sample.

### **4. Study Limitations**

- **Not a randomized experiment**: Branches self-selected into switching, which could introduce bias
- **Limited post-switch data**: Only 8 weeks after implementation
- **Moderate sample size**: 34 branches provides limited statistical power
- **Unmeasured factors**: Other changes during this period could affect results

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Cautious Expansion, Not Full Rollout**

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **1. Expand to 10-15 additional branches** | Gather more data before committing all 34 branches |
| **2. Extend monitoring to 6 months** | Determine if effects sustain or fade |
| **3. Document implementation processes** | Identify what distinguishes successful adopters |
| **4. Re-analyze after expansion** | Narrower confidence intervals will support clearer decision |
| **5. Hold full rollout decision for Q2 2027** | Allow time for additional evidence to accumulate |

### **Decision Framework**

| If follow-up shows... | Then... |
|-----------------------|---------|
| Effect ≥ 2 points, p < 0.05 | Proceed with system-wide rollout |
| Effect 1-2 points, any significance | Continue phased expansion |
| Effect < 1 point or negative | Pause and investigate; consider reverting |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure **may improve service quality**, but the evidence is not yet strong enough to justify immediate system-wide implementation. A measured expansion—treating the next phase as a larger pilot—balances the potential benefits against the uncertainty. This approach protects the cooperative from committing to a change that may not deliver meaningful gains while keeping the door open if stronger evidence emerges.
