# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

✅ **The new procedure improves service scores. Roll it out.**

The data provide clear, statistically robust evidence that the new shelving and stock rotation procedure increased service scores by **1.8 points (3.6%)**. This conclusion is supported by multiple analytical methods, all yielding consistent results with high statistical confidence (p < 0.001).

**Board Decision Required:** Approve system-wide implementation.

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and stock rotation procedure achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure—a **3.6% improvement**. This confirms the expected benefit and supports a system-wide rollout.

**Recommendation:** **Approve immediate rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +1.8 service score points |
| **95% confidence interval** | 1.26 to 2.36 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Relative improvement** | 3.6% above baseline |

### Study Overview

- **709 branches** participated: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 continued with the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** collected (weeks 6-22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- Service scores average approximately 50 points across the system (higher is better)

### Why We Are Confident

1. **Multiple analytical methods** all produce the same conclusion (effects range from 1.77 to 1.96 points)
2. **Controls for confounding factors** including staffing levels, visitor volumes, branch-specific characteristics, and week-to-week system-wide variations
3. **Large sample size** with over 12,000 observations provides strong statistical power
4. **No evidence of negative effects** at any branch

---

## **What This Means**

The 1.8-point improvement represents a **meaningful, measurable gain in service quality** for patrons at branches using the new procedure. Given that service scores naturally fluctuate by about 10 points week-to-week, this improvement is both statistically reliable and practically significant.

The result validates the investment in developing and testing the new procedure. Early-adopter branches are already delivering better service, and there is no indication that the effect will diminish when extended to all branches.
