# **REVISED MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a positive signal, but the evidence is not definitive.** Service scores improved modestly at branches that adopted the new procedure (+1.65 points, p=0.005), but there are concerns about whether this fully reflects the procedure's effect. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate systemwide adoption.

---

## **What We Found**

| | **Old Procedure** | **New Procedure** |
|---|---|---|
| Number of branches | 60 | 90 |
| Average score before | 49.15 | 48.49 |
| Average score after | 48.59 | 49.58 |
| **Change** | **-0.56** | **+1.09** |

**Estimated improvement: +1.65 points** (approximately 3.3%, or 0.17 standard deviations)

---

## **Important Caveats**

### 1. **Pre-Existing Differences Between Groups**

In the weeks immediately before the switch (weeks 12–14), treatment branches were scoring **1–2 points lower** than control branches. The post-switch improvement may partly reflect these branches returning to their normal performance (regression to the mean) rather than the procedure itself.

### 2. **Different Pre-Period Trends**

Statistical testing suggests treatment and control branches were on slightly different trajectories even before the switch (p=0.09). This weakens our confidence that the observed effect is purely due to the new procedure.

### 3. **Modest Effect Size**

The 1.65-point improvement is:
- Statistically significant (unlikely to be random chance)
- But modest in practical terms (less than 2 points on a 50-point scale)

### 4. **Original Expectation Was "No Difference"**

The hypothesis was that the procedure change would not affect service scores. We found evidence against this, but the effect is smaller than typical operational improvements that warrant systemwide rollout.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased rollout:**

1. **Expand to 20–30 additional branches** over the next quarter
2. **Monitor service scores closely** during implementation
3. **Reconvene in 90 days** to review whether the effect replicates
4. **Then decide** on full systemwide adoption

This approach balances the positive signal against the uncertainty in the evidence.
