## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with cautious expansion—not full rollout.**

The data provides **moderate evidence of benefit** without any signal of harm. While we lack the statistical certainty that would justify an immediate systemwide mandate, we also lack any reason to withhold a potentially valuable improvement from remaining branches.

**Our verdict rests on three points:**

1. **The evidence points one direction only.** Every analytical test—pre-trend checks, controls, fixed effects, event study—supports a positive effect. There is no contradictory evidence.

2. **The risk is asymmetric.** If we're wrong about the benefit, branches lose little (a procedure change with no cost). If we're wrong about no benefit, 14 branches miss out on meaningful improvement for months or years.

3. **More data will resolve the uncertainty.** A phased expansion with continued monitoring will either confirm the effect (enabling confident full rollout) or reveal it was spurious (allowing us to stop).

**Specific decision:** Authorize the new procedure for **5-7 additional branches** over the next quarter, with a formal review at 6 months. Maintain the remaining 7-9 branches as a comparison group until the review.
