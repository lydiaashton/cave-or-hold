# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** and is statistically significant (p < 0.001). The evidence supports proceeding with a full system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Works
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (425) | 48.8 | 52.9 | +4.1 |
| Non-switched branches (284) | 47.8 | 50.0 | +2.2 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

- **Statistical confidence:** 95% confidence interval is 1.4 to 2.5 points
- **Significance level:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)

### 2. Benefits Are Widespread
- **87% of switched branches** improved their service scores
- Only **13% saw no improvement or decline**
- The effect holds even after accounting for staffing levels and branch traffic

### 3. Study Design Notes
- **709 branches** studied over **17 weeks**
- There was a modest pre-existing upward trend in the gap between groups (+0.25 points/week before the switch)
- Even accounting for this, the post-switch improvement is clear and substantial

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Statistical significance** | ✓ Confirmed (p < 0.001) |
| **Practical significance** | ✓ Meaningful (4% improvement) |
| **Consistency** | ✓ 87% of branches benefited |
| **Robustness** | ✓ Holds with controls for staffing and traffic |
| **Pre-trend concern** | ⚠ Minor—may slightly inflate estimate |

**VERDICT: Proceed with Rollout**

The pre-trend suggests the true effect may be closer to **1.5 points** rather than 1.9, but this remains a meaningful improvement. The weight of evidence clearly favors system-wide adoption.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Approve full rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches |
| **2** | **Develop implementation guidance** based on best practices from top-performing switched branches |
| **3** | **Monitor the 54 switched branches** that did not improve to identify and address barriers |
| **4** | **Schedule follow-up review** in 6 months to confirm sustained gains |

---

## **Expected Impact**

If rolled out to all branches, the new procedure is projected to raise system-wide service scores by approximately **1.5 to 2.0 points**, benefiting patrons across the cooperative.

---

*Analysis based on weekly service scores from 709 branches, weeks 6-22. Standard errors clustered by branch. Difference-in-differences regression with robust standard errors.*
