# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests the new procedure has a positive effect, but the impact is modest.** Switched branches saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (95% CI: 0.8 to 3.3) relative to non-switched branches. This represents about a **4% improvement** over baseline. However, only **59% of switched branches** actually improved, and the effect size is small-to-moderate.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but with measured expectations and close monitoring. The procedure appears beneficial on average, but may not suit all branches equally.

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Statistically Significant but Modest Effect
- **Estimated effect:** +2.1 points (p < 0.01)
- **95% confidence interval:** 0.8 to 3.3 points
- **Effect size:** 0.22 standard deviations (small-to-moderate)
- **Context:** Baseline average is ~50 points; typical week-to-week variation is ~9.5 points

### 2. Divergent Outcomes Between Groups

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| **Switched branches** | 49.7 | 50.7 | **+1.0** |
| **Non-switched branches** | 49.9 | 48.8 | **-1.1** |

The control group's decline during the same period is notable and worth investigating.

### 3. Mixed Results Across Branches
- **59% of switched branches** improved after adoption
- **41% of switched branches** saw no improvement or decline
- **35% of control branches** improved during the same period

This suggests the procedure works well for some branches but not others.

### 4. Methodologically Sound Analysis
- **Parallel pre-trends confirmed** (p = 0.13)
- **Large sample:** 150 branches, 17 weeks
- **Robust to controls** for staffing and visitor volume
