# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores** (93% probability of positive effect) by approximately **1.4 points** (~3% improvement). While not statistically significant at the conventional 5% threshold (p=0.13), there is **no evidence of harm** and **suggestive evidence of benefit** that grows over time. Given the low risk and potential upside, we recommend **expanding the trial to additional branches** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated Effect** | +1.4 points on ~50-point scale |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.13 (not significant at 5% level) |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | -0.4 to +3.2 points |
| **Probability Effect Is Positive** | 93.5% |
| **Branches Studied** | 34 total (20 new procedure, 14 old procedure) |
| **Data Period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22; switch at Week 15) |

### **What the Data Shows**

1. **Modest Positive Effect**: Treatment branches improved by 2.6 points after the switch, compared to 1.2 points for control branches—a net gain of 1.4 points attributable to the new procedure.

2. **Effects Grow Over Time**: The benefit was minimal in weeks 15–16 but increased to 2–3 points by weeks 20–22, suggesting staff need time to fully implement the new procedures.

3. **Pre-Existing Differences Accounted For**: Treatment branches scored ~2.6 points higher than control branches before the switch. Our analysis controls for this baseline difference.

4. **Other Drivers Matter**: Visit volume significantly affects service scores (p=0.007). Branches with higher traffic tend to score better regardless of procedure.

5. **No Evidence of Harm**: The lower bound of the 95% confidence interval (-0.4 points) represents a trivial negative effect. The data do not suggest the new procedure damages service quality.

### **Recommendation: Proceed with Phased Expansion**

**We recommend expanding the new procedure to additional branches while continuing rigorous monitoring.**

**Reasoning:**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|--------------|------------|
| **Direction of evidence** | ✅ Favors expansion (93% probability of positive effect) |
| **Risk of harm** | ✅ Low (no meaningful negative effects detected) |
| **Trajectory** | ✅ Improving (effects grow over time) |
| **Statistical certainty** | ⚠️ Moderate (p=0.13; more data would help) |
| **Operational feasibility** | ? Board must assess costs |

**This is a "green light with caution" decision.** The evidence does not justify system-wide mandatory rollout today, but it does support expanding the trial. The combination of likely benefit, negligible risk, and improving trends over time favors action over delay—provided the cooperative can absorb the operational costs of broader implementation.

**Specific next step:** Approve expansion to 5–10 additional branches by Q1, with a formal re-evaluation scheduled after 12 weeks of post-implementation data.
