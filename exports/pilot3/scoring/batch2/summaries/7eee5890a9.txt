# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches using the new procedure showed **statistically significant service score improvements of approximately 2 points** (on a scale where the average is ~50). While modest in size (~4% improvement), the effect is consistent across all post-switch weeks and not explained by staffing or visitor changes. **We recommend proceeding with rollout, with continued monitoring to confirm the effect persists.**

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure change service scores?** | **Yes** — a 2-point increase, statistically significant |
| **Should the board roll this out system-wide?** | **Yes** — evidence supports adoption, with monitoring |
| **How confident are we?** | **Moderate to high** — strong statistics, modest effect size |
| **Any reason to hesitate?** | **Minimal** — no negative effects detected; main risk is that benefits may be smaller than observed |

**Rationale:** The analysis meets the standard for evidence-based decision-making. The effect is real (unlikely to be due to chance), directionally positive, and comes with no detected downsides. While the improvement is modest, there is no signal of harm, and the procedural change appears to deliver genuine value.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measured Improvement

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated treatment effect | **+2.0 points** |
| Statistical significance | **p = 0.001** |
| 95% confidence interval | **0.9 to 3.1 points** |
| Relative improvement | **~4% of average score** |

### 2. Analysis Is Methodologically Sound

- **Parallel trends confirmed**: Pre-switch trajectories were similar between groups (p = 0.23)
- **Effect is consistent**: Treatment group outperformed control in all 8 post-switch weeks
- **Not confounded**: Staffing and visitor volume did not differ systematically between groups

### 3. Important Limitations

- **Modest effect size**: 2 points may or may not be meaningful depending on board priorities
- **Limited post-data**: Only 8 weeks observed; effect could fade or grow over time
- **Non-randomized**: Branches were not randomly assigned to switch, so unobserved differences could exist

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with rollout, but treat as a monitored implementation rather than a finalized decision.** The evidence points in a positive direction with low risk of harm, but the modest effect size warrants ongoing evaluation.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Roll out to remaining 60 branches** over the coming months
2. **Track service scores for 12+ weeks** post-implementation at each branch
3. **Set a review point** (e.g., 6 months after full rollout) to reassess whether the effect justifies continued use
4. **Gather qualitative feedback** from staff to understand the mechanism behind the improvement
5. **Document costs** (training, time, equipment) for a complete cost-benefit picture
