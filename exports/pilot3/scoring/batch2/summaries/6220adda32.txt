# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

---

## **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. The estimated improvement is +1.8 points (~3.5%), but the data is also consistent with no effect at all. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued evaluation** rather than an immediate system-wide mandate, allowing the cooperative to gather more evidence before full commitment.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

**Recommendation: Proceed with Phased Rollout**

After weighing the evidence, we advise the Board to **approve a phased implementation** of the new procedure. Our reasoning:

| Factor | Assessment | Weight |
|--------|------------|--------|
| Direction of effect | Positive (+1.8 points) | ✓ Favors rollout |
| Statistical certainty | Inconclusive (p = 0.11) | ⚠ Caution warranted |
| Risk of harm | None detected | ✓ Low risk |
| Cost of reversal | Low (procedure can be adjusted) | ✓ Flexible |
| Opportunity cost | Delaying denies potential benefit | ✓ Favors rollout |

**Verdict:** The potential upside (+3.5% service improvement) outweighs the risks given the low cost of reversal. However, the uncertainty demands continued monitoring rather than blind commitment.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch Date** | Week 15 |
| **Branches Analyzed** | 34 total |
| **Treatment Group** | 20 branches (new procedure) |
| **Control Group** | 14 branches (old procedure) |
| **Post-Switch Data** | 8 weeks per branch (limited) |

### Results Summary

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 51.4 | 50.7 | −0.7 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 51.1 | 52.2 | +1.1 |
| **Net Treatment Effect** | | | **+1.8** |

### What the Statistics Tell Us

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | +1.79 points | Best estimate from the data |
| 95% Confidence Interval | −0.43 to +4.00 | True effect could be zero or modestly positive |
| p-value | 0.114 | Not statistically significant at conventional levels |
| Probability effect is positive | ~94% | Suggestive, but not definitive |

### Important Considerations

1. **Limited post-switch data**: Only 8 weeks of observation after the change may be insufficient to detect a true effect with confidence

2. **Non-random assignment**: Branches were not randomly assigned to switch, which could introduce bias (though pre-switch trends were similar between groups)

3. **Modest effect size**: Even if the +1.8 point effect is real, it represents a ~3.5% improvement—meaningful but not transformative

4. **Control group decline**: The control group's scores dropped slightly post-switch while treatment branches improved, suggesting the procedure may help buffer against external pressures

---

## **IMPLEMENTATION PLAN**

**Phase 1 (Weeks 1–4):** Roll out to 5–7 additional branches  
**Phase 2 (Weeks 5–8):** Evaluate interim results; proceed to remaining branches if trends hold  
**Phase 3 (Weeks 9–12):** Full system adoption pending positive confirmation  

**Success Criteria for Full Rollout:**
- Effect remains positive (≥ +1.0 points)
- p-value improves to ≤ 0.10 with additional data
- No significant implementation costs or staff complaints emerge

**Stop/Modify Triggers:**
- Effect turns negative
- Implementation proves more costly than anticipated
- Staff feedback indicates operational problems
