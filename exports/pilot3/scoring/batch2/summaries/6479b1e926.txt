# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is promising but not conclusive.** Branches using the new procedure show service score improvements of approximately **2 points**, but we cannot rule out the possibility that the true effect is zero. The decision to roll out should depend on implementation costs:

- **If costs are low:** Proceed with rollout—the likely benefit outweighs the risk
- **If costs are substantial:** Gather more evidence before committing

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Studied**
- **34 branches** over **17 weeks**
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| New procedure branches | 47.9 | 51.3 | +3.4 |
| Old procedure branches | 51.0 | 52.3 | +1.3 |
| **Attributed to new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

### **How Confident Are We?**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +2.3 points | Best guess of the effect |
| P-value | 0.11 | Not statistically significant |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.5 to +5.1 | True effect could be slightly negative or substantially positive |
| Probability effect is positive | ~94% | Based on the observed data |

### **Supporting Evidence**
- **All post-switch weeks show positive effects**—no negative results
- **Effects appear to grow over time**, suggesting staff proficiency improves
- **Pre-switch trends were parallel**, validating our comparison

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with a phased rollout, with continued monitoring.**

**Rationale:**
1. **The expected value is positive.** Even accounting for uncertainty, there is approximately a 94% probability the effect is beneficial, and the point estimate (+2.3 points) represents meaningful service improvement.

2. **No evidence of harm.** None of the 20 treatment branches showed negative effects, and the lower bound of our confidence interval (-0.5) suggests any downside risk is minimal.

3. **Effects may grow over time.** The event study shows larger effects in later weeks, suggesting the full benefit may not yet be realized.

4. **Waiting has costs.** Delaying rollout means patrons at 14 branches continue receiving lower-quality service than they might otherwise.

**Recommended approach:**
- Roll out to remaining 14 branches over the next 8-12 weeks
- Continue tracking service scores for at least one full quarter post-rollout
- If effects remain positive but inconclusive after additional data, the accumulated benefit will still justify the decision
