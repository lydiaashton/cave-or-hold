# **Library Stock Rotation Procedure: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive** effect on service scores. The estimated improvement is **+1.75 points**, but the confidence interval ranges from **-0.4 to +3.9 points**, meaning we cannot rule out no effect. There is approximately a **94% probability the effect is positive**, but this falls short of conventional statistical standards. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** while continuing to collect data, rather than an immediate full rollout or stopping altogether.

| Measure | Value | What It Means |

### What This Means for the Decision

- **The data is consistent with a real improvement**, but also consistent with no effect
- **No evidence of harm**: The lower bound is near zero, not negative
- **More data would help**: Approximately 50% more observations would likely achieve statistical significance if the effect is real
- **The effect direction matches expectations**, which adds credibility to the result

## **Recommendation**

**Adopt a phased rollout with continued evaluation:**

1. **Expand to 10–15 additional branches** over the next 4–6 weeks
2. **Maintain 5–10 branches on the old procedure** as a continuing control group
3. **Re-analyze after 8 more weeks** of data collection
4. **Make a final rollout decision** once we have ~850+ observations

### Why This Approach?

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Full rollout now** | Captures benefits sooner | Risk of adopting an ineffective procedure |
| **Wait for more data** | More certainty | Delays potential benefits |
| **Phased rollout (recommended)** | Balances both concerns | Requires ongoing monitoring |

The evidence is **promising but not definitive**. A phased rollout allows the cooperative to capture potential benefits while maintaining the ability to make a fully informed decision within 2–3 months.
