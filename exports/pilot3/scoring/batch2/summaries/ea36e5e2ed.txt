# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a statistically significant but modest improvement in service scores, with important caveats.** The average effect is **+2.1 points (4%)**, but the weekly effects are inconsistent—some weeks show no benefit or even negative effects. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide adoption.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Statistical Evidence** | ✅ Significant (p < 0.001) |
| **Practical Significance** | ⚠️ Modest (4% improvement, 0.21 SD) |
| **Consistency** | ⚠️ Variable across weeks |
| **Data Sufficiency** | ⚠️ Limited (8 weeks post-switch) |
| **Recommendation** | **Proceed with caution—phased rollout** |

**Verdict: The new procedure likely helps, but the evidence is not yet strong enough to justify immediate system-wide adoption. Expand carefully, monitor closely, and revisit the decision after 12 weeks of additional data.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Positive Average Effect, But Variable
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Average service score increase | +2.08 points |
| 95% Confidence interval | 1.0 to 3.2 points |
| Percentage improvement | 4.2% |
| Effect size | 0.21 standard deviations (small-to-moderate) |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |

### 2. Weekly Effects Are Inconsistent
The treatment effect varied considerably across the 8 post-switch weeks:
- **Weeks 15-18:** Consistent positive effect (+1.7 to +2.5 points)
- **Weeks 19-21:** Effect diminished or reversed (-1.4 to +0.6 points)
- **Week 22:** Effect recovered (+1.9 points)

This pattern suggests the benefit may not be stable over time, or that other factors (seasonal, operational) are influencing scores.

### 3. Limited Observation Window
- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** limits our ability to assess:
  - Whether the effect is durable long-term
  - Whether there are learning/adaptation effects
  - Whether the variability is normal fluctuation or a concerning pattern

### 4. Research Design Is Sound
- Parallel pre-trends confirmed (p = 0.28)
- 150 branches, 2,550 observations
- Effect persists after controlling for staffing and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased rollout, not immediate system-wide adoption.**

| Rationale | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Effect is real but modest | Expand to additional branches, but not all |
| Weekly variability is concerning | Monitor closely before full commitment |
| Only 8 weeks of data | Gather more evidence before locking in |
