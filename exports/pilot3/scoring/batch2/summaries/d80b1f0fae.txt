# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with rollout, but temper expectations.** The new procedure likely improves service scores, though the true effect is probably smaller than our initial estimate of +2.1 points.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Metric | Result | Confidence |
|--------|--------|------------|
| **Estimated Effect** | +2.1 points | Moderate |
| **Likely True Effect** | +1.0 to +2.0 points | — |
| **Direction of Impact** | Positive | High |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 | High |
| **Recommendation** | **Roll out with monitoring** | — |

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Measured
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure in Week 15
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (comparison group)
- Service scores tracked weekly from Week 6 through Week 22

### Results

| | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branches with new procedure | 49.8 | 53.3 | +3.5 |
| Branches with old procedure | 48.2 | 49.6 | +1.4 |
| **Net improvement (raw estimate)** | | | **+2.1** |

### Important Caveat

**Branches that switched were already improving faster before the change.** Our pre-trend analysis shows switched branches gained ~0.25 points/week more than control branches even before Week 15 (p = 0.012). This suggests:

- The +2.1 point estimate likely **overstates** the true effect
- A more realistic estimate is **+1.0 to +2.0 points**
- The **direction is still positive**—the procedure helps, just perhaps less than it appears

### Why We Still Recommend Rollout

1. **No risk of harm:** There is no evidence the new procedure negatively affects any metric.

2. **Lower bound is still meaningful:** Even the conservative end of the confidence interval (+1.4 points) represents a ~3% service improvement.

3. **Effect is sustained:** The gap between switched and control branches did not shrink over the 8-week post-switch period.

4. **Operational benefits may exist:** Service score is one metric; staff efficiency or patron satisfaction may also improved (not measured in this analysis).

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout with the following conditions:**

1. Set realistic expectations: anticipate ~1–2 point score improvements, not 2+ points
2. Track service scores for 12 weeks after each branch switches to confirm gains materialize
3. Investigate whether certain branch types benefit more or less from the procedure
