**Study Design:**
- 150 branches total: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 kept the old procedure
- 17 weeks of data (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- Analysis uses a difference-in-differences approach, comparing changes in switched branches against changes in non-switched branches

**Validity Checks:**
- **Parallel trends assumption confirmed**: Before the switch, there was no statistically significant difference in trends between the two groups (p = 0.28), supporting the causal interpretation of the results
- Results are robust to clustering standard errors by branch

**What the numbers mean:**
- Branches that switched improved by about 1.0 point after the change
- Branches that didn't switch actually declined by about 1.1 point over the same period
- The net effect of the new procedure is the 2.1-point difference between these two trends
