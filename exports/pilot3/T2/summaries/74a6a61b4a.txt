# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Yes, the new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (about 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and represents a meaningful improvement in branch service quality. We recommend proceeding with a full rollout.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Design
- **150 branches** total: 90 switched to the new procedure, 60 kept the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (Weeks 6–22), with the switch implemented in Week 15
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach, comparing changes in switched branches against non-switched branches

### Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.68 | 48.80 | -0.88 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 49.25 | 50.47 | +1.22 |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+2.10** |

### Statistical Evidence
- **Treatment effect: +2.10 points** (95% confidence interval: 0.82 to 3.38)
- **Statistically significant at p < 0.01**
- Effect remains significant after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Parallel trends validated**: Before the switch, both groups showed similar week-to-week patterns, confirming the comparison is valid

### What This Means
- Branches using the new procedure improved while control branches slightly declined
- The 2.1-point gain represents roughly a **4% improvement** relative to baseline scores
- The benefit appears consistent and is not explained by changes in staffing or visitor traffic

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve full rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches
2. **Monitor implementation** to ensure the new procedure is followed consistently across all locations
3. **Track service scores** for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm the effect persists at scale
4. **Document best practices** from high-performing switched branches to support training
5. **Consider cost-benefit analysis** if the new procedure involves additional operational expenses
