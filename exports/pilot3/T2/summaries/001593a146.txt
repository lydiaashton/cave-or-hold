**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Yes, the new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **1.85 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.3 to 2.4 points) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **3.7% improvement** over the baseline average score of 50.5. The effect is statistically significant and has grown over time since implementation.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. Study Design
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure (treatment group)
- **284 branches** kept the old procedure (control group)
- Switch occurred at **week 15**
- Analysis uses difference-in-differences methodology, comparing changes in treatment branches to changes in control branches

### 2. Main Result
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.85 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **[1.30, 2.41]** |
| Statistical significance | **p < 0.001** |
| Relative improvement | **3.7%** |

### 3. Effect Over Time
The benefit of the new procedure **increased over time**:
- Weeks 15–17 (immediate): ~1.2 to 1.4 point improvement
- Weeks 18–22 (later): ~2.0 to 2.7 point improvement

This pattern suggests branches may have needed a few weeks to fully realize the benefits of the new procedure.

### 4. Validity Checks
- **Parallel trends:** Service score trends for treatment and control branches were similar before the switch, supporting the validity of the comparison
- **Controls accounted for:** The result holds after adjusting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Robustness:** Bootstrap analysis confirms the 95% confidence interval of [1.4, 2.3] points

### 5. Practical Significance
- The 1.85-point improvement equals about **0.19 standard deviations**—a modest but meaningful effect for a service quality metric
- Given the cooperative serves hundreds of branches, even a small per-branch improvement aggregates to substantial system-wide gains
- The growing effect over time suggests continued benefits as staff gain familiarity with the new procedure
