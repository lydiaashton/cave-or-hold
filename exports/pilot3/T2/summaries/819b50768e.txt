# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**Yes, the new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by an average of **2.1 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and represents a meaningful gain in service quality. We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**Study Design**
- Analyzed 17 weeks of data (weeks 6–22) from all 709 library branches
- 425 branches adopted the new procedure starting week 15; 284 branches continued with the old procedure
- Used a difference-in-differences approach to isolate the effect of the new procedure

**Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 48.15 | 49.59 | +1.44 |
| Branches with new procedure | 49.80 | 53.31 | +3.51 |
| **Effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.07** |

- The new procedure increased service scores by **2.07 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.53 to 2.61)
- This result is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)
- The effect represents approximately a **4% improvement** relative to the average service score of 50

**Effect Over Time**
- The improvement was not immediate but grew over time
- Service scores for branches with the new procedure showed increasingly positive effects in weeks 5–8 after implementation
- This pattern suggests staff may have needed time to fully adapt to the new procedure
