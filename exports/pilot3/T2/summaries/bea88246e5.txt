# **Board Summary: Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to initial expectations that the change would have no effect, branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.0 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.2 to 2.8 points) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **3.9% improvement** relative to the average service score. The result is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001).

**Recommendation:** The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Study Design
- **150 branches** total: 90 adopted the new procedure (treatment), 60 kept the old procedure (control)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach, which compares changes in treatment branches to changes in control branches, controlling for branch-specific and week-specific factors

### 2. Main Result
| Period | Treatment Branches | Control Branches | Difference |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|------------|
| Pre-switch (weeks 6–14) | 51.13 | 50.57 | +0.56 |
| Post-switch (weeks 15–22) | 53.42 | 50.87 | +2.55 |
| **Change** | **+2.29** | **+0.30** | **+1.99** ★ |

★ The difference-in-differences estimate of **+1.99 points** is the causal effect of the new procedure.

### 3. Statistical Confidence
- **Coefficient:** +1.99 points
- **95% Confidence Interval:** [1.22, 2.75] points
- **P-value:** < 0.001 (highly significant)
- The confidence interval excludes zero, indicating we can be confident the effect is real and positive

### 4. Parallel Trends Validation
A critical assumption for this analysis is that treatment and control branches would have followed similar trends absent the intervention. The event study analysis confirms:
- **Pre-switch trends were parallel:** All pre-switch week coefficients are statistically indistinguishable from zero (all p > 0.05)
- **Post-switch effects emerged after implementation:** Positive effects appear starting in week 15 and persist through week 22
- This pattern supports a causal interpretation of the results

### 5. Effect Size Context
- Average service score across all branches: **51.6 points**
- The new procedure's effect of **+2.0 points** represents a **3.9% improvement**
- For comparison, typical week-to-week variation in service scores has a standard deviation of about 10 points, so this is a meaningful and sustained improvement

- **Data:** 2,550 branch-week observations (150 branches × 17 weeks)
