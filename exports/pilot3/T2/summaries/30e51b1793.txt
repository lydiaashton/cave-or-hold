# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**Yes, the new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new shelving and rotation procedure saw their service scores increase by an average of **2.1 points** (approximately **4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and the recommendation is to **proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores**
- **Treatment branches** (425 branches that switched): Service scores increased from 51.1 to 54.0 after the switch (+2.9 points)
- **Control branches** (284 branches that did not switch): Service scores increased from 49.9 to 50.7 (+0.8 points)
- **Net effect of the new procedure**: **+2.1 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.5 to 2.7 points)
- The result is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)

### **2. Broad-Based Improvement**
- **79% of branches** that adopted the new procedure saw their scores improve
- Only **57% of branches** keeping the old procedure saw improvement
- This difference is statistically significant, confirming the new procedure benefits most branches

### **3. Analysis Method**
The analysis used a **difference-in-differences approach**, comparing:
- Branches that switched vs. those that didn't
- Before the switch (weeks 6-14) vs. after (weeks 15-22)
- This method controls for overall trends and branch-specific characteristics

### **4. Robustness Checks**
- Results hold when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Treatment and control branches were well-balanced on observable characteristics before the switch
- The effect is consistent across the post-switch period

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches

2. **Monitor implementation** during rollout:
   - Track service scores weekly for early detection of any issues
   - Expect approximately 2-point improvement per branch on average

   - About 21% of branches did not see improvement even with the new procedure

- **Switch date**: Week 15
