# **MEMORANDUM**

## **To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors
## **From:** Data Analysis Team
## **Date:** November 14, 2026
## **Subject:** Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to the initial expectation that the change would make no difference, branches that adopted the new procedure maintained their service levels while control branches experienced a notable decline. The estimated improvement is **+1.74 points** (95% CI: 0.64 to 2.83), statistically significant at the 1% level (p=0.002). **We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Design**
- **150 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15 (treatment group)
- **60 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Analysis uses difference-in-differences methodology, which isolates the effect of the procedure change from other time-related factors

### **Results**

| Metric | Treatment (New) | Control (Old) |
|--------|-----------------|---------------|
| Pre-switch average (weeks 6–14) | 49.91 | 53.34 |
| Post-switch average (weeks 15–22) | 49.79 | 51.57 |
| **Change** | **-0.12** | **-1.78** |

**Treatment Effect: +1.74 points** (p=0.002)

### **What This Means**
- Control branches declined by 1.78 points after week 15
- Treatment branches remained essentially flat (-0.12 points)
- **The new procedure prevented an expected decline**, effectively improving scores by 1.74 points relative to what would have happened
- This represents approximately **3.4% improvement** relative to baseline scores
- **44% of treatment branches** showed score improvement vs. only **32% of control branches**

### **Validity Checks**
- **Parallel trends assumption confirmed**: Pre-switch trends were statistically similar between groups (p=0.55)
- **Robust to controls**: Effect remains significant after adjusting for staffing and visit levels
- **Cluster-robust standard errors**: Account for correlation within branches over time

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to remaining 60 branches

2. **Monitor implementation**: Track service scores for the next 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale

3. **Document best practices**: Investigate whether certain branches benefited more than others to identify implementation factors that maximize impact

4. **Cost-benefit analysis**: Quantify the operational costs of the new procedure against the service score improvement to assess ROI

5. **Consider complementary measures**: Since staffing and visit levels also correlate with service scores, explore whether the new procedure interacts with these factors
