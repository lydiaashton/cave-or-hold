# **Analysis of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores** by approximately **2.1 points** (on a scale where the average branch scores about 50). This effect is **statistically significant** (p < 0.01), meaning it is very unlikely to be due to random chance. Contrary to the expectation that the procedure change would make no difference, the data shows a meaningful positive impact on service quality.

**Recommendation:** The board should consider rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Study Design
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **60 branches** continued with the old procedure
- This "natural experiment" design allows us to isolate the effect of the procedure change

### 2. Main Result: Service Score Improvement
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect of new procedure | **+2.10 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **+0.78 to +3.42 points** |
| Statistical significance | **p = 0.002** (highly significant) |

Branches using the new procedure saw service scores approximately 2 points higher than they would have under the old procedure, after accounting for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week variation.

### 3. Validation: Parallel Trends Assumption
A critical requirement for this analysis is that treated and control branches were on similar trajectories before the switch. The data supports this:
- **Pre-switch trend test:** p = 0.74 (no significant difference in trends)
- **Week-by-week gaps before week 15:** Fluctuated around zero (range: -1.7 to +0.8)
- **Week-by-week gaps after week 15:** Consistently positive (range: +1.3 to +2.0)

This pattern—no systematic difference before the switch, then a sustained positive gap after—strengthens confidence that the procedure change caused the improvement.

### 4. Magnitude of Effect
A 2.1-point increase represents approximately a **4% improvement** relative to the average service score of 50. This is a meaningful gain in service quality.

## **Technical Notes**
