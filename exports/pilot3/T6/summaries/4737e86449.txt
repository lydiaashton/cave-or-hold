# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure produced a **statistically significant but modest improvement** in service scores. Switched branches gained an average of **1.9 points** (~4% improvement) compared to non-switched branches. However, **32% of switched branches saw scores decline**, indicating the procedure does not benefit all locations equally.

**Recommendation: Approve rollout with implementation support and 6-month review.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. Measured Impact

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | **+1.88 points** (95% CI: 1.2 to 2.6) |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Relative improvement | ~3.7% of baseline |
| Effect size | ~19% of one standard deviation |

### 2. Before-and-After Comparison

| Group | Branches | Before | After | Change |
|-------|----------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched to new procedure | 425 | 50.8 | 52.6 | **+1.8** |
| Kept old procedure | 284 | 50.2 | 50.1 | -0.1 |

### 3. Branch-Level Outcomes

| Outcome | Treatment Branches | Control Branches |
|---------|-------------------|------------------|
| Scores improved | 67.5% | 47.9% |
| Scores declined | 32.5% | 52.1% |

**Key insight:** The new procedure appears to *protect against declines* as much as it drives improvements.

### 4. Important Caveats

- **Variable effectiveness:** Nearly 1 in 3 switched branches did not benefit
- **Limited timeframe:** Only 8 weeks of post-implementation data
- **No cost data:** Implementation costs not analyzed
- **Modest effect:** 1.9 points may be meaningful but is not transformative

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve phased rollout** to remaining 284 branches over 3-6 months

2. **Implement support measures:**
   - Training materials based on high-performing branches
   - Designated implementation contacts for troubleshooting
   - Early warning system for branches showing no improvement after 4 weeks

3. **Monitor key metrics:**
   - Service scores at 4, 8, and 12 weeks post-switch
   - Staff time/cost requirements (currently unmeasured)
   - Branch-level variation to identify who benefits most

4. **Conduct 6-month review** before full system-wide commitment:
   - Confirm sustained improvements
   - Assess cost-benefit ratio
   - Identify branches where alternative approaches may be needed

---

## **METHODOLOGY NOTE**

Difference-in-differences analysis of 709 branches over 17 weeks (weeks 6-22), with procedure switch in week 15. Model controls for branch characteristics, time trends, staffing, and visitor volume. 425 treatment branches, 284 control branches.
