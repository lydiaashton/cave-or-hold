# **New Shelving and Rotation Procedure: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The data shows a positive effect, though this was not anticipated.** Branches using the new procedure had service scores approximately **2 points higher** than expected based on comparison branches. The result is statistically significant (p < 0.001), but given that no effect was expected, we recommend **phased rollout with verification** rather than immediate systemwide adoption.

### What Supports This Finding

1. **Clean identification:** Switched and non-switched branches had identical average scores before the change (49.78 vs. 49.78)
2. **Divergence after week 15:** Treated branches improved (+1.53); control branches declined slightly (-0.64)
3. **Robust to controls:** Effect persists accounting for staffing and visitor volume
4. **Statistically significant:** Confidence interval excludes zero

### What Gives Us Pause

1. **Unexpected result:** This contradicts prior expectations about the procedure's impact
2. **Modest magnitude:** 2 points is meaningful but not transformative
3. **Limited post-period:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data
4. **Mechanism unclear:** We don't yet know *why* scores improved
