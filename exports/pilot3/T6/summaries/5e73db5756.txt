# **REVISED MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Analysis of New Branch Procedure – Recommendation with Important Caveats

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure is associated with higher service scores, but we cannot be fully confident the procedure *caused* the improvement.** The estimated effect ranges from **0.7 to 2.0 points** depending on analytical approach. We recommend **proceeding with rollout, but with careful monitoring** to confirm the effect persists.

---

## **Critical Finding: Pre-Existing Differences**

Our analysis revealed an important complication:

| Week | Treatment Group | Control Group | Gap |
|------|-----------------|---------------|-----|
| 6 (earliest) | 48.01 | 48.82 | **−0.81** |
| 10 | 51.66 | 51.50 | **+0.16** |
| 14 (before switch) | 50.36 | 49.26 | **+1.11** |

**The treatment branches were already trending upward relative to control branches *before* the switch.** This means the 1.8-point estimate may capture pre-existing differences, not just the treatment effect.

---

## **Range of Estimates**

| Analytical Approach | Estimated Effect | Confidence |
|---------------------|------------------|------------|
| Standard DiD (all data) | 1.81 points | High statistical significance |
| Excluding anomalous Week 13 | 1.92 points | High statistical significance |
| Matched comparison (similar pre-scores) | 1.98 points | High statistical significance |
| **Recent weeks only (most comparable)** | **0.71 points** | **Marginal significance (p=0.04)** |

**Most conservative credible estimate: ~0.7 to 1.0 points (1.4% to 2.0% improvement)**

---

## **Why We Still Recommend Rollout**

1. **All estimates are positive** – no analysis suggests the procedure harms service scores
2. **Statistically significant in most specifications** – the effect is unlikely to be zero
3. **No downside detected** – branches that switched showed no negative consequences
4. **Operational momentum** – 425 branches have already invested in the change
5. **Low risk of reversal** – if the effect proves smaller than expected, the procedure can be adjusted

---

## **Revised Recommendation**

**Proceed with rollout, but implement a rigorous monitoring plan:**

### During Rollout
- Track service scores monthly for all transitioning branches
- Compare actual post-switch performance against the 0.7–1.8 point expected range
- Identify branches where scores don't improve and investigate why

### After Rollout (90-day review)
- Conduct formal evaluation comparing actual outcomes to projections
- If effect is <0.5 points, reconsider whether continued investment is warranted
- If effect is ≥1.0 points, confirm full success and document best practices

## **Technical Note**

The standard Difference-in-Differences method assumes treatment and control groups would have followed parallel trends absent the intervention. Our data shows the groups had different pre-treatment trajectories, which introduces uncertainty about the causal effect. The true effect likely lies between our most conservative (0.7 points) and most liberal (2.0 points) estimates.

---

**We stand behind the recommendation to proceed, but the board should expect modest rather than dramatic improvements, and should verify the effect materializes as expected.**
