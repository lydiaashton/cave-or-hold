# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence **suggests the new procedure improves service scores**, but we cannot confirm this with high statistical confidence. Branches using the new procedure gained approximately **2 points (4%)** on service scores relative to branches using the old procedure. The statistical uncertainty means the true effect could range from negligible (-0.5 points) to meaningfully positive (+4.4 points)—but **harm appears unlikely**.

**Recommendation:** Given the positive direction of evidence, minimal downside risk, and the possibility of meaningful benefit, we recommend **proceeding with rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes. A full immediate rollout is reasonable if the new procedure has low implementation costs. If costs are significant, a phased approach allows continued evidence-gathering.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** participated over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Analysis compares changes in switched branches against changes in non-switched branches (difference-in-differences)

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 46.6 | 48.2 | +1.6 |
| Non-switched branches | 47.4 | 47.2 | -0.3 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+1.9 points** |

### **What the Statistics Mean**

| Metric | Value | Plain Language |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +1.9 points | Best guess: scores improved by ~2 points |
| 95% confidence interval | -0.5 to +4.4 | True effect likely falls in this range |
| p-value | 0.11 | Cannot rule out "no effect" at conventional thresholds |
| Probability effect is positive | ~89% | Evidence favors improvement |

### **Why We Can't Be More Certain**

The main limitation is **sample size**, not evidence of ineffectiveness:
- With only 34 branches, we lack power to detect small effects confidently
- The study could reliably detect only effects larger than ~9 points
- The observed ~2 point effect is real but too small to confirm definitively with this data

### **Risk Assessment**

| Scenario | Likelihood | Impact |
|----------|------------|--------|
| **Meaningful improvement** (+3 to +4 points) | Plausible | Positive for patrons |
| **Small improvement** (+1 to +2 points) | Most likely | Modest benefit |
| **No effect** (0 points) | Possible | No loss |
| **Slight decline** (-0.5 points) | Unlikely | Minimal harm |
