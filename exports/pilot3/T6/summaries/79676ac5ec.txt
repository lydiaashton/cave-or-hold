## **BOTTOM LINE**

The data show a **positive signal but are statistically inconclusive**. The estimated effect is +1.8 points, but the 95% confidence interval (−0.4 to +4.0) includes zero. **We cannot confidently say the new procedure improves service scores**—though we also find no evidence of harm. The Board must decide whether to act on an uncertain but promising signal.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### What the Data Show

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +1.79 points |
| Standard error | 1.13 points |
| p-value | 0.12 |
| 95% Confidence Interval | −0.4 to +4.0 |

### What This Means

- **The result is not statistically significant** at conventional levels (p < 0.05)
- **The data are compatible with multiple scenarios:**
  - No real effect (0 points)
  - Modest improvement (~2 points)
  - Moderate improvement (~4 points)
  - Very small negative effect (down to −0.4 points)

- **Study limitations:** With 34 branches over 17 weeks, we can only reliably detect effects of ~2.2 points or larger. Smaller effects cannot be distinguished from random variation.

### What We Can Say With Confidence

1. **No evidence of harm:** The lower bound of the confidence interval is −0.4 points—a negligible decrease
2. **Consistent direction:** All model specifications show positive estimates
3. **Valid design:** Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups

## **RECOMMENDATION**

Given the uncertainty, we present **two options** for Board consideration:

| Option | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **A. Phased rollout with monitoring** | Proceed cautiously—expand to additional branches while continuing to measure outcomes. This balances potential benefit against uncertainty. |
| **B. Extend evaluation period** | Continue the current pilot for 8–12 more weeks to reduce uncertainty before system-wide decision. |

**Our preference: Option A.** The absence of negative evidence, combined with a positive point estimate, suggests proceeding is reasonable—but with continued monitoring to confirm the effect.
