- **Post-switch (weeks 15–22):** The gap widened to an average of 2.55 points
- **All 8 post-switch weeks** showed positive effects (range: +1.6 to +3.3 points)
- **Staffing and visit patterns** did not differ systematically between groups

**3. Important Context**
- Service scores have substantial week-to-week variation (SD ≈ 10 points)
- The treatment indicators explain only ~1.3% of total score variance
- A 2-point shift is detectable but modest relative to normal fluctuations
- This was an observational study—unmeasured factors could still play a role

**4. Why We're Cautious**
- We expected **no effect** on service scores from this operational change
- The mechanism is unclear—rotation/shelving procedures shouldn't directly affect patron service
- Possible explanations (staff efficiency, signaling effects, measurement artifact) need investigation
- Replication at remaining branches would strengthen confidence
