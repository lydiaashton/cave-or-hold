# **Board Summary: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure likely improved service scores, but the effect is smaller and less certain than initial analysis suggested.** The best estimate is a **0.5 to 1.5 point improvement** (not 1.85 points), representing roughly **1-3% gain** in service quality. No branches were harmed. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but set modest expectations and monitor closely.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Positive Effect, But Smaller Than Initial Estimate

| Analysis Approach | Estimated Effect | Statistical Significance |
|-------------------|------------------|--------------------------|
| Full sample (weeks 6-22) | +1.85 points | Significant (p<0.001) |
| Immediate pre/post (weeks 13-18) | +0.32 points | Not significant (p=0.37) |
| Simple 3-week comparison | +0.47 points | — |

**Why the difference?** Branches that switched were already improving faster than non-switched branches *before* the procedure change. This inflates the full-sample estimate. The more conservative immediate pre/post comparison suggests a smaller true effect.

### 2. Consistent Positive Direction Across All Measures

- **92.9%** of switched branches improved vs. **84.5%** of non-switched branches
- **Zero branches** showed harm from the switch
- The treatment-control gap **grew over time** in the post-period (suggesting effects may accumulate with experience)

### 3. Important Caveat

The study's key assumption—that switched and non-switched branches would have followed similar trends absent the intervention—is **not fully met**. Switched branches were on a stronger upward trajectory before the switch. This means:
- The true effect is **uncertain**
- The 1.85 point estimate is likely an **upper bound**
- A more realistic expectation is **0.5 to 1.5 points**
