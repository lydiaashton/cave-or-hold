# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure is associated with a **2.7-point improvement in service scores** (approximately 5%). However, this result **does not meet conventional statistical significance thresholds** (p=0.087; 95% CI includes zero). The evidence points positive—all post-switch effects favor the new procedure—but we cannot conclusively rule out chance variation. We recommend **continued pilot expansion with additional data collection** before committing to system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** implemented the new procedure; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Switch implemented at **week 15**
- Difference-in-differences analysis with cluster-robust standard errors

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 52.85 | 56.09 | **+3.23** |
| Old Procedure Branches | 50.84 | 51.63 | +0.78 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.45** |

### **Statistical Summary**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point Estimate | +2.67 points | Estimated improvement |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.39 to +5.74 | Range of plausible values |
| P-value | 0.087 | Not significant at 0.05 level |
| Probability Effect > 0 | ~96% | Likelihood of positive impact |

### **What the Data Shows**

**Supporting Evidence:**
- ✅ Pre-switch trends are parallel (validates comparison)
- ✅ All post-switch point estimates are positive
- ✅ Effect persists after controlling for staffing and visits
- ✅ No negative effects observed in any week

**Limitations:**
- ⚠️ Confidence interval includes zero (no effect is plausible)
- ⚠️ P-value exceeds 0.05 threshold (conventional standard)
- ⚠️ Only 8 weeks of post-switch data
- ⚠️ 34 branches may be insufficient for definitive conclusions

## **Recommendation**

### **Expand Pilot, Delay Full Rollout**

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Full rollout now** | Captures benefits sooner | Risk if effect is spurious |
| **Abandon procedure** | Avoids implementation costs | May discard real improvement |
| **Expand pilot (recommended)** | Gathers more data, limits risk | Delays potential benefits |

### **Implementation Plan**

1. **Add 10–15 branches** to the treatment group over the next 8 weeks
2. **Continue weekly tracking** for all branches (target: 16+ weeks post-switch data)
3. **Reconvene in 3 months** with expanded dataset for final rollout decision

### **Decision Threshold**

We recommend proceeding to full rollout if follow-up analysis shows:
- P-value < 0.05, **and**
- Lower bound of 95% CI > 0, **and**
- Effect size remains ≥2 points

---

## **Conclusion**

The data are **encouraging but not conclusive**. The new procedure shows a positive association with service scores, and there is no evidence of harm. However, the statistical uncertainty means we cannot confidently attribute the improvement to the procedure alone. A modest expansion of the pilot—with a clear decision timeline—balances the opportunity for improvement against the risk of implementing an ineffective change system-wide.

**We recommend board approval to expand the pilot program and reconvene in Q1 for a final rollout decision.**
