# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure is associated with higher service scores, but causality is uncertain.** Branches using the new procedure score ~1.8 points higher than expected, but because switching branches were already improving faster before adoption, we cannot definitively attribute this to the procedure itself. **Recommend proceeding with caution**—consider a controlled pilot rollout rather than immediate system-wide adoption.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What the Data Shows

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Statistical association** | +1.84 points (p < 0.001) |
| **95% Confidence interval** | 1.28 to 2.41 points |
| **Relative difference** | ~4% over baseline |

### Important Caveat

**Pre-existing differences between groups:**
- Treatment branches scored 1.2 points higher than control even before switching
- The gap was widening *before* week 15 (from -0.23 to +2.73 points)
- This suggests branches that switched may have been systematically different (better managed, more resources, etc.)

### What This Means

The analysis **cannot rule out selection bias**. The observed improvement may reflect:
1. The procedure's true effect (likely positive, but possibly smaller than 1.8 points)
2. Pre-existing advantages of switching branches
3. Some combination of both

**Proceed with rollout, but implement monitoring safeguards:**

| Action | Rationale |
