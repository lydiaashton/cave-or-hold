Before the switch, treatment and control branches were already converging:

| Week | Treatment-Control Gap |
|------|----------------------|

This **narrowing gap before the intervention** raises questions about whether the post-switch improvement is truly caused by the new procedure or reflects an ongoing trend. This weakens confidence in the causal interpretation.
