# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. Switched branches improved by approximately **1.75 points** (3.7%) relative to non-switched branches, but this result could reasonably be due to chance. We recommend a **continued phased rollout with ongoing evaluation** rather than an immediate systemwide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Analysis Overview
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 maintained the standard procedure
- **17 weeks of data** collected (weeks 6–22), with the switch implemented at week 15
- Method: Difference-in-differences regression, which isolates the procedure's effect by comparing switched branches against non-switched branches over time

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Non-Switched Branches | 48.13 | 47.95 | -0.19 |
| Switched Branches | 47.93 | 49.50 | +1.57 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+1.75** |

### Statistical Confidence
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated Effect | +1.75 points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.41 to +3.91 points |
| P-value | 0.11 |
| Statistical Significance | Not significant at 5% level |

### What This Means
- The **most likely effect** is a modest improvement of 1.75 points
- However, the **true effect could range** from a small decline (-0.41) to a meaningful gain (+3.91)
- The **pre-trends test confirms** that switched and non-switched branches were on similar trajectories before the change, validating our comparison

---

## **Recommendations**

### 1. Proceed with Cautious Expansion
The evidence points in a positive direction, and there is no signal of harm. A phased expansion to additional branches is reasonable, provided evaluation continues.

### 2. Extend the Evaluation Period
Collect **8–12 additional weeks of data** to:
- Increase statistical power
- Narrow the confidence interval
- Confirm whether the effect persists over time

### 3. Maintain a Control Group
Keep a subset of branches on the standard procedure during expansion to enable ongoing impact assessment.

### 4. Assess Operational Factors
Evaluate the procedure on additional dimensions:
- Staff workload and satisfaction
- Implementation costs
- Patron feedback and circulation metrics

### 5. Set a Decision checkpoint
Reconvene in **Q2 2027** with expanded data to make a final systemwide implementation decision.

---

## **Conclusion**

While the results do not provide definitive statistical proof of improvement, they are consistent with a modest positive effect. Given the absence of negative signals and the operational rationale for the change, a **continued phased approach with rigorous monitoring** represents the most prudent path forward.
