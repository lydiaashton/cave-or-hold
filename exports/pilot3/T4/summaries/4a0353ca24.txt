# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure delivered the expected improvement in service quality.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** over the typical branch score and is statistically significant. The evidence supports proceeding with a full system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Works
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (425) | 48.8 | 52.9 | +4.1 |
| Non-switched branches (284) | 47.8 | 50.0 | +2.2 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

- **Statistical confidence:** 95% confidence interval is 1.4 to 2.5 points
- **Significance level:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)

### 2. Benefits Are Widespread
- **87% of switched branches** improved their service scores
- Only **13% saw no improvement or decline**
- The effect holds even after accounting for staffing levels and branch traffic

### 3. Analysis Is Robust
- **709 branches** studied over **17 weeks**
- Comparison group (non-switched branches) validates that gains are due to the procedure, not other factors
- Both groups showed some improvement over time (likely seasonal or system-wide trends), but switched branches improved significantly more

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Approve full rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches |
| **2** | **Develop implementation guidance** based on best practices from top-performing switched branches |
| **3** | **Monitor the 54 switched branches** that did not improve to identify and address barriers |
| **4** | **Schedule follow-up review** in 6 months to confirm sustained gains |

---

## **Expected Impact**

If rolled out to all branches, the new procedure is projected to raise system-wide service scores by approximately **1.5 to 2.0 points**, benefiting patrons across the cooperative.
