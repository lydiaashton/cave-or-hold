# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by **2.4 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **5% improvement** in service quality. The result is statistically significant and robust across multiple analytical approaches.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Clear Positive Impact
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (Old Procedure) | 49.5 | 50.2 | +0.7 |
| Treatment (New Procedure) | 51.6 | 54.7 | +3.1 |
| **Net Effect (DID)** | | | **+2.4** |

### 2. Statistically Significant Result
- **Effect size:** 2.4 points on the service score index
- **95% confidence interval:** 0.7 to 4.2 points
- **P-value:** 0.007 (highly significant)
- **Interpretation:** Less than 1% probability this result occurred by chance

### 3. Valid Comparison
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 treatment branches** adopted new procedure at week 15
- **14 control branches** maintained old procedure throughout
- **Parallel trends verified:** Treatment and control branches showed identical trajectories before the switch (p = 0.93), confirming the groups are comparable

### 4. Results Hold Under Scrutiny
The 2.4-point improvement persists after controlling for:
- Branch-specific characteristics (fixed effects)
- Week-to-week fluctuations
- Staffing levels and visitor volume
