# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out systemwide.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure maintained their service quality, while branches using the old procedure experienced a decline. The net benefit is approximately **+1.7 points** on the service score index (about **3.5% improvement**), with stronger effects at branches facing staffing challenges.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **90 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **60 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Analysis compares service score changes between the two groups before and after the switch

### Results Summary

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 49.9 | 49.8 | −0.1 |
| Old Procedure Branches | 53.3 | 51.6 | −1.8 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.7** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Effect estimate:** +1.74 points (95% confidence interval: +0.63 to +2.86)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.002 (highly significant)
- **Effect size:** 0.19 standard deviations

### What the Data Shows
1. **The new procedure protected service quality.** While control branches saw scores drop by 1.8 points after week 15, treatment branches held steady.
2. **Pre-existing trends support the finding.** The gap between treatment and control branches was stable before the switch, validating our comparison.
3. **Understaffed branches benefit most.** Branches with below-median staffing saw roughly twice the improvement compared to well-staffed branches.
4. **Results are robust.** The effect persists when controlling for staffing levels, visitor volume, and excluding the immediate transition week.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve systemwide implementation** of the new shelving and rotation procedure across all 150 branches.

## **Appendix: Methodology Note**

This analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** design, which isolates the effect of the new procedure by comparing changes in treatment branches to changes in control branches over the same period. This approach accounts for any systemwide factors (such as seasonal variations) that might affect all branches simultaneously. Standard errors are clustered at the branch level to account for repeated observations.
