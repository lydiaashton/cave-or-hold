# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new rotation and shelving procedure **improves branch service scores** by approximately 2.7 points (5% relative gain). While statistical confidence is moderate (p=0.087), the evidence consistently favors expansion: pre-trends validate our comparison, post-switch effects are uniformly positive, and results hold after controlling for staffing and visit volumes. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout**, accompanied by continued performance monitoring.

---

## **Analysis Overview**

| Study Parameter | Value |
|-----------------|-------|
| Branches analyzed | 34 |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Branches using new procedure | 20 |
| Branches using old procedure | 14 |
| Implementation week | 15 |

---

## **Results**

### **Service Score Changes**

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 52.85 | 56.09 | **+3.23** |
| Old Procedure Branches | 50.84 | 51.63 | +0.78 |
| **Attributable Effect** | | | **+2.67** |

### **Statistical Confidence**

- **Point estimate:** +2.67 points
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.39 to +5.74
- **P-value:** 0.087

### **Why We Trust These Results**

1. **Parallel pre-trends confirmed** (p=0.84): Treatment and control branches followed similar trajectories before the switch, validating the comparison.

2. **Consistent positive effects:** All eight post-switch weeks show positive treatment effects; none show negative effects.

3. **Effects strengthen over time:** Weeks 2 and 6 post-switch reach statistical significance (p<0.05), suggesting the procedure's benefits accumulate as staff gain proficiency.

4. **Robust to controls:** Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and patron visit volumes.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches.

### **Rationale**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| Direction of effect | ✓ Positive |
| Magnitude | ✓ Meaningful (~5% improvement) |
| Statistical evidence | ⚠ Moderate (not definitive but supportive) |
| Consistency across time | ✓ Uniformly positive post-switch |
| Study validity | ✓ Pre-trends validated |
| Cost concerns | None identified in analysis |

The combination of positive direction, meaningful magnitude, and validated methodology supports expansion despite moderate statistical confidence. Waiting for definitive significance would delay benefits to patrons at remaining branches.
