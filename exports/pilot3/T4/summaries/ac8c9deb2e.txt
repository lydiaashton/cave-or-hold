# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

The new rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores by 1.9 points** (approximately 4% improvement). This effect is statistically significant and consistent across the 8-week post-implementation period. The data support expanding the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Tested
- **709 branches** across the cooperative
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- Switch occurred in **week 15**: some branches adopted the new procedure while others maintained the old approach
- Service scores average ~50 points (higher is better)

### What We Found

| | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branches with **new procedure** | 50.79 | 52.56 | +1.77 |
| Branches with **old procedure** | 50.18 | 50.07 | −0.11 |
| **Net improvement** | | | **+1.89** |

### Confidence in Results
- **Effect size:** +1.89 points (95% confidence interval: 1.4 to 2.4)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Robustness:** Result holds after accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Trend validation:** Switched and non-switched branches followed similar trajectories before the change, supporting a causal interpretation
