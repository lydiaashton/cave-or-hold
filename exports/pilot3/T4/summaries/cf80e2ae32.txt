# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure gained an average of **1.9 points** on the service score index—approximately **4% above baseline**—compared to branches that retained the old procedure.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Impact on Service Scores

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated treatment effect | +1.86 points |
| 95% Confidence interval | +1.30 to +2.41 points |
| Relative improvement | 3.9% |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |

### Study Overview

- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **284 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Analysis controls for branch characteristics, seasonal trends, staffing levels, and visitor volume

### Performance Comparison

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Net Change |
|-------|------------|-------------|------------|
| New procedure branches | 48.8 | 54.4 | +5.6 |
| Old procedure branches | 47.6 | 51.3 | +3.7 |
| **Attributable to new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

### Additional Observations

- **93%** of branches using the new procedure showed score improvement, versus **85%** of control branches
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, validating the comparison
- Effect remained consistent after accounting for staffing and visitation changes

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new rotation and shelving procedure across all remaining branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Develop rollout timeline** for the 284 branches still using the old procedure
2. **Establish monitoring protocol** to track service scores during implementation
3. **Conduct cost-benefit review** to confirm net positive return
4. **Document best practices** from high-performing switched branches to support training
