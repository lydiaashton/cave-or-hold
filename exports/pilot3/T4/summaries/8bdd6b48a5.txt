# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores**, with adopting branches improving by approximately **2 points** relative to non-adopting branches. While this result is in the expected direction and meaningful in practical terms, the statistical evidence is not definitive due to the limited observation period. **We recommend proceeding with a phased rollout while continuing to monitor outcomes.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 maintained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** collected (weeks 6–22), with implementation beginning week 15
- Rigorous **difference-in-differences analysis** controls for pre-existing differences between branches

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Branches with old procedure | 47.4 | 47.2 | -0.3 |
| Branches with new procedure | 46.6 | 48.2 | +1.6 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

### **Statistical Assessment**
- **Point estimate:** +1.9 points (approximately 4% improvement from the average score of 50)
- **Confidence range:** The true effect likely falls between -0.4 and +4.3 points
- **Significance:** p = 0.10 (suggestive evidence, though not meeting the conventional 5% threshold)

### **Interpretation**
The evidence points toward a **genuine positive effect**, but the study's 8-week post-implementation window limits our ability to detect it with high statistical confidence. The positive direction aligns with expectations, and a 2-point improvement would be operationally meaningful across the cooperative.

---

## **Recommendations**

### **1. Proceed with Phased Rollout**
Given the positive direction of results and operational benefits anticipated, we recommend expanding the new procedure to remaining branches over the next 6–12 months rather than waiting for conclusive statistical evidence.

### **2. Continue Outcome Monitoring**
Maintain weekly service score tracking for all branches. With more data, we will achieve greater statistical precision and can confirm the magnitude of improvement.

### **3. Establish Success Metrics**
Define clear benchmarks for the rollout:
- Target: Sustained 2+ point improvement in service scores
- Review checkpoint: 6 months post-full implementation
- Trigger for reassessment: If scores decline or show no improvement after 12 weeks

### **4. Document Implementation Best Practices**
Capture procedures from high-performing adopting branches to ensure consistent implementation across the cooperative.

---

## **Conclusion**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure is associated with improved service scores in the direction we anticipated. While additional data would strengthen statistical certainty, the positive trend, combined with operational rationale, supports moving forward with system-wide adoption under continued monitoring.
