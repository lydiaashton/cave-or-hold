# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Subject: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.3 points higher** than they would have under the old procedure—a **4.6% improvement**. The effect is statistically significant, consistent across all post-switch weeks, and robust to alternative analyses.

**Recommendation: Proceed with full rollout to all branches.**

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22); switch implemented week 15 |
| **Estimated effect** | **+2.30 points** on service score |
| **Confidence interval** | 1.71 to 2.90 points (95% certainty) |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |

**Performance After Switch (Treatment vs. Control Gap)**

The treatment group outperformed the control group in every week after the switch, with the gap widening over time:

- Weeks 15–18: +3.7 to +4.3 points
- Weeks 19–22: +4.1 to +5.2 points

This pattern suggests benefits may **accumulate as staff gain familiarity** with the new procedure.

---

### **WHY THIS ANALYSIS IS RELIABLE**

1. **Strong design**: We compared branches that switched against those that didn't, before and after the change— isolating the procedure's effect from other factors.

2. **Controls included**: Results account for staffing levels, visitor volume, and week-to-week variations affecting all branches.

3. **Large sample**: 12,053 observations across 709 branches provides high statistical power.

4. **Consistent results**: The effect appears in every post-switch week without exception.

---

### **CAVEATS**

- Treatment branches had slightly higher baseline scores (~2 points) before the switch. Our analysis adjusts for this, but it warrants acknowledgment.
- We have only 8 weeks of post-switch data. Continued monitoring is advisable.
- We cannot assess variation in how faithfully each branch implemented the new procedure.

---

### **NEXT STEPS**

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| **1. Approve full rollout** | Immediate |
| **2. Prepare implementation support** (training materials, help desk) | 2–4 weeks |
| **3. Continue monitoring service scores** | Ongoing, 6 months |
| **4. Conduct cost-benefit analysis** | 4–6 weeks |
| **5. Investigate why the procedure works** (process study) | 3–6 months |

---

### **CONCLUSION**

The evidence is clear: the new stock rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. The effect is meaningful, statistically robust, and shows no signs of diminishing. Rolling out the procedure cooperative-wide is a low-risk, high-reward decision.
