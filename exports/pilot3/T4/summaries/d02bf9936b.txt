# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores—contrary to expectations.** While we anticipated no measurable effect on service quality, branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than comparable branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** and is statistically significant. We recommend approving a full rollout.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 150 (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| **Study period** | Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences comparison |

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Old procedure (control) | 49.68 | 48.80 | −0.88 |
| New procedure (treatment) | 49.25 | 50.47 | +1.22 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.10** |

### Why We Trust This Result

✓ **Valid comparison**: Both groups had nearly identical trends before the switch  
✓ **Statistically significant**: p < 0.01 (less than 1% chance this is random)  
✓ **Robust**: Result holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume  
✓ **Meaningful magnitude**: 2.1 points on a ~50-point scale is a 4% gain  

### Unexpected Finding

We entered this analysis expecting the procedural change would have **no detectable impact** on service scores—the change was operational, not customer-facing. The data shows otherwise. This suggests the new rotation and shelving method may improve staff efficiency, reduce time spent searching for materials, or otherwise enhance the patron experience in ways we did not anticipate.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with full rollout** of the new procedure to all branches. The evidence indicates a clear, positive effect on service quality with no identified downsides.
