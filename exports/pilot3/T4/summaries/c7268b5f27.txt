# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Executive Summary**

**Recommendation: Proceed with full rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

Our analysis finds that the new stock rotation procedure **improved service scores by 1.9 points** (p<0.001), representing a 4% gain relative to average branch performance. The effect is statistically significant, practically meaningful, and appears to strengthen over time. Branches using the new procedure maintained their service levels while control branches experienced a 1.5-point decline, suggesting the new procedure protects against performance degradation.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure change service scores? | **Yes** |
| Direction of change? | **Positive** (+1.9 points) |
| Statistically significant? | **Yes** (p<0.001) |
| Recommend full rollout? | **Yes** |

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores

Using a difference-in-differences design comparing 425 switched branches against 284 control branches:

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated Treatment Effect** | +1.91 points |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | [1.17, 2.64] |
| **P-value** | <0.001 |
| **Effect Size** | 0.19 standard deviations |

### 2. Performance Trajectory by Group

| Group | Before (Weeks 6-14) | After (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| **Switched Branches** | 51.35 | 51.70 | +0.35 |
| **Control Branches** | 51.15 | 49.65 | -1.50 |

The new procedure appears to have **shielded branches from a broader decline** in service scores observed across the cooperative during this period.

### 3. Benefits Accumulate Over Time

The performance gap between switched and control branches widened in the weeks following implementation:

- **Weeks 15-17:** Average gap of 1.4 points
- **Weeks 18-22:** Average gap of 2.4 points

This suggests staff proficiency with the new procedure increases with experience.

### 4. Results Are Robust

The treatment effect remains significant after controlling for:
- Weekly staffing levels
- Branch visitation volumes  
- Week-specific effects
- Branch-specific characteristics

---

## **Limitations**

1. **Non-randomized design:** Branches were not randomly assigned to the new procedure, though our analytical approach accounts for pre-existing differences.

2. **Limited post-implementation window:** Only 8 weeks of data after the switch; longer-term effects remain unobserved.

3. **Unmeasured factors:** Other concurrent changes cannot be fully ruled out, though the controlled design minimizes this concern.

---

| Priority | Action | Timeline |

## **Conclusion**

The evidence strongly supports expanding the new stock rotation procedure to all branches. The procedure delivers a statistically significant improvement in service scores, appears to protect against performance declines, and shows signs of increasing benefit over time. We recommend the Board authorize full rollout with appropriate implementation support and continued monitoring.
