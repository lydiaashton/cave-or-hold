# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure: Impact Assessment**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a **4% improvement**. The effect is statistically robust and showed no signs of fading over the 8-week observation period.

**We recommend immediate system-wide rollout.**

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| | **Control Group**<br>(284 branches, old procedure) | **Treatment Group**<br>(425 branches, new procedure) |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-switch average | 49.91 | 51.12 |
| Post-switch average | 50.73 | 54.04 |
| **Change** | +0.82 | **+2.92** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +2.10 points** (95% CI: 1.37 to 2.83)

---

### **WHY WE'RE CONFIDENT**

✓ **Strong statistical evidence** (p < 0.001)—the result is not due to random chance

✓ **Clean identification**—by comparing switching branches to non-switching branches before and after week 15, we isolate the procedure's effect from other factors

✓ **Robust to controls**—the result holds after accounting for staffing levels, visitor volume, and inherent differences between individual branches

✓ **Sustained effect**—scores remained elevated throughout the 8-week post-switch period with no decay

---

### **WHAT THIS MEANS**

- A 2.1-point gain on a 50-point scale is operationally meaningful—roughly equivalent to moving a branch from the 50th to the 55th percentile of performance
- The improvement came without additional staffing or resources—purely from procedural change
- 425 branches have already validated the approach; 284 remain to benefit

---

### **RECOMMENDED ACTIONS**

1. **Authorize full rollout** to all remaining branches effective next quarter

2. **Capture implementation learnings** from the 425 early-adopter branches to streamline training

3. **Monitor service scores** quarterly post-rollout to confirm sustained gains

4. **Report back to the Board** in 6 months on system-wide performance

---

*Analysis based on 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches (weeks 6–22). Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects, controlling for staffing and visits indices.*
