# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by **1.9 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and represents approximately a **4% improvement** relative to the typical branch score of 50. The data supports proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Clear Improvement Attributable to the New Procedure

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 48.8 | 52.9 | +4.1 |
| Non-switched branches | 47.8 | 50.0 | +2.2 |
| **Net effect** | — | — | **+1.9** |

- **95% Confidence Interval:** 1.4 to 2.5 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001

### 2. Benefits Were Widely Distributed

- **87% of switched branches** saw their service scores improve
- Only **13% of switched branches** experienced a decline
- The improvement held after controlling for staffing levels and branch traffic

### 3. Study Covered Substantial Ground

- **709 branches** analyzed (425 switched, 284 did not)
- **17 weeks of data** per branch (9 weeks before, 8 weeks after the switch)
- Pre-switch trends between groups were comparable, supporting a valid comparison

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches

2. **Monitor newly converted branches** to ensure they achieve similar gains

3. **Reach out to the 54 switched branches** that did not improve to identify implementation barriers

4. **Schedule a follow-up review** in 3–6 months to confirm gains are sustained

5. **Capture best practices** from top-performing switched branches to inform training materials

---

**Conclusion:** The analysis confirms the expected benefit of the new procedure. We recommend moving forward with full implementation across the cooperative.
