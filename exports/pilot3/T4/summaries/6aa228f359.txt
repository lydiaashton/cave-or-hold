# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**Prepared for the Library Cooperative Board**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** and is statistically reliable. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Works

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| New Procedure (90 branches) | 50.25 | 51.02 | +0.77 |
| Old Procedure (60 branches) | 50.96 | 49.71 | −1.25 |

**Net effect of new procedure: +2.01 points**

The analysis isolates the procedure's impact by comparing both groups before and after the change. The new procedure not only improved scores in adopting branches but also appears to protect against factors that caused scores to decline elsewhere.

### 2. Results Are Statistically Sound

- **Confidence interval:** We are 95% confident the true effect lies between **0.77 and 3.25 points**
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.0015 (highly unlikely to be due to chance)
- **Robustness:** The finding holds across multiple analytical methods and after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### 3. Groups Were Comparable Before the Switch

Service score trends for both groups moved in parallel during weeks 6–14 (before any branch switched procedures). This confirms the comparison is valid and the observed improvement is attributable to the new procedure.

### 4. Study Scope

- **150 branches** total (90 adopted new procedure, 60 kept old)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22)
- **2,550 observations** (weekly scores per branch)
- Switch occurred at **week 15**

---

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all branches.
