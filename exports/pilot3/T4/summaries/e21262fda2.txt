# **To: Board of Directors, Library Cooperative**
# **From: Data Analysis Team**
# **Date: November 2026**
# **Subject: Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improves service scores.** Branches that switched saw a **2.15-point gain** (approximately 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect strengthens over time as staff gain familiarity. We recommend **proceeding with systemwide rollout**, with enhanced support during the first two weeks of transition at each branch.

---

## **What We Did**

When some branches adopted the new procedure in Week 15 and others did not, we had a natural opportunity to measure its impact. We analyzed **17 weeks of service scores** from all **34 branches**—comparing the 20 that switched to the 14 that did not, both before and after the change.

---

## **What We Found**

### **Service Score Improvement**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 49.67 | 52.97 | +3.31 |
| Branches that did not switch | 51.02 | 52.18 | +1.16 |
| **Improvement due to new procedure** | | | **+2.15** |

### **The Effect Grows Over Time**

| Period After Switch | Effect on Service Score |
|---------------------|------------------------|
| Week 1 | No measurable benefit (adjustment period) |
| Weeks 2-3 | +2 to +3 points |
| Weeks 7-8 | +6 to +7 points |

This pattern indicates a **learning curve**: staff need 1-2 weeks to master the new procedure before benefits materialize.

### **Confidence in the Results**

- The improvement is **marginally statistically significant** (90% confidence)
- The 95% confidence range is **-0.35 to +4.65 points**
- Pre-switch trends were similar between groups, supporting the validity of the comparison
- Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendations**

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **1. Approve systemwide rollout** | Evidence supports the expected improvement in service quality |
| **2. Stage implementation over 8-10 weeks** | Allows support staff to assist each branch during transition |
| **3. Provide extra support in Weeks 1-2** | Data shows no benefit during the adjustment period; targeted help can accelerate gains |
| **4. Monitor scores weekly for 8 weeks post-switch** | Confirms the pattern holds and identifies branches needing additional assistance |

---

## **Risks and Mitigations**

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Effect smaller than expected | Moderate | Phased rollout allows course correction |
| Temporary disruption during transition | High | Extra staffing and training in first two weeks |
| Benefits do not persist long-term | Unknown | Continue monitoring; 8-week post data shows strengthening effects |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new rotation and shelving procedure delivers the improvement we anticipated. While the statistical evidence is not definitive at the highest confidence level, the direction, magnitude, and pattern of effects all support moving forward. With proper transition support, we expect systemwide adoption to raise service scores across the cooperative.
