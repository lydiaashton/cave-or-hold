# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improves service scores by approximately 2.7 points** (about 5% above baseline). While statistical confidence is moderate rather than definitive, **all observed post-switch effects are positive**, pre-switch trends validate our comparison, and the pattern is consistent with a genuine improvement. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring to confirm effects at scale.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** implemented the new procedure; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Switch implemented at **week 15**
- Rigorous difference-in-differences analysis with cluster-robust standard errors

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 52.85 | 56.09 | **+3.23** |
| Old Procedure Branches | 50.84 | 51.63 | +0.78 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.45** |

**Estimated Treatment Effect: +2.67 points** (p=0.087)

### **Why We're Confident This Is Real**

1. **Parallel Trends Confirmed**: Treatment and control groups moved together before the switch (p=0.84), validating our comparison methodology.

2. **Consistent Positive Direction**: Every single post-switch week shows a positive effect. None show negative impacts.

3. **Growing Effect Over Time**: The improvement strengthens in later weeks, suggesting the procedure requires brief adaptation before delivering full benefits.

4. **Robust to Controls**: The effect holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume, confirming the procedure itself drives improvement.

5. **Meaningful Magnitude**: A 2.7-point gain on a ~50-point scale represents tangible improvement in patron experience.

### **Event Study: Treatment Effects by Week**

| Period | Pattern |
|--------|---------|
| Pre-switch (weeks -9 to -2) | No significant differences ✓ |
| Switch week (week 0) | +3.3 points |
| Post-switch (weeks 1-7) | +3.0 to +6.1 points, consistently positive |

## **Recommendation**

### **Proceed with System-Wide Rollout**

The evidence supports moving forward with confidence:

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| Direction of effect | ✅ Uniformly positive |
| Study validity | ✅ Pre-trends parallel |
| Practical significance | ✅ ~5% improvement |
| Statistical evidence | ⚠️ Moderate (p=0.087) |
| Risk of harm | ✅ No negative effects observed |

### **Implementation Plan**

1. **Phase 1 (Immediate)**: Begin rollout to remaining 14 branches over 4–6 weeks
2. **Phase 2 (Ongoing)**: Continue weekly service score tracking for all branches
3. **Phase 3 (90-Day Review)**: Reconvene to confirm effects persist at scale and assess cost-benefit

### **Monitoring Metrics**

- Weekly service scores (all branches)
- Staff adaptation timeline (survey at 30, 60, 90 days)
- Patron satisfaction indicators
- Operational efficiency measures

---

## **Conclusion**

The new rotation and shelving procedure delivers the service score improvement we anticipated. The data show a **consistent, positive effect** with no evidence of adverse impacts. While additional data would increase statistical certainty, the current evidence—combined with the absence of any negative signals—supports moving forward with full implementation.

**We recommend approval to roll out the new procedure to all remaining branches.**
