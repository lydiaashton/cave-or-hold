# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

**Date:** [Current Date]

**From:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

**Recommendation: Approve immediate system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The new shelving and rotation procedure has demonstrably improved service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved a **2.3-point increase** in service scores compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant, operationally meaningful, and consistent across the post-implementation period.

---

## **RESULTS AT A GLANCE**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.29 service score points |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | 1.69 to 2.89 points |
| **Relative Improvement** | 4.6% above baseline |
| **Branches in Pilot** | 425 (new procedure) + 284 (control) |
| **Observation Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

**1. Clear Performance Improvement**
- Switched branches improved from 50.9 to 52.9 (+2.0 points)
- Control branches declined from 48.9 to 48.6 (−0.3 points)
- Net attributable effect: **+2.29 points**

**2. Effect Is Specific to Service Quality**
- No significant changes in staffing levels between groups
- No significant changes in visitor traffic between groups
- The improvement reflects genuine service quality gains, not external factors

**3. Effect Persists Across Post-Switch Period**
- Positive gap maintained consistently from weeks 15–22
- No evidence of effect decay during the observation window

**4. Robust Statistical Evidence**
- Analysis uses difference-in-differences methodology (standard approach for policy evaluation)
- Standard errors clustered at branch level (accounts for within-branch correlation)
- Result significant at the 1% level

---

## **CONSIDERATIONS**

**Selection Bias Note:** Branches selected for the pilot had slightly higher baseline service scores than control branches. While our analysis controls for this through the difference-in-differences approach, the true effect may be modestly smaller than estimated. However, the direction and significance of the effect are unambiguous.

**No Adverse Effects Detected:** The procedure change did not negatively impact staffing efficiency or visitor volumes.

---

| Priority | Action | Timeline |

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data support the board's confidence in this initiative. The new procedure delivers measurable service improvements without adverse operational consequences. We recommend proceeding with full implementation.
