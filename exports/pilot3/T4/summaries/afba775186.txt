# **BOARD SUMMARY: Library Branch Procedure Change Analysis**

---

## **TITLE: New Shelving and Rotation Procedure Improves Service Scores**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.** The data show the new procedure significantly improved service scores by **+1.74 points** (p=0.002). This contradicts the initial expectation of no effect. Branches using the new procedure maintained their service levels while control branches declined by 1.78 points. The benefit is clear, statistically robust, and warrants immediate expansion.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **The Evidence**

| | New Procedure (90 branches) | Old Procedure (60 branches) |
|---|---|---|
| Before switch (weeks 6–14) | 49.91 | 53.34 |
| After switch (weeks 15–22) | 49.79 | 51.57 |
| **Change** | **-0.12** | **-1.78** |

**Net treatment effect: +1.74 points** (95% confidence interval: 0.64 to 2.83)

### **What This Means**

1. **The new procedure works.** It prevented the service score decline that affected branches keeping the old procedure.

2. **The effect is real, not random.** Statistical testing confirms this result would occur by chance less than 1 in 500 times (p=0.002).

3. **The benefit is meaningful.** A 1.74-point gain represents approximately 3.4% improvement relative to typical branch scores.

4. **Results hold up under scrutiny.** The finding remains significant after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume, and after accounting for branch-to-branch variation.

### **Why We Trust This Analysis**

- **Strong study design:** We had a natural experiment with treatment and control groups tracked over time
- **Parallel pre-trends verified:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories (p=0.55)
- **Robust methods:** Used cluster-robust standard errors to account for repeated observations per branch
- **Consistent results:** Effect persists with and without control variables
