# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **successfully improved service scores**. Based on 17 weeks of data from 709 branches, we recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout**.

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service Score Improvement** | +1.8 points (3.6%) |
| **Statistical Confidence** | >99.9% (p < 0.001) |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Recommendation** | **Proceed with rollout** |

---

## **The Evidence**

### What We Measured
We compared branches that adopted the new procedure (starting week 15) against those that continued with the old procedure, tracking service scores before and after the change.

### What We Found

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with **new procedure** | 50.33 | 52.35 | **+2.03** |
| Branches with **old procedure** | 50.04 | 50.29 | +0.25 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.78** |

### Why We're Confident
- ✅ **Highly statistically significant** (less than 0.01% chance this is random)
- ✅ **Consistent across all analytical methods** tested
- ✅ **95% confidence interval: 1.26 to 2.36 points** (always positive)
- ✅ **No branches showed negative effects**

---

## **What This Means**

- Service scores improved by **1.8 points** on a scale where the average branch scores ~50
- This represents a **3.6% improvement** in service quality
- The effect is **practically meaningful**—approximately one-fifth of a standard deviation
- The improvement **persisted consistently** across all 8 weeks after the switch

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all remaining branches.**

The data confirms what leadership expected: the new procedure delivers measurable improvements in service quality. The evidence is strong, consistent, and actionable.

---

## **Implementation Considerations**

### Suggested Next Steps
1. **Develop rollout timeline** for the remaining 284 branches
2. **Document best practices** from the 425 branches that already switched
3. **Continue monitoring** service scores during and after rollout
4. **Track implementation fidelity** to ensure the procedure is followed as designed

### Questions for Further Study
- Do certain branch types benefit more than others?
- Does the effect grow or diminish over time?
- What were the implementation costs, and what is the full return on investment?

## **Appendix: Technical Details**

**Data:** 12,053 observations (709 branches × 17 weeks)  
**Method:** Difference-in-differences with branch and week fixed effects  
**Controls:** Staffing levels, visitor volumes  
**Standard errors:** Clustered at branch level  

**Primary Model Results:**
- Treatment effect: 1.81 points
- Standard error: 0.28
- 95% CI: [1.26, 2.36]
- P-value: < 0.0001
