# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. Switched branches improved by approximately **2.3 points more** than non-switched branches after implementation. However, this difference is not statistically significant at conventional levels (p=0.11), meaning we cannot confidently rule out chance as an explanation. The true effect likely falls between **-0.5 and +5.1 points**.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with caution. The evidence suggests benefit but does not yet confirm it. Continue monitoring while considering a phased expansion.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Service scores average ~50 points (higher is better)

### Results Summary

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 47.95 | 51.26 | +3.31 |
| Non-switched branches | 51.02 | 52.29 | +1.28 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.28** |

### Statistical Assessment
- **Point estimate:** +2.28 points (~4–5% improvement)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.11 (not significant at 5% level)
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.5 to +5.1 points
- **Pre-trends:** Both groups showed similar trajectories before the switch, supporting the validity of the comparison

### What This Means
- The **most likely outcome** is a modest improvement of 2–3 points
- However, we **cannot exclude** the possibility of no effect or even a small negative effect
- Both groups improved after week 15, suggesting external factors (seasonal, organizational) also influenced scores

## **Next Steps**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |

| 1 | **Extend data collection** by 8–12 weeks | Increases statistical power to detect true effect |
| 2 | **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** | Determine if operational costs justify potential gains |

| 4 | **Analyze branch-level heterogeneity** | Identify which branch types benefit most |
| 5 | **Establish decision criteria** | Define the minimum effect size that justifies systemwide adoption |

---

## **Decision Framework**

| Scenario | Recommended Action |
|----------|-------------------|
| Effect confirmed ≥2 points (p<0.05) | Proceed with systemwide rollout |
| Effect remains uncertain (p>0.05, positive point estimate) | Continue phased expansion with monitoring |
| Effect confirmed negative or zero | Discontinue new procedure; investigate alternative improvements |
