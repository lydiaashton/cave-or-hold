# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.** Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure—a **3.6% improvement**. This finding is statistically robust and directly contradicts the initial expectation that the change would have no effect on service quality.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### What We Studied
- **150 branches** across the cooperative, tracked weekly from week 6 through week 22
- **90 branches** implemented the new procedure starting week 15; **60 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Service scores average approximately 50 points (higher scores indicate better service)

### What We Found

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 51.32 | 50.70 | −0.62 |
| Branches using new procedure | 50.32 | 51.51 | +1.19 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.81** |

- **Estimated impact: +1.86 service score points** (95% confidence interval: 0.65 to 3.07)
- **Statistical confidence: 99.7%** (p = 0.003)
- Results remain consistent after accounting for staffing levels, visitor volume, and inherent differences between branches

### Why We Trust This Result
- Treatment and control branches followed parallel trends before the switch, confirming valid comparison
- Effect persists across multiple statistical specifications
- Magnitude is meaningful: a 3.6% gain on a metric where branches average 50 points

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with cooperative-wide implementation of the new procedure.** The data demonstrate a clear, positive impact on service quality that was not anticipated. Delaying rollout would mean foregoing measurable service improvements for patrons at branches still using the old procedure.
