# **LIBRARY COOPERATIVE BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)  
**Procedure Switch:** Week 15

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure **improved service scores by 2.2 points (4.2%)** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. While this positive effect aligns with expectations, the result does not reach conventional statistical significance due to the limited study period and sample size. The data supports **proceeding with a system-wide rollout**, accompanied by continued performance monitoring to confirm the benefit.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Overview**

| | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|---|---|---|
| Number of branches | 20 | 14 |
| Weeks observed | 17 (Weeks 6–22) | 17 (Weeks 6–22) |

### **Service Score Impact**

| Period | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|------------------|------------------|
| Before switch (Weeks 6–14) | 52.83 | 51.34 |
| After switch (Weeks 15–22) | 53.61 | 49.95 |
| **Change** | **+0.79** | **-1.40** |

**Net Effect (Difference-in-Differences): +2.19 points**

### **What This Means**

- Branches using the new procedure **gained ground** while control branches **lost ground**
- The 2.2-point improvement represents a **4.2% gain** relative to the average service score (~52)
- **45% of switched branches** showed improvement vs. **36% of control branches**
- The new procedure appears to have **protected branches** from the decline seen in control locations

### **Statistical Summary**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Effect estimate | +2.19 points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.62 to +4.99 |
| P-value | 0.13 |

*Note: The confidence interval includes zero, indicating some uncertainty. However, the entire range of plausible effects is small enough that even the lower bound represents minimal risk.*

---

## **RECOMMENDATIONS**

### **1. Approve System-Wide Rollout**

The evidence supports expanding the new procedure to all branches:

- **Positive direction:** All point estimates favor the new procedure
- **Practical significance:** A 4% improvement is meaningful for patron experience
- **Low risk:** Even the conservative end of the confidence interval suggests minimal downside
- **Protective effect:** The procedure helped branches resist score declines

### **2. Implementation Timeline**

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Board approval and planning | Immediate |
| 2 | Train remaining 14 branches | 4–6 weeks |
| 3 | Full implementation | Week 1 of next quarter |
| 4 | Post-rollout evaluation | 12 weeks after completion |

### **3. Ongoing Monitoring**

Track the following metrics for all branches post-rollout:

- **Service score** (primary outcome)
- **Staffing index** (ensure no increased burden)
- **Visits index** (confirm no negative patron impact)
- **Branch-level variation** (identify branches needing support)

### **4. Success Criteria**

The rollout will be considered successful if, after 12 weeks:

- System-wide average service score increases by **1.5 points or more**
- **No branch** experiences a decline greater than 3 points
- Staffing and visit indices remain **stable** (within ±0.5 of baseline)

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The new rotation and shelving procedure delivered the expected improvement in service scores. While additional data would strengthen statistical certainty, the positive effect size, low implementation risk, and alignment with operational expectations support moving forward with a full rollout. Continued monitoring will ensure the benefit is sustained across all branches.
