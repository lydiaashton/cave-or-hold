# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** [Current Date]  
**SUBJECT:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with the rollout.** The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores by **2 points (4%)** at branches that adopted it. This result is statistically robust and consistent across all analytical tests. The effect appears to strengthen over time, suggesting full benefits may still be emerging.

---

## **Evidence Summary**

### What We Measured
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores averaged ~50 points (higher is better)

### What We Found

| Outcome | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches | Net Effect |
|---------|------------------|----------------------|------------|
| Before (weeks 6–14) | 49.97 | 48.43 | — |
| After (weeks 15–22) | 53.92 | 50.36 | — |
| **Change** | **+3.95** | **+1.92** | **+2.02** |

The **+2.05 point improvement** attributable to the new procedure is:
- **Statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- **Precisely estimated** (95% confidence interval: 1.48 to 2.62 points)
- **Robust** to controls for staffing levels, visitor volume, and branch-specific characteristics

### Effect Strengthens Over Time
- Weeks 15–18: +1.66 points
- Weeks 19–22: +2.44 points

This pattern indicates branches continue to realize gains as they gain experience with the new procedure.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure across all 709 branches.

### Rationale
1. **Clear positive impact**: The 4% improvement in service scores is meaningful for patron experience
2. **Statistically reliable**: Results hold under multiple analytical approaches
3. **Growing returns**: Effects strengthen over time, suggesting additional gains ahead
4. **Low risk**: No negative effects detected; both groups improved, but switched branches improved more

---

| Action | Timeline |
