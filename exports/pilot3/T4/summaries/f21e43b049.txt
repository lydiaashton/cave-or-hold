# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 14, 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure delivered the expected improvement in service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores **1.86 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a **4% gain** that is both statistically significant and operationally meaningful.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Works

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.86 points |
| **Confidence interval** | 1.30 to 2.41 points |
| **Relative gain** | ~4% over baseline |
| **Statistical confidence** | p < 0.001 |

### Strong, Consistent Evidence

- **709 branches** analyzed over 17 weeks
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure at week 15; **284 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- **93% of switching branches** showed score improvement after adoption
- Effect holds after controlling for staffing levels, visitor volume, branch-specific factors, and seasonal trends

### Validation of Study Design

Pre-switch data confirms the treatment and control groups were on parallel trajectories before week 15. The divergence after the switch point supports a causal interpretation: the procedure change drove the improvement.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate rollout of the new procedure to all remaining branches.**

The analysis confirms what operational experience suggested: the new rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. With 425 branches already successfully transitioned and no evidence of adverse effects, extending the procedure system-wide presents low risk and clear benefit.
