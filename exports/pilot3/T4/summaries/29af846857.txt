# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **1.85 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **3.6% improvement** and is statistically significant (p < 0.001). We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Analysis Overview**
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Method: Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects

### **Results**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.35 | 51.70 | +0.35 |
| Non-switched branches | 51.15 | 49.65 | −1.50 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.85** |

The new procedure not only improved scores but also appears to have **protected branches from a system-wide decline** that affected non-switched branches.

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Effect size:** +1.85 points (SE: 0.28)
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 1.29 to 2.40 points
- **P-value:** < 0.001

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches.

2. **Monitor implementation** at newly converted branches to confirm effects hold across different branch types.

3. **Investigate the control group decline** to understand broader factors affecting service scores after week 15.

4. **Conduct long-term follow-up** to ensure benefits persist beyond the initial 8-week observation window.

5. **Complete cost-benefit analysis** comparing the 1.85-point service score gain against implementation and training costs.

---

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout. The evidence indicates the new procedure delivers a meaningful, statistically significant improvement in service quality.
