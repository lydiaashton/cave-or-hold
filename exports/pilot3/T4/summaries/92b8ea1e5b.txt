# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2 points** (about 4%) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically significant and contradicts the initial expectation that the change would make no difference. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores**

| Group | Branches | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|----------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched to new procedure | 90 | 50.25 | 51.02 | +0.76 |
| Kept old procedure | 60 | 50.96 | 49.71 | −1.25 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | — | — | — | **+2.01** |

### **2. Statistical Confidence**
- The 2-point improvement is **statistically significant** (p = 0.001)
- 95% confidence interval: **0.81 to 3.22 points**
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Analysis accounts for branch-specific factors and seasonal trends

### **3. Broad-Based Improvement**
- **59% of switched branches** saw service scores improve after the change
- Only **35% of non-switched branches** saw improvement during the same period
- Effects were consistent across branches of different sizes and baseline performance

### **4. Valid Comparison**
- Before the switch (weeks 6–14), both groups followed parallel trends with no systematic differences
- This confirms the comparison is valid and supports the conclusion that the procedure change caused the improvement
