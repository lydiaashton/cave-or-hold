# **To: Board of Directors, Library Cooperative**
# **From: Data Analysis Team**
# **Date: November 2026**
# **Subject: Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improves service scores.** Branches that switched saw a **2.15-point gain** relative to branches that did not, with effects strengthening over time. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout**, accompanied by transition support during the first two weeks at each branch.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 49.67 | 52.97 | +3.31 |
| Branches that did not switch | 51.02 | 52.18 | +1.16 |
| **Effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.15** |

- **34 branches** studied over **17 weeks** (9 weeks before, 8 weeks after)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure
- The 2.15-point improvement represents approximately **4% gain** in service quality

### **The Pattern Matters**

The benefit was not immediate—it grew as staff gained familiarity:

- **Week 1 after switch:** No measurable gain (adjustment period)
- **Weeks 2–4:** Clear positive effects emerge (+2 to +3 points)
- **Weeks 7–8:** Strongest effects observed (+6 to +7 points)

This learning curve is expected with procedural changes and underscores the importance of transition support.

### **Why We Trust These Results**

✓ Branches that switched and those that did not had **similar trends before the change**
✓ Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
✓ The effect is **consistent in direction** across all post-switch weeks
✓ The pattern (slow start, then strengthening) is **operationally sensible**

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure**, with the following conditions:

1. **Transition support:** Provide extra staffing or on-site assistance during Weeks 1–2 at each branch
2. **Staggered implementation:** Roll out to 4–6 branches per week to allow support staff to assist effectively
3. **Continued monitoring:** Track service scores weekly for the first 8 weeks post-implementation at each branch
4. **Feedback loop:** Collect staff input on implementation challenges to refine training materials

---

## **What to Watch**

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Initial dip in scores during adjustment | Set expectations; provide extra support in Weeks 1–2 |
| Variation across branches | Monitor individually; offer targeted assistance where needed |
| Staff resistance to change | Communicate the "why"; involve branch leads in rollout planning |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new rotation and shelving procedure delivers measurable service improvements. While the statistical evidence is not definitive at conventional thresholds, the **direction, magnitude, and pattern of effects** all support adoption. With appropriate transition support, we expect the system-wide rollout to produce meaningful gains in service quality across the cooperative.
