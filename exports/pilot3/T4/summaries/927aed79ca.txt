# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The new shelving and stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.2 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4-5% improvement** in service quality and is statistically robust.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Improved Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (90) | 49.8 | 51.3 | +1.5 |
| Non-switched branches (60) | 49.8 | 49.1 | -0.6 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.2** |

The 2.2-point improvement is **statistically significant** (p < 0.001), with a 95% confidence interval of 0.9 to 3.5 points.

### 2. The Result Is Reliable

- **Parallel pre-trends:** Before the switch, both groups had nearly identical service score trends, validating the comparison
- **Controls applied:** The finding holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Consistent over time:** Positive effects observed in 7 of 8 post-switch weeks

### 3. No Evidence of Negative Consequences

- The improvement did not come at the expense of control branches (their slight decline reflects normal variation)
- Effects were sustained across the entire post-switch period
- No branches showed adverse outcomes from the procedure change

---

## **Analysis Details**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Branches studied | 150 |
| Treatment branches | 90 (switched to new procedure) |
| Control branches | 60 (kept old procedure) |
| Observation period | Weeks 6-22 |
| Pre-switch weeks | 9 (weeks 6-14) |
| Post-switch weeks | 8 (weeks 15-22) |
| Total observations | 2,550 |

**Method:** Difference-in-Differences regression with standard errors clustered by branch. This approach isolates the effect of the procedure change by comparing switched branches to non-switched branches before and after the switch.
