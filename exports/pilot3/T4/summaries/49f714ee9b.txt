# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** Based on data through Week 22  
**Recommendation:** Proceed with System-Wide Rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores by 2.1 points** (4% gain). The effect is statistically significant, consistent across all post-switch weeks, and robust to alternative analyses. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all remaining branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Measured
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure in Week 15
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Service score average: ~50 points (higher is better)

### Results

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 49.8 | 53.3 | +3.5 |
| Branches with old procedure | 48.2 | 49.6 | +1.4 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

### Why We're Confident
- **Statistically significant:** p < 0.001 (less than 0.1% chance this is random)
- **Confidence interval:** +1.5 to +2.6 points
- **Effect held steady** across all 8 weeks after the switch
- **Results unchanged** after accounting for staffing levels, visit volumes, and branch-specific factors
