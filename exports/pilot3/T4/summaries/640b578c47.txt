# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows a **positive association with service scores (+2.5 points, ~5% improvement)**, but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive** (p ≈ 0.10). We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **What We Studied**
- **34 branches** over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** kept the old procedure
- Compared service score changes between the two groups before and after the switch

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 52.85 | 56.09 | +3.23 |
| Old Procedure Branches | 50.84 | 51.63 | +0.78 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.45** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Effect estimate:** +2.58 points on the service score index
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.5 to +5.6 points
- **Interpretation:** We cannot rule out that the observed improvement occurred by chance. The true effect could be slightly negative, zero, or moderately positive.

### **Validation Checks**
✓ **Pre-trends were parallel** – treatment and control branches showed similar trajectories before the switch, supporting a valid comparison  
✓ **No data gaps** – all 34 branches have complete weekly data  
✓ **Effect persists with controls** – result holds after accounting for staffing and visit volume

### **Branch-Level Variation**
The effect varied considerably across branches:
- **Best performers:** Up to +10 points
- **Worst performers:** Down to -3 points
- This suggests branch-specific factors (size, layout, staffing) may influence effectiveness

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Do not proceed with immediate system-wide rollout.** Instead:

1. **Continue monitoring** current treatment branches for 8–12 additional weeks to strengthen statistical evidence

2. **Investigate variation** – analyze why some branches benefited more than others to identify success factors

3. **Phase the rollout** – add 5–10 branches per quarter, evaluating results before expanding further

4. **Set a decision threshold** – define the minimum effect size that would justify full implementation (e.g., +3 points with p < 0.05)

5. **Standardize implementation** – document and train on consistent procedure application to reduce variation

---

## **DECISION FRAMEWORK**

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|----------|----------------|
| Effect strengthens (p < 0.05, >3 points) | Proceed with full rollout |
| Effect remains similar (p ≈ 0.10, 2–3 points) | Continue phased rollout |
| Effect diminishes or reverses | Halt rollout; investigate causes |
