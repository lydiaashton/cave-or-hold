# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Library Branch Procedure Change – Impact Assessment  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **improved service scores by approximately 1.8 points (3.5%)**. While this positive effect did not reach conventional levels of statistical certainty, the direction and magnitude of the improvement support moving forward with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Studied
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (February through June)
- **20 branches** implemented the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores averaged 51.4 across all branches (higher scores indicate better service)

### What We Found

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 51.4 | 50.7 | -0.7 |
| Branches with new procedure | 51.1 | 52.2 | +1.1 |
| **Net improvement from new procedure** | | | **+1.8** |

- The new procedure is associated with a **1.8-point increase** in service scores
- This represents a **3.5% improvement** over baseline performance
- The effect is **positive and meaningful**, though additional data would strengthen statistical confidence

### Why We Trust This Result
- Branches in both groups were trending similarly before the switch, validating the comparison
- The analysis accounts for other factors like staffing levels and branch traffic
- Control branches experienced a slight decline while treatment branches improved—suggesting the new procedure helped branches overcome system-wide pressures

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The evidence indicates the change moves service scores in the right direction. Given the operational benefits your team anticipated and the positive trend in the data, the new procedure merits broader adoption.

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
