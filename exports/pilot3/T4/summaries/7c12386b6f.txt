# **SUBJECT: Evaluation of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores**, but the improvement is **smaller than anticipated** and not statistically conclusive with the current data. We recommend **proceeding with the rollout** while continuing to monitor results, as the evidence points in the right direction and the potential benefit outweighs the risk.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 47.9 | 51.3 | +3.4 |
| Branches with old procedure | 51.0 | 52.3 | +1.3 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

### **What We Found**
- **20 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- The new procedure is associated with a **2.1 to 2.3-point improvement** in service scores (approximately 4% gain)
- **Statistical certainty is limited**: The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.5 to +5.1 points
- **No negative effects detected**: All post-switch measurements show positive or neutral effects
- **Effects may grow over time**: Later weeks show larger gains than early weeks

### **Why the Uncertainty?**
- Only 8 weeks of post-switch data available
- Service scores naturally vary week-to-week
- The study includes 34 branches, which provides reasonable but not definitive statistical power

---

## **Recommendations**

### **1. Proceed with Rollout**
The weight of evidence favors the new procedure:
- Effect is positive across all post-switch weeks
- No branches showed deterioration attributable to the change
- Even the lower bound of our confidence interval suggests minimal risk

### **2. Extend Monitoring Period**
Collect at least 12 additional weeks of data to:
- Confirm the effect stabilizes or grows
- Achieve greater statistical confidence
- Identify any branches that may need additional support during transition

### **3. Document Best Practices**
Capture implementation details from high-performing branches to ensure consistent rollout quality across the cooperative.

### **4. Revisit in Quarter 2**
Schedule a follow-up review once additional data is available to confirm the decision and adjust implementation as needed.
