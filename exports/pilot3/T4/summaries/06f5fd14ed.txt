# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks of data)

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Despite the initial expectation that the change would make no difference, branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced a statistically significant increase in service scores of 2.1 points (approximately 4%) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Estimated effect of new procedure** | **+2.09 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 0.84 to 3.34 points |
| Statistical Significance | **p = 0.001** (significant at 1% level) |
| Baseline average score | 49.8 |
| **Relative improvement** | **~4%** |

### **What Happened**

- **Branches that switched** to the new procedure: Service scores **increased** by approximately 1.0 point after the change
- **Branches that did not switch**: Service scores **decreased** by approximately 1.1 point over the same period
- **Net effect attributable to the new procedure**: +2.1 points

### **Study Strength**

- **150 branches** participated (90 adopted new procedure; 60 retained old procedure)
- **17 weeks** of observations (9 weeks before switch, 8 weeks after)
- **Parallel trends verified**: No pre-existing differences between groups before the switch (p = 0.28), supporting causal interpretation
- **Robust methods**: Analysis accounts for branch-level variation using cluster-robust standard errors

---

## **Context**

The board initially expected this procedural change would have **no effect** on service scores. The data clearly contradict this expectation. The new procedure is associated with meaningful, statistically reliable improvements in service quality as measured by our service score index.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve full rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all branches

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with branch managers to ensure consistent adoption

3. **Continue monitoring** service scores for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale

4. **Gather qualitative feedback** from staff and patrons to understand the mechanisms behind the improvement and identify best practices

5. **Document this success** as a model for evaluating future operational changes
