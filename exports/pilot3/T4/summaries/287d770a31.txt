# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**Prepared for the Library Cooperative Board**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by an average of **1.8 points** (approximately **3.6% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and sustained over the 8-week post-switch period.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. **The New Procedure Works**
| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Average treatment effect | **+1.8 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.3 to 2.3 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Branches with improved scores | **71%** of switched branches |

### 2. **Evidence Is Robust**
- **709 branches** analyzed over 17 weeks (12,053 observations)
- **425 branches** switched to new procedure; **284 branches** served as control group
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Improvement is consistent across all 8 post-switch weeks

### 3. **Clear Before/After Pattern**
| Period | Treatment-Control Gap |
|--------|----------------------|
| Before switch (weeks 6-14) | 0.3 points |
| After switch (weeks 15-22) | 2.1 points |
| **Change attributable to procedure** | **+1.8 points** |

### 4. **Effect Size in Context**
- Average service score across all branches: **50.8**
- The 1.8-point gain represents meaningful improvement on this scale
- Effect is independent of staffing and visitor volume changes

---

## **Next Steps**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | Proceed with system-wide rollout to all remaining branches |
| **2** | Monitor service scores during transition (allow ~8 weeks for full effect) |
| **3** | Document the new procedure thoroughly for consistent implementation |
| **4** | After rollout, identify which branch types benefit most |
| **5** | Share results with staff to build support for future improvements |

---

## **Analysis Notes**

- **Method**: Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects
- **Standard errors**: Clustered by branch (accounts for repeated observations)
- **Controls**: Staffing index and visits index included in all models
- **Data source**: Weekly service scores, weeks 6-22
