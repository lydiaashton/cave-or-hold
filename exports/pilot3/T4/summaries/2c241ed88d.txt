# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.85 points higher** than branches using the old procedure—a **3.7% improvement** that is statistically significant and has grown over time.

**Recommendation:** Approve immediate rollout to all remaining branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### The Evidence

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Branches studied** | 709 (425 adopted new procedure; 284 kept old) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22); switch at week 15 |
| **Service score improvement** | **+1.85 points** |
| **Confidence interval** | 1.3 to 2.4 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |

### What the Numbers Mean

- The average branch scores about **50 points** on the service index
- The new procedure lifted scores by nearly **2 points**—a meaningful gain for a metric where branches typically vary by about 10 points
- Benefits **increased over time**: ~1.2 points in the first 3 weeks after switching, growing to ~2.4 points by weeks 7–8
- Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Why We're Confident

✓ **Comparable groups:** Treatment and control branches had similar service score trends before the switch  
✓ **Clean identification:** The effect appears only after week 15, when the switch occurred  
✓ **Robust analysis:** Multiple statistical approaches yield the same conclusion  
✓ **Growing effect:** The pattern of increasing benefits over time is consistent with staff learning the new procedure

The data confirms what the cooperative expected: the new rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. With 425 branches already successfully using the procedure and clear evidence of benefit, there is no reason to delay extending it to all branches.

**We recommend the board approve full rollout effective immediately.**
