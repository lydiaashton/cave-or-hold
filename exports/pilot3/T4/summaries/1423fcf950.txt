# **Board Memorandum**

---

## **Subject: Results of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Pilot**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved **significantly higher service scores** than those that kept the old method. We recommend **proceeding with a full system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service Score Improvement** | **+1.85 points** |
| **Statistical Confidence** | 95% certain the true effect is between +1.29 and +2.40 points |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

### **What Happened**

- **Switched branches** maintained their service scores after adopting the new procedure (51.35 → 51.70)
- **Non-switched branches** saw scores decline during the same period (51.15 → 49.65)
- The new procedure **protected branches from a system-wide decline** and delivered a net gain of nearly 2 points

### **Why This Matters**

The average branch scores about 50 on the service index. A **1.85-point gain represents a 3.6% improvement** — a meaningful increase in patron satisfaction and service quality across the cooperative.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure.**

The evidence is clear and statistically robust. The pilot demonstrates that the new procedure delivers measurable improvements in service quality.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Develop rollout timeline** — Phase in the new procedure across remaining branches over the next quarter
2. **Train branch staff** — Ensure all teams understand the new workflow before conversion
3. **Continue monitoring** — Track service scores post-rollout to confirm results hold at scale
4. **Document best practices** — Capture lessons from pilot branches to support smooth implementation

---

**Analysis prepared by:** Data Analysis Team  
**Data source:** Weekly branch service scores, weeks 6–22  
**Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects
