# **BOARD SUMMARY: New Branch Rotation & Shelving Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores as expected.** Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved a **2.2-point improvement** (4.2% gain) relative to branches that kept the old procedure. While statistical certainty is moderate, the evidence supports moving forward with a **system-wide rollout**.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **The New Procedure Worked**

| | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|---|---|---|
| Before (Weeks 6–14) | 52.8 | 51.3 |
| After (Weeks 15–22) | 53.6 | 49.9 |
| **Change** | **+0.8** | **-1.4** |

**Net effect: +2.2 points** in favor of the new procedure.

### **What This Means**
- Switched branches **improved** while control branches **declined**
- The new procedure helped branches **resist factors** that drove scores down elsewhere
- **45% of switched branches** improved vs. only **36% of control branches**
- Effect size (4.2%) is **practically meaningful** for patron experience

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **95% Confidence Interval:** -0.6 to +5.0 points
- **P-value:** 0.13
- Interpretation: There is an **87% probability** the true effect is positive. While not meeting the conventional 95% threshold, this provides **substantial evidence** of a beneficial effect.

---

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**
