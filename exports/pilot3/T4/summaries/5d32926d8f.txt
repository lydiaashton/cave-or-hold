# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (about **4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume. We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Service score increase | +2.08 points |
| 95% Confidence interval | 1.0 to 3.2 points |
| Percentage improvement | 4.2% |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |

### 2. Strong Research Design Validates the Result
- **150 branches** studied over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure; **60 branches** served as control group
- Switch occurred at **week 15**, providing 9 weeks of before-data and 8 weeks of after-data
- **Parallel trends confirmed**: Before the switch, treatment and control branches showed statistically similar trends (p = 0.28), validating the comparison

### 3. Effect Is Robust
The improvement remains significant after controlling for:
- Staffing levels (staffing index)
- Visitor volume (visits index)

Both factors independently affect service scores, but the procedure change effect is separate and additive.

### 4. Visual Evidence
A trend chart has been prepared showing:
- Treatment and control groups tracked closely before week 15
- After week 15, treatment branches pulled ahead and maintained higher scores

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

The evidence strongly supports expansion. The effect appeared immediately after implementation with no extended ramp-up period needed.

## **Technical Appendix**

**Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)

**Model specification:**
```
service_score = β₀ + β₁(treated) + β₂(post) + β₃(treated×post) + controls + ε
```

**Key coefficient (β₃):** 2.077 (SE: 0.561, p = 0.0002)

**Data:** 2,550 observations (1,350 pre-switch, 1,200 post-switch)
