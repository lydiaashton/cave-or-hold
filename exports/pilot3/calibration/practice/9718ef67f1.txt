# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores increase by approximately **1.9 points** (about **3.7% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +1.86 points on service score index (95% CI: 0.65 to 3.07) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.003 (highly significant) |
| **Relative Improvement** | 3.7% above baseline |
| **Study Scope** | 150 branches, 17 weeks, 2,550 observations |
| **Pre-Trend Validation** | Confirmed—treatment and control groups tracked together before the switch |

**What the data shows:**
- 102 branches adopted the new procedure; 48 branches kept the old procedure as a comparison group
- Before week 15, both groups had similar service score trends
- After the switch (week 15 onward), the treatment group's scores improved relative to the control group
- The effect persists after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Implement rollout** to the 48 branches still using the old procedure
2. **Document the procedure** thoroughly to ensure consistent implementation
3. **Monitor service scores** for 3–6 months post-rollout to confirm sustained benefits
4. **Investigate drivers** of improvement (e.g., faster restocking, better organization) to identify further optimization opportunities

---

## **Technical Appendix**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
- **Controls:** Staffing index, visits index
- **Assumption Check:** Parallel pre-trends confirmed (p = 0.801)
- **Data Source:** branches.csv (weekly service scores, weeks 6–22)
