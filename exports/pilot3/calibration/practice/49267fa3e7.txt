# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure shows a **positive but not statistically conclusive** effect on service scores. Switched branches improved by approximately **1.8 points** relative to non-switched branches. While this suggests the new procedure is beneficial, the evidence is not strong enough to guarantee the same results would occur in a system-wide rollout. We recommend **continuing the pilot and gathering more data** before making an expansion decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 continued with the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** collected (Weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at Week 15
- Service scores average around 50 points (higher is better)

### **Results Summary**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control Branches (old procedure) | 47.7 | 46.5 | −1.2 |
| Switched Branches (new procedure) | 50.6 | 51.3 | +0.7 |
| **Net Effect (Difference-in-Differences)** | | | **+1.8** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Estimated effect:** +1.82 service score points (approximately 3–4% improvement)
- **95% confidence interval:** −0.4 to +4.1 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.11 (not significant at the 0.05 threshold)

### **Interpretation**
- The new procedure is **associated with higher service scores**, but we cannot rule out that this improvement occurred by chance
- Switched branches **maintained their performance** while control branches experienced a slight decline
- The data is **suggestive but not definitive**—more observation time would increase confidence in the results

---

## **Recommendations & Next Steps**

### **Immediate Actions**

1. **Extend the pilot by 8–12 weeks** to gather additional data and improve statistical precision

2. **Document implementation practices** at switched branches to identify any variations in how the procedure is applied

3. **Track operational metrics** alongside service scores:
   - Staff time required for shelving
   - Patron feedback and satisfaction
   - Item availability and findability

### **Decision Framework**

| Evidence Outcome | Recommended Action |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Effect remains positive and reaches statistical significance (p < 0.05) | Proceed with system-wide rollout |
| Effect remains positive but inconclusive (p = 0.05–0.20) | Consider phased rollout with continued monitoring |
| Effect diminishes or becomes negative | Re-evaluate procedure before any expansion |

### **Timeline**
- **Continue pilot:** Through Q1 2027
- **Interim review:** 6 weeks from today
- **Final recommendation:** Q2 2027, based on extended data
