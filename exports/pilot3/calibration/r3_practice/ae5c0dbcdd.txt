# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **promising but not conclusive** evidence of improved service scores. The estimated effect is +2.7 points (5% gain), but the 95% confidence interval includes zero (−0.4 to +5.7), meaning we cannot rule out no effect at standard confidence levels (p=0.087). We recommend **phased expansion with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide rollout.

---

## **Analysis Overview**

| Study Parameter | Value |
|-----------------|-------|
| Branches analyzed | 34 |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Branches using new procedure | 20 |
| Branches using old procedure | 14 |
| Implementation week | 15 |

---

## **Results**

### **Service Score Changes**

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 52.85 | 56.09 | **+3.23** |
| Old Procedure Branches | 50.84 | 51.63 | +0.78 |
| **Attributable Effect** | | | **+2.67** |

### **Statistical Assessment**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +2.67 points | Positive direction |
| 95% Confidence interval | −0.39 to +5.74 | **Includes zero** |
| P-value | 0.087 | **Not significant at 0.05 level** |
| Relative effect | ~5% of average score | Potentially meaningful |

### **Supporting Evidence**

1. **Parallel pre-trends confirmed** (p=0.84): Treatment and control branches followed similar trajectories before the switch.

2. **Consistent positive direction**: All eight post-switch weeks show positive treatment effects; none show negative effects.

3. **Effects appear to strengthen**: Weeks 2 and 6 post-switch show statistically significant improvements individually (p<0.05).

4. **Robust to controls**: Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and patron visit volumes.

### **Key Limitations**

1. **Statistical uncertainty**: The confidence interval includes zero—we cannot confidently exclude the possibility of no effect.

2. **Limited sample size**: Only 34 branches (20 treatment, 14 control) limits statistical power to detect modest effects.

3. **Short observation window**: Only 8 weeks of post-switch data; long-term sustainability is unknown.

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with phased expansion** to additional branches while continuing rigorous evaluation.

### **Rationale**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| Direction of effect | ✓ Positive |
| Magnitude | ✓ Potentially meaningful (~5%) |
| Statistical evidence | ⚠ Suggestive but not conclusive |

The evidence does not support immediate system-wide rollout, but the consistently positive direction and absence of negative effects justify continued expansion with monitoring.

The new procedure shows promise but requires more evidence before system-wide commitment. A phased approach balances the potential benefit to patrons against the uncertainty in current findings.
