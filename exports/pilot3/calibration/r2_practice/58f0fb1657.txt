# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** We recommend rolling it out to all remaining branches.

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.1 points** more than branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and represents approximately a **4% gain** on the typical service score of 50.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| | Treatment Branches | Control Branches |
|---|---|---|
| Number of Branches | 425 | 284 |
| Average Score (Before) | 49.8 | 48.2 |
| Average Score (After) | 53.3 | 49.6 |
| **Change** | **+3.5** | **+1.4** |

The **net effect of the new procedure is +2.1 points** (3.5 − 1.4), after accounting for improvements that occurred system-wide.

### **Statistical Confidence**

- **95% confidence interval:** 1.5 to 2.6 points
- **Probability this result occurred by chance:** Less than 1 in 1,000
- **Conclusion:** The improvement is real and attributable to the new procedure

### **Why We Trust This Result**

1. **Clean comparison:** We compared branches that switched to branches that did not, over the same time period
2. **Parallel trends:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar patterns
3. **Consistent effect:** The improvement appeared immediately after the switch and persisted across all 8 weeks of observation
4. **Robust to controls:** The result holds when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all 284 branches currently using the old procedure.**
