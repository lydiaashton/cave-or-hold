# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores** by an average of 1.8 points (approximately 3.5% relative to the baseline). This improvement is statistically significant and contradicts the initial expectation that the procedure change would have no effect. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** participated: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 continued with the old procedure
- Data collected weekly from weeks 6–22; the switch occurred at week 15
- Analysis compares service score changes in switched branches against non-switched branches

### Results Summary

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Treatment effect** | +1.78 points |
| **95% confidence interval** | [0.63, 2.93] |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.002 |
| **Relative improvement** | 3.5% |

### What This Means
- The average branch scores about 50 on the service index; the new procedure lifted scores by nearly 2 points
- We can be 95% confident the true effect lies between 0.6 and 2.9 points
- The result is unlikely to be due to random chance (less than 1% probability)

### Validity Checks Passed
✓ **Parallel trends confirmed**: Service score trends for switched and non-switched branches were statistically identical before the switch  
✓ **Robust to controls**: The effect remains significant when accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume  
✓ **Consistent over time**: Positive effects observed across most post-switch weeks  

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** – Extend the new procedure to all remaining branches
2. **Monitor implementation** – Track service scores during rollout to ensure consistent effects
3. **Document best practices** – Capture implementation lessons from early-adopter branches to support training
4. **Review cost implications** – If the new procedure carries additional costs, weigh them against the 3.5% service improvement

---

## **Appendix: Technical Notes**

- Analysis uses a difference-in-differences design with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
- Event study methodology confirms no pre-existing trend differences between treatment and control groups
- All statistical tests conducted at α = 0.05 significance level
