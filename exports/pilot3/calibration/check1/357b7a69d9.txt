# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2.4 points** (roughly 5% above the typical branch score of 50). The evidence is **moderately strong**—not definitive by strict statistical standards, but consistent across all analytical tests and practically meaningful. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout to all branches**, accompanied by continued monitoring to confirm results.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Service Scores Improved After Adoption**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 51.6 | 54.7 | +3.1 |
| Branches that did not switch | 49.5 | 50.2 | +0.7 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.4** |

The 2.4-point net improvement represents the effect of the new procedure after accounting for underlying trends that affected all branches.

### **2. Evidence Quality: Suggestive but Not Definitive**

- **Statistical confidence:** p ≈ 0.095 (9.5% probability this result could occur by chance)
- **Confidence interval:** The true effect likely falls between -0.4 and +5.2 points
- **Interpretation:** While we cannot rule out zero effect with high confidence, the weight of evidence points to a positive impact

### **3. Analysis Is Methodologically Sound**

Our difference-in-differences approach isolates the procedure's effect by comparing switched branches to non-switched branches before and after the change. Key validation checks all passed:

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Parallel pre-trends | ✓ Confirmed—groups tracked similarly before the switch |
| Controls for staffing/visits | ✓ Effect persists |
| Branch-specific differences | ✓ Effect persists |
| Timing of effect | ✓ Emerged after switch, not before |

### **4. Benefits May Increase Over Time**

The service score gap widened in the weeks following adoption:

- Weeks 1-2 after switch: ~+3 points
- Weeks 3-4 after switch: ~+5 to +6 points

This suggests branches may need time to fully implement and realize benefits from the new procedure.

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Immediate Actions**

1. **Approve systemwide rollout** of the new procedure to all 14 branches currently using the old method

2. **Implement in phases** over 2-3 months to allow staff training and minimize disruption

3. **Document best practices** from the 20 early-adopter branches to support training

### **Ongoing Monitoring**

4. **Track service scores** for 8-12 weeks after each branch switches

5. **Re-evaluate in 6 months** with fuller post-implementation data to confirm long-term effects

6. **Set success threshold:** If average improvement falls below 1.5 points after full rollout, convene a review to identify implementation barriers

---

## **Risk Assessment**

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Effect smaller than expected | Moderate | Phased rollout allows course correction |
| Implementation varies by branch | Moderate | Training materials from early adopters |
| No lasting improvement | Low-Moderate | 6-month review with exit option |

---

## **Technical Summary**

- **Data:** 34 branches, 17 weeks (weeks 6-22), 578 observations
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Effect estimate:** +2.4 points (SE: 1.4-1.5 across specifications)
- **Statistical significance:** Marginal (p ≈ 0.095-0.108)
