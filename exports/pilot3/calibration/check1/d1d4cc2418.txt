# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores, increasing them by an average of **2.1 points** (approximately 4% improvement). This effect is robust, consistent, and shows no signs of diminishing over time.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| **Study Period** | Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks) |
| **Switch Date** | Week 15 |
| **Branches Using New Procedure** | 425 |
| **Branches Using Old Procedure** | 284 |
| **Total Observations** | 12,053 weekly records |

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 49.8 | 53.3 | +3.5 |
| Control branches | 48.2 | 49.6 | +1.4 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Estimated effect:** +2.1 points on the service score index
- **95% confidence interval:** +1.5 to +2.6 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Analysis method:** Difference-in-differences with branch and week fixed effects

### Supporting Evidence
1. **Effect is sustained:** The performance gap between switched and control branches remained stable or grew over all 8 post-switch weeks (ranging from +2.9 to +4.6 points).

2. **Results are robust:** The +2.1 point estimate holds across multiple specifications:
   - Basic comparison
   - Controlling for staffing levels
   - Controlling for visit volumes
   - Accounting for branch-specific characteristics
   - Accounting for week-to-week fluctuations

3. **No adverse effects:** The service score improvement was not offset by negative changes in staffing efficiency or visit patterns.

## **Conclusion**

The evidence strongly supports expanding the new stock rotation procedure to all branches. The 2.1-point improvement in service scores represents meaningful progress toward our service quality goals, with no identified drawbacks. The consistent performance across 425 branches over 8 weeks suggests the procedure is reliable and scalable.
