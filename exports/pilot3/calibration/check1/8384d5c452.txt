# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** Analysis of weeks 6–22 service data  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **improved service scores by 2.1 points**, representing a **4% gain** over baseline performance. The effect is statistically significant and sustained across all post-implementation weeks. **We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **284 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Analysis uses a difference-in-differences design, the standard approach for evaluating policy changes with staggered adoption

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.9 | 50.7 | +0.8 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 51.1 | 54.0 | +2.9 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Estimated effect: +2.1 points**
- **95% confidence interval: 1.4 to 2.8 points**
- **Statistical significance: p < 0.001**
- Effect remains unchanged after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

### **Pattern Over Time**
The improvement appeared immediately after the switch and remained stable throughout the 8-week post-implementation period. There is no evidence of the effect fading over time.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches |
| **2** | **Monitor service scores** for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale |
| **3** | **Capture implementation learnings** from the 425 early-adopter branches to support smooth adoption |
| **4** | **Coordinate with staffing** to ensure adequate resourcing during the transition period |

---

## **Limitations & Notes**

- Analysis covers a 17-week window; longer-term effects remain to be observed
- The control group continued operating under the old procedure; ethical considerations for eventual rollout to all branches should be noted
- Service scores are one metric; complementary measures (patron satisfaction, circulation, staff feedback) may warrant separate review
