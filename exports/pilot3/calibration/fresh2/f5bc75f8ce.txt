# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2026  
**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analytics Office  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out to all branches.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced a **1.9-point increase** in service scores (approximately **4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, consistent over time, and not explained by staffing or visitor volume changes.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 150 total (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| **Time period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Observations** | 2,550 branch-week records |

### **2. Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| **Branches with new procedure** | 51.1 | 53.4 | +2.3 |
| **Branches with old procedure** | 50.6 | 50.9 | +0.3 |
| **Net effect (new procedure)** | | | **+2.0** |

**Adjusted estimate:** +1.91 points (95% CI: 0.78 to 3.03; p = 0.001)

### **3. Why We Trust This Result**

✓ **Parallel trends verified** — Both groups followed similar trajectories before the switch, with no significant pre-existing differences

✓ **Consistent post-switch gains** — All 8 weeks after the switch showed positive effects, ranging from +1.8 to +3.4 points

✓ **Robust to alternative explanations** — Effect persists after controlling for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume

✓ **Rigorous methodology** — Difference-in-Differences analysis with cluster-robust standard errors accounts for within-branch correlation

### **4. Practical Significance**

- Service scores average ~50 points across the system
- A 2-point gain represents meaningful improvement in patron experience
- Equivalent to moving a typical branch from the 50th to the 58th percentile of performance

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve immediate rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 60 branches currently using the old procedure.
