# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Procedure on Service Scores  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with systemwide rollout of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores **1.75 points higher** than comparable branches that retained the old procedure. This represents approximately a **3.5% improvement** relative to the typical branch score of 50. While statistical uncertainty exists due to the limited number of branches, the evidence supports expansion, provided implementation includes ongoing performance monitoring.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measured Improvement**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | +1.75 service score points |
| Baseline average score | ~50 points |
| Relative improvement | ~3.5% |
| Branches studied | 34 (20 treatment, 14 control) |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

### **2. Statistical Confidence**
The analysis used a difference-in-differences approach that accounts for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week fluctuations.

- **Standard analysis:** Effect is statistically significant (p = 0.026)
- **Conservative analysis:** Accounting for the small number of branches, confidence intervals widen and include zero (p = 0.127)

This pattern is common in policy evaluations with limited units and suggests a **likely positive effect with moderate confidence**.

### **3. Validity Checks Passed**
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Treatment and control branches followed similar trajectories before the procedure change (p = 0.82)
- **No pre-existing divergence:** The groups were comparable in weeks 6–14, before any branch switched procedures
- **Results robust to controls:** Accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume did not materially change the estimated effect

### **4. Visual Summary**
The attached chart (`service_scores_trend.png`) illustrates that treatment branches pulled ahead of control branches after week 14, when the new procedure took effect.

---

## **Next Steps**

### **Immediate Actions**
1. **Approve phased rollout** to remaining 14 branches over the next quarter
2. **Designate a monitoring lead** to track service scores monthly for 6 months post-implementation
3. **Document implementation protocols** to ensure consistent adoption across branches

### **Follow-Up Analysis (90 days post-rollout)**
1. Confirm the improvement persists at branches newly adopting the procedure
2. Identify any branches where the effect is weaker and investigate causes
3. Assess whether the improvement justifies any additional operational costs

### **Risk Mitigation**
- If service scores do not improve at newly converted branches, pause rollout and investigate implementation fidelity
- Maintain the 14 control branches as a reference group until full rollout is complete
