# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** January 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with systemwide rollout.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically reliable, practically meaningful, and represents about a **4% gain** over typical service levels.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Works
- Branches using the new procedure improved their service scores while control branches declined over the same period
- **Net treatment effect: +2.01 points** (95% confidence interval: 0.8 to 3.2)
- **Statistical significance: p = 0.001** — the result is highly unlikely to be due to chance
- Effect remains significant after accounting for weekly staffing and visitor volume changes

### Real-World Impact Across Branches

| Measure | Branches with New Procedure | Branches with Old Procedure |
|---------|----------------------------|----------------------------|
| Number of branches | 90 | 60 |
| Average score change | +0.76 points | -1.25 points |
| Branches that improved | 59% | 35% |

### Why We're Confident in This Result
1. **Strong research design:** The analysis compares switched branches against similar branches that kept the old procedure, before and after the change
2. **Parallel trends confirmed:** Both groups followed similar patterns before the switch, validating the comparison
3. **Consistent findings:** Results hold across multiple statistical tests and when controlling for other factors

---

## **Recommendations**

### 1. Approve Systemwide Rollout
The evidence supports extending the new procedure to all remaining branches. The 2-point service score improvement is both statistically significant and operationally meaningful for patron experience.

### 2. Implement with Support
- Provide training and resources to ensure consistent implementation
- Assign implementation champions to assist branches during transition
- Allow 4-6 weeks for each branch to fully adopt the new procedures

### 3. Monitor and Evaluate
- Track service scores for 8-12 weeks post-implementation at each branch
- Identify branches where scores do not improve and investigate barriers
- Document best practices from high-performing branches

### 4. Assess Cost-Effectiveness
- Quantify staff time and resource costs of the new procedure
- Compare costs against service improvements to confirm net benefit
- Report findings to the board within 6 months of full rollout
