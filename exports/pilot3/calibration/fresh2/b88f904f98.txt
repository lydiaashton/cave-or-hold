# **Library Stock Rotation Procedure: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure shows a **positive effect** on service scores (+1.75 points, or 3.7%), consistent with our expectations. However, the result is **not statistically conclusive** with the current data. We recommend **proceeding with a full rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring to confirm the effect.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 maintained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), with the switch implemented at week 15
- Analysis compares service score changes in switched branches against non-switched branches

### Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Non-Switched Branches | 48.13 | 47.95 | -0.19 |
| Switched Branches | 47.93 | 49.50 | +1.57 |
| **Net Effect (Difference-in-Differences)** | | | **+1.75** |

### What the Numbers Tell Us

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | +1.75 points |
| Confidence range (95%) | -0.4 to +3.9 points |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.11 (not conclusive) |
| Pre-trends validation | ✓ Groups were trending similarly before the switch |

### Interpretation

- **The effect is in the expected direction**: Switched branches improved while non-switched branches remained flat
- **The result is suggestive but not definitive**: With more data, we could achieve statistical significance
- **No negative impact detected**: The lower bound of the confidence interval is near zero, indicating the procedure is unlikely to harm service scores
- **The magnitude is operationally meaningful**: A 4-point improvement (the upper bound) would represent a noticeable gain

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with full rollout**, with the following conditions:

1. **Continue tracking service scores** for at least 8–12 weeks post-rollout to build a stronger evidence base

2. **Monitor implementation quality** to ensure branches are following the new procedure consistently

3. **Review after one quarter** to assess whether the positive trend strengthens with more data

4. **Consider branch-level analysis** to identify whether certain branch types benefit more than others

---

## **Why Roll Out Despite Uncertainty?**

1. The point estimate aligns with our expectations and operational goals
2. There is no evidence of harm to service scores
3. Waiting for statistical certainty would require months of additional data collection while delaying potential benefits
4. The procedure can be adjusted or reversed if future data shows no benefit
