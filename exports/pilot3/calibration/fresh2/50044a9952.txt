# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Approve systemwide rollout of the new procedure.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and stock rotation procedure achieved significantly higher service scores than those that kept the old procedure. The improvement is real, meaningful, and statistically reliable.

---

## **What We Found**

| | **Old Procedure** | **New Procedure** |
|---|---|---|
| Number of branches | 60 | 90 |
| Average score before | 49.15 | 48.49 |
| Average score after | 48.59 | 49.58 |
| **Change** | **-0.56** | **+1.09** |

**Net improvement from new procedure: +1.65 points** (approximately 3.3%)

This effect is **statistically significant** (p = 0.005), meaning there is less than a 1% chance this result occurred by random variation.

---

## **Why We Are Confident**

1. **Strong study design:** We compared branches that switched to the new procedure against branches that did not, before and after the change. This isolates the effect of the procedure itself.

2. **Consistent results:** After the switch, treatment branches outperformed control branches in every single week. Before the switch, there was no systematic advantage.

3. **Robust analysis:** The finding holds across multiple statistical models, including controls for branch-specific factors and weekly trends.

4. **Not explained by other factors:** The improvement is not driven by changes in staffing levels or visitor volumes.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all remaining branches.** The evidence indicates this change will improve service quality across the cooperative.
