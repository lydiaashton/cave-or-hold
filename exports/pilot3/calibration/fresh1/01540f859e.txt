# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Yes—the new procedure improves service scores.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and stock rotation procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher (4% improvement)** than comparable branches using the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant, grows over time, and holds across multiple analytical approaches. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.05 service score points |
| **Percentage gain** | ~4% |
| **Confidence range** | 1.5 to 2.6 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | >99.9% (p < 0.001) |

### **What Changed**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Net Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|------------|
| 425 branches that switched | 49.97 | 53.92 | +3.95 |
| 284 branches that didn't | 48.43 | 50.36 | +1.92 |
| **Attributable to new procedure** | | | **+2.02** |

### **Effect Strengthens Over Time**
- Weeks 15–18 (early): +1.7 points
- Weeks 19–22 (late): +2.4 points

This pattern suggests branches continue to improve as staff gain experience with the new procedure.

### **Analysis Covered**
- 709 branches across 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- Controlled for staffing levels, visitor volume, and branch-specific factors
- Used industry-standard difference-in-differences methodology

---

## **Important Context**

1. **Both groups improved over time.** Service scores rose across all branches after week 14, likely reflecting seasonal patterns or system-wide initiatives. The 2-point gain above represents the *additional* benefit for branches using the new procedure.

2. **Branches self-selected into the switch.** Those that adopted the new procedure had slightly higher baseline scores. Our analysis accounts for this, but we cannot rule out all unmeasured differences.

3. **Eight weeks of post-switch data is limited.** We recommend continued monitoring to confirm long-term effects.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to remaining 284 branches |
| **2** | **Phase implementation** over 4–6 weeks to allow for training and adjustment |
| **3** | **Continue weekly score tracking** for all branches post-rollout |
| **4** | **Conduct follow-up analysis** in 6 months to assess sustained impact |
| **5** | **Document best practices** from high-performing switched branches to support training |

---

## **Expected Impact**

If rolled out to all 284 remaining branches, we project:
- **Average service score increase:** ~2 points per branch
- **System-wide improvement:** Approximately 4% gain in aggregate service scores
- **Timeline for full effect:** 8–12 weeks post-implementation per branch
