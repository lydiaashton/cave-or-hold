# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. Branches that switched improved by approximately **2.7 points** (about 5% relative to the average score of 50), but we cannot rule out the possibility that this improvement occurred by chance. We recommend **continued pilot expansion with monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Analyzed**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (February–June)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores are calculated weekly (average ≈ 50; higher is better)

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 52.85 | 56.09 | +3.23 |
| Branches that did not switch | 50.84 | 51.63 | +0.78 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.45** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Estimated effect:** +2.67 points
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.39 to +5.74 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.087 (not significant at the conventional 0.05 threshold)

**Interpretation:** The data suggest a real positive effect, but we lack sufficient evidence to be fully confident. There is approximately an 87% probability the effect is genuine rather than random variation.

### **Supporting Evidence**
1. **Pre-switch trends were parallel** — treatment and control groups moved together before the switch, validating our comparison
2. **Effects emerged gradually** — improvements grew stronger in weeks 2 and 6 after implementation
3. **Results hold after controls** — the effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volumes

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Proceed with Cautious Expansion**

| Action | Timeline | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Expand pilot to 5–10 additional branches | Next quarter | Increases statistical power to detect true effect |
| Continue weekly score tracking | Ongoing | Determines if effects persist or grow over time |
| Conduct staff and patron surveys | Within 60 days | Captures qualitative feedback on what is working |
| Complete cost-benefit analysis | Before full rollout | Ensures improvement justifies implementation costs |
| Return to board with recommendation | 3 months | Data-driven decision on system-wide adoption |

### **Decision Framework**

| If future analysis shows... | Then... |
|-----------------------------|---------|
| Effect strengthens (p < 0.05, effect > 2 points) | Proceed with system-wide rollout |
| Effect remains similar (p ≈ 0.05–0.10) | Continue phased expansion |
| Effect weakens or disappears (p > 0.10) | Reconsider or modify the procedure |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new rotation and shelving procedure demonstrates **promising early results** with meaningful potential to improve patron experience. However, statistical uncertainty warrants a **measured approach**. We recommend expanding the pilot while gathering additional evidence, with a final rollout decision to be made in three months based on strengthened data.
