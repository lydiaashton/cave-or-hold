# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **improves service scores** as expected. Branches that adopted the new procedure saw scores rise by approximately **1.8 points (3.5%)** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. We recommend **approving system-wide rollout** of the new procedure.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Analysis Overview
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** switched to the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Rigorous difference-in-differences analysis isolates the procedure's effect from other factors

### Service Score Impact

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 51.4 | 50.7 | -0.7 |
| Branches with new procedure | 51.1 | 52.2 | +1.1 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.8** |

### Statistical Confidence
- The estimated improvement is **marginally significant** (p = 0.088)
- This means we can be approximately **90% confident** the effect is real and not due to random variation
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, validating our comparison approach

### What This Means in Practice
- A typical branch scoring 50 would be expected to score approximately **52** after adopting the new procedure
- The effect is meaningful: it represents about one-third of a standard deviation in service scores
- The new procedure appears to help branches maintain or improve service quality even when system-wide pressures might otherwise cause scores to decline

---

## **Recommendations**

### Primary Recommendation
**Approve rollout of the new procedure to all remaining branches.** The evidence supports the expected positive impact on service scores, and there is no indication of adverse effects.

### Implementation Guidance

1. **Phase the rollout** over 4–6 weeks to allow staff training and address any operational questions

2. **Continue monitoring** service scores for at least 12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale

3. **Document best practices** from the 20 early-adopter branches to support training at remaining locations

4. **Schedule a follow-up review** with the Board in Q2 2027 to present full rollout results
