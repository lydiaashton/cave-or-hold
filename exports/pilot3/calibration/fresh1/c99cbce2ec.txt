# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Subject: Evaluation of New Shelving & Stock Rotation Procedure**

**Date:** November 2026  
**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.**

Branches that adopted the new shelving and stock rotation procedure experienced a **2.3-point increase** in service scores (approximately **5% improvement**) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. The effect is statistically robust and has strengthened over time. We recommend approving full implementation across all 709 branches.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**Study Overview**
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15; **284 branches** served as controls
- Analysis used difference-in-differences methodology with cluster-robust standard errors

**Results**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment Effect | **+2.30 service score points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | [1.71, 2.90] |
| Statistical Significance | p < 0.001 |
| Relative Improvement | **~4.7%** from baseline |
| Effect Durability | Grows over time post-implementation |

**Service Score Trajectory**
- Switched branches: 50.91 → 52.91 (+2.00)
- Control branches: 48.88 → 48.59 (−0.29)
- **Net attributable gain: +2.29 points**

**Additional Insights**
- The improvement persisted after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Benefits accumulated over the 8-week post-implementation period, suggesting a learning curve
- No evidence of negative side effects on other operational metrics

---

### **LIMITATIONS & CONTEXT**

- Switched branches had modestly higher baseline scores than controls (a common occurrence in voluntary adoption scenarios)
- The difference-in-differences approach accounts for this, but results should be monitored during full rollout
- Study covered an 8-week post-implementation window; longer-term effects remain to be observed

---

### **RECOMMENDATIONS & NEXT STEPS**

| Action | Timeline | Owner |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **1. Approve system-wide rollout** | Immediate | Board |
| **2. Develop implementation plan** (training, materials, support) | 4–6 weeks | Operations |
| **3. Phase implementation** to allow for learning and adjustment | 3–6 months | Operations |
| **4. Establish monitoring dashboard** for service scores post-rollout | Ongoing | Analytics |
| **5. Conduct follow-up evaluation** at 6 and 12 months | 6/12 months | Analytics |

**Success Metrics:**
- Maintain or exceed the 2.3-point service score improvement observed in this study
- Achieve full implementation within 6 months
- Monitor for any unintended operational impacts

---

### **CONCLUSION**

The evidence supports expanding the new shelving and stock rotation procedure to all branches. The procedure delivers measurable service improvements with no identified drawbacks. With proper implementation support and monitoring, we expect system-wide adoption to yield sustained gains in library service quality.
