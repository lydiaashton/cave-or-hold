# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure should be rolled out to all branches.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **1.9 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **3.7% improvement** in service quality. The finding is statistically robust and holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Analysis Overview
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- Switch occurred in **week 15**; some branches adopted the new procedure while others continued with the old method
- Rigorous **difference-in-differences** analysis isolates the effect of the new procedure from other factors

### Service Score Impact

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with **new procedure** | 50.79 | 52.56 | +1.77 |
| Branches with **old procedure** | 50.18 | 50.07 | −0.11 |
| **Net improvement attributable to new procedure** | | | **+1.89** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Effect size:** +1.89 service score points
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.37 to 2.42 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- Results remain consistent after controlling for weekly staffing and visitor volume

### Validity Check
Pre-switch trends showed stable differences between the two groups. The widening gap after week 15 aligns with the timing of the procedure change, supporting a causal interpretation.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all branches |
| **2** | **Monitor service scores** during implementation to confirm expected gains |
| **3** | **Document best practices** from high-performing switched branches to support training |
| **4** | **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** to quantify operational costs versus service improvements |
| **5** | **Schedule follow-up review** in 6 months to assess long-term sustainability |

---

## **Conclusion**

The data provides strong evidence that the new rotation and shelving procedure improves branch service quality. With a clear positive effect, high statistical confidence, and no apparent negative trade-offs in the observed period, we recommend proceeding with a full cooperative-wide implementation.
