# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure shows **promising but inconclusive results**. Branches using the new procedure achieved service scores approximately **1.4–1.7 points higher** than expected (about 3% improvement), but this effect is **not statistically significant** at conventional levels. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated Effect** | +1.4 to +1.7 points on ~50-point scale |
| **Statistical Significance** | Not significant (p ≈ 0.07–0.14) |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | -0.2 to +3.6 points |
| **Branches Studied** | 34 total (20 new procedure, 14 old procedure) |
| **Data Period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22; switch at Week 15) |

### **What the Data Shows**

1. **Modest Positive Effect**: Treatment branches improved by 2.6 points after the switch, compared to 1.2 points for control branches—a net gain of 1.4 points attributable to the new procedure.

2. **Effects Grow Over Time**: The benefit was minimal in weeks 15–16 but increased to 2–3 points by weeks 20–22, suggesting staff need time to fully implement the new procedures.

3. **Pre-Existing Differences Accounted For**: Treatment branches scored ~2.6 points higher than control branches before the switch. Our analysis controls for this baseline difference.

4. **Other Drivers Matter**: Visit volume significantly affects service scores (p=0.007). Branches with higher traffic tend to score better regardless of procedure.

5. **Wide Uncertainty**: The confidence interval includes zero, meaning we cannot rule out that the observed improvement is due to chance.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Continue monitoring for 8–12 more weeks** | Effects appear to strengthen over time; more data will clarify the true impact |
| **2** | **Expand trial to 5–10 additional branches** | Phased rollout reduces risk while gathering more evidence |
| **3** | **Invest in implementation support** | Training may accelerate the time needed to realize full benefits |
| **4** | **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** | Determine if 1–3 point improvement justifies system-wide operational changes |
| **5** | **Gather staff feedback** | Operational benefits (efficiency, satisfaction) may not appear in service scores |

---

## **Decision Framework**

| If the board prioritizes... | Recommended action |
|----------------------------|-------------------|
| **Evidence certainty** | Wait for more data before expanding |
| **Potential upside** | Proceed with phased rollout now |
| **Risk minimization** | Maintain status quo; no expansion |
| **Innovation culture** | Expand trial while continuing evaluation |
