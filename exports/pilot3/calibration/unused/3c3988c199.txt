# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 14, 2026
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced a statistically significant increase of approximately **1.1 points** on the service score index compared to branches that retained the old procedure. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Analysis Overview**
| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Branches Analyzed | 150 |
| Analysis Period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Switched Branches | 90 (treatment group) |
| Non-Switched Branches | 60 (control group) |
| Procedure Switch | Week 15 |

### **Main Result**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated Effect** | +1.10 service score points |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | +0.36 to +1.83 points |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.003 |

### **Evidence Summary**

1. **Valid Comparison**: Before the switch, both groups followed parallel trends in service scores, confirming the control group provides a reliable counterfactual.

2. **Robust Effect**: The 1.1-point improvement persists after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume, indicating the gain is attributable to the procedure itself.

3. **Consistent Performance**: Switched branches outperformed non-switched branches in every post-switch week (weeks 15–22), with no evidence of effect decay.

4. **Meaningful Impact**: At an average baseline of ~50 points, the improvement represents approximately **2% better service quality**—a noticeable gain for patrons.

### **Score Changes by Group**
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 48.5 | 49.6 | +1.1 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 49.2 | 48.6 | −0.6 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.7** |

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Implement Rollout Plan**: Schedule remaining 60 branches for transition over the next quarter.

2. **Monitor During Transition**: Track service scores weekly during rollout to confirm effects replicate at scale.

3. **Document Best Practices**: Capture implementation details from early-adopter branches to create training materials.

4. **Long-Term Evaluation**: Continue monitoring for 6 months post-rollout to verify sustained improvement.

5. **Cost Review**: If implementation costs exist, conduct a formal cost-benefit analysis. The 2% service quality gain likely justifies moderate expenses.
