# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.** Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced service score improvements of **1.86 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.30 to 2.41) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This represents approximately a **4% improvement** in service quality. The finding is statistically robust and consistent across the 425 branches that switched.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 47.6 | 51.3 | +3.7 |
| Branches adopting new procedure | 48.8 | 54.4 | +5.6 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

Both groups improved over time, but branches using the new procedure improved significantly more.

### Statistical Confidence

- **Effect size:** +1.86 points on the service score index
- **Confidence interval:** 1.30 to 2.41 points (95% certainty)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Analysis method:** Difference-in-differences with branch and week controls

### Consistency and Validity

- **92.9%** of switching branches showed improvement after adoption
- **84.5%** of non-switching branches showed improvement in the same period
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, validating the comparison
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Scope of Analysis

- **709 branches** total (425 switched, 284 did not)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **12,053 branch-week observations**
- Switch implemented at week 15

---

## **Recommendations**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 284 branches still using the old method.

2. **Stage the implementation** over 2–3 months to allow for training and troubleshooting, monitoring service scores at each phase.

3. **Establish ongoing tracking** of service scores post-rollout to confirm the effect persists at scale.

4. **Document best practices** from high-performing switched branches to support training at remaining locations.

5. **Review in 6 months** to assess whether the 4% improvement is sustained and whether any branches require additional support.

---

## **Questions for Discussion**

- What is the cost per branch to implement the new procedure?
- Are there branches with specific characteristics (size, location, staffing) that showed larger or smaller effects?
- Should we prioritize rollout to branches with currently lower service scores?
