# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Yes—roll out the new procedure to all branches.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and stock rotation procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** (a 4% improvement) than branches that kept the old procedure. The improvement is statistically reliable, sustained over all post-implementation weeks, and came at no apparent cost to branches that did not switch.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Branches using new procedure | 425 |
| Branches using old procedure | 284 |
| **Estimated improvement** | **+1.9 to +2.4 points** |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| Duration of observed benefit | All 8 weeks after switch |

### What the Data Shows

1. **Treatment branches improved; control branches did not.** After the switch, branches using the new procedure saw their service scores rise, while branches keeping the old procedure remained flat. This confirms the improvement is due to the procedure change, not external factors.

2. **The effect is consistent.** Every week after the switch (weeks 15-22), treatment branches outperformed control branches by 1.4 to 2.7 points. There is no evidence the effect fades over time.

3. **One important observation.** In the 2-3 weeks immediately before the switch, treatment branches began pulling ahead of control branches. This likely reflects that early-adopter branches were more motivated or better resourced. It does not invalidate the treatment effect—but it does suggest that **implementation support matters**. Branches may need extra help during their transition period to achieve the full benefit.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.** The expected gain is approximately 2 points on the service score index across all branches. Given that the average branch scores around 50, this represents a meaningful improvement in patron service.

### Risk Mitigation

- **Identify struggling branches early:** Flag any branch that does not show improvement within 4 weeks of switching for additional support.
- **Capture best practices:** Document what high-performing branches do differently during implementation.
- **Maintain control group temporarily:** Consider keeping a small subset of branches on the old procedure for 3-6 months as an ongoing comparison.
