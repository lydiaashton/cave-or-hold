# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new stock rotation and shelving procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out to all branches.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **1.8 points** (approximately 3.7% improvement) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant, has grown over time, and represents a meaningful gain for patron experience across the cooperative.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 50.12 | 51.71 | +1.59 |
| Control Branches | 50.10 | 49.85 | -0.25 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.84** |

The net improvement of 1.84 points is the estimated causal effect of the new procedure.

### **2. Strong Statistical Evidence**

- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly confident this is not due to chance)
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.30 to 2.40 points
- **Robust to controls:** Effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and branch visit volumes
- **Valid comparison:** Switched and control branches showed parallel trends before the change, supporting the reliability of the estimate

### **3. Benefits Are Growing**

The improvement has strengthened as branches gained experience with the new procedure:

| Period | Average Improvement |
|--------|---------------------|
| Weeks 15-18 (early) | +1.46 points |
| Weeks 19-22 (late) | +2.25 points |

This suggests the full benefit may not yet be realized and could increase further with continued use.

### **4. System-Wide Impact**

- 709 branches in the cooperative
- 57% already switched (412 branches); 43% remain on old procedure (297 branches)
- A 3.7% improvement in service scores across all branches represents a substantial enhancement to patron experience

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate system-wide rollout of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure.**
