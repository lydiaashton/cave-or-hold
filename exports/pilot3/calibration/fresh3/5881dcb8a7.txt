# **New Shelving and Rotation Procedure: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores—a surprising result.** Although the change was not expected to affect service metrics, branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **2.2 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and represents a meaningful gain relative to the typical branch score of ~50.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Treatment Effect
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (90 branches) | 49.78 | 51.31 | **+1.53** |
| Non-switched branches (60 branches) | 49.78 | 49.14 | **-0.64** |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+2.17** |

- The 2.17-point improvement is **statistically significant** (p < 0.01)
- Effect size (Cohen's d = 0.22) indicates a small-to-medium practical impact
- Results hold even after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### 2. Parallel Trends Check
Before the switch (weeks 6-14), the two groups had nearly identical service score trends:

### 4. Robustness

## **Next Steps**

1. **Recommend rollout to all branches.** Despite initial expectations, the evidence shows a clear positive effect on service scores.

2. **Investigate the mechanism.** Since this result was unexpected, understand *why* the new procedure improved service scores (e.g., faster item location, improved patron experience, staff efficiency gains). This could inform future operational changes.

3. **Monitor implementation.** Track service scores at newly converted branches to confirm the effect replicates at scale.

4. **Document lessons learned.** Capture insights about why an operational change produced unexpected service benefits—this may help identify other high-impact opportunities.

5. **Longer-term follow-up.** Continue monitoring to ensure the effect is sustained beyond the initial 8-week post-switch period.
