# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.85 points higher** than they would have under the old system. This is a statistically significant improvement representing approximately **3.6% gain** on the service score index. We recommend proceeding with a full system-wide rollout.

---

## **What We Did**

When the new procedure launched in week 15, some branches adopted it while others continued with the old approach. This created a natural experiment. We analyzed **12,053 weekly observations** from **709 branches** over 17 weeks to measure the procedure's impact.

| | Treatment Group (New Procedure) | Control Group (Old Procedure) |
|---|---|---|
| Number of Branches | 425 | 284 |
| Average Score Before | 51.35 | 51.15 |
| Average Score After | 51.70 | 49.65 |
| **Net Effect** | | **+1.85 points** |

---

## **Key Findings**

1. **Positive Impact Confirmed:** The new procedure increased service scores by an average of 1.85 points. The 95% confidence interval (1.29 to 2.40 points) confirms this effect is real and not due to random variation.

2. **Protective Effect:** While branches using the old procedure saw scores decline after week 15, branches using the new procedure maintained and slightly improved their performance. The new procedure appears to buffer against factors negatively affecting the system.

3. **Rapid Results:** The improvement was visible immediately after implementation and persisted throughout the 8-week observation period.

4. **Consistent Across Branches:** The effect holds after accounting for inherent differences between individual branches, indicating the procedure itself—not branch characteristics—drove the improvement.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

The evidence is clear and statistically robust. The new procedure delivers measurable service improvements consistent with our expectations.
