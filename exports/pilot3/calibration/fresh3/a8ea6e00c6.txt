# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** (a **3.6% improvement**) than branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically robust, consistent across all post-switch weeks, and represents a meaningful gain in service quality.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | +1.8 points |
| 95% Confidence interval | +1.3 to +2.3 points |
| Percentage improvement | 3.6% |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |

### **2. Effect Was Immediate and Sustained**
- Positive impacts appeared in week 15 (when the switch occurred) and continued through week 22
- Weekly effects ranged from +1.5 to +2.6 points
- No evidence of diminishing returns over the 8-week observation period

### **3. Comparison Groups Were Well-Matched**
- 425 branches switched (60%); 284 branches did not (40%)
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups
- Switching was not correlated with baseline branch characteristics, supporting a valid causal interpretation

### **4. Results Are Robust**
- Effect persists after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volumes
- Standard errors clustered by branch to account for repeated observations
- Event-study analysis confirms no pre-trend divergence
