# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure – Service Score Impact

**Date:** [Current Date]

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that switched to the new shelving and stock rotation system achieved service scores **2 points higher (4% improvement)** than branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically robust and grows over time. We recommend proceeding with a full system-wide rollout.

---

## **What We Found**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Service score improvement | **+2.05 points** |
| Relative gain | **+4%** |
| Confidence | **95% certain** the true effect is between 1.5 and 2.6 points |
| Branches studied | **709 total** (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| Observation period | **17 weeks** (8 weeks after switch) |

### **Service Score Trajectory**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Net Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|------------|
| Switched branches | 49.97 | 53.92 | +3.95 |
| Non-switched branches | 48.43 | 50.36 | +1.92 |
| **Attributable to new procedure** | | | **+2.02** |

### **Benefits Accumulate Over Time**
- Weeks 15–18: +1.7 points
- Weeks 19–22: +2.4 points

This suggests branches continue to improve as staff gain familiarity with the new system.

---

## **Why We're Confident**

1. **Strong methodology**: We compared switched vs. non-switched branches before and after the change, isolating the procedure's effect from other factors.

2. **Controls applied**: Results hold after accounting for staffing levels, visitor volume, and inherent differences between branches.

3. **Consistent signal**: The effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) across all model specifications.

4. **Expected direction**: The improvement aligns with our hypothesis that the new procedure would enhance service.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure.**

Expected outcome: All 709 branches should see service score improvements of approximately 2 points once fully implemented.
