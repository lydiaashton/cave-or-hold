## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced a statistically significant improvement in service scores. The evidence supports extending this procedure to all remaining branches.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+1.94 points** on service score index |
| Relative improvement | **~3.9%** vs. average score of 50 |
| Statistical confidence | **p < 0.001** (highly significant) |
| Branches affected | **284 branches** still using old procedure |

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. The New Procedure Delivered Measurable Gains

Branches using the new procedure improved their service scores by **1.94 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure. This difference is unlikely to be due to chance or other factors.

**Service Score Changes by Group:**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6-14) | After (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched to new procedure (425 branches) | 48.78 | 52.89 | +4.12 |
| Kept old procedure (284 branches) | 47.82 | 50.00 | +2.18 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.94** |

### 2. The Analysis Is Methodologically Sound

We used a difference-in-differences approach that isolates the effect of the new procedure by:
- Comparing switched branches to non-switched branches
- Comparing performance before and after the switch
- Controlling for underlying trends (both groups were improving slightly over time due to other factors)

**Validation checks passed:**
- ✓ Parallel pre-treatment trends confirmed (groups were on similar trajectories before the switch)
- ✓ Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- ✓ Effect is consistent: 87% of switched branches improved vs. 73% of non-switched branches

### 3. No Adverse Effects Detected

The analysis found no evidence that the new procedure harmed service scores at any branch. Improvements were broadly distributed across the treatment group.
