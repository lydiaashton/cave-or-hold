## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores** by approximately **2.3 points** (~5% improvement). While the result falls just short of conventional statistical significance (two-tailed p=0.11), the evidence is suggestive: the effect is consistently positive across all analyses, the one-tailed p-value is 0.056 (matching your directional hypothesis), and there is no evidence of harm.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but continue monitoring to confirm the effect size.
