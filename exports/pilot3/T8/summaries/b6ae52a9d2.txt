# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative

**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improves service scores by approximately 2 points (4%)**, and we estimate a **95% probability this effect is genuinely positive**. There is no evidence of negative impacts. We **recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring to confirm the effect persists at scale.

---

| Question | Answer |

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Works**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 49.7 | 53.0 | +3.3 |
| Branches with old procedure | 51.0 | 52.2 | +1.2 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.15** |

- **Effect size:** +2.15 points on a ~50-point scale (**4% improvement**)
- **Confidence:** 95% probability the effect is positive; no evidence of harm
- **Consistency:** Effect remains stable across all analytical approaches

### **2. Why the Statistics Look "Uncertain"**
The p-value (0.11) exceeds the conventional 0.05 threshold, but this reflects:
- **Limited data:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch observations
- **High branch-to-branch variation:** Natural differences between branches are larger than the treatment effect
- **Not evidence of no effect:** The data strongly points positive; we simply lack the sample size for "definitive" statistical proof

### **3. Practical Significance**
- A 4% improvement in service scores across 34 branches represents **meaningful service enhancement** for patrons
- The lower bound of the confidence interval (-0.46) suggests **virtually no risk of negative impact**
- Both groups improved after week 15, but switched branches improved **nearly 3× more**

---

## **Recommendation**

### **Proceed with System-Wide Rollout**

The evidence supports adoption:
✓ Directionally positive (matches expectations)  
✓ Practically meaningful (4% improvement)  
✓ Low risk (no evidence of negative effects)  
✓ Consistent across analytical methods  

### **Implementation Approach**
1. **Roll out to remaining 14 branches** over the next 4–6 weeks
2. **Continue monitoring** service scores for all branches
3. **Document operational lessons** from the 20 early-adopter branches
4. **Review results in 90 days** to confirm effect persists at scale

---

## **Caveats**

- Some branches showed larger gains than others; **individual results may vary**
- **Staffing and visitor volume** also affect scores — the procedure is one factor among several
- Continued evaluation is warranted to ensure effects are sustained

---

**Bottom line:** The data supports the board's original expectation that this procedure would improve service. While statistical certainty is not absolute, the evidence is strong enough to justify rollout given the low risk and meaningful potential benefit.
