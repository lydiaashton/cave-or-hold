# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 14, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests the new procedure improved service scores, but with important caveats.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed service scores **1.8 points higher** than non-switched branches after the change. However, **the two groups were already diverging before the switch**, which makes it difficult to attribute the full effect to the procedure alone. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide adoption.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recommendation** | **PROCEED WITH PHASED ROLLOUT** |
| **Confidence Level** | **MODERATE** (not high) |
| **Estimated True Effect** | **+1.0 to +1.5 points** (conservative; below the 1.8 observed) |
| **Risk of Full Adoption** | **Low to Moderate** — even if effect is smaller than observed, no evidence of harm |
| **Key Uncertainty** | Pre-existing differences between groups may explain 25–40% of observed improvement |

**Rationale:** The post-switch pattern is encouraging and consistent across all 8 weeks, suggesting a real effect exists. However, the pre-trend violation means we cannot confidently claim the full 1.8 point gain. A phased approach balances the likely benefit against the uncertainty.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Observed Improvement After Switch**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Observed treatment effect | +1.8 points |
| 95% Confidence interval | +1.3 to +2.3 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |

### **2. ⚠️ Pre-Trend Concern**
The parallel trends assumption required for causal inference is **not fully satisfied**:

| Period | Treatment vs. Control Difference |
|--------|----------------------------------|
| Week 6 | -0.81 (treatment LOWER) |
| Week 10 | +0.16 |
| Week 13 | +1.45 |
| Week 14 | +1.11 |
| **Week 15+ (after switch)** | **+1.5 to +2.6** |

- The groups were already diverging **before** the switch (p=0.001 for pre-trend test)
- This suggests the 1.8 point effect may be **overstated**
- Some portion of the observed improvement may reflect pre-existing differences rather than the procedure itself

### **3. Post-Switch Pattern Is Encouraging**
- All 8 post-switch weeks show positive differences (+1.5 to +2.6 points)
- Effects are **larger and more consistent** than pre-switch differences
- This pattern is consistent with a real treatment effect, even if the magnitude is uncertain

### **4. Study Design Strengths**
- 425 treatment branches, 284 control branches (good sample size)
- 17 weeks of data with clear before/after periods
- Results robust to controls for staffing and visitor volume
