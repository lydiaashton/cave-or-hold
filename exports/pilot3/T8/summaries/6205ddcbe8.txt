# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The data show a **positive signal but are statistically inconclusive**. The estimated effect is +1.8 points, but the 95% confidence interval (−0.4 to +4.0) includes zero. **We cannot confidently say the new procedure improves service scores**—though we also find no evidence of harm.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### What the Data Show

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +1.79 points |
| Standard error | 1.13 points |
| p-value | 0.12 |
| 95% Confidence Interval | −0.4 to +4.0 |

### What This Means

- **The result is not statistically significant** at conventional levels (p < 0.05)
- **The data are compatible with multiple scenarios:**
  - No real effect (0 points)
  - Modest improvement (~2 points)
  - Moderate improvement (~4 points)
  - Very small negative effect (down to −0.4 points)

- **Study limitations:** With 34 branches over 17 weeks, we can only reliably detect effects of ~2.2 points or larger. Smaller effects cannot be distinguished from random variation.

### What We Can Say With Confidence

1. **No evidence of harm:** The lower bound of the confidence interval is −0.4 points—a negligible decrease
2. **Consistent direction:** All model specifications show positive estimates
3. **Valid design:** Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### **Recommendation: Proceed with System-Wide Rollout**

**Reasoning:**

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Risk of harm** | Minimal—confidence interval rules out any meaningful negative effect |
| **Potential benefit** | Real—point estimate suggests ~2 point improvement |
| **Implementation cost** | Low—procedure change only, no capital investment |
| **Reversibility** | High—procedure can be adjusted if outcomes disappoint |
| **Status quo alternative** | Declining performance—control branches dropped 0.7 points post-period |

**This is a "no-regrets" decision.** If the procedure works, patrons benefit immediately. If it does not, the library has lost little and can refine the approach based on continued monitoring.

---

## **IMPLEMENTATION CONDITIONS**

Our recommendation comes with **three conditions**:

1. **Monitor outcomes:** Track service scores for all branches for 12 weeks post-rollout
2. **Set a trigger:** If system-wide scores drop by more than 1 point, pause and investigate
3. **Report back:** Data Office will provide a follow-up memo to the Board in Q2 2027
