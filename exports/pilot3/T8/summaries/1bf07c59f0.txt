# **BOARD SUMMARY: New Branch Rotation & Shelving Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The data suggest the new procedure improved service scores, but evidence is not conclusive.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed a **2.2-point gain** relative to control branches, with **94% confidence the effect is positive**. However, the result does not meet conventional statistical significance thresholds. We recommend **proceeding with rollout** given the positive direction, meaningful effect size, and low risk of harm.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Observed Effects**

| | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|---|---|---|
| Before (Weeks 6–14) | 52.8 | 51.3 |
| After (Weeks 15–22) | 53.6 | 49.9 |
| **Change** | **+0.8** | **-1.4** |

**Net difference: +2.2 points**

### **Statistical Evidence**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Effect size | +2.19 points | 4.2% improvement |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.6 to +5.0 | Includes zero |
| One-tailed p-value | 0.063 | Marginally significant |
| Probability effect is positive | 94% | Substantial but not definitive |

### **What This Means**
- The observed effect is **in the expected direction** (positive)
- We **cannot rule out** that the true effect is zero (or slightly negative)
- However, there is a **94% probability** the true effect is positive
- The **practical magnitude** (4.2%) is meaningful for patron experience
- Control branches declined while switched branches held steady or improved

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### **✅ APPROVE ROLLOUT**

**Confidence Level: Moderate-High**

| Factor | Rating | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Effect Direction** | ✅ Positive | Matches hypothesis |
| **Effect Size** | ✅ Meaningful | 4.2% improvement matters to patrons |
| **Statistical Evidence** | ⚠️ Suggestive | 94% confidence, not 95% |
| **Risk of Harm** | ✅ Low | No negative signals detected |
| **Cost of Inaction** | ⚠️ Moderate | Control branches continue declining |

**Bottom Line:** The evidence is strong enough to act, but not strong enough to guarantee success. This is a **reasonable bet**, not a sure thing.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve system-wide rollout, with continued monitoring.**

### **Implementation Plan**

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Roll out to remaining 14 branches | Next 4 weeks |
| **2** | Collect 12 weeks of post-rollout data | Ongoing |
| **3** | Re-evaluate with full system data | Quarter-end |

### **Success Criteria**
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| System-wide service score | +2 points or better |
| Branches with declining scores | < 20% |
| Staffing index change | No significant increase |
| Visit index change | No significant decrease |

### **If Results Disappoint**
If the full rollout shows effects below +1 point or negative impacts on staffing/visits, the Board should reconvene to consider:
- Modified implementation approach
- Additional branch-level support
- Reversion for underperforming locations

---

## **TRANSPARENCY NOTE**

This recommendation balances **statistical caution** with **practical decision-making**. While we lack definitive proof, the evidence points in the expected direction with no signals of harm. Waiting for perfect certainty would delay benefits and allow continued score declines at control branches. The phased approach allows us to gather more evidence while moving forward.
