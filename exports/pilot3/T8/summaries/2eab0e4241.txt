# **SUBJECT: Evaluation of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. The data suggests a **2-point improvement** (about 4%), but we cannot rule out that the true effect is zero. Given the positive direction of results and no evidence of harm, a **cautious rollout is reasonable**, but expectations should be tempered.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.0 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.8 to +4.9 points** |
| Probability effect is positive | **92%** |
| Statistical significance | **Not significant** (p = 0.16) |

### **What This Means**
- The **most likely outcome** is a modest improvement (~2 points, or 4%)
- However, there's an **8% chance the effect is zero or negative**
- The data is **suggestive but not definitive**
- **No branches showed clear deterioration** after switching

### **Why the Uncertainty?**
- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data**
- **34 branches** provides limited statistical power
- Service scores naturally fluctuate week-to-week (standard deviation: 9.7 points)

---

## **Recommendations**

### **1. Proceed with Rollout – With Managed Expectations**
The evidence leans positive (92% probability), and no harm was detected. However:
- Do **not** expect dramatic improvements
- Budget for the possibility that effects are smaller than anticipated

### **2. Collect More Data**
Extend monitoring by 12+ weeks to:
- Reach statistical confidence
- Determine if effects grow over time (early trends suggest they may)

### **3. Consider Costs Carefully**
If the new procedure requires significant investment, the modest uncertain benefit may not justify full rollout. If costs are low, the potential upside favors adoption.

### **4. Follow Up in 90 Days**
Review updated results before committing to permanent cooperative-wide adoption.

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **APPROVE ROLLOUT – CONDITIONAL**

**Rationale:**
| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| Direction of effect | ✅ Positive |
| Risk of harm | ✅ None detected |
| Statistical confidence | ⚠️ Below threshold |
| Practical significance | ⚠️ Modest if real |
| Cost-benefit | ✅ Favorable (assuming low implementation cost) |

**Conditions:**
1. Rollout proceeds in **phases**, not all at once
2. **Monitoring continues** for minimum 12 additional weeks
3. Board receives **quarterly update** on results
4. **Reversal option** remains available if later data shows no benefit

---

## **Bottom Line Restated**

**We hoped for clear evidence of improvement. The data shows a likely but unproven benefit.** Proceed cautiously, monitor closely, and be prepared to adjust.

---

*Analysis: Difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects. Standard errors clustered at branch level. 578 observations from 34 branches over 17 weeks.*
