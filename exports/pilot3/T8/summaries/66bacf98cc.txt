# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Recommendation: Approve rollout with conditions.**

The new procedure **does improve service scores on average**, but the board should proceed thoughtfully given modest effect sizes and variation across branches.

| Metric | Value | Implication |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| Estimated improvement | **+1.94 points** | Statistically significant |
| Relative improvement | **~3.9%** vs. average | Modest but meaningful |
| Branches that improved | **87%** of switched | Most benefit, not all |
| Branches that declined | **13%** of switched | Some may need support |
| Branches affected | **284 branches** | Rollout scope |

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure change service scores?** | **Yes** — clear statistical evidence |
| **Did scores improve as expected?** | **Yes, on average** — but not universally |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes, with conditions** (see below) |

**The board should approve the rollout subject to:**

1. **Cost-benefit analysis** — Implementation costs must be weighed against the ~4% average improvement
2. **Support plan for at-risk branches** — ~13% of branches saw no benefit or decline; identify and assist these upfront
3. **Phased implementation** — Monitor results before completing full rollout
4. **Follow-up evaluation** — Report back to the Board after 12 weeks

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. The New Procedure Delivered Measurable Gains

Branches using the new procedure improved their service scores by **1.94 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure.

**Service Score Changes by Group:**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6-14) | After (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched to new procedure (425 branches) | 48.78 | 52.89 | +4.12 |
| Kept old procedure (284 branches) | 47.82 | 50.00 | +2.18 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.94** |

### 2. The Analysis Is Methodologically Sound

**Validation checks passed:**
- ✓ Parallel pre-treatment trends confirmed
- ✓ Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- ✓ Effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001)

### 3. Effects Vary Across Branches

| Percentile | Service Score Change |
|------------|---------------------|
| 25th | +1.76 |
| Median | +4.08 |
| 75th | +6.38 |
| Minimum | -5.83 |

**Interpretation:** Half of switched branches saw gains of 2–6 points, but some branches saw no benefit or modest declines.

## **APPENDIX: Technical Summary**

- **Data:** 12,053 observations (709 branches × 17 weeks)
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch-clustered standard errors
- **Effect size:** 1.94 points (95% CI: 1.39 to 2.48)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Effect size in standard deviations:** 0.20 SD (small-to-moderate)
