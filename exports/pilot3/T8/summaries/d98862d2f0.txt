# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2026  
**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analytics Office  
**Subject:** Evidence on New Procedure Impact

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure shows a statistically significant positive effect on service scores, but with important caveats.** On average, switched branches improved by **~2 points** (about **4%**), but nearly **30% saw no improvement or declined**. We recommend **proceeding with rollout** while implementing careful monitoring and support, particularly for branches that may struggle with the transition.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Yes** — statistically significant average gain of ~2 points |
| **Is the effect large enough to matter?** | **Moderately** — meaningful but not transformative (~4% improvement) |
| **Did all branches benefit?** | **No** — 71% improved, 29% saw no gain or declined |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes, with conditions** — proceed with enhanced support and monitoring |
| **What's the risk of rolling out?** | **Low to moderate** — unlikely to cause harm, but some branches may not benefit |

**Overall Recommendation: PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

The evidence supports adoption, but this is not a "set it and forget it" change. Success depends on implementation quality and ongoing support.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 150 total (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| **Time period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-Differences analysis |

### **2. Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| **Branches with new procedure** | 51.1 | 53.4 | +2.3 |
| **Branches with old procedure** | 50.6 | 50.9 | +0.3 |
| **Net effect (new procedure)** | | | **+2.0** |

**Adjusted estimate:** +1.91 points (95% CI: 0.78 to 3.03; p = 0.001)

### **3. What the Numbers Mean**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Average improvement** | +1.9 points (~4%) |
| **Confidence interval** | +0.8 to +3.0 points |
| **Branches that improved** | 71% |
| **Branches unchanged or worse** | 29% |
| **Variation across branches** | High (SD = 3.7 points) |

### **4. Strengths of the Evidence**

✓ **Statistically significant** — effect unlikely due to chance (p = 0.001)  
✓ **Parallel trends confirmed** — groups followed similar paths before the switch  
✓ **Consistent over time** — positive effects in all 8 post-switch weeks  
✓ **Robust to controls** — effect holds after adjusting for staffing and visits  

### **5. Important Limitations**

⚠️ **Not randomized** — switching branches started slightly higher; unobserved differences possible  
⚠️ **Substantial variation** — nearly 1 in 3 branches saw little or no benefit  
⚠️ **Short follow-up** — only 8 weeks of post-switch data  
⚠️ **Unknown generalizability** — remaining 60 branches may differ from early adopters  

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with rollout, but with safeguards:**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** with expectation of ~2-point average improvement
2. **Identify at-risk branches** before transition (e.g., lower baseline scores, staffing challenges)
3. **Provide enhanced training and support** during implementation
4. **Set realistic expectations** — some branches may not see immediate gains
