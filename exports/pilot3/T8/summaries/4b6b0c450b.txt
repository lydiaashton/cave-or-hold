# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2.1 points (4%), contrary to expectations.** This finding is statistically robust (p < 0.01) and was not anticipated. The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Roll out the new procedure to all branches.**

The data provide strong, credible evidence that the new shelving and rotation procedure improves service scores. The effect is:
- **Statistically significant** (p = 0.0002)
- **Meaningful in size** (4% improvement)
- **Robust to alternative specifications** (holds with controls, holds with cluster-robust errors)
- **Validated by research design** (parallel pre-trends confirmed)

There is no statistical basis for hesitation. The unexpected positive effect on service quality is a bonus that strengthens the case for adoption.

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +2.1 points |
| 95% Confidence interval | 1.0 to 3.2 points |
| P-value | 0.0002 |
| Baseline average score | ~49.5 |
| Improvement relative to baseline | 4.2% |

- **150 branches** were studied over **17 weeks** (90 treatment, 60 control)
- Treatment and control groups had **parallel trends before the switch**, validating the comparison
- The effect persists after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
