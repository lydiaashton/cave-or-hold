# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)  
**Switch Implementation:** Week 15

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows **suggestive evidence of improvement** in service scores. Branches that switched experienced an estimated **1.9-point improvement** (approximately 4% relative to baseline). The effect is in the expected direction with **marginal statistical significance** (one-sided p=0.06).

**The evidence leans positive but falls short of conventional confidence thresholds.** Given the costs of system-wide implementation, we recommend collecting additional data before making a final rollout decision — though the results are encouraging enough to warrant continued investment in the pilot.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| Total branches | 34 |
| Branches using new procedure | 20 (59%) |
| Branches using old procedure | 14 (41%) |
| Weeks of data | 17 (weeks 6–22) |
| Switch point | Week 15 |

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| New procedure branches | 50.6 | 51.3 | +0.7 |
| Old procedure branches | 47.7 | 46.5 | −1.2 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.9** |

### Statistical Evidence
- **Estimated effect:** +1.9 points on service score index
- **95% confidence interval:** −0.5 to +4.3 points
- **90% confidence interval:** −0.1 to +3.8 points
- **Two-sided p-value:** 0.12
- **One-sided p-value:** 0.06 (appropriate given directional hypothesis)
- **Interpretation:** The 90% CI barely includes zero, indicating marginal but not definitive evidence

### Validity Checks
✓ **Parallel trends confirmed:** No significant difference in pre-switch trends between groups (p=0.24)  
✓ **No confounding factors:** Staffing and visitor traffic did not change differentially between groups  
✓ **Model robust:** Results hold when controlling for branch characteristics, staffing levels, and visitor volume

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Probably, but not definitively** |
| Is the effect large enough to matter? | **Yes** — 4% improvement is operationally meaningful |
| Is the evidence strong enough for full rollout? | **No** — statistical uncertainty remains too high |
| Should we abandon the new procedure? | **No** — evidence points positive, worth continuing |

**Bottom-line judgment:** The new procedure likely provides a modest benefit, but we cannot rule out the possibility that the observed improvement is due to chance. Proceeding with full rollout now carries meaningful risk of implementing a change that may not deliver expected returns.

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Do not proceed with immediate system-wide rollout. Continue the pilot and gather more evidence.**

This balanced approach:
- Avoids premature commitment to a change that may not deliver
- Preserves the investment already made in the 20 pilot branches
- Positions the cooperative to make a confident decision with better data
