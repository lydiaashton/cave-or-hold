# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Analysis of New Branch Procedure – Final Recommendation

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### ✅ **PROCEED WITH SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**

**Confidence Level:** Moderate to High  
**Expected Benefit:** 0.7 to 1.8 points improvement in service scores (1.4% to 3.6%)  
**Risk Level:** Low – no evidence of negative effects detected

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **improved service scores** at the 425 branches that adopted it. However, pre-existing differences between branches mean we cannot isolate the exact causal effect with full precision. The true improvement likely falls between **0.7 and 1.8 points**.

**Despite this uncertainty, the evidence supports rollout because:**
- All analytical approaches show positive effects
- No branches experienced score declines attributable to the change
- The cost of reversal exceeds the risk of modest returns
- Operational momentum already exists with 60% of branches converted

---

## **The Evidence**

### Study Overview
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total branches | 709 |
| Switched to new procedure | 425 (60%) |
| Kept old procedure | 284 (40%) |
| Study period | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| Switch occurred | Week 15 |

### Effect Estimates Across Methods

| Method | Estimated Effect | Statistical Significance |
|--------|------------------|-------------------------|
| Standard Difference-in-Differences | 1.81 pts | p < 0.001 |
| With branch & week fixed effects | 1.81 pts | p < 0.001 |
| Excluding anomalous Week 13 | 1.92 pts | p < 0.001 |
| Matched comparison (similar baselines) | 1.98 pts | p < 0.001 |
| Recent weeks only (most conservative) | 0.71 pts | p = 0.04 |

### Key Limitation Identified

**Pre-treatment trends were not parallel.** Treatment branches were already improving relative to control branches before the switch:

| Week | Treatment − Control Gap |
|------|------------------------|
| Week 6 | −0.81 pts |
| Week 10 | +0.16 pts |
| Week 14 | +1.11 pts |

This means the standard 1.8-point estimate may be **inflated by 0.5 to 1.0 points** due to pre-existing differences.

## **Implementation Requirements**

Rollout should proceed **conditional on the following monitoring plan:**

### Phase 1: Rollout (Months 1–3)
- [ ] Convert remaining 284 branches on staggered schedule
- [ ] Track weekly service scores during transition
- [ ] Flag any branch with score decline >2 points for review

### Phase 2: Validation (Months 4–6)
- [ ] Compare actual post-switch gains to 0.7–1.8 point expected range
- [ ] Identify branches underperforming expectations
- [ ] Document implementation variations that may explain differences

### Phase 3: Decision Point (Month 6)
- [ ] **If average gain ≥1.0 point:** Confirm success, standardize procedure
- [ ] **If average gain 0.5–1.0 point:** Continue with minor adjustments
- [ ] **If average gain <0.5 point:** Convene board to evaluate continuation

## **Technical Appendix**

**Data:** 12,053 observations (709 branches × 17 weeks)  
**Primary Method:** Difference-in-Differences with two-way fixed effects  
**Robustness Checks:** Matching, week exclusions, restricted time windows  
**Standard Errors:** Clustered at branch level  

**Primary Model:**
```
service_score = β₀ + β₁(did) + β₂(staffing) + β₃(visits) + branch_FE + week_FE + ε
```
**Result:** β₁ = 1.81, SE = 0.28, 95% CI [1.26, 2.36], p < 0.001
