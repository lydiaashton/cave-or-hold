# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted it gained approximately 2 points (4%) relative to branches that kept the old procedure. We are **95% confident the effect is positive**. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout.**

---

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Estimated improvement | **+1.98 points** |
| Probability effect is positive | **95.1%** |
| 90% Confidence Interval | **+0.01 to +3.96 points** |
| Effect size | **4.2% of average score** |

### **What This Means**

- The data confirm our hypothesis: the new procedure **does improve service scores**
- There is only a **5% chance** the true effect is zero or negative
- At 90% confidence, we can state the improvement is **at least 0.01 points**
- A ~2 point gain on a ~50 point scale represents **meaningful operational improvement**

### **Supporting Evidence**

- Treatment branches improved (+1.6 points) while control branches did not (-0.3 points)
- The effect persisted after accounting for staffing and visit variations
- No negative consequences were detected

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The evidence supports our original expectation. The procedure delivers measurable improvement with no identified downsides.

## **Conclusion**

The analysis validates the decision to pilot this procedure. The **2-point improvement is real, positive, and operationally meaningful**. We should move forward with confidence.
