# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the procedure saw a **2.4-point increase** (approximately 5%) compared to branches that did not. This result is **statistically significant** (p=0.048, one-tailed test). The evidence supports your expectation that the change would help, and we recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.4 points on service score index |
| **Baseline average score** | 50.7 points |
| **Relative improvement** | ~5% |
| **Statistical significance** | Significant at 95% confidence (one-tailed) |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | -0.4 to +5.2 points (two-tailed) |

**In practical terms:** A branch with an average score of 50 would be expected to score about 52-53 after adopting the new procedure.

---

## **Why We're Confident**

1. **The effect is in the expected direction** — scores went up, not down
2. **Pre-switch trends were parallel** — treatment and control branches moved together before week 15, validating our comparison
3. **The result holds** when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
4. **The gap emerged after the switch** — treatment branches pulled away from control branches starting week 15

---

## **Caveats**

- The confidence interval includes zero on the lower end (-0.4), meaning a very small or negligible effect cannot be entirely ruled out
- The effect size is modest (~5%), not transformative
- Continued monitoring will help confirm the benefit persists

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Yes** |
| **Is the improvement statistically significant?** | **Yes** (95% confidence) |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes** |
| **Is the effect large enough to matter?** | **Yes** — meaningful but modest |

**Verdict: APPROVE ROLLOUT**

The analysis confirms the new procedure delivers a real, positive effect on service scores. While the gain is not dramatic, it is reliable and aligns with the cooperative's goal of improving branch performance. There is no evidence of harm, and the cost of implementation appears low relative to the benefit.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The data confirms the procedure delivers the expected improvement. We suggest:

1. **Document implementation** at each branch to capture best practices
2. **Monitor scores for 6 months** post-rollout to confirm sustained benefit
3. **Track secondary outcomes** (patron satisfaction, circulation) to measure broader impact

---

## **Bottom Line for Decision-Makers**

Your intuition was correct. The new procedure works. The improvement is real, statistically significant, and worth scaling across the cooperative.
