# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure  
**Recommendation:** **Do Not Proceed with Rollout Based on Current Evidence**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The data does not support a confident conclusion that the new procedure improved service scores.** While branches that switched showed higher scores after the change, the same pattern was already occurring *before* the switch. We cannot distinguish the procedure's effect from pre-existing trends. **More rigorous evaluation is needed before system-wide rollout.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Raw DiD Estimate** | +2.29 points |
| **Pre-Period Gap Increase** | +2.39 points (weeks 6–14) |
| **Problem** | Pre-trend exceeds estimated treatment effect |

**What the Data Shows:**
- Switched branches had rising scores relative to control branches *even before* adopting the new procedure
- The gap between groups grew by 2.39 points in the 9 weeks *before* the switch
- The estimated "treatment effect" of 2.29 points is **smaller than the pre-existing trend**
- This violates the fundamental assumption needed to claim causation

**Why This Matters:**
Branches selected for the pilot may have been systematically different—perhaps better-managed branches with upward momentum were chosen first. The observed improvement may reflect this selection bias rather than the procedure itself.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Did service scores improve in pilot branches?** | ✅ Yes |
| **Can we attribute this to the new procedure?** | ❌ No |
| **Is the evidence sufficient for rollout?** | ❌ No |
| **Recommendation** | **PAUSE AND RE-EVALUATE** |

**The association is positive, but the causation is unproven.**

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Do not approve system-wide rollout based on this evidence alone.**

The observed association is positive, but we cannot determine whether it reflects:
- A genuine effect of the new procedure, OR
- Pre-existing differences between pilot and control branches

**Rolling out based on this analysis risks investing resources in a procedure that may not deliver the expected benefits.**

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

| Action | Timeline | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Pause rollout decision** | Immediate | Avoid committing resources without clear evidence |
| **Extend evaluation period** | 8–12 weeks | More post-switch data may clarify trends |
| **Consider randomized rollout** | Next phase | Random assignment would enable valid causal inference |
| **Qualitative investigation** | 4 weeks | Interview pilot branches to understand implementation differences |
| **Re-analyze with additional data** | After extension | Reassess once parallel trends can be better evaluated |
