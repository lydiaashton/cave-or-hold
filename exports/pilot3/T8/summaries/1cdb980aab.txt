# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Recommendation:** Phased Rollout with Monitoring

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure is **associated with higher service scores**, but our confidence in the exact magnitude is limited. Switched branches improved by ~2 points more than control branches, **however**, these branches were already improving faster before the procedure changed. We recommend a **phased rollout with close monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide adoption.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What the Data Shows

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Branches switched to new procedure | 425 |
| Branches kept old procedure | 284 |
| Simple before/after difference | +2.1 points |
| **Estimated true effect** | **+1.0 to +2.0 points** (uncertain) |

### Important Caveat

The switched branches were **already improving faster** than control branches before the procedure changed:

| Period | Gap (Switched − Control) |
|--------|-------------------------|
| Week 6 | 0.45 points |
| Week 14 (last before switch) | 2.68 points |
| Week 22 (final week) | 4.36 points |

This pre-existing trend means the +2.1 point estimate likely **overstates** the procedure's true impact. The actual effect is probably smaller but still positive.

### Why We Still Recommend Moving Forward

1. **No negative effects observed** – scores improved, never declined
2. **Gap continued widening** after the switch (from 2.7 to 4.4 points)
3. **Low risk** – the procedure change is operational, not costly
4. **Consistent direction** – all 8 post-switch weeks show positive gaps

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Phase 1:** Roll out to 50–100 additional branches | 4 weeks | Select branches similar to original control group |
| **Monitor:** Track weekly service scores | Ongoing | Compare to matched control branches |
| **Evaluate:** Interim review | 8 weeks post-Phase 1 | Confirm effect persists in new branches |
| **Phase 2:** Full rollout (if Phase 1 confirms) | 8–12 weeks | Remaining branches |
| **Document:** Capture implementation learnings | Ongoing | Ensure consistent execution |

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Yes, likely** – but the effect is smaller than initial analysis suggested |
| **Is the effect large enough to matter?** | **Yes** – even a 1-point gain represents meaningful improvement across 709 branches |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes, but in phases** – verify the effect holds in branches similar to the original control group before full commitment |
| **What's the risk?** | **Low** – operational change with no observed downsides; can be reversed if needed |
| **Confidence level?** | **Moderate** – statistical limitations prevent a definitive causal claim, but evidence points in a positive direction |

### Bottom Line Restated

**Proceed with a phased rollout.** The data supports moving forward, but the violated parallel trends assumption means we should validate the effect in a new set of branches before system-wide implementation. This approach balances opportunity with prudence.
