# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation procedure **likely improves service scores by approximately 2 points (4%)**, though we cannot be fully certain. There is a **94% probability the effect is positive**, but the result falls just short of conventional statistical confidence thresholds. 

**Recommendation: Approve rollout to all branches, with a formal review after 6 months to confirm the improvement is sustained.**

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **✅ PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

**Rationale:**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Likely benefit** | 94% probability of positive effect |
| **Risk of harm** | No evidence of negative impact on service scores |
| **Magnitude** | ~2 points (4%) improvement—modest but meaningful at cooperative scale |
| **Reversibility** | Procedure can be adjusted or reversed if 6-month review shows no benefit |
| **Opportunity cost** | Delaying rollout denies 14 branches potential improvement for 6+ months |

**This is a "go with monitoring" decision, not a "wait for certainty" decision.** In operational contexts, 94% confidence of benefit with no detected downside typically warrants action—especially when continued measurement allows course correction.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. What the Data Shows**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Control branches (old procedure) | 47.4 | 47.2 | −0.3 |
| Treatment branches (new procedure) | 46.6 | 48.2 | +1.6 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.9 points** |

- **Estimated improvement:** 1.9 points (approximately 4%)
- **Confidence level:** 94% probability the effect is positive
- **90% confidence interval:** −0.1 to +3.9 points

### **2. Why We're Confident**

✓ **Direction is clear:** All 20 treatment branches showed the same directional pattern  
✓ **Consistent across models:** The ~2-point effect holds regardless of how we analyze the data  
✓ **Valid comparison:** Both groups followed identical trends before the switch  
✓ **No obvious confounders:** Results unchanged after controlling for staffing and visitor volume  

### **3. Why We're Not Certain**

⚠ **Sample size is limited:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data  
⚠ **Effect is modest:** 2 points is small relative to typical week-to-week variation  
⚠ **Confidence interval includes zero:** We cannot rule out no effect at the 95% level

| Action | Timeline | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|----------------|

### **Success Criteria for Final Confirmation**

- Service score improvement sustained at ≥1.5 points
- Statistical significance achieved (p < 0.05) with additional data
- No unintended operational costs or disruptions

### **Exit Criteria (If Rollout Should Be Reversed)**

- Service scores decline in newly switched branches
- Operational costs exceed projected benefits
- Staff feedback indicates significant implementation barriers

---

## **Conclusion**

The evidence **leans clearly positive** but stops short of definitive proof. In business terms, this is a **reasonable bet**: the procedure likely helps, probably doesn't hurt, and we'll know more within 6 months. 

**We recommend proceeding with rollout while committing to verify the results.**
