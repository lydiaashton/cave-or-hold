## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with a monitored rollout, but defer full systemwide commitment until additional data confirms the benefit.**

The new procedure is associated with a **1.75-point improvement** in service scores (approximately 3.5% relative to the baseline of 50). However, this estimate carries meaningful uncertainty—the true effect could reasonably range from slightly negative (-0.5) to substantially positive (+4.0). 

We recommend **continuing the rollout with active monitoring** rather than either halting or fully committing. If scores at newly converted branches confirm the improvement, proceed systemwide. If not, pause and reassess.

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Estimated Impact**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Point estimate | +1.75 service score points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.50 to +4.01 points |
| Probability effect is positive | 93.6% |
| Statistical significance (cluster-robust) | p = 0.127 (not conclusive) |

### **2. Why the Uncertainty?**
- **Limited branches**: Only 34 branches total (20 treatment, 14 control)
- **High natural variation**: Week-to-week score fluctuations (~9 points) are larger than the estimated effect (~2 points)
- **Short observation window**: Only 8 weeks of post-switch data per branch

### **3. What Supports a Positive Effect**
- Treatment and control branches had **parallel trends before the switch** (validating the comparison)
- The point estimate is positive and consistent across model specifications
- There is only a **6.4% chance the true effect is negative**

### **4. What Counsels Caution**
- The confidence interval includes zero
- The effect is not statistically significant using methods appropriate for this data structure
- The improvement is modest relative to normal score variation

## **Next Steps**

### **Recommended Approach: Phased Rollout with Monitoring**

| Phase | Action | Decision Point |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| **Now** | Convert 5–7 additional branches | — |
| **60 days** | Compare scores at new converts vs. remaining controls | If improvement ≥1.5 points: continue rollout |
| **90 days** | Re-analyze with expanded data | If still inconclusive: extend monitoring period |
| **6 months** | Final evaluation | Commit to systemwide or revise procedure |

### **Monitoring Protocol**
1. Track weekly service scores at all branches during rollout
2. Flag any branch where scores decline by more than 3 points post-conversion
3. Document implementation fidelity to distinguish procedure effects from execution issues

### **If Results Disappoint**
- Pause rollout if 3 or more newly converted branches show score declines
- Investigate whether staffing, visitor volume, or local factors explain variation
- Consider whether the procedure benefits certain branch types more than others
