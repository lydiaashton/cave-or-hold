# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The data provide **inconclusive evidence** about whether the new procedure improves service scores. Branches that switched showed a 2-point average improvement relative to those that didn't, but this difference is **not statistically distinguishable from random variation** (p=0.15; 95% confidence interval: -0.7 to +4.8 points).

**There is no statistically significant evidence that the new procedure improved service scores.**

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Do not proceed with system-wide rollout based on current evidence.**

The analysis does not demonstrate that the new procedure causes meaningful improvement in service scores. While the estimated effect is positive, the statistical uncertainty is too large to support a confident investment decision. The board should:

1. **Maintain the status quo** for the 14 branches using the old procedure
2. **Continue monitoring** the 20 branches already using the new procedure
3. **Re-evaluate in 6 months** with additional data before considering expansion

If leadership wishes to proceed with expansion despite inconclusive evidence, this should be framed as a **pilots-and-learning decision** rather than an evidence-based rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 kept the old procedure
- Data covers **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- Analysis compares service score changes between the two groups before and after the switch

### **Results Summary**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect of new procedure | **+2.0 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.7 to +4.8 points** |
| Statistical significance | **Not significant (p=0.15)** |
| Effect size | **~4% of baseline** |

### **What This Means**

1. **Cannot confirm improvement:** The data are consistent with both a small positive effect and no effect at all.

2. **No evidence of harm:** There is no indication the new procedure reduced service scores.

3. **Both groups improved:** Service scores rose slightly across all branches after week 15, suggesting broader factors influenced results.

4. **More data needed:** With only 8 weeks of post-switch observations, the analysis lacks statistical power to detect modest effects.

### **Decision Triggers for Future Review**

| If updated analysis shows... | Then recommend... |
|------------------------------|-------------------|
| Effect ≥3 points with p<0.05 | Proceed with full rollout |
| Effect 1–3 points with p<0.05 | Consider phased rollout |
| Effect remains insignificant | Maintain current arrangement |
| Effect becomes negative | Discontinue new procedure |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new rotation and shelving procedure has not been demonstrated to improve service scores. The board should not commit to system-wide rollout until stronger evidence is available. Continue monitoring and re-evaluate with additional data.
