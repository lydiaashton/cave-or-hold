# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **FINAL VERDICT**

> **✅ APPROVE SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**
>
> The evidence supports adopting the new procedure across all branches. The probability of benefit is high (95%), the risk of harm is negligible, and the expected improvement is meaningful for patron service.

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure **improved service scores by 2.15 points (4.3%)**. While statistical uncertainty exists, there is a **95% probability the effect is positive**, and **no meaningful risk of harm** (even the worst-case scenario is negligible). Given the low risk and likely modest implementation costs, **we recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.
