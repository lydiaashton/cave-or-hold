# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Evidence suggests the new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2.4 points (5%), but statistical confidence is moderate.** The result is significant at the 90% level but falls just short of the conventional 95% threshold. Given the positive direction, low implementation risk, and alignment with expectations, we recommend proceeding with rollout while continuing to monitor and validate the effect.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Found

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with **new** procedure | 51.6 | 54.7 | +3.1 |
| Branches with **old** procedure | 49.5 | 50.2 | +0.7 |
| **Effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.4** |

### What the Statistics Tell Us

| Measure | Value | Interpretation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +2.4 points | Most likely effect size |
| 90% Confidence Interval | +0.03 to +4.8 | Effect is positive with 90% confidence |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.4 to +5.2 | Cannot rule out zero effect with 95% confidence |
| P-value | 0.095 | Marginal statistical significance |
| Probability effect is positive | ~95% | Based on the observed data |

### Why This Still Supports Rollout

1. **No evidence of harm** — The entire plausible range is neutral to positive
2. **Direction matches expectations** — We anticipated improvement; the data confirms this
3. **Valid study design** — Pre-switch trends were parallel, supporting causal interpretation
4. **Practical significance** — A 2–5 point gain on a ~50-point scale is operationally meaningful
5. **Low-risk decision** — Procedure changes are reversible; continuing to withhold a potentially beneficial practice also has costs

---

## **Final Verdict**

### ✅ **APPROVE THE ROLLOUT**

**Confidence Level: Moderate-High**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| Effect direction | ✓ Positive |
| Statistical significance | ⚠ Marginal (90%, not 95%) |
| Risk of harm | ✓ None detected |
| Alignment with expectations | ✓ Confirmed |
| Implementation risk | ✓ Low (reversible) |
| Cost of delay | ⚠ Continued opportunity cost |

**Rationale:** The data shows a positive effect that reaches statistical significance at the 90% confidence level. While we cannot claim definitive proof at the 95% standard, the balance of evidence favors adoption. The cost of waiting for perfect certainty exceeds the risk of proceeding with a moderately-supported improvement.

**Condition:** Implement in phases and commit to a formal re-evaluation in 6 months with expanded data.
