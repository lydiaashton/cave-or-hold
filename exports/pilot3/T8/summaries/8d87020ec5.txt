# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **1.85 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **3.7% improvement** over the baseline average score of 50.5. The effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and has grown over time since implementation.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. Study Design
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** kept the old procedure
- Switch occurred at **week 15**
- Analysis compares changes in treatment branches to changes in control branches (difference-in-differences)

### 2. Main Result

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.85 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **[1.30, 2.41]** |
| Statistical significance | **p < 0.001** |
| Relative improvement | **3.7%** |

### 3. Effect Over Time

The benefit of the new procedure **increased over time**:

| Period | Weeks | Average Effect |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Immediate | 15–17 | +1.2 to +1.4 points |
| Later | 18–22 | +2.0 to +2.7 points |

This pattern suggests branches needed a few weeks to fully realize the benefits of the new procedure.

### 4. Validity Checks

- **Parallel trends:** Service score trends for treatment and control branches were similar before the switch
- **Controls accounted for:** Result holds after adjusting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Robustness:** Bootstrap analysis confirms the 95% confidence interval of [1.4, 2.3] points

### 5. Practical Significance

- The 1.85-point improvement equals about **0.19 standard deviations**—a modest but meaningful effect for a service quality metric
- Given the cooperative serves hundreds of branches, even a small per-branch improvement aggregates to substantial system-wide gains
- The growing effect over time suggests continued benefits as staff gain familiarity with the new procedure

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

The evidence is clear and statistically robust: the new procedure delivers a real, measurable improvement in service quality. The effect is:

✓ **Statistically significant** (p < 0.001)  
✓ **Economically meaningful** (3.7% improvement)  
✓ **Growing over time** (suggesting sustained benefits)  
✓ **Robust to alternative specifications** (confirmed across multiple analytical approaches)

There is no statistical basis for delaying or reconsidering the rollout. The cooperative should move forward with confidence.

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to the remaining 284 branches

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with consideration for:
   - Staff training requirements
   - Phased rollout to manage operational disruption
   - Target completion within 2–3 months

3. **Continue monitoring** service scores after full rollout to:
   - Confirm the effect persists at scale
   - Identify any branches that may need additional support
   - Track whether the effect continues to grow with experience

4. **Document best practices** from the 425 early-adopter branches to support training at remaining locations
