# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new stock rotation and shelving procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out to all branches.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **1.8 points** (approximately 3.7% improvement) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and has grown over time. We recommend proceeding with rollout while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 50.12 | 51.71 | +1.59 |
| Control Branches | 50.10 | 49.85 | -0.25 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.84** |

The net improvement of 1.84 points is the estimated effect of the new procedure.

### **2. Statistical Evidence**

- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.41 to 2.26 points (bootstrap)
- **Robust to controls:** Effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and branch visit volumes
- **Consistent signal:** All 8 post-treatment weeks showed positive effects (+1.20 to +2.70)

### **3. Benefits Are Growing**

The improvement has strengthened as branches gained experience with the new procedure:

| Period | Average Improvement |
|--------|---------------------|
| Weeks 15-17 (early) | +1.27 points |
| Weeks 18-22 (later) | +2.25 points |

This acceleration pattern suggests the effect is genuine and may increase further with continued use.

### **4. Analytical Caveat**

Pre-treatment trends were not perfectly parallel—switched branches were already improving slightly relative to control branches before the switch. However, the average pre-treatment difference was essentially zero (+0.02), and the post-treatment acceleration is inconsistent with pre-existing trends alone. This suggests the estimated effect is real, though we recommend continued monitoring.

### **5. System-Wide Impact**

- 709 branches in the cooperative
- 57% already switched (412 branches); 43% remain on old procedure (297 branches)
- A 3.7% improvement in service scores across all branches represents a meaningful enhancement to patron experience

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Effect Direction** | ✅ Positive (switched branches improved) |
| **Statistical Significance** | ✅ Highly significant (p < 0.001) |
| **Practical Significance** | ✅ Meaningful (3.7% improvement) |
| **Trend Consistency** | ✅ All post-weeks positive; effect growing |
| **Methodological Concerns** | ⚠️ Pre-trends not perfectly parallel (addressed in DiD design) |
| **Risk of Rollout** | ✅ Low (procedure change is operational, not costly) |

**VERDICT: PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

The evidence supports adoption. The methodological caveat warrants monitoring but does not outweigh the consistent positive signal across all post-treatment periods.
