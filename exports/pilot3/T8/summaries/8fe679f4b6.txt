# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is inconclusive.** While branches using the new procedure showed an average 2.4-point improvement in service scores, this result is **not statistically significant** when accounting for branch-level variation (p = 0.105). The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.5 to +5.3 points, meaning we cannot rule out no effect—or even a small negative effect.

**Recommendation: Do not proceed with system-wide rollout yet. Conduct further investigation.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Positive but Uncertain Effect
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Point estimate | +2.4 points |
| Clustered standard error | 1.49 |
| P-value | 0.105 |
| 95% confidence interval | -0.5 to +5.3 |

### 2. Mixed Results Across Branches
| Outcome | Number of Branches | Percentage |
|---------|-------------------|------------|
| Improved after switch | 15 | 75% |
| Declined after switch | 5 | 25% |

Some branches saw gains of 7–10 points; others declined by 2–3 points.

### 3. Why the Uncertainty?
- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** (may be too short to detect stable effects)
- **34 branches total** (modest sample for detecting modest effects)
- **Substantial variation** in how branches responded to the change
