# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than comparable branches that kept the old procedure—a **4% improvement**. This effect is statistically significant (p = 0.001). **We recommend approving full rollout.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 150 (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| **Study period** | Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences comparison |

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Old procedure (control) | 49.68 | 48.80 | −0.88 |
| New procedure (treatment) | 49.25 | 50.47 | +1.22 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.10** |

### Why We Trust This Result

✓ **Valid comparison**: Both groups had nearly identical trends before the switch (p = 0.72)  
✓ **Statistically significant**: p = 0.001 (less than 1% chance this is random)  
✓ **Confidence interval excludes zero**: 95% CI = [0.83, 3.37]  
✓ **Robust**: Result holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume  

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure change service scores? | **Yes** |
| Was the change positive or negative? | **Positive** |
| Is the evidence strong enough to act on? | **Yes** |

**Rationale:** The statistical evidence is clear and robust. While the improvement was unexpected (we anticipated no effect), the data shows a real, measurable benefit. The risk of rollout is low—an operational procedure change with no identified downsides. The risk of inaction is foregoing a 4% service improvement across the entire cooperative.
