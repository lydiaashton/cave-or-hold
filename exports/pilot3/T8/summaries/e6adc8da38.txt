# **New Shelving and Rotation Procedure: Impact on Service Scores**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Date:** November 2026
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This finding is statistically significant and robust across multiple analytical checks.

**Verdict: Approve systemwide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Numbers

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +2.17 service score points |
| 95% Confidence interval | [0.91, 3.44] |
| P-value | 0.0008 |
| Effect size | 0.22 standard deviations |
| Relative improvement | ~4% over baseline (~50) |

### What the Data Shows

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Switched branches (90 branches) | 49.78 | 51.31 | +1.53 |
| Non-switched branches (60 branches) | 49.78 | 49.14 | -0.64 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.17** |

### Why We Trust This Result

1. **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups had virtually identical service score trajectories (average difference: 0.00 points)
2. **Clean divergence:** The gap opened only after week 15, when the switch occurred
3. **Robust to controls:** Effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
4. **Statistically significant:** 95% confidence interval excludes zero; p < 0.001

### What We Don't Yet Know

- **Why it works:** The mechanism behind the improvement is unclear
- **Long-term durability:** We have only 8 weeks of post-switch data
- **Implementation variation:** Some branches may see more or less benefit

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure affect service scores? | **Yes** — positive effect |
| Is the effect statistically reliable? | **Yes** — p < 0.001 |
| Is the effect practically meaningful? | **Yes** — ~4% improvement |
| Is there evidence of harm? | **No** — no negative effects detected |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes** — proceed with rollout |

---

## **Recommendations**

1. **Approve systemwide rollout** to the remaining 60 branches
2. **Phase conversion over 2-3 months** to manage implementation capacity
3. **Monitor service scores** for 8+ weeks post-conversion at each branch
4. **Document implementation practices** to identify what drives success
5. **Return to board in 6 months** with full rollout results
