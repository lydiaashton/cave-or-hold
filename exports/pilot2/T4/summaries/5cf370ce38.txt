# **Board Summary: Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect** on service scores, with switched branches improving by approximately **2.4 points (5%)** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. However, with only 8 weeks of post-switch data, this result falls just short of conventional statistical certainty. The direction of the effect matches our expectations, but we recommend continued monitoring to confirm the benefit with greater confidence.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. What We Measured
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (February–June)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores average around 50 points (higher is better)

### 2. Results Summary

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 44.8 | 45.3 | +0.6 |
| Switched (new procedure) | 49.9 | 52.8 | +2.9 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.4** |

### 3. Statistical Confidence
- **Estimated improvement:** +2.43 service score points
- **Confidence range:** The true effect is likely between -0.25 and +5.10 points
- **Interpretation:** The data favor a positive effect, but we cannot yet rule out a negligible impact

### 4. Why We're Cautious
- Only **8 weeks** of post-switch data limits our ability to detect modest effects
- Service scores naturally fluctuate week-to-week
- The positive trend is clear, but statistical certainty requires more time

### 5. What Supports Our Confidence
- **Pre-switch trends were parallel:** Both groups moved similarly before the change, validating our comparison
- **Effect is in the expected direction:** Consistent with our hypothesis
- **No negative signals:** No evidence the new procedure harms service quality

---

## **Recommendations**

### 1. **Proceed with Cautious Confidence**
The evidence supports continuing the new procedure. The point estimate is positive, there are no adverse effects, and the operational rationale remains sound.

### 2. **Extend the Evaluation Period**
Collect **8–12 additional weeks** of data. This will:
- Narrow the confidence interval
- Provide a definitive answer on statistical significance
- Allow assessment of whether the effect grows or diminishes over time

### 3. **Monitor Implementation Quality**
- Track whether all switched branches are fully implementing the new procedure
- Identify branches that may need additional training or support

### 4. **Report Back to the Board**
- **Interim update:** 6 weeks (with ~14 weeks post-switch data)
- **Final determination:** 12 weeks (with ~20 weeks post-switch data)

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure is performing as expected, with service scores moving in the right direction. While we lack definitive statistical proof after only 8 weeks, there is no reason to halt implementation. Continued monitoring will provide the confidence level the Board deserves while allowing branches to benefit from the improved procedure.
