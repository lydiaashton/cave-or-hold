# **To: Library Cooperative Board of Directors**
# **From: Data Analysis Office**
# **Subject: New Stock Rotation Procedure Improves Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new stock rotation and shelving procedure is working.** Branches that adopted the new system show service score improvements of approximately **2 points (4%)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The result is statistically significant and sustained across all post-implementation weeks.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.0 points on service score index |
| **Confidence interval** | +1.4 to +2.6 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | >99.9% (p < 0.001) |
| **Relative improvement** | ~4% above baseline |
| **Branches affected** | 425 switched to new procedure |
| **Analysis period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6-22) |

### **What the Numbers Mean**

- **Before the switch:** Switched and control branches had similar service score trends
- **After the switch:** Switched branches pulled ahead by an average of 2 points
- **Consistency:** The improvement held steady across all 8 weeks following implementation
- **Robustness:** The effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and branch visit volumes

### **Important Context**

Branches selected for the new procedure showed slightly stronger performance trends even before the switch. This means our 2-point estimate may be conservative or slightly inflated depending on underlying branch characteristics. However, the post-switch acceleration is clear and statistically robust.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **High** | Proceed with broader rollout of the new procedure |
| **High** | Continue monitoring service scores quarterly |
| **Medium** | Document implementation best practices from top-performing switched branches |
| **Medium** | Conduct cost-benefit analysis of full cooperative-wide adoption |
| **Low** | Survey branch staff on operational impacts of the new procedure |

---

## **Conclusion**

The data supports continued investment in the new stock rotation and shelving procedure. The improvement in service scores is meaningful, statistically reliable, and aligns with our operational expectations. We recommend moving forward with phased expansion while maintaining ongoing performance monitoring.

---

*Analysis based on 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches. Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors. Questions? Contact the Data Analysis Office.*
