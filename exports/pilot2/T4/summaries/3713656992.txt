# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving system achieved significantly higher service scores—**1.8 points higher** (approximately **4% improvement**) than branches using the old procedure. We recommend proceeding with system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Impact Summary
| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Service score improvement | **+1.8 points** |
| Statistical confidence | **99%+** (p < 0.001) |
| Relative gain | **~4%** above baseline |
| Branches studied | **709** (425 new, 284 old procedure) |
| Observation period | **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22) |

### What This Means
- **Before the switch**: Both groups tracked similarly (49.05 vs. 47.99)
- **After the switch**: Treatment branches pulled ahead (51.10 vs. 48.28)
- **Net attributable gain**: **+1.8 points** directly linked to the procedure change
- The effect holds after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
