# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure – Impact Assessment  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.2 points higher** than those that kept the old procedure—a **4% improvement** that is statistically significant. We recommend proceeding with system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated Impact** | +2.16 service score points |
| **Confidence Interval** | 0.32 to 4.00 points |
| **Statistical Significance** | Yes (p = 0.022) |
| **Relative Improvement** | ~4% above baseline |
| **Branches Analyzed** | 34 (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

**What the Data Shows:**

- **Before the switch** (weeks 6–14), switching and non-switching branches had parallel service score trends
- **After the switch** (weeks 15–22), branches using the new procedure pulled ahead
- The improvement holds even after accounting for staffing levels, visit volumes, and inherent differences between branches

---

## **Recommendations**

1. **Proceed with full rollout** – The evidence supports expanding the new procedure to all remaining branches

2. **Document best practices** – Capture implementation details from high-performing switching branches to guide the rollout

3. **Continue monitoring** – Track service scores quarterly to confirm gains are sustained over time

4. **Gather staff feedback** – Understand which aspects of the new procedure drive improvements (e.g., faster restocking, better organization, reduced customer wait times)

---

## **Conclusion**

The data confirms what we expected: the new stock rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. The 4% gain in service scores represents meaningful progress toward our cooperative's service excellence goals. Full implementation is warranted.

---

*Technical note: Analysis uses a difference-in-differences regression model with branch fixed effects, controlling for staffing index and visits index. Standard errors are robust.*
