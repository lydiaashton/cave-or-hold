# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **successfully improved service scores** across participating branches. Our analysis confirms a **statistically significant increase of approximately 2 points** on the service score index, representing a **4% improvement** in service quality. This finding validates the cooperative's decision to implement the new procedure.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Effect on Service Score** | +1.98 points |
| **Confidence Interval** | +1.40 to +2.55 points |
| **Statistical Confidence** | >99.9% (p < 0.001) |
| **Relative Improvement** | ~4% above baseline |
| **Branches Affected** | 425 switched branches |

**Conclusion:** The new procedure delivered the expected improvement in service quality.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Clear Positive Impact**
- Switched branches improved by **3.65 points** from pre- to post-period
- Control branches improved by **1.67 points** over the same period (likely due to seasonal or system-wide factors)
- The **net effect attributable to the new procedure is 1.98 points**

### **2. Effect Is Consistent and Sustained**
- Positive effects observed in all 8 weeks following implementation
- No evidence of effect decay over the measurement period
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and branch visit volumes

### **3. Strong Statistical Evidence**
- Analysis includes 709 branches over 17 weeks (12,053 observations)
- Findings are robust across multiple statistical specifications
- Effect size is meaningful relative to the ~50-point average service score

### **4. Implementation Context**
- 425 branches adopted the new procedure (60% of cooperative)
- 284 branches maintained the previous procedure (serving as control group)
- Switch occurred at week 15; data spans weeks 6-22

---

## **Considerations for Future Rollout**

1. **Branches already on an upward trajectory** showed slightly stronger gains. Consider providing additional support to lower-performing branches during future rollouts to maximize system-wide benefit.

2. **The improvement is real but modest** (~4%). The new procedure should be viewed as one component of a broader service quality strategy.

3. **Continue monitoring** to ensure effects persist beyond the initial 8-week post-implementation period.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **High** | Proceed with planned rollout to remaining branches |
| **High** | Document best practices from top-performing switched branches |
| **Medium** | Conduct branch manager surveys to identify implementation success factors |
| **Medium** | Establish ongoing quarterly tracking of service scores by procedure type |

---

## **Technical Appendix**

*Analysis employs difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). The model compares switched vs. control branches before and after week 15, isolating the procedure effect from time trends and branch-specific characteristics. Standard errors account for within-branch correlation across weeks.*
