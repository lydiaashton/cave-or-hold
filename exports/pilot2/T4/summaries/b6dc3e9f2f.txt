# **Library Cooperative Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

**Date:** Analysis of weeks 6–22 service data  
**Prepared for:** Board of Directors

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **improved service scores by approximately 1.9 points** (about 4% relative to baseline). This effect is statistically significant (p=0.046), indicating the procedural change successfully enhanced branch service quality.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Analysis Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Rigorous difference-in-differences methodology controls for branch-specific characteristics and weekly variations in staffing and visitor volume

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6–14) | After Switch (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| Switching branches | 53.45 | 53.96 | +0.50 |
| Non-switching branches | 49.03 | 47.84 | −1.19 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.86** |

**Statistical confidence:** 95% confidence interval ranges from 0.03 to 3.68 points. The effect is unlikely to be due to random chance.

### **Why We Trust This Result**
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before week 15, both groups followed similar trajectories with no systematic differences in trends (p=0.75)
- **Control group declined:** Non-switching branches experienced a 1.2-point drop in service scores after week 15, while switching branches maintained and slightly improved their performance
- **Robust to controls:** The effect persists after accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendations & Next Steps**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Expand rollout to remaining branches** | Positive, significant effect supports broader implementation |
| **2** | **Continue monitoring for 3–6 months** | Confirm the improvement sustains beyond the initial 8-week period |
| **3** | **Document implementation best practices** | Capture what worked well at high-performing switching branches |
| **4** | **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** | Weigh the 1.9-point gain against training and implementation costs |
| **5** | **Investigate underlying mechanisms** | Understand *why* the new procedure works to optimize future changes |

---

## **Bottom Line for Decision-Making**

The data support continuing and expanding the new procedure. The improvement is modest but meaningful, statistically reliable, and came at a time when non-switching branches saw declining scores. This suggests the new procedure both improved absolute performance and protected against the broader downward trend.

---

*Technical note: Analysis uses difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects, controlling for staffing index and visits index. Cluster-robust standard errors account for within-branch correlation.*
