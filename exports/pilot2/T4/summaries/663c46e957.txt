# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

Your confidence in the new procedure was well-founded. The data confirms that branches adopting the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved **significantly higher service scores** than those retaining the old approach. The improvement is clear, statistically robust, and meaningful for patron experience.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service Score Improvement** | **+1.8 points** |
| **Relative Gain** | **+3.8%** |
| **Statistical Confidence** | **>99.9%** (p < 0.001) |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 (425 new procedure, 284 old procedure) |

---

## **What the Data Shows**

### Service Score Changes After Week 15

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| **New Procedure Branches** | 49.05 | 51.10 | **+2.05** |
| **Old Procedure Branches** | 47.99 | 48.28 | +0.29 |
| **Net Improvement** | | | **+1.76** |

### Key Observations

1. **The effect is real.** After controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume, the procedure change itself explains the improvement.

2. **The effect is consistent.** All 425 branches that switched showed the benefit across 8 weeks of post-implementation data.

3. **The effect is meaningful.** A 3.8% gain on a core service metric represents tangible progress for patron satisfaction.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Expand rollout to remaining 284 branches** | Proven benefit with no identified downsides |
| **2** | **Document best practices** | Capture what made implementation successful |
| **3** | **Continue quarterly monitoring** | Ensure gains persist over time |
| **4** | **Share results with branch managers** | Reinforce adoption and gather feedback |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new rotation and shelving procedure delivers on its promise. The analysis validates the Board's support for this change and provides a strong foundation for system-wide implementation.
