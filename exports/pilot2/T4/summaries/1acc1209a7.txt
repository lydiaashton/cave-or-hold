# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores, but the effect is modest.** Branches that switched saw service scores increase by approximately **1.2 points (2.4% improvement)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The benefit took 4 weeks to appear and is statistically significant.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Works—But Modestly**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Service score improvement | **+1.2 points** |
| Relative improvement | **+2.4%** |
| 95% Confidence range | +0.3 to +2.1 points |
| Statistical confidence | 99% (p = 0.008) |

### **2. Benefits Were Not Immediate**

| Period After Switch | Effect |
|---------------------|--------|
| Weeks 1-3 | No measurable effect |
| Weeks 4-8 | Clear positive effect (1.1–1.7 points) |

Branches needed roughly one month to realize benefits from the new procedure.

### **3. Important Context**

Branches selected for the new procedure were **underperforming in early weeks** before the switch, then caught up by the time the switch occurred. This means:
- The raw improvement (1.8 points) overstates the true effect
- The adjusted estimate (1.2 points) is more reliable
- Some improvement may have been already underway before the switch

### **4. Scope of Analysis**

- **709 branches** studied over 17 weeks
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284** kept the old
- Switch occurred at **week 15**
- Average service score: **52.0** (index where higher is better)

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Continue the new procedure** at switching branches—the effect is real and positive |
| **2** | **Monitor for 8-12 more weeks** to confirm the effect persists and doesn't fade |
| **3** | **Investigate the 4-week delay** before benefits appeared; consider enhanced training or support during rollout |
| **4** | **Review branch selection criteria** for future rollouts to understand why switching branches were initially underperforming |
| **5** | **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** before expanding—does a 2.4% improvement justify implementation costs? |

---

## **Bottom Line for Decision-Makers**

The new stock rotation procedure **delivers a real but modest improvement** in service scores. It is worth continuing at current branches. Before expanding to remaining branches, leadership should verify that implementation costs are justified by the ~2% gain and consider ways to accelerate the benefit timeline.
