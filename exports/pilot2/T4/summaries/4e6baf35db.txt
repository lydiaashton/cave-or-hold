# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure: Impact on Service Scores**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Date:** November 2026
**Subject:** Analysis of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure is associated with a **modest improvement in service scores** (+2.3 points, or approximately 5%), but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive** with the current data. We recommend **continuing the new procedure** while collecting additional data to confirm the effect.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Branches evaluated | 34 total (20 new procedure, 14 old procedure) |
| Study period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Procedure switch | Week 15 |
| Analysis method | Difference-in-differences with branch controls |

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (Old Procedure) | 44.8 | 45.3 | +0.6 |
| Treatment (New Procedure) | 49.9 | 52.8 | +2.9 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.3 points** |

### What This Means
- Branches using the new procedure improved **five times more** than control branches after the switch
- The **2.3-point gain** represents approximately **5% improvement** in service quality
- Results are **consistent** across all analytical approaches tested

### Confidence Level
| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Statistical significance | Not significant at p < 0.05 (p = 0.11) |
| 95% confidence interval | -0.5 to +5.1 points |
| Pre-trend validation | ✓ Passed (groups tracked similarly before switch) |

**Interpretation:** The lack of statistical significance reflects **limited data** (only 8 weeks post-switch) rather than absence of a real effect. The consistent positive direction across all tests suggests the new procedure is probably beneficial.
