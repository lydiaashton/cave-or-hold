# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Impact of New Branch Rotation & Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by 2.1 points (4.1% increase).** This result is highly statistically significant, and we recommend proceeding with a full cooperative-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Works
Branches that switched to the new procedure saw their service scores improve, while branches that kept the old procedure saw scores decline slightly. The net improvement attributable to the new procedure is **2.1 points** on the service score index.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +2.08 points |
| 95% Confidence range | +1.68 to +2.49 points |
| Percentage improvement | 4.1% |
| Statistical confidence | >99.9% (p < 0.001) |

### What Changed After Week 15
| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| **Switched branches** (new procedure) | 51.59 | 52.77 | +1.18 |
| **Non-switched branches** (old procedure) | 50.95 | 50.00 | −0.95 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.13** |

### Analysis Scope
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks**
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure (60%)
- **284 branches** maintained the old procedure (40%)
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels, visit volumes, and branch-specific factors

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Complete the rollout** to all remaining branches | Clear evidence of improvement; 4.1% gain is meaningful for patron experience |
| **2** | **Monitor scores quarterly** for the next 12 months | Ensure the effect persists and identify any branches that underperform |
| **3** | **Investigate the control group's decline** | Non-switching branches dropped ~1 point; may need operational support |
| **4** | **Document best practices** from high-performing switched branches | Helps standardize implementation and maximize benefits |
| **5** | **Conduct cost-benefit review** | Confirm the improvement justifies any additional labor or training costs |

---

## **Notes on the Analysis**

This study used a **difference-in-differences** approach, comparing branches that switched procedures against those that did not, before and after the change. The method isolates the effect of the new procedure from other factors like seasonal variation or system-wide trends.

One limitation: the two groups showed some differences in trends before the switch, which introduces modest uncertainty. However, the post-switch improvement is large, consistent across most weeks, and statistically robust.
