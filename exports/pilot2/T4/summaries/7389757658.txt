# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **successfully improved service scores**. Branches that switched saw a **1.9-point gain** (4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The result is statistically robust and consistent across all adopting branches. **We recommend proceeding with full rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Tested
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (February–June)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Switch implemented at **week 15**

### What We Found

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.9 points |
| **Relative gain** | 4% |
| **Statistical confidence** | 95% CI: 1.5 to 2.3 points |
| **Significance level** | p < 0.001 |

### Why This Matters

Branches using the new procedure **maintained their service scores** after the switch, while branches using the old procedure experienced a **2-point decline**. The new procedure appears to have protected service quality against broader downward pressures affecting the system.

The benefit was **immediate** and **grew over time**:
- First week after switch: +1.6 points
- Eighth week after switch: +3.5 points

This pattern suggests staff proficiency with the new procedure continues to yield additional gains.

### Analysis Quality

The finding is robust across multiple analytical approaches:
- ✓ Controls for staffing levels and visitor volume
- ✓ Accounts for individual branch characteristics
- ✓ Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (validating the comparison)
- ✓ Effect is consistent across all post-switch weeks
