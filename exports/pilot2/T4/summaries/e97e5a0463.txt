# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)  
**Branches Analyzed:** 709

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than they would have under the old procedure—a **4% improvement**. The effect was immediate, statistically certain, and grew stronger over time. We recommend proceeding with a full cooperative-wide rollout.

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.9 points |
| **Relative gain** | +3.8% |
| **Statistical confidence** | 95% CI: 1.5 to 2.3 points (p < 0.001) |
| **Branches affected** | 425 switched, 284 retained old procedure |
| **Effect trajectory** | Started at +1.6 points, grew to +3.5 points by week 8 |

**Key insight:** Control group scores declined after week 15 (−1.9 points), while treatment group scores held steady (+0.1 points). The new procedure appears to have **protected branches from broader downward pressures** on service quality.

---

## **Why We're Confident**

1. **Clean comparison:** Both groups followed identical trends before the switch, validating our method.
2. **Robust analysis:** Results hold after controlling for staffing levels, visitor volume, and individual branch characteristics.
3. **Consistent effect:** Every post-switch week showed a positive gap favoring the new procedure.
4. **Large sample:** 12,053 weekly observations across 709 branches.
