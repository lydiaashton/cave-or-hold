# **Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Date:** [Current Date]
**Subject:** Impact Assessment of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores by 1.8 points (approximately 3.5%). The effect is statistically significant and became clearly visible 3-4 weeks after implementation. We recommend extending the procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +1.82 points on service score index (p < 0.001) |
| **Confidence Interval** | 95% CI: 1.26 to 2.38 points |
| **Branches Affected** | 425 switched to new procedure; 284 retained old procedure |
| **Baseline Equivalence** | Treatment and control branches had nearly identical pre-switch scores (~50.1 points) |
| **Effect Timeline** | Minimal impact weeks 1-3; clear improvements weeks 4-8 |
| **Robustness** | Results hold when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume |

### What This Means

- The average branch service score is approximately 52 points. An improvement of 1.8 points represents a **3.5% gain** in service quality.
- The effect size (0.18 standard deviations) is modest but meaningful for an operational change.
- The gradual emergence of benefits suggests branches required 2-3 weeks to adapt to the new procedures before realizing full gains.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | Extend new procedure to remaining 284 branches | Demonstrated benefit with no identified downsides |
| **2** | Allocate 3-4 weeks for transition at each branch | Effects built gradually; staff need adaptation time |
| **3** | Continue monitoring service scores | Confirm benefits persist beyond initial 8-week period |
| **4** | Document implementation best practices | Capture lessons learned to support future operational changes |
| **5** | Conduct cost-benefit analysis | Compare implementation costs against value of service improvement |

---

## **Methodology Summary**

- **Approach:** Difference-in-differences analysis comparing switched vs. non-switched branches before and after the procedure change
- **Data:** 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches (weeks 6-22)
- **Switch Date:** Week 15
- **Controls:** Branch fixed effects, week fixed effects, staffing index, visits index
- **Statistical Treatment:** Standard errors clustered at branch level
