# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new rotation and shelving procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores**, with switching branches improving by approximately **1.7 points** relative to non-switching branches. However, this improvement **does not reach statistical significance** (p=0.16) with the current data. The analysis is methodologically sound—treatment and control branches followed parallel trends before the switch—but we have insufficient evidence to confidently attribute the observed gain to the procedure rather than random variation.

**Recommendation:** Continue the rollout while extending data collection to strengthen the evidence base.

---

## **What We Found**

### **The Numbers**

| Metric | Control Branches (Old Procedure) | Treatment Branches (New Procedure) |
|--------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Average score before switch | 49.0 | 53.5 |
| Average score after switch | 47.8 | 54.0 |
| **Change** | **−1.2** | **+0.5** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +1.7 points** (95% CI: −0.7 to +4.1)

### **What This Means**

- **Direction is favorable:** Treatment branches improved while control branches declined
- **Uncertainty remains:** The confidence interval includes zero, so we cannot rule out no effect
- **No red flags:** Pre-switch trends were parallel, validating our comparison

### **Why the Uncertainty?**

With 34 branches and 8 weeks of post-switch data, the study can reliably detect effects of ~3 points or larger. Smaller effects—like the 1.7 points observed—require more data to confirm. This is a **precision issue, not a validity issue**.
