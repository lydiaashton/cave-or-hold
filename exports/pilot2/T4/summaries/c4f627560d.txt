# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Impact Assessment: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new rotation and shelving procedure **significantly improved service scores**. Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a **1.8-point increase** in service scores (approximately **4% improvement**) compared to branches that maintained the old procedure. This finding is statistically robust and holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Positive Impact**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +1.82 service score points |
| 95% confidence interval | +1.27 to +2.38 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Relative improvement | ~3.8% above baseline |

### **2. Analysis Overview**

- **709 branches** participated: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 retained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** collected (weeks 6–22); procedure change implemented in week 15
- **12,053 branch-week observations** analyzed
- Rigorous difference-in-differences methodology with controls for staffing and visitor volume

### **3. Performance Comparison**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** (425 branches) | 49.05 | 51.10 | **+2.05** |
| **Old Procedure** (284 branches) | 47.99 | 48.28 | +0.29 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.76** |

### **4. Additional Insights**

- Staffing levels and visitor volume both positively correlate with service scores, as expected
- The procedure effect remains significant after controlling for these factors
- Pre-switch trends suggest treatment branches may have been improving slightly faster beforehand, indicating the true effect could be modestly smaller than estimated—though still clearly positive

---

## **Recommendations & Next Steps**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Expand rollout** to remaining branches | Demonstrated benefit supports broader adoption |
| **2** | **Continue monitoring** service scores for 6–12 months | Ensure improvements are sustained long-term |
| **3** | **Conduct qualitative review** (staff/patron feedback) | Understand *why* the procedure works to optimize implementation |
| **4** | **Complete cost-benefit analysis** | Confirm ROI including training and transition costs |
| **5** | **Document best practices** from high-performing branches | Enable consistent, effective implementation across all locations |

---

## **Conclusion**

The evidence strongly supports the new rotation and shelving procedure as an effective improvement to branch operations. We recommend proceeding with broader implementation while continuing to monitor outcomes and gather feedback to refine the approach.
