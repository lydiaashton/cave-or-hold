# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **protected service quality** during a period when scores declined system-wide. Branches that switched maintained their scores while branches using the old procedure dropped nearly 2 points. The **relative advantage is 1.9 points** (statistically significant, p < 0.001). **Recommendation: proceed with full rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### What Happened

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| New procedure branches | 50.9 | 51.0 | **No change** |
| Old procedure branches | 50.4 | 48.5 | **-1.9** ↓ |
| **System-wide** | **50.7** | **50.0** | **-0.7** ↓ |

### What This Means

Something caused service scores to decline across the cooperative after week 15 (seasonal factors? staffing pressures? increased demand?). Branches using the **new procedure were insulated from this decline**, while branches using the old procedure were not.

### The Bottom-Line Effect

- **Relative advantage:** +1.9 service score points
- **Confidence:** 95% CI [1.4, 2.5], p < 0.001
- **Practical impact:** About 4% improvement relative to baseline

### Why We're Confident

✓ Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups  
✓ Effect is consistent across all 8 post-switch weeks  
✓ Result holds controlling for staffing and visitor volume  
✓ Effect grew over time (suggesting learning benefits)

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **The new stock rotation procedure should be adopted cooperative-wide.**
>
> The evidence is clear and statistically robust: branches using the new procedure avoided a service score decline that affected all other branches. This represents a meaningful competitive advantage in service quality. With 425 branches already successfully implementing the procedure and no evidence of negative side effects, there is no analytical reason to delay full rollout.
>
> **Vote recommendation: Approve immediate expansion to all remaining branches.**
