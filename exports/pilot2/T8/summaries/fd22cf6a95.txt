# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure – Impact Assessment  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The data suggests a positive effect, but we cannot be confident the new procedure caused the improvement.** Branches that switched showed a 2.2-point higher service score than expected, but this result is not statistically significant (p = 0.16). The true effect could reasonably range from slightly negative to moderately positive. We recommend cautious optimism but not yet a full rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated Impact** | +2.16 service score points |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | -0.88 to +5.21 points |
| **Statistical Significance** | No (p = 0.16) |
| **Branches Analyzed** | 34 (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

**What the Data Shows:**

- Switching branches improved **more** than non-switching branches after week 15
- However, the difference is not large enough to rule out random variation
- The true effect could be zero, slightly negative, or as high as 5 points

---

## **Recommendations**

1. **Continue the pilot** – Gather more data before making a system-wide decision

2. **Extend the study period** – More post-switch weeks would increase statistical power

3. **Consider qualitative evidence** – Staff and patron feedback may provide additional insight

4. **Reassess in 3–6 months** – With more data, we can reach a more definitive conclusion

---

## **Conclusion**

While the point estimate is positive and aligns with our expectations, the evidence is not strong enough to confirm the new procedure caused the improvement. A measured approach—continuing to monitor and gather more data—is warranted before committing to full implementation.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**DO NOT PROCEED WITH FULL ROLLOUT AT THIS TIME.**

The analysis does not provide sufficient evidence to confirm the new procedure improves service scores. While the estimated effect is positive, it is not statistically distinguishable from zero. Approving a system-wide rollout based on this evidence would be premature. Continue the pilot, collect additional data, and return to the Board for a decision once the evidence is more definitive.

---

*Technical note: Analysis uses difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). The lack of statistical significance reflects limited statistical power rather than evidence of no effect.*
