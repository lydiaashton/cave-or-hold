# **Library Cooperative Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

**Date:** Analysis of weeks 6–22 service data  
**Prepared for:** Board of Directors

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence **suggests** the new procedure improved service scores by approximately **1.9 points** (about 4%), but this finding is **statistically marginal** (p=0.046) and the confidence interval [0.03 to 3.68] includes values near zero. The data are consistent with a modest positive effect, but uncertainty is substantial. We cannot rule out a small or negligible impact.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Analysis Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** served as controls
- Difference-in-differences methodology controls for branch characteristics and weekly variations

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|--------------------|--------------------|--------|
| Switching branches | 53.45 | 53.96 | +0.50 |
| Non-switching branches | 49.03 | 47.84 | −1.19 |
| **Estimated Net Effect** | | | **+1.86** |

**Statistical uncertainty:** 95% confidence interval is 0.03 to 3.68 points. The true effect could be negligible.

### **Important Caveats**

| Concern | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| **Marginal significance (p=0.046)** | Barely crosses conventional threshold; result could be due to chance |
| **Wide confidence interval** | Lower bound near zero means small effect cannot be ruled out |
| **Pre-switch gap varied** | Treatment-control difference ranged from 2.6 to 6.9 points before the switch |
| **Limited post-period data** | Only 8 weeks after implementation; sustainability unknown |
| **Control group declined** | Entire estimate depends on assumption this trend would have continued |

---

## **Recommendations & Next Steps**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Continue monitoring switching branches** | Gather more post-implementation data to reduce uncertainty |
| **2** | **Delay system-wide rollout decision** | Evidence is suggestive but not definitive; wait for stronger signal |
| **3** | **Track additional outcomes** | Consider circulation, patron satisfaction, or staff time metrics |
| **4** | **Document implementation experiences** | Qualitative feedback may clarify whether the change is worthwhile |
| **5** | **Re-analyze in 3 months** | With more data, statistical confidence will improve |

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **PROCEED WITH CAUTION**
>
> The new procedure shows **promising but unproven** benefits. The data lean positive—switching branches held steady while non-switching branches declined—but the statistical evidence is too weak to justify immediate system-wide rollout.
>
> **Recommended path:** Continue the pilot at current switching branches, gather 3–6 more months of data, and revisit the rollout decision once uncertainty is reduced. If the effect is real, it will become clearer with more observation. If it was chance, you'll avoid investing in a change that doesn't deliver.
>
> **Do not:** Expand to all branches based on this evidence alone.
> **Do:** Keep the new procedure where it's working and monitor closely.

---

*Technical note: Analysis uses difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects. P-value of 0.046 indicates marginal statistical significance. Confidence interval suggests true effect could range from negligible to moderately positive.*
