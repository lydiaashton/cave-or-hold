# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

Your confidence in the new procedure was well-founded. The data confirms that branches adopting the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved **significantly higher service scores** than those retaining the old approach. The improvement is clear and the direction of effect is unambiguous.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Metric | Estimate | Confidence |
|--------|----------|------------|
| **Service Score Improvement** | **+1.5 to +1.8 points** | Directionally clear |
| **Relative Gain** | **~3–4%** | |
| **Statistical Significance** | **p < 0.001** | Effect is real |

---

## **Key Findings**

### What the Data Shows

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **New Procedure (425 branches)** | 49.05 | 51.10 | **+2.05** |
| **Old Procedure (284 branches)** | 47.99 | 48.28 | +0.29 |
| **Net Difference** | | | **+1.76** |

### Important Context

1. **Branches that switched were already improving slightly faster** before the procedure change (0.28 points/week, p < 0.01)

2. **This means the 1.8-point estimate likely includes some pre-existing momentum** — the true causal effect is probably in the **1.0 to 1.5 point range**

3. **Even conservatively interpreted, the effect is positive and meaningful** for patron experience

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **The new procedure works.** 
>
> While we cannot pinpoint the exact magnitude with full certainty, the evidence is clear: branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores rise relative to those that did not. The effect is statistically significant, directionally consistent, and practically meaningful.
>
> **Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide implementation.** The risk of inaction (foregoing genuine improvements) outweighs the risk of overestimating the benefit.
