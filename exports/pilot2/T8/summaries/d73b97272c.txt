# **Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Date:** [Current Date]
**Subject:** Impact Assessment of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Evidence indicates the new procedure improved service scores by approximately 1.8 to 2.2 points (3-4%). The effect is statistically significant, though pre-existing differences between branches introduce some uncertainty. We recommend proceeding with system-wide implementation while continuing to monitor outcomes.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Primary Estimate** | +1.82 points (95% CI: 1.26 to 2.38; p < 0.001) |
| **Matched Comparison** | +2.19 points when comparing similar branches |
| **Branches Studied** | 425 switched to new procedure; 284 retained old procedure |
| **Effect Timeline** | Minimal impact weeks 1-3; clear improvements weeks 4-8 |
| **Robustness** | Results hold when controlling for staffing and visitor volume |

### Important Methodological Note

The analysis has one limitation: branches that switched to the new procedure were performing slightly worse than non-switching branches in the weeks before the change. This means:

- **The true effect could be smaller** if some improvement was due to natural recovery
- **The true effect could be larger** because our main method may understate gains for initially lower-performing branches
- **Our matched analysis** (comparing branches with similar pre-switch scores) suggests the larger estimate (~2.2 points) may be more accurate

Despite this uncertainty, the evidence strongly favors a positive effect:
- The direction of change aligns with the intervention timing
- Effects persisted and grew over the 8-week post-switch period
- Multiple analytical approaches point to improvement

## **Methodology Summary**

- **Primary Approach:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects
- **Secondary Approach:** Matched comparison of branches with similar pre-switch performance
- **Data:** 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches (weeks 6-22)
- **Switch Date:** Week 15
- **Limitation:** Pre-existing performance differences between treatment and control groups
