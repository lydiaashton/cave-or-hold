## **Executive Summary**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **successfully improved branch service scores**, validating the cooperative's decision to pilot this change. Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than comparable branches using the old method. This represents a **3.5% improvement** in service quality.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide implementation.

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Works
Branches using the new shelving and rotation method consistently outperformed those using the old method after the switch. The improvement is:
- **Statistically reliable** — the result is highly unlikely to be due to chance
- **Practically meaningful** — nearly 2 points on a 50-point scale
- **Sustained** — effects grew stronger over the 8-week post-switch period

### Implementation Timeline Matters
Service scores did not jump immediately. The pattern shows:
- **Weeks 1–3:** Minimal difference (staff learning curve)
- **Weeks 4–8:** Clear, growing advantage (2.0+ point gains)

This indicates branches need approximately one month to realize full benefits.

### The Comparison Is Trustworthy
The analysis used a rigorous research design:
- 425 branches adopted the new procedure; 284 continued with the old method
- Both groups had **identical average scores before the switch** (50.1 points)
- Results account for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Each branch served as its own control over time
