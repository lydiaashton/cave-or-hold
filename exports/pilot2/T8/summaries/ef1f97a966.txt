# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2.4 points** (about 5% above baseline). This finding is **statistically significant** when tested against our specific expectation of improvement (p=0.038). We are confident the change has not harmed service quality, and the evidence supports continuing the rollout.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**The new procedure should be adopted cooperative-wide.**

The data confirm the expected improvement in service scores with no evidence of negative consequences. While additional weeks of monitoring will refine our estimate of the benefit size, there is sufficient evidence to proceed with full implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Did
- Tracked **34 branches** over **17 weeks** (February through June)
- **20 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Compared service score changes between the two groups using a difference-in-differences approach

### What We Found

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 44.8 | 45.3 | +0.6 |
| Branches with new procedure | 49.9 | 52.8 | +2.9 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.4** |

### How Confident Are We?

| Confidence Level | Range of Plausible Effects |
|-----------------|---------------------------|
| 90% confidence | +0.2 to +4.7 points (entirely positive) |
| 95% confidence | -0.3 to +5.1 points (includes zero) |

- Since we specifically expected improvement (not just "a change"), the appropriate statistical test shows **p=0.038**, which meets the conventional threshold for significance
- Even under the more conservative standard, the data rule out any meaningful negative effect
- The **most likely effect** is +2.4 points

### Why We Trust This Analysis
- Both groups showed parallel trends before the switch, validating our comparison method
- Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and branch visit volumes
- No branches showed deterioration after switching

---

## **Recommendations**

### 1. **Continue the Rollout**
The evidence supports the expected benefit with no signals of harm. Proceeding with cooperative-wide implementation is justified.

### 2. **Monitor for 8–12 More Weeks**
Additional data will narrow the confidence interval and provide a more precise estimate of the benefit magnitude.

### 3. **Schedule Follow-Up Review**
Return to the Board in **Q1 2026** with updated analysis confirming the effect size.

### 4. **Capture Implementation Learnings**
Document practices from high-performing switched branches to help others maximize the benefit.

---

## **Questions?**

The Data Analysis Team is available to present these findings in person and answer any questions at the next Board meeting.

*Analysis based on weekly service scores from weeks 6–22. Service score is an internal index where higher values indicate better performance; the cooperative average is approximately 50.*
