# **Board Memorandum (Final)**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We cannot yet determine whether the new procedure improved service scores.** The observed effect is positive (+1.7 points) and aligns with expectations, but the data is too limited to distinguish this from random variation. The true effect could reasonably be zero, modestly beneficial, or slightly negative.

---

## **What the Data Shows**

| Finding | What It Means |
|---------|---------------|
| **Point estimate: +1.7 points** | On average, switching branches improved relative to non-switching branches |
| **95% CI: [−1.6, +5.0]** | The true effect could plausibly be anywhere in this range |
| **P-value: 0.31** | Not statistically significant; cannot reject "no effect" |
| **Parallel trends: Passed** | The comparison between groups is valid |

---

## **Why the Uncertainty?**

With 34 branches and only 8 weeks of post-switch data, our study can only reliably detect effects larger than **~4.7 points**. The observed effect of 1.7 points is smaller than this threshold.

**This is not evidence the procedure failed.** It is evidence we need more data to answer the question confidently.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **Continue data collection** (8–12 more weeks) | Would reduce uncertainty by ~40% |
| **Do not halt rollout** | No evidence of harm; potential benefit remains plausible |
| **Do not declare success yet** | Evidence is insufficient to confirm the expected improvement |
| **Re-evaluate in Q3 2026** | With additional data, we can reach a definitive conclusion |

---

## **Bottom Line for Decision-Makers**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did we see improvement? | Yes, but small (+1.7 points) |
| Can we confirm it's real? | **No**—data is inconclusive |
| Should we stop the rollout? | No—no evidence of harm |
| Should we claim success? | **No**—evidence is insufficient |
| What do we need? | More time and data |

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **INCONCLUSIVE — CONTINUE MONITORING**
>
> The evaluation neither confirms nor refutes the expected benefit. The procedure shows a positive direction consistent with expectations, but the evidence is insufficient to conclude the new procedure caused a meaningful improvement in service scores. The board should **continue the rollout while extending data collection** and **refrain from public claims of success** until additional evidence is gathered.

---

**The procedure may be working as expected, but the current data cannot confirm this. Continued evaluation is essential before drawing conclusions.**
