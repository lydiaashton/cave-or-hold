# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Impact Assessment of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows a **positive direction of effect (+1.8 points)** but the result is **not statistically significant**. The data cannot confirm whether the procedure improved service scores or had no effect. The study lacks sufficient power to detect modest improvements—only effects larger than ~4 points would be reliably detected with current data.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores average around 50 points (higher is better)

### **Results Summary**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 48.1 | 46.6 | **-1.5** |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 54.4 | 54.6 | **+0.3** |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.8** |

### **Statistical Assessment**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Point estimate | +1.8 points |
| 95% confidence interval | -0.8 to +4.4 points |
| p-value | 0.17 |
| Statistical significance | Not significant |

### **Critical Observation**
Treatment branches **maintained their scores** while control branches **declined** after week 15. The new procedure may have **prevented deterioration** rather than driving active improvement.

### **Study Limitation**
The analysis is **underpowered**. With current sample size and variability, only effects larger than ~4 points would be detected with confidence. A real but modest improvement could exist undetected.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

**Proceed with the new procedure, but continue monitoring.**

**Rationale:**
1. **No evidence of harm** — The effect is positive, not negative
2. **Plausible benefit** — A 1.8-point gain is meaningful if real (~3.6% improvement)
3. **Low reversal cost** — Rolling back the procedure would incur operational disruption
4. **Uncertainty is manageable** — Extending the evaluation period will provide clearer answers

**Decision confidence:** Moderate. The evidence does not prove the procedure works, but it supports continuing while gathering more data.

---

## **RECOMMENDATIONS / NEXT STEPS**

1. **Continue the new procedure** across all switched branches.

2. **Extend data collection** by 8–12 weeks to improve statistical power.

3. **Investigate the control group decline** — Understanding why scores dropped in branches keeping the old procedure is essential.

4. **Gather qualitative feedback** from staff and patrons on workflow efficiency and satisfaction.

5. **Schedule follow-up review** in Q1 2026 with extended data for a more definitive assessment.
