**We cannot confidently conclude the new procedure caused the improvement.** While branches using the new procedure did achieve higher service scores (+1.8 points), these branches were already improving faster than others *before* the switch. ### What the Data Shows
| Metric | Result |

### Why This Matters
The standard analysis method (difference-in-differences) assumes treatment and control branches would have followed similar trends without the intervention. **This assumption is not met.** Treatment branches were already on a stronger upward trajectory before adopting the new procedure.
