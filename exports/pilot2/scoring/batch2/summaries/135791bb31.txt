# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)  
**Branches Studied:** 34 (20 switched to new procedure, 14 maintained old procedure)

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The data is **inconclusive**. The new procedure is associated with a 2.3-point service score improvement, but this effect **cannot be distinguished from random variation** (p = 0.12). We recommend **continuing the pilot** while collecting more data before making expansion decisions.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

> **Cannot approve system-wide expansion at this time.**
>
> The evidence is **suggestive but not sufficient** for a rollout decision. The observed improvement is consistent with the hypothesis that the new procedure helps, but the study lacks the statistical power to confirm this. Proceeding with expansion would be a **bet rather than a data-driven decision**.
>
> **Path forward:** Extend the pilot by 12 weeks. If the effect persists and strengthens, revisit expansion with greater confidence.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Service Score Changes

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched Branches** (20) | 49.9 | 52.8 | **+2.9** |
| **Non-Switched Branches** (14) | 44.8 | 45.3 | **+0.6** |
| **Net Effect** | — | — | **+2.3** |

### What the Data Shows

- **Point estimate:** +2.3 points (4.6% improvement) — in the expected direction
- **Statistical significance:** Not significant at conventional levels (p = 0.12)
- **Confidence interval:** -0.5 to +5.1 points — true effect could be zero or negative
- **Branch consistency:** 14 of 20 switched branches outperformed the average control branch

### Why the Uncertainty?

- **Small sample:** Only 20 treatment branches and 14 control branches
- **High variability:** Service scores fluctuate week-to-week (standard deviation ~10 points)
- **Short observation:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data
