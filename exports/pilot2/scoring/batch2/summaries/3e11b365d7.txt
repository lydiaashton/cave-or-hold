## **Key Findings**

### What the Numbers Show

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | +2.15 points | Point estimate is positive |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.8 to +5.1 points | True effect could be zero or negative |
| P-value | 0.16 | **Not statistically significant** (threshold is 0.05) |

**In plain terms:** If the new procedure actually had no effect, we would still observe a difference this large (or larger) about 16% of the time due to random variation. This is too common to claim a real effect.

### Week-by-Week Patterns

Three post-switch weeks showed statistically significant improvements. However, one **pre-switch week** also showed a significant difference, and with 15 weeks of comparisons, some significant results are expected by chance alone. This pattern does not provide strong evidence of a consistent treatment effect.

### Study Limitations

- **34 branches** with only **8 weeks of post-switch data**
- To detect a statistically significant effect, we would need an impact larger than **3.0 points**
- The study is **underpowered** to detect modest but meaningful improvements

### No Evidence of Harm

Importantly, there is **no evidence that the new procedure reduced service scores**. The lower bound of the confidence interval is -0.8 points, suggesting any negative effect would be small.
