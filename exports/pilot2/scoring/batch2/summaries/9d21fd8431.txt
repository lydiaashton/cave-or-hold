# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure protected service quality during a period of system-wide decline.** Branches using the new procedure maintained their scores (essentially flat), while branches using the old procedure declined by 1.9 points. The net benefit is **+1.9 points relative to the control group** (statistically significant, p < 0.001).

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

**✅ PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT — WITH CONDITIONS**

The new procedure delivers a **real, measurable benefit** and should be adopted cooperative-wide. However, the board should understand:

1. **The benefit is defensive, not offensive.** The procedure shields branches from service quality decline; it does not inherently raise scores above baseline.

2. **The value depends on context.** If the cooperative faces ongoing operational pressures (seasonal stress, staffing constraints, etc.), this procedure becomes more valuable. In stable periods, the measurable benefit may be smaller.

3. **The effect strengthens with experience.** Branches showed larger protective effects in later weeks, suggesting training and familiarity matter.

**Recommendation:** Approve full rollout, but pair implementation with (a) investigation of what caused the system-wide decline after week 14, and (b) robust training and support for adopting branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **What We Tested**
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure (starting week 15)
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Service scores average ~50 points; higher scores indicate better service

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 50.36 | 48.50 | **−1.86** |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.92 | 51.00 | **+0.08** |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.94** |

### **Statistical Confidence**

- **95% confidence interval:** 1.5 to 2.3 points
- **p-value:** < 0.001 (highly reliable)
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (valid comparison)

### **Effect Over Time**

| Weeks After Switch | Service Score Advantage |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Week 15–16 | +1.6 to +1.9 points |
| Week 17–19 | +2.3 to +2.7 points |
| Week 20–22 | +2.7 to +3.5 points |
