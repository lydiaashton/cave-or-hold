# **Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22  
**Prepared for:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure is associated with **higher service scores of approximately 1.5 to 2 points** (3–4% improvement). The effect is statistically significant and grows over time. However, pre-existing differences between branches that switched versus those that didn't mean we cannot isolate the exact causal impact with full confidence. The evidence supports continuing the rollout, with ongoing monitoring.
