# **BOARD MEMORANDUM (FINAL)**

## **Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks of data)  
**Scope:** 709 branches (425 switched; 284 retained old procedure)

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### ⚠️ **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE FOR ROLLOUT**

**The data do not support a system-wide expansion of the new procedure at this time.** While switched branches achieved higher service scores, the analysis cannot distinguish whether this resulted from the procedure itself or from pre-existing differences between branches that chose to switch and those that did not. A randomized evaluation is required before committing resources to a full rollout.

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**We cannot confidently conclude the new procedure caused the improvement.** Branches that switched were already outperforming control branches before the change, and the gap continued widening at the same rate afterward. The apparent 2.1-point gain likely reflects selection bias, not causal impact. **We recommend a randomized pilot before any system-wide expansion.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. Parallel Trends Assumption Violated

| Week | Control Avg | Switched Avg | Gap |
|------|-------------|--------------|-----|
| 6 | 49.56 | 48.90 | -0.66 |
| 10 | 52.42 | 53.22 | +0.80 |
| 14 (last pre-switch) | 48.83 | 50.31 | +1.47 |
| 15 (first post-switch) | 48.50 | 50.83 | +2.33 |
| 22 | 51.90 | 55.60 | +3.71 |

The gap was already growing by **0.25 points/week before the switch**. After the switch, it grew at **0.22 points/week**—virtually unchanged.

### 2. Standard DiD Estimate Is Likely Biased

The conventional difference-in-differences estimate of 2.1 points assumes parallel pre-trends. Since this assumption fails, the estimate likely captures selection bias (better-performing or more motivated branches chose to switch) rather than causal impact.

### 3. What We Can Say

- Switched branches did achieve higher scores after week 15
- However, they were already on an upward trajectory relative to controls
- The procedure *may* have helped, but we cannot isolate its effect from pre-existing differences

### 4. Control Branches Declined Slightly

Branches keeping the old procedure saw scores drop ~1 point after week 15. This warrants investigation regardless of the procedure question.
