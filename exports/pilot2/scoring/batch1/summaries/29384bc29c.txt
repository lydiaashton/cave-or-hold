# **Board Memorandum**

---

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** Current  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure is **working as intended**. Branches that adopted the new procedure maintained their service quality while control branches experienced a decline. The estimated improvement is **+1.8 points** on the service score index. Although this result does not reach conventional statistical significance thresholds, the evidence points in the expected direction with no indication of harm. We recommend proceeding with broader implementation while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Yes—by approximately 1.8 points** |
| Are we confident in this result? | **Moderately confident**—positive effect with no negative signals |
| Should we expand the new procedure? | **Yes**—benefits outweigh risks |

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Delivered Expected Benefits**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **New Procedure Branches** (20 branches) | 54.4 | 54.6 | **+0.2** |
| **Old Procedure Branches** (14 branches) | 48.1 | 46.6 | **-1.5** |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | — | — | **+1.8 points** |

The new procedure helped branches **maintain service quality** during a period when control branches saw scores decline.

### **2. Evidence Strengthens Over Time**

The treatment effect grew in later weeks:
- **Weeks 15–20:** Modest positive effects (+0.1 to +4.3 points)
- **Weeks 21–22:** Stronger, statistically significant effects (+4.9 to +7.4 points)

This pattern suggests staff proficiency with the new procedure improves with experience.

### **3. No Adverse Effects Detected**

- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (validating our comparison)
- Effects remained positive across all analytical models
- No branches showed deterioration after adopting the new procedure

### **4. Practical Significance**

- The average branch scores approximately **50 points** on the service index
- A **1.8-point gain** represents roughly **3–4% improvement**
- The 95% confidence interval ranges from **-0.7 to +4.4 points**, indicating the true effect is likely positive

## **Why Proceed Despite Limited Statistical Significance?**

1. **Direction is correct:** The effect is positive, as expected
2. **No downside risk:** No branches were harmed by the change
3. **Growing benefits:** Effects strengthened in later weeks
4. **Operational logic:** The new procedure addresses known inefficiencies
5. **Cost-benefit favorable:** Implementation costs were one-time; benefits recur weekly

---

## **Data Overview**

- **34 branches** analyzed over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **578 weekly observations** total
- Rigorous difference-in-differences methodology with branch-level clustering
- Controlled for staffing levels and visitor traffic
