# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new procedure showed **higher service scores** than those that kept the old procedure. The estimated improvement is **2.4 points** (about 5% above baseline). While this finding is encouraging and aligns with our expectations, the result is **not yet statistically conclusive**. We recommend continuing the rollout while collecting more data to confirm the benefit.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Did
- Tracked **34 branches** over **17 weeks** (February through June)
- **20 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Compared service score changes between the two groups

### What We Found

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 44.8 | 45.3 | +0.6 |
| Branches with new procedure | 49.9 | 52.8 | +2.9 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.4** |

### How Confident Are We?
- The data suggests a positive effect, but there is some uncertainty
- There is approximately a **93% probability** the effect is genuinely positive
- Traditional statistical standards require 95% confidence to declare "significance"
- **Bottom line:** The results are promising but not yet definitive

### Why We Trust This Analysis
- Both groups were trending similarly before the switch, validating our comparison
- The finding holds even after accounting for staffing levels and branch visit volumes
- No branches showed negative effects from the change

---

## **Recommendations**

### 1. **Proceed with Rollout**
The evidence points in the expected direction with no signals of harm. Continuing the cooperative-wide implementation is reasonable.

### 2. **Extend Monitoring Period**
Collect **8–12 additional weeks** of data. This will increase our confidence and narrow the uncertainty range around the estimated benefit.

### 3. **Schedule Formal Review**
Return to the Board in **Q1 2026** with updated analysis. By then, we should have sufficient data to confirm whether the improvement is statistically significant.

### 4. **Document Best Practices**
Begin gathering qualitative feedback from branches that have switched. Identifying successful implementation patterns can help other branches maximize the benefit.

---

## **Questions?**

The Data Analysis Team is available to present this findings in person and answer any questions at the next Board meeting.

---

*Analysis based on weekly service scores from weeks 6–22. Service score is an internal index where higher values indicate better performance; the cooperative average is approximately 50.*
