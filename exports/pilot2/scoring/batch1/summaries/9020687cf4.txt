# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

---

## **Impact of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores**

**Date:** Analysis of Weeks 6-22  
**Prepared by:** Data Analytics Team

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores by 1.9 points (3.8%).** This is a statistically robust finding with high confidence. Importantly, the new procedure protected adopting branches from a broader decline in service quality that affected non-adopting branches during the same period.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout to the remaining 284 branches.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +1.94 points (95% CI: 1.35 to 2.54) |
| **Statistical Confidence** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Sample Size** | 709 branches (425 adopted, 284 did not) |
| **Baseline Score** | ~50 points (treatment group, pre-switch) |
| **Relative Improvement** | 3.8% increase |

**1. The New Procedure Worked**
Branches that switched to the new procedure maintained their service scores while control branches declined by nearly 2 points. The net benefit of switching was +1.94 points.

**2. Benefits Grew Over Time**
The effect was not immediate. Staff appeared to need 1-2 weeks to adapt. By weeks 17-22, the treatment effect was consistently positive and significant, reaching over 2 points by week 22.

**3. Analysis Is Methodologically Sound**
Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (p = 0.057), validating the comparison. Results hold after controlling for branch-specific factors, staffing levels, and visitor volume.

**4. Effect Is Direct, Not Indirect**
The procedure did not significantly change staffing or visit patterns. Improvements stem from the procedure itself—likely better organization and faster item retrieval.

**5. Risk of Not Switching**
Control branches experienced a 1.86-point decline post-week-14. Adopting the new procedure appears to shield branches from this downward trend.

### **APPENDIX: METHODOLOGY**

- **Approach:** Difference-in-Differences with branch fixed effects
- **Standard Errors:** Cluster-robust at branch level
- **Data:** 12,053 observations (weekly scores per branch)
- **Period:** Weeks 6-22 (9 weeks pre-switch, 8 weeks post-switch)
- **Switch Point:** Week 15
