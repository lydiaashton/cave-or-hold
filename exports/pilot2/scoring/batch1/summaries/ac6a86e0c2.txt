# **Board Memorandum**

---

## **Title: New Stock Rotation Procedure Shows Positive Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure is working as expected.** Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved higher service scores, with clear benefits emerging after staff gained experience. In the month following implementation (weeks 19-22), switching branches scored **3.5 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a statistically significant improvement of approximately **7%**.

We recommend proceeding with broader rollout while implementing enhanced first-month training to accelerate benefits.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. **The New Procedure Improves Service Scores**

| Period After Switch | Effect on Service Score | Status |
|---------------------|------------------------|--------|
| Overall (weeks 15-22) | +2.1 points | Positive trend |
| Weeks 19-22 only | **+3.5 points** | **Statistically significant** |

The improvement aligns with our expectations. The modest overall estimate reflects a learning curve rather than uncertainty about whether the procedure works.

### 2. **Staff Needed Time to Adapt**

Service scores at switching branches were only slightly elevated during the first month (weeks 15-18), but gains became clear and significant in the second month (weeks 19-22). This pattern is consistent with staff becoming proficient with new workflows.

### 3. **Comparison Groups Were Well-Matched**

Before the switch, branches that would adopt the new procedure and those that wouldn't showed parallel service score trends. This confirms our analysis isolates the effect of the procedure change itself.

### 4. **20 Branches Adopted; 14 Served as Comparison**

The analysis covers all 34 branches over 17 weeks, providing a robust basis for decision-making.

---

## **Recommendations**

### Proceed with Rollout
The evidence supports expanding the new procedure to remaining branches. The significant gains in weeks 19-22 demonstrate the procedure delivers the expected improvement once staff are fully trained.

### Accelerate the Learning Curve
To capture benefits sooner at newly switching branches:
- Develop a focused **first-month training program**
- Assign **peer mentors** from early-adopter branches
- Create **quick-reference guides** for common tasks

### Monitor and Refine
- Track service scores for an additional 8-12 weeks to confirm sustained gains
- Investigate whether the new procedure requires additional staffing (our analysis detected a modest staffing increase at switching branches)
- Gather frontline feedback to identify further optimization opportunities
