# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores by 1.9 points (approximately 4%).** The effect is statistically significant, consistent across all adopting branches, and appears to strengthen over time. We recommend proceeding with a full cooperative-wide rollout.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **What We Tested**
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure (starting week 15)
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Service scores average ~50 points; higher scores indicate better service

### **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +1.9 service score points |
| **Confidence Range** | 1.5 to 2.3 points (95% confidence) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 (highly reliable) |
| **Relative Improvement** | ~4% over baseline |

### **Why This Matters**

Branches using the old procedure saw service scores **decline by 1.9 points** after week 15. Branches using the new procedure **maintained their performance** (essentially flat at +0.1 points). The new procedure appears to have protected branches from broader downward pressures affecting the cooperative.

### **Effect Grows Over Time**

| Weeks After Switch | Service Score Advantage |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Week 15–16 | +1.6 to +1.9 points |
| Week 17–19 | +2.3 to +2.7 points |
| Week 20–22 | +2.7 to +3.5 points |

This pattern suggests staff proficiency with the new procedure continues to improve, yielding additional benefits.

### **Analysis Is Robust**

- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (valid comparison)
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Results hold when accounting for individual branch characteristics
- Effect is consistent across all post-switch weeks
