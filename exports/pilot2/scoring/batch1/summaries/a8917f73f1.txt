# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)  
**Branches Studied:** 34 (20 switched to new procedure, 14 maintained old procedure)

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2.3 points** (4.6% relative gain). The effect is positive and consistent but carries **moderate statistical confidence**. We recommend **continuing the new procedure** and collecting additional data to confirm the benefit before system-wide expansion.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Service Score Changes

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched Branches** (20) | 49.9 | 52.8 | **+2.9** |
| **Non-Switched Branches** (14) | 44.8 | 45.3 | **+0.6** |
| **Net Effect** | — | — | **+2.3** |

### What This Means

- Branches that adopted the new procedure improved **five times more** than those that did not
- The improvement is visible starting in Week 15 and persists through Week 22
- The effect holds after controlling for staffing levels and branch visit volumes
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, supporting the validity of the comparison

### Statistical Confidence

| Measure | Value | Interpretation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Effect Size | +2.3 points | Meaningful on a ~50-point scale |
| P-value | 0.08–0.12 | Suggestive but not definitive |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.5 to +5.1 points | Effect likely positive, magnitude uncertain |

---

## **RECOMMENDATIONS**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Continue new procedure at current branches** | Evidence points to improvement; no negative effects detected |
| **2** | **Collect 8–12 additional weeks of data** | Will increase statistical confidence and confirm persistence |
| **3** | **Prepare expansion plan contingent on confirmation** | If effect holds, roll out to remaining 14 branches |
| **4** | **Track implementation costs** | Ensure service gains justify any operational expenses |
