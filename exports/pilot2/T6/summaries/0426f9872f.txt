## **Bottom Line**

We **cannot confirm** that the new procedure improved service scores with statistical confidence. The estimated effect is positive (+1.7 points, or ~3%), but the result is not statistically significant (p=0.31), and the confidence interval ranges from -1.6 to +5.0 points. The study is underpowered to detect modest effects. However, **no negative impact was detected**, and all 8 post-switch weeks showed positive trends. We recommend **extending the evaluation period** before making a full rollout decision.
