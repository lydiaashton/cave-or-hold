# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** January 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive direction of effect** (+1.8 points on service scores), but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive** with the current data. The results are consistent with a modest improvement, but we cannot rule out no effect or a small negative effect. Given the positive signal, absence of harm, and growing effects in later weeks, we recommend **continuing the rollout while collecting more data** to reach a definitive conclusion.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches analyzed | 34 |
| Treatment branches (new procedure) | 20 |
| Control branches (old procedure) | 14 |
| Observation period | Weeks 6–22 |
| Switch date | Week 15 |

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment (new procedure) | 54.4 | 54.6 | **+0.2** |
| Control (old procedure) | 48.1 | 46.6 | **-1.5** |
| **Net Effect (DiD Estimate)** | | | **+1.8** |

### **Statistical Summary**
| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | +1.82 points | Best estimate from the data |
| P-value | 0.159 | Not statistically significant at 5% level |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.71 to +4.36 | True effect could be slightly negative to moderately positive |
| Probability effect is positive | ~92% | Based on one-tailed test |

### **What the Confidence Interval Means**
The data is compatible with several scenarios:
- **No meaningful effect** (0 points)
- **Small negative effect** (up to -0.7 points)
- **Modest positive effect** (up to +4.4 points)

Our best estimate remains +1.8 points, but we lack precision to confirm this with high confidence.

### **Supporting Evidence**
1. **Pre-trends validated:** No systematic differences between groups before the switch
2. **Effects strengthened over time:** Weeks 21–22 showed larger gains (+4.9 to +7.4 points, statistically significant)
3. **Control branches declined:** The new procedure may have protected treatment branches from a system-wide downturn

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **High** | Continue current rollout | Positive signal with no evidence of harm |
| **High** | Extend monitoring 8–12 weeks | More data will narrow the confidence interval |
| **Medium** | Gather staff feedback | Identify which aspects of the procedure are working |
| **Medium** | Plan phased expansion | Current evidence supports cautious expansion |
| **Low** | Re-evaluate in 6 months | Larger sample will provide definitive evidence |

---

## **Context for Decision-Making**

- The average branch service score is approximately **50 points**
- A 1.8-point gain represents roughly **3–4% improvement**
- The lack of statistical significance reflects **limited data** (17 weeks, 34 branches), not necessarily absence of effect
- Control branches declined after week 15, suggesting the new procedure may have **prevented deterioration** rather than driving improvement

---

## **Conclusion**

The evidence is **suggestive but not definitive**. The positive direction aligns with expectations, and there are no red flags. However, we cannot claim with high confidence that the procedure improves service scores based on this data alone. The prudent path is to continue implementation while gathering more evidence before making irreversible commitments.
