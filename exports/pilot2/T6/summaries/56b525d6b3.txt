## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure protected service quality during a period of system-wide decline.** Branches using the new procedure maintained their scores (essentially flat), while branches using the old procedure declined by 1.9 points. The net benefit is **+1.9 points relative to the control group** (statistically significant, p < 0.001). We recommend proceeding with rollout, with emphasis on the procedure's resilience benefits.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **What We Tested**
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure (starting week 15)
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Service scores average ~50 points; higher scores indicate better service

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |

| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.92 | 51.00 | **+0.08** |

### **Why This Matters**

Service scores across the cooperative showed considerable week-to-week volatility after week 14, suggesting external factors affected all branches (possibly seasonal patterns, staffing challenges, or operational pressures). The new procedure appears to have **buffered adopting branches against these pressures**.

### **Effect Is Consistent**
