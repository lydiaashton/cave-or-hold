# **Library Branch Shelving Procedure: Impact Analysis**

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2.1 points (4% improvement)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This difference is **statistically significant** (p < 0.05), supporting a recommendation to roll out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (Weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting Week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Analysis uses a difference-in-differences approach, comparing changes in switched branches against changes in non-switched branches

### **Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 50.8 | 53.9 | +3.2 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 52.2 | 53.2 | +1.1 |
| **Net Effect (DID)** | | | **+2.1** |

- **Estimated impact:** +2.1 points on the service score index (95% CI: 0.4 to 3.8)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.016
- **Relative improvement:** ~4% above baseline

### **Validity Checks**
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups showed similar trends (p = 0.93), supporting the validity of the comparison
- **No confounding changes:** Staffing levels did not differ systematically between groups after the switch
- **Effect persists with controls:** Result remains significant (p = 0.011) after accounting for staffing and visit volume

### **Implementation Pattern**
The effect was strongest in the first two weeks after implementation (Weeks 16–17), moderated in Weeks 18–20, and remained positive through Week 22. This suggests an initial boost that may stabilize over time.

## **Next Steps**

1. **Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new shelving procedure, given the statistically significant improvement in service scores

2. **Monitor post-rollout performance** to confirm the effect stabilizes and does not diminish over time

3. **Document best practices** from the 20 pilot branches to support training during rollout

4. **Consider a follow-up evaluation** after 3–6 months to assess long-term sustainability of the improvement
