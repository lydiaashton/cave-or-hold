## Summary for the Board

---

### **TITLE: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect** on service scores, but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive** at conventional levels. The estimated improvement is about **2.2 points** (approximately 4% of the average score), with a 95% confidence interval ranging from **-0.3 to +4.8 points**. While the direction is favorable, we cannot rule out the possibility of no effect.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Study Design** | 34 branches tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6-22); 20 branches adopted the new procedure at week 15, 14 branches continued with the old procedure |
| **Service Score Changes** | Control group: 52.45 → 51.90 (−0.55); Treatment group: 47.83 → 49.14 (+1.31) |
| **Estimated Treatment Effect** | **+2.23 points** (difference-in-differences with branch fixed effects) |
| **Statistical Significance** | P-value: 0.087; 95% CI: [−0.33, +4.78] — includes zero |
| **Effect Size** | ~4.5% of average score; 0.25 standard deviations (small-to-moderate) |
| **Parallel Trends** | Pre-switch trends show no significant differential pattern — supports validity of the analysis |
| **Weekly Pattern** | Effects vary week-to-week; some weeks show larger gains, others minimal change |

---

### **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Continue Monitoring** — Collect 4-8 additional weeks of data to increase statistical power and precision.

2. **Consider Pilot Expansion** — Given the positive (though not conclusive) results and low implementation risk, consider rolling out to a few more branches as an expanded pilot.

3. **Investigate Heterogeneity** — Analyze whether effects differ by branch characteristics (size, location, staffing) to identify where the procedure works best.

4. **Conduct Cost-Benefit Analysis** — Compare the estimated service score improvement against any additional costs of the new procedure.

5. **Decision Framework**:
   - *If risk-averse*: Wait for more data before full rollout
   - *If comfortable with uncertainty*: The positive point estimate and absence of negative effects support a cautious, phased rollout

---

### **RECOMMENDATION**

The data suggest a **modest positive effect** from the new procedure, but the evidence is not definitive. We recommend either **(a) continuing to monitor for additional weeks** to achieve greater statistical certainty, or **(b) proceeding with a phased rollout** while continuing to track outcomes. The board should weigh the potential 4% service score improvement against implementation costs and organizational capacity for change.
