# **Library Cooperative Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **2.1 points** (approximately **4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and robust across multiple analytical approaches. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure; **60 branches** continued with the old procedure
- The switch occurred at **week 15**, creating a natural experiment
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach, comparing changes in treated branches to changes in control branches

### **Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 51.27 | 49.65 | -1.62 |
| Treated (new procedure) | 50.52 | 51.03 | +0.51 |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+2.13** |

### **Statistical Significance**
- **Treatment effect: +2.13 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.01 to 3.25)
- **P-value: < 0.001** (highly statistically significant)
- Results remain consistent when controlling for staffing levels, visit volumes, and branch-specific characteristics

### **Practical Significance**
- Represents a **4.2% improvement** in service scores
- Effect size of **0.21 standard deviations** (small-to-moderate practical impact)
- The improvement is meaningful given that service scores average around 50 across the cooperative

### **Validity Checks**
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories with no statistically significant differences in trends (p = 0.20)
- **No pre-existing differences:** Treated and control branches had similar service score levels before the intervention (p = 0.58)
- **Consistent post-switch effects:** The positive effect appears in most weeks after the switch, with the strongest effects in weeks 15–17
