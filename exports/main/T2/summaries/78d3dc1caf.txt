**Yes, the new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **2.1 points** (approximately **4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. 4. **Robust Result**: The finding holds even when accounting for other factors like staffing levels and visit volumes, suggesting the improvement is attributable to the procedure change itself rather than external factors.

5. **Study Scope**: The analysis covers **150 branches** over **17 weeks** (weeks 6-22), with 90 branches adopting the new procedure and 60 branches serving as controls. The switch occurred in week 15.
