## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

### **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation procedure is associated with a **positive but statistically uncertain** improvement in service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure showed an average improvement of approximately **2 points** on the service score index compared to branches that kept the old procedure. However, this result is not statistically significant at conventional levels (p=0.12), meaning we cannot confidently rule out that the observed improvement occurred by chance. The 95% confidence interval ranges from **-0.5 to +4.4 points**, indicating the true effect could be negligible or meaningfully positive.

### **Key Findings**

**1. Study Design**
- 34 branches total: 20 adopted the new procedure (treatment), 14 kept the old procedure (control)
- Data covers 17 weeks (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- Analysis uses a difference-in-differences approach, comparing changes in service scores before and after the switch between the two groups
