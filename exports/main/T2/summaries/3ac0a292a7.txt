# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new stock rotation and shelving procedure improved service scores by approximately 1.8 points (3.5% above average). This effect is statistically significant and consistent across the 8-week post-implementation period. We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality**
- Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **+1.76 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure
- This represents a **3.5% improvement** relative to the system-wide average score of 50.7
- The effect is equivalent to **0.18 standard deviations**—a small but meaningful improvement in service quality

### **2. Statistically Robust Result**
- The improvement is **highly statistically significant** (p = 0.002)
- We are 95% confident the true effect lies between **+0.7 and +2.9 points**
- The result holds after controlling for staffing levels and branch visit volumes

### **3. Valid Comparison Between Groups**
- **90 branches** switched to the new procedure; **60 branches** kept the old procedure
- Before the switch (weeks 6–14), both groups had nearly identical service scores (49.85 vs. 50.34)
- This similarity before implementation supports the conclusion that the observed improvement is due to the new procedure, not pre-existing differences

### **4. Consistent Post-Switch Performance**
- After week 15, switched branches consistently outperformed non-switched branches
- Weekly differences ranged from +0.6 to +1.9 points in favor of switched branches
- The effect persisted throughout the 8-week observation period with no signs of diminishing
