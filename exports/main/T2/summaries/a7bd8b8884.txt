# **Board Summary: New Branch Procedure Evaluation**

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and rotation procedure is associated with a **2.4-point improvement in service scores** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. While this result is promising and consistent across most switched branches, the evidence is **not statistically conclusive** at conventional standards. We recommend a **cautious expansion** with continued monitoring.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Service Score Impact
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (n=20) | 47.8 | 49.5 | **+1.7** |
| Non-switched branches (n=14) | 48.3 | 47.6 | **-0.7** |
| **Net effect (Difference-in-Differences)** | | | **+2.4 points** |

### 2. Statistical Confidence
- The estimated effect is **2.4 points** (approximately 0.25 standard deviations—a small-to-moderate effect)
- **95% confidence interval: -0.5 to +5.3 points**
- **p-value: 0.08–0.10** (marginally significant; not meeting the conventional 0.05 threshold)
- Interpretation: We cannot rule out no effect, but the data suggest a likely positive impact

### 3. Consistency Across Branches
- **75% of switched branches** (15 of 20) showed improvement after the change
- **50% of non-switched branches** (7 of 14) showed improvement
- This pattern held in both the immediate weeks after the switch (weeks 15–17) and later weeks (18–22)

### 4. Important Context
- **Pre-existing trends**: Both groups had similar service scores before the switch, validating our comparison
- **Overall decline**: Service scores were trending downward across all branches over time (approximately 1 point per week). Switched branches **reversed this decline**, while non-switched branches continued to fall
- **Controlled analysis**: The effect persists (2.6 points) even after accounting for weekly staffing and visitor volume variations

1. **Proceed with phased rollout** to remaining branches, but treat this as a **continuing evaluation** rather than a final decision

   - Increase statistical power
   - Determine if the effect strengthens, weakens, or stabilizes over time

3. **Monitor implementation quality** at switched branches to identify best practices that could maximize the benefit

4. **Track secondary outcomes** beyond service scores, such as:

The data provide **encouraging but not definitive** evidence that the new procedure improves service scores.
