## Summary for the Board

**TITLE: New Shelving and Rotation Procedure Improves Branch Service Scores**

### BOTTOM LINE

The new procedure **significantly improves** service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores increase by approximately **2.4 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and robust across multiple analytical approaches.

**RECOMMENDATION: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

### KEY FINDINGS

| | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|---|---|---|---|

**Study Design:**
- 709 total branches analyzed over 17 weeks (weeks 6-22)
- Switch occurred at week 15
- Natural experiment with treatment (switched) and control (non-switched) groups

**Statistical Confidence:**
- 95% confidence interval: 1.78 to 2.98 points
- Results hold when controlling for branch characteristics, week effects, staffing levels, and visitor volumes
- The gap between groups widened consistently after the switch (from 1.4 to 3.8 points average)
