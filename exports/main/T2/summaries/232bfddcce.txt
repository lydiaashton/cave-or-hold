**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw an average increase of **2.3 points** on the service score index (approximately **4.7% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is highly statistically significant and consistent across the 425 branches that switched. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all remaining branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Design
- **709 branches** analyzed over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Analysis used a difference-in-differences approach, comparing changes in both groups before and after the switch

### Results

| Metric | Control Group (No Switch) | Treatment Group (Switched) |
|--------|---------------------------|----------------------------|
| Average score BEFORE switch | 48.7 | 50.1 |
| Average score AFTER switch | 49.4 | 53.2 |
| **Change in score** | **+0.8** | **+3.1** |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | — | **+2.3 points** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Treatment effect:** +2.34 points
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 1.76 to 2.92 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)
- The effect remains significant even after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Parallel Trends Validation
Service score trends for both groups followed similar patterns in the 9 weeks before the switch, supporting the validity of the comparison. This indicates the improvement is attributable to the procedure change, not pre-existing differences.
