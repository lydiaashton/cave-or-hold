# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a positive but statistically inconclusive effect on service scores.** While branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a modest improvement relative to those that did not (approximately +1.6 points on the service score index), this difference is not statistically distinguishable from zero at conventional confidence levels. **We cannot confidently conclude that the new procedure improved service quality based on the current data.** We recommend continuing to monitor the pilot branches and deferring a system-wide rollout decision until more evidence is available.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** participated in the evaluation (20 adopted the new procedure, 14 maintained the old procedure)
- Data collected weekly from **Week 6 through Week 22**
- The procedure switch occurred at **Week 15**
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach with branch and week fixed effects

### **Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment (switched) | 47.95 | 51.83 | +3.88 |
| Control (not switched) | 49.38 | 51.65 | +2.27 |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+1.62** |

### **Statistical Significance**
- **Estimated treatment effect:** +1.62 points
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.50 to +3.74 points
- **P-value:** 0.135 (not statistically significant at the 5% level)

### **Validity Check: Parallel Trends**
The difference-in-differences method requires that treatment and control branches would have followed similar trends absent the intervention. Our event study analysis confirms:
- **No evidence of differential pre-trends** (F-test p = 0.54)
- Pre-switch differences between groups were not systematically different from zero
- This supports the validity of our causal inference approach

---

## **Interpretation**

1. **Both groups improved after Week 15.** Service scores increased for both treatment and control branches, suggesting broader factors (seasonal patterns, system-wide initiatives, etc.) may have influenced scores during this period.

2. **The treatment group improved slightly more,** but the additional improvement (~1.6 points, or about 3% of the average score of 50) is within the range of normal variation.

3. **Statistical uncertainty is substantial.** The confidence interval indicates the true effect could plausibly be:
   - Slightly negative (up to -0.5 points)
   - Zero (no effect)
   - Modestly positive (up to +3.7 points)

4. **The analysis is well-powered for detecting moderate effects** but may lack precision for detecting small effects. With 578 observations across 34 branches, we have reasonable but not definitive evidence.

---

## **Next Steps**

### **Recommended Actions**

1. **Do not proceed with system-wide rollout at this time.** The evidence is insufficient to justify the costs and disruption of implementing the new procedure across all 34 branches.

2. **Continue monitoring pilot branches.** Collect additional weeks of data to:
   - Increase statistical precision
   - Assess whether effects persist, grow, or diminish over time
   - Identify any delayed or cumulative benefits

3. **Consider a phased expansion.** If operational constraints allow, gradually introduce the procedure to additional branches while maintaining a control group for comparison.

4. **Investigate heterogeneity.** Examine whether the procedure works better for certain types of branches (e.g., by size, location, or baseline performance) to identify where it may be most effective.

5. **Review implementation fidelity.** Verify that branches adopted the new procedure as intended; inconsistent implementation could mask true effects.

### **Timeline Recommendation**
- **Continue data collection** for at least 8-12 additional weeks
- **Re-evaluate** with expanded dataset in Q1 2027
- **Make rollout decision** based on updated analysis
