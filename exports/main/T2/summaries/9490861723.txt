## **LIBRARY BRANCH PROCEDURE CHANGE ANALYSIS**
### Summary for the Board of Directors

---

### **TITLE:** Impact of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure is associated with a **statistically significant IMPROVEMENT** in service scores of approximately **2.0 points** (on a scale where the average branch scores about 50). This represents roughly a **4% improvement**. However, we note important caveats about the analysis below.

We **RECOMMEND PROCEEDING WITH CAUTION** on systemwide rollout, with continued monitoring to confirm these results hold across all branches.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

1. **TREATMENT EFFECT ESTIMATE**
   - Branches that adopted the new procedure showed service score improvements of **+1.98 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure
   - 95% Confidence Interval: [+1.58, +2.38] points
   - Statistical significance: p < 0.001 (highly significant)
   - This estimate controls for branch-specific characteristics and time trends

2. **SCOPE OF ANALYSIS**
   - 709 total branches analyzed (425 switched, 284 did not)
   - 12,053 weekly observations from weeks 6-22
   - Switch occurred at week 15; analysis covers 9 weeks before and 8 weeks after

3. **SERVICE SCORE CHANGES**
   - Switched branches: 52.1 (before) → 53.8 (after) = +1.6 points
   - Control branches: 52.3 (before) → 51.9 (after) = -0.3 points
   - Net improvement attributable to new procedure: ~2.0 points

4. **IMPORTANT CAVEATS**
   - Pre-treatment trends were not perfectly parallel: branches that switched were already showing improving scores relative to control branches before the procedure change
   - This suggests some branches may have been selected for the new procedure based on other factors, or were already on an improvement trajectory
   - The estimated effect may be partially inflated by these pre-existing trends
   - The effect appears to strengthen over time, with larger gains in weeks 20-22

5. **EFFECT TIMING (Event Study Results)**
   - Weeks 1-4 after switch: Small, statistically insignificant effects
   - Weeks 5-7 after switch: Effects of +1.2 to +1.3 points, statistically significant
   - This suggests the procedure may take several weeks to show full benefits

---

### **NEXT STEPS**

1. **PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT** but implement in phases rather than all at once
   - This allows for continued monitoring and adjustment

2. **ESTABLISH ONGOING MONITORING**
   - Track service scores for all branches after they switch
   - Compare actual performance to the ~2 point improvement expected
   - Set up alerts if branches show no improvement after 4-5 weeks

3. **INVESTIGATE MECHANISMS**
   - Why does the effect take several weeks to appear?
   - Are there branch characteristics that predict better outcomes?
   - Consider qualitative feedback from staff about the new procedure

4. **DOCUMENT LEARNINGS**
   - Create a playbook for branches transitioning to the new procedure
   - Identify any branches that struggle and provide additional support

5. **RE-EVALUATE IN 6 MONTHS**
   - Assess whether improvements are sustained over longer time periods
   - Determine if the procedure requires refinement
