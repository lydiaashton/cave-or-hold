# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure is associated with a **2.1 point improvement in service scores** (approximately 4% increase). While the overall effect is marginally significant (p=0.10), the evidence suggests the benefit grows over time, reaching **2.8 points in the later weeks** (statistically significant at p=0.035). We recommend proceeding with a full rollout, with continued monitoring to confirm the effect strengthens as branches gain experience with the new procedure.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Service Score Improvement
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (treatment) | 53.63 | 56.88 | +3.25 |
| Non-switched branches (control) | 49.67 | 50.89 | +1.22 |
| **Net effect (Difference-in-Differences)** | | | **+2.03** |

The switched branches improved by 3.25 points, but the control branches also improved by 1.22 points (possibly due to seasonal factors or system-wide changes). After accounting for this, the **net attributable effect of the new procedure is approximately 2.1 points**.

### 2. Effect Strengthens Over Time
The benefit was not immediate but grew as branches gained experience:
- **Weeks 15-18 (early period)**: +1.2 points (not statistically significant)
- **Weeks 19-22 (later period)**: +2.8 points (statistically significant, p=0.035)

This pattern suggests a learning curve—branches may need several weeks to fully realize the benefits of the new procedure.

### 3. Analysis Validity
- **Parallel trends confirmed**: Before the switch, both groups showed similar trends in service scores, supporting the validity of the comparison.
- **Controls included**: The results hold even after accounting for weekly staffing and visitor volume variations.
- **34 branches analyzed**: 20 branches switched to the new procedure; 14 branches served as the control group.

### 4. Practical Significance
A 2-3 point improvement on a ~50 point scale represents a **4-6% gain in service quality**. For a library cooperative, this translates to meaningfully better patron experience across all switched branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve full rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches, with implementation support and training.

2. **Allow 4-6 weeks per branch** for the new procedure to show its full effect before evaluating individual branch performance.

3. **Continue monitoring** service scores for the next 8-12 weeks to:
   - Confirm the effect persists and potentially strengthens
   - Identify any branches that may need additional support during transition

4. **Document best practices** from early-adopter branches to accelerate the learning curve for newly switching branches.

5. **Re-evaluate in Q2** with a full quarter of post-rollout data to confirm the long-term impact and calculate ROI.

---

*Analysis based on weekly service scores from 34 branches, weeks 6-22. The switch occurred at week 15. Statistical models use cluster-robust standard errors at the branch level.*
