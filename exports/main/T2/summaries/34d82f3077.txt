# **Library Cooperative Shelving Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

**Yes, the new shelving and stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **1.9 points** (about **4%**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. ## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Improved Service Scores
- **Treatment effect: +1.92 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.38 to 2.47)
- This represents roughly a **4% improvement** relative to baseline scores (~50)
- The result is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)

### 2. The Effect Grows Over Time
The event study analysis shows the benefit of the new procedure increased steadily after implementation:

| Period | Average Effect |
|--------|---------------|
| Week 15 (immediately after) | +1.6 points |
| Weeks 16-18 | +2.2 points |
| Weeks 19-22 | +3.2 points |

This suggests branches may be getting better at implementing the procedure, or the benefits accumulate as the new system settles in.

### 3. Results Are Credible
- **Parallel trends check passed**: Before the switch, treatment and control branches had similar trends (difference not statistically significant, p=0.24)
- **Large sample**: 709 branches, 12,053 weekly observations
- **Strong design**: 40% of branches kept the old procedure, providing a valid comparison group

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
