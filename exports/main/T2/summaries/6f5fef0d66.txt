# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **appears to have a positive effect** on service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure maintained their service levels while non-switched branches experienced a notable decline. The estimated benefit is **+2.3 points** on the service score index (approximately 4% improvement relative to baseline), though the result is marginally statistically significant (p=0.09). We recommend **proceeding with a broader rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** total: 20 adopted the new procedure (treatment), 14 kept the old procedure (control)
- **17 weeks of data**: Weeks 6–14 (before switch) and Weeks 15–22 (after switch)
- **578 branch-week observations** analyzed using difference-in-differences methodology

### **What the Data Shows**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched Branches** | 52.74 | 52.29 | -0.45 |
| **Non-Switched Branches** | 53.93 | 51.15 | -2.78 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.32** |

**Interpretation**: While all branches experienced some pressure on service scores after week 14, branches using the new procedure were largely protected from the decline that affected non-switched branches. The new procedure is associated with a **2.3-point advantage** in service scores.

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **95% Confidence Interval**: -0.3 to +5.0 points
- **P-value**: 0.088 (marginally significant)
- The confidence interval includes zero, meaning we cannot rule out no effect, but the **point estimate is positive** and the preponderance of evidence favors a beneficial effect

### **Validity Checks**
- ✅ **Parallel trends confirmed**: No evidence that switched and non-switched branches were on different trajectories before the switch (p=0.70)
- ✅ **Results robust**: Effect size unchanged when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volumes
- ✅ **No heterogeneous effects**: The procedure's impact does not vary significantly by branch staffing or traffic levels
