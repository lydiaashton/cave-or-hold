# Library Branch Stock Rotation Procedure: Impact Analysis

## Bottom Line

**The new stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to the expectation that it would make no difference, branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **1.9 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.0 to 2.8 points) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **3.9% improvement** over the average service score. The board should consider rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Study Design
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6-22)
- **102 branches** adopted the new procedure (treatment group); **48 branches** kept the old procedure (control group)
- Switch occurred in **week 15**
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach, which compares how treatment and control groups changed before vs. after the switch

### 2. Main Result: Service Scores Improved
| Period | Control Group (Old Procedure) | Treatment Group (New Procedure) | Difference |
|--------|------------------------------|--------------------------------|------------|
| Before (weeks 6-14) | 47.27 | 48.02 | +0.76 |
| After (weeks 15-22) | 48.73 | 51.43 | +2.70 |
| **Change** | **+1.46** | **+3.41** | **+1.95** |

The **1.95-point improvement** is the estimated effect of the new procedure, after accounting for underlying trends that affected all branches.

### 3. Statistical Confidence
- The result is **highly statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- 95% confidence interval: **1.0 to 2.8 points**
- We can be confident the true effect is positive and meaningful

### 4. Validity Checks
- **Parallel trends confirmed**: Before the switch, treatment and control groups showed similar week-to-week patterns (no significant divergence, p = 0.56)
- **Robust to controls**: The result holds when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Branch-level differences accounted for**: Results remain significant even after controlling for inherent differences between individual branches

### 5. Important Observation
The improvement gap appears to be **narrowing over time** in the post-switch period. While treatment branches started ~3.3 points ahead in week 15, the gap declined to ~2.0 points by week 22. This could indicate:
- The initial boost may partially fade over time
- Control branches may be naturally improving
- Further monitoring is warranted
