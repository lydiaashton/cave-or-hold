## Bottom Line for the Board

The evidence suggests the new procedure **improves service scores by about 1.8 points** (roughly 3.6% of the average score), but this effect is **not statistically significant** at conventional levels. The true effect could range from slightly negative to moderately positive.

**Recommendation:** A phased rollout with continued monitoring is advisable rather than an immediate system-wide mandate. This allows the cooperative to gather more data while beginning to realize potential benefits.
