# **Analysis of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**Yes, the new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new stock rotation procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and represents about a **4% improvement** relative to the average branch score of 50. The analysis supports rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. **The New Procedure Improved Service Scores**

Using a difference-in-differences analysis (comparing branches that switched to those that didn't, before and after week 15), we estimate the new procedure increased service scores by:

| Model Specification | Effect Size | 95% Confidence Interval | P-value |
|---------------------|-------------|------------------------|---------|
| Basic DiD | +2.12 points | [1.43, 2.82] | < 0.001 |
| With Branch & Week Fixed Effects | +2.14 points | [1.60, 2.68] | < 0.001 |
| With Staffing & Visits Controls | +2.14 points | [1.60, 2.68] | < 0.001 |

The result is **robust** across all model specifications.

### 2. **The Effect Grew Over Time**

An event study analysis shows the improvement was not immediate but grew steadily after implementation:

- **Week 15 (first week after switch):** +2.0 points
- **Week 18:** +3.0 points  
- **Week 22 (final week observed):** +4.0 points

This pattern suggests branches needed time to fully adapt to the new procedure, and benefits accumulated with experience.

### 3. **Pre-Existing Trends Were Accounted For**

We examined whether branches that switched were already on different trajectories before the change:

- In the 9 weeks before the switch, the treatment and control groups showed **no statistically significant divergence** in most weeks
- There was a slight upward trend in the treatment group in weeks 12-13, but our models with week fixed effects account for common time trends
- The PanelOLS model with branch and week fixed effects isolates the causal effect of the procedure change

### 4. **Scale of the Impact**

- **Average service score:** ~51 points (baseline)
- **Estimated improvement:** +2.1 points (about 4% increase)
- **Number of branches affected:** 425 branches switched; 284 did not
- **Total observations:** 12,053 branch-week records
