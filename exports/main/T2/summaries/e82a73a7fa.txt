# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**Yes, the new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (about 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant and has grown over the 8 weeks since implementation. We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **709 branches** total: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 kept the old (control group)
- **17 weeks of data**: Weeks 6–14 (before switch), Weeks 15–22 (after switch)
- Analysis uses a difference-in-differences approach, comparing changes in switched branches to changes in non-switched branches

### Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.19 | 52.66 | +1.47 |
| Non-switched branches | 50.48 | 49.86 | -0.62 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.09** |

- **Estimated effect: +2.11 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.76 to 2.46)
- **Statistical significance: p < 0.001** (highly significant)
- **Practical significance: ~4% improvement** relative to the average service score of 51

### Effect Over Time
The benefit of the new procedure has **increased over time**:
- Weeks 1–2 after switch: No significant effect yet (adjustment period)
- Weeks 3–5: +1.3 to +1.5 points
- Weeks 6–8: +1.8 to +2.5 points

This pattern suggests branches needed a short learning period before realizing the full benefits.

### Robustness Checks
- Effect remains significant after controlling for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume
- Effect holds when accounting for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week variations
- Pre-switch trends in the two weeks immediately before implementation show no significant differences between groups, supporting the validity of the comparison

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve systemwide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 284 branches still using the old method

2. **Plan implementation support**:
   - Allow 2–3 weeks for staff training and adjustment
   - Expect full benefits to materialize after 4–6 weeks

3. **Monitor outcomes**:
   - Continue tracking service scores post-rollout
   - Watch for any branches that struggle with the transition and may need additional support

4. **Consider further analysis**:
   - Investigate whether the effect varies by branch size, location, or staffing level
   - Assess long-term sustainability of the improvement beyond the initial 8-week window

---

## **Caveats**

- The study covers only 8 weeks post-implementation; long-term effects remain to be seen
- Non-switched branches experienced a slight decline in scores after week 15, which may reflect external factors (seasonal patterns, budget changes, etc.) that the analysis accounts for but cannot fully explain
- Some branches showed pre-existing differences in early weeks, but these resolved before the switch occurred
