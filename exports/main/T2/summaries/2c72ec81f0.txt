# **Library Branch Stock Rotation Procedure: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new stock rotation and shelving procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to the expectation of no difference, branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **1.8 points** (approximately **3.7% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p=0.003) and robust across multiple analytical approaches.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality
- **Treatment effect:** +1.80 points on the service score index (95% CI: 0.61 to 2.99)
- **Statistical significance:** p=0.003 (highly unlikely to occur by chance)
- **Practical significance:** ~3.7% improvement relative to the average branch score of 48

### 2. Strong Causal Evidence
The analysis used a **difference-in-differences** design comparing:
- **90 branches** that switched to the new procedure (treatment group)
- **60 branches** that kept the old procedure (control group)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6-22), with the switch occurring at week 15

This design isolates the effect of the procedure change from other factors affecting all branches.

### 3. Parallel Trends Validated
Pre-switch data (weeks 6-14) shows **no significant difference** in trends between treatment and control branches (p=0.44). This confirms the two groups were on similar trajectories before the intervention, strengthening confidence in the causal interpretation.

### 4. Effect Persists After Switch
The event study analysis shows:
- **Pre-switch (weeks 6-13):** No meaningful differences between groups
- **Post-switch (weeks 15-22):** Consistently positive effects, with the strongest gains in weeks 15-16
- The effect remains significant even after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volumes

### 5. Effect Is Consistent
- Treated branches improved by an average of **2.91 points** from before to after
- Control branches improved by **1.11 points** (likely due to seasonal or system-wide factors)
- The **net effect attributable to the new procedure is 1.80 points**
