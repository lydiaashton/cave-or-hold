# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure is associated with a **statistically significant improvement in service scores of approximately 2 points** (95% CI: 1.4 to 2.5 points). This represents about a **4% improvement** relative to the average branch score. However, branches that adopted the new procedure were already showing stronger performance trends before the switch, so the true effect may be somewhat smaller than estimated.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with cautious optimism. The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure, but continued monitoring is essential to confirm the effect persists and to identify any branches that may not benefit.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measured Impact**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.94 points** on service score |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.41 to 2.48 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| Relative improvement | **3.9%** above average |
| Effect size | 0.19 standard deviations (small-to-moderate) |

### **2. Performance by Group and Period**
| Period | Non-Switched Branches | Switched Branches | Gap |
|--------|----------------------|-------------------|-----|
| **Pre-switch** (Weeks 6-14) | 48.85 | 50.75 | 1.91 pts |
| **Post-switch** (Weeks 15-22) | 48.27 | 52.14 | 3.87 pts |
| **Change** | -0.58 pts | +1.39 pts | **+1.96 pts** |

### **3. Important Caveat: Pre-Existing Trends**
The analysis detected a **significant pre-existing trend**: branches that switched were already improving faster than non-switched branches before the procedure change (0.29 points/week, p < 0.001). 

Looking at the weekly gap between groups:
- **Week 6:** 0.2 points
- **Week 14 (last pre-switch week):** 2.6 points
- **Week 22 (final week):** 4.9 points

This suggests the gap was already widening before the switch. The estimated 1.94-point effect may therefore be **partially inflated** by these pre-existing differences.

### **4. Effect Persists After Controls**
The positive effect remains statistically significant even after accounting for:
- **Staffing levels** (coefficient: 0.56, p < 0.001)
- **Visitor volume** (coefficient: 0.50, p < 0.001)

### **5. Effect Appears to Grow Over Time**
The treatment-control gap continued widening through the post-switch period:
- Weeks 15-16: ~2.8 points
- Weeks 17-18: ~3.6 points
- Weeks 19-22: ~4.5 points

This pattern is consistent with branches learning and optimizing the new procedure over time.

## **Analysis Details**

- **Data:** 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches
- **Treatment group:** 425 branches that switched to the new procedure
- **Control group:** 284 branches that kept the old procedure
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Analysis period:** Weeks 6-22 (switch occurred at Week 15)
