# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and rotation procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2.3 points** (on a scale where the average branch scores about 50). This represents a **4-5% improvement** over baseline performance. The evidence suggests the change had a positive effect, with 98.7% of statistical simulations showing improvement. We recommend proceeding with a full rollout, while continuing to monitor results.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6-22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15 (treatment group)
- **14 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Analysis uses a difference-in-differences approach, comparing changes in the treatment group to changes in the control group

### **Results**

| Period | Control Group (Old) | Treatment Group (New) | Gap |
|--------|---------------------|----------------------|-----|
| **Before Switch** (weeks 6-14) | 48.0 | 50.2 | 2.2 pts |
| **After Switch** (weeks 15-22) | 48.1 | 52.6 | 4.5 pts |
| **Change** | +0.1 | **+2.4** | **+2.3 pts** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Estimated effect:** +2.3 points (95% confidence interval: 0.3 to 4.4 points)
- **Probability of positive effect:** 98.7%
- **Parallel trends validated:** Treatment and control branches showed similar trends before the switch, supporting the validity of the comparison

### **What This Means**
- The new procedure is associated with a **meaningful improvement** in service quality
- Control branches showed virtually no change (+0.1 points), while treatment branches improved by +2.4 points
- The effect is **consistent across post-switch weeks**, with no evidence of the benefit fading over time
