# **Library Cooperative Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

## **Bottom Line**

**Yes, the new shelving and rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores increase by an estimated **+2.34 points** (95% CI: 1.76 to 2.92) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4.4% improvement** relative to the average score. The result is highly statistically significant (p < 0.0001), and the analysis meets all validity requirements for causal inference.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all remaining branches.
