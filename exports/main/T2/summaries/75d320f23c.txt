# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to initial expectations that the change would have no effect, branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (a 4.3% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically significant and robust across multiple analytical approaches.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores
- **Treatment effect:** +2.10 points on the service score index (95% confidence interval: 0.8 to 3.4 points)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.01 (less than 1% chance this result occurred by random variation)
- **Effect size:** 4.3% improvement relative to the baseline average score of 48.75

### 2. Strong Evidence from Multiple Analyses
The finding held consistently across five different statistical models:
| Model Specification | Treatment Effect | P-value |
|---------------------|------------------|---------|
| Simple comparison | +2.10 | 0.001 |
| With branch fixed effects | +2.10 | 0.002 |
| With branch + week fixed effects | +2.10 | 0.002 |
| With staffing/visits controls | +1.99 | 0.002 |
| Full model (all controls) | +2.02 | 0.002 |

### 3. Valid Comparison Between Groups
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch (weeks 6-14), treatment and control branches followed nearly identical trends (p = 0.9999), validating our comparison approach
- **Group sizes:** 90 branches adopted the new procedure; 60 branches kept the old procedure
- **Pre-switch scores were similar:** Treatment group averaged 48.64; Control group averaged 48.92

### 4. Branch-Level Impact
- **66.7%** of branches using the new procedure saw their scores improve
- Only **41.7%** of branches using the old procedure saw improvement
- Average branch change: **+1.76** (treatment) vs **-0.34** (control)

### 5. Visual Evidence
The attached chart shows service score trends for both groups. Before week 15, the lines track closely together. After the switch, the treatment group (green) clearly separates upward from the control group (blue).
