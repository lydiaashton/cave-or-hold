# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure is associated with a modest improvement in service scores (+1.9 points), but this effect is **not statistically significant** at conventional levels. The evidence does not strongly support a system-wide rollout at this time. We recommend continuing to monitor the pilot branches and gathering additional data before making a final decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** total: 20 adopted the new procedure (treatment group), 14 kept the old procedure (control group)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring in week 15
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach, comparing changes in service scores between the two groups before and after the switch

### **Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 53.0 | 57.9 | +4.9 |
| Non-switched branches | 48.5 | 51.5 | +3.1 |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+1.9** |

- Branches that switched improved by **1.9 points more** than branches that didn't switch
- This estimate has a **p-value of 0.18**, meaning there is an 18% chance this result could occur even if the new procedure had no real effect
- The effect is **not statistically significant** at the conventional 5% threshold

### **Validity Check**
- Pre-switch trends were **parallel** between the two groups (p=0.75), which supports the validity of our analysis
- Both groups showed improvement after week 15, suggesting broader factors (e.g., seasonal effects) may have influenced scores

## **Next Steps**

1. **Continue monitoring** the 20 pilot branches to see if the positive trend persists or strengthens over time

2. **Consider expanding the pilot** to more branches to increase statistical power and obtain a more precise estimate of the effect

3. **Evaluate additional outcomes** beyond service scores, such as:
   - Staff time and labor costs
   - Patron satisfaction surveys
   - Circulation or visit patterns

4. **Revisit the decision** after 3–6 months of additional data, or after expanding the pilot to more branches

---

## **Technical Notes**

- Analysis controls for branch-specific characteristics using fixed effects
- Standard errors are clustered at the branch level to account for repeated weekly observations
- Service scores average around 50 across the system; a 1.9-point change represents approximately a 4% improvement
