# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores.** Contrary to the expectation that the change would make no difference, branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **1.85 points** (approximately 3.6%) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and consistent across the post-switch period. **We recommend proceeding with a full rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **150 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure (treatment group); **60 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- The switch occurred at **week 15**, providing 9 weeks of before-data and 8 weeks of after-data
- This "difference-in-differences" design isolates the effect of the new procedure from other factors affecting all branches

### Results

| Metric | Treatment Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| Average score before switch | 50.74 | 50.04 |
| Average score after switch | 53.94 | 51.39 |
| **Change** | **+3.20** | **+1.35** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +1.85 points** (95% confidence interval: 0.97 to 2.72)

### Statistical Significance
- **p-value < 0.001** — the result is highly unlikely to be due to chance
- The improvement is visible in every post-switch week (weeks 15–22)
- Pre-switch trends were similar between groups, supporting the validity of the comparison

### Effect Size Context
- Average service score across all branches: ~51.6
- A 1.85-point increase represents a **3.6% improvement**
- For a typical branch scoring 50, the new procedure would raise the score to approximately **51.9**

### Additional Observations
- The improvement persisted after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- No evidence that the new procedure negatively affected other operational measures

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve systemwide rollout** of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure to all branches

2. **Develop implementation timeline** — consider phasing the rollout to manage training and transition logistics

3. **Monitor service scores** during rollout to confirm the effect holds at scale

4. **Document the new procedure** — capture best practices from the 90 early-adopter branches to support training

5. **Continue tracking** staffing and visits indices to ensure no unintended consequences emerge
