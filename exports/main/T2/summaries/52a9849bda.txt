| Period | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches | Gap |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|-----|

Notably, non-switched branches experienced a decline in service scores after week 15, while switched branches maintained and slightly improved their performance. The new procedure appears to have **protected branches from declining service levels** while others deteriorated.

The effect was not immediate but strengthened in the weeks following implementation:

| Weeks After Switch | Estimated Effect |

## **Appendix: Analysis Method**

This analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** design, which compares:
- The change in service scores at branches that switched procedures (treatment group)
- To the change in service scores at branches that did not switch (control group)

This approach isolates the effect of the procedure change from other factors that might affect all branches simultaneously (such as seasonal variations or system-wide events). Standard errors are clustered by branch to account for repeated observations over time.
