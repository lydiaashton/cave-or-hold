# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.4 points** (about **5% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and consistent across the post-switch period. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Study Design
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure; **60 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Switch occurred in **week 15**
- Complete data for all branches with no missing observations

### 2. Main Result: Positive Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.2 | 52.6 | +2.4 |
| Non-switched branches | 49.8 | 49.6 | -0.2 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.4 points** |

- **Effect size**: +2.44 points (95% confidence interval: 1.14 to 3.73)
- **Statistical significance**: p < 0.001
- **Relative improvement**: ~5% increase from baseline

### 3. Validity Checks

**Parallel Trends Assumption Supported**: Before the switch (weeks 6–14), treatment and control branches showed no statistically different trends in service scores (p = 0.39). This validates our comparison approach.

**Effect is Stable**: The improvement persisted throughout the post-switch period with no evidence of fading or growing over time.

**Robust to Controls**: The result holds when accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume, confirming the effect is attributable to the procedure change itself.

### 4. What This Means

Contrary to the expectation that the procedure change would make no difference, the data shows a **clear, meaningful improvement** in service quality. The effect is:
- **Statistically reliable** (highly unlikely to be due to chance)
- **Practically meaningful** (5% improvement on a ~50-point scale)
- **Consistent** across branches and over time
