## **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

### **Bottom Line**

**Yes, the new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by an additional **1.9 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and represents a meaningful improvement in service quality.

---

### **Key Findings**

**1. Treatment Effect**
- The new procedure increased service scores by **+1.9 points** (p < 0.001)
- This is a **3.8% improvement** relative to the average service score of 50.6
- The effect is equivalent to about **0.19 standard deviations** — a modest but practically meaningful gain

**2. Study Design**
- **709 branches** analyzed over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure (treatment group)
- **284 branches** kept the old procedure (control group)
- The switch occurred at **week 15**, providing clear before/after comparison

**4. Statistical Confidence**
- The result holds across multiple analytical approaches
- Cluster-robust standard errors (accounting for repeated observations per branch) confirm significance
- The effect remains stable when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
