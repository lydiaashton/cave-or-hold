# **Library Branch Rotation & Shelving Procedure: Impact Analysis**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by an average of **2.5 points (approximately 5%)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and represents a meaningful improvement in branch service quality.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure (treatment group)
- **60 branches** kept the old procedure (control group)
- Switch occurred in **week 15**

### Results Summary

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Treatment (New Procedure) | 50.21 | 52.55 | +2.34 |
| Control (Old Procedure) | 49.82 | 49.64 | -0.18 |

**Difference-in-Differences Estimate: +2.53 points**

### Statistical Confidence
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 1.22 to 3.83 points
- **p-value:** < 0.001 (highly significant)
- The result remains robust after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Visual Evidence

The trends plot shows:
1. **Parallel pre-trends:** Both groups followed similar patterns before the switch (weeks 6–14), supporting the validity of the comparison
2. **Clear divergence:** After week 15, treatment branch scores rose while control branch scores remained flat
3. **Sustained effect:** The improvement persisted throughout the 8-week post-switch period

---

## **Next Steps**

### Immediate Actions
1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to remaining 60 branches
2. **Develop implementation timeline** – consider phasing over 4–6 weeks to manage training load
3. **Prepare training materials** based on practices from the 90 early-adopter branches

### Monitoring & Evaluation
1. **Continue tracking service scores** for all branches post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale
2. **Document best practices** from high-performing treatment branches to share during training
3. **Review after 3 months** to assess whether the 2.5-point improvement is maintained

### Additional Considerations
- The effect size (~5% improvement) is meaningful for patron experience
- No negative unintended consequences were detected in staffing or visit patterns
- Early-adopter branches can serve as mentors during the rollout
