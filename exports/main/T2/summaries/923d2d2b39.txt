# **Memorandum to the Board**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

### **Bottom Line**

**Yes, the new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by an average of **2.1 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and represents approximately a **4% gain** in service quality. The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

### **Key Findings**

**1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores**
- The new procedure increased service scores by **+2.15 points** (95% confidence interval: +0.96 to +3.33 points)
- This effect is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)
- For context, the average branch service score is approximately 50 points, so this represents a **4.3% improvement**

**2. Effect Is Consistent Over Time**
- Early post-switch period (weeks 15-18): +2.32 points
- Later post-switch period (weeks 19-22): +1.97 points
- The improvement was immediate and sustained; there is no evidence the effect is fading

**3. Analysis Method Is Rigorous**
- We used a **difference-in-differences** approach, comparing switched branches to non-switched branches before and after week 15
- This method controls for:
  - General time trends affecting all branches (e.g., seasonal patterns)
  - Fixed differences between branches (some branches naturally score higher)
  - Weekly variations in staffing and visitor volume
- The key assumption (that switched and non-switched branches would have followed similar trends without the intervention) is supported by the data

**4. Study Scope**
- **150 branches** total: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 kept the old
- **17 weeks** of data per branch (weeks 6-22)
- **2,550 observations** in total

### **Next Steps**

1. **Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to remaining branches

2. **Monitor implementation** during the transition:
   - Track service scores for newly switched branches to confirm they achieve similar gains
   - Watch for any branches that underperform and may need additional support

3. **Consider complementary investments**:
