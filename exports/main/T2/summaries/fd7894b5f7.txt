I've completed the analysis of the branch procedure change data. Here's what I found:

## Summary for the Board

**Bottom Line:** The new procedure shows a **positive but not statistically significant** effect on service scores. Branches that switched saw scores increase by about 1.9 points more than branches that didn't switch, but this could plausibly be due to random variation.

### Key Findings

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect (DiD estimate) | +1.86 points |
| 95% Confidence interval | [-0.78, +4.49] |
| p-value | 0.176 |
| Effect size | 3.8% of baseline |

### What This Means

- **Positive direction:** All evidence points to improvement, not harm
- **Not conclusive:** We can't rule out that the true effect is zero
- **Parallel trends validated:** Pre-switch trends were similar between groups (p=0.806), supporting causal interpretation
- **Robust:** Effect remains positive (+2.10, p=0.109) when controlling for staffing and visitor volume

### Recommendation

**Phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation:

1. Continue collecting data for 12+ more weeks
2. Randomly assign 10-15 additional branches to adopt the procedure
3. Re-evaluate in 3 months with stronger evidence
