# **Board Summary: New Branch Procedure Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and rotation procedure is associated with a **modest improvement in service scores** (+2.0 points, approximately 4%), but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive** at conventional confidence levels. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than an immediate full deployment.

- However, effects varied considerably: some branches improved by up to 9.5 points, while others declined by up to 5.9 points
- This suggests the procedure may work better in some contexts than others

### 4. Important Context
- Switched branches started from a **lower baseline** (48.2 vs. 51.6) before the procedure change

## **Next Steps**

1. **Proceed with Cautious Rollout**
   - The point estimate is positive and practically meaningful (4% improvement)
   - The lack of statistical significance may reflect limited data (only 8 post-switch weeks) rather than absence of effect
   - Consider rolling out to additional branches while monitoring outcomes

2. **Extend the Evaluation Period**
   - Collect **8–12 more weeks of data** to increase statistical power
   - This would help determine whether the observed effect is real or due to random variation

3. **Investigate Branch-Level Differences**

   - Use findings to tailor implementation support for branches likely to benefit less
