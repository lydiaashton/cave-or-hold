# **Board Summary: Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **promising but inconclusive evidence** of improving service scores. Our best estimate suggests a **4.7-point improvement** (approximately 9% increase from the baseline of ~50), but this result is only **marginally statistically significant** (p=0.051). The 95% confidence interval ranges from 0.2 to 9.3 points, meaning we cannot rule out either no effect or a meaningful improvement.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with caution. Consider a phased rollout with continued monitoring rather than an immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Data covers **17 weeks** (weeks 6-22): 9 weeks before and 8 weeks after the switch
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach, comparing changes in the treatment group to changes in the control group

### **Results**

| Specification | Estimated Effect | Standard Error | P-value | 95% Confidence Interval |
|--------------|-----------------|----------------|---------|------------------------|
| Basic analysis | +1.9 points | 1.26 | 0.140 | [-0.6, 4.4] |
| **Preferred (with branch & week controls)** | **+4.7 points** | **2.33** | **0.051** | **[0.2, 9.3]** |

### **What the Numbers Mean**
- **Control branches** (old procedure): Service scores remained stable (~49.2 before → ~49.3 after)
- **Treatment branches** (new procedure): Service scores increased (~49.9 before → ~52.0 after)
- The **4.7-point estimate** represents the additional improvement attributable to the new procedure, after accounting for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week variation

### **Statistical Uncertainty**
- The p-value of 0.051 is just above the conventional 0.05 threshold for statistical significance
- The confidence interval includes values as low as 0.2 points (minimal effect) and as high as 9.3 points (substantial improvement)
- This uncertainty reflects the relatively short observation period and natural week-to-week variation in service scores

## **Next Steps**

### **Recommended Actions**

1. **Continue the pilot for 8-12 more weeks** to gather additional data and reduce uncertainty. More observations would narrow the confidence interval and provide a clearer answer.

2. **Monitor implementation fidelity** at the 20 switched branches. Ensure the new procedure is being followed consistently, as variation in implementation could mask true effects.

3. **Consider a phased rollout** rather than immediate system-wide adoption:
   - Roll out to an additional 5-10 branches
   - Continue monitoring all branches
   - Make final decision after 3-4 months of additional data

4. **Track secondary outcomes** beyond service scores:
   - Staff satisfaction and workload
   - Patron feedback
   - Operational efficiency metrics

### **Decision Framework**

| If future data shows... | Recommended action |
|------------------------|-------------------|
| Effect > 3 points, p < 0.05 | Proceed with full rollout |
| Effect 1-3 points, p < 0.10 | Continue phased rollout with monitoring |
| Effect < 1 point or p > 0.20 | Reconsider or modify the procedure |
