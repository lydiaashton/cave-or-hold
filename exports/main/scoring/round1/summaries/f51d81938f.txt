# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new procedure **maintained their service scores** while non-switched branches experienced a **notable decline**. The new procedure is associated with an estimated **+1.9-point improvement** in service scores relative to the control group. However, this effect is **not statistically significant** (p=0.14), meaning we cannot rule out random variation. The evidence suggests a **positive but uncertain benefit**. We recommend **proceeding with cautious expansion** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Performance**

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6-14) | After Switch (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| **Switched Branches** (20 branches) | 51.4 | 51.3 | **-0.1** |
| **Non-Switched Branches** (14 branches) | 49.3 | 47.2 | **-2.1** |

### **Statistical Results**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated Treatment Effect | +1.93 points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.6 to +4.5 points |
| P-value | 0.14 |
| Statistical Significance | Not significant at p < 0.05 |

### **What the Data Shows**

1. **Control branches declined significantly**: Non-switched branches saw service scores drop by ~2 points after week 15.

2. **Treatment branches held steady**: Switched branches maintained their service scores with virtually no change.

3. **The performance gap widened**: The difference between switched and non-switched branches grew from 2.1 points before the switch to 4.1 points after.

4. **No pre-existing differences**: Statistical testing confirms both groups were on parallel trends before the switch, validating our comparison.

### **Rationale for Recommendation**

- ✓ **No evidence of harm** — scores did not decline in switched branches
- ✓ **Positive direction** — point estimate favors the new procedure
- ✓ **Control group deterioration** — suggests external factors may be affecting service scores, and the new procedure may provide protection
- ⚠ **Statistical uncertainty** — more data needed for definitive conclusion

---

## **Appendix: Methodology**

- **Approach**: Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Sample**: 34 branches, 578 observations, 17 weeks (weeks 6-22)
- **Treatment**: 20 branches switched to new procedure at week 15
- **Control**: 14 branches maintained old procedure
- **Validation**: Parallel trends assumption confirmed (p=0.32 for pre-treatment trend difference)
