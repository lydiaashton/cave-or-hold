# **Board Summary: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.0 points** (about 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** served as controls (kept old procedure)
- Switch occurred at **week 15**
- Analysis uses a "difference-in-differences" approach to isolate the procedure's effect from other factors

### Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.7 | 50.9 | +1.2 |
| Treated (new procedure) | 53.6 | 56.9 | +3.3 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

- **Estimated impact: +2.03 points** on the service score index
- **95% confidence interval: 0.5 to 3.6 points**
- **Statistical significance: p = 0.011** (highly unlikely to be due to chance)
- **Effect size: ~4% improvement** relative to the baseline average score of 52

### Validity Checks Passed
- **Pre-trends were parallel:** Before week 15, both groups followed similar trends (p = 0.21), supporting the conclusion that the post-switch improvement was caused by the new procedure
- **Results are robust:** The effect holds after controlling for branch characteristics, week-to-week variation, staffing levels, and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Implement rollout** to the 14 control branches and any others not yet converted

2. **Monitor during transition** — track service scores to ensure the effect replicates across all branches

3. **Conduct follow-up evaluation** in 3–6 months to confirm sustained improvements

4. **Capture best practices** from the 20 early-adopter branches to create training materials and smooth the transition
