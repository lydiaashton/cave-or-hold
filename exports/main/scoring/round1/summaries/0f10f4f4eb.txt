# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a meaningful improvement on our ~50-point service score index. We recommend proceeding with a full rollout while continuing to monitor results.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The New Procedure Worked**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Branches with **old** procedure | 46.2 | 45.6 | -0.6 |
| Branches with **new** procedure | 49.6 | 51.0 | +1.5 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

Branches using the new procedure improved while those using the old procedure slightly declined, resulting in a **2.1-point net gain** attributable to the new procedure.

### **Evidence Supports the Result**

- **Pre-trends were parallel**: Before the switch, both groups of branches moved together week-to-week, confirming the comparison is valid
- **Effect is positive across most post-switch weeks**: Six of eight weeks after the switch show positive effects
- **Results hold after accounting for staffing and visitor volume**: The improvement is not explained by changes in these factors

### **Statistical Confidence**

The result is **marginally significant** (p≈0.09). In practical terms:
- The best estimate is a **2-point improvement**
- We are reasonably confident the true effect is positive
- The most likely range is **0 to 4 points** improvement

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with full rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

**Rationale:**
1. The effect is in the expected direction and of meaningful magnitude
2. There is no evidence of harm to any branches
3. The cost of waiting for additional statistical certainty outweighs the risk of expansion
4. Early adopters have already demonstrated the procedure can be implemented successfully

## **Appendix: Study Details**

- **34 branches** participated (20 treatment, 14 control)
- **17 weeks** of data collected (9 before switch, 8 after)
- **Service score** is our standard branch performance index (average ≈50, higher is better)
- Analysis uses difference-in-differences regression with branch-level controls
