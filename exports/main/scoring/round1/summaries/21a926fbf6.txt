# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

**The new procedure shows a positive effect on service scores, but the evidence is not yet strong enough to guarantee success at every branch.** We recommend a **cautious phased rollout** while continuing to collect data.

---

## What We Found

### The Numbers

| Finding | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Average improvement from new procedure | **+2.3 points** |
| Typical branch service score | ~50 points |
| Estimated improvement | **~4-5%** |
| Statistical confidence | **Moderate** (not definitive) |

### What This Means

Branches that adopted the new procedure improved their service scores by about 2.3 points more than branches that kept the old procedure. This is a **meaningful improvement** in the expected direction, but our data does not rule out the possibility that the true effect is smaller or even zero.

### The Evidence

- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 continued with the old
- **17 weeks** of data collected (9 weeks before, 8 weeks after the switch)
- Treatment branches started lower but **narrowed the gap** after switching
- Pre-existing trends were similar between groups, supporting a valid comparison

---

## Recommendation

### Proceed with Phased Rollout

We recommend expanding the new procedure to additional branches while continuing to monitor results. This approach balances:

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Direction of effect** | ✓ Positive (as expected) |
| **Risk of harm** | ✓ No evidence of negative impact |
| **Statistical certainty** | ⚠ Not yet conclusive |
| **Practical significance** | ✓ Modest but meaningful improvement |

### Implementation Plan

1. **Expand to 10-15 additional branches** over the next quarter
2. **Continue weekly tracking** of service scores for all branches
3. **Re-evaluate in 12 weeks** with expanded data to confirm the effect
4. **Document implementation details** to identify best practices

---

## Important Caveats

- The improvement is **not statistically guaranteed**—there is roughly a 12% chance this result occurred by random variation
- We have only **8 weeks of post-switch data**; longer-term effects remain unknown
- Some branches may respond differently based on size, staffing, or local conditions

---

## Questions for the Board

1. Does the potential 4-5% service improvement justify the cost of system-wide implementation?
2. Should we prioritize rolling out to branches with currently lower service scores?
3. What timeline is appropriate for a full evaluation before complete rollout?
