# Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure Evaluation

---

## Bottom Line

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. Switched branches improved by an estimated **1.8 points** (3.4% above baseline) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. However, this result is not statistically significant—we cannot rule out that the observed improvement occurred by chance. The evidence suggests modest benefit, but not enough to guarantee success at every branch.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** while continuing to monitor results, rather than an immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## Key Findings

### What We Measured
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (February–June)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores average around 50 points; higher scores indicate better service

### Results Summary

| Metric | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Average score before | 54.79 | 48.74 |
| Average score after | 57.22 | 49.39 |
| **Change** | **+2.42** | **+0.65** |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | **+1.77 points** |

### What This Means

- **Direction is positive:** 85% of switched branches improved vs. 57% of non-switched branches
- **Effect size is modest:** +1.77 points represents about a 3–4% improvement
- **Uncertainty is high:** The true effect could range from -0.9 to +4.5 points
- **Not statistically significant:** p-value of 0.19 means we cannot confidently distinguish this from random variation

### Important Context

1. **Selection bias:** Branches that switched started with higher baseline scores, suggesting they may have been higher-performing branches selected for early adoption
2. **Short evaluation window:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data limits our ability to detect true effects
3. **External factors:** Staffing changes during the study period affected service scores independently of the procedure change

## Next Steps

### Immediate Actions (Next 30 Days)

1. **Expand to 5–10 additional branches** using the new procedure
   - Select branches across performance levels to test generalizability
   - Maintain 10+ control branches for continued comparison

2. **Extend data collection through week 35** (additional 13 weeks)
   - This will double the post-switch observation window
   - Improves statistical power to detect true effects

3. **Document implementation practices** at high-performing switched branches
   - Some branches saw gains of 8–10 points; others saw declines
   - Identify best practices that could improve overall results
