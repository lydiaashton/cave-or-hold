# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced service score increases of approximately **2.2 points** (about 4.5%) compared to branches that maintained the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant. **We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total branches | 34 |
| Treatment branches (switched) | 20 |
| Control branches (no switch) | 14 |
| Observation period | Weeks 6–22 |
| Pre-switch period | Weeks 6–14 (9 weeks) |
| Post-switch period | Weeks 15–22 (8 weeks) |
| Total observations | 578 |

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Treatment (switched to new procedure) | 46.7 | 48.5 | +1.7 |
| Control (kept old procedure) | 50.3 | 49.8 | -0.4 |
| **Net Effect (Difference-in-Differences)** | | | **+2.2** |

**Statistical Summary:**
- Effect size: **+2.2 points** on the service score index
- 95% confidence interval: **0.5 to 3.9 points**
- Statistical significance: **p = 0.012** (significant at the 5% level)
- Relative improvement: **~4.5%** above baseline

### Validation
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Both groups followed similar trajectories before the switch, supporting the validity of our comparison
- **Results hold after controls:** The effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **No missing data:** Complete records for all branches across all weeks

---

## **Recommendations & Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 14 branches currently using the old procedure

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with phased conversion to ensure adequate training and support

3. **Establish monitoring protocol** to track service scores at newly converted branches and confirm the effect replicates at scale

4. **Document procedural details** that drove improvement to support consistent training and implementation across all locations

5. **Schedule follow-up review** in 6–12 months to assess long-term sustainability of the improvement

---

## **Conclusion**

The evidence supports adopting the new procedure cooperative-wide. The 2.2-point improvement in service scores represents a meaningful gain in branch performance, achieved without additional staffing or resources. Rolling out this procedure to all branches is expected to raise overall cooperative service quality.
