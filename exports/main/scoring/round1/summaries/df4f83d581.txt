# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure experienced a **statistically significant improvement in service scores**. The evidence strongly supports expanding the new procedure to all 150 branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Improved Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| 90 branches (new procedure) | 51.1 | 53.6 | +2.5 |
| 60 branches (old procedure) | 52.3 | 52.8 | +0.5 |

**Net effect of new procedure: +2.0 points** (approximately 4% improvement)

### The Result Is Statistically Solid

- **Confidence Interval:** 1.2 to 2.9 points (95% confidence)
- **Statistical Significance:** p < 0.001
- **Analysis Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects, controlling for staffing and visitor volume
- **Data:** 2,550 observations across 150 branches over 17 weeks

### The Comparison Is Valid

Branches that switched and those that did not were on parallel trajectories before the change (p = 0.84), confirming that the observed improvement is attributable to the new procedure rather than pre-existing differences.

---

## **Recommendation & Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches.

2. **Implement in phases** over 4–6 weeks to allow for training and support.

3. **Continue monitoring** service scores for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale.

4. **Document lessons learned** from the 90 early-adopter branches to support smooth implementation.
