# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout of the new procedure.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed improved service scores relative to those that did not. The estimated gain is **+2.1 points (approximately 4%)** on our service score index. While statistical certainty is moderate (p = 0.10), the effect is in the expected direction, meaningful in practical terms, and supported by the data pattern.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches studied | 34 |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Branches using new procedure | 20 |
| Branches using old procedure | 14 |
| Procedure switch date | Week 15 |

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **New procedure branches** | 52.2 | 52.5 | **+0.3** |
| **Old procedure branches** | 51.9 | 50.3 | **-1.6** |
| **Net effect (DiD estimate)** | | | **+2.1 points** |

### Statistical Summary
- **Estimated effect:** +2.07 points
- **Confidence interval:** -0.4 to +4.5 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.10
- **Effect size:** 0.22 standard deviations (small-to-moderate)

### What This Means
The new procedure appears to **protect service quality** during a period when branches using the old procedure experienced declining scores. A typical branch can expect service scores to improve by approximately **2 points (4%)** after adopting the new procedure.

### Validation Checks Passed
✓ Treatment and control branches had parallel trends before the switch  
✓ Results hold after controlling for staffing and visitor volume  
✓ Effect is consistent across post-switch weeks  

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve systemwide rollout of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure.**

**Rationale:**
1. The effect is positive and in the expected direction
2. A 4% improvement in service scores is operationally meaningful
3. The alternative (keeping old procedure) is associated with declining scores
4. No negative effects or unintended consequences were detected

---

## **Implementation Plan**

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Roll out to remaining 14 branches | Next 4 weeks |
| 2 | Monitor service scores at all branches | Ongoing, weekly |
| 3 | Conduct follow-up analysis | 12 weeks post-rollout |
| 4 | Report results to board | Q2 2026 |

---

## **Risks and Mitigation**

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Effect smaller than estimated | Moderate | Continue monitoring; adjust if needed |
| Effect not sustained long-term | Moderate | Schedule 6-month follow-up evaluation |
| Implementation challenges at some branches | Low | Provide training support during rollout |

---

## **Conclusion**

The data support adopting the new procedure across all branches. The improvement in service scores, combined with the absence of negative effects, makes this a low-risk decision with meaningful potential benefit to patron experience.
