# **Library Cooperative Procedure Change: Service Score Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores**, with switched branches improving by approximately **2 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. However, this result is **not statistically significant** at conventional levels, meaning we cannot rule out chance as an explanation. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** to all branches. The evidence suggests the procedure does no harm and likely provides modest benefits. Continued monitoring will provide greater certainty as more data accumulates.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 34 total (20 switched, 14 control) |
| **Observation period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Metric** | Service score index (average ≈50; higher is better) |

### What Changed

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 47.8 | 49.1 | **+1.3** |
| Control branches | 52.4 | 51.9 | **-0.5** |
| **Net effect** | | | **+1.9 points** |

### Confidence in Results
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.7 to +4.4 points
- **Interpretation:** The true effect is most likely positive but could plausibly range from slightly negative to moderately positive
- **Study limitation:** With 34 branches and 17 weeks of data, we can reliably detect effects of 4+ points; smaller effects require more data to confirm

### Important Context
- Switched branches started with lower baseline scores, but this gap remained stable before the switch
- The narrowing of this gap after the switch supports a positive treatment effect
- Staffing levels and visit volumes did not meaningfully influence results

## **Next Steps**

### Immediate Actions

| Action | Timeline | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| Approve phased rollout to remaining branches | Next board meeting | Board |
| Continue weekly service score tracking | Ongoing | Operations |
| Document implementation practices | During rollout | Branch Managers |

### Monitoring Plan

1. **Track service scores** for all branches for at least 6 months post-rollout
2. **Conduct follow-up analysis** once 12 months of data is available
3. **Evaluate complementary metrics:**
   - Staff time efficiency
   - Patron satisfaction
   - Material availability rates
   - Operational costs

### Success Criteria

| Outcome | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Effect confirmed positive (p<0.05) | Continue current procedure; document as best practice |
| Effect remains uncertain | Maintain procedure; assess qualitative benefits |
| Effect turns negative | Revert to old procedure; investigate causes |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure shows promise for improving branch service scores without evidence of adverse effects. While statistical certainty is limited by sample size, the direction of the effect and absence of negative outcomes support moving forward with implementation. A disciplined monitoring approach will ensure the cooperative can confirm benefits and adjust course if needed.
