# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure shows **modest positive effects** on service scores, but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive**. Branches using the new procedure maintained their service levels while control branches experienced a notable decline. The estimated improvement is **2.3 points (approximately 4%)** on the service score index.

**Recommendation:** Continue the pilot for 8-12 additional weeks before making a system-wide rollout decision. The current data suggests benefit but lacks the statistical precision needed for a confident investment decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Estimated Impact**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | +2.3 points |
| Relative improvement | ~4% |
| Statistical significance | Not significant (p=0.11) |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.4 to +5.1 points |

The effect is **positive but imprecisely estimated**. We cannot rule out that the observed difference occurred by chance, though the point estimate favors the new procedure.

### **2. What the Data Shows**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** (20 branches) | 52.7 | 52.3 | -0.5 |
| **Control branches** (14 branches) | 53.9 | 51.2 | **-2.8** |

**Key insight:** All branches faced downward pressure on service scores after week 14. The new procedure appears to have **protected branches from decline** rather than dramatically improving scores.

### **3. Validation Checks Passed**

- **Pre-trends:** Before the switch (weeks 6-14), both groups followed similar trajectories with no systematic differences
- **Balance:** Switched and control branches had comparable staffing and visit patterns before implementation
- **Consistency:** In 5 of 8 post-switch weeks, switched branches outperformed control branches

### **4. Study Limitations**

| Limitation | Implication |
|------------|-------------|
| 34 branches total | Limited statistical power |
| 8 weeks post-switch | May not capture full effect |
| Minimum detectable effect: ~4 points | Our 2.3-point estimate falls below this threshold |

### **Decision Criteria**

| Outcome | Recommendation |
|---------|----------------|
| Effect ≥3 points, p<0.10 | Proceed with system-wide rollout |
| Effect 1-3 points, stable | Consider phased rollout by region |
| Effect <1 point or negative | Halt expansion; investigate barriers |

---

## **Appendix: Methodology**

- **Design:** Difference-in-differences with branch fixed effects
- **Data:** 578 observations (34 branches × 17 weeks)
- **Treatment:** 20 branches adopted new procedure starting week 15
- **Analysis:** Regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
