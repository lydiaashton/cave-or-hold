# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **1.96 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **4% improvement**, is statistically significant (p < 0.001), and we recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Study Overview**

We analyzed weekly service scores from **709 library branches** over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22). The switch to the new procedure occurred in week 15, with 425 branches adopting the new procedure and 284 branches continuing with the old procedure. This natural experiment allows us to isolate the effect of the new procedure by comparing changes between the two groups.

| Group | Branches | Pre-Switch Avg | Post-Switch Avg | Change |
|-------|----------|----------------|-----------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** | 425 | 50.8 | 51.9 | +1.1 |
| **Old Procedure** | 284 | 49.4 | 48.5 | -0.8 |

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Statistically Significant Improvement
- **Treatment effect: +1.96 service score points**
- 95% confidence interval: **1.42 to 2.50 points**
- Probability this result occurred by chance: **less than 0.1%**

### 2. Meaningful Practical Impact
- Average service score across all branches: **50.4 points**
- The improvement represents approximately **4%** of the average score
- The new procedure appears to protect against downward pressures on service quality (the control group declined by 0.8 points while the treatment group improved)

### 3. Robust Analysis
Results hold after controlling for:
- Weekly staffing levels and visitor volume
- Branch-specific characteristics
- Week-to-week seasonal variations

Standard errors account for repeated observations at each branch.

### 4. Valid Comparison
Before the switch (weeks 6–14), both groups showed similar patterns of week-to-week fluctuation, supporting the validity of the comparison between treatment and control groups.

---

## **Recommendations**

### Immediate Actions:

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with clear milestones and accountability

### During Rollout:

3. **Ensure staff training** is completed before each branch switches to the new procedure

4. **Monitor service scores** weekly during the rollout to confirm improvements are being realized

### After Rollout:

5. **Track performance** for at least 8–12 weeks post-implementation to confirm the effect persists over time

6. **Document best practices** from the 425 branches that successfully implemented the new procedure to support remaining branches
