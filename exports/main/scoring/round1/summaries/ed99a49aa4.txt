## **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

### **Subject: New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure – Impact Evaluation**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

The new procedure **improved service scores by 2.0 points (4%)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant, consistent across all post-implementation weeks, and robust to controls for staffing and customer volume. The evidence supports expanding the new procedure to all 709 branches.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| Average score before week 15 | 49.5 | 48.5 |
| Average score after week 14 | 52.3 | 49.2 |
| **Change** | **+2.8** | **+0.8** |

**Net effect attributable to new procedure: +2.0 points**

1. **Clear Positive Impact**
   - The 2.0-point improvement is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)
   - 95% confidence interval: 1.5 to 2.6 points
   - Effect persists after controlling for staffing levels and customer visits

2. **Consistent Performance**
   - Improvement observed in all 8 weeks since implementation (weeks 15-22)
   - No evidence of the effect diminishing over time

3. **Study Strength**
   - 709 branches tracked over 17 weeks (12,053 observations)
   - Natural experiment design: 425 branches switched, 284 served as control
   - Analysis accounts for branch-level differences and pre-existing trends

4. **Visual Summary**
   - See attached chart: *service_score_trends.png*
   - Shows diverging trajectories after week 14 when the new procedure took effect
