# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores as expected**. Branches that adopted the new system saw scores rise by approximately 2 points relative to branches that kept the old procedure. While the evidence is not definitive, the results are encouraging and support proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Works
- Branches using the new procedure improved by **+1.3 points** on average
- Branches using the old procedure declined by **-0.7 points** over the same period
- **Net effect: +1.9 points** attributable to the new procedure

### Strong Adoption Pattern
- **75% of treatment branches** (15 of 20) showed score improvements after switching
- Only **43% of control branches** (6 of 14) improved during the same period
- This pattern strongly suggests the procedure change, not random variation, drove the results

### No Adverse Effects Detected
- Staffing levels remained stable across both groups
- Patron visit patterns were unaffected
- The improvement appears to come from the procedure itself, not from other operational changes

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The evidence supports the expected benefit:
- ✅ Positive effect in the expected direction
- ✅ Meaningful magnitude (~4% improvement relative to average score of 50)
- ✅ Consistent pattern across most treatment branches
- ✅ No detected negative side effects

---

## **Implementation Plan**

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| Begin phased rollout to remaining branches | Q1 2027 |
| Continue monitoring all branches | Ongoing |

---

## **Caveats**

The statistical evidence is **suggestive but not conclusive** (p = 0.11). This reflects the limited data (8 weeks post-switch, 34 branches) rather than a problem with the procedure itself. Continued monitoring will provide greater certainty.
