# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced a **2.1-point increase in service scores** (approximately **4% improvement**) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and grew over time following implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** analyzed over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **284 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Analysis compares service score changes between the two groups before and after the switch

### Results Summary

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 49.47 | 52.31 | +2.84 |
| Non-switched branches | 48.47 | 49.24 | +0.77 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.06** |

- **Estimated impact:** +2.1 points on the service score index
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.5 to 2.6 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001

### Effect Timeline
The benefit accumulated gradually after implementation:
- **Weeks 16–18:** Modest gains (+0.2 to +0.4 points)
- **Weeks 19–22:** Stronger gains (+0.7 to +1.1 points)

This pattern suggests branches required an adjustment period to fully realize the procedure's benefits.

### Analytical Note
Branches that opted into the new procedure had different baseline performance patterns than those that did not. The analysis accounts for these differences, and the timing of the improvement (appearing only after the switch and growing thereafter) supports a causal interpretation.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Implement rollout** to remaining 284 branches on a phased schedule
2. **Provide training materials** documenting best practices from early-adopter branches
3. **Monitor service scores** for 6–8 weeks post-implementation at each branch
4. **Conduct follow-up evaluation** in Q2 2026 to confirm sustained improvement
