# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** and is statistically significant. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.05 points on service score |
| **Confidence Interval** | 0.3 to 3.8 points (95%) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.02 (significant) |
| **Branches Studied** | 34 total (20 switched, 14 control) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |

### What We Compared
- **Treatment group:** 20 branches that adopted the new procedure in Week 15
- **Control group:** 14 branches that continued with the old procedure
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences analysis controlling for branch characteristics, staffing levels, and visitor volume

### Why We're Confident
1. **Pre-trends align:** Before the switch, both groups showed similar service score patterns with no systematic differences
2. **Controls unchanged:** Staffing and visitor patterns did not differ between groups after the switch, confirming the improvement stems from the procedure itself
3. **Effect persisted:** The improvement was visible immediately after implementation and sustained through the 8-week observation period

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

The evidence supports the expected benefit: the new procedure improves service quality without requiring additional staffing or resources.
