# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure is associated with a **2.9-point improvement** in service scores, consistent with our expectations. However, the evidence is **not statistically conclusive** (p = 0.10). We recommend a **phased rollout** while continuing to collect data to confirm the effect.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Rigorous difference-in-differences analysis controls for pre-existing differences and time trends

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** | 48.65 | 50.45 | **+1.80** |
| **Old Procedure** | 53.61 | 52.54 | **−1.07** |

### **Estimated Effect of New Procedure**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Point Estimate** | +2.87 points |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | −0.54 to +6.28 |
| **p-value** | 0.10 |

### **What This Means**

✓ **Effect is in the expected direction** – service scores improved where the new procedure was implemented

✓ **Magnitude is meaningful** – a 2.9-point gain represents approximately 6% of the average branch score

⚠ **Statistical uncertainty remains** – we cannot rule out that some or all of the observed improvement occurred by chance

⚠ **Effects vary across branches** – 11 branches improved; 9 showed no change or decline

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Responsible |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| Continue weekly data collection | Ongoing | Operations |
| Expand to 10–15 additional branches | Q1 2027 | Operations |
| Analyze branch characteristics linked to success | 60 days | Data Office |
| Gather staff feedback on implementation | 30 days | HR/Operations |
| Reconvene to review expanded data | Q2 2027 | Board |

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve a phased rollout** of the new procedure to additional branches while continuing systematic evaluation. The positive direction of the effect supports expansion, but the statistical uncertainty warrants continued monitoring before committing to system-wide implementation.
