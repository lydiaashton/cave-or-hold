# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. Switched branches improved by approximately **1.9 points more** than non-switched branches, but this difference could plausibly be due to random variation. We recommend **continuing the pilot and gathering more data** before committing to a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Analyzed**
- **34 branches** over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** kept the old procedure
- Service scores range around 50 on average (higher is better)

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 53.0 | 57.9 | +4.9 |
| Branches that didn't switch | 48.5 | 51.5 | +3.1 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

- The new procedure is associated with a **1.9-point improvement** in service scores
- However, this effect is **not statistically significant** (p = 0.18), meaning we cannot rule out that the observed difference occurred by chance
- Both groups improved after week 15, suggesting external factors (e.g., seasonal patterns) may have boosted scores across the system

### **Analysis Quality**
- Pre-switch trends were **parallel** between groups, supporting the validity of our comparison
- Results account for inherent differences between branches and weekly variation

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **1** | Continue monitoring current pilot branches | Ongoing |
| **2** | Expand pilot to 5–10 additional branches | Next quarter |
| **3** | Track complementary metrics (staff hours, patron feedback, circulation) | Ongoing |
| **4** | Reconvene to review expanded data | 3–6 months |

---

## **Decision Framework**

| If the true effect is... | System-wide rollout would... |
|--------------------------|------------------------------|
| ~2 points (our estimate) | Provide modest benefit |
| Zero (possible given uncertainty) | Have no impact |
| Negative (unlikely but possible) | Harm service quality |

Given this uncertainty, a **phased expansion** rather than immediate full rollout minimizes risk while allowing us to learn more.
