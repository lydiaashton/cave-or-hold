# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain** effect on service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by approximately **2.3 points** more than branches that kept the old procedure. However, the evidence is not strong enough to conclude the improvement is real rather than due to chance. We recommend **continued monitoring with a cautious, phased expansion** rather than an immediate system-wide rollout.

---

## Key Findings

### Impact Estimate

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Estimated effect on service score | **+2.3 points** |
| Statistical significance | Not significant (p = 0.12) |
| 95% confidence interval | **-0.6 to +5.1 points** |
| Baseline average score | ~50 points |

The confidence interval means the true effect could range from a small negative impact to a meaningful 5-point improvement. We cannot rule out either possibility with the current data.

### Study Overview

- **34 branches** participated (20 adopted new procedure, 14 kept old procedure)
- **17 weeks** of data collected (9 weeks before, 8 weeks after the switch)
- **Rigorous method** used: Difference-in-differences with branch-level controls and robust statistical techniques

### Important Validation

The analysis is credible because treatment and control branches showed **similar trends before the switch**, supporting the conclusion that observed differences after Week 15 are likely related to the procedure change rather than pre-existing differences.

### Context

- Treatment branches started with lower scores (~3 points below control)
- After the switch, this gap narrowed considerably
- The positive effect holds even when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## Recommendations

### Immediate Actions

1. **Extend the evaluation period** — Collect 8-12 additional weeks of data to improve statistical confidence

2. **Proceed with cautious expansion** — Consider offering the new procedure to 5-10 additional branches while maintaining a control group for continued comparison

3. **Document implementation details** — Capture any variations in how branches adopted the procedure to identify best practices

### Decision Framework

| If the Board prioritizes... | Recommended Action |
|----------------------------|-------------------|
| **Certainty before action** | Wait for more data (3-6 months) |
| **Early adoption of potential improvements** | Phased rollout with continued evaluation |
| **Cost minimization** | Delay rollout until effect is confirmed |

---

## Limitations

- Only 8 weeks of post-switch data limits confidence in the estimates
- The effect is not statistically distinguishable from zero at conventional standards
- Long-term sustainability of any improvement is unknown
