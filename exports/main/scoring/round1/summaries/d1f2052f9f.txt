# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive directional effect** on service scores (+2.4 points), consistent with our expectations. However, with the current data, we **cannot confirm statistical significance**. We recommend **continuing the pilot for one additional quarter** before deciding on system-wide implementation.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did service scores improve? | **Yes, modestly** (+2.4 points) |
| Are we confident this wasn't random chance? | **Not yet** (p = 0.11) |
| Should we roll out to all branches now? | **Not recommended** — extend evaluation |

---

## **What We Did**

We compared 20 branches that adopted the new procedure (starting week 15) against 14 branches that continued with the old procedure. Using a difference-in-differences design, we isolated the effect of the procedure change from other factors affecting all branches.

**Data:** 34 branches × 17 weeks = 578 observations  
**Service Score Scale:** Average branch scores ~50; higher is better

---

## **What We Found**

### Service Score Changes

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with New Procedure | 46.0 | 47.1 | **+1.1** |
| Branches with Old Procedure | 51.4 | 50.1 | **−1.3** |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.4** |

### Statistical Confidence

- **Point Estimate:** +2.4 points (≈5% improvement)
- **95% Confidence Interval:** −0.15 to +4.59 points
- **Interpretation:** The true effect could reasonably be anywhere from negligible to moderately positive

---

## **Why This Matters**

1. **The effect is in the expected direction** — scores improved in switched branches relative to controls
2. **The magnitude is meaningful** — if real, a 2.4-point gain represents noticeable service improvement
3. **The evidence is not yet definitive** — we need more data to rule out random variation

---

## **Recommendations**

### Immediate Actions

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Continue pilot with current 20 branches | Ongoing |
| 2 | Collect 8–12 additional weeks of data | Q1 2027 |
| 3 | Document implementation practices at high-performing switched branches | 30 days |

### Decision Framework

| If Follow-Up Shows | Then |
|-------------------|------|
| Effect ≥2.0 points, p < 0.05 | **Proceed with system-wide rollout** |
| Effect 1.0–2.0 points, any p-value | **Consider phased rollout** |
| Effect <1.0 points or negative | **Discontinue and explore alternatives** |

---

## **Risks and Considerations**

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| False positive (rolling out something that doesn't work) | Extended evaluation reduces this risk |
| False negative (abandoning something that does work) | Current data suggests benefit; continuation preserves option |
| Implementation variation across branches | Document and standardize best practices during extension |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure is **performing as hoped** but requires **more time to confirm**. We advise the board to authorize a **one-quarter extension** of the pilot evaluation, with a final rollout decision scheduled for Q2 2027.
