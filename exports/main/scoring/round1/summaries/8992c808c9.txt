# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure is associated with a **modest improvement in service scores**—approximately **2 points higher** (a 4% increase) for branches that adopted it. However, this effect is **not statistically significant** at conventional levels, meaning we cannot confidently rule out chance as an explanation. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** while continuing to monitor results. Do not commit to system-wide implementation until we have more conclusive evidence.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** kept the old procedure
- Analysis compares changes in service scores between groups before and after the switch

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.2 | 51.1 | +1.9 |
| Treated (new procedure) | 47.0 | 51.0 | +3.9 |

- **Estimated effect of new procedure: +2.0 points**
- **95% confidence interval: -0.5 to +4.6 points**
- **Statistical significance: p = 0.12** (not significant at the 0.05 threshold)

### **What This Means**
- Both groups improved after week 15, but branches using the new procedure improved slightly more
- The true effect could reasonably be anywhere from slightly harmful to meaningfully beneficial
- The data **does not confirm** the expected improvement, but also **does not rule it out**

### **Validity Checks**
- ✓ **Parallel trends confirmed**: Both groups followed similar trajectories before the switch
- ✓ **Complete data**: All 34 branches reported for all 17 weeks
- ✓ **Robust methods**: Analysis accounts for branch-specific characteristics and statistical uncertainty

### **Decision Criteria for Full Rollout**
We recommend proceeding to system-wide implementation only if:
- The effect remains positive (**+2 points or higher**)
- Statistical significance is achieved (**p < 0.05**)
- No negative effects emerge in specific branch types

---

## **INVESTMENT IMPLICATIONS**

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|----------|----------------|
| **Effect confirmed (+2 pts, p<0.05)** | Full rollout justified; training costs warranted |
| **Effect remains uncertain** | Maintain mixed approach; target high-benefit branches |
| **Effect disappears or reverses** | Halt rollout; investigate implementation issues |
