# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Date:** November 2026
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure shows a **modest positive effect** on service scores (+2 points, or 4%), but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive**. We recommend a **phased expansion with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 maintained the old procedure
- **17 weeks** of data collected (weeks 6–22), with the switch implemented in week 15
- Analysis compares service score changes between switched and non-switched branches

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 53.8 | 55.1 | **+1.3 points** |
| Non-switched branches | 46.5 | 45.7 | **-0.8 points** |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.1 points** |

### **Statistical Assessment**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | +2.1 points | 4% improvement over baseline |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.3 to +4.6 | True effect could be zero or modestly positive |
| P-value | 0.09 | 9% probability result occurred by chance |
| Statistical significance | Not achieved | Conventional threshold is p < 0.05 |

### **Validation Checks**
✓ **Parallel trends confirmed:** Both groups followed similar patterns before the switch
✓ **Result is robust:** Effect size remains consistent when controlling for staffing and visitor volume
✓ **Directionally positive:** Switched branches improved while non-switched branches declined slightly

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Continue data collection to strengthen statistical evidence | 8–12 weeks |
| 2 | Expand procedure to 5–10 additional branches for further testing | Immediate |
| 3 | Analyze whether certain branch types benefit more than others | 4 weeks |
| 4 | Conduct cost-benefit analysis of system-wide implementation | 6 weeks |
| 5 | Reconvene board with updated data for final rollout decision | 3 months |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure demonstrates **promise but not proof**. The positive direction and meaningful magnitude of the effect warrant continued investment in evaluation, but the statistical uncertainty does not yet justify the cost and disruption of system-wide implementation. A measured, evidence-based expansion approach minimizes risk while allowing the cooperative to capture potential benefits.
