# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.5 to 2.0 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure—a **3-4% improvement** in service quality. The effect is statistically significant, consistent across all post-implementation weeks, and holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Control Branches (Old Procedure) | Treatment Branches (New Procedure) |
|--------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Before Week 15 | 50.3 | 51.2 |
| After Week 15 | 49.6 | 52.5 |
| **Change** | **-0.7** | **+1.3** |

**The Gap:** Treatment branches improved by **2.05 points** relative to control branches (p < 0.001).

**Conservative Estimate:** Using only the 5 weeks immediately before the switch (weeks 10-14), the effect is **+1.46 points** (p < 0.001).

**Consistency:** The treatment effect is positive in every single post-switch week (weeks 15-22), ranging from +2.3 to +3.8 points.

---

## **What This Means**

1. **The new procedure delivers measurable improvement** in service quality without requiring additional staffing or resources.

2. **Control branches declined slightly** after week 14, while treatment branches improved—suggesting the new procedure also helps branches resist external pressures that might otherwise degrade service.

3. **The effect is robust** across different analytical approaches and after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume.
