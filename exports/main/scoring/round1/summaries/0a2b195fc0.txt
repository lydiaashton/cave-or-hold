# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new branch rotation and shelving procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. Treatment branches improved by 1.6 points more than control branches after implementation—a 3% gain over baseline. However, this effect is not statistically distinguishable from zero at conventional confidence levels.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** while continuing data collection. The procedure shows no evidence of harm, and the potential upside warrants broader implementation with ongoing monitoring.

---

## **Study Overview**

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Branches studied** | 34 total (20 switched to new procedure; 14 retained old procedure) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22 of the year) |
| **Implementation date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences analysis with cluster-robust standard errors |

---

## **Results**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Estimated effect** | +1.62 service score points |
| **95% confidence interval** | -0.44 to +3.67 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.12 (not significant at p < 0.05) |
| **Effect size** | +3.3% relative to baseline |

**Interpretation:** The data suggest a modest improvement, but we cannot rule out the possibility that the true effect is zero or slightly negative. The confidence interval indicates the true effect likely falls between a small decline and a moderate gain.

---

## **Key Observations**

1. **Both groups improved after week 15**, but treatment branches improved slightly more
2. **Pre-implementation trends were similar** between treatment and control branches, supporting the validity of the comparison
3. **Week-to-week variation is substantial**, suggesting the effect may depend on implementation quality or external factors
4. **No negative effects detected**—the procedure does not appear to harm service quality

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Immediate Actions**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Expand procedure to 5–10 additional volunteer branches | Next quarter |
| 2 | Continue weekly service score tracking for all branches | Ongoing |
| 3 | Collect staff feedback on implementation experience | 30 days |

### **Decision Triggers**

| Outcome | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Effect grows to +2.5 points with p < 0.05 | Proceed with system-wide rollout |
| Effect remains between 0 and +2 points | Continue phased expansion |
| Effect becomes negative | Pause and investigate implementation issues |

### **Additional Data to Collect**

- Staff time spent on rotation/shelving under each procedure
- Patron satisfaction surveys by branch
- Inventory accuracy metrics
- Branch-level implementation fidelity measures

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure demonstrates **promise without definitive proof**. Given the absence of negative effects and the potential for meaningful service improvement, a cautious expansion is warranted. Continued measurement will enable a confident system-wide decision within 6–12 months.
