# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced a statistically significant increase of **2.1 points** on the service score index (95% CI: 1.6 to 2.7 points), representing a **4.2% improvement**. We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Clear Positive Effect
- **Treatment effect**: +2.1 points (p < 0.001)
- **Branches that switched** (425 branches): Scores rose from 50.7 to 54.0
- **Branches that did not switch** (284 branches): Scores rose from 50.7 to 51.9
- Both groups started at identical baseline levels, strengthening confidence in the comparison

### 2. Widespread Benefits
- **81% of switching branches** saw improvement in their service scores
- Only **63% of non-switching branches** improved during the same period
- Benefits were consistent across branches of different sizes

### 3. Implementation Pattern
- **Week 1 after switching**: Temporary dip of 2.2 points (adjustment period)
- **Weeks 2-8**: Sustained gains of 1.4 to 3.0 points
- Results remain robust even when excluding an outlier week (+2.0 points)

### 4. Analysis Quality
- Controls for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Validated with multiple statistical approaches
- Pre-switch trends examined to confirm groups were comparable
