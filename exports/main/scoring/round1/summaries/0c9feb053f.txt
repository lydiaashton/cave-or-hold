# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2 points** (4% relative to baseline) in branches that adopted it. While this positive effect did not reach statistical significance with the current data, the direction of the effect aligns with expectations, and 70% of switching branches saw improvements. **We recommend proceeding with a systemwide rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring to confirm and refine the expected benefits.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Treatment Effect**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+2.0 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.5 to +4.8 points |
| Branches showing improvement | **14 of 20 (70%)** |

### **Service Score Changes**
| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| **Switched branches** (new procedure) | 47.9 | 49.6 | **+1.7** |
| Non-switched branches (old procedure) | 51.1 | 50.8 | -0.3 |

The switched branches improved while the control branches slightly declined, resulting in a **net gain of 2.0 points** attributable to the new procedure.

### **Branch-Level Variation**
- **Best performer:** +6.8 points
- **Worst performer:** -5.1 points
- **Average among switchers:** +1.7 points

This variation suggests implementation quality or branch-specific factors may influence outcomes—an opportunity for optimization during rollout.

### **Validation**
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, confirming valid comparison
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Analysis covers 34 branches, 578 weekly observations (weeks 6–22)

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout**, with the following conditions:

1. **Implement across all remaining branches** over the next 8–12 weeks
2. **Document implementation practices** at high-performing branches to share best practices
3. **Continue tracking service scores** for at least 6 months post-rollout to confirm sustained benefits
4. **Investigate underperforming branches** (the 6 of 20 that saw declines) to identify and address barriers

---

## **Rationale**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|--------------|------------|
| Direction of effect | ✅ Positive, as expected |
| Magnitude | ✅ Meaningful (~4% improvement) |
| Consistency | ✅ 70% of branches improved |
| Statistical certainty | ⚠️ Not yet conclusive, but promising |
| Risk of waiting | Delaying denies 14 branches continued benefits |
| Risk of acting | Minimal—procedure can be adjusted if needed |

Given the positive direction, meaningful effect size, and low risk of reversal, the evidence supports moving forward while gathering additional data to strengthen confidence.
