# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Recommendation on New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with systemwide rollout.**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **improves service scores by approximately 2 points** (about 4% above baseline). This improvement is statistically significant, consistent across analytical methods, and aligns with our expectations. We recommend expanding the procedure to all 709 branches.

---

## **What We Did**

When some branches adopted the new procedure in week 15 and others did not, we had an opportunity to measure its impact. We analyzed weekly service scores for all 709 branches over 17 weeks, comparing branches that switched to those that did not.

---

## **What We Found**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with **new procedure** | 52.8 | 53.8 | +1.0 |
| Branches with **old procedure** | 50.9 | 49.9 | −1.0 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

**Key points:**
- The 2-point improvement is **highly reliable** (p < 0.001)
- The effect holds after accounting for staffing levels and patron visits
- Branches using the old procedure actually declined slightly, suggesting the new procedure also helps branches stay competitive

---

## **Why We're Confident**

1. **Large sample:** 709 branches, 12,000+ observations
2. **Clear comparison:** 425 switched branches vs. 284 control branches
3. **Robust analysis:** Multiple statistical methods all point to the same conclusion
4. **Expected direction:** Results match our hypothesis that the new procedure would help

---

## **Risks & Considerations**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| Branches weren't randomly assigned | Switched branches started with slightly higher scores; we adjusted for this statistically |
| Only 8 weeks of post-switch data | Effect is clear but we should monitor for sustainability |
| Implementation may vary by branch | Provide clear rollout guidance to ensure consistency |

**Overall risk level: Low** — The evidence is strong and the expected benefit is meaningful.
