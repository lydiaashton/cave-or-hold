# **Library Branch Shelving Procedure: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Shelving Procedure Pilot

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Yes—roll out the new procedure to all branches.** The pilot demonstrates a **statistically significant improvement of 2.1 points** (approximately 4%) in service scores for branches that adopted the new shelving procedure, compared to branches that continued with the old method.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (Weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting Week 15; **14 branches** served as a control group
- Analysis compares changes in service scores before and after the switch, accounting for underlying trends

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 50.8 | 53.9 | +3.2 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 52.2 | 53.2 | +1.1 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.1** |

- **Estimated impact:** +2.1 points on the service score index
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 0.4 to 3.8 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.016 (significant at the 5% level)
- **Relative improvement:** ~4% above baseline

### **Why We Trust This Result**

✓ **Parallel trends verified:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories (p = 0.93), validating the comparison method

✓ **Not driven by other factors:** The effect remains significant after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

✓ **Consistent direction:** Switched branches outperformed control branches in 6 of 8 post-switch weeks

---

## **Recommendation & Next Steps**

### **Recommendation**
**Approve system-wide rollout** of the new shelving procedure to all 34 branches.

### **Implementation Plan**

1. **Phase 1 (Immediate):** Begin training and rollout to remaining 14 branches
2. **Phase 2 (3 months):** Monitor service scores to confirm improvement sustains at the system level
3. **Phase 3 (6 months):** Document lessons learned and best practices from pilot branches for ongoing training

### **Expected Outcome**
If the pilot effect holds across all branches, the cooperative can expect an average service score increase of approximately **2 points system-wide**, representing meaningful improvement in patron service quality.

---

*Analysis based on 578 weekly observations from Weeks 6–22. The service score index averages approximately 50 across all branches; higher scores indicate better service.*
