# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. Switched branches improved by approximately **3 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. However, this result is not statistically significant at conventional levels (p = 0.10), meaning we cannot confidently rule out chance as an explanation. 

**Recommendation:** Do not proceed with an immediate system-wide rollout. Instead, continue monitoring and consider a controlled expansion to gather more evidence.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a comparison group
- Analysis compares service score changes between the two groups using a difference-in-differences approach

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 48.6 | 50.4 | **+1.8** |
| Branches with old procedure | 53.6 | 52.5 | **–1.1** |
| **Estimated effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.9** |

### **Statistical Assessment**
- **Point estimate:** +2.9 points on the service score index
- **95% confidence interval:** –0.5 to +6.3 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.10 (not significant at the standard 5% threshold)
- **Practical significance:** The effect represents approximately 6% of the average service score—a modest but potentially meaningful improvement

### **Additional Observations**
- Branches that switched to the new procedure started with lower baseline service scores (about 5 points lower on average)
- The analysis accounts for this baseline difference
- Service scores across all branches showed a slight declining trend during the study period; switched branches reversed this trend
- Controlling for staffing levels and branch visit volumes did not materially change the results

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Continue data collection** | Ongoing | Additional weeks of data will increase statistical precision |
| **Expand to 5–10 additional branches** | Next quarter | A controlled expansion provides more evidence without full commitment |
| **Document implementation practices** | Immediate | Identify whether some branches implemented the procedure more effectively |
| **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** | 30 days | Determine whether operational costs justify the observed improvement |
| **Reconvene to review** | After 8–12 more weeks of data | Make a final rollout decision with stronger evidence |

---

## **Decision Framework**

| If... | Then... |
|-------|---------|
| Effect remains positive and becomes statistically significant (p < 0.05) | Proceed with system-wide rollout |
| Effect remains positive but uncertain (p between 0.05 and 0.15) | Continue phased expansion with monitoring |
| Effect diminishes or becomes negative | Halt expansion and investigate implementation issues |
