# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores—and we didn't expect it to.** Branches that switched saw a **2.1-point increase** (4% improvement) compared to those that kept the old procedure. This is a statistically significant, unexpected benefit. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Measured**
- **150 branches** over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **60 branches** kept the old procedure
- Service scores averaged ~50 across the system (higher is better)

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.8 | 53.7 | +2.9 |
| Control branches | 47.6 | 48.4 | +0.9 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.1** |

- **77% of switched branches** improved vs. **60% of control branches**
- Result is **statistically significant** (p = 0.001)
- Effect held after controlling for branch-specific factors and common trends

### **Why This Matters**
The board expected **no change** to service scores from this operational update. Instead, we got a **meaningful improvement at no apparent cost**. The gain does not appear driven by staffing changes or visit volume shifts—the procedure itself is delivering better service.
