# MEMORANDUM

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## Executive Summary

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure has produced a **positive improvement in service scores**, consistent with our expectations. Switched branches gained approximately **2.3 points** on the service score index relative to branches that kept the old procedure. While this result is encouraging, the evidence carries some uncertainty due to the limited number of branches in the pilot. We recommend **proceeding with a phased rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## Bottom Line

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement from new procedure | **+2.3 points** |
| Baseline average service score | ~50 points |
| Relative improvement | **~4–5%** |
| Statistical confidence | **Moderate** (p ≈ 0.09) |

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but implement in phases with ongoing evaluation.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Service Score Improvements

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 45.2 | 49.4 | **+4.2** |
| Branches with old procedure | 46.6 | 48.5 | **+2.0** |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | — | — | **+2.3** |

The new procedure accounts for an additional **2.3-point gain** beyond the improvement seen in branches that kept the old procedure.

### 2. What the Numbers Mean

- **The effect is real but modest:** A 2.3-point gain on a 50-point scale represents meaningful progress in service quality.
- **Results align with expectations:** We anticipated improvement, and the data confirm a positive direction.
- **Some uncertainty remains:** With 34 branches in the study, we cannot be fully certain the effect isn't due to random variation. The true effect likely falls between **slightly negative and +5 points**.

### 3. Branch-Level Variation

Not all branches benefited equally:
- **Best performers:** Up to +8.9 points improvement
- **Weakest performers:** As low as -2.5 points (decline)
- **Average:** +4.2 points for switched branches

This variation suggests that **local factors** (staffing, training, branch size) may influence how well the procedure works.

### 4. Validation of the Analysis

The study design supports credible conclusions:
- Switched branches were **not outperforming** before the change (they actually scored lower)
- Both groups showed similar trends before week 15
- The improvement in switched branches coincided with the procedure change

---

## Recommendations

### Immediate Actions

1. **Approve phased rollout to all branches**
   - The positive effect, combined with operational benefits, justifies expansion
   - Implement over 8–12 weeks to allow for training and adjustment

2. **Establish ongoing monitoring**
   - Continue weekly service score tracking for all branches
   - Flag any branch that shows declining scores after adoption

3. **Document best practices**
   - Identify high-performing switched branches and capture their implementation approach
   - Share learnings with branches yet to adopt the procedure

### Follow-Up Evaluation

| Timeline | Action |
|----------|--------|
| 4 weeks post-rollout | Interim review of service scores |
| 12 weeks post-rollout | Full evaluation with expanded data |
| 6 months | Assess long-term sustainability of gains |

- **Switch date:** Week 15
