# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure saw a statistically significant increase of **1.75 points** (approximately 3.3% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** total: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 kept the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data**: Weeks 6–14 (before), Weeks 15–22 (after)
- **Analysis method**: Difference-in-Differences with branch and week fixed effects

### **Main Result**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | **+1.75 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | [0.28, 3.21] |
| p-value | **0.020** |

### **Effect Size**
- Average service score: **52.9 points**
- Improvement: **3.3%** of average score
- Effect size: **0.17 standard deviations** (small-to-moderate, meaningful for service quality)

### **Validity Checks**
| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Parallel trends (pre-switch) | ✓ Confirmed (p=0.86) |
| Robust to staffing/visits controls | ✓ Effect stable at 1.76 points |
| Consistent across post-switch weeks | ✓ 7 of 8 weeks showed positive effect |

### **Score Changes by Group**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.26 | 56.37 | +6.10 |
| Control branches | 50.60 | 54.96 | +4.36 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+1.75** |

*Both groups improved after week 14 (likely seasonal), but switched branches improved significantly more.*

---

## **Next Steps**

### **Recommended Actions**
1. **Proceed with system-wide rollout** to all 14 branches currently using the old procedure
2. **Monitor implementation**: Track service scores for 8–12 weeks after each branch switches
3. **Document best practices** from early adopters to support training

### **Considerations**
- Effect is **statistically significant but modest**—expect gradual improvement, not dramatic transformation
- Largest boost occurred in **Week 15** (+4.0 points); subsequent weeks settled to +1 to +2 points
- Continue tracking **staffing and visitor volume**, as these also influence service scores

### **Expected Outcome**
If the effect holds system-wide, the cooperative could expect average service scores to rise from ~53 to ~55 points—a meaningful improvement in patron experience.
