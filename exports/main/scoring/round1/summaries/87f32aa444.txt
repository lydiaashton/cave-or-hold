# **Library Cooperative Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation system achieved service scores **2.0 points higher** than branches that kept the old system. This confirms the expected improvement and supports a cooperative-wide rollout.

**Recommendation:** **Proceed with full rollout** to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Numbers
| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Branches studied | 34 (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| Study period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Service score improvement | **+2.04 points** |
| Statistical confidence | **95%** (p = 0.01) |
| Relative gain | **~4%** above baseline |

### What Happened
- **Switched branches** improved from 48.2 to 50.3 after adopting the new procedure
- **Non-switched branches** remained flat at 51.6 (no change)
- The 2-point gain is **not due to chance** – it persists after accounting for branch differences, staffing levels, and normal week-to-week variation

### Why We're Confident
1. **Strong study design:** We compared switched branches against non-switched branches over the same time period
2. **Clean results:** Non-switched branches showed no change, ruling out external factors (seasonality, system-wide trends)
3. **Statistically significant:** The probability this result occurred by chance is only 1%

---

## **Recommendations**

### Approve Full Rollout
The evidence supports extending the new procedure to all 14 remaining branches. The expected cooperative-wide impact is a **2-point average increase** in service scores.

### Implementation Plan
| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Board approval | Immediate |
| 2 | Develop training materials (from early adopters) | 2–4 weeks |
| 3 | Phase rollout (4–5 branches per wave) | 8–12 weeks |
| 4 | Monitor and adjust | Ongoing |

### Success Metrics
- Track service scores for 8 weeks post-switch for each branch
- Target: Maintain or exceed the 2-point improvement observed in early adopters
- Monitor staffing levels during transition (adequate staffing supports better outcomes)

---

## **Risks and Mitigations**

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Implementation disruption | Phase the rollout; provide hands-on training |
| Staffing gaps during transition | Coordinate with HR to ensure adequate coverage |
| Variable results across branches | Share best practices from top-performing early adopters |

---

## **Appendix: Analysis Details**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects
- **Data:** 578 weekly observations across 34 branches
- **Controls:** Staffing index, visits index, branch characteristics, seasonal effects
