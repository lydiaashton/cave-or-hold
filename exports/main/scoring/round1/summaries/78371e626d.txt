# Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 2025  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (switch implemented Week 15)  
**Data:** 709 branches, 12,053 weekly observations

---

## Bottom Line

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2.1 points (4% above baseline).** The effect is statistically significant and grew stronger over time. We recommend proceeding with a full rollout to all branches.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Positive Impact Confirmed

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Branches with new procedure** | 51.85 | 52.52 | +0.67 |
| **Branches with old procedure** | 51.09 | 49.64 | −1.45 |

**Net effect: +2.12 points** (p < 0.001)

Branches that adopted the new procedure not only improved their scores, but also outperformed branches that kept the old procedure, which experienced a decline during the same period.

### 2. Effect Strengthens Over Time

The benefit was not immediate but grew steadily:
- **Weeks 15–17:** Small, not yet significant
- **Weeks 18–22:** Clear positive effect, reaching +2.2 points by Week 22

This suggests branches need a few weeks to fully realize the benefits as staff gain familiarity with the new procedure.

### 3. Results Are Robust

The 2.1-point estimate holds across multiple analytical approaches:
- Simple comparison of before/after changes
- Regression controlling for branch-specific characteristics
- Regression additionally controlling for staffing and visitor volume

---

## Recommendations

### Proceed with Full Rollout

The evidence supports expanding the new procedure to all remaining branches. The expected benefit is approximately **2 points per branch** on the service score index.

### Support Successful Implementation

1. **Allow a ramp-up period** – Benefits accumulate over 3–4 weeks as staff gain experience
2. **Provide implementation guidance** – Share best practices from early-adopting branches
3. **Monitor during transition** – Track scores weekly for the first 8 weeks at each new branch

### Continue Evaluation

1. **Extend data collection** through Week 30+ to confirm effects persist
2. **Investigate control branch decline** – Understand why non-switched branches saw scores drop
3. **Review at 6 months** – Assess whether the 2-point gain is sustained long-term

---
