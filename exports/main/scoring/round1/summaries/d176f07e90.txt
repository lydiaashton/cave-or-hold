# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2025  
**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Procedure

---

### **Bottom Line**

The new rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2.4 points (5%)** in branches that adopted it. While the result is not statistically significant at conventional levels, the effect is **consistently positive across all post-switch weeks**, and the pre-switch trends between switched and non-switched branches were nearly identical. We **recommend proceeding with a phased system-wide rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring.

---

### **Key Findings**

**Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** served as controls
- Service scores average ~50 points; higher scores indicate better service

**Results Summary**

| | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|---|---|---|
| **Before week 15** | 49.21 | 49.38 |
| **After week 15** | 51.24 | 48.97 |
| **Change** | **+2.03** | **-0.42** |

**Net treatment effect: +2.45 points** (difference-in-differences estimate)

**Statistical Confidence**
- **95% Confidence Interval:** -0.5 to +5.4 points
- **P-value:** ~0.10 (marginally significant)
- **Interpretation:** We estimate a positive effect, but the sample size limits our ability to rule out smaller effects or no effect with high confidence

**Supporting Evidence**
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, switched and non-switched branches had virtually identical service scores (difference of only 0.18 points)
- **Consistent post-switch performance:** Switched branches outperformed non-switched branches in **all 8 weeks** after the change
- **No confounding factors:** The effect persists after controlling for staffing levels and visit volume

---

### **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased rollout** of the new procedure to all branches, with the following conditions:

1. **Implement in waves** over the next 2–3 months to allow for troubleshooting and adjustment
2. **Continue data collection** for at least 12 additional weeks to strengthen statistical evidence
3. **Establish implementation standards** to ensure consistent adoption across branches
4. **Set a review checkpoint** after one quarter to confirm the effect persists at scale

---

### **Rationale**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---|---|
| **Direction of effect** | ✅ Positive (service scores improved) |
| **Magnitude** | ✅ Meaningful (~5% improvement) |
| **Consistency** | ✅ Strong (positive in all 8 post-switch weeks) |
| **Statistical certainty** | ⚠️ Moderate (p ≈ 0.10) |
| **Risk of harm** | ✅ Low (no negative effects observed) |
| **Cost of reversal** | ⚠️ Moderate (training and adjustment required) |

Given the **low risk of harm**, **consistent positive signal**, and **operational importance** of service quality, the evidence supports moving forward while gathering additional data.
