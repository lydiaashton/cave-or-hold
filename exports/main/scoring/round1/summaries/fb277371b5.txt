# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect** on service scores (+1.9 points), consistent with our expectations. However, the evidence is **not yet conclusive**. We recommend a **phased expansion** to additional branches while continuing to collect data, rather than an immediate system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Tested**
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- We compared service score changes before and after the switch between these two groups

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 53.0 | 57.9 | +4.9 |
| Branches with old procedure | 48.5 | 51.5 | +3.1 |
| **Effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

- The new procedure is associated with a **1.9-point improvement** in service scores
- This effect is in the **expected direction** but carries statistical uncertainty
- The analysis meets key validity checks (parallel pre-trends between groups)

### **Context**
- System-wide average service score is approximately 50
- A 1.9-point gain represents roughly a **4% improvement**
- Both groups improved after week 15, suggesting favorable seasonal or system-wide factors

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased expansion:**

1. **Expand the pilot** to 10–15 additional branches in the next quarter
2. **Continue monitoring** all pilot branches for 3–6 months
3. **Reconvene the board** with updated results to decide on system-wide rollout

This approach balances the positive signal we're seeing with appropriate caution given the current uncertainty.
