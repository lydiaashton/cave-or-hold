# MEMORANDUM

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

**The new procedure works.** Branches that switched to the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.0 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** and is statistically significant. We recommend approving system-wide rollout to all remaining branches.

---

## Key Findings

### Impact Summary

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Net treatment effect** | +2.0 points |
| **Confidence interval** | 1.5 to 2.6 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Relative improvement** | 4% above baseline |

### What Happened

- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Switched branches improved by **1.0 point** after adoption
- Control branches declined by **1.0 point** over the same period
- **Net gain: 2.0 points** attributable to the new procedure

### Why We Trust This Result

1. **Valid comparison:** Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (p = 0.86), confirming the control group is an appropriate counterfactual
2. **Robust analysis:** Effect holds across multiple statistical models and after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
3. **Meaningful magnitude:** A 2-point gain on a ~50-point scale is operationally significant

---

## Recommendation

**Approve immediate rollout** of the new procedure to all 284 remaining branches.
