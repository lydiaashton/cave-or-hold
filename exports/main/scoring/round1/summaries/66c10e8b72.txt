# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores increase by approximately **1.9 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **3.7% improvement** on the service score index. The effect is statistically significant and appears to grow over time as staff gain experience.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Had a Positive, Measurable Impact

| Period | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches | Gap |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|-----|
| Before (Weeks 6–14) | 51.64 | 50.18 | 1.46 |
| After (Weeks 15–22) | 52.36 | 49.00 | 3.36 |
| **Change** | **+0.72** | **-1.18** | **+1.90** |

Switched branches maintained and slightly improved their performance, while non-switched branches experienced a decline. The new procedure appears to have **protected branches from declining service levels**.

### 2. Benefits Grow Over Time

| Weeks After Switch | Estimated Effect |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Week 15 (first week) | +1.10 points |
| Week 16–17 | +1.2 to +1.4 points |
| Week 18–20 | +2.0 to +2.3 points |
| Week 21–22 | +2.4 to +2.6 points |

This pattern suggests a **learning curve**—full benefits may not be realized until several weeks after implementation.

### 3. Results Are Robust

- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.34 to 2.45 points
- **Sample:** 709 branches (425 switched, 284 control), 17 weeks of data
- Effect remains unchanged when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** to the remaining 284 branches.

2. **Plan for a transition period:** Expect 2–4 weeks for staff to become fully proficient.

3. **Monitor implementation:** Track service scores at newly switched branches to confirm they follow the same improvement trajectory.

4. **Document best practices:** Share insights from high-performing branches during rollout.
