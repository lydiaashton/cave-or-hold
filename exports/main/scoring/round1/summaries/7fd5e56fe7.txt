# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure is associated with a **modest improvement in service scores of approximately 2 points** (about 4% relative to baseline). This effect is **marginally statistically significant** (p ≈ 0.10) using rigorous methods that account for branch-level differences. The evidence suggests the new procedure has a positive effect on service quality.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout to all branches while continuing to monitor results.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 retained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** collected (weeks 6–22)
- Procedure switch occurred at **week 15**

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** (20 branches) | 51.13 | 52.84 | **+1.71** |
| **Old Procedure** (14 branches) | 52.56 | 52.36 | **-0.20** |

**Net Effect (Difference-in-Differences): +1.91 points**

Branches that switched to the new procedure improved by approximately 2 points more than branches that kept the old procedure, after accounting for underlying trends.

### **Statistical Confidence**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Effect Size | +2.0 points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.4 to +4.4 points |
| P-value | 0.096 |
| Statistical Significance | Marginal (significant at 10% level) |

The confidence interval includes zero, indicating some uncertainty. However, the point estimate is positive and consistent across multiple model specifications. With less conservative standard errors, the result reaches conventional significance (p = 0.02).

### **Validation Checks**

✓ **Parallel trends:** Treatment and control groups showed similar patterns before the switch  
✓ **Staffing correlation:** Staffing levels strongly predict service scores (p = 0.003), confirming the measure captures real service quality  
✓ **Branch differences:** Significant variation exists between branches (p < 0.001), validating our use of branch fixed effects

## **Technical Appendix**

**Methodology:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects and clustered standard errors (clustered by branch).

**Model Results (PanelOLS with Entity Fixed Effects):**
- Treatment effect coefficient: 1.997
- Robust standard error: 1.199
- P-value: 0.096
- Observations: 578 (34 branches × 17 weeks)

**Data Notes:**
- Service score is an index where ~50 represents average performance; higher scores indicate better service
- Staffing and visits indices are scaled with 0 as the average
- All branches have complete data for the study period
