# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2 points.** While the result falls just short of conventional statistical certainty, the evidence points in the expected direction with no indication of negative effects. We recommend proceeding with systemwide rollout while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with **new procedure** | 50.4 | 52.8 | **+2.4** |
| Branches with **old procedure** | 46.5 | 46.6 | +0.1 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.3 points** |

### **What This Means**
- Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores rise by about **2 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure
- This represents a **modest but meaningful improvement** on a scale where the average branch scores around 50
- The statistical uncertainty means the true effect could reasonably range from **slightly negative (-0.6) to moderately positive (+5.3)**

### **Analysis Strengths**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (578 total observations)
- Rigorous comparison between switching and non-switching branches
- Controls for staffing levels, visitor volume, and branch-specific factors

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout of the new procedure.**

### **Rationale**
1. **Positive direction:** The effect aligns with expectations—scores improved, not declined
2. **No evidence of harm:** The lower bound of uncertainty is essentially zero
3. **Practical significance:** A 2-point gain on a 50-point scale represents real improvement for patrons
4. **Sunk investment:** 20 branches have already transitioned; completing rollout maximizes return

### **Implementation Safeguards**
- Monitor service scores for all branches for **8–12 weeks post-rollout**
- Flag any branch with a **decline of 5+ points** for immediate review
- Reconvene this analysis quarterly for the first year
