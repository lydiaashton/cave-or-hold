# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure appears to have **moderately benefited service scores**, but the evidence is not statistically conclusive. Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a smaller decline in service scores compared to branches that kept the old procedure. However, we cannot rule out that this difference occurred by chance. **We recommend a cautious, phased rollout with continued monitoring.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** total: 20 adopted the new procedure (treatment group), 14 kept the old procedure (control group)
- Data covers **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at **week 15**
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach, comparing changes in service scores before and after the switch between the two groups

### **Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** (n=20) | 54.27 | 52.69 | **-1.58** |
| **Control branches** (n=14) | 50.35 | 46.84 | **-3.52** |

- **Estimated effect of new procedure: +1.94 points** (switched branches declined 1.94 points less than control branches)
- **95% confidence interval: -0.62 to +4.50 points**
- **Not statistically significant** (p = 0.14)

### **Interpretation**

1. **Both groups saw service score declines** after week 15, suggesting broader factors (seasonal, operational, or external) affected all branches
2. **The new procedure appears to have cushioned the decline**—switched branches fell less than control branches
3. **The effect is imprecisely estimated**—the true impact could range from slightly negative (-0.6) to meaningfully positive (+4.5)
4. **Pre-switch trends were parallel**, supporting the validity of our comparison

### **Context**

- Average branch service score is approximately 50
- A 2-point improvement would represent about **4% of the average score**
- The control group's larger decline (-3.5 points) raises questions about external factors affecting service during this period

---

## **Next Steps**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Proceed with cautious expansion** | The point estimate is positive and practically meaningful, even if not statistically significant |
| **2** | **Extend the evaluation period** | More post-switch data will increase statistical power and clarify whether effects persist |
| **3** | **Investigate the overall decline** | Understanding why all branches saw drops would help isolate the procedure's true effect |
| **4** | **Monitor implementation fidelity** | Variation in how branches execute the new procedure could mask true effects |
| **5** | **Consider complementary metrics** | Examine staff efficiency, patron satisfaction, and circulation rates alongside service scores |
