# **Library Cooperative Board Summary**

## **New Branch Procedure Evaluation**

---

### **Bottom Line**

**Roll out the new procedure to all branches.** The new rotation and shelving procedure increased service scores by **2.35 points** (5% improvement). The result is statistically significant, and 80% of early-adopter branches saw gains.

---

### **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +2.35 points (p < 0.01) |
| **Branches with gains** | 16 of 20 switched branches (80%) |
| **Comparison group improvement** | Only 57% of control branches improved |
| **Analysis method** | Difference-in-differences with branch/week controls |

**Why we're confident:**
- Switched and non-switched branches had parallel trends before the change
- The effect held consistently across most post-implementation weeks
- Results controlled for staffing levels, visit volumes, and branch-specific factors

---

### **Next Steps**

1. **Approve cooperative-wide rollout** to the 14 branches still using the old procedure
2. **Set implementation timeline** (recommended: within next quarter)
3. **Track service scores** for 8–12 weeks post-conversion at each branch
4. **Review outliers** — 4 switched branches did not improve; investigate barriers

---

### **Decision Requested**

**Vote to authorize system-wide adoption of the new rotation and shelving procedure.**

---

*Analysis based on 17 weeks of data from 34 branches (578 observations). Full technical appendix available.*
