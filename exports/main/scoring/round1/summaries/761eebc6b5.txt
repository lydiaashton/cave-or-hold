# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** and is statistically significant. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Impact on Service Scores
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+2.05 points** |
| 95% confidence interval | +0.3 to +3.8 points |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.02 |
| Relative improvement | ~4% |

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 maintained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** collected (weeks 6–22); procedure change implemented at week 15
- Analysis accounts for branch-specific characteristics, staffing levels, and visitor volume
- Staffing and visitor patterns did not differ between groups, confirming the improvement is attributable to the procedure change

### Trend Analysis
Service score trends for treatment and control branches moved similarly before the switch, supporting the validity of the comparison. After the switch, treatment branches showed sustained improvement relative to control branches.

---

## **Recommendations**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches
2. **Develop implementation timeline** with phased adoption to allow for support and monitoring
3. **Create training materials** documenting the new procedure for staff at transitioning branches
4. **Monitor service scores** for 8–12 weeks post-implementation at each branch to confirm expected improvements
5. **Schedule follow-up review** in 6 months to assess long-term sustainability of gains

---

## **Questions?**

The analysis team is available to present these findings in person and address any questions about the methodology or results.

---

*Analysis based on weekly service score data from 34 branches, weeks 6–22. Difference-in-differences model with branch fixed effects.*
