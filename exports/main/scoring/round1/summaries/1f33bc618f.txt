# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. We estimate a **+2.4 point improvement**, but the data cannot rule out that the true effect is zero. **We recommend extending the pilot period before approving a system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Analyzed
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (February through June)
- **20 branches** implemented the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** continued standard operations
- Service scores are calculated weekly (average branch scores ~50; higher is better)

### What We Found

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with New Procedure | 46.0 | 47.1 | +1.1 |
| Branches with Old Procedure | 51.4 | 50.1 | −1.3 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.4** |

### What This Means
- The **estimated improvement is 2.4 points** (approximately 5% relative to average scores)
- However, the **95% confidence interval ranges from −0.15 to +4.59 points**
- This means we **cannot confidently distinguish** the effect from zero
- The result is **not statistically significant** (p = 0.11)

### Why the Uncertainty?
- The pilot period was relatively short (8 weeks post-switch)
- Natural week-to-week variation in service scores is substantial
- The sample size limits our ability to detect modest effects

---

## **Recommendation**

**Do not approve system-wide rollout at this time.** Instead:

1. **Continue the pilot** for an additional 8–12 weeks to gather more data
2. **Monitor implementation** to ensure switched branches are following the new procedure consistently
3. **Reconvene in Q2 2027** with expanded data to make a final decision

---

## **Decision Criteria for Future Review**

We recommend the board establish clear thresholds for rollout approval:

| Outcome | Recommendation |
|---------|----------------|
| Effect > 3 points, statistically significant | Proceed with rollout |
| Effect 1–3 points, statistically significant | Consider phased rollout |
| Effect not statistically significant | Extend pilot or discontinue |
