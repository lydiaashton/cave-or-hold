# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure showed a **2.6-point improvement in service scores** (approximately 5% relative to baseline). While this result is not statistically definitive, the evidence points in the expected direction with no apparent negative effects. We **recommend proceeding with a systemwide rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **20 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Service scores average approximately 50 points; higher scores indicate better service quality

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 46.9 | 49.4 | **+2.5** |
| Branches with old procedure | 44.9 | 44.9 | **0.0** |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.6 points** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Estimated effect:** +2.6 points
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.6 to +5.7 points
- **Interpretation:** The data are most consistent with a modest improvement, though we cannot rule out no effect

### Supporting Evidence
- **Pre-trend validation:** Switched and control branches showed similar trends before the change, supporting a valid comparison
- **Branch-level results:** 13 of 20 switched branches (65%) improved, versus 7 of 14 control branches (50%)
- **No negative effects detected:** No switched branches showed substantial declines attributable to the procedure

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Roll out new procedure to remaining 14 branches | Next 4–6 weeks |
| 2 | Continue weekly service score tracking for all branches | Ongoing |
| 3 | Investigate why 7 switched branches did not improve | 60 days |
| 4 | Review staffing and visit metrics for additional benefits | 90 days |
| 5 | Board review of full-year results | Q1 2027 |

---

## **Risks and Limitations**

- **Statistical uncertainty:** The observed effect could partially reflect random variation (11% probability of seeing this result if there were no true effect)
- **Limited observation window:** Only 8 weeks of post-implementation data; longer-term sustainability is unproven
- **Contextual factors:** Unmeasured variables (e.g., seasonal demand, staff turnover) could influence scores, though the control group accounts for systemwide trends

---

## **Conclusion**

The new rotation and shelving procedure shows promise for improving service quality across the cooperative. The evidence supports moving forward with implementation while maintaining vigilance through continued measurement. We will provide an updated assessment after one full quarter of systemwide operation.
