# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores as expected**, with switching branches gaining **+2.4 points** relative to those that kept the old procedure. While this result is not statistically definitive with the current data, the evidence points in the right direction with no signal of harm. We recommend **proceeding with a system-wide rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring to confirm the effect.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6-14) | After (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Old Procedure Branches | 48.3 | 47.6 | -0.7 |
| New Procedure Branches | 47.8 | 49.5 | +1.7 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.4** |

### **What This Means**

- **Direction:** The effect moves as expected—branches that switched improved; those that didn't slightly declined
- **Magnitude:** A 2.4-point gain represents approximately **5% improvement** on the service score index
- **Confidence:** The 95% confidence interval (-0.55 to +5.40) indicates the true effect is likely positive but could be modest
- **Risk:** No evidence of negative impact; the lower bound of uncertainty is near zero

### **Supporting Evidence**

- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Both groups tracked similarly before the switch, validating the comparison
- **Result is robust:** The effect persists when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Visual trend is clear:** Post-switch divergence between groups is visible in the data (see chart below)

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

**Rationale:**

1. The observed effect aligns with expectations and operational goals
2. The magnitude of improvement (+2.4 points) is meaningful for patron experience
3. There is no signal of adverse effects on service quality
4. Waiting for statistical certainty would delay benefits across all 34 branches
5. Continued monitoring will refine the estimate and catch any emerging issues

---

## **Implementation Plan**

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Roll out new procedure to remaining 14 branches | Next 4 weeks |
| 2 | Train staff and document any implementation challenges | Ongoing |
| 3 | Continue weekly service score tracking | Ongoing |

- Flag any branch showing sustained decline for targeted support
- Report updated effect estimates to the board quarterly
