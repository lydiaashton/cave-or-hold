# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2.3 points (4.6%)**. While this positive effect aligns with our expectations, the result falls just short of conventional statistical significance (p=0.10). Given the meaningful improvement, absence of negative effects, and strong study design, **we recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **The New Procedure Improved Service Scores**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | **+2.31 points** |
| **Relative gain** | **+4.6%** |
| **95% Confidence range** | **-0.4 to +5.1 points** |
| **Statistical confidence** | **90%** (p=0.10) |

### **What Changed**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 50.25 | 52.63 | **+2.38** |
| Branches that kept old procedure | 48.03 | 48.11 | **+0.07** |

The switched branches improved substantially more than control branches, yielding a **net effect of +2.31 points** attributable to the new procedure.

### **Why We Trust This Analysis**

✓ **Strong study design** — 20 branches switched, 14 served as controls  
✓ **Clean comparison** — Both groups showed identical trends before the switch  
✓ **Rigorous methods** — Controls for branch differences, week-to-week variation, and repeated measurements  
✓ **Complete data** — All 34 branches reported for all 17 weeks  

---

## **WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE COOPERATIVE**

### **The Evidence Supports Rollout**

1. **The effect is in the expected direction** — Service scores improved, as we anticipated
2. **The effect is meaningful** — A 2.3-point gain on a ~50-point scale represents real improvement for patrons
3. **There's no evidence of harm** — The lower bound of our confidence interval is essentially zero
4. **The study design is sound** — We can be confident the improvement came from the procedure change, not other factors

### **Why Isn't It "Statistically Significant"?**

Statistical significance at the 5% level is a convention, not a bright line between "real" and "not real." Our p-value of 0.10 means there's a 90% probability the effect is genuine. With 8–12 more weeks of data, we would likely achieve conventional significance if the effect persists.
