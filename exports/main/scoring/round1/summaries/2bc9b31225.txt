# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **promising but inconclusive evidence** of improving service scores. We estimate a **2.2-point increase** (approximately 4% improvement), but statistical uncertainty means we cannot confidently rule out no effect. We recommend **continuing the pilot program with expanded monitoring** before committing to a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Analysis compares changes in service scores between the two groups before and after the switch

### Main Results

| Measure | Estimate |
|---------|----------|
| Effect on service score | **+2.18 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **−0.34 to +4.70 points** |
| Statistical Significance | **p = 0.09** (marginally significant) |
| Baseline average score | **49.4 points** |
| Estimated new average | **51.6 points** |
| Relative improvement | **~4.4%** |

### What the Numbers Mean

The confidence interval tells us the true effect could reasonably be:
- As low as a **0.3-point decrease** (no meaningful impact)
- As high as a **4.7-point increase** (substantial improvement)

Because the interval includes zero, we lack definitive proof that the procedure causes improvement.

### Supporting Evidence

| Indicator | Finding |
|-----------|---------|
| **Branch-level outcomes** | 70% of switched branches improved vs. 50% of control branches |
| **Pre-switch trends** | Treatment and control groups moved in parallel before week 15 ✓ |
| **Post-switch performance** | Switched branches outperformed controls in 6 of 8 weeks |
| **Effect consistency** | Positive effects observed across most post-switch weeks |

---

## **Interpretation**

**Reasons for cautious optimism:**
- Point estimate is positive and meaningful (4% improvement)
- Majority of switched branches showed improvement
- Pre-trends support the validity of the comparison
- Effect direction is consistent across most post-switch weeks

**Reasons for caution:**
- Statistical evidence does not meet conventional significance thresholds
- Substantial week-to-week variability in scores
- Limited post-switch data (only 8 weeks)
- Some pre-period weeks showed elevated differences between groups

---

## **Recommendations**

### Immediate Actions (Next 90 Days)

1. **Continue the pilot** — Maintain the new procedure at the 20 current branches and keep collecting data

2. **Extend monitoring period** — Gather at least 8–12 additional weeks of data to improve estimate precision

3. **Document implementation** — Capture operational details (staff time, training costs, workflow changes) to enable cost-benefit analysis

### Medium-Term Actions (6 Months)

4. **Consider limited expansion** — Add 5–10 branches to the pilot to test the procedure across different branch types and increase statistical power

5. **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** — Quantify implementation costs against the estimated service score improvement

6. **Investigate heterogeneity** — Identify whether certain branch characteristics (size, location, traffic) predict stronger effects

### Decision Point

**Reconvene in Q2 2026** with updated analysis. Proceed with system-wide rollout if:
- The effect remains positive with additional data
- Confidence interval excludes zero (or narrows substantially)
- Cost-benefit analysis supports the investment

---

## **Data Quality Notes**

- Complete data with no missing values
- All 34 branches have observations for all 17 weeks
- Service scores range from 23 to 90 (average ~50)
- Analysis accounts for correlation within branches using cluster-robust standard errors
