# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure is associated with a **1.7-point improvement in service scores** (approximately 3% above baseline). While the statistical evidence is suggestive rather than definitive, the effect is positive, consistent across post-switch weeks, and meaningful for patron experience. We recommend **proceeding with a phased rollout** to all branches while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Analysis compares changes in service scores between the two groups before and after the switch

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 50.89 | 50.92 | +0.03 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 52.01 | 53.79 | +1.78 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.75** |

- **Estimated improvement:** 1.75 points on the service score index
- **Relative gain:** ~3.3% above the baseline average of 52
- **Statistical confidence:** Moderate (p ≈ 0.10); the effect is positive and consistent but would benefit from additional data to reach conventional significance thresholds

### Why We Trust This Analysis
- **Valid comparison:** Before the switch, there was no significant difference in trends between the two groups, supporting our analytical approach
- **Controls accounted for:** The effect holds even after adjusting for staffing levels and branch visit volumes
- **Consistent pattern:** The improvement persisted across all post-switch weeks (15–22)

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Begin phased rollout** to remaining branches | Positive effect with practical significance; low risk of harm |
| **2** | **Monitor scores for 8–12 weeks** post-rollout | Confirms effect at scale and increases statistical confidence |
| **3** | **Document implementation practices** from early adopters | Captures lessons learned to support training and consistency |
| **4** | **Set clear success metrics** for full adoption | Example: sustain 1.5+ point improvement over two quarters |

---

## **Questions for the Board**

1. Should we prioritize rollout to specific branch types first (e.g., high-traffic vs. low-traffic)?
2. What timeline is feasible for full cooperative adoption?
3. Are there additional metrics beyond service scores the board would like tracked during rollout?
