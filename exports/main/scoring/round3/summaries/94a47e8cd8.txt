# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. The estimated improvement is approximately **2 points** (about 4% of baseline), but the evidence is **not statistically significant at conventional levels** (p = 0.10). The data is consistent with effects ranging from no impact to a meaningful improvement of up to 4 points.

**Recommendation:** Do not proceed with full rollout yet. Instead, **continue monitoring for 8–12 additional weeks** to reduce uncertainty before making a system-wide decision. If operational pressures favor a decision now, consider a **phased rollout** with continued evaluation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches**: 20 adopted new procedure, 14 retained old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22); switch occurred at week 15
- **Only 8 weeks of post-switch data** per branch

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** | 51.13 | 52.84 | +1.71 |
| **Old Procedure** | 52.56 | 52.36 | -0.20 |

**Net Effect: +1.91 points**

### **Statistical Evidence**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Effect Estimate | +2.00 points | Modest positive effect |
| P-value | 0.096 | Not significant at 5% level |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.36 to +4.35 | **Includes zero** |

**What this means:** We cannot rule out that the true effect is zero (no impact) or even slightly negative. The data suggests a positive effect, but more evidence is needed for confidence.

### **Why the Uncertainty?**

1. **Limited post-switch data** — Only 8 weeks after the change
2. **Modest sample size** — 34 branches total
3. **Natural variation** — Service scores fluctuate week-to-week

## **Recommendations**

### **Option 1: Gather More Evidence (Recommended)**
- Continue collecting data for 8–12 additional weeks
- Re-analyze with 16–20 weeks of post-switch data
- This would substantially narrow the confidence interval
- Decision timeline: 2–3 months

### **Option 2: Phased Rollout**
- Implement at 5–7 additional branches now
- Keep remaining control branches for continued comparison
- Monitor closely and be prepared to adjust
- Balances action with ongoing learning

### **Option 3: Full Rollout**
- Implement at all remaining branches
- **Risk:** If the effect is actually zero or negative, resources were spent without benefit
- **Mitigation:** Continue tracking service scores to detect any decline

---

## **Additional Considerations**

**Staffing matters:** Staffing levels are strongly associated with service scores (p = 0.003). Regardless of the shelving procedure decision, adequate staffing appears critical for service quality.

**Pre-existing differences:** Treatment branches scored slightly lower than control branches before the switch (about 1.5 points on average). The analysis accounts for this, but it adds uncertainty.

**Practical significance:** Even if the 2-point effect is real, the board should consider whether this improvement justifies the operational costs of the new procedure.

**Methodology:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects and clustered standard errors.

**Model:** PanelOLS with entity fixed effects, controls for staffing and visits indices.
