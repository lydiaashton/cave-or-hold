# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows evidence of a modest improvement in service scores, but the effect is uncertain.** Our analysis found a statistically significant increase of **1.75 points** (p=0.020), but the 95% confidence interval [0.28, 3.21] indicates the true effect could be as small as 0.3 points or as large as 3.2 points.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but with measured expectations and monitoring.**

**Rationale:**
- The statistical evidence points to a **genuine positive effect** (p=0.020, lower CI bound above zero)
- Even the conservative estimate (~0.3 points) represents a non-negative outcome
- The methodology is sound (parallel trends confirmed, robust to controls)
- Service quality improvements, even modest ones, accumulate in patron satisfaction over time

**Caveats:**
- Do **not** expect dramatic transformation—this is a incremental improvement
- Implementation costs should be **modest** for this to be worthwhile
- Monitor results closely; be prepared to adjust if expected gains don't materialize

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Main Result**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.75 points** | Average improvement |
| 95% Confidence interval | **[0.28, 3.21]** | True effect likely in this range |
| p-value | **0.020** | Statistically significant at 5% level |
| Effect size | **0.17 SD** | Small-to-moderate |

### **What This Means**
- **Best case**: ~3.2 point improvement (6% of average score)
- **Most likely**: ~1.75 point improvement (3.3% of average score)  
- **Worst case (still positive)**: ~0.3 point improvement (0.5% of average score)

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches**: 20 switched to new procedure, 14 kept old procedure
- **17 weeks**: Weeks 6–14 (before), Weeks 15–22 (after)
- **Method**: Difference-in-Differences with branch and week fixed effects

### **Validity Assessment**

| Check | Result | Implication |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Parallel trends (pre-switch) | ✓ Passed (p=0.86) | Groups were comparable before switch |
| Robust to controls | ✓ Effect stable | Not explained by staffing/visits |
| Consistency across weeks | ⚠ 7 of 8 weeks positive | One week showed negative effect |
| Confidence interval | ⚠ Lower bound near zero | Effect could be very small |

### **Score Changes by Group**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.26 | 56.37 | +6.10 |
| Control branches | 50.60 | 54.96 | +4.36 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+1.75** |

## **Next Steps**

### **If Proceeding with Rollout**
1. **Phase implementation** across remaining 14 branches over 2–3 months
2. **Set success threshold**: Minimum 0.5 point improvement per branch
3. **Track weekly scores** for 8–12 weeks post-switch at each branch
4. **Document implementation** details to identify best practices

### **If Seeking More Certainty**
- Extend observation period to narrow confidence intervals
- Consider cost-benefit analysis if implementation costs are substantial
- Pilot at 3–5 branches first before full commitment

### **Expected Outcome**
If the estimated effect holds, system-wide scores would rise from ~53 to ~55 points. Given the confidence interval, actual improvement could range from negligible (~0.3 points) to meaningful (~3 points).
