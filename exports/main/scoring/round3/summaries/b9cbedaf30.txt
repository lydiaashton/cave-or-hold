# **Library Cooperative Procedure Change: Service Score Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The data does not provide statistically reliable evidence that the new procedure changed service scores.** The best estimate suggests a modest improvement of about **2 points** (roughly 4% of the average score), but this result could plausibly be due to random variation. The 95% confidence interval ranges from **-0.7 to +4.4 points**, meaning the true effect could be slightly negative, zero, or moderately positive.

**Recommendation:** The board faces a decision under uncertainty. There is **no evidence of harm**, and the weight of evidence (approximately 92% probability) leans toward a positive effect. If the new procedure offers operational benefits beyond service scores, a continued rollout is reasonable — but expectations should be modest, and monitoring is essential.

| Measure | Value | Interpretation |

### Why We Can't Be More Certain

- **Small sample size:** Only 34 branches limits statistical power
- **Short observation window:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data
- **Minimum detectable effect:** This study could reliably detect changes of 4+ points; smaller effects require more data
