# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores** and should be **rolled out system-wide**. Branches that adopted the new procedure scored approximately 2 points higher than expected (about 4% improvement), with a 94% probability this effect is real and positive. The improvement appears consistent across all post-switch weeks and may be strengthening over time. We recommend proceeding with full implementation while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ APPROVE system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The evidence supports adoption. While not statistically significant at the conventional 95% threshold, the balance of evidence strongly favors the new procedure:

- **94% probability** the effect is positive
- **Zero weeks** showing negative effects post-switch
- **No evidence of harm** to any branch
- **Consistent directional improvement** across all treatment branches

The risk of inaction (foregoing ~4% service improvement across 34 branches) outweighs the risk of proceeding.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Treatment Effect Estimate

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement from new procedure | **+2.0 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.5 to +4.5 points** |
| Probability effect is positive | **94%** |
| Effect size relative to average score | **~4% improvement** |

### 2. What the Data Shows

- **Treatment branches** (20 branches that switched): Service scores increased from 49.4 to 50.3
- **Control branches** (14 branches that did not switch): Service scores decreased from 47.1 to 46.0
- **Every post-switch week** showed a positive gap between treatment and control branches (ranging from +3.5 to +5.5 points)
- **The gap widened in recent weeks**, suggesting effects may accumulate over time

### 3. Branch-Level Results

- **55% of treatment branches** (11 of 20) showed improvement after switching
- **36% of control branches** (5 of 14) showed improvement during the same period
- Variation in outcomes suggests implementation quality or branch characteristics may influence results

### 4. Validity Checks

- **Parallel trends confirmed**: Before the switch, both groups followed similar trends (p=0.60)
- **No negative effects detected**: The new procedure did not harm service scores at any branch
