# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive signal** (+2 points on average) but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive**. Given the uncertainty and the fact that 30% of switching branches saw declines, we recommend **expanding the pilot** rather than immediate systemwide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Treatment Effect**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+2.0 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.5 to +4.8 points |
| P-value | 0.11 |
| Branches showing improvement | **14 of 20 (70%)** |

### **Service Score Changes**
| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| **Switched branches** (new procedure) | 47.9 | 49.6 | **+1.7** |
| Non-switched branches (old procedure) | 51.1 | 50.8 | -0.3 |

### **Branch-Level Variation**
- **Best performer:** +6.8 points
- **Worst performer:** -5.1 points
- **6 of 20 switched branches (30%)** saw scores decline

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the procedure improve scores?** | Probably, but not certain |
| **Is the evidence strong enough for systemwide rollout?** | **No** |
| **Should we abandon the procedure?** | **No** — the signal is promising |
| **Recommended path forward** | **Expand pilot, then decide** |

### **Reasoning**

1. **The effect could be zero.** The confidence interval includes zero, meaning the true effect might be negligible.

2. **30% of branches struggled.** A systemwide rollout risks harming service at roughly 10 of 34 branches.

3. **More data is inexpensive.** Adding 8 weeks of observation across an expanded pilot would narrow the confidence interval substantially.

4. **The signal is worth pursuing.** A +2 point improvement would be meaningful if real. Premature abandonment would be wasteful.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Expand the pilot to 10 additional branches for 8 weeks, then reassess.**

| Action | Timeline | Success Metric |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| Add 10 branches to pilot | Weeks 1–2 | Implementation complete |
| Collect post-switch data | Weeks 3–10 | 8 weeks per branch |
| Analyze combined results | Week 11 | P-value < 0.05 or CI excludes zero |
| Board decision on rollout | Week 12 | Go/No-Go based on evidence |

---

## **What We'll Know After Expansion**

With ~30 branches in the treatment group and 16+ weeks of post-switch data:

- Confidence interval should narrow to approximately **±1.0 point**
- We can identify **branch characteristics** that predict success
- We can document **implementation practices** at high-performing branches
