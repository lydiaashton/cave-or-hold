# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation procedure shows a **positive association with service scores**, but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive**. Branches that switched improved by approximately 2.6 points more than those that did not, representing a 5% gain. However, due to limited data, we cannot rule out chance as an explanation. We recommend **continued monitoring with a cautious phased expansion** rather than immediate system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Show**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Estimated improvement from new procedure | **+2.6 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.4 to +5.7 points** |
| Statistical significance | **p = 0.09** (not significant at p<0.05) |
| Practical significance | **~5% improvement** over baseline score of 49 |

### **How We Analyzed This**

- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- We compared service score changes in switching branches against non-switching branches (difference-in-differences design)
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories, validating our comparison

### **What This Means**

✓ **Encouraging signal:** All switched branches showed greater improvement than non-switched branches after week 15  
✓ **No negative effects:** The procedure did not harm service scores in any measurable way  
⚠️ **Uncertainty remains:** The confidence interval includes zero, so the true effect could be small or nonexistent  
⚠️ **Limited data:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch observations across 34 branches constrains our ability to detect smaller effects

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recommendation** | **Proceed with phased rollout — do not yet expand system-wide** |
| **Confidence** | **Moderate** — evidence is suggestive but not definitive |
| **Risk Assessment** | **Low** — no evidence of harm; upside potential exceeds downside |

**Rationale:**

1. **The signal is positive and consistent.** Every switched branch outperformed the control group after week 15.
2. **The uncertainty is quantifiable.** With 8–12 additional weeks of data, we could narrow the confidence interval sufficiently for a definitive conclusion.
3. **The cost of waiting exceeds the cost of cautious action.** If the effect is real, delaying rollout deprives patrons of better service. If the effect proves illusory, a phased approach limits exposure.

**What we are NOT recommending:**
- ❌ Immediate system-wide rollout (evidence too weak)
- ❌ Abandoning the new procedure (no evidence of harm, positive signal exists)
- ❌ Indefinite pilot status (actionable threshold has been met for expansion)

---

## **Next Steps**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |

### **Decision Triggers for Full Rollout**

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Effect remains positive with p < 0.05 | Proceed with system-wide rollout |
| Effect shrinks toward zero or turns negative | Halt expansion; investigate causes |
| Effect remains positive but p > 0.05 after 6 more months | Board decision based on cost-benefit |

---

## **Conclusion**

**Verdict: Cautious Go.** The new procedure earns a conditional approval for expanded use pending stronger evidence. This balances innovation with fiscal responsibility and positions the cooperative to act decisively once uncertainty is resolved.
