# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive trend but inconclusive evidence**. Switching branches improved by an estimated **1.6 points** more than non-switching branches, consistent with expectations. However, this result is **not statistically significant** at conventional standards, meaning we cannot confidently rule out that the observed improvement is due to random variation. 

**Recommendation:** Do not proceed with immediate system-wide rollout. Instead, extend the pilot period to gather more data before making a final decision.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **NOT READY FOR SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**
> 
> The evidence is **promising but insufficient**. Extend the pilot for 10–12 additional weeks, then re-evaluate.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Study Overview
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** available

### 2. Service Score Impact

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.4 | 51.6 | +2.3 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 47.9 | 51.8 | +3.9 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.6** |

- **Estimated benefit:** +1.62 service score points (approximately 3% improvement)
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.4 to +3.7 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.12 (not significant at p < 0.05)

### 3. What This Means

| Scenario | Plausibility |
|----------|--------------|
| New procedure has no effect | ✅ Consistent with data |
| New procedure improves scores by 1-3 points | ✅ Consistent with data |
| New procedure slightly harms scores | ⚠️ Unlikely but possible |

The data is **inconclusive**, not negative. The effect points in the expected direction, but we lack sufficient evidence to be confident.

### 4. Why the Uncertainty?
- **Limited post-switch data:** Only 8 weeks after the change
- **Natural variation:** Service scores fluctuate week-to-week
- **Sample size:** 34 branches provides moderate but not definitive statistical power

---

## **Recommendation**

**Extend the pilot and continue monitoring** before system-wide rollout:

1. **Continue current arrangement** for 8–12 additional weeks
2. **Maintain the control group** (branches using old procedure) during this period
3. **Re-analyze with more data** to reduce uncertainty
4. **Make rollout decision** once we have 16+ weeks of post-switch data

This approach minimizes the risk of adopting a procedure that may not deliver meaningful benefits while preserving the option to roll out if stronger evidence emerges.
