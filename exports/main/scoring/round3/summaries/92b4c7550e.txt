# **Board Memorandum (Revised)**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence for the new procedure's benefit is **inconclusive**. While switched branches showed modest improvement (+1.2 points), the statistical analysis does not confidently distinguish this from random variation (p ≈ 0.10; 95% confidence interval includes zero). **We recommend against a full rollout at this time.** Instead, we suggest extending the pilot with continued monitoring to gather more definitive evidence before making a systemwide decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What the Data Shows

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** (20) | 49.7 | 50.9 | +1.2 |
| **Non-switched branches** (14) | 49.6 | 48.9 | -0.8 |
| **Difference-in-differences** | — | — | **+1.9** |

### Statistical Certainty

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| **P-value** | 0.10 | Not statistically significant by conventional standards |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | -0.3 to +4.4 points | Includes zero — no effect cannot be ruled out |
| **Probability of positive effect** (bootstrap) | 95% | Suggestive, but not definitive |

### Critical Context

The estimated 1.9-point advantage comes from **two factors**:
1. Switched branches improved modestly (+1.2 points)
2. Non-switched branches declined (-0.8 points)

**Important question:** Did the new procedure help switched branches, or did something else hurt non-switched branches? The data cannot definitively answer this.

---

## **Recommendations**

### 1. **Do Not Proceed with Full Rollout** (at this time)
The evidence is too uncertain to justify systemwide implementation costs.

### 2. **Extend the Pilot Period**
- Continue monitoring all 34 branches for an additional 8–12 weeks
- This will double the post-implementation data and improve statistical power

### 3. **Investigate Control Group Decline**
The drop in non-switched branch scores warrants investigation. Possible causes:
- Seasonal effects
- Staffing changes
- External factors unrelated to the procedure

### 4. **Revisit Decision in 3 Months**
With more data, we can provide a more definitive recommendation.

---

## **What Would Change Our Recommendation?**

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|----------|----------------|
| Effect grows to +3 points with p < 0.05 | Proceed with rollout |
| Effect shrinks toward zero | Abandon the new procedure |
| Effect remains ~+2 points with p < 0.10 | Consider cost-benefit; phased rollout may be warranted |

---

## **Limitations**

- **Short observation window**: Only 8 weeks of post-implementation data
- **Modest sample size**: 34 branches limits statistical power
- **Unmeasured confounders**: Other changes during this period could affect results

---

## **Decision Request**

The Board is asked to:

1. **Decline full rollout** pending stronger evidence
2. **Authorize continued pilot monitoring** with monthly reporting
3. **Request follow-up analysis** in 3 months with recommendation

---

**Bottom line restated:** The new procedure *may* help, but we don't yet know with sufficient confidence. More data is needed before committing cooperative-wide resources.
