## **Bottom Line**

**The data do not provide statistically conclusive evidence that the new procedure improves service scores.** The estimated effect is positive (+2.1 points) and aligns with expectations, but the result is not statistically significant (p = 0.13). The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.5 to +4.6 points, meaning the true effect could be zero, slightly negative, or meaningfully positive.

**Recommendation:** Given the positive direction, absence of harm signals, and limited sample size, we recommend **continuing the pilot with expanded data collection** before deciding on full rollout.

### **What We Cannot Conclude**

✗ We **cannot confirm** the procedure improves scores  
✗ We **cannot rule out** no effect or minimal effect

The primary limitation is **sample size**. With 34 branches, the analysis lacks power to detect modest effects reliably. This is a data limitation, not evidence against the procedure.
