# **Library Cooperative Board Memorandum**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence **suggests the new procedure improves service scores**, but we lack statistical certainty. Branches that switched improved by an estimated **1.6 points** (about 3% above baseline) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. There is approximately a **93% probability the effect is positive**, and no evidence of negative side effects.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** to remaining branches while continuing to monitor service scores. The potential benefit appears to outweigh the risk, but we should verify the effect strengthens with more data.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches studied | 34 |
| Switched to new procedure | 20 |
| Kept old procedure | 14 |
| Weeks of data | 17 (weeks 6–22) |
| Switch implemented | Week 15 |

### Service Score Impact

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.4 | 51.6 | +2.3 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 47.9 | 51.8 | +3.9 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.6** |

- **Estimated effect:** +1.62 points (95% CI: -0.44 to +3.67)
- **Probability effect is positive:** ~93%
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.123 (not significant at conventional p < 0.05 threshold)

### Why Isn't This Statistically Significant?
The study has **limited statistical power**. With only 8 weeks of post-switch data, we could only reliably detect effects larger than ~2.9 points. The observed 1.6-point effect could be real but too modest to confirm with this sample size.

### Validity Checks Passed
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trends (p = 0.66)
- **No negative side effects:** Staffing levels and visitor volume were unaffected
