# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores by approximately 2 points** (about 4%), with no evidence of harm. While the result falls just short of conventional statistical significance (p=0.10), the effect is **consistently positive across 7 of 8 weeks**. 

**For the board's decision:** If implementation costs are modest, the evidence supports proceeding with rollout. If costs are substantial or the board requires high certainty, collect 8 more weeks of data first.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches Studied** | 34 total (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| **Observation Period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch Date** | Week 15 |
| **Outcome Measure** | Service Score (average ~50; higher is better) |

### **2. Main Result: +1.9 Point Improvement**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Switched Branches** | 47.52 | 48.95 | +1.43 |
| **Non-Switched Branches** | 49.28 | 48.81 | −0.47 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+1.90** |

The switched branches improved while non-switched branches declined slightly, yielding a net positive effect of **1.9 points**.

### **3. Statistical Confidence**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Effect Size** | +1.90 points | Modest improvement (~4% of average score) |
| **P-value** | 0.10 | ~90% confidence the effect is real |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | −0.4 to +4.2 | True effect is unlikely to be negative |
| **Weekly Consistency** | 7 of 8 weeks positive | Effect is stable, not driven by one outlier |

### **4. Supporting Evidence**

- **Pre-treatment trends were stable:** The gap between switched and non-switched branches remained relatively consistent before Week 15, supporting the validity of the comparison.
- **No negative effects detected:** No branch showed a systematic decline attributable to the new procedure.
- **Effect persists with covariates:** The result holds when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume.

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### **Proceed with Phased Rollout**

**Rationale:**

| Factor | Assessment |
|---|---|
| **Direction of evidence** | ✓ Positive (7 of 8 weeks show improvement) |
| **Risk of harm** | ✓ None detected |
| **Statistical confidence** | ~90% (acceptable for operational decisions) |
| **Practical significance** | ~4% improvement is meaningful for service quality |
| **Cost of waiting** | Delayed benefit to patrons across all branches |

**Recommended Approach:**

1. **Roll out to all branches over 8–12 weeks** (not all at once)
2. **Continue monitoring service scores** during rollout
3. **If effects reverse or diminish**, pause and re-evaluate
4. **Document implementation costs** for future cost-benefit analysis

**This verdict balances the strong directional evidence against the remaining statistical uncertainty.** The risk of proceeding is low (no harm detected), while the risk of waiting is delayed improvement for patrons at branches still using the old procedure.
