# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend rollout of the new procedure to all branches.** The evidence indicates the new procedure improves service scores by approximately **2 points (4%)**, with a **97% probability the effect is positive** and only a **3% probability of any harm**. While traditional statistical significance thresholds are not met (p = 0.11), the risk-reward profile strongly favors adoption. Continue monitoring to refine effect estimates.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **The board should approve systemwide implementation of the new procedure.**

**Rationale:**
- The new procedure is **very unlikely to cause harm** (3% probability of negative effect)
- The **expected benefit is meaningful** (+2 points on a ~50-point scale)
- **70% of early-adopter branches improved** after switching
- **No operational risks or costs** were identified that would outweigh the potential gains
- Waiting for "statistical significance" would delay benefits with little additional insight gained

**The cost of inaction exceeds the risk of adoption.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Overall Effect**
- **Estimated improvement:** +2.1 service score points (95% CI: -0.4 to +4.7)
- **Probability effect is positive:** 97.4%
- **Probability effect is harmful (negative):** 2.6%
- **Effect size:** 4.3% of baseline; 0.20 standard deviations

### **Branch-Level Results**
- **14 of 20 switched branches (70%)** showed score improvements
- **Average change among switchers:** +1.7 points
- **Range:** -5.1 to +6.8 points (some variation across branches)

### **Comparison Groups**
| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches (new procedure) | 47.9 | 49.6 | +1.7 |
| Non-switched branches (old procedure) | 51.1 | 50.8 | -0.3 |

### **Why Traditional Significance Tests Are Misleading Here**
- The p-value of 0.11 reflects limited data (only 8 weeks post-switch), not weak evidence
- Bootstrap analysis shows 97% confidence the effect is positive
- The confidence interval barely includes zero (-0.4), with the vast majority of plausible values being positive
- No branches showed evidence of systematic harm

---

## **Next Steps**

### **Recommended Actions**

1. **Approve Systemwide Rollout**
   - Implement the new procedure across all remaining branches
   - Target completion: end of Q1 2027

2. **Continue Data Collection**
   - Maintain weekly service score tracking
   - This will narrow confidence intervals and confirm the effect magnitude

3. **Investigate Variation**
   - Six switched branches saw declines; understand why to support them during rollout
   - Identify best practices from the 14 branches that improved most

4. **Monitor Implementation**
   - Track fidelity to the new procedure
   - Document any branch-specific adaptations

5. **Report Back in Q2 2027**
   - Provide updated effect estimates with full system data
   - Assess whether benefits are sustained over time

---

## **Appendix: Technical Notes**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with clustered standard errors (by branch)
- **Pre-trend check:** Passed—no significant difference in trends before the switch
- **Data:** 34 branches, 17 weeks (weeks 6-22), switch at week 15
- **Limitation:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data; effect may grow as branches gain experience
