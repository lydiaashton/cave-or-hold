# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores as expected**, with switching branches gaining approximately **+2 points** relative to non-switching branches. However, the evidence is **not statistically definitive** after 8 weeks of observation. We recommend **proceeding with the rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes to confirm the effect strengthens over time.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Had a Positive Effect

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Average service score improvement | **+1.9 points** |
| Baseline average score | ~50 points |
| Relative improvement | **~4%** |
| Statistical confidence | Marginal (p ≈ 0.10) |

The direction of the effect matches our expectation: branches that adopted the new procedure improved relative to those that did not.

### 2. Study Overview

- **34 branches** participated (20 switched, 14 did not)
- **17 weeks** of data collected (9 weeks before switch, 8 weeks after)
- Switch occurred at **Week 15**
- Analysis controls for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week variation

### 3. Service Score Trends

```
Before Switch (Weeks 6-14):
  • Control branches: 49.5 average
  • Treatment branches: 45.7 average
  • Gap: -3.8 points

After Switch (Weeks 15-22):
  • Control branches: 50.0 average
  • Treatment branches: 48.1 average
  • Gap: -1.9 points

Improvement in gap: +1.9 points ✓
```

### 4. Why the Evidence Is Not Definitive

- **Sample size**: 34 branches over 17 weeks provides moderate statistical power
- **Variability**: Service scores fluctuate week-to-week due to factors beyond the procedure
- **Time horizon**: 8 weeks post-switch may be too short to observe the full effect
- **P-value of 0.10**: There is approximately a 10% chance this result could occur by random variation (conventional threshold is 5%)

### 5. What the Data Does Show

- The treatment group **closed the gap** with the control group after switching
- No negative effects were detected
- The improvement is **consistent with our hypothesis**
- Effects appear to **strengthen over time** within the post-switch period

---

## **Recommendations**

### 1. Proceed with Rollout (Recommended)

Given that:
- The effect is in the expected direction
- The magnitude (+2 points) is meaningful for service quality
- No adverse effects were observed
- The procedure has already been successfully implemented at 20 branches

**Action:** Begin rolling out the new procedure to remaining branches over the next quarter.

### 2. Continue Monitoring

**Action:** Extend data collection for at least 12 additional weeks to:
- Confirm the effect stabilizes or grows
- Identify any branches where the procedure is less effective
- Build stronger statistical evidence for future decisions

### 3. Set Review Milestones

| Timeline | Action |
|----------|--------|
| 4 weeks | Interim check on rollout progress |
| 12 weeks | Formal re-analysis with extended data |
| 6 months | Final evaluation and adjustment if needed |

### 4. Investigate Branch-Level Variation

**Action:** Analyze whether certain branch characteristics (size, location, baseline performance) predict stronger or weaker responses to the new procedure. This can help optimize implementation.

---

## **Conclusion**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure is **working as intended**. While 8 weeks of data does not provide definitive statistical proof, the positive effect (+2 points), absence of negative outcomes, and alignment with our expectations support moving forward with the rollout. Continued monitoring will strengthen the evidence base and allow for course correction if needed.
