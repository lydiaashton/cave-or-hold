# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Re:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores as expected**. Branches that switched saw a **2.2-point gain** (approximately 4% improvement) relative to branches that kept the old procedure. While the result is marginally significant rather than definitive, the evidence points in the expected direction with no indication of negative effects. **We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring to confirm the effect persists.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched Branches (20) | 51.72 | 52.86 | +1.14 |
| Non-Switched Branches (14) | 53.35 | 52.40 | −0.95 |
| **Net Effect** | — | — | **+2.09** |

### **Statistical Confidence**

- **Estimated effect:** +2.21 points on the service score index
- **Significance level:** p = 0.094 (marginally significant)
- **95% confidence interval:** −0.38 to +4.79 points
- **Interpretation:** The most likely effect is positive; we cannot rule out a small null effect, but negative effects are unlikely

### **Why We Trust This Analysis**

✓ **Valid comparison:** Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, confirming the control branches provide a valid counterfactual  

✓ **Controls included:** Analysis accounts for weekly staffing levels and visitor traffic  

✓ **No red flags:** The procedure shows no evidence of harming service quality at any branch  

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

**Rationale:**
1. The effect moves in the expected direction with meaningful magnitude (~4% improvement)
2. No branches experienced service score declines attributable to the switch
3. The procedure has already been tested in 20 branches without operational issues
4. Waiting for stronger statistical certainty would delay benefits across the cooperative

---

| Action | Timeline | Owner |

## **Caveats**

- The 8-week post-switch window is relatively short; longer-term effects remain to be confirmed
- Statistical uncertainty means the true effect could be smaller than estimated (or occasionally zero)
- Implementation quality may vary; training and support during rollout will help maximize benefits
