# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**Date:** Analysis of weeks 6–22 service score data  
**Prepared for:** Board Decision on System-Wide Rollout

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by **+1.8 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **3.4% improvement** and is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001).

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Study scope** | 709 branches over 17 weeks (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| **Treatment effect** | +1.82 points on service score index |
| **Confidence interval** | 1.29 to 2.35 points (95% confidence) |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Relative improvement** | ~3.4% over baseline average of 52.7 |
| **Effect consistency** | Positive in all 8 post-switch weeks |

**What this means:** A branch with an average service score of 50 would be expected to score approximately 51.8 after adopting the new procedure, holding other factors constant.

**Controls validated:** The effect remains significant after accounting for staffing levels and visit volume, both of which also positively influence service scores.

**Important note:** Branches that switched showed some pre-existing improvement momentum before the switch. The true effect may be modestly smaller than estimated, but the post-switch gains exceed what the pre-existing trend would predict, and the effect is consistent across all post-implementation weeks.

### **ANALYTICAL APPROACH**

This analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** design, which isolates the effect of the new procedure by comparing:
- Changes in service scores at branches that switched **vs.**
- Changes in service scores at branches that did not switch

Statistical models include **cluster-robust standard errors** at the branch level to account for repeated weekly observations. A visualization of service score trends by group is attached.
