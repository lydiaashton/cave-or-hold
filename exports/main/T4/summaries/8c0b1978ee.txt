# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. Branches that switched improved by approximately **2 points** relative to those that did not, but this result is not statistically significant at conventional levels. The weekly effects have been highly volatile, suggesting branches are still in an adjustment period. 

**Recommendation: Do not proceed with system-wide rollout at this time.** Continue monitoring for 8–12 additional weeks before making a final decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total branches | 34 |
| Branches using new procedure | 20 |
| Branches using old procedure | 14 |
| Observation period | Weeks 6–22 |
| Procedure switch date | Week 15 |
| Service score scale | ~50 average (higher is better) |

### **Main Result**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| New procedure branches | 47.51 | 48.77 | +1.26 |
| Old procedure branches | 51.23 | 50.55 | −0.68 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.94 points** |

- **95% Confidence Interval:** −0.45 to +4.33 points
- **Statistical Significance:** Not significant (p = 0.11)

**Interpretation:** While the point estimate suggests a modest improvement, the confidence interval includes zero. We cannot rule out that the true effect is negligible or even slightly negative.

### **Weekly Performance After Switch**

The effects have fluctuated considerably week-to-week:

- **Weeks with significant gains:** 17 (+2.65), 19 (+6.21)
- **Weeks with significant declines:** 15 (−4.03), 18 (−5.03)
- **Recent weeks (20–22):** Small positive effects, not statistically significant

This pattern is consistent with an **ongoing adjustment period** as staff learn and refine the new procedures.

### **Validity Check**

✓ **Pre-trend analysis confirms** that switched and non-switched branches followed similar trajectories before the change. This validates our comparison method.

✓ **Results are robust** when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume.

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **Pause system-wide rollout** | Immediate | Board / Operations |
| **Continue data collection** | 8–12 weeks | Data Team |
| **Conduct branch manager interviews** to understand sources of weekly volatility | 4 weeks | Operations |
| **Review staffing adequacy** at switched branches | 2 weeks | HR / Operations |
| **Return to Board with updated recommendation** | Q1 2027 | Data Team |

---

## **What Would Change Our Recommendation**

We would recommend system-wide rollout if future analysis shows:

1. **Statistically significant positive effect** (p < 0.05) sustained over multiple weeks
2. **Reduced volatility** as branches become proficient with the new procedure
3. **Effect size of 2+ points** with a confidence interval entirely above zero
