# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**SUBJECT:** New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure – Impact Evaluation

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout.**

The new procedure **improved service scores by 1.8 points (3.4%)**, a statistically significant gain that held steady across all eight weeks after implementation. The effect is real, meaningful, and consistent with expectations.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| What We Measured | Result |
|------------------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.8 points |
| **Confidence in result** | 95% certain the true effect is between +1.5 and +2.2 points |
| **Relative gain** | 3.4% improvement over baseline |
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (March–July) |

### The Effect Was Consistent

Every week after the switch showed improvement:

```
Week 15: +1.6 pts    Week 19: +1.2 pts
Week 16: +1.0 pts    Week 20: +2.0 pts
Week 17: +1.9 pts    Week 21: +2.6 pts
Week 18: +1.9 pts    Week 22: +2.3 pts
```

### The Result Is Robust

The improvement holds even after accounting for:
- Weekly staffing variations
- Changes in visitor volume
- Inherent differences between branches

---

## **WHAT THIS MEANS**

For a typical branch scoring around 50 points, the new procedure adds roughly **2 points** to the service score. This is a **moderate but reliable gain**—comparable to the benefit of adding an extra staff member during peak hours.

The fact that the effect appeared immediately and persisted suggests the procedure change itself, not temporary enthusiasm, drove the improvement.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all 709 branches.

## **RISKS TO MONITOR**

1. **Implementation variance** – Some branches may need additional support during transition.
2. **Diminishing returns** – The effect could attenuate as the novelty wears off.
3. **Resource constraints** – Ensure staffing and supplies keep pace with expanded rollout.
