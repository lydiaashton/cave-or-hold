# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Roll out the new procedure to all branches.**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores of **2.0 points** (95% CI: 1.5 to 2.5), representing approximately **4% improvement** relative to the average branch score of 50.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** analyzed over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** retained the old procedure
- Switch occurred at week 15, creating a natural experiment for rigorous evaluation

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 52.0 | 48.9 | −3.1 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 53.0 | 51.9 | −1.1 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.0** ✓ |

### Statistical Confidence
- Effect is **highly significant** (p < 0.001)
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels, visitor volume, and branch-specific factors
- **39% of treatment branches** improved post-switch vs. **19% of control branches**

### Important Context
Both groups experienced score declines after week 15 (likely seasonal or external factors). The new procedure made branches **more resilient**—treatment branches declined by only one-third as much as control branches, effectively preserving service quality during a challenging period.

### Analysis Validity
Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, confirming the comparison is valid and the estimated effect can be attributed to the procedure change.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches
2. **Monitor implementation quality** to ensure effects persist at scale
3. **Capture best practices** from top-performing treatment branches for training materials
4. **Continue quarterly tracking** of service scores post-rollout
5. **Investigate system-wide decline** after week 15 to identify additional improvement opportunities

---

*Analysis based on 12,053 weekly observations. Models include clustered standard errors by branch and controls for staffing and visitor volume. Charts available in supporting materials.*
