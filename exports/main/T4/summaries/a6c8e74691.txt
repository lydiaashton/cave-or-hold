# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a meaningful 4% improvement on the standard service index. We recommend approving system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Studied**
- **709 branches** across the cooperative
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- **17 weeks** of weekly service scores (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring in week 15

### **The Result**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 52.0 | 54.8 | +2.8 |
| Branches with old procedure | 50.6 | 51.4 | +0.8 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

The new procedure is associated with a **2.0-point increase** in service scores. This effect is:
- **Statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- **Precisely estimated** (95% confidence interval: 1.4 to 2.6 points)
- **Robust** to controls for staffing levels and branch visit volumes

### **Why We're Confident**
Our analysis accounts for:
- Natural differences between branches (some consistently score higher or lower)
- Overall trends affecting all branches during the study period
- Proper statistical methods for repeated observations at the same branches

The evidence strongly supports a causal interpretation: the new procedure caused the improvement.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate rollout of the new procedure to all 284 branches still using the old method.**

Based on the observed effect, we estimate this could raise average system-wide service scores by approximately 1–2 points, benefiting patrons across the cooperative.
