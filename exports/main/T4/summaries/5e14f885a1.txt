# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**
## Analysis for Board Decision on System-Wide Rollout

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new shelving and rotation procedure is associated with a **modest improvement in service scores (+2.3 points, or ~5%)**, but this effect is **not statistically significant** at conventional confidence levels. The data provides suggestive but inconclusive evidence that the new procedure improves service quality. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than an immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Design**
- **34 branches** analyzed over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** kept the old procedure
- Switch occurred at **week 15**; analysis compares before/after for both groups
- This "difference-in-differences" approach isolates the procedure effect from other factors affecting all branches

### **2. Service Score Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.0 | 52.3 | **+2.3** |
| Control (old procedure) | 49.1 | 49.1 | **0.0** |
| **Estimated Effect** | | | **+2.3 points** |

### **3. Statistical Confidence**
- **p-value: 0.11** (above the conventional 0.05 threshold for statistical significance)
- **95% confidence interval: -0.5 to +5.1 points**
- **Interpretation:** The true effect could range from slightly negative to moderately positive; we cannot rule out random chance as an explanation

### **4. Methodology Validation**
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** No significant pre-existing difference between groups before the switch (p=0.55), validating our comparison approach
- **Effect consistency:** Improvement appears sustained across both early and late post-switch periods
- **Robust to controls:** Results unchanged when accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume

### **5. Practical Significance**
- A 2.3-point improvement represents approximately **5% relative to baseline** service scores
- For context: if a typical branch scores 50, the new procedure would move it to approximately 52

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

### **Recommended Approach: Phased Rollout with Evaluation**

| Action | Rationale | Timeline |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| **1. Expand to 5–10 additional branches** | Positive point estimate with no negative effects suggests the procedure is at worst neutral and likely beneficial | Next quarter |
| **2. Extend data collection** | Current data covers only 8 weeks post-switch; longer observation improves statistical power | 12–16 additional weeks |
| **3. Monitor implementation fidelity** | Variation in how branches follow the new procedure could be masking true effects | Ongoing |
| **4. Define success criteria** | Establish clear thresholds (e.g., +2 points sustained over 12 weeks) for full rollout decision | Before expansion |
| **5. Analyze subgroup effects** | Investigate whether effects differ by branch size, location, or baseline performance to target rollout effectively | During expansion |
