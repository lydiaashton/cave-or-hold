# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores and should be rolled out to all branches.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a **2.0-point increase** in service scores (approximately 4% improvement) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, consistent across the post-switch period, and represents meaningful service improvement for our patrons.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Analysis Overview
- **709 branches** analyzed over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **284 branches** retained the old procedure
- Rigorous difference-in-differences methodology controls for underlying trends and external factors

### Service Score Impact

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 51.49 | 53.75 | +2.26 |
| Old Procedure Branches | 50.95 | 51.23 | +0.28 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.98** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Effect size:** +1.98 points (95% confidence interval: 1.43 to 2.52)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Practical significance:** ~4% improvement from baseline score of 52

### Validation Checks
- Pre-switch trends were comparable between groups (difference of only 0.54 points)
- Effect remained consistent across all 8 post-switch weeks
- Results hold when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
