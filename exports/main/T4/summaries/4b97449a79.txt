# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** December 2025  
**RE:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **1.9 points** (about 4%) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and has grown over time since implementation.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Works
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (425) | 50.4 | 52.4 | +2.1 |
| Control branches (284) | 50.1 | 50.3 | +0.2 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+1.9** |

- The 1.9-point improvement is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)
- 95% confidence interval: 1.4 to 2.5 points
- Effect represents approximately 4% improvement relative to average scores

### Benefits Accumulated Over Time
The improvement was not immediate—branches needed time to realize full benefits:

| Period After Switch | Estimated Effect |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Weeks 1-2 | +1.4 to +1.7 points |
| Weeks 3-5 | +1.7 to +2.3 points |
| Weeks 6-8 | +3.1 to +3.2 points |

This pattern is consistent with a learning curve and suggests the procedure's value increases as staff gain familiarity.

### Analysis Scope
- **709 branches** total (425 adopted new procedure; 284 maintained old procedure)
- **17 weeks** of data per branch (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- Procedure switch occurred at **week 15**
- Service scores average approximately 50 points (higher is better)

### Analytical Note
Branches that switched showed modestly different trends before the switch compared to control branches. While this does not invalidate our findings—the post-switch improvements are substantially larger than any pre-existing differences—it suggests the true effect may be slightly smaller than our point estimate. Continued monitoring is warranted.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining 284 branches

2. **Implement phased adoption** to allow staff training and support during transition

3. **Monitor service scores** for 8 weeks post-switch at each newly converted branch to confirm expected improvements

4. **Capture implementation learnings** from early-adopter branches to inform training materials

5. **Return to Board in Q2 2026** with updated analysis confirming sustained benefits across all branches

---
