# **Board Summary: New Shelving & Rotation Procedure Impact Analysis**

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend system-wide rollout.** Contrary to initial expectations, the new procedure produced a **statistically significant improvement** in service scores of approximately **2.4 points** (about **5% gain**). The evidence is robust and consistent across all analytical tests.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The Unexpected Result
The analysis revealed a clear positive effect where none was anticipated:

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment (switched) | 49.96 | 51.40 | +1.44 |
| Control (no switch) | 51.28 | 50.26 | −1.01 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.45** |

Branches that adopted the new procedure improved; those that didn't, slightly declined.

### 2. Statistical Confidence
- **Effect size**: +2.4 points on a ~50-point scale
- **95% Confidence Interval**: 1.7 to 3.2 points
- **p-value**: < 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Robustness**: Result holds across all model specifications, including controls for branch characteristics, seasonal week effects, staffing levels, and visitor volume

### 3. Why We Can Trust This Result

| Validation Check | Result |
|------------------|--------|
| Parallel pre-trends (Weeks 6–14) | ✓ Groups tracked closely before switch |
| Covariate balance | ✓ Staffing and visits similar across groups |
| Multiple model specifications | ✓ Consistent effect size throughout |
| Sample size | ✓ 2,550 observations (150 branches × 17 weeks) |

### 4. What This Means
- The new procedure delivers measurable service improvement at no apparent cost
- The effect is large enough to be operationally meaningful, not just statistically detectable
- No negative impacts observed on staffing efficiency or visitor experience

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve rollout** to remaining 60 branches

2. **Track outcomes** for 8–12 weeks post-implementation to confirm effects persist

3. **Capture learnings** from the 90 early-adopter branches to streamline training

4. **Revisit assumptions** — this finding suggests the procedure may affect patron experience in ways not previously recognized; consider qualitative follow-up to understand the mechanism
