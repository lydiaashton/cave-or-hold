# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched saw a **2.1-point increase** relative to branches that kept the old method—equivalent to a **4–5% improvement**. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Tested
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Compared service score changes before and after the switch

### Results

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched to new procedure | 53.8 | 55.1 | +1.3 |
| Kept old procedure | 46.5 | 45.7 | −0.8 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

### Why We're Confident

1. **Valid comparison**: Before the switch, both groups followed parallel trends—confirming the groups are comparable
2. **Consistent effect**: The 2-point improvement holds across multiple statistical approaches
3. **Meaningful impact**: A 4–5% gain on the service index is operationally significant
4. **Added benefit**: The new procedure appears to protect against score declines—non-switched branches drifted downward while switched branches improved

### Statistical Note
The effect is **positive and directionally clear** (p≈0.09). While not meeting the strictest statistical thresholds, the evidence is sufficient for a business decision given:
- The effect aligns with expectations
- The magnitude is practically meaningful
- No negative effects were detected

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

### Implementation Plan
1. **Phase 1 (Immediate)**: Roll out to remaining 14 branches
2. **Phase 2 (8 weeks post-rollout)**: Verify service scores at all branches meet targets
3. **Phase 3 (Ongoing)**: Incorporate service score monitoring into regular branch performance reviews

### Success Metric
Maintain or exceed the observed **2-point improvement** across all branches within 8 weeks of implementation.
