# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than they would have under the old system—a **3.5% improvement**. The evidence is strong and statistically significant. We recommend approving system-wide rollout.

---

## **What We Did**

When some branches switched to the new procedure in week 15 and others did not, it created a natural experiment. We compared:

- **425 branches** that switched to the new procedure
- **284 branches** that kept the old procedure

Using 17 weeks of data (before and after the switch), we isolated the effect of the new procedure from other factors like staffing levels and visitor volume.

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.8 points |
| **Percentage gain** | 3.5% |
| **Statistical confidence** | 95% (p < 0.001) |
| **Confidence range** | 1.3 to 2.3 points |

**In plain terms:** A branch that previously scored 50 would be expected to score about 52 after switching to the new procedure.

### **Why We're Confident**

1. **Clean comparison:** Switched and non-switched branches followed similar trends before the change
2. **Large sample:** 709 branches over 17 weeks (12,053 observations)
3. **Robust analysis:** Results hold after controlling for staffing and visitor volume
4. **Consistent effect:** The improvement persisted throughout the post-switch period

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure delivers measurable improvement in service quality with no identified downsides in the data.
