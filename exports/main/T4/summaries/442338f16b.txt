# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure showed **modest improvement in service scores** compared to branches that retained the old procedure. However, the evidence is **not statistically definitive**. We recommend **continuing the pilot for 8–12 additional weeks** before committing to a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Yes, modestly** (+1.6 points, ~3% gain) |
| Are we confident this wasn't chance variation? | **No**—results are suggestive but not conclusive |
| Should we roll out to all branches now? | **Not yet**—extend the pilot and gather more evidence |

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** switched to the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores average ~50 points (higher is better)

### Results Summary

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Old Procedure (14 branches) | 48.05 | 47.49 | −0.56 |
| New Procedure (20 branches) | 49.19 | 50.20 | +1.00 |
| **Net Effect (Difference-in-Differences)** | | | **+1.56** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Estimated effect:** +1.56 service score points
- **95% Confidence Interval:** −0.50 to +3.62 points
- **Interpretation:** The true effect could range from a slight decline to a meaningful improvement. We cannot rule out chance variation with current data.

### Supporting Evidence
- **55%** of switched branches improved vs. **36%** of control branches
- **Pre-switch trends were parallel**, supporting the validity of the comparison
- No branch showed dramatic negative effects from the new procedure

---

## **Recommendations**

### Immediate Actions (Next 30 Days)

1. **Extend the pilot period** by 8–12 weeks to strengthen statistical evidence
2. **Document implementation practices** at top-performing switched branches
3. **Analyze secondary metrics** (staffing efficiency, visit patterns) for additional insights

### Rollout Plan (Conditional on Continued Positive Results)

| Phase | Timeline | Branches | Success Criteria |
|-------|----------|----------|------------------|
| Phase 1 | Current | 20 branches | Complete |
| Phase 2 | Q1 2027 | 10–15 additional branches | +2 points sustained over 12 weeks |
| Phase 3 | Q2 2027 | Remaining branches | Statistically significant improvement (p < 0.05) |

### Decision Threshold for Full Rollout

Proceed to system-wide implementation only if:
- [ ] Lower bound of 95% confidence interval exceeds 0
- [ ] At least 60% of switched branches show improvement
- [ ] No significant negative effects on staffing or operations

---

## **Risk Assessment**

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Procedure shows no real benefit | Moderate | Extended pilot will clarify |
| Implementation quality varies | High | Document and share best practices |
| Staff resistance to change | Moderate | Involve branch managers in rollout planning |
| Costs exceed benefits | Low | Service scores are primary metric; monitor staffing costs |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure shows **promise but not proof**. A measured, evidence-based approach will protect the cooperative from unnecessary disruption while allowing us to capture potential benefits. We recommend the board authorize the extended pilot and establish clear decision criteria for full rollout.
