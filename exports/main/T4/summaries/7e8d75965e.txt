# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Results of Branch Procedure Change Pilot – Recommendation for System-Wide Rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores.** We recommend approving system-wide rollout to all branches.

Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** (a 4% improvement) than branches that retained the old procedure. This result is statistically significant and validates the pilot's success.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Treatment effect | **+1.95 service score points** |
| Statistical confidence | **p = 0.032** (significant at 5% level) |
| 95% Confidence interval | **+0.17 to +3.74 points** |
| Relative improvement | **~4%** above baseline |

### **Study Overview**

- **34 branches** participated over 17 weeks
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure (starting week 15)
- **14 branches** maintained the old procedure (control group)
- Analysis compared score changes between groups before and after the switch

### **Why We Trust This Result**

1. **Comparable groups:** Before the switch, both groups had nearly identical service scores (~50 points), confirming no pre-existing differences.

2. **Clean identification:** The difference-in-differences method isolates the procedure's effect from other factors like seasonal trends or branch-specific characteristics.

3. **Controls validated:** The effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume, confirming the improvement is attributable to the procedure itself.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure.**

The pilot demonstrates a clear, measurable improvement in service quality. With 14 branches still using the old procedure, there is an opportunity to extend these benefits across the entire cooperative.
