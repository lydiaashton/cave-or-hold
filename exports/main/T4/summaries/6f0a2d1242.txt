# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.2 points** (about 4.4%) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and robust. **We recommend approving a systemwide rollout.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Overview**
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **60 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores averaged approximately 50 points across all branches (higher is better)

### **Results**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 49.46 | 53.24 | +3.78 |
| Branches that did not switch | 48.55 | 50.30 | +1.75 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.18*** |

*After controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **P-value:** 0.001 (less than 0.2% probability this result occurred by chance)
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 0.93 to 3.44 points
- **Pre-existing trends:** No significant difference between groups before the switch, validating the comparison

### **Practical Significance**
- The improvement represents a **4–5% gain** in service quality
- Both groups improved after week 15, but switched branches improved more than twice as much
- The effect held after accounting for weekly variations in staffing and visitor volume

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve full implementation** across all 150 branches
2. **Develop implementation timeline** with support for branches during transition
3. **Document procedures** based on best practices from early-adopting branches
4. **Monitor outcomes** for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale

---

## **APPENDIX: TECHNICAL NOTES**

Analysis employed a difference-in-differences regression design with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). The model controlled for staffing index and visits index. A pre-trends test confirmed parallel trends between treatment and control groups prior to the intervention (p=0.194), supporting causal interpretation of the results.

**Data source:** branches.csv (2,550 observations)
