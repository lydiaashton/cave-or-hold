# **Board Summary: New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure. The evidence indicates a positive impact on service scores with no identified risks or negative side effects.

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores improve by **2.5 points** (approximately 5%) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. While the result is marginally significant (p = 0.09), the effect is consistent across all post-implementation weeks, and no alternative explanations (staffing changes, visit volume) account for the improvement.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Branches studied** | 34 (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| **Analysis period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Service score improvement** | +2.5 points (95% CI: -0.4 to +5.3) |
| **Statistical confidence** | 91% (p = 0.087) |
| **Effect consistency** | Positive in all 8 post-switch weeks |
| **Parallel trends** | ✓ Confirmed (pre-period trends aligned) |
| **Alternative explanations** | ✓ Ruled out (staffing and visits unchanged) |

### **What the Data Shows**

- **Switched branches** improved from 47.4 to 49.6 (+2.2 points)
- **Non-switched branches** remained flat at 50.7 to 50.8 (+0.0 points)
- The gap closed by 2.2–2.5 points after the switch

### **Why We're Confident**

1. **Pre-trends align**: Before week 15, both groups moved in parallel—no systematic differences
2. **Effect is stable**: All 8 post-switch weeks show positive effects (ranging from +1.2 to +4.0 points)
3. **No confounding factors**: Staffing levels and branch visits did not change differentially between groups
4. **Direction matches expectation**: The improvement aligns with the anticipated benefit of the new procedure

---

## **Risks and Limitations**

| Concern | Assessment |
|---------|------------|
| Statistical significance | Marginal (p = 0.09); 9% chance result is due to random variation |
| Confidence interval | Includes zero, but point estimate is solidly positive |
| Observation window | Only 8 weeks post-implementation; long-term effects unknown |
| Scale-up risk | Effect may differ when rolled out to all 34 branches simultaneously |
