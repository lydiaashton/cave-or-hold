# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We cannot conclude that the new procedure improved service scores.** While branches using the new procedure show higher scores, this difference existed before the change and reflects pre-existing differences between branches rather than the procedure's effect. **We recommend against a system-wide rollout at this time.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores average ~50 points (higher is better)

### What the Numbers Show

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.83 | 52.50 | +0.67 |
| Non-switched branches | 49.82 | 48.62 | −1.20 |
| **Difference** | | | **+1.86** |

On the surface, switched branches appear to have gained 1.86 points relative to non-switched branches.

### Why This Is Misleading

**The critical flaw:** Switched branches were already pulling ahead *before* the new procedure began.

| Period | Gap (Switched − Non-Switched) |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| Weeks 6–14 (before) | 2.02 points |
| Weeks 15–22 (after) | 3.88 points |

The gap widened steadily **before** the switch (0.32 points per week) and continued at a similar rate **after** (0.23 points per week). There is no evidence of a change attributable to the new procedure.

### Statistical Tests Confirm the Problem

| Test | Result | What It Means |
|------|--------|---------------|
| Pre-trend analysis | Gap widening before switch (p<0.001) | Groups were not on parallel trajectories |
| Placebo test (fake switch at week 12) | "Effect" of 1.56 points (significant) | Method detects effects even when none exist |
| Narrow window (weeks 11–18) | Effect of 0.67 points (not significant) | Effect disappears near the actual switch date |

---

## **Conclusion**

The analysis cannot separate the procedure's effect from pre-existing differences between branches. Branches that chose to switch were already higher-performing and improving faster. **The apparent benefit is not credible evidence of causation.**

---

## **Recommendations**

### Immediate Action
1. **Do not proceed with system-wide rollout.** The evidence does not support the expected improvement.

### Next Steps
2. **Extend data collection** for 8–12 additional weeks to observe longer-term patterns.

3. **Improve future evaluation design:**
   - Use **randomized assignment** rather than self-selection for pilot programs
   - Collect branch characteristics (size, location, baseline performance) to control for differences
   - Track multiple outcomes (patron satisfaction, circulation, staff feedback)

4. **Conduct qualitative assessment** through staff interviews to understand implementation experience and perceived impacts.

5. **Reconvene in Q1** with additional data and a strengthened evaluation framework.
