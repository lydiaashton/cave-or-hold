# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving Procedure – Service Score Impact

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out cooperative-wide.** Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores approximately **2.2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This represents roughly a **4% improvement** and is statistically reliable.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Changes by Group**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure (284) | 51.03 | 49.87 | −1.16 |
| Branches using new procedure (425) | 51.00 | 52.02 | +1.02 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.19** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Estimated effect:** +2.2 points
- **95% confidence interval:** +1.65 to +2.76 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly reliable)

### **What This Means**
While branches keeping the old procedure saw service scores decline after Week 15 (possibly due to seasonal factors), branches using the new procedure saw scores improve. The 2.2-point difference isolates the effect of the procedure change itself.

### **Analysis Scope**
- 709 branches total (425 adopted new procedure, 284 did not)
- 17 weeks of data (Weeks 6–22, with switch at Week 15)
- 12,053 branch-week observations
- Analysis accounts for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve cooperative-wide rollout of the new shelving procedure.**

The evidence strongly supports expanding the new procedure to all branches. The improvement is meaningful, statistically reliable, and came without requiring additional staffing or resources.

---

---

## **Technical Appendix**

**Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). This approach controls for time trends affecting all branches and isolates the procedure change effect.

**Model Results:**
- Coefficient on treatment effect: 2.21 (SE: 0.28)
- t-statistic: 7.83
- Controls included: Staffing index, visits index

**Data Quality Note:** Pre-trend analysis suggests early-adopter branches may have had slightly stronger momentum before the switch. The true effect could be modestly smaller than 2.2 points, but the positive direction and practical significance remain clear.
