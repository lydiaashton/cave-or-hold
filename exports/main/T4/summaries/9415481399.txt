# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2 points higher** than branches that kept the old method. This improvement is statistically reliable and practically meaningful. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **WHAT WE DID**

When some branches switched to the new procedure in week 15 and others did not, we had a natural experiment. We compared:

| Group | Branches | What Happened |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| **Switched** | 425 | Adopted new procedure at week 15 |
| **Did Not Switch** | 284 | Continued with old procedure |

We tracked service scores for 9 weeks before and 8 weeks after the switch.

---

## **WHAT WE FOUND**

### Service Score Impact

| Period | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Before (Weeks 6-14) | 51.2 | 50.9 |
| After (Weeks 15-22) | 51.9 | 49.6 |
| **Change** | **+0.7** | **-1.3** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +2.05 points**

### Why This Matters

- **Statistically significant:** The result is highly unlikely to be due to chance (p < 0.001)
- **Meaningful improvement:** A 2-point gain represents a 4% improvement on the average score of 51
- **Consistent over time:** The benefit persisted across all 8 weeks after implementation
- **Not explained by other factors:** The effect holds even after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

### What the Data Shows


*(See attached charts: "Service Score Trends" and "Event Study")*

The charts show that switched branches pulled away from non-switched branches starting in week 15, with the gap widening over time.
