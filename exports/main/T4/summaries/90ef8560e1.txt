# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores by approximately 1.7 points (3.3%)**. While the statistical evidence is suggestive rather than definitive (p=0.10), the effect is in the expected direction, the study design is sound, and the improvement is operationally meaningful. **We recommend proceeding with a phased rollout** to all branches, accompanied by continued monitoring to confirm the benefit persists at scale.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The New Procedure Improved Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches Using New Procedure | 50.26 | 56.37 | +6.11 |
| Branches Using Old Procedure | 50.60 | 54.96 | +4.36 |
| **Attributable to New Procedure** | | | **+1.75** |

- **Treatment effect: +1.75 points** on the service score index (3.3% improvement)
- **Confidence level: 90%** (p=0.10; 95% CI ranges from -0.3 to +3.8)
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 maintained the old procedure

### **Study Design Validates the Result**

✓ **Parallel trends confirmed**: Switched and non-switched branches followed identical trajectories before the switch (p=0.87), supporting the validity of the comparison

✓ **Clean identification**: The difference-in-differences approach isolates the procedure's effect from other factors affecting all branches (both groups improved after week 15, but switched branches improved more)

✓ **Robust to controls**: Results hold when accounting for weekly staffing and visit levels

### **Important Context**

- Service scores rose across **all branches** after week 15 (+4 to +6 points), suggesting favorable external factors (seasonal patterns, patron behavior, or system-wide initiatives)
- The new procedure delivered **additional improvement beyond this general uplift**
- The effect size (+1.75 points) represents a **modest but meaningful gain** on an index where the average branch scores ~50

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with phased rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

**Rationale:**
1. The effect is in the expected direction and operationally meaningful
2. No evidence of negative consequences or unintended effects
3. The marginal statistical significance (p=0.10) reflects limited sample size (34 branches × 17 weeks) rather than absence of effect
4. Waiting for stronger statistical certainty would delay benefits to patrons at remaining branches
