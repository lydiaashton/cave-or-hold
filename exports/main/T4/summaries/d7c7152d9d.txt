# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores and should be rolled out to all branches.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores increase by approximately **2.2 points** (about **4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, consistent over time, and independent of staffing or visitor volume.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Clear Improvement in Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches (425) | 51.57 | 53.95 | +2.38 |
| Control Branches (284) | 50.64 | 50.84 | +0.21 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.17** |

### 2. Statistically Significant Result
- **Effect size:** 2.17 points (95% CI: 1.46 to 2.87)
- **Confidence level:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Interpretation:** We can be confident this improvement is real and not due to random variation

### 3. Effect Was Consistent and Sustained
The improvement appeared immediately after the switch and persisted across all 8 weeks of observation, with weekly effects ranging from 2.39 to 3.78 points.

### 4. Not Driven by External Factors
The effect remains unchanged even after accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume, confirming the procedure itself—not other operational factors—drove the improvement.

### 5. Valid Comparison
Before the switch, both groups followed similar trends. The divergence occurred only after switched branches adopted the new procedure, supporting a causal interpretation.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 284 branches currently using the old method.
