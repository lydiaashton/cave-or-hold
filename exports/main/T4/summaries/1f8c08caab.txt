# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than would have been expected under the old procedure. This represents a **3.7% improvement** and is statistically significant.

**Recommendation:** Approve system-wide rollout to all remaining branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **284 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Analysis compares changes in switched branches against concurrent changes in non-switched branches

### Main Result

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Treatment Effect** | **+1.81 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.27 to 2.36 points |
| Statistical Significance | p < 0.001 |
| Effect Size | 3.7% improvement over baseline |

### What This Means in Practice

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Non-switched branches | 49.2 | 51.1 | +2.0 |
| Switched branches | 49.7 | 53.5 | +3.8 |
| **Attributable to new procedure** | — | — | **+1.8** |

Both groups improved after week 15 (likely due to seasonal factors), but switched branches improved nearly twice as much.

### Effect Over Time

The benefit of the new procedure appears immediately and grows as branches gain experience:

- **Pre-switch average gap:** 0.5 points
- **Week 15 (first week after switch):** 2.0 points
- **Week 22 (most recent):** 3.1 points

This pattern suggests the full benefit may not yet be realized.

### Robustness

The finding holds under multiple analytical approaches:
- ✓ Controls for branch-specific characteristics (e.g., location, size)
- ✓ Controls for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume
- ✓ Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, validating the comparison

## **APPENDIX: ANALYSIS NOTES**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects
- **Standard errors:** Clustered at branch level to account for repeated observations
- **Data source:** Weekly service scores from branch management system
- **Baseline service score:** ~50 points (higher is better)
