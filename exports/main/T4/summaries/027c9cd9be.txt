# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores and should be rolled out to all branches.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a **1.85-point increase** in service scores (approximately **3.7% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically significant and consistent across multiple analytical approaches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Impact Summary**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Service score improvement | +1.85 points |
| Confidence interval | 1.30 to 2.43 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Relative improvement | 3.7% above baseline |
| Branches studied | 709 (425 treatment, 284 control) |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

### **Before and After Comparison**

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 49.33 | 49.44 | +0.11 |
| Branches using new procedure | 50.61 | 52.58 | +1.96 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | — | — | **+1.85** |

### **Why We Trust This Result**

1. **Strong research design:** We compared branches that switched to those that didn't, before and after the change, isolating the procedure's effect from other factors.

2. **Consistent findings:** The 1.85-point improvement held across three different statistical models:
   - Basic comparison: +1.85 points
   - Adjusted for staffing and visits: +1.87 points
   - Accounting for branch-specific characteristics: +1.87 points

3. **No alternative explanation:** Control branches showed virtually no change (+0.11), confirming the improvement is due to the procedure change, not system-wide trends.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate rollout of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure to all remaining 284 branches.**
