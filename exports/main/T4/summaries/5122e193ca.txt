# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 15, 2026
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure is associated with improved service scores. However, we recommend a **phased implementation with continued monitoring** to confirm the effect holds across all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Positive Association with Service Scores
Branches that adopted the new procedure showed service scores approximately **1.8 points higher** than comparison branches after the switch. This represents roughly a **3.6% improvement** over the typical branch score of 50.

### 2. Effect Appears to Grow Over Time
The advantage for treatment branches increased steadily through the 8-week observation period:

| Period | Treatment Advantage |
|--------|---------------------|
| Weeks 1-4 after switch | +1.6 to +2.4 points |
| Weeks 5-8 after switch | +2.9 to +3.6 points |

This pattern is encouraging and suggests staff may become more effective with the new procedure as they gain experience.

### 3. Majority of Branches Benefited
- **67%** of treatment branches showed improvement
- **60%** improved by more than 1 point
- Effects held after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

### 4. Important Limitation
Our analysis detected that treatment branches were already on a slightly more positive trajectory *before* adopting the new procedure. This means **the true effect may be smaller than our 1.8-point estimate**. We cannot rule out that some portion of the observed improvement reflects pre-existing differences rather than the procedure change itself.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve a phased rollout** of the new procedure with the following conditions:

1. **Implement in batches** over 6-8 weeks rather than all at once
2. **Continue tracking service scores** for all branches during and after rollout
3. **Provide additional support** to branches that do not show improvement within 4 weeks of adoption
4. **Conduct a formal re-evaluation** after 6 months with full post-rollout data

---

## **Questions for Discussion**

1. What is the minimum improvement threshold that would justify the operational costs of this change?
2. Should we prioritize rollout to branches currently scoring below average?
3. What additional training or resources might maximize the benefit at each branch?
