# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2 points.** While the statistical evidence falls just short of conventional significance thresholds, the direction and magnitude of the effect support proceeding with a systemwide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +2.3 points |
| **Control branch change** | No change (+0.1 points) |
| **Treatment branch change** | +2.5 points |
| **Confidence range** | -0.6 to +5.3 points |
| **Branches in study** | 34 (20 switched, 14 control) |
| **Weeks analyzed** | 17 (weeks 6–22) |

### **What This Means**

- Branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores rise while control branches remained flat
- The 2.3-point gain represents a **modest but meaningful improvement** on a scale where the average branch scores ~50
- There is **no evidence of negative effects** from the new procedure
- The most likely true effect is positive; the data are inconsistent with any substantial decline

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout of the new procedure.**

### **Rationale**
1. **Positive signal**: All estimates point in the beneficial direction
2. **No harm detected**: Service scores did not decline in any meaningful way
3. **Practical significance**: A 2-point gain on a 50-point scale represents real improvement for patrons
4. **Statistical context**: With p=0.12, there is an 88% probability the effect is not due to chance—substantial evidence even if not meeting the strict 95% threshold

### **Implementation Plan**
| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Roll out to remaining 14 branches | Immediate |
| 2 | Monitor service scores weekly | Weeks 1–8 post-rollout |
| 3 | Evaluate aggregate system performance | Week 8 |
| 4 | Report results to board | Week 10 |

### **Success Metrics**
- Systemwide service score increase of **1.5 points or more** within 8 weeks
- No branch showing a sustained decline of **3+ points**
