# **Library Cooperative Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores increase by **+2.34 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4.4% improvement** and is highly statistically significant (p < 0.0001).

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** kept the old procedure
- Switch implemented at **week 15**
- Complete data with no missing observations

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| New Procedure | 51.83 | 54.86 | +3.02 |
| Old Procedure | 52.53 | 53.21 | +0.68 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.34** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Estimated effect:** +2.34 service score points
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 1.76 to 2.92 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.0001
- **Pre-trend validation:** Passed — both groups showed parallel trends before the switch

### What This Means
The analysis isolates the effect of the procedure change from other factors (such as seasonal trends or branch-specific characteristics). The 2.34-point improvement is attributable to the new procedure itself, not to pre-existing differences between branches.
