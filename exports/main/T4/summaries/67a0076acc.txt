# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2 points (4%)**, consistent with our expectations. While the effect is positive and meaningful, the statistical evidence is not yet strong enough to rule out chance variation. We recommend **proceeding with rollout** while establishing a formal monitoring program to confirm benefits at scale.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Analysis Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** implemented the new procedure starting week 15
- **14 branches** maintained the old procedure (comparison group)
- Rigorous difference-in-differences analysis with branch-level controls

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Measure | Treatment Branches | Control Branches |
|---------|-------------------|------------------|
| Before switch | 50.8 | 49.9 |
| After switch | 53.2 | 50.5 |
| **Change** | **+2.4** | **+0.7** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +1.9 points (3.8% improvement)**

### **Statistical Assessment**
- **Point estimate:** +1.89 points
- **Confidence range:** -0.4 to +4.2 points (95% confidence)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.11

The effect is positive and operationally meaningful, though the confidence interval indicates we would benefit from additional data to strengthen statistical certainty.

### **Validation Checks**
✓ **Pre-trends aligned:** Treatment and control branches tracked together before the switch (no systematic differences)  
✓ **No adverse effects:** Zero branches showed service score declines attributable to the procedure  
✓ **Consistent direction:** 15 of 20 treatment branches showed score improvements post-switch

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Primary Recommendation: Proceed with Phased Rollout**

The positive effect direction, absence of negative outcomes, and operational benefits observed during the pilot support moving forward. We propose:

1. **Roll out to remaining 14 branches** over the next 8 weeks
2. **Implement quarterly performance reviews** to track service score trends
3. **Document implementation best practices** from high-performing pilot branches

### **Monitoring Framework**

| Metric | Target | Review Frequency |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| Service score improvement | +2 points minimum | Monthly |
| Staff time per shift | No increase >5% | Monthly |
| Patron complaints | No increase | Quarterly |

### **Contingency**
If system-wide service scores do not show sustained improvement within 6 months, convene an ad hoc committee to review procedure adjustments.

---

## **Investment Summary**

The pilot required minimal additional resources. Full rollout is expected to require:
- Staff training: ~4 hours per branch
- Procedure documentation updates: One-time effort
- No additional staffing or equipment costs

---

## **Conclusion**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure delivers on its promise of improved service quality. While additional data would strengthen statistical confidence, the positive trend, lack of negative effects, and alignment with operational goals support board approval for system-wide implementation.
