# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** [Current Date]  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **2.0 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **4% improvement** and is statistically robust. We recommend proceeding with full implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Clear Positive Impact

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Estimated effect | +2.03 service score points |
| 95% Confidence interval | +1.31 to +2.75 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| Relative improvement | ~4% over baseline |

### 2. Divergent Trends Between Groups

| Metric | Switched Branches (425) | Non-Switched Branches (284) |
|--------|------------------------|----------------------------|
| Average score change | +0.88 points | -1.15 points |
| Branches that improved | 59.5% | 35.6% |

During the same 8-week period, switched branches generally improved while non-switched branches generally declined.

### 3. Effect Strengthens Over Time

The gap between switched and non-switched branches widened from **1.9 points** in the first week after the switch to **3.6 points** by week 8. This suggests benefits may accumulate as staff gain familiarity with the new procedure.

### 4. Result Holds After Accounting for Other Factors

The improvement remains significant after controlling for:
- Weekly staffing levels
- Branch visitor volume

This confirms the effect is attributable to the procedure change itself.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The evidence indicates a genuine, meaningful improvement in service quality that justifies the investment in training and implementation across all 709 branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Owner |

| Develop rollout plan and timeline | 2 weeks | Operations |

### Implementation Considerations

1. **Phased approach:** Roll out over 4-8 weeks to manage training capacity
2. **Priority targeting:** Consider starting with branches showing lowest current service scores
3. **Support structure:** Approximately 40% of switched branches did not see improvement; establish a help channel for struggling branches
4. **Best practice sharing:** Document and share what worked well in high-performing switched branches

---

## **Appendix: Analysis Summary**

**Data:** 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches over 17 weeks (weeks 6-22)

**Method:** Difference-in-differences regression comparing branches that switched at week 15 (treatment, n=425) to those that did not (control, n=284)

**Key assumption:** Treatment and control branches would have followed similar trends absent the intervention. Pre-period data shows some baseline differences, but the post-switch effect substantially exceeds these and persists consistently across all 8 post-switch weeks.
