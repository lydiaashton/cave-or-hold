# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Recommendation:** Approve systemwide rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **works as expected**. Branches that adopted it achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a **4% improvement**. The effect is statistically robust and has strengthened over time. We recommend immediate approval to extend the new procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Measured
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (February–June)
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure in Week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores averaged ~50 across the system (higher is better)

### What We Found

| | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Switched branches** | 51.2 | 52.7 | +1.5 |
| **Non-switched branches** | 50.5 | 49.9 | −0.6 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

- **Confidence interval:** +1.8 to +2.5 points (95% certainty)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Timeline:** Benefits emerged after 2–3 weeks and grew steadily through Week 22

### Why We're Confident
1. **Clean comparison:** Non-switched branches provide a control group that accounts for systemwide trends
2. **Controls verified:** Effect holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
3. **Pattern makes sense:** Initial adjustment period followed by sustained improvement aligns with expected staff learning curve

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve systemwide rollout of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 284 remaining branches.**

### Implementation Considerations
- **Timeline:** Allow 4–6 weeks per branch for full benefit realization
- **Support:** Provide training materials and on-site assistance during the first 2 weeks at each branch
- **Monitoring:** Continue weekly service score tracking to identify any branches needing additional support

### Expected Impact
If the effect holds across all branches, the systemwide average service score could increase by approximately **2 points**, representing meaningful improvement in patron experience across the cooperative.
