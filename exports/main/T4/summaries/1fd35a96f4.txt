# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Title: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2026  
**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.3 points** (approximately **4.5% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and consistent across the post-switch period. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Study Overview
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total branches studied | 709 |
| Branches using new procedure | 425 |
| Branches keeping old procedure | 284 |
| Weeks of data | 17 (Weeks 6–22) |
| Switch date | Week 15 |

### 2. Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 52.53 | 53.21 | +0.68 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 51.83 | 54.86 | +3.02 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.34** |

- **Statistical confidence:** 95% confidence interval of 1.75 to 2.93 points
- **Significance level:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Percentage improvement:** 4.5% relative to baseline

### 3. Analysis Validity

✓ **Parallel pre-trends confirmed:** Both groups showed similar trends before the switch, validating the comparison method

✓ **Effect is persistent:** Improvement appeared immediately after the switch and remained stable or grew over the 8-week observation period

✓ **Robust to other factors:** Result holds after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### 4. Important Context

- Branches that switched were scoring slightly **lower** than control branches before the change (−0.7 points), making the post-switch improvement more notable
- The new procedure appears to have helped switched branches not only catch up but surpass the control group
