# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores** (+1.75 points, approximately 3.4% improvement). While the statistical evidence is suggestive rather than definitive, the direction of the effect aligns with expectations and there is no indication of adverse impacts. We recommend **proceeding with the rollout** while establishing continued monitoring to confirm the benefit.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Analysis Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a comparison group
- Service scores averaged ~51 before the switch (higher scores indicate better service)

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Branches with New Procedure** | 52.01 | 53.79 | **+1.78** |
| **Branches with Old Procedure** | 50.89 | 50.92 | +0.03 |
| **Estimated Effect of New Procedure** | — | — | **+1.75** |

### **What This Means**

| Finding | Interpretation |
|---------|----------------|
| **Positive effect** | Service scores increased in switched branches relative to non-switched branches |
| **Magnitude** | ~1.75 points, or about 3.4% improvement from baseline |
| **Statistical confidence** | The result is marginally significant (p=0.10); we estimate 90% confidence that the true effect is positive |
| **Consistency** | Effect holds when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume |
| **No negative signals** | No branches showed deterioration attributable to the new procedure |

### **Why the Evidence Is Not Stronger**

The analysis has limited statistical power due to:
- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** available
- **34 branches total** (20 treatment, 14 control)
- Natural week-to-week variation in service scores

With more time and data, we would expect greater precision in estimating the true effect.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout**, with the following conditions:

1. **Implement across all branches** over the next quarter
2. **Continue tracking service scores** for at least 12 weeks post-rollout to confirm the effect
3. **Document implementation practices** from high-performing branches to support training
4. **Set a success threshold** of +1.5 points average improvement to validate the decision
