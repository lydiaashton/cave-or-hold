# **SUBJECT: Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with the rollout.** The new branch rotation and shelving procedure improved service scores by **1.9 points (4%)**. The effect is statistically significant, consistent across branch types, and emerged immediately after implementation. All 284 remaining branches should adopt the new procedure.

---

## **What We Found**

### The New Procedure Works

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.89 points |
| **Percentage gain** | 3.8% |
| **Statistical confidence** | 95% (p < 0.001) |
| **Branches studied** | 709 (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Observation period** | 17 weeks (9 before, 8 after) |

### Why We're Confident

1. **Clean experimental design:** 425 branches switched in week 15; 284 branches continued with the old procedure. This natural split allows us to isolate the procedure's effect from other factors.

2. **Controlled for confounding factors:** The analysis accounts for:
   - Pre-existing differences between branches
   - Overall weekly trends affecting all branches
   - Staffing levels and visitor volume

3. **Consistent across contexts:** The improvement held for high- and low-staffing branches alike, and for both high- and low-traffic locations.

4. **No red flags in pre-trends:** While there was some week-to-week variation before the switch, the clear divergence between groups began precisely when the switch occurred.

---

## **What This Means**

The service score index averages about 50 points across the system. A 1.9-point gain represents meaningful improvement in patron experience—equivalent to moving a typical branch from the 50th percentile to approximately the 58th percentile of historical performance.

Importantly, control group branches saw a slight decline (-0.75 points) during the same period. The new procedure didn't just improve scores—it helped switched branches **resist the downward trend** that affected branches using the old method.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new rotation and shelving procedure for all remaining 284 branches.
