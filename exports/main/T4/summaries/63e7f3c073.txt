# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than branches using the old procedure—a **4% improvement** that is statistically significant and consistent across the post-implementation period.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Impact Summary**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Service Score Improvement | +1.91 points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.38 to 2.45 points |
| Statistical Confidence | >99.9% (p < 0.001) |
| Relative Improvement | 3.8% above baseline |
| Effect Durability | Sustained over 8 weeks |

### **Study Overview**

- **709 branches** participated (425 switched, 284 maintained prior procedure)
- **17 weeks** of observation (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- New procedure implemented starting **week 15**
- Analysis controlled for staffing levels, visitor volume, and branch-specific factors

### **Evidence Quality**

The study design isolates the procedure's effect by comparing switched branches against non-switched branches before and after implementation. Key validation points:

1. **Parallel pre-trends:** Before week 15, both groups tracked together with no systematic differences
2. **Clean break at implementation:** The divergence began precisely when the new procedure launched
3. **Growing effect:** The improvement strengthened over time, suggesting branches mastered the new approach

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Owner |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Approve system-wide rollout | Immediate | Board |
| Develop implementation schedule | 2 weeks | Operations |
| Prepare training materials | 4 weeks | Training Team |
| Begin phased rollout | Week 1 of next quarter | Operations |
| Monitor service scores during rollout | Ongoing | Analytics |

### **Implementation Considerations**

1. **Phased approach recommended:** The effect grew from ~1.4 points to ~3.2 points over the 8-week observation period, suggesting branches improve with experience. A staggered rollout allows for learning and adjustment.

2. **Training investment:** Document the specific practices that drove improvement to ensure consistent adoption.

3. **Continue monitoring:** Track service scores during rollout to confirm the effect replicates at scale and identify any branches needing additional support.

---

## **Conclusion**

The data confirms what the cooperative expected: the new stock rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. With strong statistical evidence and a clear implementation path, we recommend moving forward with a system-wide rollout.
