# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improves service scores by approximately 2 points** (about 4% above baseline). While this falls short of the hoped-for impact, the effect is **consistently positive across all analyses** and **strengthens over time** as branches gain experience. We recommend **proceeding with the rollout**, paired with implementation support to help branches realize the full benefit.

---

### **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.2 service score points (≈4.4% gain) |
| **Statistical confidence** | Marginal (p ≈ 0.08); 90% confidence the effect is positive |
| **Effect over time** | Grows from +0.7 (weeks 15–18) to +3.3 (weeks 19–22) |
| **Branch adoption** | 13 of 20 switching branches (65%) showed improvement |
| **No negative consequences** | No evidence of harm to service quality |

**What This Means:** The procedure does improve service, but the benefit is modest and takes time to materialize. Branches appear to need 4–6 weeks to fully adapt.

---

### **Why the Effect May Be Smaller Than Expected**

1. **Learning curve**: The strengthening effect over time suggests staff need practice to execute the new procedure effectively.

2. **Implementation variation**: Branch-level results ranged from -4.5 to +11.6 points, indicating execution quality varies considerably.

3. **External factors**: Staffing levels and visitor volume affect service scores and may mask or amplify the procedure's impact.

---

### **Recommendations**

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **Continue rollout** | Positive effect with no downside; benefits accumulate over time |
| **Add 4-week support window** | Assign experienced staff to new adopters during their first month |
| **Share best practices** | Document what high-performing branches (those with +5+ point gains) do differently |
| **Set realistic expectations** | Communicate that 2-point improvement is the baseline; better execution can yield more |
| **Re-measure at 6 months** | Confirm sustained improvement and identify branches needing additional support |

---

### **Data Summary**

- **34 branches** analyzed (20 adopted new procedure, 14 retained old)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22); switch occurred week 15
- **578 observations** (branch-weeks)
- **Analysis method**: Difference-in-differences with branch-clustered standard errors
- **Validation**: Pre-switch trends were parallel, confirming valid comparison
