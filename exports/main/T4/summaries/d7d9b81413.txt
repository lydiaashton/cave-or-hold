# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** New Branch Shelving & Stock Rotation Procedure – Impact Assessment  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and stock rotation procedure achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than branches that kept the old method—a **4% improvement** that is statistically significant and operationally meaningful. We recommend **immediate system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Delivers Results
| Metric | Control Branches (Old Procedure) | Treatment Branches (New Procedure) |
|--------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Pre-switch average | 49.6 | 50.5 |
| Post-switch average | 49.2 | 52.3 |
| **Change** | **−0.4** | **+1.8** |

The **net effect of +2.1 points** isolates the procedure's impact from other factors like staffing or visitor volume.

### Evidence Is Strong
- **Statistical confidence:** 95% confidence interval ranges from +1.5 to +2.6 points (p < 0.001)
- **Consistent over time:** The improvement held steady across all 8 weeks after implementation
- **Not explained by other factors:** The effect persists after controlling for staffing levels and branch traffic

### Context on Branch Selection
Branches that volunteered for the new procedure had slightly higher scores before the switch (+0.9 points on average). However, the post-switch gap (+3.0 points) more than tripled this baseline difference, confirming the procedure itself drove the improvement.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all 709 branches.**

Based on the observed effect:
- **Expected system-wide gain:** ~2 points average service score increase
- **Timeline:** Full implementation within 8–12 weeks
- **Investment:** Minimal (procedure change only; no additional staffing or equipment required)

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Authorize rollout** at the next board meeting
2. **Develop implementation timeline** with operations team (target: complete by Q2)
3. **Create training materials** to ensure consistent adoption across all branches
4. **Establish monitoring dashboard** to track service scores post-rollout and confirm sustained improvement
