# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office
**DATE:** November 15, 2026
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved **significantly higher service scores** than those that kept the old procedure. We recommend **proceeding with systemwide rollout**, with continued monitoring to ensure results are sustained.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated treatment effect** | **+1.8 points** on service score index |
| **Confidence interval** | 1.3 to 2.4 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | >99.9% (p < 0.001) |
| **Relative improvement** | ~3.6% above baseline |

For context: the average branch scores approximately 50 points. A 1.8-point gain represents a meaningful improvement in patron service quality.

### **2. Performance Comparison**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with **new procedure** | 49.7 | 53.5 | **+3.8** |
| Branches with **old procedure** | 49.2 | 51.1 | +2.0 |
| **Net benefit of new procedure** | | | **+1.8** |

### **3. Analysis Scope**

- **709 branches** total (425 adopted new procedure; 284 kept old procedure)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **12,053 observations** analyzed
- Results control for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve systemwide rollout of the new procedure.**

The evidence supports expanding the new rotation and shelving procedure to all branches. The improvement is statistically significant, practically meaningful, and aligns with our expectations for this initiative.

---

## **Implementation Plan**

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **1** | Begin rollout to remaining branches | Immediate |
| **2** | Complete training for all branch staff | 4–6 weeks |
| **3** | Full implementation across cooperative | 8–10 weeks |
| **4** | Post-implementation review | Quarterly |

---

## **Risk Mitigation**

To ensure sustained success, we recommend:

1. **Continued monitoring:** Track service scores monthly for all branches post-rollout
2. **Support resources:** Provide additional training support during transition period
3. **Feedback loop:** Collect staff input to identify and address implementation challenges
4. **Success benchmarks:** Set minimum service score thresholds; investigate any branch falling below

---

## **Conclusion**

The new rotation and shelving procedure delivers on its promise to improve service quality. With a measured gain of 1.8 points on the service score index and strong statistical confidence, this initiative represents a successful operational improvement. We recommend moving forward with confidence while maintaining oversight to ensure results are sustained across all branches.
