# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Impact of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores by an average of 1.9 points** (approximately 4% above baseline). This result was statistically significant and exceeded initial expectations. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure change service scores? | **Yes – positively** |
| How large was the effect? | **+1.9 points** on the service index |
| How confident are we? | **98% confidence** (p = 0.002) |
| Recommendation | **Roll out to all branches** |

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Service Scores Improved After the Switch

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores rise relative to branches that kept the old procedure.

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Net Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| Control (old procedure) | 51.2 | 50.2 | **-1.0** ↓ |
| Treated (new procedure) | 50.8 | 51.5 | **+0.7** ↑ |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+1.9** ✓ |

**Interpretation:** The new procedure not only improved scores but also appears to have prevented the slight decline observed in branches using the old procedure.

### 2. The Result Is Statistically Reliable

- **Effect size:** +1.88 points (95% CI: 0.69 to 3.06)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.002
- **Analysis method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch-level clustering
- **Sample:** 150 branches, 2,550 weekly observations (weeks 6–22)

### 3. Validation Checks Support Causality

| Check | Result | Implication |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Parallel pre-trends | ✓ Passed (p = 0.42) | Groups were comparable before the switch |
| Effect persistence | ✓ Consistent across 8 post-switch weeks | Not driven by a single week |
| Spillover effects | ✓ None detected on staffing or visits | Effect reflects service quality, not other changes |

### 4. Variation Across Branches

While the average effect was positive, individual branch results varied:

- **Top performers:** Up to +9 points improvement
- **Median treated branch:** +0.9 points
- **Some branches:** Minimal or no measurable gain

This variation suggests implementation quality or local conditions may influence outcomes—an opportunity for optimization during rollout.

---

## **Recommendations**

### Immediate Actions

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all 60 remaining branches

2. **Document best practices** from the 90 early-adopter branches, especially those with above-average gains

3. **Establish a monitoring dashboard** to track service scores during rollout (8–12 weeks post-switch per branch)

### Follow-Up Items

4. **Investigate variation:** Analyze what distinguished high-performing adopters to maximize benefits across all branches

5. **Set realistic expectations:** Communicate that average improvements of ~2 points are typical; some branches may see more, some less

6. **Review in 6 months:** Assess whether gains persist long-term and whether additional refinements are warranted

---

## **Appendix: Analysis Details**

- **Data source:** Weekly branch-level service scores, weeks 6–22
- **Treatment:** New stock rotation and shelving procedure (implemented week 15)
- **Control:** Branches continuing with the old procedure
- **Model:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Controls:** Staffing index and visits index (neither changed differentially)
