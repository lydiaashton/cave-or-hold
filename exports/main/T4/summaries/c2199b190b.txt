# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure increased service scores by **2.2 points** (approximately **4% improvement**). The effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001), consistent across branches, and not explained by other factors such as staffing or visitor volume changes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Studied
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches** | 150 total (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| **Time Period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch Point** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences with branch and week controls |

### What We Found
| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.45 | 53.11 | +2.66 |
| Non-switched branches | 49.65 | 50.12 | +0.46 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.20** |

### Why We Trust This Result
- ✓ **Parallel trends verified**: Switched and non-switched branches moved together before the change (p = 0.999)
- ✓ **Statistically significant**: Less than 0.1% chance this result is due to random variation
- ✓ **Precise estimate**: 95% confidence interval is 1.4 to 3.0 points
- ✓ **Robust**: Effect persists after controlling for branch characteristics, week effects, staffing, and visitor volume
- ✓ **Widespread**: 69% of switched branches showed improvement
