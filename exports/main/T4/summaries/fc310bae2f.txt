# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure increased service scores by **2.16 points (4.4%)** with high statistical confidence. This validates the expected improvement and supports expanding to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Effect on Service Scores** | +2.16 points (95% CI: 1.60 to 2.71) |
| **Statistical Confidence** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Branches Benefiting** | 80.5% of treatment branches improved vs. 62.0% of control |
| **Study Scope** | 709 branches, 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |

**What This Means:** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw meaningful, sustained improvements in service quality. The effect is robust—it holds after controlling for staffing levels, visitor volume, and branch-specific characteristics.

---

## **Evidence Summary**

1. **Clean Comparison:** 425 branches switched at Week 15; 284 branches did not. Both groups had nearly identical score trends before the switch, making the post-switch divergence attributable to the procedure change.

2. **Consistent Gains:** The treatment group's average score rose from 49.7 to 52.7, while the control group rose only from 49.1 to 50.0. The 2.16-point gap is the estimated causal effect.

3. **Broad-Based Improvement:** Over 80% of switching branches saw score gains, indicating the procedure works across diverse branch contexts—not just in a few high-performers.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate rollout** of the new procedure to all 284 branches currently using the old method.

**Expected Outcome:** System-wide service score increase of approximately **2 points**, affecting all 709 branches.

---

## **Risk Mitigation**

Approximately 20% of treatment branches did not see score improvements. We recommend:
- Tracking branch-level outcomes during rollout
- Providing targeted support to branches that do not show gains within 4–6 weeks
- Documenting best practices from high-performing branches for training
