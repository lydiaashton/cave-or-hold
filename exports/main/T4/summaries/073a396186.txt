# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to initial expectations that the change would make no difference, branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores increase by approximately **2.0 points (about 4%)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically significant and robust.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **150 branches** total: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 kept the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22): 9 weeks before the switch, 8 weeks after
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach, comparing changes in the treatment group to changes in the control group

### Results

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect on service score | **+2.04 points** |
| Statistical significance | **p = 0.001** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **[0.81, 3.27]** |
| Relative improvement | **~4%** from baseline |

### Validity Checks
- **Parallel trends supported**: Treatment and control groups showed similar trends before the switch
- **Result is robust**: The effect persists when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **No pre-existing differences**: Groups were comparable before the procedure change

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new stock rotation procedure to all branches

2. **Monitor implementation** during rollout to ensure consistent adoption

3. **Continue tracking service scores** to confirm the effect persists at scale

4. **Document and share best practices** from the 90 early-adopter branches

5. **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** if the new procedure carries additional costs

---

## **Technical Notes**

Analysis used difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). Baseline average service score: ~51.7. The 2-point effect represents a meaningful improvement in service quality.
