# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improves service scores by approximately 2 points.** The effect is statistically significant and consistent across analyses. However, pre-existing differences between branches suggest the true benefit may be somewhat smaller. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Effect on service score** | +2.07 points (95% CI: 1.91 to 2.23) |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Effect size** | ~4% improvement over baseline |
| **Branches studied** | 709 (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |

### What This Means in Practice
- Average service scores are around 50 points
- A 2-point gain moves a typical branch from the 50th to approximately the 55th percentile
- The effect is **meaningful but modest**—noticeable to patrons, but not transformative

### Important Consideration
Branches selected for the new procedure were already showing slightly stronger performance trends before the switch. Accounting for this, the **conservative estimate is +0.5 to +2.0 points**. The effect is real, but may be at the lower end of our initial estimate.

---

## **Recommendation**

| Option | Recommendation |
|--------|----------------|
| **Immediate full rollout** | ❌ Not recommended |
| **Phased expansion with monitoring** | ✅ Recommended |
| **Abandon new procedure** | ❌ Not supported by evidence |

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Expand to 100–150 additional branches** over the next quarter
2. **Track service scores for 12 weeks** post-expansion
3. **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** comparing implementation costs to service improvements
4. **Report back to the Board** with updated findings before deciding on full system-wide adoption

---

## **Conclusion**

The new rotation and shelving procedure delivers a **real, measurable improvement** in branch service quality. While the benefit is not large, it is consistent, statistically robust, and comes at a time when control branches showed declining scores. Proceeding with a measured, evidence-based expansion is the prudent course.

---

*Analysis based on 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches. Difference-in-differences methodology with cluster-robust standard errors.*
