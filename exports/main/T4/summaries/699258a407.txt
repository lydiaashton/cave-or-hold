# **Library Cooperative Board Memo**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service quality. Branches that switched saw service scores rise by about **2 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure. This suggests the new procedure helps, but the evidence is not strong enough to guarantee the same result at every branch. We recommend **proceeding with a phased rollout** while continuing to collect data.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.1 service score points (≈4% gain) |
| **Statistical confidence** | Not conclusive (p=0.11); true effect could range from -0.5 to +4.7 points |
| **Study scope** | 34 branches, 17 weeks, 578 observations |
| **Adoption pattern** | 20 branches switched to new procedure; 14 kept old procedure |
| **Pre-switch trends** | Treatment and control branches were on similar trajectories before the change |

**What the data show:**
- Treatment branches improved by **3.2 points** on average after switching (from 50.8 to 53.9)
- Control branches improved by **1.1 points** over the same period (from 52.2 to 53.2)
- The **extra 2.1-point gain** for treatment branches is the estimated effect of the new procedure
- This pattern holds even after accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume

**What we cannot rule out:**
- There is an 11% probability that the observed improvement is due to random chance rather than the procedure itself
- Some branches may see larger or smaller effects than the average

---

### **NEXT STEPS**

| Action | Timeline | Owner |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **1. Expand to 10-15 additional branches** | Next 4 weeks | Operations |
| **2. Continue weekly score tracking** | Ongoing | Data Team |
| **3. Identify high-performing adopters** | 6 weeks | Operations + Data |
| **4. Re-evaluate with full data** | 3 months | Board |
| **5. Decide on full rollout** | 4 months | Board |

**Success criteria for full rollout:**
- Effect remains positive (≥1.5 points) with additional data
- Confidence interval excludes zero (statistically significant)
- No branches show sustained negative effects

---

### **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve a phased expansion** of the new procedure to additional branches while maintaining rigorous monitoring. The weight of evidence favors the new procedure, and the risk of harm appears low. A full rollout decision should be revisited in 3 months with the benefit of additional data.
