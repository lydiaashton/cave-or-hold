# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new stock rotation and shelving procedure improved service scores by approximately 1.8 points (3.5% above average). This effect is statistically significant and sustained over the 8-week post-implementation period. We recommend approving system-wide rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.76 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **+0.66 to +2.87 points** |
| Improvement vs. average | **3.5%** |
| Effect size | **0.18 standard deviations** |

Branches that adopted the new procedure showed consistently higher service scores than those that retained the old procedure.

### **2. Statistically Robust Result**

- **P-value: 0.002** (highly significant)
- Result holds after controlling for staffing levels and branch visit volumes
- Standard errors clustered by branch to account for repeated measurements

### **3. Valid Comparison Between Groups**

| Group | Number of Branches | Pre-Switch Average Score |
|-------|-------------------|-------------------------|
| Switched to new procedure | 90 | 49.85 |
| Kept old procedure | 60 | 50.34 |

The two groups had nearly identical service scores before implementation (difference of only 0.5 points), supporting the conclusion that the observed improvement is attributable to the new procedure.

### **4. Sustained Performance Post-Switch**

Weekly performance differences (switched vs. non-switched branches):

| Period | Average Difference |
|--------|-------------------|
| Pre-switch (weeks 6–14) | −0.50 points |
| Post-switch (weeks 15–22) | +1.24 points |
| **Net improvement (DiD)** | **+1.74 points** |

The effect persisted throughout the observation period with no signs of diminishing.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve immediate system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all 60 remaining branches.**

## **TECHNICAL APPENDIX**

- **Analysis method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch-clustered standard errors
- **Data source:** Weekly service scores from all 150 branches
- **Observation period:** Weeks 6–22 (9 pre-switch, 8 post-switch)
- **Total observations:** 2,550 branch-week records
- **Control variables:** Staffing index, visits index
- **Switch implementation date:** Week 15
