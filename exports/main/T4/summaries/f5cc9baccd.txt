# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows a **positive but not statistically conclusive** effect on service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by approximately **2.2 points (4.2%)** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. While the evidence points in the expected direction, we cannot rule out chance variation with high confidence. **We recommend proceeding with rollout while continuing to monitor outcomes.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** participated (20 adopted new procedure, 14 kept old procedure)
- **17 weeks** of data collected (weeks 6–22); switch occurred at week 15
- Analysis compares changes in treatment branches against changes in control branches

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (Old Procedure) | 53.35 | 52.40 | −0.95 |
| Treatment (New Procedure) | 51.72 | 52.86 | +1.14 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.09** |

### Statistical Evidence
- **Estimated effect:** +2.21 points (4.2% of baseline)
- **95% Confidence Interval:** −0.38 to +4.79 points
- **p-value:** 0.094
- **Interpretation:** Approximately 90% probability the effect is positive; does not meet the conventional 95% confidence threshold

### Validity Checks
| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Parallel pre-trends | ✅ Passed (p=0.57) |
| Controls significant | ✅ Staffing (p=0.015), Visits (p=0.001) |
| No pre-existing differences | ✅ Confirmed |

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout, with continued monitoring.**

**Rationale:**
1. Evidence points in the expected direction (improvement, not decline)
2. No signal of harm from the new procedure
3. A 4% improvement would be operationally meaningful if real
4. Further data collection will strengthen future evaluations
