# **TO: Board of Directors, Library Cooperative**
# **FROM: Data Analysis Team**
# **DATE: November 2025**
# **SUBJECT: Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores nearly **2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. The evidence supports proceeding with a full system-wide rollout.

---

## **What We Found**

### **The New Procedure Delivered Measurable Gains**

| | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branches with **New Procedure** | 51.05 | 54.75 | +3.70 |
| Branches with **Old Procedure** | 50.17 | 51.92 | +1.75 |
| **Net Improvement from New Procedure** | | | **+1.95** |

The **1.95-point net gain** is the estimated effect of the new procedure itself, separate from other factors that affected all branches.

### **Results Are Statistically Solid**

- Analysis covered **709 branches** over **17 weeks** (12,053 observations)
- Effect is **highly statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- Findings hold after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Benefits **grew over time**, indicating sustained improvement rather than a temporary bump

### **Implementation Pattern**

The data shows a brief adjustment period followed by clear gains:
- **First 2 weeks:** Minimal detectable effect (staff learning the new system)
- **Weeks 3-8:** Consistent improvements of **3 to 7 points** above the control group

This suggests investing in training during the first few weeks will accelerate returns.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all remaining branches.**

Given that:
1. The effect is real, positive, and statistically confirmed
2. Benefits accumulate over time rather than fade
3. No adverse effects were detected
4. Your expectations for improvement were validated by the data

## **Appendix: Analysis Details**

- **Method:** Difference-in-Differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Treatment group:** 425 branches (switched to new procedure at week 15)
- **Control group:** 284 branches (continued with old procedure)
- **Controls:** Staffing index, visits index, branch fixed effects
- **Data source:** Weekly service scores, weeks 6-22
