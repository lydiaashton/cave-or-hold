# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a meaningful 4% improvement and is statistically robust.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| 425 branches using new procedure | 53.09 | 52.29 | -0.80 |
| 284 branches using old procedure | 53.06 | 50.10 | -2.96 |

**Net improvement from new procedure: +2.14 points**

While service scores declined across all branches after week 14 (likely due to seasonal factors), branches using the new procedure experienced a significantly smaller decline. The new procedure effectively protected service quality.

### 2. Benefits Accumulate Over Time

The improvement was not immediate but grew steadily over the 8 weeks following implementation:

| Weeks After Switch | Effect on Service Score |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Week 1 | +0.85 |
| Week 4 | +1.84 |
| Week 8 | +3.18 |

This suggests staff may need 4-6 weeks to fully realize the benefits of the new procedure.

### 3. Effect Is Consistent Across Branches

The positive impact held regardless of staffing levels, indicating the procedure works well in various operational contexts:

- Low-staffing branches: +2.12 points
- Medium-staffing branches: +2.06 points
- High-staffing branches: +2.34 points

### 4. Analysis Is Statistically Sound

- Pre-implementation trends were similar between groups, supporting a valid comparison
- Results hold when controlling for branch-specific characteristics and weekly variations
- Effect is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all 284 branches currently using the old method.

---

## **Considerations for Implementation**

1. **Allow a learning period:** Expect full benefits to materialize 4-6 weeks after implementation at each branch

2. **Leverage early adopters:** Document best practices from the 425 branches already using the new procedure to support training

3. **Track outcomes:** Continue monitoring service scores to confirm the ~2 point improvement is realized across all branches

4. **Evaluate costs:** Conduct a follow-up analysis of implementation costs (training, staff time) relative to service improvements
