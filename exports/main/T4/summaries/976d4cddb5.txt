# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** Analysis of weeks 6–22 service score data  
**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.2 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure. This represents a **4.4% improvement** and is statistically conclusive. We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Measured Impact

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Service score improvement | **+2.20 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | 1.65 to 2.76 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 (highly reliable) |
| Percentage gain | **4.4%** above baseline |

### What the Data Shows

- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure; **284 branches** kept the old procedure
- Service scores were tracked for **17 weeks** (9 weeks before, 8 weeks after the switch)
- Switched branches improved by **4.06 points** after adoption
- Non-switched branches improved by **1.86 points** over the same period (likely seasonal/system-wide trends)
- **Net attributable improvement: 2.20 points** from the new procedure alone

### Why We're Confident

1. **Control group comparison:** The 284 branches that kept the old procedure serve as a reliable baseline, accounting for system-wide trends
2. **Statistical rigor:** Analysis controls for staffing levels and visitor volume, isolating the procedure's effect
3. **Consistent pattern:** The performance gap between switched and non-switched branches widened steadily after week 15 and remained elevated

---

## **Considerations**

- Switched branches had slightly higher scores even before the change (+1.14 points), but the post-switch gap more than doubled
- The improvement held steady across all 8 post-switch weeks, suggesting durable gains rather than a temporary effect
- Both staffing and visitor volume positively affect scores, but the procedure effect remains significant after accounting for these factors

---

## **Decision Requested**

**Approve rolling out the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all branches.**

The evidence is clear: the new procedure improves service quality. With 425 branches already successfully using it and a measured 4.4% gain in service scores, extending this to all 709 branches will raise service quality across the entire cooperative.

---

*Analysis based on 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches. Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors.*
