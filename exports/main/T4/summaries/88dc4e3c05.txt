# Memorandum to the Board

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 2026

---

## Bottom Line

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. While the direction of change aligns with expectations, the evidence is not strong enough to justify an immediate system-wide rollout. We recommend **continuing the pilot with expanded monitoring** before making a final decision.

---

## Key Findings

### Effect on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 48.9 | 49.3 | +0.4 |
| Branches with old procedure | 54.3 | 53.1 | −1.2 |
| **Net effect (Difference-in-Differences)** | | | **+1.6** |

- The new procedure is associated with a **1.6-point improvement** in service scores (approximately 3% above baseline)
- This effect is **positive, as expected**, but **not statistically significant** (p = 0.14)
- The 95% confidence interval (−0.5 to +3.8 points) means the true effect could range from slightly negative to moderately positive

### Important Context

1. **Baseline differences**: Branches selected for the new procedure had lower average service scores before the switch (48.9 vs. 54.3). This suggests the new procedure was targeted to branches that needed improvement.

2. **Parallel pre-trends**: Before week 15, both groups showed similar week-to-week patterns, supporting the validity of our comparison.

3. **High weekly variation**: Service scores naturally fluctuate by 8–9 points week-to-week, making it difficult to isolate the procedure's effect with only 8 weeks of post-switch data.

---

## Recommendation

We **do not recommend a full system-wide rollout at this time**. Instead:

1. **Extend the pilot** for at least 8–12 more weeks to improve statistical precision
2. **Add 5–10 more branches** to the treatment group to increase sample size
3. **Conduct a cost-benefit analysis** to determine whether a 1.6-point improvement (if real) justifies the operational costs
4. **Reconvene in Q2** with additional data for a final rollout decision

---

## Technical Appendix

- **Analysis method**: Difference-in-Differences regression with branch and week fixed effects
- **Sample**: 34 branches (20 treatment, 14 control), 17 weeks, 578 observations
- **Standard errors**: Clustered at the branch level
- **Software**: Python with statsmodels
