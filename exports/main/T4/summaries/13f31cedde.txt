# **Analysis of New Shelving Procedure Impact on Library Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores—contrary to expectations.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure saw their service scores increase by approximately **2.3 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. Given that the average branch scores around 50 points, this represents roughly a **5% improvement** in service quality. The result is statistically significant and robust across multiple analytical approaches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 150 total (90 switched, 60 kept old procedure) |
| **Time period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6-22 of the year) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences with branch-level controls |

### 2. The Effect
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 49.06 | 52.24 | +3.18 |
| Old Procedure Branches | 49.05 | 49.95 | +0.90 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.28** |

The **2.28-point improvement** is statistically significant (p < 0.001). This means there is less than a 0.1% probability this result occurred by chance.

### 3. Why We Can Trust This Result

**Pre-trends were parallel.** Before the switch, treated and control branches moved together—their scores differed by less than 1 point on average. A formal statistical test confirms no meaningful pre-existing differences (p = 0.40).

**The effect appeared after the switch.** Service scores diverged only after week 15, when the new procedure began. This timing supports a causal interpretation.

**Results hold with controls.** The effect persists even after accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume, suggesting the improvement is due to the procedure itself, not other factors.

### 4. Effect Over Time
The improvement was not a one-week anomaly:
- **Week 1 of switch:** +2.6 points
- **Average across 8 weeks:** +2.9 points
- **All 8 post-switch weeks:** positive effects

---

## **Conclusion**

This analysis tested whether the new shelving and rotation procedure affects service scores. While we anticipated no difference, the data shows a clear, statistically significant **improvement of 2.3 points**. The study design supports a causal interpretation, and the effect has been consistent across all post-implementation weeks.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.
