# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** [Current Date]  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a **2.0-point increase** in service scores compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant, practically meaningful (approximately 4% above baseline), and consistent across different types of branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Average improvement** | +2.0 points on service score index |
| **Confidence range** | +1.5 to +2.5 points (95% certainty) |
| **Statistical confidence** | Greater than 99.9% (p < 0.001) |
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| **Observation period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

### **What This Means**

- A typical branch scoring 50 before the change would be expected to score **52** after adopting the new procedure.
- The benefit **grows over time**—branches show continued improvement in the weeks following implementation.
- **All branches benefit**, whether they were high-performing or lower-performing before the change.

### **Why We Trust This Result**

1. **Comparison group**: We compared switching branches against branches that kept the old procedure during the same time period.
2. **Controlled for other factors**: The result holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.
3. **Multiple tests confirm it**: The finding is consistent across different statistical approaches.
4. **Effects accumulate**: Scores continue improving in the weeks after implementation, which is what we would expect if the procedure itself is causing the change.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.

---
