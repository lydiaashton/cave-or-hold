**Subject:** Evaluation of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and stock rotation procedure achieved a statistically significant improvement in service scores of **+2.1 points** (approximately 4% above baseline). The evidence strongly supports expanding this procedure to all 284 branches still using the old method.

---

| | |
