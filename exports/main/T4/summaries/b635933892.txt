# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative

**Subject:** Results of New Branch Procedure Pilot — Recommendation for System-Wide Rollout

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new shelving and rotation procedure **successfully improved service scores** across pilot branches. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide implementation**.

**Bottom Line:** Branches using the new procedure achieved service scores **2 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** and is statistically conclusive.

---

## **Results at a Glance**

| Metric | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| **Service Score Impact** | +2.07 points (p < 0.001) |
| **Confidence Level** | 95% confident the true effect is between 1.5 and 2.6 points |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 total (425 pilot, 284 control) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Recommendation** | ✅ Proceed with full rollout |

---

## **What We Did**

When the new procedure launched in week 15, some branches adopted it while others continued with the old method. This created a natural experiment:

- **Pilot group:** 425 branches using the new procedure
- **Control group:** 284 branches using the old procedure
- **Method:** Compared score changes in pilot branches against score changes in control branches

This approach isolates the effect of the new procedure from other factors (seasonal trends, staffing changes, visit volume fluctuations).

---

## **What We Found**

### Service Score Changes After Week 15

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **New Procedure Branches** | 52.8 | 53.8 | **+1.0** |
| **Old Procedure Branches** | 50.9 | 49.9 | **−1.0** |
| **Net Effect (New vs. Old)** | — | — | **+2.0** |

The new procedure branches improved while control branches declined, resulting in a **2-point net gain** attributable to the procedure change.

### The Effect Holds Up Under Scrutiny

We tested the finding multiple ways:
- ✅ Controlling for staffing levels and visit volumes
- ✅ Accounting for inherent differences between individual branches
- ✅ Checking week-by-week trends before and after the switch

All analyses confirm the **~2-point improvement** is real and not due to chance or confounding factors.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The evidence is clear and actionable:
1. The improvement is **statistically significant** (virtually no chance this is random noise)
2. The effect size is **meaningful** (4% improvement on a key performance metric)
3. The finding is **robust** across multiple analytical approaches
