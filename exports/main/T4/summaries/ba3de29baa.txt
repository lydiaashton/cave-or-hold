# **Library Cooperative Board Memorandum**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. Branches that switched improved by an estimated **1.6 points** more than branches that kept the old procedure, but this difference is not statistically significant (p = 0.12). The data neither confirms nor rules out a meaningful benefit.

**Recommendation:** Do not proceed with immediate system-wide rollout. Instead, continue the current pilot with expanded monitoring to gather more evidence before making a final decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches studied | 34 |
| Switched to new procedure | 20 |
| Kept old procedure | 14 |
| Weeks of data | 17 (weeks 6–22) |
| Switch implemented | Week 15 |

### Service Score Impact

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.4 | 51.6 | +2.3 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 47.9 | 51.8 | +3.9 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.6** |

- **Estimated effect:** +1.62 points on the service score index
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.44 to +3.67 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.123 (not significant)

### What This Means
Both groups improved after week 15, but switching branches improved slightly more. However, the difference is small enough that it could reasonably be due to random variation rather than the procedure change itself.

### Validity Checks Passed
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trends (p = 0.66), supporting the validity of the comparison.
- **No side effects:** The new procedure did not significantly affect staffing levels or visitor volume.

## **Decision Framework**

| If future analysis shows... | Then... |
|----------------------------|---------|
| Statistically significant positive effect (p < 0.05) | Proceed with system-wide rollout |
| Effect remains positive but inconclusive | Continue pilot; gather more data |
| No effect or negative effect | Discontinue new procedure; investigate why |

---

## **Technical Notes**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Clustering:** By branch (accounts for within-branch correlation over time)
- **Outcome measure:** Service score index (scale average ~50; higher is better)
- **Data:** 578 observations (34 branches × 17 weeks)
