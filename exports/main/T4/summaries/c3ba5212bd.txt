# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Results of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure Pilot

---

## **EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

The new rotation and shelving procedure **successfully improved service quality**. Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores **1.5 to 2.0 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure. This represents a **3-4% improvement** in service quality. The evidence strongly supports rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Yes** |
| How large is the improvement? | **+1.5 to +2.0 points** (3-4%) |
| Is the result statistically reliable? | **Yes** (p < 0.001) |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Yes** |

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Clear Positive Impact**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Treatment Branches** (switched) | 51.19 | 52.49 | **+1.30** |
| **Control Branches** (no switch) | 50.33 | 49.59 | **-0.75** |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.05** |

Branches using the new procedure improved while those using the old procedure declined, resulting in a **2.05-point net gain** attributable to the new procedure.

### **2. Effect Is Consistent and Robust**

- **All 8 post-switch weeks** showed improvement (ranging from +2.3 to +3.8 points vs. control)
- **Conservative estimate** (using only weeks 10-14 as baseline): **+1.46 points**
- Effect remains significant after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### **3. Meaningful Service Improvement**

- Baseline average service score: **51 points**
- Treatment effect: **+1.5 to +2.0 points**
- Relative improvement: **3-4%**
- No additional staffing or budget required

## **RECOMMENDATIONS**

### **Immediate Actions**

| Action | Timeline | Owner |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Approve system-wide rollout | Next board meeting | Board |
| Develop implementation schedule | 2 weeks | Operations |

### **Rollout Approach**

1. **Phased implementation** over 8-12 weeks to manage support capacity
2. **Training support** for each branch during transition week
3. **Monitoring dashboard** to track service scores post-implementation

### **Success Metrics**

- Target: **+1.5 points** average improvement per branch
- Monitor: Service scores for 8 weeks post-switch
- Flag: Any branch with <1.0 point improvement for additional support

## **RISKS AND MITIGATION**

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Implementation varies by branch | Medium | Standardized training and checklists |
| Effect diminishes over time | Low | Continue monitoring; reinforce best practices |

## **CONCLUSION**

The pilot results confirm what the board expected: **the new procedure works**. With strong statistical evidence, consistent week-to-week results, and meaningful service improvements, we recommend immediate approval for system-wide rollout.
