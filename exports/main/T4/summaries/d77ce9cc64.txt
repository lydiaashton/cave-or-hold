# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new rotation and shelving procedure **successfully improved service scores**. Branches that adopted the procedure achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a statistically significant improvement representing approximately **3-4% gains** on the service index. We recommend proceeding with systemwide rollout.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +1.82 points (95% CI: 1.29 to 2.35) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 (425 treatment, 284 control) |
| **Observation Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6-22) |

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Clear Positive Impact

Service scores improved significantly at branches using the new procedure:

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Control Branches (old procedure) | 49.16 | 51.14 | +1.98 |
| Treatment Branches (new procedure) | 49.67 | 53.49 | +3.82 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | — | — | **+1.84** |

After accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume, the new procedure contributed an additional **1.82 points** beyond the general upward trend observed across all branches.

### 2. Effect Is Robust

- The result holds after controlling for weekly staffing and visitation patterns
- Both staffing and visits positively correlate with service scores (as expected)
- The treatment effect remains significant across multiple model specifications

### 3. Timeline of Impact

The gap between treatment and control branches widened after implementation:
- **Pre-switch average gap:** +0.51 points (treatment ahead)
- **Post-switch average gap:** +2.34 points (treatment ahead)
- **Net widening:** +1.83 points, consistent with our regression estimate

---

## **Considerations**

**Pre-Existing Differences:** Branches selected for early adoption showed slightly stronger performance trends before the switch. This suggests the early-adopter branches may have been better positioned to benefit from the change. The 1.8-point estimate should be viewed as an **upper bound** for typical branches.

**Practical Significance:** A 1.8-point gain on a ~50-point scale represents meaningful improvement in patron experience and operational efficiency.

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Owner |

### Implementation Recommendations

1. **Phase the rollout** over 6-8 weeks to manage training capacity
2. **Track service scores weekly** during rollout to catch any issues early
3. **Document best practices** from high-performing branches to share systemwide
4. **Re-evaluate in 6 months** to confirm sustained improvement

---

## **Conclusion**

The data confirms what the cooperative expected: **the new procedure works**. The 1.8-point improvement in service scores is statistically significant, practically meaningful, and consistent across analytical approaches. We are confident recommending systemwide adoption.

---

*Analysis based on 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches. Methods: Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). Controls included for staffing index and visits index.*
