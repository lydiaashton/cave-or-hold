# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and rotation procedure is associated with a **2.4-point improvement in service scores** compared to branches that retained the old procedure. While this finding is promising, the statistical evidence is **suggestive but not definitive** (p ≈ 0.08–0.10). We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than an immediate full-scale implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measured Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6–14) | After Switch (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| Switched branches (n=20) | 47.8 | 49.5 | **+1.7** |
| Non-switched branches (n=14) | 48.3 | 47.6 | **–0.7** |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.4 points** |

### **2. Statistical Confidence**

- **Effect size:** 2.4 points (approximately ¼ of a standard deviation)
- **95% confidence interval:** –0.5 to +5.3 points
- **Statistical significance:** Marginal (p ≈ 0.08–0.10)
- **Interpretation:** The data favor a positive effect, but we cannot rule out no effect with high confidence

### **3. Consistency Across Branches**

- **75% of switched branches** (15 of 20) improved after the change
- **50% of non-switched branches** (7 of 14) improved during the same period
- The benefit appeared in both early weeks (15–17) and later weeks (18–22) after the switch

### **4. Important Context**

- Service scores were **declining across all branches** over time (approximately 1 point per week)
- Switched branches **reversed this decline**; non-switched branches continued to deteriorate
- The effect holds even after accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume
- Both groups had **similar baseline scores** before the switch, supporting a valid comparison

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Immediate Actions**

1. **Approve phased expansion** to additional branches, beginning with 5–10 locations
2. **Extend data collection** by 8–12 weeks to strengthen statistical evidence
3. **Document implementation practices** at high-performing switched branches to identify success factors

### **Monitoring Requirements**

4. **Track additional metrics** beyond service scores:
   - Staff time allocated to shelving
   - Patron satisfaction feedback
   - Item availability and retrieval times

5. **Establish a review checkpoint** in 90 days to assess:
   - Whether the effect size persists or grows
   - Whether confidence intervals narrow with more data
   - Any operational challenges or unintended consequences

### **Decision Framework**

| Outcome at 90-Day Review | Recommended Action |
|-------------------------|-------------------|
| Effect remains positive with improved precision | Proceed with full cooperative-wide rollout |
| Effect diminishes or becomes unclear | Pause expansion; investigate implementation issues |
| Negative effects emerge | Halt rollout; revert switched branches to old procedure |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure shows **promise without proof**. Given the modest cost of implementation and the absence of detected harms, a **cautious expansion with rigorous follow-up** represents the most prudent path forward. We request board authorization to proceed with the phased rollout and commit to returning with a definitive recommendation after the 90-day review period.
