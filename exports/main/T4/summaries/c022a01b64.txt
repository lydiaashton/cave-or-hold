# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## Bottom Line

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw an average increase of **1.8 points** in their service scores compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **3.7% improvement** over baseline scores. The effect is statistically significant and robust. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## Key Findings

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure; **60 branches** kept the old procedure
- The switch occurred in **week 15**
- Analysis compares changes in the treatment group to changes in the control group

### Results Summary

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect on service score | **+1.84 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | [0.76, 2.93] |
| Statistical significance | **Yes (p = 0.001)** |
| Effect size (vs. baseline) | **+3.7%** |

### What the Numbers Mean
- Branches using the new procedure improved their service scores by **1.8 points more** than branches using the old procedure
- Both groups saw some improvement after week 15, but the treatment group improved significantly more
- The result holds even after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volumes

### Validity Check
Before the switch (weeks 6–14), both groups showed similar trends in service scores. This supports the conclusion that the improvement is due to the new procedure, not pre-existing differences between branches.

---

## Recommendation

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## Next Steps

1. **Implement rollout** to all 60 branches currently using the old procedure
2. **Monitor implementation** to ensure branches achieve similar gains as the pilot group
3. **Track service scores** for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm sustained improvement
4. **Document best practices** from high-performing branches to support training
5. **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** if the new procedure involves additional costs (training, equipment, or staff time)
