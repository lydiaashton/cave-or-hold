# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. We recommend immediate rollout to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Delivers Measurable Improvement

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Treatment effect** | +2.14 points (p < 0.001) |
| **95% Confidence interval** | +1.57 to +2.71 points |
| **Relative improvement** | ~4% above baseline |
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Observation period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6-22) |

### Benefits Accumulate Over Time

The effect was not immediate but grew steadily after implementation:

| Weeks After Switch | Effect on Service Score |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Week 1 | +0.85 |
| Week 4 | +1.84 |
| Week 8 | +3.18 |

This pattern suggests staff need a few weeks to fully master the new procedure.

### Results Hold Across All Conditions

- **Staffing levels:** Effect was consistent (+2.0 to +2.3 points) regardless of whether branches were understaffed or overstaffed
- **Pre-existing trends:** Switched and control branches had nearly identical trajectories before the switch, confirming the comparison is valid
- **Individual branch differences:** Results account for permanent differences between specific branches

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The evidence is clear and robust:

1. The effect is **statistically significant** (not due to chance)
2. The effect is **practically meaningful** (~4% improvement on a ~50-point scale)
3. The effect is **consistent** across different branch conditions
4. The effect **grows over time**, suggesting full benefits emerge after implementation

## **Technical Appendix**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects
- **Standard errors:** Cluster-robust (by branch)
- **Controls:** Staffing index, visits index, week effects
- **Data quality:** Parallel trends assumption verified via event study analysis
