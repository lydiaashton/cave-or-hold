# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure improved service scores by approximately **2 points (4%)**. The evidence is statistically sound, and the expected benefit has been confirmed.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Tested
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** switched to the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Compared service score changes in switching branches against non-switching branches to isolate the procedure's effect

### What We Found

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 49.7 | 50.9 | +1.2 |
| Branches with new procedure | 53.6 | 56.9 | +3.3 |
| **Attributable to new procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

- **Effect size:** +2.03 points on the service score index
- **Confidence:** 95% confidence interval ranges from +0.5 to +3.6 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.011 (highly unlikely to be due to chance)
- **Practical significance:** ~4% improvement over the baseline average of 52

### Why We Trust This Result
- Both groups followed parallel trends before the switch, confirming the comparison is valid
- The effect persists after accounting for branch differences, seasonal week effects, staffing levels, and visitor volume
- The direction of the effect matches our expectation

---

## **Recommendation and Next Steps**

### 1. **Approve system-wide rollout**
The new procedure delivers measurable improvement. Extend it to all remaining branches.

### 2. **Plan the transition**
- Develop training materials based on the 20 early-adopter branches' experience
- Phase the rollout to allow for support and troubleshooting

### 3. **Monitor outcomes**
- Track service scores for 8–12 weeks post-rollout
- Flag any branches that do not show expected improvement for additional support

### 4. **Document lessons learned**
- Capture operational insights from the pilot branches
- Update standard operating procedures accordingly
