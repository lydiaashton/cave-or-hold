# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Subject: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service score improvements of **2.0–2.3 points (approximately 4–5%)** compared to branches using the old procedure. The evidence is strong and consistent.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.3 points average improvement for switched branches |
| **Statistical Confidence** | p < 0.001; 95% CI: [+1.9, +2.6] points |
| **Branch Adoption Rate** | 73% of switched branches saw improvement |
| **Biggest Winners** | Lowest-performing branches gained +3.8 points on average |
| **Validation** | Pre-switch trends were parallel; effect is attributable to the procedure change |

---

## **EVIDENCE SUMMARY**

**Study Design:** Difference-in-differences analysis comparing 425 switched branches against 284 control branches over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22), with the procedure change occurring at week 15.

**Results:**

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Control (Old Procedure) | 51.0 | 51.2 | +0.2 |
| Treatment (New Procedure) | 51.3 | 53.5 | **+2.3** |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+2.0** |

**Robustness:** Results hold after controlling for staffing levels, visitor volume, and branch-specific characteristics.

---

## **STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS**

1. **Equity Benefit:** The procedure disproportionately helps struggling branches, reducing performance gaps across the system.

2. **Scalable Impact:** A 4–5% service score improvement system-wide represents meaningful progress on our core mission.

3. **Low Risk:** 73% of branches improved; few experienced declines. The procedure appears robust across different branch contexts.
