# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** Analysis of weeks 6–22 service score data  
**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **1.8 points higher** (a **3.5% improvement**) than would have been expected without the change. This effect is statistically robust and consistent across the post-switch period. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Analysis Overview**
- **709 branches** analyzed: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 maintained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), with the switch implemented at week 15
- Method: Difference-in-differences regression with controls for staffing and visitor volume

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 51.07 | 52.28 | +1.21 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 50.17 | 49.58 | −0.59 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.80** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Estimated effect:** +1.81 service score points
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.27 to 2.34 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Result remains significant** after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### **Validity Check**
The analysis relies on the assumption that switched and non-switched branches would have followed similar trends absent the intervention. Pre-switch data supports this: the gap between groups was stable (average 0.9 points) before week 15, then widened systematically afterward (average 2.7 points), consistent with a genuine treatment effect.

The evidence strongly supports expanding the new rotation and shelving procedure across the cooperative. The improvement in service scores is meaningful, statistically reliable, and achieved without requiring additional staffing or resources. A full rollout would benefit patrons across all 709 branches.
