# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores**, with switched branches improving approximately **2.6 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure. While this result is encouraging and aligns with expectations, the evidence is **not yet statistically definitive**. We recommend **continuing the evaluation while considering a phased expansion**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Measured**
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (February–June)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores average ~50 points; higher scores indicate better service

### **Results**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment effect** | +2.6 service score points |
| **Improvement magnitude** | ~5% above baseline |
| **Statistical confidence** | 90% confident the effect is positive; 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.4 to +5.7 |
| **Pre-existing trends** | ✓ Treatment and control branches showed similar patterns before the switch |

### **What This Means**

Branches that switched to the new procedure improved their service scores by **3.5 points** after the change, compared to only **0.9 points** for branches that kept the old procedure. The difference of 2.6 points is the estimated effect of the new procedure.

The result is **positive and directionally correct** but carries some uncertainty due to the limited number of branches and post-switch observation weeks.

---

## **Recommendations & Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **1. Continue current evaluation** | Ongoing | Additional weeks of data will strengthen statistical confidence |
| **2. Expand to 5–10 additional branches** | Next quarter | Phased rollout allows continued learning while extending benefits |
| **3. Analyze branch-level variation** | 4–6 weeks | Identify which branch types benefit most to prioritize rollout |
| **4. Revisit full rollout decision** | After 12+ post-switch weeks | Board can decide with stronger evidence base |

---

## **Decision Options for the Board**

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **A. Full rollout now** | Maximizes potential benefit quickly | Risk of scaling if effect proves smaller than estimated |
| **B. Phased expansion (recommended)** | Balances benefit with continued learning | Slower path to system-wide implementation |
| **C. Wait for more data** | Strongest evidence before committing | Delivers benefits to fewer branches in near term |
