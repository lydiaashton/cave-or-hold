# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** New Branch Procedure Impact on Service Scores  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks of data)

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new stocking and rotation procedure achieved service scores **2.0 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This confirms the expected improvement and supports a system-wide rollout.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Estimated improvement** | +1.98 service score points |
| **Confidence range** | +1.4 to +2.6 points |
| **Relative gain** | 3.8% above baseline |
| **Statistical confidence** | >99.9% (p < 0.001) |

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence
- **709 branches** were tracked over 17 weeks
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure at week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Before the switch, both groups had nearly identical scores (~52 points)
- After the switch, treatment branches improved while control branches declined slightly

### What Changed
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| New procedure branches | 52.15 | 53.77 | **+1.62** |
| Old procedure branches | 52.27 | 51.94 | **−0.33** |
| **Net effect** | | | **+1.95** |

### Why We're Confident
1. **Clean comparison:** Control branches provide a counterfactual—what would have happened without the change
2. **Parallel trends:** Before week 15, both groups moved together; the divergence began exactly at the switch
3. **Robust to other factors:** The effect holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
4. **Consistent over time:** Gains persisted across the 8-week post-switch period

---

## **Recommendations**

### 1. Approve System-Wide Rollout
The data supports expanding the new procedure to all remaining branches. The 2-point improvement is meaningful for patron experience and comes with no identified downside.

### 2. Implement in Phases
Roll out over 2–3 months to allow for training and troubleshooting. Prioritize branches with:
- Higher visitor volumes (larger absolute impact)
- Staff capacity to adopt new procedures quickly

### 3. Track and Support
- Continue weekly service score monitoring
- Flag branches that don't show improvement within 4 weeks
- Provide additional training where needed

### 4. Document Best Practices
Capture the specific elements of the new procedure that drive improvement. This will streamline training and ensure consistent implementation.
