# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by an additional **+1.9 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and represents a meaningful gain in service quality. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 709 total |
| **Treatment group** | 425 branches (new procedure) |
| **Control group** | 284 branches (old procedure) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch point** | Week 15 |

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 48.1 | 49.2 | +1.1 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.5 | 53.6 | +3.1 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.9** |

The **+1.9 point net improvement** is:
- **Statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- **95% confidence interval:** +1.4 to +2.5 points
- **Robust** after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### **Performance vs. Benchmark**
- System-wide average service score: **~50**
- Treatment branches post-switch: **53.6** (+3.6 vs. benchmark)
- Control branches post-switch: **49.2** (−0.8 vs. benchmark)

---

## **Analysis Notes**

1. **Methodology:** Difference-in-differences analysis comparing changes in treatment vs. control groups, accounting for pre-existing differences between branches.

2. **Validity checks:** Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, supporting the causal interpretation of the results.

3. **Controls:** Results hold after adjusting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches.

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with support for training and transition.

3. **Document best practices** from the 425 early-adopter branches.

4. **Monitor outcomes** for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm sustained improvement.

5. **Track additional metrics** (staff efficiency, patron satisfaction, circulation) to capture full value.
