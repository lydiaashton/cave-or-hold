# **TO: Library Cooperative Board of Directors**
# **FROM: Data Analysis Team**
# **DATE: November 2025**
# **SUBJECT: New Branch Procedure Rollout – Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched saw a **statistically significant increase of approximately 2 points** (about 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The evidence supports proceeding with systemwide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The New Procedure Worked**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | **+1.9 points** |
| **Confidence range** | 1.4 to 2.5 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | Greater than 99.9% |
| **Relative gain** | **~4%** above baseline |

Branches using the new procedure improved from an average of 50.8 to 52.1, while comparison branches remained flat (48.8 to 48.3).

### **The Gap Widened After Switch**

| Period | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches | Difference |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|------------|
| Before (Weeks 6-14) | 50.75 | 48.85 | 1.9 pts |
| After (Weeks 15-22) | 52.14 | 48.27 | 3.9 pts |
| **Change** | **+1.39** | **-0.58** | **+2.0 pts** |

### **Effect Grew Over Time**

The benefit increased as branches gained experience with the new procedure:
- **First 2 weeks:** ~2.8 point gap
- **Weeks 3-4:** ~3.6 point gap  
- **Weeks 5-8:** ~4.5 point gap

This suggests branches continue to optimize and benefit from the new procedure with practice.

### **Effect Holds After Accounting for Other Factors**

The improvement remains significant even after controlling for:
- Staffing levels
- Visitor volume

This indicates the procedure change itself—not external factors—drove the improvement.

---

## **Important Context**

Branches selected for early adoption were already showing slightly stronger performance trends before the switch. Our analysis accounts for this, but the true effect may be modestly smaller than the 1.9-point estimate. **Even with this consideration, the procedure clearly delivered positive results.**

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout.** The new procedure:
- ✅ Delivered measurable service score improvements
- ✅ Showed consistent benefits across the post-switch period
- ✅ Benefits grew as branches gained experience
- ✅ Remained significant after accounting for staffing and traffic
