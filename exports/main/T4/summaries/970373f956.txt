# **Library Cooperative Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure gained approximately **2.1 points** on the service score index compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and meaningful for branch performance. **We recommend proceeding with a full rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Analysis Overview**
- **709 branches** studied: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 maintained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- Method: Difference-in-differences comparison, controlling for staffing and visitor volume

### **Service Score Results**

| Period | Switched Branches | Control Branches | Difference |
|--------|------------------|------------------|------------|
| Before switch (weeks 6–14) | 51.18 | 49.07 | 2.11 |
| After switch (weeks 15–22) | 55.09 | 50.85 | 4.24 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.13** |

- **Effect size: +2.13 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.56 to 2.70)
- **Statistical significance: p < 0.001**
- Effect remains robust after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

### **Supporting Evidence**

1. **Benefits accumulate over time**: The advantage grew from 3.6 points in the first post-switch week to 6.0 points by week 22, indicating staff proficiency improves with experience.

2. **Valid comparison**: Pre-switch trends were stable between groups, confirming the control branches provide an appropriate baseline.

3. **Consistent positive effect**: Every post-switch week showed a larger gap than the pre-switch average, with no weeks showing negative effects.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Action | Priority | Timeline |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Roll out new procedure to all 284 remaining branches | High | Immediate |
| Develop training materials from early-adopter branches | High | 1–2 months |
| Monitor service scores during rollout | Medium | Ongoing |
| Conduct follow-up evaluation post-rollout | Medium | 3–6 months |

---

## **Expected Impact**

If the remaining 284 branches achieve similar gains:
- System-wide average service score would increase by approximately **2 points**
- Given the index scale (average ~50), this represents a **4% improvement** in service quality
- Benefits are likely to grow as staff across all branches gain experience with the new procedure
