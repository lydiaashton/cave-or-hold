# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

---

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new branch rotation and shelving procedure **significantly improves service scores**. After analyzing 17 weeks of data from 709 branches, we find the new procedure increased service scores by **2.16 points (4.4% improvement)** with high statistical confidence. 

**We recommend immediate system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Yes** |
| By how much? | **+2.16 points** (4.4% improvement) |
| How confident are we? | **95% confidence** the effect is real and between 1.6–2.7 points |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Yes** |

---

## **Evidence Summary**

### Study Scope
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (Weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** switched to new procedure at Week 15
- **284 branches** continued with old procedure (control group)
- **12,053 weekly observations** analyzed

### Key Results

| Metric | Treatment (New) | Control (Old) |
|--------|-----------------|---------------|
| Average score before switch | 49.72 | 49.13 |
| Average score after switch | 52.75 | 50.00 |
| **Change** | **+3.02** | **+0.87** |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | **+2.16 points** | — |

### Branch-Level Impact
- **81%** of branches using the new procedure improved their scores
- Only **62%** of branches using the old procedure improved
- The new procedure delivers consistent benefits across most branches

---

## **Why We Are Confident**

1. **Strong research design**: The control group (branches keeping the old procedure) isolates the effect of the new procedure from other factors like seasonal changes or system-wide trends.

2. **Statistically rigorous**: The result is highly significant (p < 0.001), meaning there is less than a 0.1% chance this finding is due to random variation.

3. **Validated assumptions**: Before the switch, both groups had nearly identical score trends, confirming the comparison is fair.

4. **Robust to other factors**: The effect holds even after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.

---

## **Recommendations**

### Immediate Actions
1. **Approve system-wide rollout** to all 284 branches still using the old procedure
2. **Target completion** within 4–6 weeks to maximize benefit

### Follow-Up Actions
3. **Monitor scores** for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale
4. **Support underperformers**: Approximately 20% of treatment branches did not see improvement. These branches may need additional training or resources.
5. **Set board expectations**: Anticipate a **2-point average increase** in system-wide service scores following full implementation.

---

## **Expected Impact**

Based on current data, full rollout should deliver:

- **System-wide service score increase**: ~2 points
- **Branches benefiting**: ~80% of all branches
- **Timeline to see results**: 4–8 weeks after implementation
