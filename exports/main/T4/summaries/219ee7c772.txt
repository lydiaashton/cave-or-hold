# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.1 points** (approximately 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, appeared immediately after implementation, and persisted throughout the observation period.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Main Effect** | +2.1 points (95% CI: 0.9 to 3.4) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Effect Size** | ~4% improvement over average score of 49.5 |
| **Robustness** | Effect unchanged (+2.2 pts) after controlling for staffing and visits |
| **Pre-Trend Validation** | No differential trends before switch (p = 0.51) ✓ |

**Effect by Week After Switch:**
- Week 15: +3.2 points*** 
- Week 16: +2.4 points**
- Week 17: +3.0 points***
- Week 18: +2.6 points***
- Weeks 19-22: +1.3 to +1.8 points (some attenuation, still positive)

***p<0.01, **p<0.05

**Sample:** 150 branches (90 switched, 60 control), 17 weeks of data, 2,550 observations

---

## **VISUAL EVIDENCE**

Three charts support this analysis:

1. **trends_plot.png** — Service score trends over time. Treatment and control branches tracked closely before week 15, then diverged after the switch.

2. **event_study.png** — Treatment effect for each post-switch week. All weeks show positive effects; strongest in the first month.

3. **before_after.png** — Box plots comparing before/after scores. Treatment branches show a clear upward shift; control branches show minimal change.
