# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **successfully improved service scores**. Branches that adopted the change saw scores increase by **2.35 points** (5% improvement) relative to branches using the old procedure. The result is statistically significant and consistent. **We recommend proceeding with a cooperative-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.35 points on service score index |
| **Statistical Confidence** | p < 0.01 (highly significant) |
| **Branches Improved** | 16 of 20 switched branches (80%) |
| **Baseline Comparison** | Control branches improved only 1.42 points |

### What the Data Shows

1. **Clear Causal Impact**: Using a rigorous difference-in-differences design, we isolated the procedure's effect from other factors (staffing, visit volume, branch characteristics, and seasonal trends). The 2.35-point gain is attributable to the new procedure itself.

2. **Validated Assumptions**: Before the switch, treatment and control branches followed parallel trends—confirming our comparison is sound. The improvement emerged only after implementation.

3. **Consistent Performance**: The effect held across most post-implementation weeks, with particularly strong gains in the first three weeks after the switch.

4. **Meaningful Scale**: On a service score index where 50 represents the average branch, a 2.35-point gain represents tangible improvement in patron experience.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve cooperative-wide implementation** of the new rotation and shelving procedure. The evidence supports your expectation that this change would improve service quality.

## **Appendix: Analysis Summary**

- **Data**: 578 weekly observations across 34 branches (weeks 6–22)
- **Treatment**: 20 branches adopted new procedure at week 15; 14 branches served as controls
- **Method**: Difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects
- **Controls**: Staffing index, visits index, branch-specific factors, temporal trends
