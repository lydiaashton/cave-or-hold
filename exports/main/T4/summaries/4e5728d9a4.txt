# **Library Cooperative Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **significantly improved service scores** at branches that adopted it. Branches using the new procedure saw an average **1.85-point increase** in service scores compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p = 0.001) and represents approximately a **3.7% improvement** relative to the typical branch score of 50.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **60 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Analysis accounts for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week variations

### Service Score Changes

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 50.04 | 51.39 | +1.35 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.74 | 53.94 | +3.20 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.85** |

The 1.85-point net effect is the estimated impact of the new procedure, isolated from other factors that may have affected all branches.

### Statistical Confidence
- **95% confidence interval:** 0.73 to 2.97 points
- **P-value:** 0.001 (highly statistically significant)
- **Pre-trend validation:** No significant differences between groups before the switch, supporting a causal interpretation

### Effect Timeline
The improvement appeared immediately and was sustained:
- **First week after switch:** +2.44 points (p = 0.003)
- **Weeks 3–5:** Effects ranged from +2.3 to +3.4 points (all significant)
- Effects remained positive through the end of the observation period

## **Technical Appendix**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects
- **Standard errors:** Clustered at the branch level
- **Data source:** branches.csv (2,550 observations)
- **Validation:** Event study analysis confirms parallel pre-treatment trends
