# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by an average of **2.1 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and represents approximately a **4% gain** in service quality.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**1. Clear, Measurable Improvement**
- Service scores increased by **+2.15 points** after adoption (95% confidence interval: +0.96 to +3.33)
- The result is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)
- Against a baseline average of ~50 points, this represents a **4.3% improvement**

**2. Effect Was Immediate and Sustained**
- Weeks 15-18 (early post-switch): +2.32 points
- Weeks 19-22 (later post-switch): +1.97 points
- No evidence of fading effects over the observation period

**3. Rigorous Analysis Approach**
- Used **difference-in-differences** methodology comparing 90 switched branches against 60 control branches
- Controlled for branch-specific characteristics, time trends, staffing levels, and visitor volume
- Pre-switch trends between groups were not significantly different, supporting the validity of the comparison

**4. Study Scope**
- 150 branches total across the cooperative
- 17 weeks of data per branch (weeks 6-22)
- Switch occurred at week 15

---

### **NEXT STEPS**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to remaining 60 branches |
| **2** | Monitor service scores during transition to confirm expected gains are realized |
| **3** | Identify branches that may need additional implementation support |
| **4** | Schedule follow-up evaluation 3-6 months after full rollout |

---

### **RISKS AND CONSIDERATIONS**

- **Low risk:** The procedure has already been tested on 90 branches with positive results
- **Implementation variance:** Some branches may see larger or smaller gains depending on local conditions
- **Staffing matters:** Analysis confirms that adequate staffing amplifies service score improvements
