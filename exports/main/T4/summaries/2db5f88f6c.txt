# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout.** The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure maintained performance better than those that kept the old procedure, with a net benefit of **+1.9 points** on the service score index (approximately 4% improvement).

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement**
| Group | Pre-Switch Avg | Post-Switch Avg | Change |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|--------|
| New Procedure (90 branches) | 49.63 | 48.93 | -0.70 |
| Old Procedure (60 branches) | 50.56 | 47.98 | -2.57 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+1.87** |

Both groups experienced score declines after week 15 (likely due to external factors affecting all branches). However, branches with the new procedure declined **much less**—preserving roughly 2 points of service quality compared to the old procedure.

### **2. Statistical Confidence**
- **Effect size:** +1.86 points (95% confidence interval: 0.73 to 2.99)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Practical significance:** ~4% improvement relative to average scores

### **3. Analysis Validity**
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Pre-switch performance trends were statistically similar between groups (p = 0.86), supporting the credibility of the comparison
- **Robust to controls:** Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and patron visit volumes
- **Adequate sample:** 150 branches, 17 weeks, 2,550 observations

### **4. Implementation Pattern**
- Strongest benefits appeared in the first two weeks after switch (+1.0 to +1.3 points)
- Effects varied in subsequent weeks but remained positive on average
- No evidence of implementation fatigue or deterioration over the 8-week post-switch period

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Immediate Actions**
1. **Approve systemwide rollout** to the remaining 60 branches currently using the old procedure
2. **Schedule implementation** over the next quarter to allow for training and support

### **Ongoing Monitoring**
3. **Track service scores** during rollout using the same weekly index
4. **Flag any branches** showing unexpected declines for additional support
5. **Document lessons learned** from the 90 early-adopter branches

### **Follow-Up Evaluation**
6. **Reassess in 90 days** with full system data to confirm effects persist at scale
7. **Consider cost-benefit analysis** if implementation costs were tracked
