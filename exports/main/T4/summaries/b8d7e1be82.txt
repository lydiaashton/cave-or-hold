# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**Date:** Analysis of Weeks 6–22  
**Data:** 12,053 weekly observations across 709 branches

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed an average increase of **+1.94 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **4% improvement** over baseline service levels. The effect is statistically significant and persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout, with continued monitoring to ensure benefits are sustained.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +1.94 points (95% CI: +1.40 to +2.49) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Percentage Improvement** | ~4% over baseline |
| **Branches Adopting New Procedure** | 425 (60%) |
| **Branches Retaining Old Procedure** | 284 (40%) |

**1. Clear Improvement After Switch**
- Switched branches improved from 50.69 to 52.16 (+1.47 points)
- Non-switched branches declined from 49.52 to 49.05 (−0.47 points)
- The gap between groups widened significantly after week 15

**2. Benefits Grow Over Time**
- Immediate post-switch effect: ~+2.5 points
- Effect by weeks 7–8 post-switch: ~+3.3 to +3.4 points
- This suggests branches improve with experience using the new procedure

**3. Effect Is Robust**
- Unchanged when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Both staffing and visits independently affect service scores, but do not explain the procedure effect

---

### **IMPORTANT CAVEAT**

Switched branches showed **gradual improvement relative to non-switched branches even before the procedure change**. This suggests:

- Early-adopter branches may have had more capacity for improvement
- The true causal effect may be somewhat smaller than our main estimate (potentially +1.3 to +1.6 points rather than +1.9 points)

**However**, the acceleration in improvement after week 15 is clear and statistically significant, indicating the procedure change did contribute meaningfully to service quality.

---

### **NEXT STEPS**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Proceed with system-wide rollout** to remaining 284 branches |
| **2** | **Provide training and support** — benefits grow with experience |
| **3** | **Monitor outcomes for 3–6 months** post-rollout to confirm sustained effects |
| **4** | **Review selection criteria** for early adoption to ensure equitable implementation |
| **5** | **Coordinate with staffing decisions** — staffing and procedure improvements are complementary |
