# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

## **New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure: Impact Evaluation**

---

### **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2 points** (4% above baseline). The effect is statistically significant and strengthened over the 8 weeks following implementation. **We recommend proceeding with systemwide rollout.**

---

### **Key Findings**

**Study Overview**
- 709 branches tracked over 17 weeks (February–June)
- 425 branches adopted the new procedure in week 15; 284 branches continued with the old procedure
- Service scores average ~50 points (higher is better)

**Results**

| | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branches with old procedure | 50.5 | 50.9 | +0.4 |
| Branches with new procedure | 51.4 | 54.0 | +2.6 |
| **Attributable to new procedure** | | | **+2.2** |

- **Treatment effect: +2.2 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.7 to 2.8 points)
- **Statistical significance: p < 0.001**
- Effects grew over time, from ~1 point in weeks 15–18 to ~1.5 points in weeks 19–22

**Why This Matters**
A 2-point improvement represents meaningful progress in member experience across the cooperative. At scale, this improvement applies to all 709 branches.

---

### **Recommendations**

1. **Approve systemwide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches

2. **Allow 6–8 weeks for full effect** – Benefits accumulated over time; branches should not expect immediate maximum impact

3. **Monitor scores during transition** – Track service scores weekly for the first 12 weeks post-implementation at each branch

4. **Document implementation practices** – Capture what early-adopter branches did during transition to support training materials

---

### **Decision Requested**

Authorize the operations team to begin systemwide rollout of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure, with implementation completed within the next quarter.
