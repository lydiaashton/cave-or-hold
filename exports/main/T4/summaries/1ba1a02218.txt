# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** Analysis of weeks 6–22 service data  
**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a **3.5% improvement** that is statistically significant and operationally meaningful. We recommend immediate approval to extend the new procedure to all 709 branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **The Evidence**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| Average score before switch | 51.07 | 50.17 |
| Average score after switch | 52.28 | 49.58 |
| **Change** | **+1.21** | **-0.59** |

**Net improvement attributable to new procedure: +1.81 points**

### **Why We're Confident**

- **Strong statistical evidence:** The result is highly significant (p < 0.001), with a 95% confidence interval of 1.27 to 2.34 points
- **Clean comparison:** 425 branches switched, 284 did not—both groups followed parallel trends before the switch
- **Robust to other factors:** The effect holds even after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Consistent performance:** Improvement persisted across all 8 weeks after the switch, with no sign of fading

### **What This Means**

The average branch scores about 51 on the service index. An 1.8-point gain represents meaningful progress toward service excellence. Moreover, non-switched branches drifted downward after week 15, suggesting the new procedure may also help branches maintain service quality amid operational pressures.

The data confirm what operational experience suggested: the new rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. With 425 branches already successfully using the procedure and a clear, measurable benefit demonstrated, there is no reason to delay extending it system-wide.

**We request Board approval to proceed with full rollout.**
