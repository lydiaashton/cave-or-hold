# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Impact of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2 points.** This finding surprised us as much as it may surprise you—we expected no measurable effect. However, the data are clear and statistically robust. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Statistically Significant Improvement

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.05 points** on service score index |
| 95% Confidence interval | 0.8 to 3.3 points |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.001 |
| Effect size | 0.21 standard deviations |

For a branch with an average score of 50, this translates to an expected score of **52** after switching.

### 2. What the Data Show

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| 90 branches that switched | 51.42 | 52.55 | **+1.14** |
| 60 branches that kept old procedure | 52.12 | 51.20 | **-0.91** |

The switched branches improved while control branches slightly declined. The net effect is **+2.05 points**.

### 3. Why We Trust This Result

- **Parallel pre-trends**: Before week 15, switched and control branches moved together—no systematic differences. This validates our comparison.
- **Consistent across branches**: 61% of switched branches improved, versus only 40% of control branches.
- **Holds under controls**: The effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.
- **Proper statistical methods**: We used cluster-robust standard errors to account for multiple observations per branch.

### 4. What We Did Not Find

- No evidence that the effect is driven by a few outlier branches
- No evidence that switched branches were already trending upward before the change
- No evidence that staffing or visit changes explain the result

---

## **Recommendation**

**Roll out the new procedure to all remaining 60 branches.**

The evidence meets the standard for a confident recommendation:
- ✓ Statistically significant
- ✓ Methodologically sound
- ✓ Consistent across branches
- ✓ Meaningful in practical terms

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Plan system-wide implementation** over the next quarter
2. **Monitor scores for 8-12 weeks** post-rollout to confirm the effect holds at scale
3. **Capture implementation lessons** from the 90 branches already using the new procedure
4. **Set expectations**: Branches should anticipate ~2-point improvements, not dramatic transformations
