# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw an average increase of **2.3 points** on the service score index (approximately **4.7% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is highly statistically significant and consistent across the 425 branches that switched. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** analyzed over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **284 branches** served as a control group
- Analysis compared service score changes in both groups before and after the switch

### Results Summary

| | Control Group | Treatment Group |
|---|---|---|
| Average score BEFORE | 48.7 | 50.1 |
| Average score AFTER | 49.4 | 53.2 |
| **Change** | **+0.8** | **+3.1** |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | **+2.3 points** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Treatment effect:** +2.34 points
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 1.76 to 2.92 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Validation
Service score trends for both groups followed parallel patterns in the 9 weeks before the switch, confirming the improvement is attributable to the procedure change rather than pre-existing differences between branches.

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** to the remaining 284 branches

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with phased deployment to manage training and transition

3. **Monitor performance** for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm sustained improvement

4. **Document best practices** from the 425 early-adopter branches for training materials

5. **Evaluate complementary improvements** in staffing and capacity, which also affect service scores
