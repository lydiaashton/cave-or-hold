# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Roll out the new procedure to all branches.** The evidence shows the new procedure significantly improved service scores by protecting branches from the decline experienced by those keeping the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant, practically meaningful, and the analysis is methodologically sound.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Worked**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| **New procedure** (90 branches) | 50.30 | 50.56 | +0.26 |
| **Old procedure** (60 branches) | 48.88 | 46.88 | −2.00 |

**Estimated effect: +2.26 points** (95% CI: 0.95 to 3.56; p=0.0009)

This represents approximately a **4.5% improvement** relative to baseline scores.

### **2. What This Means**

The new procedure did not dramatically boost scores—it **prevented the decline** that affected branches keeping the old procedure. Branches using the old procedure saw service scores drop by 2 points after week 15, while branches using the new procedure held steady.

### **3. Why We Trust These Results**

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| **Parallel trends before switch** | ✓ Verified (p=0.46) |
| **Results hold controlling for staffing & visits** | ✓ Yes |
| **Complete data** | ✓ All 150 branches, all 17 weeks |
| **Statistical method** | Difference-in-differences with cluster-robust standard errors |

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate system-wide rollout** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all 60 branches still using the old procedure.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Authorize rollout** to remaining branches within the next quarter
2. **Develop training materials** to ensure consistent implementation
3. **Monitor service scores** for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effect persists
4. **Investigate the decline** in old-procedure branches—understanding this may yield additional operational insights

## **Appendix: Analysis Details**

- **Data:** 2,550 observations (150 branches × 17 weeks)
- **Study period:** Weeks 6–22; switch occurred week 15
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch-clustered standard errors
- **Software:** Python (pandas, statsmodels)
