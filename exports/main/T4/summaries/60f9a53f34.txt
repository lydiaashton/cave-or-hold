# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks of data from 150 branches)

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Roll out the new procedure to all branches.** The new rotation and shelving procedure improved service scores by approximately **2 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This contradicts the initial expectation that the change would have no effect. The improvement is statistically significant and represents meaningful progress in branch service quality.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### What We Analyzed
- **150 library branches** tracked weekly from week 6 through week 22
- **90 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **60 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores average around 50 points (higher is better)

### What We Found

| | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branches with **New Procedure** | 49.6 | 48.9 | −0.7 |
| Branches with **Old Procedure** | 50.6 | 48.0 | −2.6 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

**Interpretation:** While service scores declined across all branches after week 14 (likely due to seasonal factors), branches using the new procedure declined much less. The new procedure **protected branches from roughly 2 points of score erosion**.

### Why We Trust This Result

1. **Comparable groups:** Before the switch, both groups followed nearly identical trends
2. **Statistically significant:** The probability this result occurred by chance is less than 1% (p = 0.001)
3. **Robust to controls:** The effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
4. **Meaningful magnitude:** A 2-point gain on a ~50-point scale represents approximately a **4% improvement**

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure. The evidence indicates this change delivers measurable service quality benefits at no apparent cost to branch operations.
