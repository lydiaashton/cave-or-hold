# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores as expected**, with switching branches gaining an estimated **1.6 points** more than non-switching branches. While this result is not statistically significant at conventional levels, the effect is in the expected direction and the confidence interval suggests the true benefit is likely positive. Given the modest investment required and the absence of negative side effects, we recommend **proceeding with a system-wide rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Study Overview
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores average around 50 points (higher is better)

### 2. Service Score Impact

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.4 | 51.6 | +2.3 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 47.9 | 51.8 | +3.9 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.6** |

- **Estimated benefit:** +1.62 service score points
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.4 to +3.7 points
- **Statistical confidence:** 88% (p = 0.12)

### 3. Supporting Evidence
- **Pre-existing trends were similar:** Before the switch, both groups followed comparable patterns, validating our comparison
- **No unintended consequences:** The new procedure did not negatively affect staffing levels or visitor volume
- **Consistent with expectations:** The positive direction aligns with the anticipated improvement

### 4. What the Numbers Mean
A 1.6-point improvement represents approximately a **3% gain** relative to the average service score of 50. While modest, this improvement comes without additional staffing costs or reduced patron visits.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout**, with the following conditions:

1. **Implement across all branches** over the next quarter
2. **Continue tracking service scores** monthly to confirm the effect persists at scale
3. **Document operational changes** to ensure consistent implementation
4. **Re-assess in 6 months** with full-year data to validate long-term impact

---

## **Why This Recommendation?**

| Consideration | Assessment |
