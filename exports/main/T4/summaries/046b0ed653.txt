# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2 points.** This effect is statistically significant and grew stronger over time as staff became familiar with the new system. We recommend proceeding with a full rollout to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.0 points on service score index |
| **95% confidence interval** | +1.5 to +2.5 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

### **What Happened**

Both groups experienced a decline in service scores after week 15, but branches using the new procedure declined **significantly less**:

- **Control branches** (old procedure): −3.1 points
- **Treatment branches** (new procedure): −1.1 points
- **Net benefit of new procedure**: +2.0 points

### **The Effect Strengthened Over Time**

| Period After Switch | Estimated Effect |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Weeks 15–17 (adjustment) | −0.8 to +0.5 points |
| Weeks 18–22 (stabilized) | +2.7 to +4.4 points |

This pattern indicates a learning curve. The full benefit emerged after 3–4 weeks as staff gained proficiency with the new procedure.

### **Robustness Checks**

The +2.0 point estimate holds across multiple analytical approaches:
- Simple comparison of means
- Regression with controls for staffing and visit volumes
- Models accounting for branch-specific characteristics
- Standard errors adjusted for repeated observations per branch

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Approve full rollout** to all 284 remaining branches | Clear evidence of positive impact |
| **2** | **Plan 4-week transition support** for each switching branch | Benefits grow after initial adjustment period |
| **3** | **Share implementation guides** from early-adopter branches | Accelerates learning curve |
| **4** | **Monitor scores for 8–12 weeks post-rollout** | Confirms effect holds at cooperative-wide scale |
| **5** | **Investigate system-wide score decline** after week 15 | External factors may need attention |

---

## **Risk Assessment**

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Effect smaller at scale | Low | Effect is robust across 425 diverse branches |
| Implementation challenges | Medium | Transition support and peer learning recommended |
| Staff resistance | Medium | Early adopters can serve as champions |

---

## **Conclusion**

The data confirms what we hoped: **the new shelving and rotation procedure improves service quality.** The 2-point gain on a ~50-point index represents a meaningful 4% improvement. With proper transition support, we expect similar gains across all branches.

**Decision requested:** Authorization to proceed with cooperative-wide rollout.

---

*Analysis based on weekly service scores from 709 branches, weeks 6–22. Difference-in-differences methodology with branch fixed effects and clustered standard errors.*
