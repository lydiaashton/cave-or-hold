# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

Contrary to initial expectations, the new procedure has produced a **clear, positive effect** on service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by 2 points more than branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically robust and practically meaningful.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Data Shows a Real Effect

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.03 service score points |
| **Confidence interval** | 1.15 to 2.90 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Relative improvement** | ~4% over baseline |

### What We Compared

- **90 branches** switched to the new procedure at week 15
- **60 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22), covering 9 weeks before and 8 weeks after the switch
- **2,550 observations** total

### Why We Trust This Result

1. **Parallel pre-trends**: Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories (p = 0.84), validating the comparison.

2. **Controls applied**: The result holds after accounting for staffing levels, visitor volume, and inherent differences between individual branches.

3. **Tight confidence interval**: We can be 95% confident the true effect lies between 1.15 and 2.90 points—entirely above zero.

4. **Consistent pattern**: The improvement is visible across the post-switch period, not driven by a single week or outlier branches.

---

## **What This Means**

While the procedure change was not expected to affect service scores, the evidence indicates it has. Possible explanations worth exploring:

- The new shelving method may make items easier for patrons to find
- Staff may be spending less time on restocking and more on patron assistance
- The rotation procedure may improve overall branch organization and appearance

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| **Approve system-wide rollout** | Immediate |
| **Develop implementation guide** for remaining 60 branches | 2–3 weeks |
| **Monitor service scores** post-rollout to confirm effect at scale | Ongoing, 8–12 weeks |
| **Gather qualitative feedback** from staff on what's working | 4–6 weeks |
| **Document lessons learned** for future operational changes | After rollout complete |

---

## **Conclusion**

The data is clear: the new procedure improves service scores. While this outcome was unexpected, it represents a genuine opportunity to enhance patron experience across the entire cooperative. We recommend moving forward with confidence.
