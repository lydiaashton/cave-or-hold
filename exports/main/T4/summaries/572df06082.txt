# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a **1.9-point increase** in service scores compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **4% improvement** on the typical service score of 50.

**Recommendation:** Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Design
We analyzed weekly service scores from **709 branches** over 17 weeks. When the new procedure launched in week 15, **425 branches adopted it** while **284 branches continued with the old procedure**. This created a natural experiment allowing us to isolate the procedure's effect from other factors.

### Results Summary

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 49.09 | 50.19 | +1.10 |
| Branches adopting new procedure | 49.99 | 53.01 | +3.02 |
| **Effect attributable to new procedure** | | | **+1.92** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Estimated effect:** +1.92 service score points
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 1.37 to 2.46 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Robustness:** Result holds after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Why This Evidence Is Strong

1. **Clean comparison:** By comparing branches that switched to those that didn't—both before and after the change—we isolated the procedure's effect from system-wide trends.

2. **Validated assumptions:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories, confirming the comparison is valid.

3. **Immediate and sustained impact:** The improvement appeared right after implementation and persisted throughout the observation period.

4. **Not explained by other factors:** The effect remains after accounting for staffing changes and visitor volume fluctuations.
