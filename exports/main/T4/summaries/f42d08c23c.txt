# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Procedure

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**We recommend AGAINST proceeding with a system-wide rollout of the new procedure at this time.**

The data do not provide statistically reliable evidence that the new procedure improves service scores. While the estimated effect is modestly positive (+1.6 points), it is not distinguishable from zero with confidence. The true effect could be slightly harmful or moderately beneficial—we simply cannot tell from the current data.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Branches in study | 34 (20 treatment, 14 control) |
| Study period | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| Switch date | Week 15 |
| **Estimated treatment effect** | **+1.59 points** |
| **Statistical significance** | **p = 0.144 (not significant)** |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | **-0.54 to +3.72 points** |

**What This Means:**
- Treatment branches improved slightly relative to control branches after the switch
- However, this improvement could easily be due to random variation
- We cannot conclude the new procedure caused any real change in service quality

**Supporting Evidence:**
- Week-by-week analysis shows **zero statistically significant improvements** in any post-switch week
- Effects fluctuated randomly between -1.9 and +2.6 points with no clear pattern
- Pre-switch trends were stable, validating our comparison method

---

### **CONTEXT**

The organization expected this procedure change to improve service scores. The data do not support that expectation—but they also do not prove the procedure is ineffective. The study simply lacks the statistical power to detect modest effects with confidence.

Notably, branches assigned to the new procedure started with lower average service scores than control branches (48.9 vs. 54.3). Our analysis accounts for this baseline difference, but it underscores the importance of careful evaluation before broader implementation.

---

### **RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **1** | **Pause system-wide rollout** | Immediate |
| **2** | Extend pilot data collection by 8–12 weeks to improve statistical precision | Q1 2026 |
| **3** | Conduct implementation audit at treatment branches to verify procedure fidelity | 4 weeks |
| **4** | Analyze whether effects vary by branch characteristics (size, location, staffing) | 6 weeks |
| **5** | Reconvene Board with updated evidence for final rollout decision | Q2 2026 |

---

### **DECISION REQUIRED**

We request Board approval to:
1. **Halt the planned system-wide rollout** pending additional evidence
2. **Authorize continued data collection** for the pilot branches
3. **Schedule a follow-up review** for Q2 2026
