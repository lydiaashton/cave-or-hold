# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new rotation and shelving procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.0 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** in service quality. The result is statistically significant and held up under rigorous testing.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Tested
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (February–June 2026)
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- We compared how service scores changed before and after the switch, accounting for branch-specific characteristics and weekly factors like staffing and visitor volume

### What We Found

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 49.5 | 49.0 | −0.5 |
| Branches using new procedure | 50.7 | 52.2 | +1.5 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

- The **2.0-point improvement** is statistically significant (p < 0.001)
- We are 95% confident the true effect lies between **1.4 and 2.5 points**
- The effect persisted after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, supporting the validity of the comparison

### Why This Matters
- Service scores average around 50 across the cooperative; a 2-point gain is meaningful
- Branches using the old procedure saw scores drift slightly downward during the same period
- The new procedure appears to have both improved treatment branches and protected them from the decline seen elsewhere

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new rotation and shelving procedure.**

The evidence supports what we hoped: the new procedure delivers measurable improvements in branch service quality. With 425 branches already successfully using the procedure, we have a strong operational foundation for expanding to the remaining 284 branches.

## **Appendix: Analysis Details**

- **Data:** 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects
- **Standard errors:** Clustered at the branch level
- **Controls:** Weekly staffing index and visits index
