# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new procedure showed **higher service scores** than those that did not, with an estimated improvement of **approximately 2 points** on the service score index. However, because branches self-selected into the new procedure, we cannot definitively attribute this improvement to the procedure itself. **We recommend a controlled pilot expansion before system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Service Score Impact**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Estimated effect of new procedure | **+1.9 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | +1.3 to +2.4 points |
| Statistical significance | Yes (p < 0.001) |
| Relative improvement | ~4% above average |

Branches using the new procedure maintained higher service scores after the switch compared to branches using the old procedure.

### **2. What the Data Shows**

- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure; **284 branches** kept the old procedure
- The switch occurred in **week 15** of the study period
- After the switch, switched branches averaged **52.5** vs. **48.6** for non-switched branches
- Both groups experienced some score decline over time, but switched branches declined **more slowly**

### **3. Important Limitation**

Branches that chose to switch were **already different** from those that did not:

- Switched branches had higher baseline service scores
- The gap between the two groups was **widening even before the switch**
- This suggests branches that adopted the new procedure may have been **more motivated, better-staffed, or better-managed** independent of the procedure itself

**What this means:** The 2-point improvement likely **overstates** the true effect of the procedure. The actual benefit could be smaller.

---

## **Recommendation**

### **Proceed with Phased Rollout**

| Phase | Action | Purpose |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **1** | Randomly assign 50-100 additional branches to adopt the procedure | Establish causal evidence |
| **2** | Monitor service scores for 8-12 weeks | Measure true treatment effect |
| **3** | If results confirm benefit, proceed to system-wide rollout | Minimize risk |

### **Rationale**

1. **The signal is positive** — all analyses show switched branches performing better
2. **The evidence is not definitive** — selection bias may explain part of the observed effect
3. **A randomized pilot is low-cost** — compared to the risk of system-wide implementation that may not deliver expected benefits

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Identify 50-100 branches** for randomized assignment to the new procedure
2. **Establish baseline measurements** before assignment
3. **Track service scores weekly** for at least 8 weeks post-implementation
4. **Collect qualitative feedback** from branch staff on implementation experience
5. **Reconvene in 3 months** to review pilot results and decide on full rollout
