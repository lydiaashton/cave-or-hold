# Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

The new shelving and rotation procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. Branches that switched saw an average improvement of **1.6 points** (about 3% of the typical score), but this result is not statistically significant at conventional levels. The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.4 to +3.6 points, meaning we cannot rule out either no effect or a modest benefit.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **cautious, phased rollout** with continued monitoring rather than an immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Treatment Effect

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Point estimate | +1.61 points |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.11 (not significant at 0.05 level) |
| 95% confidence interval | -0.36 to +3.58 points |
| Effect size | 3% of average score |

### 2. Service Score Changes

| Period | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Before switch (Weeks 6-14) | 50.38 | 52.30 |
| After switch (Weeks 15-22) | 51.96 | 52.27 |
| **Change** | **+1.57** | **-0.03** |

The difference-in-differences estimate of **+1.61 points** represents the estimated causal effect of the new procedure.

### 3. Post-Switch Weekly Pattern

The effect varied week-to-week after implementation:
- **Positive weeks (15, 21, 22):** Switched branches outperformed control branches by 1.6 to 2.8 points
- **Negative weeks (16-20):** Switched branches underperformed by 1.1 to 2.8 points

This variability suggests effects may still be stabilizing or that other factors are influencing scores.

### 4. Important Limitations

- **Parallel trends concern:** Some evidence (p = 0.09) suggests switched branches were on a slightly different trajectory than control branches even before the switch.
- **Limited data:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data from 34 branches.
- **Uncertain precision:** Confidence interval includes both zero and meaningful positive effects.

---

## Next Steps

### Recommended Actions

1. **Extend the pilot for 8-12 additional weeks** to gather more data and observe whether effects stabilize.

2. **Continue monitoring** switched branches for operational challenges and staff feedback.

3. **Consider staggered rollout** to remaining branches, using each new cohort to validate findings.

4. **Track secondary metrics** (staffing levels, visit volumes) alongside service scores.

5. **Establish decision criteria** before expanding:
   - **Full rollout:** Effect becomes statistically significant (p < 0.05) with positive estimate
   - **Continue phased approach:** Effect remains positive but uncertain (current status)
   - **Halt and investigate:** Effect turns negative or confidence interval shifts below zero

---

## Conclusion

The new procedure shows promise but lacks definitive evidence of effectiveness. A measured, data-driven expansion allows the cooperative to capture potential benefits while managing risk and building a stronger evidence base for final adoption decisions.

---

*Analysis based on 578 weekly observations from 34 branches (20 switched, 14 non-switched) over weeks 6-22. Standard errors clustered at branch level.*
