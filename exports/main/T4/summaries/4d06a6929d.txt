# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** and is statistically significant. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout** to all remaining branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality**

Our analysis compared 425 branches that switched to the new procedure against 284 branches that kept the old procedure, tracking weekly service scores before and after the week 15 switch date.

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 53.02 | 51.91 | -1.11 |
| Branches with old procedure | 51.98 | 48.87 | -3.10 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.96** |

While all branches experienced some decline in scores after week 15 (likely seasonal), branches using the new procedure declined **much less**—preserving roughly 2 points of service quality.

**Statistical confidence:** 95% confidence interval is 1.45 to 2.48 points (p < 0.001).

### **2. Benefits Increased Over Time**

The improvement was not immediate but grew as branches adapted:

- **Weeks 15-17:** Modest gains (+0.7 to +1.6 points)
- **Weeks 18-22:** Stronger, consistent gains (+1.7 to +1.9 points)

This suggests a 1-2 week learning curve, after which the full benefit is realized.

### **3. Results Are Reliable**

- **709 branches** studied over **17 weeks** (12,053 observations)
- Analysis accounts for staffing levels and visitor volume
- No pre-existing differences in trends between the two groups
- Results are robust across multiple statistical specifications

### **4. System-Wide Impact Potential**

If extended to all 284 branches still using the old procedure, we would expect similar improvements across the cooperative, raising overall service quality by approximately 2 points on the 50-point average scale.

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.** The evidence is clear: the new stock rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality, the effect is meaningful and growing, and the analysis is methodologically sound. The cooperative should move forward with implementation while monitoring results and supporting branches through the transition.

*Analysis based on branches.csv containing weekly service scores for 709 branches, weeks 6-22. Difference-in-differences regression with branch-clustered standard errors, controlling for staffing and visits.*
