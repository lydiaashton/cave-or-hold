# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Evaluation of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** [Current Date]  
**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

### **Bottom Line**

**Roll out the new procedure to all branches.** 

Contrary to our initial expectations, the new stock rotation and shelving procedure produced a **statistically significant improvement** in service scores. Branches using the new procedure scored **2.2 points higher** (approximately **4% improvement**) than they would have under the old procedure. This is a meaningful gain on an index where the average branch scores about 50.

---

### **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.20 points (95% CI: 0.89 to 3.51) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Branches Studied** | 150 total (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (9 before switch, 8 after) |
| **Parallel Trends** | Confirmed—groups were comparable before the switch |

**What the numbers mean:**
- Branches that kept the old procedure saw service scores **decline by 2 points** after week 15
- Branches that adopted the new procedure **maintained their scores** (essentially flat)
- The **net benefit** of the new procedure is the 2.2-point difference between these trajectories

**Why we're confident:**
- The analysis accounts for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Pre-switch trends were similar between groups (validating our comparison)
- Results are robust to different statistical specifications
- Over half of treatment branches showed individual improvement

---

### **Why This Matters**

We entered this analysis expecting no measurable effect. The data show otherwise. A 4% improvement in service scores is operationally meaningful, especially since:

1. **No downside detected**—scores did not decline in any systematic way
2. **Low-risk intervention**—the procedure change does not require major capital investment
3. **Scalable benefit**—if the effect holds across all 60 remaining branches, the cooperative-wide gain would be substantial

---

### **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate rollout** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all branches currently using the old method.
