# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** New Shelving Procedure – Service Score Impact Analysis

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving procedure improved service scores by 2.2 points.** This represents a meaningful 4% gain over the average branch score of 50. The result is statistically robust and supports proceeding with a cooperative-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure (284) | 51.03 | 49.87 | −1.16 |
| Branches adopting new procedure (425) | 51.00 | 52.02 | +1.02 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.19** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Effect size:** +2.2 points
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.65 to 2.76 points
- **Significance level:** p < 0.001 (highly reliable)

### **Analysis Approach**
We compared branches that switched to the new procedure against those that did not, before and after Week 15. This difference-in-differences method isolates the procedure's effect from other factors like seasonal trends, staffing changes, and visitor volume.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve cooperative-wide rollout of the new shelving procedure.**

The evidence confirms what we expected: the new procedure delivers measurable improvements in service quality. With 425 branches already successfully implementing the change, we have a proven model to scale.
