# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by an average of **2.05 points** (approximately **4% improvement**) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This effect is highly statistically significant and consistent across all analytical approaches. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Overview**
- **709 branches** participated: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 retained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** collected (weeks 6–22), with the switch implemented in week 15
- Analysis compares service score changes between the two groups before and after the switch

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Estimated Effect** | **+2.05 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.50 to 2.60 points |
| Statistical Significance | p < 0.001 |
| Percentage Improvement | 4.0% from baseline |

### **What This Means**
- Treatment group branches averaged **51.2** before the switch and **52.5** after
- Control group branches averaged **50.3** before and **49.6** after
- The net improvement attributable to the new procedure is **2.05 points**
- This is equivalent to moving a typical branch from the 50th to approximately the 58th percentile of service scores

### **Analysis Rigor**
The finding is robust across multiple statistical specifications:
- Basic difference-in-differences comparison
- Models controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Models accounting for inherent differences between branches
- Models accounting for week-to-week variation

All approaches yield consistent estimates between **2.0 and 2.1 points** with high statistical confidence.

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

### **Recommended Actions**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches
   - Evidence strongly supports effectiveness
   - Expected improvement: ~2 points on service score index across all branches

2. **Establish implementation timeline**
   - Phase the rollout to manage training and transition capacity
   - Target completion within the next quarter

3. **Continue performance monitoring**
   - Track service scores post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale
   - Monitor for any operational impacts (staffing, workflow, costs)

4. **Capture and share best practices**
   - Document lessons learned from the 425 early-adopter branches
   - Develop training materials based on successful implementations

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data provide clear, statistically robust evidence that the new rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. The effect is meaningful (4% improvement), consistent, and highly significant. **The board should proceed with confidence in approving system-wide implementation.**

---

*Analysis based on 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches. Standard errors clustered at the branch level to account for repeated measurements.*
