# **SUBJECT: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced service score increases of **2.1 points** beyond what would have occurred naturally. This represents a **4% improvement** in service quality with high statistical confidence.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Service scores average ~50 points; higher scores indicate better service

### **Results Summary**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 50.7 | 54.0 | +3.3 |
| Old Procedure Branches | 50.7 | 51.9 | +1.2 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Effect size:** +2.13 service score points
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.6 to 2.7 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Practical significance:** ~4% improvement in service quality

### **Why We Trust These Results**

1. **Valid Comparison:** Before the switch, both groups had identical average scores (50.7) and showed no meaningful difference in trends. This confirms the control group provides a reliable counterfactual.

2. **Effects Strengthen Over Time:** The improvement started modestly (~1 point) and grew to over 3 points by week 7 post-switch. This pattern suggests genuine adaptation rather than random fluctuation.

3. **Robust Finding:** The result holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume, confirming the effect stems from the procedure itself.

4. **Consistent Direction:** All 8 post-switch weeks showed positive effects; none showed decline.

## **Technical Appendix**

**Methodology:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch) to account for repeated observations.

**Data:** 12,053 branch-week observations from branches.csv

**Model Specification:**
```
service_score = β₀ + β₁(treatment) + β₂(post_period) + β₃(treatment × post) + ε
```

**Key Coefficient:** β₃ = 2.13 (SE = 0.28, p < 0.001)
