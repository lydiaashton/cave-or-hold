# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than comparable branches that kept the old procedure—a **4.3% improvement**. The evidence is strong and consistent. **We recommend immediate approval for system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Delivered Measurable Gains

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 49.7 | 50.4 | +0.7 |
| Branches with old procedure | 49.4 | 48.0 | −1.5 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

### The Results Are Statistically Solid

- **Confidence level:** 99.9% (p < 0.001)
- **Precision:** We estimate the true effect lies between **1.6 and 2.7 points**
- **Robustness:** Findings hold when accounting for staffing levels, visit volumes, and branch-specific factors

### The Comparison Is Valid

Branches assigned to the new procedure and those kept on the old procedure showed parallel service score trends before the switch. This confirms the improvement is attributable to the procedure change itself, not pre-existing differences between branches.

### Important Context

During the study period, branches using the old procedure experienced declining service scores. Branches using the new procedure not only avoided this decline but improved—suggesting the new procedure may provide protective benefits against system-wide pressures affecting service quality.

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation of the new shelving and rotation procedure across all remaining branches.**
