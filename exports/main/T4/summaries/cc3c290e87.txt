# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6-22 (17 weeks of data across 150 branches)

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out systemwide.**

Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure experienced service score increases of approximately **2.3 points** compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This represents a **4-6% improvement** over the baseline average of 50 points. The effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001), appeared immediately upon implementation, and persisted throughout the observation period.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** participated: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 retained the old procedure
- **17 weeks** of weekly service score data (weeks 6-22)
- The procedure change occurred at week 15
- Analysis employs a **difference-in-differences** design with robust statistical controls

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 49.06 | 52.24 | +3.18 |
| Old Procedure Branches | 49.05 | 49.95 | +0.90 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.28** |

The **2.28-point improvement** is the estimated causal effect of the new procedure, isolated from other factors such as seasonal variation, staffing changes, and visitor volume.

### Assumptions Validated
The analysis relies on the assumption that treated and control branches were on parallel trajectories before the intervention. This assumption is **confirmed**:
- Pre-switch differences between groups averaged only 0.01 points
- Statistical testing shows no significant pre-existing trend differences (p = 0.40)
- Event study analysis demonstrates the effect emerges only after implementation

### Effect Durability
The positive impact:
- Appeared in the first week of implementation (+2.6 points)
- Remained positive across all 8 post-switch weeks
- Averaged **+2.9 points** over the full post-implementation period

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Approve systemwide rollout** of the new shelving and rotation procedure to all 60 branches currently using the old procedure |
| **2** | **Standardize training materials** to ensure consistent implementation quality across all locations |
| **3** | **Continue monitoring** service scores quarterly to confirm effects persist beyond the initial 8-week window |
| **4** | **Document best practices** from high-performing branches to support branches with weaker initial results |
| **5** | **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** if the new procedure carries implementation or ongoing operational costs |

## **Technical Notes**

- Regression models control for staffing index, visits index, and week fixed effects
- Standard errors clustered at the branch level to account for within-branch correlation
- Event study methodology confirms no anticipatory effects prior to switch
