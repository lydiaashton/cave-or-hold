# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.** The evidence shows the new procedure **improved service scores by 2.45 points** (approximately 5% relative to the average score of 50). This finding is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and directly contradicts the initial expectation that the change would make no difference.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Impact on Service Scores
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.45 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | 1.20 to 3.70 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Practical significance | ~5% improvement |

### Study Overview
- **Treatment group:** 90 branches adopted the new procedure
- **Control group:** 60 branches retained the old procedure
- **Observation period:** 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **Switch date:** Week 15
- **Total observations:** 2,550 branch-week records

### Analysis Validity
✓ **Parallel trends confirmed** — Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories (p = 0.754 for differential pre-trend)

✓ **No confounding detected** — The improvement was not accompanied by changes in staffing levels (p = 0.24) or visitor volume (p = 0.19)

✓ **Robust methodology** — Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors at the branch level

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with full rollout to all branches.** The data demonstrates a clear benefit with no identified drawbacks.

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Implement system-wide** — Extend the new procedure to remaining 60 branches
2. **Monitor during transition** — Track service scores monthly for 6 months post-rollout
3. **Capture implementation learnings** — Document practices from the 90 early-adopter branches to support training
4. **Verify sustained impact** — Confirm the effect persists beyond the initial adoption period
5. **Review operational costs** — Ensure any additional costs are justified by the service improvement
