# **Library Cooperative Procedure Switch: Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by **2.1 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4.3% improvement**, is highly statistically significant, and was experienced by the vast majority of switched branches. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** participated: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 maintained the old procedure
- **17 weeks** of data collected (Weeks 6–22); the switch occurred in Week 15
- Analysis compares score changes in switched branches against concurrent changes in non-switched branches

### Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.2 | 55.1 | +3.9 |
| Non-switched branches | 49.1 | 50.8 | +1.8 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Effect size:** 0.21 standard deviations
- **Adoption success:** 85% of switched branches improved vs. 68% of non-switched branches

### Why This Result Is Reliable

1. **Comparable groups:** Both groups followed similar trends in the 9 weeks before the switch, validating the comparison.

2. **Consistent across methods:** The 2.1-point effect holds across multiple statistical specifications, including controls for branch identity, week effects, staffing levels, and visitor volume.

3. **Widespread benefit:** Improvement was not driven by a few outliers—most switched branches saw gains.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** to the remaining 284 branches

2. **Monitor during implementation:** Track service scores as each branch transitions to ensure expected gains materialize

3. **Support underperforming branches:** Approximately 15% of switched branches did not improve; investigate distinguishing factors to assist struggling branches during rollout

4. **Coordinate with staffing plans:** Since staffing levels independently affect service scores, ensure adequate staffing during the transition to maximize the procedure's benefit
