# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure gained approximately **2.2 points** on the service score index compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a meaningful 4% improvement. We recommend proceeding with a full rollout.

---

### **Key Findings**

**1. The New Procedure Works**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 50.45 | 50.85 | +0.40 |
| Branches using new procedure | 51.40 | 53.99 | +2.59 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.19** |

- **2.2-point improvement** is statistically significant (p < 0.001)
- Effect represents **4% above baseline** service scores
- Benefits appear to **grow over time**, suggesting continued gains as staff gain experience

**2. Strong Evidence Across Analyses**

- Result holds when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Effect is consistent across the 425 branches that adopted the change
- No negative unintended consequences detected in available metrics

**3. Implementation Context**

- 709 branches total participated in the study period (weeks 6–22)
- Switch occurred in week 15; 8 weeks of post-implementation data available
- Early-adopting branches showed stronger pre-existing improvement trends, suggesting the true effect may be modestly smaller than 2.2 points—but still clearly positive

---

### **Recommendation**

**Approve full rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

The data confirm the expected improvement in service scores. The effect is statistically robust, practically meaningful, and appears to strengthen with time.
