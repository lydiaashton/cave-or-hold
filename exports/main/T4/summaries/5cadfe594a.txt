# Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

## Bottom Line

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

The new procedure **improved service scores by 2.3 points on average** (4.4% gain). The effect is statistically significant (p = 0.005), and the analysis confirms the improvement is attributable to the procedure change itself.

---

## Key Findings

### What We Measured
- **34 branches** over **17 weeks** (February–June)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Service scores average ~50 points; higher scores indicate better service

### Results

| | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Switched branches** | 50.4 | 52.1 | +1.6 |
| **Control branches** | 53.9 | 53.3 | −0.7 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.3** |

The control group's slight decline suggests external factors (seasonal, operational) may have put downward pressure on scores. The switched branches overcame this pressure and still gained ground.

### Why We Trust This Result

✓ **Parallel trends validated**: Treatment and control branches moved together before the switch (p = 0.50), confirming a valid comparison  
✓ **Effect is robust**: Result holds when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume  
✓ **Precise estimate**: 95% confidence interval [0.7, 3.9] — even the conservative bound represents a meaningful gain
