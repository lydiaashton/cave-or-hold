# Memorandum

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

**The new procedure improved service scores by 2.1 points (4% above baseline).** The effect is statistically significant and consistent across analytical approaches. We recommend proceeding with a full rollout to all branches.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. The New Procedure Works

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.12 service score points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | 1.58 to 2.66 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Branches analyzed** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Observation period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |

### 2. What the Data Shows

**Branches that switched to the new procedure:**
- Maintained their service scores after the switch (51.85 → 52.52)

**Branches that kept the old procedure:**
- Experienced a notable decline (51.09 → 49.64)

The new procedure appears to have protected branches from broader downward pressures affecting the cooperative during this period.

### 3. Benefits Accumulate Over Time

The improvement grew stronger in the weeks following implementation:
- Weeks 15–17: Minimal detectable effect
- Weeks 18–22: Clear, significant gains (up to +2.2 points)

This suggests staff become more effective with the new procedure as they gain experience.

### 4. Effect Holds Under Rigorous Testing

The 2.1-point improvement remains consistent when we:
- Control for inherent differences between branches
- Account for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Use conservative statistical methods

---

## Recommendation

**Approve full rollout of the new procedure to all remaining branches.**

The analysis confirms what leadership expected: the new rotation and shelving procedure delivers measurable service improvements. The effect is meaningful in practical terms (4% gain on a ~50-point scale) and statistically robust.
