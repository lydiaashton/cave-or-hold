# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This confirms our expectation that the change would improve service quality. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with **new procedure** | 51.59 | 52.83 | +1.24 |
| Branches with **old procedure** | 51.31 | 50.37 | −0.95 |
| **Net improvement from new procedure** | | | **+2.19** |

### **What This Means**

- **709 branches** were studied over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- The **2.2-point gain** represents a **4% improvement** in service scores
- Results are **statistically significant** (p < 0.001) — the likelihood this occurred by chance is less than 1 in 1,000

### **Why We're Confident**

1. **Valid comparison:** Before the switch, both groups of branches followed the same trends. The divergence after week 15 is attributable to the procedure change.

2. **Robust analysis:** The result holds whether we account for staffing levels, visitor volume, or inherent differences between individual branches.

3. **Meaningful impact:** A 2.2-point improvement on a ~50-point scale is noticeable to patrons and staff.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new rotation and shelving procedure across all remaining branches.
