# **Library Cooperative Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.2 points** (about **4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and strengthened after branches had time to adjust.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | +2.21 points on service score index |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 0.99 to 3.43 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Relative improvement | ~4% from baseline |

### 2. Strong Evidence, Not Chance
The analysis compared **90 branches** that switched to the new procedure against **60 branches** that did not, across **17 weeks** of data. Before the switch, both groups followed similar trends—confirming that the improvement is attributable to the procedure change itself.

### 3. Benefits Increased Over Time
- **Weeks 15-18:** No significant effect (branches were still adjusting)
- **Weeks 19-22:** Clear positive effect (+1.54 points, p=0.03)

This indicates a **3-4 week learning curve** before full benefits are realized.

### 4. More Branches Improved Than Declined
| Group | Average Change | % with Improvement |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| Switched branches | +0.79 points | 51% |
| Control branches | -1.33 points | 28% |

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** to the remaining 60 branches.

2. **Plan for a 4-week adjustment period** per branch, with:
   - Enhanced training during the first month
   - Weekly check-ins to address implementation issues
   - Temporary staffing support if needed

3. **Track service scores** for 8-12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale.

4. **Capture lessons learned** from the 90 early-adopter branches, especially top performers, to accelerate adoption elsewhere.

5. **Schedule a 6-month follow-up review** to assess long-term sustainability and identify any operational impacts (staff workload, costs, etc.).

---

*Analysis based on 2,550 weekly observations from 150 branches (weeks 6-22). Standard errors clustered by branch. Full technical documentation available upon request.*
