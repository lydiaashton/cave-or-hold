# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **2.3 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **4-5% improvement** relative to the typical branch score of 50. The effect is statistically significant and sustained throughout the observation period.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** analyzed: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 continued with the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6-22): 9 weeks before implementation, 8 weeks after
- Analysis compared changes in switched branches against non-switched branches to isolate the procedure's effect

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 50.37 | 52.76 | **+2.39** |
| Branches with old procedure | 48.68 | 48.79 | +0.11 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.27** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Estimated effect:** +2.27 points
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.67 to 2.80 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### What This Means
- The improvement is **statistically reliable** — very unlikely to be due to chance
- The improvement is **practically meaningful** — a 2.3-point gain on a ~50-point scale
- The improvement is **sustained** — maintained across all 8 weeks after implementation
- Branches using the old procedure showed **no meaningful change** over the same period

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all 284 remaining branches

2. **Develop implementation timeline** — phase the rollout to manage training and transition logistics

3. **Monitor service scores** during rollout to confirm the effect replicates across all branches

4. **Document best practices** — capture what makes the new procedure effective to ensure consistent implementation

5. **Continue tracking** staffing and visit metrics to identify whether benefits vary by branch type or size

---

## **METHODOLOGY NOTE**

This analysis used a quasi-experimental "difference-in-differences" design, comparing branches that switched to the new procedure against those that did not. This approach isolates the effect of the procedure change from other factors affecting all branches simultaneously (such as seasonal variations or system-wide trends). Standard errors were clustered at the branch level to account for repeated weekly observations.
