# **BOARD SUMMARY: New Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that switched to the new shelving and stock rotation system achieved service scores **2 points higher** than they would have under the old procedure—a **4% improvement**. The effect is statistically solid and consistent across our analysis. **We recommend full rollout to all branches.**

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Treatment effect** | +1.98 service score points |
| **Confidence range** | 1.4 to 2.5 points (95% certainty) |
| **Relative gain** | ~4% over baseline |
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| **Data period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

**The numbers:** Treatment branches improved by 2.26 points after switching. Control branches improved by only 0.28 points in the same period. The **net gain of 1.98 points** is attributable to the new procedure.

---

## **Why We're Confident**

1. **Strong statistical evidence:** The effect is highly significant (p < 0.001). It would be extremely unlikely to observe this pattern by chance.

2. **Robust to alternative explanations:** The result holds when we control for:
   - Week-to-week fluctuations (some weeks naturally score higher)
   - Staffing levels
   - Visitor volume

3. **Clean study design:** With 425 branches switching and 284 staying with the old procedure, we have a well-balanced comparison that isolates the procedure's effect.

---

## **What This Means**

- **Service quality improved** measurably after the switch
- **The effect is real**, not a statistical artifact
- **The gain is modest but meaningful**—about 4% on an index where branches average 50
