# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. We estimate an improvement of approximately **2.5 points (5%)**, but the data do not rule out the possibility of no effect. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Estimated Impact**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.5 points** on service score |
| Typical baseline | ~50 points |
| Relative improvement | **~5%** |
| Statistical confidence | **Not significant** (p = 0.12) |
| 95% confidence range | **-0.6 to +5.5 points** |

### **What the Data Show**

- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 continued with the old
- **17 weeks** of observations (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- Before the switch, both groups had nearly identical average scores (~49.3 vs. 49.2)
- After the switch, branches using the new procedure averaged **51.2**, while others averaged **49.0**

### **What the Statistics Tell Us**

The analysis cannot conclusively distinguish the observed improvement from random variation. The true effect could be:
- **No effect** (the procedure makes no difference)
- **A modest benefit** (up to +5.5 points, or ~11%)
- **A small negative effect** (down to -0.6 points)

This uncertainty stems from limited data and natural week-to-week score fluctuations.

---

## **RECOMMENDATIONS**

### **1. Proceed with Cautious Expansion**
The estimated effect is positive and practically meaningful. Given the low risk of the procedure change, expanding to additional branches is reasonable.

### **2. Continue Systematic Monitoring**
Collect at least 8–12 more weeks of data to increase statistical confidence and confirm whether the effect persists.

### **3. Ensure Consistent Implementation**
Track whether branches are following the new procedure as designed. Inconsistent implementation could mask the true effect.

### **4. Schedule a Follow-Up Review**
Reconvene in **2–3 months** to review updated results. If the effect remains positive and achieves statistical significance, proceed with full system-wide rollout.

## **APPENDIX: Technical Summary**

| Item | Detail |
