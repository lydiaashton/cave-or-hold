# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **1.9 points** (about **4%**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant and grows over time. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Improved Service Scores

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | **+1.92 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | 1.38 to 2.47 points |
| Improvement vs. baseline | **~4%** |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |

### 2. The Benefit Grows Over Time

The positive effect increased steadily after implementation:

| Period After Switch | Average Effect |
|---------------------|---------------|
| Week 15 (first week) | +1.6 points |
| Weeks 16-18 | +2.2 points |
| Weeks 19-22 | +3.2 points |

This pattern suggests branches improve at implementing the procedure over time, or the benefits accumulate as the new system matures.

### 3. The Analysis Is Reliable

- **Valid comparison:** Before the switch, treatment and control branches followed similar trends (no significant difference, p=0.24)
- **Large sample:** 709 branches, 12,053 weekly observations
- **Strong design:** 40% of branches kept the old procedure, providing a valid control group

### 4. What Changed

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 49.60 | 53.51 | +3.91 |
| Branches that did not switch | 48.86 | 50.85 | +1.99 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.92** |

*Note: Both groups improved over time, but switched branches improved nearly twice as much.*

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Authorize full rollout** to the remaining branches using a phased implementation schedule.

2. **Monitor during implementation:** Track service scores for 8-12 weeks after each branch switches to ensure they realize the full benefit.

3. **Share best practices:** Branches with the largest improvements may have implementation insights worth sharing during rollout.

4. **Evaluate costs:** If the new procedure requires additional staff time or resources, compare those costs against the 4% service improvement to confirm net benefit.

5. **Long-term tracking:** Once all branches have switched, monitor whether gains are sustained over time.

---

## **Appendix: Analysis Details**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Data period:** Weeks 6-22 (9 weeks before switch, 8 weeks after)
- **Treatment group:** 425 branches switched in week 15
- **Control group:** 284 branches maintained old procedure
- **Outcome measure:** Service score index (average branch scores ~50; higher is better)
