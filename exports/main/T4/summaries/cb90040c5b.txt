# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks of data from 150 branches)

---

## **Bottom Line**

Contrary to initial expectations, the new procedure **produced a measurable improvement** in service scores. Branches that switched to the new procedure gained approximately **2 service score points** more than branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant and consistent across analytical methods.

**Recommendation:** Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Measured

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 50.59 | 51.02 | +0.43 |
| Branches using new procedure | 50.26 | 52.62 | +2.36 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.98** |

### Statistical Confidence

- **Effect size:** +1.98 service score points
- **95% confidence interval:** +1.2 to +2.8 points
- **Significance level:** p < 0.001 (less than 1 in 1,000 chance this is random variation)
- **Relative improvement:** ~4% above the baseline score of 50

### Why We Trust This Result

1. **Comparable groups:** Before the switch, treatment and control branches had indistinguishable service scores (p = 0.56)
2. **Controls for confounders:** The analysis accounts for branch-specific factors, week-to-week variation, staffing levels, and visitor volume
3. **Consistent pattern:** Weekly gaps between treatment and control branches turned positive after week 15 and stayed positive

---

## **Context**

The board's prior expectation—that this operational change would not affect service scores—was reasonable. Shelving and rotation procedures are back-office functions that might not obviously connect to patron-facing service quality. However, the data indicate otherwise. Possible explanations worth exploring:

- Faster item retrieval by staff
- Improved shelf organization noticed by patrons
- Staff morale or efficiency gains translating to better service interactions

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **Approve system-wide rollout** | Immediate | Board |

## **Conclusion**

This is a rare instance where an operational change delivered measurable improvement without additional cost. The new procedure should be adopted across all branches. We recommend proceeding with the rollout while continuing to monitor outcomes.
