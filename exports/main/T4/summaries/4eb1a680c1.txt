# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** December 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.** 

Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved significantly higher service scores than branches that retained the old procedure. The evidence strongly supports expanding the new procedure to all 709 branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### The New Procedure Improved Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with **new procedure** | 49.7 | 54.8 | **+5.0** |
| Branches with **old procedure** | 49.2 | 52.4 | +3.2 |
| **Attributable to new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

### What This Means

- **1.9-point average improvement** in service scores directly attributable to the new procedure
- **Statistically significant** result (p < 0.001); we can be highly confident this is not due to chance
- **95% confidence interval:** +1.3 to +2.4 points
- Represents approximately **4% improvement** over the baseline service score of 50

### Study Scope

- **709 branches** across the cooperative (425 adopted new procedure; 284 retained old)
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22); switch implemented at week 15
- Analysis controls for staffing levels, visitor volume, and baseline branch differences

---

## **WHY THIS MATTERS**

1. **Both groups improved** after week 15 (likely seasonal factors), but branches with the new procedure improved **significantly more**.

2. **The effect is consistent:** The treatment group outperformed the control group in every single post-switch week, indicating a stable, reliable improvement.

3. **The effect is meaningful:** A 1.9-point gain on a ~50-point scale represents a noticeable enhancement in patron experience across the cooperative.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with full rollout** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.

**Expected outcome:** System-wide service score improvement of 1.5–2.5 points, benefiting all patrons across the cooperative.

---

## **RISKS & MITIGATIONS**

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Inconsistent implementation across branches | Standardized training materials; implementation checklist |
| Temporary disruption during transition | Phased rollout; dedicated support staff during switch |
| Effect may vary by branch size/type | Track outcomes by branch characteristics; adjust support as needed |

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data confirms what we hoped: the new stock rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. With strong statistical evidence and consistent results across all post-switch weeks, we recommend moving forward with confidence.
