# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** Current  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure improved service scores by **2.17 points** (4.3% above baseline). This effect is statistically certain (p < 0.001) and practically meaningful. We recommend approving immediate rollout to all remaining branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### What We Studied
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure in Week 15
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Compared service score changes between the two groups

### What We Found

| | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Switched Branches** | 49.72 | 52.75 | +3.02 |
| **Non-Switched Branches** | 49.13 | 50.00 | +0.87 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.17** |

### Why We're Confident
- **Statistically significant:** Less than 1 in 10 billion chance this result is random (p < 0.001)
- **Precise estimate:** 95% confident the true effect is between 1.62 and 2.72 points
- **Robust:** Effect holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Sustained:** Improvement persisted throughout the 8-week post-switch period

### What This Means
- Service scores average ~50 points; a 2+ point gain is operationally meaningful
- Even the conservative estimate (1.62 points) represents a 3%+ improvement
- Applied across the entire system, this represents substantial cumulative service improvement
