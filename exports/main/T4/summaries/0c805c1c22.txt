# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than they would have under the old procedure. This represents a **3.6% improvement** in service quality and is statistically significant.

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**Study Overview**
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **284 branches** maintained the old procedure (control group)
- Analysis compares service score changes between groups before and after the switch

**Results**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 50.75 | 53.49 | +2.74 |
| Old Procedure Branches | 49.08 | 49.99 | +0.91 |
| **Net Improvement** | | | **+1.82** |

**Statistical Confidence**
- Effect size: **+1.82 points** (p < 0.001)
- 95% Confidence Interval: **1.27 to 2.37 points**
- Result holds after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

**What This Means**
- The average branch scores about 50 on the service index
- A 1.8-point gain is a **meaningful, measurable improvement** in patron experience
- The effect is consistent across post-switch weeks, with no evidence of fade-out

---

### **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Authorize full rollout** – Extend the new procedure to all 284 branches still using the old method

2. **Set implementation timeline** – Phase the rollout over 2–3 months to manage training and operational capacity

3. **Continue monitoring** – Track service scores for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale

4. **Capture lessons learned** – Document practices from the 425 early-adopter branches to support training materials

5. **Review resource needs** – Since staffing and visitor volume also affect service scores, ensure branches have adequate support to maximize the new procedure's benefits
