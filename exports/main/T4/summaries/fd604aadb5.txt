# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure improved service scores.** Although this outcome was not anticipated, branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (about 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and consistent across most branches. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Clear Improvement in Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6-14) | After Switch (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches (90 branches) | 50.11 | 52.35 | **+2.23** |
| Old Procedure Branches (60 branches) | 50.61 | 50.73 | +0.12 |

**Net effect of the new procedure: +2.1 points**

### **2. Strong Statistical Evidence**

- The improvement is **statistically significant** (p < 0.01)
- 95% confidence interval: **+0.9 to +3.4 points**
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### **3. Benefits Across Most Branches**

- **76% of switched branches** saw improvement after adopting the new procedure
- Only **53% of control branches** improved during the same period (normal variation)
- No branches experienced severe negative effects

### **4. Rigorous Comparison**

- Before the switch, both groups followed nearly identical weekly trends
- This confirms the improvement is attributable to the procedure change, not pre-existing differences between branches
- Analysis covers **150 branches** over **17 weeks** (2,550 observations)

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all 60 branches still using the old method.

2. **Monitor the transition** to confirm newly switched branches see similar gains and provide support where needed.

3. **Document the procedure** thoroughly to ensure consistent implementation.

4. **Consider a follow-up review** in 3-6 months to confirm sustained improvement and identify any additional operational benefits.
