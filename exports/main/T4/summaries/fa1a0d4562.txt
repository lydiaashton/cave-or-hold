# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **improves service scores** and should be rolled out to all branches. Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **1.9 points** (approximately 4%) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and consistent across all analytical methods we applied.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Treatment Effect on Service Scores**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+1.87 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | 1.30 to 2.44 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Relative improvement | **3.7%** over baseline |

### **Study Scope**

- **709 branches** total (425 adopted new procedure; 284 kept old procedure)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22, 2026)
- **Switch occurred at week 15**
- **12,053 branch-week observations** analyzed

### **Effect Strengthens Over Time**

The positive effect grew in the weeks following implementation:

| Period | Average Effect |
|--------|----------------|
| Weeks 6–14 (before switch) | Baseline |
| Weeks 15–18 (early adoption) | +0.6 points |
| Weeks 19–22 (later adoption) | +1.5 points |

This pattern suggests staff proficiency with the new procedure improves with experience.

### **Results Hold Across Multiple Tests**

The ~1.9 point improvement remained consistent when we:
- Controlled for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Accounted for inherent differences between branches
- Accounted for week-to-week variation across the cooperative

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all 284 remaining branches.**

The evidence is clear: the new procedure delivers measurable service improvements. At scale, this represents a meaningful enhancement to patron experience across the cooperative.
