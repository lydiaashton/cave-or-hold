# Library Cooperative Stock Rotation Procedure: Impact Analysis

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores rise by approximately **2.3 points** (about 5% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout** while continuing to monitor results.

---

## Key Findings

### Performance Impact

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches (20) | 45.19 | 49.43 | **+4.24** |
| Control Branches (14) | 46.58 | 48.55 | +1.96 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.28** |

- The new procedure is associated with a **2.3-point gain** in service scores beyond normal variation
- This represents approximately a **5% improvement** on the service score index
- All 20 switched branches showed positive or neutral changes; none declined substantially

### Validation of Results

- **Pre-switch trends were favorable:** Before week 15, switched branches actually scored *lower* than control branches (−1.4 points on average), confirming the improvement occurred after the procedure change
- **Effect persists after controls:** The result holds even after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Consistent direction:** 7 of 8 post-switch weeks showed switched branches outperforming control branches

### Statistical Note

The effect size is meaningful for operations, though the study's sample size (34 branches over 17 weeks) limits statistical precision. The data suggest a **90% probability the effect is positive**. Continued monitoring will strengthen confidence in these findings.

---

## Recommendation

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

**Rationale:**
1. The observed improvement aligns with operational expectations
2. The effect is practically significant for patron experience
3. No branches were harmed by the change
4. Delaying rollout forfeits potential service gains
