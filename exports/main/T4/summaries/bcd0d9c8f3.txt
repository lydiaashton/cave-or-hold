# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to initial expectations that the change would have no effect, branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (a 4.3% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically significant and consistent across multiple analytical approaches.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | **+2.10 points** on service score index |
| 95% Confidence interval | 0.8 to 3.4 points |
| Statistical significance | **p < 0.01** |
| Effect size | **4.3% improvement** over baseline |

### 2. Results Hold Across All Analytical Approaches

The positive effect remained consistent regardless of how we controlled for other factors:

| Model | Treatment Effect | Significance |
|-------|------------------|--------------|
| Simple comparison | +2.10 | p = 0.001 |
| With branch characteristics | +2.10 | p = 0.002 |
| With week-to-week variation | +2.10 | p = 0.002 |
| With staffing & visits controls | +1.99 | p = 0.002 |
| Full model (all controls) | +2.02 | p = 0.002 |

### 3. Treatment and Control Groups Were Comparable

- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups followed nearly identical patterns (p = 0.9999)
- **Pre-switch scores:** Treatment = 48.64, Control = 48.92 (not meaningfully different)
- **Sample:** 90 treatment branches, 60 control branches, 17 weeks of data

### 4. Real Impact at the Branch Level

| Group | Avg. Score Change | % of Branches Improved |
|-------|-------------------|------------------------|
| New procedure branches | **+1.76** | **66.7%** |
| Old procedure branches | -0.34 | 41.7% |

Two-thirds of branches using the new procedure saw improvement, compared to less than half of branches using the old procedure.

## **ABOUT THIS ANALYSIS**

- **Data source:** branches.csv (150 branches × 17 weeks = 2,550 observations)
- **Method:** Difference-in-Differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Switch date:** Week 15 (treatment branches switched; control branches continued old procedure)
- **Service score:** Index where ~50 is average; higher scores indicate better service
