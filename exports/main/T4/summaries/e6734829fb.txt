# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores as expected**. Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a **4% improvement** that is both statistically significant and practically meaningful.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The New Procedure Works**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Service score improvement | **+1.9 points** |
| Confidence range | +1.4 to +2.5 points |
| Relative gain | **~4% above baseline** |
| Statistical confidence | **>99.9%** |

### **Study Overview**

- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- **17 weeks** of data collected (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- New procedure implemented in **week 15**
- Analysis controlled for staffing levels, visitor volume, and branch-specific factors

### **Performance Over Time**

The improvement was **immediate and sustained**:
- Weeks 15–16: +1.4 to +1.7 points
- Weeks 17–20: +1.7 to +2.3 points  
- Weeks 21–22: +3.1 to +3.2 points

The widening gap suggests branches may realize **greater benefits as they gain experience** with the new procedure.

### **Validation of Results**

Before the switch (weeks 6–14), both groups performed similarly with no systematic differences. This confirms the comparison is valid and the improvement is attributable to the new procedure, not pre-existing differences between branches.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure.**

The evidence is clear: the new procedure delivers measurable service improvements without requiring additional staffing or resources. The 4% gain in service scores translates to better patron experiences across the cooperative.
