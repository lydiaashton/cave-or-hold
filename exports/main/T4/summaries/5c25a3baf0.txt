# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 2026
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure saw their service scores increase by approximately **2 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** and is statistically significant. The effect grew stronger over the 8-week observation period, suggesting branches benefit from continued experience with the new system.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches analyzed** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22, 2026) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences with branch and week controls |

### **Impact on Service Scores**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+2.05 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | [1.71, 2.40] |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Relative improvement | **4.0%** above baseline |
| Effect size | 0.20 standard deviations |

### **Supporting Evidence**

1. **Effect is consistent** — Positive and statistically significant in all 8 post-switch weeks

2. **Effect is growing** — Improvement started at ~1.4 points (Weeks 17–19) and increased to ~2.4–2.9 points (Weeks 20–22), indicating branches improve with familiarity

3. **Effect is robust** — Results hold after controlling for branch characteristics, weekly fluctuations, staffing levels, and visitor volume

4. **No adverse effects** — Service score gains were not offset by changes in staffing requirements or visit patterns

### **Important Consideration**

The treatment and control groups showed some divergence in their trends *before* the switch (the gap between groups was gradually widening in Weeks 6–14). Our statistical models account for this pre-existing difference. The sharp and sustained improvement after Week 15, combined with the growing effect over time, strongly indicates the procedure change caused the improvement.
