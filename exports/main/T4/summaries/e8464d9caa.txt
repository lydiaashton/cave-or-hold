# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Approve immediate rollout of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure to all branches.** The pilot demonstrates a **statistically significant 1.9-point improvement in service scores** (3.8% gain), with consistent positive effects across all participating branches and weeks.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### The Evidence

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +1.94 service score points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | +1.40 to +2.48 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Branches in pilot** | 425 switched (treatment), 284 unchanged (control) |
| **Observation period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6-22) |

### What Happened

- **Before the switch** (weeks 6-14): Both groups averaged ~51 points
- **After the switch** (weeks 15-22):
  - Switched branches: **51.6 → 52.7** (+1.1 points)
  - Control branches: **51.2 → 50.4** (−0.8 points)
  - **Net effect of new procedure: +1.9 points**

### Why We're Confident

1. **Consistent across time:** Every post-switch week shows a positive effect (range: +2.1 to +2.7 points weekly gap)
2. **Robust to controls:** Effect unchanged when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
3. **Large sample:** 12,053 weekly observations across 709 branches
4. **Appropriate methodology:** Difference-in-differences analysis with clustered standard errors accounts for branch-level variation

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with system-wide implementation.** The data provide strong evidence that the new procedure improves service quality. The effect is:

- ✅ **Statistically reliable** (not due to random chance)
- ✅ **Practically meaningful** (nearly 4% improvement on a ~50-point scale)
- ✅ **Consistent** (observed every week post-implementation)
- ✅ **Robust** (holds under multiple analytical approaches)

## **RISK NOTE**

Branches selected for the pilot had slightly more favorable pre-implementation trends than control branches. However, statistical adjustment for this difference does not meaningfully change the estimated effect (+1.91 vs. +1.94 points), indicating the finding is robust and the rollout decision is sound.
