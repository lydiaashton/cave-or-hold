# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure—a **4% improvement**. The result is statistically robust and consistent across all post-switch weeks. We recommend immediate system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Delivered Measurable Gains

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches (425) | 51.57 | 53.95 | **+2.38** |
| Control Branches (284) | 50.64 | 50.84 | +0.21 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.17** |

### The Result Is Statistically Solid
- **Effect size:** 2.17 points (95% CI: 1.46 to 2.87)
- **Confidence level:** p < 0.001 (less than 1 in 1,000 chance this is random)
- **Analysis method:** Difference-in-differences regression on 12,053 observations

### The Effect Held Steady Week After Week
Every single week after the switch showed improvement:
- **Smallest weekly gain:** 2.39 points (week 19)
- **Largest weekly gain:** 3.78 points (weeks 21–22)
- **No week showed a decline** relative to control branches

### The Gain Is Real, Not an Artifact
The improvement remains unchanged even after accounting for:
- Weekly staffing fluctuations
- Visitor volume variations

This confirms the procedure itself—not external factors—drove the improvement.
