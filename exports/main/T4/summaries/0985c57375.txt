# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **helped branches maintain service scores** during a period when scores otherwise declined. Switched branches showed an estimated **1.9-point advantage** over non-switched branches. While this result is not statistically significant at conventional levels, the evidence points in the expected direction with no signs of harm. We recommend **proceeding with rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Performance**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6-14) | After (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| **Switched Branches** (20 branches) | 51.4 | 51.3 | −0.1 |
| **Non-Switched Branches** (14 branches) | 49.3 | 47.2 | −2.1 |

### **Treatment Effect**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated Effect | **+1.9 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | −0.6 to +4.5 |
| P-value | 0.14 |
| Statistical Significance | Not significant at p < 0.05 |

### **What the Data Shows**

1. **Switched branches held steady** — Service scores remained essentially unchanged after adopting the new procedure.

2. **Control branches declined** — Non-switched branches experienced a 2.1-point drop, which was statistically significant.

3. **The gap widened** — The difference between groups grew from 2.1 points before the switch to 4.1 points after.

4. **No evidence of harm** — The new procedure did not negatively affect service scores, staffing, or visit patterns.

5. **Uncertainty remains** — With only 8 weeks of post-switch data, we lack the statistical power to confirm the effect with high confidence.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring.

**Rationale:**
- The effect is in the expected direction (positive)
- Switched branches outperformed control branches
- No adverse effects were detected
- Waiting for statistical certainty may delay benefits to patrons

## **Appendix: Methodology**

- **Approach:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
- **Data:** 578 observations across 34 branches over 17 weeks
- **Treatment:** Branches that adopted new procedure (n=20) vs. those that did not (n=14)
- **Switch date:** Week 15
- **Parallel trends assumption:** Verified — no pre-treatment differences in trends between groups
