# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with systemwide rollout.** The new stock rotation and shelving procedure improved service scores by approximately **2 points** (about 4% above the baseline average of 50). This improvement is statistically significant and consistent across multiple analytical approaches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores are calculated weekly; higher scores indicate better service

### **Impact Estimate**
| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Estimated improvement from new procedure | **+2.07 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.51 to 2.62 points |
| Statistical Significance | p < 0.001 |
| Relative Improvement | ~4% |

### **What Changed**
- **Switched branches:** Scores increased from 52.8 to 53.8 after adopting the new procedure (+1.0 point)
- **Control branches:** Scores decreased from 50.9 to 49.9 over the same period (−1.0 point)
- **Net effect:** The 2-point difference represents the procedure's impact, accounting for underlying trends

### **Robustness Checks**
- Effect remains significant after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volumes
- Results hold when accounting for inherent differences between individual branches
- Post-switch improvement is visible across all 8 weeks of the treatment period

---

## **Important Considerations**

1. **Branch selection:** Branches that switched to the new procedure had slightly higher service scores throughout the study, even before the switch. This suggests they may have differed systematically from control branches.

2. **Pre-existing trends:** The gap between groups was gradually widening before week 15. Our analysis accounts for this, but it introduces some uncertainty into the exact magnitude of the effect.

3. **Limited timeframe:** We have only 8 weeks of post-implementation data. Long-term sustainability of the improvement cannot yet be confirmed.

Despite these caveats, the evidence consistently points to a positive effect from the new procedure.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Approve systemwide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches |
| **2** | **Monitor scores during rollout** to confirm effects hold across different branch types |
| **3** | **Continue data collection** for at least 6 months to assess long-term sustainability |
| **4** | **Document implementation best practices** from early-adopter branches to support training |
