# **Library Cooperative Service Procedure Change**
## **Impact Analysis & Recommendation**

---

### **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure gained **+1.9 points** on the service score index compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **3.7% improvement** and is statistically significant. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

### **Key Findings**

**Study Overview**
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)

**Results**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** | 51.6 | 52.4 | +0.7 |
| **Old Procedure** | 50.2 | 49.0 | −1.2 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.9** |

**Statistical Confidence**
- Effect size: **+1.90 points**
- 95% confidence interval: **1.34 to 2.45 points**
- Statistical significance: **p < 0.001**

**What This Means**
- The new procedure not only improved scores but also protected against the decline seen in control branches
- Results hold after accounting for staffing levels, visitor volume, seasonal patterns, and branch-specific factors
- The effect is consistent and reliable across analytical methods

---

### **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate rollout of the new procedure to all remaining branches.**

The evidence is clear: the new procedure delivers measurable service improvements. With 425 branches already successfully implementing the change and demonstrating positive results, extending this to all 709 branches aligns with our service quality goals.
