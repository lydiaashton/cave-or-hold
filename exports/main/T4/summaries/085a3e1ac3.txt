# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Subject: Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores**

**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Despite initial expectations that the change would make no difference, branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** (a 4% improvement) than branches using the old procedure. The evidence strongly supports rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### The New Procedure Works

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +1.98 service score points (95% CI: 0.80 to 3.15) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Practical Impact** | ~4% improvement over average score of 51 |
| **Branch Adoption** | 70% of new-procedure branches improved vs. 60% of old-procedure branches |

### The Benefit Grows Over Time

The improvement was not immediate but strengthened across the 8 weeks following implementation:

| Weeks After Switch | Estimated Effect |
|-------------------|------------------|
| 1-2 weeks | +2 to +3 points |
| 3-4 weeks | +2 to +6 points |
| 7-8 weeks | +5 to +8 points |

This pattern suggests staff require an adjustment period before realizing the full benefits of the new procedure.

### Study Design

- **150 branches** total (90 adopted new procedure, 60 kept old procedure)
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6-22), with switch occurring in week 15
- **2,550 observations** analyzed using difference-in-differences regression
- Controlled for branch characteristics, weekly variations, staffing levels, and visitor volume

---

## **RECOMMENDATIONS**

### Immediate Actions

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches

2. **Allocate transition support** for the first 4-6 weeks at each branch, as benefits accumulate over time

3. **Capture implementation learnings** from the 90 early-adopter branches to inform training materials

### Monitoring Plan

4. **Track service scores** for 3 months post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale

5. **Report back to the Board** within one quarter on rollout progress and outcomes

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data contradicts the initial assumption that the procedure change would have no effect. Instead, the new shelving and rotation procedure delivers a meaningful, statistically significant improvement in service scores. The Board should proceed with confidence in approving system-wide implementation.
