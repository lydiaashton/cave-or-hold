# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by approximately **2 points (4%)** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. However, the effect varies substantially across branches, and we cannot rule out the possibility of no effect with high confidence.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** while gathering additional data and investigating why some branches benefited more than others.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Is Associated with Improved Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment branches (new procedure) | 49.6 | 51.0 | +1.46 |
| Control branches (old procedure) | 46.2 | 45.6 | -0.58 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.05** |

The 2-point improvement represents approximately **4% above the baseline** service score of 48.5.

### **2. Statistical Uncertainty Remains**

- The estimated effect is **not statistically significant at the 5% level** (p=0.105)
- The 95% confidence interval ranges from **-0.4 to +4.5 points**
- Interpretation: The true effect could be slightly negative, zero, or meaningfully positive
- This uncertainty is primarily due to the limited sample size (34 branches, 8 post-switch weeks)

### **3. Effects Vary Substantially Across Branches**

Among the 20 branches that switched:
- **12 branches (60%) improved** — some by as much as 10 points
- **8 branches (40%) declined** — some by as much as 5 points

This variation suggests that **local conditions or implementation quality** may influence outcomes.

### **4. Analysis Is Methodologically Sound**

- Treatment and control groups showed **similar trends before the switch**, supporting the validity of the comparison
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visit volume
- Standard errors account for correlation within branches over time

| Action | Timeline | Responsible Party |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| **1. Continue phased rollout** to additional willing branches | Immediate | Operations Team |
| **2. Investigate branch-level variation** — interview high and low performers to identify success factors | 4-6 weeks | Program Manager |

## **Decision Framework**

We recommend the board consider the following criteria for full system-wide adoption:

| Criterion | Current Status | Target for Full Rollout |
|-----------|----------------|------------------------|
| Effect size | +2.0 points | Sustained +2.0 or higher |
| Statistical confidence | p=0.105 | p<0.05 |
| Branch consistency | 60% improved | 75%+ improved |
| Implementation learnings | Preliminary | Documented best practices |
