# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence does not support rolling out the new procedure to all branches at this time.**

The new procedure shows a positive but **statistically insignificant** effect on service scores. We cannot conclude with confidence that it improves service. We recommend **continuing the current mixed approach** while collecting more data before making a system-wide decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Analysis compares service score changes between groups before and after the switch

### **Results**

| Finding | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Estimated effect on service score | **+2.3 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.7 to +5.2 points** |
| Statistical significance | **Not significant** (p = 0.14) |

### **Interpretation**

✓ **Positive direction:** Switching branches showed higher scores after the change  
✗ **Not conclusive:** The effect could plausibly be zero (or as high as +5 points)  
✗ **Not reliable enough:** We lack statistical confidence to justify system-wide costs

### **Context**
- Service scores average around 50; a 2-point gain would be modest but meaningful
- Treatment branches had slightly higher baseline scores, which our analysis accounts for
- Staffing levels and visit volumes did not explain the observed patterns

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **1** | **Do not proceed with full rollout** | Immediate |
| **2** | Continue monitoring both groups | Ongoing |
| **3** | Collect 8–12 additional weeks of data | Next quarter |
| **4** | Re-evaluate with expanded dataset | Q1 2027 |
| **5** | Consider randomized assignment for future changes | Future initiatives |

---

## **Why This Matters**

A full rollout would involve:
- Staff retraining across all 34 branches
- Potential disruption to member services
- Implementation costs with uncertain returns

Given the **inconclusive evidence**, proceeding would risk investing resources in a change that may not deliver expected benefits.

---

## **Technical Notes**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch-clustered standard errors
- **Controls:** Staffing index and visits index
- **Data quality:** 578 branch-week observations; no missing data
- **Limitation:** Pre-switch trends differed slightly between groups (p = 0.09), which adds uncertainty
