## **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

### **Bottom Line**
Contrary to expectations, the new procedure **did affect service scores**—in a positive direction. Branches using the new procedure maintained their performance better than those using the old procedure during the post-switch period. The net benefit is **+1.9 points** on the service score index (approximately 4% improvement). We recommend **proceeding with systemwide rollout**.

---

### **Key Findings**

**1. The New Procedure Made a Positive Difference**
- We expected no change in service scores. Instead, we found a **statistically significant improvement** of +1.86 points (p=0.001; 95% CI: 0.73 to 2.99)
- This represents about a **4% improvement** relative to the average service score of 49.3
- The effect is modest but meaningful and consistent across statistical specifications

**2. How the Effect Emerged**
- Service scores declined across *all* branches after week 15 (likely seasonal or systemwide factors)
- **Control branches** (old procedure): declined by 2.57 points
- **Treatment branches** (new procedure): declined by only 0.70 points
- The new procedure appears to have **protected branches from performance declines** rather than boosting scores outright

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 50.56 | 47.98 | -2.57 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 49.63 | 48.93 | -0.70 |
| **Difference** | | | **+1.87** |

**3. Analysis Is Robust**
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (p=0.86), validating our comparison
- Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and visit volumes
- Effect is consistent whether we use simple comparisons or regression models with clustered standard errors

**4. What We Cannot Yet Determine**
- Whether the effect will persist beyond the initial 8-week post-switch period
- Whether the protective effect applies equally across all branch types and sizes
- Whether there are implementation costs not captured in service scores

---

### **Next Steps**

1. **Approve systemwide rollout** of the new procedure to remaining 60 branches

2. **Monitor during implementation** with monthly service score reviews to confirm:
   - The protective effect persists over time
   - No branches experience implementation difficulties

3. **Capture lessons learned** from the 90 early-adopter branches to support smooth transition

4. **Return to the board in Q2** with 6-month post-rollout data to confirm sustained benefits
