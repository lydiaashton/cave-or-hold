# **SUBJECT: Recommendation on New Branch Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with cooperative-wide rollout.** The new shelving procedure increased service scores by **2.2 points** (approximately 4% improvement). This result is statistically reliable and aligns with our expectations for the initiative.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Branches | Before | After | Change |
|-------|----------|--------|-------|--------|
| Kept Old Procedure | 284 | 51.03 | 49.87 | −1.16 |
| Adopted New Procedure | 425 | 51.00 | 52.02 | +1.02 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | | **+2.19** |

### **Confidence in Results**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | +2.2 points |
| 95% Confidence interval | +1.65 to +2.76 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 (highly reliable) |
| Data coverage | 709 branches, 17 weeks |

### **What This Means**

- Branches using the new procedure **improved** while those keeping the old procedure **declined** (likely due to seasonal factors)
- The 2.2-point advantage is the **isolated effect** of the procedure change
- Results held after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve cooperative-wide implementation of the new shelving procedure.**

The evidence supports our expectation that this change would improve service quality. With 425 branches already successfully using the procedure and clear positive results, extending it to the remaining 284 branches is the logical next step.
