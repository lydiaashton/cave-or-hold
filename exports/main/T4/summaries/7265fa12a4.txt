# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Roll out the new procedure to all branches.** The data show it improved service scores by **2.2 points**—a meaningful gain that contradicts our initial expectation of no change.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Works
| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Service score improvement | **+2.2 points** |
| Baseline average score | ~48 points |
| Relative improvement | **4.6%** |
| Statistical confidence | **p < 0.001** (highly significant) |
| 95% confidence interval | 1.5 to 3.0 points |

### The Evidence Is Solid
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** switched to the new procedure; **60 branches** kept the old one (control group)
- Before week 15, both groups followed **parallel trends**—validating the comparison
- The improvement holds after accounting for branch differences, weekly fluctuations, staffing levels, and visitor volumes

### What Changed
```
Before the switch (weeks 6–14):
  Switched branches:  48.16 avg score
  Non-switched:       48.17 avg score
  Difference:         -0.02 (essentially equal)

After the switch (weeks 15–22):
  Switched branches:  52.20 avg score
  Non-switched:       49.98 avg score
  Difference:         +2.22 ✓
```

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all 150 branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Implement rollout** to remaining branches within the next quarter
2. **Document procedures** and prepare training materials for staff
3. **Monitor service scores** post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale
4. **Investigate mechanisms**—understand why the new procedure works to identify further improvements

---

*Analysis based on weekly service scores from weeks 6–22. Method: difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects.*
