# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improves service scores**. Branches that switched showed a **+1.9 point gain** relative to those that did not. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Works

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 45.7 | 48.1 | **+2.4** |
| Non-Switched Branches | 49.5 | 50.0 | +0.5 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.9** |

The treatment group improved by 2.4 points while the control group improved by only 0.5 points. The **net attributable effect is +1.9 points**.

### 2. Evidence Is Consistent

- **Multiple analytical approaches** all point to the same conclusion (basic comparison, regression with controls, branch fixed effects)
- **Event study analysis** shows the treatment group's performance improved relative to their own pre-switch trajectory
- **No negative effects** observed in any post-switch week

### 3. Practical Significance

- A +1.9 point improvement on a ~50-point scale represents **nearly 4% better service**
- For a cooperative of 34 branches, this translates to meaningfully better patron experience across the system
- The procedure requires no additional staffing or budget—only a change in workflow

### 4. Study Strengths

- **34 branches** with complete data over **17 weeks** (578 observations)
- **Natural experiment design** with both treatment and control groups
- **Pre-switch trends were parallel**, validating the comparison
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visit volumes

---

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all remaining branches.**
