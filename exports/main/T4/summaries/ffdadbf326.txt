# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

Contrary to expectations, the new procedure produced a measurable improvement in service scores. Branches that switched saw scores increase by **2.0 points** (4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant, stable over time, and robust across analytical checks.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Treatment Branches (Switched) | Control Branches (Not Switched) |
|--------|-------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Pre-switch average | 50.01 | 51.02 |
| Post-switch average | 52.29 | 51.25 |
| **Change** | **+2.28** | **+0.23** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +2.05 points** (p < 0.001)

### What the Data Shows

1. **Clear improvement:** The 2-point gain is consistent across all 8 post-switch weeks, with no sign of fade-out

2. **Credible causal evidence:** Before the switch, treatment branches actually scored lower than control branches. After the switch, they scored higher. This reversal supports a genuine treatment effect rather than pre-existing differences

3. **Not explained by other factors:** The effect holds when accounting for staffing levels, visit volumes, branch-specific characteristics, and week-to-week variation

4. **Quick adoption:** Benefits appeared immediately and remained stable, suggesting branches adapted to the new procedure without a prolonged learning curve
