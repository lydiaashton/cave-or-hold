# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted it saw service scores improve by **1.9 points** (3.7%) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant, consistent across all post-switch weeks, and appears to grow over time.

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Control Branches | Net Effect |
|--------|------------------|------------------|------------|
| Average score change | +1.07 points | −0.79 points | **+1.86 points** |
| Branches that improved | 61% | 39% | — |
| Statistical significance | — | — | p < 0.001 |

**What this means:** Of the 425 branches that switched, most saw their service scores rise. Meanwhile, the 284 branches that kept the old procedure saw scores drift downward. The new procedure accounts for the difference.

**The benefit grows over time:** The gap between switched and control branches widened from +1.5 points in week 1 after the switch to +3.7 points by week 8, suggesting branches realize more value as they gain experience with the new procedure.

---

## **Why We're Confident**

- **Strong study design:** We compared switched branches against similar branches that kept the old procedure, before and after the change
- **Large sample:** 709 branches, 17 weeks of data, over 12,000 observations
- **Robust results:** The finding holds across multiple statistical approaches
- **No alternative explanation:** Staffing and visitor patterns don't account for the difference
