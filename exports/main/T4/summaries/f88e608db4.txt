# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving & Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

The new procedure **improved service scores by 2–4 points** (4–8% improvement). The effect is statistically significant, sustained across all 8 post-switch weeks, and consistent across multiple analytical methods. With 425 branches already successfully using the procedure, we recommend expanding to all remaining branches.

---

## Evidence Summary

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.0 to +3.6 service score points |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Observation Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Effect Duration** | Sustained across all 8 post-switch weeks |

### Service Score Performance

| Period | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|------------------|------------------|
| Before switch | 50.3 | 48.7 |
| After switch | **55.6** | 52.0 |
| **Improvement** | **+5.3 points** | +3.3 points |

The switched branches improved **2 points more** than control branches after accounting for underlying trends.

---

## Why We Are Confident

1. **Consistent methodology:** Three different analytical approaches (simple DiD, branch fixed effects, event study) all show positive, significant effects.

2. **Sustained impact:** The effect did not fade over time—each post-switch week showed significant improvement.

3. **Robust to controls:** The result holds even after accounting for staffing levels and visitor traffic.

4. **Within-branch validation:** When comparing each branch to its own pre-switch performance, the improvement remains significant.

---

## Implementation Considerations

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Expected gain per branch** | 2–4 service score points |
| **Risk level** | Low (procedure already tested at 425 branches) |
| **Timeline** | No urgency; effect is stable over time |
| **Monitoring needed** | Track service scores during rollout to ensure consistency |

---

## Recommended Actions

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** to remaining 284 branches.

2. **Assign implementation timeline** based on operational capacity (no rush required—effect is stable).

3. **Continue monitoring** service scores, staffing index, and visits index during rollout.

4. **Document best practices** from high-performing switched branches to support training.

---

## Conclusion

The data confirms what we expected: **the new procedure works.** Service scores improved meaningfully and sustainably at branches that adopted it. We recommend moving forward with confidence.
