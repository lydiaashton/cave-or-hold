# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2 points**—a result that exceeded expectations. Given the clear, statistically significant benefit with no observed downside, we recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **What We Found**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 52.6 | 53.9 | **+1.3** |
| Branches that did not switch | 52.0 | 51.2 | **–0.9** |
| **Net effect of switching** | | | **+2.1** |

**Key points:**
- The 2.1-point improvement is **statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- The effect was **consistent across all 8 weeks** after the switch
- The result holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Why We Trust This Result**

1. **Strong study design**: 90 branches adopted the new procedure while 60 continued with the old one, creating a natural comparison group.

2. **Parallel trends confirmed**: Before the switch, both groups followed similar score trajectories (statistical test p = 0.93). This validates the comparison.

3. **Robust analysis**: The finding persists using multiple analytical approaches, including branch-level controls and cluster-robust standard errors.

4. **No fading effect**: Benefits were sustained throughout the 8-week observation period with no sign of diminishing returns.

---

## **What This Means**

We entered this evaluation expecting no measurable change. The data show otherwise: the new rotation and shelving procedure delivers a real, meaningful improvement in service quality as measured by our service score index.

On a scale where the average branch scores about 50, a 2-point gain represents roughly a **4% improvement**. Importantly, branches that did not switch experienced a slight decline during the same period, suggesting the new procedure may also help branches resist external pressures that typically erode service quality.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **Approve system-wide rollout** | Clear evidence of benefit with no identified risks |
| **Phase implementation over 4–6 weeks** | Allows training capacity to keep pace with demand |
| **Continue monitoring service scores** | Confirms effect persists at full scale |
| **Document the new procedure** | Captures institutional knowledge for training |

---

## **Questions the Board May Have**

**Q: Could this be a temporary effect?**  
A: Possible, but we saw no decay over 8 weeks. Continued monitoring is warranted.

**Q: Are there branches that might not benefit?**  
A: We did not detect meaningful variation by branch characteristics, but individual monitoring during rollout is prudent.

**Q: What if we had expected no change—does that undermine confidence?**  
A: No. Unexpected positive findings are common in operational improvements. The rigorous design and statistical validation support the conclusion regardless of prior expectations.

---

**Decision requested:** Authorize system-wide rollout of the new rotation and shelving procedure.
