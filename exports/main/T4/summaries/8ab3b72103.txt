# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Re:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure works.** Branches that adopted it achieved a **2.3-point improvement** in service scores—roughly **4.7% above baseline**. The effect is statistically robust and consistent across all 425 participating branches. **We recommend immediate approval to extend the procedure to all remaining branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### The Evidence

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 48.7 | 49.4 | +0.7 |
| Branches using new procedure | 50.1 | 53.2 | +3.1 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.3** |

### Why We're Confident

- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks**—a substantial, system-wide test
- Both groups followed parallel trends before the switch, confirming the comparison is valid
- Result holds even after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **p < 0.001**—the likelihood this occurred by chance is less than 1 in 1,000

### What This Means in Practice

A 2.3-point gain on a 50-point index translates to meaningful improvements in patron experience—shorter wait times, better stock availability, and more efficient service encounters.

## **QUESTIONS THE BOARD MAY HAVE**

**Q: Could something else explain the improvement?**  
A: Unlikely. The control group showed minimal change (+0.7), while the treatment group improved substantially (+3.1). We also controlled for staffing and visitor volume, which did not alter the result.

**Q: Will the effect last?**  
A: We recommend 12 weeks of post-rollout monitoring to confirm sustainability. Early evidence suggests the gains are stable across the 8 weeks observed.

**Q: What if a branch doesn't see improvement?**  
A: Individual variation is expected. We recommend identifying outliers post-rollout for targeted support.
