# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved significantly higher service scores than those that kept the old method. The improvement is statistically significant, meaningful in practical terms, and consistent across the post-implementation period. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Service Scores Improved by Nearly 2 Points**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 50.59 | 51.02 | +0.43 |
| Branches using new procedure | 50.26 | 52.62 | +2.36 |

**Net effect of new procedure: +1.93 points** (95% confidence interval: 0.78 to 3.08)

This represents approximately a **4% improvement** in service quality relative to baseline scores.

### **2. The Result Is Reliable**

- **Statistically significant:** The probability this result occurred by chance is less than 1 in 1,000 (p=0.001)
- **Robust to controls:** The effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Consistent over time:** Improvement was observed in both the immediate weeks after switching and in later weeks
- **Valid comparison:** Before the switch, both groups of branches followed parallel trends, confirming the analysis design is sound

### **3. What the Numbers Mean**

Service scores average about 50 points across all branches. The new procedure delivered nearly a 2-point gain above what would have been expected from normal week-to-week variation. For context, branches that did not switch saw only a 0.4-point change during the same period.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all 60 branches still using the old method |
| **2** | Schedule staff training sessions to ensure consistent implementation |
| **3** | Continue monitoring service scores quarterly to confirm sustained improvement |
| **4** | Document best practices from high-performing switched branches to support training |

---

## **Questions for Board Consideration**

1. What is the timeline for full rollout?
2. Are there budget implications for training or materials?
3. Should we establish a formal review checkpoint (e.g., 6 months post-rollout) to assess long-term effects?

---

## **Appendix: Analysis Details**

- **Data:** 150 branches, 17 weeks of observations (weeks 6–22), 2,550 total records
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch-level clustering
- **Implementation date:** Week 15
- **Treatment group:** 90 branches that switched procedures
- **Control group:** 60 branches that retained the old procedure
