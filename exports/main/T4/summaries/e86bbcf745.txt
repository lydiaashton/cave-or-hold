# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks of data)

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Contrary to our initial expectation that the change would make no difference, branches that adopted the new procedure maintained their service scores while control branches experienced a notable decline. The net effect was a **+1.9-point improvement** (approximately 4% relative to baseline), which is statistically significant. **We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** tracked over 17 weeks
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **60 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Analysis uses difference-in-differences methodology with branch and week controls

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 49.86 | 49.56 | -0.31 |
| Branches that did not switch | 49.14 | 46.92 | -2.22 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.92** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Effect size:** +1.92 points on the service score index
- **95% confidence interval:** +0.7 to +3.1 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.002 (highly significant)
- **Validity check confirmed:** Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (p = 0.50), supporting causal interpretation

### Interpretation
1. **External headwinds affected all branches:** Service scores declined across the cooperative after week 15, suggesting seasonal or external factors
2. **The new procedure provided protection:** Treatment branches declined far less than control branches, effectively shielding them from the broader downturn
3. **Results are robust:** Findings hold when controlling for branch-specific characteristics and week-specific factors

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new stock rotation procedure to all 60 branches still using the old method. The evidence indicates the procedure delivers measurable service quality benefits.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve rollout plan** with target completion within 8–12 weeks

2. **Monitor implementation** by tracking service scores as remaining branches transition

3. **Capture best practices** from the 90 early-adopter branches to smooth the transition for others

4. **Conduct follow-up evaluation** after full rollout to confirm sustained benefits and identify any branches needing additional support
