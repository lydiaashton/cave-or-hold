# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** New Branch Shelving & Rotation Procedure – Impact Assessment  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Recommendation:** Proceed with System-Wide Rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation system achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a **4.3% improvement**. The evidence is clear and statistically robust. We recommend approving immediate rollout to all remaining branches.

---

## **What We Did**

When the new procedure launched in Week 15, some branches adopted it while others continued with the old system. This gave us a natural experiment to measure the actual impact. We analyzed **12,053 weekly observations** from **709 branches** over 17 weeks using standard difference-in-differences methodology.

---

## **What We Found**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| **New Procedure Branches** | 49.7 | 50.4 | +0.7 |
| **Old Procedure Branches** | 49.4 | 48.0 | −1.5 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

**Key Points:**

1. **Statistically significant:** Less than 0.1% probability this result occurred by chance
2. **Confidence interval:** True effect lies between 1.6 and 2.7 points (95% confidence)
3. **Validated comparison:** Treatment and control branches followed parallel trends before the switch
4. **Robust finding:** Result holds when controlling for staffing levels, visit volumes, and branch-specific factors

---

## **What This Means**

A 2.1-point improvement on the service score index represents meaningful progress in patron experience. Notably, control branches experienced a decline during this period, suggesting the new procedure also **protected** adopting branches from system-wide pressures that negatively affected service quality elsewhere.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new shelving and rotation procedure across all remaining branches.
