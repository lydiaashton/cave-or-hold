# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** [Current Date]  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improves service scores. We recommend proceeding with a phased rollout to all branches.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2 points (4% improvement)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant, consistent across all post-switch weeks, and persisted throughout the 8-week observation period.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| **Observation period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences analysis |

### **Results**

| Measure | Estimate |
|---|---|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.8 to +2.1 points |
| **Percentage gain** | ~4% (on a ~50-point baseline) |
| **Statistical confidence** | p < 0.001 |
| **95% confidence interval** | 1.5 to 2.6 points |

### **Effect Consistency**
The improvement was sustained across all 8 post-switch weeks, ranging from +1.9 to +3.2 points per week. There is no evidence of the effect diminishing over time.

### **Distributional Effects**
Lower-performing branches benefited slightly more (+2.3 points) than higher-performing branches (+1.9 points), suggesting the procedure may help reduce performance gaps across the cooperative.

---

## **LIMITATIONS**

1. **Pre-existing trends:** Branches that switched were already on a slightly more positive trajectory before the change. Our adjusted estimates account for this, but some uncertainty remains.

2. **Observation window:** We have only 8 weeks of post-switch data. Longer-term sustainability cannot yet be confirmed.

3. **Effect size:** While meaningful, a 2-point improvement is modest. The procedure should be viewed as one component of broader service quality efforts.

---

## **RECOMMENDATIONS**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Approve rollout** of new procedure to remaining branches | Immediate |
| **2** | **Phase implementation** in 2–3 batches to manage operational capacity | 2–3 months |
| **3** | **Continue monitoring** service scores for 6+ months post-rollout | Ongoing |
| **4** | **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** to quantify operational costs vs. service gains | Before full rollout |
| **5** | **Gather qualitative feedback** from branch managers on implementation experience | During rollout |

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data support expanding the new rotation and shelving procedure to all branches. The improvement in service scores is real, statistically robust, and beneficial across branch types. A phased rollout with continued monitoring will allow the cooperative to capture these gains while managing implementation risk.
