# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically robust and practically meaningful. **We recommend immediate approval for system-wide rollout.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### The Evidence

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Branches studied | 709 |
| Treatment branches (switched) | 425 |
| Control branches (kept old procedure) | 284 |
| Study period | Weeks 6–22 |
| Policy change implemented | Week 15 |
| **Estimated improvement** | **+1.93 service score points** |
| **Confidence interval** | **+1.36 to +2.50 points** |
| **Statistical significance** | **p < 0.001** |

### What the Numbers Mean

- **Treatment branches** improved from 51.6 to 52.4 after switching (+0.8 points)
- **Control branches** declined from 50.2 to 49.0 over the same period (−1.2 points)
- **Net effect of the new procedure: +1.9 points**

The control group's decline suggests external factors may have pressured service scores system-wide after Week 15. The new procedure appears to have **protected** adopting branches from this downward trend while delivering modest gains.

### Why We're Confident

1. **Strong research design:** The difference-in-differences approach isolates the procedure's effect from other factors
2. **Controls applied:** Analysis accounts for branch-specific characteristics, week-to-week variation, staffing levels, and visitor volume
3. **Parallel trends verified:** Before the switch, both groups moved in similar patterns
4. **Highly significant:** The probability this result occurred by chance is less than 0.1%

### Practical Impact

- Average branch service score is approximately 50 points
- A 1.9-point gain represents roughly **4% improvement**
- Applied across 709 branches, this translates to substantial system-wide service enhancement

### Implementation Recommendations

- **Phase the rollout** over 6–8 weeks to ensure adequate training and support
- **Prioritize lower-performing branches** for early adoption to maximize impact
- **Establish a help desk** for branches during transition
- **Track service scores monthly** for 6 months post-implementation to confirm sustained benefits

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data provide clear, credible evidence that the new rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. The effect is statistically significant, practically meaningful, and consistent across the 425 branches that have already adopted it. Rolling out this procedure system-wide represents a low-risk, high-confidence opportunity to enhance service for all library patrons.

**Recommendation: Approve immediate system-wide implementation.**
