# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 2025
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** (a 4% improvement) than branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, emerged within 3–4 weeks of implementation, and was not driven by changes in staffing or visitor volume.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total branches studied | 709 |
| Branches using new procedure | 425 (60%) |
| Branches using old procedure | 284 (40%) |
| Study period | Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks) |
| Switch implemented | Week 15 |

### Service Score Results

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** | 50.96 | 52.55 | **+1.59** |
| **Old Procedure** | 49.85 | 49.37 | **-0.48** |

**Net Treatment Effect: +2.07 points**
- 95% Confidence Interval: +1.54 to +2.60
- Statistical Significance: p < 0.001
- Effect Size: ~4% improvement over baseline

### Supporting Evidence

1. **Gradual improvement**: Benefits accumulated over time, with significant gains visible by week 18 (3 weeks post-switch). This pattern is consistent with staff learning and adapting to new procedures.

2. **Direct effect confirmed**: The improvement was not explained by changes in staffing levels or branch visits. The procedure itself drove the gain.

3. **Robust methodology**: The difference-in-differences design controls for pre-existing differences between branches and system-wide trends affecting all locations.

---

## **Recommendations**

### Immediate Actions

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** to all 284 branches currently using the old procedure.

2. **Communicate expected timeline**: Inform branch managers that full benefits typically emerge 3–4 weeks after implementation.

3. **Allocate transition support**: Consider temporary additional staffing or training resources during the first month of adoption at each branch.

### Monitoring & Evaluation

4. **Track rollout progress**: Monitor service scores for 8–12 weeks post-implementation at each newly converted branch.

5. **Capture implementation learnings**: Document practices from high-performing early adopters to accelerate success at remaining branches.

6. **Plan follow-up evaluation**: Assess whether effects persist at 6 and 12 months post-rollout.

---

## **Decision Requested**

The Board is asked to:

☐ **Approve** system-wide rollout of the new rotation and shelving procedure

☐ **Authorize** staff to develop an implementation timeline for remaining 284 branches

☐ **Direct** management to report back on rollout progress within 90 days
