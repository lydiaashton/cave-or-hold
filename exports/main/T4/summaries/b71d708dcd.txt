# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than comparable branches using the old method. This represents a **4% improvement** and is both statistically significant and practically meaningful. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Clear Positive Impact**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (90) | 50.0 | 52.3 | +2.3 |
| Non-switched branches (60) | 51.0 | 51.3 | +0.2 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

The 2-point improvement is statistically significant with 95% confidence (true effect likely between 1.3 and 2.8 points).

### **2. Strong Evidence This Was Caused by the Procedure Change**

Our analysis accounts for:
- Differences between individual branches (some consistently score higher)
- Week-to-week fluctuations affecting all branches
- Staffing levels and visit volumes

Before the switch, treatment branches actually scored *lower* than control branches. After the switch, they scored *higher*. This reversal pattern strongly indicates the procedure change drove the improvement.

### **3. Benefits Are Sustained**

The improvement was consistent across both the early weeks (15–18) and later weeks (19–22) after implementation, indicating:
- Branches adapted quickly to the new procedure
- Benefits are not temporary but persist over time

### **4. Results Hold Up Under Scrutiny**

Multiple robustness checks confirm the finding:
- Effect remains when excluding the first transition week
- Effect remains when controlling for staffing and visit patterns
- No evidence that only certain types of branches benefited

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 60 branches currently using the old method.
