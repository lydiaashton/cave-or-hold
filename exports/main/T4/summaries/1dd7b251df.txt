# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores and should be rolled out to all branches.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a **2.2-point increase** in service scores (approximately 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically significant, robust across multiple analytical methods, and sustained over the 8-week post-implementation period.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Average treatment effect | **+2.16 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | +1.60 to +2.71 |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Baseline average score | ~50 points |
| Percentage improvement | **~4.3%** |

### 2. Consistent Results Across Methods
The estimated effect is identical across four different analytical approaches, confirming the finding is not an artifact of any single method:

- Branch fixed effects model: **+2.16**
- Branch fixed effects + covariates: **+2.17**
- Branch + week fixed effects: **+2.16**
- Simple difference-in-differences: **+2.16**

### 3. Effect Timeline
- **Weeks 6–14 (before switch):** Treatment branches trailed control branches by an average of 0.76 points
- **Weeks 15–22 (after switch):** Treatment branches led control branches by an average of 1.48 points
- **Net improvement attributable to new procedure:** **+2.24 points**

The fact that treatment branches were underperforming before the switch makes this result more compelling—the new procedure reversed a negative trend.

### 4. Sustained Impact
The treatment effect was not temporary:
- Week 15 (immediate): +0.73 (adjustment period)
- Weeks 16–22: Effects ranged from +1.39 to +2.22, all statistically significant
- No evidence of effect decay over the 8-week observation period

### 5. Study Scope
- **709 branches** total (425 adopted new procedure; 284 retained old procedure)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22)
- **12,053 observations** (branch-week records)

---

## **Recommendations**

### Immediate Actions

1. **Approve systemwide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 284 branches currently using the old procedure.

2. **Establish implementation timeline** with phased deployment to allow for training and support.

3. **Allocate resources** for staff training on the new procedure at each branch.

### Monitoring Plan

4. **Track service scores weekly** during rollout to verify effects match pilot results.

5. **Flag underperforming branches** for additional support if scores do not improve within 4–6 weeks of implementation.

6. **Report progress to the board** quarterly until rollout is complete.

### Longer-Term Considerations

7. **Document best practices** from high-performing branches to inform training materials.

8. **Evaluate complementary investments** in staffing, which also positively affects service scores.

9. **Plan long-term evaluation** (6–12 months) to confirm effects persist and identify any unintended consequences.

---

## **Conclusion**

The evidence is clear and compelling: the new branch rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. The effect is large enough to be meaningful for patrons, consistent across analytical methods, and sustained over time. We recommend immediate approval for systemwide implementation.
