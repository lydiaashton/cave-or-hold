# **Library Cooperative Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores.** Contrary to the expectation of no difference, branches that switched to the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **1.7 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and has persisted throughout the 8-week post-implementation period.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 150 total (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| **Time period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Analysis method** | Difference-in-Differences with cluster-robust standard errors |

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** | 51.20 | 51.65 | +0.45 |
| **Non-switched branches** | 54.16 | 52.88 | −1.28 |
| **Net effect (DiD)** | | | **+1.73** |

### **Statistical Evidence**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated effect** | +1.73 service score points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | [0.64, 2.81] |
| **P-value** | 0.002 |
| **Statistically significant** | Yes |

### **Reliability Checks**

✓ **Parallel trends verified**: Treatment and control groups followed similar trajectories before the switch (p = 0.69)

✓ **Effect is sustained**: Positive impact observed in both early post-period weeks 15–18 (+1.82 points) and later weeks 19–22 (+1.63 points)

✓ **Robust to covariates**: Effect remains significant after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all 60 branches currently using the old method.

2. **Monitor implementation** over the next 8–12 weeks to confirm the effect persists at scale and identify any branches needing additional transition support.

3. **Capture best practices** from the 90 early-adopter branches to facilitate smoother adoption across the cooperative.

4. **Conduct cost-benefit review** to ensure the service score gains justify any additional resource requirements (staff time, training, equipment).

5. **Schedule follow-up assessment** in Q2 to evaluate long-term impacts and determine if further procedure refinements are warranted.
