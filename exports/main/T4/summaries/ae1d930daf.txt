# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure saw their service scores increase by an average of **2.0 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **4% improvement** and is statistically significant. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Clear Positive Impact

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| **Branches with new procedure** | 52.4 | 52.6 | +0.2 |
| **Branches with old procedure** | 51.0 | 49.1 | −1.9 |

Branches using the new procedure maintained their service scores, while branches using the old procedure experienced a decline. The **net benefit of the new procedure is +2.0 points** on the service score index.

### 2. Statistical Confidence

- **Effect size:** +2.04 points (95% confidence interval: 1.5 to 2.6 points)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly confident this is not due to chance)
- **Analysis method:** Difference-in-differences regression with 12,053 observations across 709 branches over 17 weeks

### 3. Benefits Across All Branch Types

The improvement was consistent regardless of branch characteristics:

- **Staffing levels:** Effects ranged from +1.7 to +2.4 points across low, medium, and high staffing branches
- **Visit volumes:** Effects ranged from +1.6 to +2.5 points across low, medium, and high traffic branches

This consistency suggests the procedure is robust and will work well across the entire cooperative.

### 4. Performance Gap Grew After Implementation

Before the switch, branches that would later adopt the new procedure scored about 1.5 points higher on average. After implementation, this gap widened to 3.0–4.5 points, confirming the new procedure created meaningful additional value.
