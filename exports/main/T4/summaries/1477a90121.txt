# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure has produced a **positive improvement in branch service scores**. Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **1.9 points (4% improvement)** relative to branches that maintained the old procedure. Based on these findings, we recommend **proceeding with systemwide rollout**.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Yes** – positive effect observed |
| How large is the improvement? | **+1.9 points** (~4% above baseline) |
| How confident are we? | **95%** of statistical simulations show a positive effect |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Yes** – with continued monitoring |

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (February–June)
- **20 branches** implemented the new procedure starting week 15
- **14 branches** continued with the standard procedure (comparison group)
- Service scores averaged ~50 points across all branches (higher = better)

### Results

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 49.7 | 50.9 | **+1.2** |
| Non-switched branches | 49.6 | 48.9 | **-0.8** |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

### What This Means

1. **Branches that switched improved** while comparison branches declined over the same period
2. **The 1.9-point advantage** is attributable to the new procedure, not broader trends
3. **95% of statistical simulations** confirm the effect is positive
4. **No negative consequences detected** – the procedure did not harm service quality at any branch

### Ongoing Monitoring

| Metric | Frequency | Responsibility |
|--------|-----------|----------------|

### Success Criteria

- Maintain service score improvement of **≥1.5 points** across all branches
- Complete rollout within **90 days**
- No increase in staff complaints or operational issues

## **Risk Assessment**

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Effect diminishes over time | Low | Continue monitoring; adjust procedure if needed |
| Implementation costs exceed benefits | Low | Track costs; procedure requires minimal new resources |

## **Conclusion**

The data supports moving forward with the new stock rotation and shelving procedure. The improvement in service scores is meaningful, consistent across branches, and achieved without negative side effects. A systemwide rollout will extend these benefits to all library patrons.
