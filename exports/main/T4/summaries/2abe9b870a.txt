# **BOARD SUMMARY: New Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores** (+2.2 points, ~4% improvement), but the evidence falls short of statistical certainty. We recommend **proceeding with rollout** while extending data collection to confirm the effect.

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.21 service score points |
| **Direction** | Positive (as expected) |
| **Statistical Confidence** | 90% confident effect is real (p=0.094) |
| **Range of True Effect** | Likely between -0.4 and +4.8 points |
| **No Evidence of Harm** | ✓ Zero branches showed sustained decline |

**What this means:** The procedure is working in the expected direction. The main question is not *whether* it helps, but *how much*—and we need more time to pin that down.

---

## **Why the Uncertainty?**

1. **Short observation window** — Only 8 weeks of post-switch data
2. **Natural score variability** — Weekly scores fluctuate ±10 points on average
3. **Limited branches** — 34 branches provides moderate statistical power

Importantly, **nothing in the data contradicts** the expectation that the new procedure improves service.

| **Continue weekly score tracking** for all branches | Ongoing |
