# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2 points** more than branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, grew stronger over time, and showed no adverse consequences.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6-14) | After Switch (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** (425 branches) | 51.05 | 54.75 | +3.70 |
| **Old Procedure** (284 branches) | 50.17 | 51.92 | +1.75 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | — | — | **+1.95** |

The **1.95-point net improvement** is the estimated causal effect of the new procedure, after accounting for trends that affected all branches equally.

### **2. Strong Statistical Evidence**

- The effect is **highly statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- Analysis covered **12,053 weekly observations** across **709 branches**
- Results control for staffing levels and visitor volume

### **3. Benefits Emerged After Brief Adjustment Period**

| Weeks After Switch | Effect Size (vs. Control) |
|-------------------|--------------------------|
| 1-2 | Minimal (staff learning phase) |
| 3-4 | +3.5 to +6.0 points |
| 5-8 | +3.3 to +6.9 points |

Staff required approximately 2-3 weeks to become proficient with the new procedure, after which benefits were sustained.

### **4. Context for the Improvement**

- The average branch scores around 50 on the service index
- A **2-point improvement represents approximately 4% gain** in service quality
- Both groups improved after week 14 (likely seasonal effects), but the new procedure group improved more than twice as much

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Immediate Actions**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all 284 branches currently using the old method.

2. **Budget for training support** during the first 2-3 weeks of implementation at each branch to accelerate the learning curve.

3. **Set realistic expectations** with branch managers that full benefits will emerge after a brief adjustment period.

### **Ongoing Monitoring**

4. **Track service scores quarterly** to confirm effects persist long-term.

5. **Identify underperforming branches** post-implementation for targeted support.

---

## **Analytical Approach**

This evaluation used a **Difference-in-Differences** design, comparing branches that adopted the new procedure (treatment group) to those that did not (control group), before and after the week-15 switch point. This method isolates the effect of the procedure change from other factors affecting all branches.
