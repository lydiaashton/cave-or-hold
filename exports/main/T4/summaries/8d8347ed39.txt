# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (about 4% improvement) relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically significant and robust. **We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.**

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**1. The New Procedure Improved Service Scores**
- Treatment branches gained **2.1 points** on the service score index relative to control branches
- 95% confidence interval: **1.0 to 3.3 points**
- Highly statistically significant (p = 0.0003)

**2. The Comparison Is Valid**
- 90 branches switched to the new procedure; 60 branches kept the old procedure
- **Pre-trends were parallel**: Before the switch (weeks 6–14), both groups followed similar trajectories (p = 0.36), confirming the groups are comparable
- Results hold when controlling for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week variation

**3. No Alternative Explanations**
- Staffing levels did not change differently between groups (p = 0.42)
- Visit volumes did not change differently between groups (p = 1.00)
- The service score improvement is attributable to the procedure change itself

**4. Practical Significance**
- On a scale where the average branch scores about 50, a 2.1-point gain represents a **4% improvement**
- This improvement was achieved without additional staffing or increased visits

---

### **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all branches

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with phased deployment to manage operational capacity

3. **Create training materials** to ensure consistent adoption across all branches

4. **Monitor service scores** post-rollout to confirm the effect persists at scale

5. **Document lessons learned** from the pilot branches to support smooth implementation
