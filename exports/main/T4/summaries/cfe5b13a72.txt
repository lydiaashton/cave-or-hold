# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure **improved service scores by a statistically significant but modest amount** (+1.6 points, approximately 3%). Branches that adopted the new procedure gained ground relative to those that did not, though they have not yet fully closed the pre-existing performance gap. We recommend **proceeding with a phased system-wide rollout** while allowing adequate time for implementation and monitoring.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. The New Procedure Had a Positive Effect

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+1.59 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | 0.23 to 2.96 points |
| Statistical significance | **Yes (p = 0.029)** |
| Relative improvement | ~3% above baseline |

### 2. Performance by Group

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** (20 branches) | 50.38 | 51.96 | **+1.57** |
| **Non-switched branches** (14 branches) | 52.30 | 52.27 | −0.03 |

Branches adopting the new procedure showed clear improvement. Branches keeping the old procedure remained essentially unchanged.

### 3. Important Context

- **Switching branches started behind:** Before the change, switching branches averaged 1.9 points below non-switching branches
- **The gap narrowed substantially:** After the switch, the gap closed to only 0.3 points
- **Results varied week-to-week:** Some post-switch weeks showed strong gains while others showed temporary dips, suggesting an adjustment period

### 4. What Did Not Drive the Results

- Staffing levels and visitor volume did not significantly affect service scores in this analysis
- The improvement is attributable to the procedure change itself, not external factors

---

## **RECOMMENDATIONS**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Proceed with phased rollout** to remaining branches | Evidence supports expansion, but gradual implementation reduces risk |
| **2** | **Allow 3–6 months for full implementation** at each branch | The 8-week post-switch window shows adjustment variability; benefits may grow over time |
| **3** | **Provide implementation support** during transition | Week-to-week variation suggests some branches need additional training or resources |
| **4** | **Continue monitoring** service scores quarterly | Track whether switching branches fully close the gap with higher-performing branches |
| **5** | **Document best practices** from top-performing switching branches | Identify what successful implementation looks like to guide other branches |

---

## **ANALYTICAL NOTES**

This evaluation used a **difference-in-differences design** with the following features:

- **578 observations** across 34 branches over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 treatment branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 control branches** did not
- **Branch fixed effects** control for permanent differences between branches
- **Cluster-robust standard errors** account for correlation within branches over time
- The switch occurred in **week 15**, providing 9 weeks of pre-data and 8 weeks of post-data

**Limitations:** The post-switch period is relatively short (8 weeks). Longer-term effects may differ from initial results.

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure delivers a **real, measurable improvement** in service quality. While the effect is modest, it is statistically reliable and directionally consistent with expectations. A thoughtful, phased expansion—paired with implementation support and ongoing monitoring—positions the cooperative to realize cumulative benefits across all branches.
