# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new stock rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by an average of **2.1 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** over baseline performance and is statistically significant. We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **709 branches** total: 425 switched to the new procedure, 284 kept the old procedure
- Data covers **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- Analysis compares changes in switching branches to changes in non-switching branches

### **Results**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | **+2.1 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | [1.4, 2.9] |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Effect size | 0.21 standard deviations |

### **Supporting Evidence**

1. **Groups were comparable before the switch.** Treatment and control branches had nearly identical service scores before week 15 (difference of only 0.1 points), validating our comparison.

2. **The effect emerged after the switch.** After week 15, treatment branches consistently outperformed control branches.

3. **The effect grows over time.** The improvement was larger in weeks 19–22 (+2.6 points) than in weeks 15–18 (+1.6 points), suggesting branches benefit more as they gain experience with the new procedure.

4. **Results hold after accounting for other factors.** The effect remains unchanged when controlling for staffing levels and visit volumes.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Implement rollout plan** with a clear timeline for remaining branches.

2. **Provide training and support** during the first 4–8 weeks after each branch switches, as the effect strengthens with experience.

3. **Continue monitoring service scores** to confirm the effect persists at scale and identify branches that may need additional support.

4. **Document best practices** from early-adopter branches to accelerate learning across the cooperative.

---

*Analysis based on weekly service scores from 709 branches over 17 weeks using difference-in-differences methodology.*
