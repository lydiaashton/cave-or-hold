# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Contrary to initial expectations, branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than branches that retained the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant and consistent across the 90 branches that switched.

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Overview**
- **150 branches** participated: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 retained the old (control group)
- **17 weeks of observation:** 9 weeks before the switch (weeks 6–14), 8 weeks after (weeks 15–22)
- Analysis compared score changes between the two groups to isolate the procedure's effect

### **2. Service Score Results**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| New Procedure (90 branches) | 51.7 | 53.5 | **+1.8** |
| Old Procedure (60 branches) | 50.2 | 50.1 | **-0.1** |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.0 points** |

### **3. Statistical Confidence**
- **p-value: 0.001** — highly unlikely to occur by chance
- **95% Confidence Interval: 0.8 to 3.1 points** — the true effect is almost certainly positive
- **Effect size: moderate** (Cohen's d = 0.56) — meaningful in practical terms

### **4. Why We Trust This Result**
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before week 15, both groups tracked similarly, ruling out pre-existing differences as an explanation
- **Large sample:** 2,550 branch-week observations across 150 branches
- **Robust methods:** Analysis accounted for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week variation
- **Consistent effect:** The improvement was observed across most switched branches, not driven by outliers

### **5. Context**
- Average branch service score is approximately 50; a 2-point gain represents roughly **4% improvement**
- Control branches showed no change, confirming the improvement is tied to the procedure change rather than system-wide factors
