# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. Switched branches improved by approximately **2.3 points** relative to non-switched branches, but this result is not statistically significant at conventional levels (p ≈ 0.12). The benefit appears strongest in the first month after implementation and fades over time.

**Recommendation: Do not mandate system-wide rollout at this time.** Continue monitoring and gather additional evidence before making an expansion decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches studied | 34 total (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Switch date | Week 15 |
| Method | Difference-in-differences analysis |

### **Results Summary**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Non-switched branches | 52.7 | 54.5 | +1.7 |
| Switched branches | 50.0 | 54.0 | +4.0 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.3 points** |

### **What This Means**
- **Direction:** The effect is positive—switched branches improved more than non-switched branches
- **Magnitude:** A 2.3-point gain represents about 4% of the average service score
- **Confidence:** We cannot rule out that this result occurred by chance (p ≈ 0.12)
- **Pattern:** The effect was strongest in weeks 1–4 after the switch (+7 to +9 points) but faded in later weeks

### **Validity Checks Passed**
✓ Pre-switch trends were similar between groups (valid comparison)  
✓ Results hold when controlling for staffing and visitor volume  
✓ Results hold when accounting for individual branch differences  

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Immediate Actions**

1. **Pause system-wide rollout.** The evidence does not support a confident recommendation to expand the procedure to all 34 branches.

2. **Extend monitoring period.** Continue tracking service scores for switched branches for 8–12 additional weeks to determine whether effects stabilize, fade further, or reverse.

3. **Gather qualitative feedback.** Interview staff at switched branches to understand:
   - What is working well with the new procedure
   - What challenges have emerged over time
   - Whether initial enthusiasm has affected results

4. **Consider optional adoption.** Allow individual branches to adopt the new procedure at their discretion while maintaining a control group for ongoing evaluation.

### **Decision Timeline**

| Timeframe | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Next 30 days | Conduct staff interviews; extend data collection |
| 60–90 days | Re-analyze with extended data; assess effect sustainability |
| 90+ days | Board decision on expansion based on updated evidence |

## **Technical Appendix**

*For board members seeking methodological detail:*

- **Analysis method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
- **Treatment effect estimate:** +2.26 points (95% CI: approximately -0.6 to +5.1)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.12 (not significant at α = 0.05)
- **Parallel trends test:** Pre-treatment interaction term p = 0.82 (assumption satisfied)
- **Software:** Python with statsmodels library
