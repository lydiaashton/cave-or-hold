# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2 points (4% relative improvement)**. The evidence is **positive but not definitive**—we recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting Week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Analysis compares service score changes between the two groups, isolating the effect of the new procedure

### **Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 50.89 | 50.90 | +0.01 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 49.69 | 51.72 | +2.03 |
| **Estimated Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.02 points** |

### **What This Means**

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| **+2 point improvement** | Meaningful gain on a ~50-point scale; represents ~4% relative improvement |
| **p-value = 0.11** | Suggestive but not conclusive evidence; 11% chance this result could occur by random variation |
| **95% confidence interval: -0.5 to +4.8** | True effect could range from slightly negative to nearly 5 points positive |
| **Pre-trends validated** | Treatment and control branches were on similar trajectories before the switch, supporting the validity of our comparison |

### **Weekly Pattern**

Before Week 15, treatment branches scored 1–2 points below control branches on average. After switching to the new procedure, treatment branches closed this gap and began outperforming control branches in most weeks. This pattern is consistent with a genuine positive effect.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased rollout** to remaining branches, implemented in stages (e.g., 5–10 branches per month) with ongoing monitoring.

---

## **Next Steps**

### **Immediate Actions**

1. **Begin phased implementation** at remaining 14 branches over the next 2–3 months
2. **Document implementation learnings** from the 20 early-adopter branches to support training
3. **Establish weekly monitoring** of service scores during rollout

### **Review Checkpoint (8–12 weeks post-rollout)**

The Board should reconvene to assess:
- Whether the 2-point improvement is sustained at full scale
- Whether implementation costs justify the benefit
- Whether any branches require additional support

### **Questions for Board Consideration**

- What level of service improvement justifies the investment?
- Are there operational costs (staff time, training) not captured in this analysis?
- Should we set a minimum threshold (e.g., +1.5 points) to continue the program?

---

## **Technical Notes**

Analysis uses a difference-in-differences design with branch fixed effects and cluster-robust standard errors. Full statistical output and data are available upon request.
