# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Pilot Results on New Branch Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new procedure showed **higher service scores** than those that did not, with an estimated improvement of **+1.9 points**. However, this result carries statistical uncertainty. We recommend **proceeding with a phased rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Measured**
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 continued with the old
- **17 weeks** of data collected (weeks 6–22), with the change implemented in week 15
- Service scores average around 50 system-wide; higher scores indicate better service

### **Results**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 53.0 | 57.9 | +4.9 |
| Branches that didn't switch | 48.5 | 51.5 | +3.1 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

- The new procedure is associated with a **1.9-point improvement** in service scores
- This represents approximately a **4% gain** relative to the system average
- The effect is **positive but imprecisely estimated** due to the limited number of branches and weeks in the pilot

### **Why We Can Trust This Analysis**
- Both groups showed **similar trends before the switch**, validating our comparison
- The analysis accounts for inherent differences between branches (some consistently score higher or lower)
- Both groups improved after week 15, suggesting favorable external conditions (e.g., seasonal factors) affected all branches

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased rollout**, with the following conditions:

1. **Expand gradually**: Roll out to an additional 10–15 branches over the next quarter
2. **Monitor closely**: Track service scores monthly for all branches post-rollout
3. **Set a review date**: Reconvene in 6 months to assess whether the improvement persists at scale
4. **Track costs**: Ensure any staffing or operational cost changes are documented alongside service improvements

---

## **Limitations to Consider**

- The pilot included only **20 branches**, limiting statistical precision
- **8 weeks of post-switch data** is relatively short; longer-term effects remain unknown
- Service scores are one metric; patron satisfaction and operational efficiency should also be evaluated

---

## **Appendix: Technical Summary**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimation method | Difference-in-Differences with branch fixed effects |
| Effect size | +1.86 service score points |
| Standard error | 1.38 (clustered at branch level) |
| Pre-trend test | Pass (no significant difference in trends before switch) |
