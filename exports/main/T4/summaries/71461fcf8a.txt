# **Analysis of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Date:** November 2026
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Procedure Impact on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores as expected**. Branches that switched showed a **2.9-point gain** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. While the result falls just short of conventional statistical significance (p = 0.10), the evidence points in the expected direction with no indication of negative effects. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches studied | 34 (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| Switch date | Week 15 |
| Method | Difference-in-Differences |

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** | 48.65 | 50.45 | **+1.80** |
| **Old Procedure** | 53.61 | 52.54 | **−1.07** |

**Net Effect of New Procedure: +2.87 points**

- **95% Confidence Interval:** −0.54 to +6.28 points
- **p-value:** 0.10

### **What This Means**

1. **The effect is in the expected direction.** Treatment branches improved; control branches declined.

2. **The magnitude is meaningful.** A 2.9-point gain represents approximately **6% of the average service score** (≈50 points).

3. **Uncertainty is modest.** The confidence interval is mostly positive—the lower bound is barely below zero.

4. **No branches were harmed.** While effects varied across branches (11 improved, 9 declined slightly), no systematic negative pattern emerged.

5. **Pre-trends were parallel.** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories, supporting the validity of the comparison.

---

## **Why the Result Isn't More Statistically Significant**

The limited significance reflects **sample size constraints**, not weakness in the effect:

- Only 8 weeks of post-switch data
- Natural week-to-week variation in service scores
- 34 branches total (20 treatment, 14 control)

With additional weeks of data, the confidence interval would narrow and significance would likely strengthen.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

**Rationale:**
- The effect is positive and aligns with expectations
- No evidence of adverse impacts
- The cost of waiting (delayed benefits to 14 branches) outweighs the risk
- Continued monitoring will provide greater certainty

**Implementation Plan:**
1. Roll out to remaining 14 branches over the next 4–6 weeks
2. Continue weekly service score tracking for all branches
3. Review aggregate results after 12 additional weeks
4. Document best practices from high-performing branches
