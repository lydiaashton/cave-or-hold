# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a meaningful 4% improvement. The effect is statistically robust and appears to strengthen over time. **We recommend proceeding with a full system-wide rollout.**

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**Study Scope**
- 709 branches across 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- 425 branches adopted the new procedure; 284 retained the old procedure
- Switch occurred at week 15

**Results**

| Metric | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +1.94 points (95% CI: 1.40 to 2.47) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Practical Impact** | ~4% improvement over average scores |
| **Trend** | Effect growing over time (3.6 pts in week 15 → 5.2 pts by week 22) |

**Score Changes by Group**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 50.48 | 53.56 | +3.08 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 48.10 | 49.25 | +1.14 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.94** |

---

### **WHY THIS MATTERS**

1. **Clear causal evidence:** The difference-in-differences design isolates the procedure's effect from other factors (seasonal trends, staffing changes, visitor volume).

2. **Consistent benefits:** The positive effect holds across all 425 switched branches.

3. **Learning curve:** The growing effect over time suggests branches improve with experience—indicating potential for even greater gains with proper training and support.

---

### **RECOMMENDATIONS**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Approve system-wide rollout** to all remaining branches |
| **2** | **Develop training materials** to accelerate the learning curve |
| **3** | **Monitor outcomes** quarterly to track sustained impact |
| **4** | **Document best practices** from high-performing switched branches |

---

### **CONCLUSION**

The data provide strong evidence that the new rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. The board can proceed with confidence that a full rollout will deliver measurable benefits across the library cooperative.
