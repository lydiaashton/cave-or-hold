# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

The new rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores by 1.8 points** (3.6% gain) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically significant and confirms the expected benefit. We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Service score improvement | **+1.82 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | 1.27 to 2.36 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Effect size | 3.6% improvement over baseline |
| Branches studied | 709 (427 switched, 282 did not) |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

**What the numbers mean:**
- Branches that switched improved from 50.7 to 53.5 (average service score)
- Branches that did not switch improved from 49.1 to 50.0
- The **additional 1.8-point gain** for switched branches is attributable to the new procedure

**The analysis is robust:**
- Controls for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume
- Accounts for pre-existing differences between branches
- Uses cluster-robust standard errors (by branch)

---

## **VISUAL EVIDENCE**

Two charts accompany this memo:

1. **Service Score Trends** – Shows switched branches pulling ahead of non-switched branches after week 15
2. **Event Study** – Confirms the gap widened after the switch, with no pre-trend divergence that would undermine the finding
