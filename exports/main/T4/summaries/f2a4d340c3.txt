# **Board Summary: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new stock rotation procedure showed **higher service scores** after implementation compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The observed improvement is approximately **+1.8 points** on the service score index. However, **this analysis cannot definitively prove the new procedure caused the improvement** because branches that switched were already improving faster than other branches before the change. We recommend **additional evaluation before system-wide rollout**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Performance Difference After Switch**
| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.69 | 52.45 | +1.76 |
| Non-switched branches | 48.96 | 48.89 | -0.07 |

The gap between groups widened from **1.7 points before** the switch to **3.6 points after**.

### **2. Statistical Estimate**
Using difference-in-differences analysis (comparing changes in switched vs. non-switched branches):
- **Estimated treatment effect: +1.8 points** (p < 0.001)
- Result is stable when controlling for staffing levels and visitor traffic

### **3. Critical Limitation: Pre-Existing Trends**
Branches that switched were **already on a different trajectory** before adoption:

| Period | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Before switch (weeks 6-14) | +0.16 points/week | -0.07 points/week |
| After switch (weeks 15-22) | +0.39 points/week | +0.12 points/week |

**Implication:** The 1.8-point estimate likely **overstates** the true effect of the new procedure because switched branches were already improving.

### **4. Study Scope**
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6-22)
- **59.9%** of branch-weeks adopted the new procedure
- Switch implemented at **week 15**

---

## **Recommendations & Next Steps**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Pause system-wide rollout** | Current evidence is suggestive but not conclusive |
| **2** | **Continue monitoring** both groups for 8-12 more weeks | More data will improve estimate precision |
| **3** | **Investigate branch characteristics** | Determine if switched branches differ systematically (size, location, resources) |
| **4** | **Consider randomized rollout** for remaining branches | Enables stronger causal inference |
| **5** | **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** | Even a smaller effect may justify implementation if costs are low |

---

## **Decision Guidance**

| If the Board... | Then... |
|-----------------|---------|
| Wants **high confidence** before rollout | Wait for additional data and/or randomized evaluation |
| Is comfortable with **moderate uncertainty** | Proceed with phased rollout while continuing evaluation |
| Faces **low implementation costs** | The potential benefit (+1.8 points or less) may justify rollout |
| Faces **significant costs** | Require stronger evidence before committing resources |
