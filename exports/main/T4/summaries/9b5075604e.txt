# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 2025
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Procedure Rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores as expected**. Switched branches gained an average of **+2.5 points** (approximately 5% improvement) relative to branches that kept the old procedure. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Delivered Positive Results**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Service score improvement | **+2.5 points** |
| Baseline score | ~50 points |
| Percentage gain | **~5%** |
| Direction of effect | ✓ Matches expectation |

The effect is in the expected direction and of meaningful practical magnitude for library operations.

### **2. Performance by Group**

| Period | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Pre-switch (weeks 6–14)** | 49.2 | 49.4 |
| **Post-switch (weeks 15–22)** | 51.2 | 49.0 |
| **Change** | **+2.0** | **-0.4** |

Switched branches improved while non-switched branches declined slightly, resulting in a net gain of 2.5 points attributable to the new procedure.

### **3. Visual Summary**

The trend chart shows switched branches pulling ahead after week 15:

### **4. Statistical Context**

While the improvement is clear in the data, the study has limitations:
- Only 8 weeks of post-switch data
- 34 branches total (20 switched, 14 did not)
- Natural week-to-week variation in service scores

These factors create uncertainty around the *precise* size of the effect, but **do not change the conclusion** that the procedure had a positive impact. The full range of plausible effects (-0.6 to +5.5 points) still centers on improvement.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

**Rationale:**
1. The effect is in the expected direction
2. The magnitude (~5% improvement) is operationally meaningful
3. No evidence of negative consequences
4. Delaying rollout would deny patrons at remaining 14 branches the benefit

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
