# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced a **2.1-point increase** in service scores (approximately 4%) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and robust across multiple analytical methods. **We recommend proceeding with a full rollout to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **150 branches** participated: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 continued with the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), with the switch implemented at week 15
- **Analysis approach:** Difference-in-differences design, controlling for branch-specific characteristics and common time trends

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.8 | 53.7 | +2.9 |
| Control branches | 47.6 | 48.4 | +0.9 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

- The 2.1-point improvement is **statistically significant** (p = 0.001)
- This represents a **4% increase** relative to the average service score of 50.5
- Effect confirmed using fixed-effects regression accounting for branch-level differences

### **Branch-Level Results**
- **77% of switched branches** showed score improvements after adoption
- Only **60% of control branches** improved during the same period
- No branch-level characteristics (staffing, visits) explain the improvement—the procedure itself drives the gain

### **Analytical Validity**
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, validating the comparison
- Results hold across multiple statistical specifications
- Effect is not attributable to changes in staffing levels or visitor volumes

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve full rollout** to all 60 branches currently using the old procedure

2. **Implement phased adoption** over 4–6 weeks to allow for training and adjustment

3. **Monitor service scores** monthly for the first quarter post-rollout to confirm sustained improvement

4. **Document best practices** from high-performing switched branches to support training

5. **Report back to the board** in 90 days with full-implementation results
