# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Impact of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

**Date:** [Current Date]

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (4% improvement) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This effect is statistically robust and consistent across all analytical tests. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Service Score Impact

| Group | Branches | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|----------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment (switched) | 90 | 50.8 | 53.7 | +3.0 |
| Control (no switch) | 60 | 47.6 | 48.4 | +0.9 |
| **Net Effect** | — | — | — | **+2.1** |

### Statistical Confidence

- **Highly significant result** (p < 0.001)
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.4 to 2.9 points
- Effect remains significant after controlling for branch characteristics, weekly variations, staffing levels, and visitor volume

### Analysis Validity

- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, confirming a valid comparison
- The improvement occurred specifically after week 15 when the switch was implemented
- Effect is consistent across early and late post-switch periods

### Practical Significance

- Represents approximately **0.22 standard deviations** improvement
- Equivalent to a **4% gain** relative to the baseline service score of ~50
- Both staffing and visitor volume positively affect scores, but the procedure effect is independent of these factors

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all 60 branches currently using the old method

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with phased deployment over 4-6 weeks to ensure adequate training and support

3. **Establish monitoring protocol** to track service scores for 8-12 weeks post-rollout and confirm effects persist at scale

4. **Update operations manuals** to incorporate the new procedure as standard best practice

5. **Conduct cost-benefit review** to document any resource implications against the demonstrated service quality gains

---

## **Appendix: Methodology**

**Data:** 2,550 weekly observations from 150 branches over 17 weeks (weeks 6-22). The procedure switch occurred at week 15.

**Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects, controlling for staffing index and visits index.
