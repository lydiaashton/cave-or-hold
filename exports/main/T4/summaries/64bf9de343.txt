# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (about 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant and robust to alternative explanations.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Worked
| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched Branches (425) | 50.5 | 52.3 | +1.8 |
| Control Branches (284) | 49.6 | 49.2 | −0.4 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.1** |

The 2.1-point improvement is statistically significant (p < 0.001) with a 95% confidence interval of 1.5 to 2.6 points.

### The Effect Is Not Driven by Other Factors
The improvement remains after accounting for:
- Weekly staffing levels
- Visitor volume
- Seasonal variation across the 17-week study period

This indicates the procedure change itself—not staffing or traffic patterns—drove the service score gains.

### The Benefit Grew Over Time
The gap between switched and control branches widened in the weeks following implementation:
- **Weeks 15–17:** Gap averaged +2.3 points
- **Weeks 18–22:** Gap averaged +3.5 points

This suggests staff became more proficient with the new procedure over time, and benefits may continue to accrue.

### One Important Consideration
Branches that volunteered to switch were already performing slightly better before the change (about 0.9 points higher on average). This suggests motivated or better-resourced branches may have self-selected into the pilot. However, the post-switch improvement (+2.1 points net) substantially exceeds this pre-existing gap, indicating the procedure itself added meaningful value.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.** The evidence supports a positive impact on service quality, and there is no indication of adverse effects.

## **Appendix: Analysis Details**

- **Data:** 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches (weeks 6–22)
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Switch date:** Week 15
- **Service score scale:** Average branch scores ~50; higher is better
