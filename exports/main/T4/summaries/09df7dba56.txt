# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new stock rotation and shelving procedure experienced a statistically significant increase of **2.4 points** (nearly **5%**) in their service scores compared to branches that kept the old procedure.

**Recommendation:** Approve rollout of the new procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.4 points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | 1.1 to 3.7 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.0003 |
| **Branches studied** | 150 (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| **Data period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

### What We Found

- **Service scores increased** at switched branches relative to non-switched branches after week 15
- The improvement is **unlikely to be due to chance** (less than 1 in 1,000 probability)
- The result **holds after accounting for** staffing levels and branch visit volumes
- **Pre-switch trends were parallel**, confirming the comparison is valid

### Context

- Average service score across all branches: ~50 points
- The 2.4-point gain represents meaningful improvement in service quality
- No evidence the new procedure harmed performance at any branch
