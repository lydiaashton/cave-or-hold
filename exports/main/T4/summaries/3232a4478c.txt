# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **New Shelving Procedure Improves Service Quality: Recommendation for System-Wide Rollout**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Approve full rollout of the new procedure.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.5 to 2.0 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure—a statistically significant improvement of approximately 3-4%. The effect is consistent, robust, and represents meaningful service quality gains without additional resource requirements.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. The New Procedure Works**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 51.2 | 52.5 | **+1.3** |
| Non-Switched Branches | 50.3 | 49.6 | **-0.7** |

**Net Effect: +2.0 points** (p < 0.001)

Branches using the new procedure not only improved—they also avoided the slight decline experienced by branches keeping the old procedure.

### **2. Results Are Consistent and Reliable**

- **All 8 post-switch weeks** show treatment branches outperforming control branches (by 2.3 to 3.8 points per week)
- **Conservative estimate** (using only weeks 10-14 as baseline): **+1.46 points** (p < 0.001)
- Effect **holds after controlling** for staffing levels and visitor volume
- No evidence the effect fades over the observation period

### **3. Practical Impact**

- Baseline average service score: **~51 points**
- Expected improvement from rollout: **~1.5-2.0 points** (3-4% gain)
- This is a **process improvement**, not a resource-intensive change

### **4. Important Context**

- 419 branches switched to the new procedure; 290 did not
- Data covers 17 weeks (weeks 6-22); switch occurred at week 15
- Some pre-existing differences between groups were observed, which is why we report both the full-sample and conservative estimates

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new rotation and shelving procedure.**

The evidence indicates this change will improve service quality across the cooperative with minimal cost or disruption.
