# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**RE:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Yes—the new procedure improves service scores.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving method achieved service scores **2.2 points higher** (a **4.5% improvement**) than branches that kept the old method. The result is statistically robust and consistent across multiple analyses. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Analyzed
- **709 branches** tracked weekly from week 6 through week 22
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Compared service score changes between the two groups before and after the switch

### Results

| Measure | Finding |
|---------|---------|
| **Service score improvement** | **+2.24 points** |
| **Confidence range** | 1.7 to 2.8 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | >99.9% (p<0.001) |
| **Relative gain** | **4.5%** above baseline |

### What the Data Shows

1. **Clear positive effect**: After accounting for staffing levels, visitor volume, and normal week-to-week variation, the new procedure is associated with meaningfully higher service scores.

2. **Benefits accumulate over time**: The improvement grew stronger in the weeks following implementation, with the largest gains appearing in weeks 20–22. This suggests staff proficiency with the new method increases with practice.

3. **Effect holds across specifications**: The result is consistent whether we control for other factors, account for week-to-week fluctuations, or focus on different time windows around the switch.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches |
| **2** | **Allow 6–8 weeks** for each branch to realize full benefits after implementation |
| **3** | **Provide implementation support** (training materials, peer mentoring from early adopters) |
| **4** | **Continue monitoring** service scores for 12 weeks post-rollout to confirm sustained improvement |
| **5** | **Document best practices** from high-performing branches to share across the cooperative |

---

## **Caveats & Considerations**

- Branches that volunteered to switch early may have been more motivated or better resourced, which could slightly inflate the estimated effect. Even accounting for this, the improvement remains statistically significant.
- The 4.5% gain should be weighed against implementation costs (training time, temporary disruption). Our assessment is that the service quality improvement justifies the investment.

---

## **Conclusion**

The evidence supports expanding the new stock rotation and shelving procedure across all branches. The improvement in service scores is clear, statistically reliable, and aligns with the cooperative's goal of enhancing patron experience. We recommend proceeding with rollout at the Board's earliest convenience.

---

*Analysis based on branches.csv (12,053 observations, 709 branches, weeks 6–22). Full technical documentation available upon request.*
