# **BOARD SUMMARY: New Shelving & Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2 points (4%)**, which is in the expected direction. However, with the current data, we cannot rule out that this result occurred by chance. The board should decide whether a **4% improvement—if real—justifies the cost of system-wide implementation**, or whether **additional data collection** is warranted before committing.

---

## **What the Data Shows**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +1.9 points on service score index |
| **Percentage gain** | ~3.8% |
| **Statistical confidence** | 85% confident the effect is positive |
| **Range of plausible effects** | -0.7 to +4.5 points |

**Branch Performance Summary:**
- **20 switched branches:** Scores rose from 49.9 to 52.0 (+2.1 points)
- **14 control branches:** Scores remained flat, 49.2 to 49.3 (+0.2 points)

The switched branches pulled ahead after week 15, while control branches showed no meaningful change. This pattern supports a genuine treatment effect, even though statistical significance falls just short of the conventional threshold.

---

## **What This Means**

| Scenario | Likelihood | Implication |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| **Real positive effect (~2–4 points)** | Most likely | Procedure delivers expected benefits |
| **No effect (0 points)** | Possible | Observed gain was random variation |
| **Negative effect** | Unlikely | Procedure may be counterproductive |

The analysis found **no evidence of harm**, and the weight of evidence points toward a **modest benefit**.

---

## **Recommendation**

We recommend the board consider one of two paths:

### **Option A: Proceed with Phased Rollout**
- Begin implementing the new procedure at remaining branches over the next 2–3 quarters
- Continue monitoring service scores during rollout
- **Rationale:** The observed improvement aligns with expectations, and early adopters show no negative effects

### **Option B: Extend Pilot Before Full Rollout**
- Keep current arrangement for 8–12 additional weeks
- Add 5–10 more branches to the treatment group
- Reconvene with updated analysis in Q1 2027
- **Rationale:** Additional data would provide greater certainty before system-wide commitment

---

## **Key Considerations for the Board**

1. **Cost of implementation** — If rollout costs are low, Option A (phased rollout) carries limited risk
2. **Opportunity cost of delay** — If the procedure genuinely helps, postponing denies branches those benefits
3. **Strategic priorities** — Does a 4% service score improvement align with cooperative goals?
