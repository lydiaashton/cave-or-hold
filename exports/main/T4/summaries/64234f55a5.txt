# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure gained approximately **2.2 points** on the service score index compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** over baseline performance. The effect is statistically reliable and appears to strengthen over time.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** to all branches, with continued monitoring to ensure consistent implementation and track long-term benefits.

---

### **Key Findings**

**1. Study Overview**
- **709 branches** participated: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 continued with the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22); the switch occurred in week 15
- Service scores average around **50 points** (higher is better)

**2. Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 50.45 | 50.85 | +0.40 |
| Branches adopting new procedure | 51.40 | 53.99 | +2.59 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.19** |

- The **2.2-point improvement** is statistically significant (95% confidence interval: 1.7 to 2.8 points)
- Benefits appear to **accumulate over time**, with larger effects visible in weeks 19–22

**3. Important Context**

Branches that volunteered to switch showed stronger performance trends even before the change. This suggests:
- Motivated or better-resourced branches may have been more likely to adopt early
- The **true effect may be modestly smaller** than 2.2 points, but remains positive and meaningful
