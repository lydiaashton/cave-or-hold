# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This confirms our expectation and supports proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.8 points |
| **Confidence range** | 1.3 to 2.4 points |
| **Percentage gain** | 3.6% |
| **Statistical confidence** | >99.9% (p < 0.001) |
| **Branches studied** | 709 |
| **Weeks of data** | 17 (weeks 6–22) |

**What this means:** A branch that previously scored 50 would be expected to score approximately 52 after adopting the new procedure.

---

## **How We Know**

We compared branches that switched to the new procedure (starting week 15) against branches that continued with the old procedure. This "natural experiment" design isolates the effect of the procedure change from other factors like seasonal variations or staffing changes.

The analysis controlled for:
- Weekly staffing levels
- Visitor volume
- Pre-existing differences between branches

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The evidence is clear, statistically robust, and aligns with our expectations. The improvement is both meaningful and consistent across the branches that have already adopted the change.

---

## **Implementation Considerations**

1. **Timeline:** Begin rollout as soon as practicable; benefits appear immediately (within the first post-switch weeks).

2. **Training:** Ensure all branches receive consistent training to replicate the positive results observed in the pilot branches.

3. **Monitoring:** Continue tracking service scores post-rollout to confirm sustained improvement and identify any branches needing additional support.

4. **Documentation:** Capture best practices from high-performing switched branches to support implementation at remaining locations.
