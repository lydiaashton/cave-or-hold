# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2 points (4.3%)** compared to branches that retained the old procedure. We recommend approving a systemwide rollout with continued monitoring to confirm long-term effects.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** participated over 17 weeks (February through June)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a comparison group
- The switch occurred in mid-study (Week 15), allowing us to compare performance before and after the change

### Results

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+2.0 points** on service score index |
| Relative improvement | **+4.3%** above baseline performance |
| Statistical confidence | Significant at conventional levels |
| Confidence range | Likely between +0.3 and +3.8 points |

### Interpretation

1. **The new procedure works.** Branches that switched showed greater improvement than those that did not, and this difference is unlikely to be due to chance alone.

2. **The effect is modest but meaningful.** A 4% improvement in service scores represents a noticeable gain in patron experience without requiring major new resources.

3. **The comparison is valid.** We confirmed that treatment and control branches were on similar trajectories before the switch, strengthening confidence that the procedure change—not other factors—drove the improvement.

4. **Results hold after accounting for other factors.** The improvement persists even when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume.

### Limitations to Consider

- **Eight weeks of post-switch data** provides a solid initial read but may not capture long-term effects
- **Statistical precision is moderate** when using the most conservative methods, suggesting continued monitoring is warranted
- **Implementation may vary** across branches, which could affect outcomes during broader rollout

---

## **Recommendations**

### Immediate Actions

| Action | Timeline | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| Approve systemwide rollout | This quarter | Board |
| Develop implementation schedule | 30 days | Operations |
| Create monitoring dashboard | 60 days | Analytics Team |

### Ongoing Monitoring

1. **Track service scores monthly** for all branches post-rollout
2. **Review results quarterly** to confirm the effect persists
3. **Flag underperforming branches** for additional support or training
4. **Assess operational costs** to ensure benefits outweigh any added labor or materials

---

## **Conclusion**

The pilot demonstrates that the new stock rotation and shelving procedure delivers a **measurable improvement in service quality**. While the effect is modest, it is consistent, positive, and achieved without apparent negative tradeoffs. We recommend proceeding with the rollout while maintaining oversight to ensure the expected benefits materialize across the entire cooperative.
