# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improves service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by approximately **1.9 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and consistent across multiple analytical approaches. 

**Recommendation:** Roll out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **New procedure** (425 branches) | 50.24 | 51.39 | **+1.14** |
| **Old procedure** (284 branches) | 49.86 | 49.12 | **-0.75** |

**Net Effect: +1.89 points** (p < 0.001)

While branches keeping the old procedure saw scores decline slightly, branches with the new procedure improved. The net difference of 1.9 points represents roughly a **4% improvement** relative to the average service score of 50.

### **2. Results Are Robust**

The 1.9-point improvement holds across multiple analytical approaches:

| Model Specification | Effect Size | Significance |
|---------------------|-------------|--------------|
| Simple comparison | +1.89 | p < 0.001 |
| Branch fixed effects | +1.89 | p < 0.001 |
| Branch + week fixed effects | +1.89 | p < 0.001 |
| With staffing/visits controls | +1.88 | p < 0.001 |

### **3. Valid Comparison Between Groups**

In the 9 weeks before the switch, treatment and control branches had similar trends (average difference: 0.38 points). After the switch, the gap widened to 2.27 points—supporting a genuine treatment effect rather than pre-existing differences.

### **4. Study Scope**

- **709 total branches** (425 treatment, 284 control)
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6-22)
- **Switch implemented at week 15**

## **Next Steps**

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with support for training and transition.

3. **Monitor service scores** post-rollout to confirm sustained improvement.

4. **Document best practices** from early-adopter branches to inform training materials.
