# **Board Summary: New Branch Procedure Impact Assessment**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 2026
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving & Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted it saw service scores improve by **2 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. The evidence is clear and statistically robust. **We recommend immediate rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +2.04 points |
| **Confidence range** | 1.5 to 2.6 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | >99.9% (p < 0.001) |
| **Branches studied** | 709 |
| **Observations** | 12,053 branch-weeks |

### The Numbers in Context

- **Switched branches** held steady at ~52.6 points after adopting the new procedure
- **Non-switched branches** dropped from 51.0 to 49.1 points over the same period
- The new procedure **prevented a decline** and produced a net gain

This 2-point improvement represents a **meaningful gain** on a scale where the average branch scores around 50.

---

## **Why We're Confident**

1. **Strong research design**: We compared switched vs. non-switched branches before and after the change, isolating the procedure's effect from other factors.

2. **Results held up under scrutiny**: The 2-point gain remained consistent when we:
   - Controlled for staffing levels
   - Controlled for visitor volume
   - Used conservative statistical methods

3. **Large, representative sample**: 709 branches over 17 weeks provides robust evidence.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

The data confirms what we expected: the new shelving and stock rotation procedure improves service quality. Delaying rollout means keeping branches on an inferior system.
