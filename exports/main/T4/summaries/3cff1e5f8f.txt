# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved significantly higher service scores than those that kept the old method. The improvement is statistically robust, meaningful in practical terms, and grows over time. **We recommend immediate rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### The New Procedure Improved Service Scores by ~1.6 Points (3.2%)

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (425) | 50.24 | 51.39 | +1.14 |
| Control branches (284) | 49.86 | 49.12 | −0.75 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+1.89** |

After controlling for branch-specific factors and weekly variations, the estimated improvement is **1.60 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.33 to 2.45 points).

### Results Are Statistically Robust

The finding holds across multiple analytical methods, all with p < 0.001:
- Simple difference-in-differences: +1.89 points
- With branch fixed effects: +1.59 points
- With branch and week fixed effects: +1.61 points
- Full model (controlling for staffing and visits): +1.60 points

### Benefits Strengthen Over Time

| Period | Treatment Effect |
|--------|------------------|
| Weeks 15-18 (early) | ~1.9 points |
| Weeks 19-22 (late) | ~2.7 points |

This pattern suggests staff require several weeks to fully realize the procedure's benefits.

### Lower-Performing Branches Benefit Most

| Baseline Performance | Average Improvement |
|---------------------|---------------------|
| Below median | +1.58 points |
| Above median | +0.72 points |

The new procedure both improves scores and prevents the decline observed in control branches.

### No Pre-Existing Differences

Analysis of weeks 6-14 (before the switch) confirms that switched and control branches followed parallel trends prior to adoption, supporting a causal interpretation of the results.

## **DATA AND METHODOLOGY**

- **Dataset:** 12,053 branch-week observations across 709 branches
- **Study period:** Weeks 6-22 (17 weeks); procedure switch occurred at week 15
- **Treatment group:** 425 branches (60%) adopted the new procedure
- **Control group:** 284 branches (40%) retained the old procedure
- **Analysis:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors at the branch level

---

**Recommendation:** Proceed with full rollout. The evidence is clear, the effect is meaningful, and delaying adoption means foregoing measurable service improvements for our patrons.
