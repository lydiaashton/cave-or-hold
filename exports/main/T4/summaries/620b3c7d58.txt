# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved a **statistically significant improvement of 1.8 points** in weekly service scores—a **3.4% gain** over baseline performance. The effect is consistent, robust, and observed across all eight weeks following implementation.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.81 points (95% CI: 1.45 to 2.18) |
| **Statistical confidence** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Relative improvement** | 3.4% above baseline |
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 treatment, 284 control) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Effect persistence** | Positive in all 8 post-switch weeks |

**Effect by Post-Switch Week:**
- Week 15: +1.6 pts | Week 16: +1.0 pts | Week 17: +1.9 pts | Week 18: +1.9 pts
- Week 19: +1.2 pts | Week 20: +2.0 pts | Week 21: +2.6 pts | Week 22: +2.3 pts

The improvement held after controlling for staffing levels, visitor volume, and inherent branch characteristics.

---

### **IMPORTANT CAVEAT**

Branches that volunteered for the new procedure showed slightly stronger performance trends even before the switch. This suggests some **selection bias**—early adopters may have been more motivated or better managed. The true effect is likely **positive but possibly modest** relative to the 1.8-point estimate. The analytical model accounts for this through branch-level controls, but the board should temper expectations accordingly.

---

### **NEXT STEPS**

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| 1. Approve system-wide rollout | Immediate |
| 2. Develop implementation plan with training | 2–4 weeks |
| 3. Execute phased rollout across all branches | 4–6 weeks |
| 4. Monitor service scores post-rollout | Ongoing |
| 5. Re-evaluate impact with full data | 3 months |

---

### **CONCLUSION**

The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure cooperative-wide. Expected gains are meaningful, statistically reliable, and show no signs of fading. With proper implementation support and continued monitoring, the board can confidently expect service score improvements across all 709 branches.
