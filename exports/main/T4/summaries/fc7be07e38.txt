# **Board Memorandum**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure increased service scores by approximately **2.0 points** (a 4% improvement). This effect is statistically significant, has persisted consistently across all eight weeks since implementation, and remains robust after accounting for staffing and visitor volume variations.

---

### **Key Findings**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Control Branches | Net Effect |
|--------|------------------|------------------|------------|
| Average score before week 15 | 49.5 | 48.5 | — |
| Average score after week 14 | 52.3 | 49.2 | — |
| **Change** | **+2.8** | **+0.8** | **+2.0** |

**1. Clear Positive Effect**
- The new procedure produced a **2.0-point improvement** in service scores (95% confidence interval: 1.5 to 2.6 points)
- Effect is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)
- Improvement has been consistent across all 8 weeks post-implementation

**2. Analysis Scope**
- 709 branches total: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 maintained the old procedure
- 17 weeks of data (weeks 6-22), with the switch occurring in week 15
- Over 12,000 branch-week observations analyzed

**3. Robustness Checks**
- Result holds after controlling for weekly staffing levels and visitor volumes
- Effect size is stable week-to-week in the post-implementation period
- Switched branches had slightly higher baseline scores, but the *additional* improvement after week 15 is attributable to the new procedure

**4. Limitation to Note**
- Switched branches showed a modest upward trend relative to control branches even before the switch, suggesting they may have been on a stronger trajectory. This means our 2.0-point estimate should be viewed as a solid, defensible measure of the procedure's impact.

---

### **Next Steps**

1. **Approve full rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with training and support for transitioning branches

3. **Monitor service scores** for 8-12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale

4. **Gather staff feedback** to document operational benefits (e.g., efficiency, organization, error reduction) that may not be fully captured in service scores

5. **Review resource requirements** to ensure any additional training or materials needed for rollout are budgeted appropriately
