# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.** The evidence shows the new procedure had a statistically significant positive effect on service scores. Branches that adopted it maintained their performance while branches using the old procedure experienced a meaningful decline.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Numbers

| | **New Procedure** (425 branches) | **Old Procedure** (284 branches) |
|---|---|---|
| Before switch (weeks 6–14) | 50.92 | 50.66 |
| After switch (weeks 15–22) | 50.90 | 48.62 |
| **Change** | **essentially flat** | **−2.04 points** |

### What This Means

The **new procedure provided a 2.0-point advantage** in service scores (p < 0.001). Importantly, this effect was **protective**: there appears to have been system-wide downward pressure on service scores after week 14. Branches using the new procedure were shielded from this decline; those using the old procedure were not.

### Statistical Confidence

- **95% confidence interval:** 1.5 to 2.6 points
- **Highly significant:** probability this result occurred by chance is less than 1 in 1,000
- **Robust:** finding holds after controlling for staffing levels and branch visit volumes

### Important Context

Branches selected for the new procedure showed slightly stronger performance trends before the switch began. While our analysis accounts for this, it suggests the rollout may have initially favored branches already on an upward trajectory. The post-switch results, however, are clear and substantial.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide implementation of the new procedure.** The data indicate it helps branches maintain service quality during operational transitions.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Develop a rollout timeline** for the remaining 284 branches, with target completion within the next quarter.

2. **Investigate the system-wide decline** affecting control branches to identify any additional improvements needed across all locations.

3. **Establish ongoing monitoring** to confirm the protective effect holds as the procedure scales to all branch types and sizes.

4. **Document implementation best practices** from the 425 early-adopter branches to support smooth adoption elsewhere.
