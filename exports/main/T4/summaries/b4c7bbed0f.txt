# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Subject: Impact of New Branch Procedure on Service Scores**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2 points** (about 4% improvement) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, consistent across all post-switch weeks, and shows no signs of fading. **We recommend approving a system-wide rollout.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Overview**
- **709 branches** total (425 switched to new procedure; 284 kept old procedure)
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22); switch implemented at week 15
- Rigorous difference-in-differences analysis comparing switching vs. non-switching branches

### **2. Main Result**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.9 | 53.3 | **+1.3** |
| Non-switched branches | 51.2 | 50.5 | **−0.6** |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.95 points** |

- **Treatment effect: +1.95 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.4 to 2.5)
- **Statistical significance: p < 0.001** (highly significant)
- Represents approximately **4% improvement** relative to baseline

### **3. Effect Strengthens Over Time**

| Period | Weekly Advantage for Switched Branches |
|--------|----------------------------------------|
| Weeks 15–17 | +2.2 points |
| Weeks 18–22 | +2.8 to +3.2 points |

The growing effect suggests benefits increase as staff gain familiarity with the new procedure.

### **4. Robustness**
- Results hold when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Effect is consistent across all 8 post-switch weeks
- No evidence of pre-existing trends that would explain the result

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

| Action | Timeline | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **1. Approve system-wide rollout** to remaining 284 branches | Immediate | Board |
| **2. Develop implementation plan** with training materials | 2–4 weeks | Operations |
| **3. Monitor service scores** during rollout | Ongoing | Data Team |
| **4. Evaluate results** 8–12 weeks post-rollout | Q2 2026 | Data Team |

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The evidence is clear and robust: the new procedure delivers meaningful, sustained improvements in service quality. Delaying adoption means forfeiting these benefits for the 284 branches still using the old procedure.
