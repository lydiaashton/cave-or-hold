# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

The new procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores**, but the evidence is **not yet strong enough** to justify immediate system-wide rollout. Switched branches improved by **1.6 points** relative to non-switched branches, but this result could reasonably be due to chance. We recommend **continuing the pilot while expanding to additional branches** to build a more definitive evidence base before committing to full implementation.

---

## Key Findings

### The Numbers

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+1.6 points** on service score index |
| Statistical confidence | **Not significant** (p = 0.11) |
| 95% confidence range | **-0.4 to +3.6 points** |
| Effect size | **3% of average score** |

### What This Means

The data points in the right direction—branches that adopted the new procedure did better than those that didn't. However, with only 8 weeks of post-switch data across 34 branches, we cannot be confident the improvement wasn't due to random variation. The true effect could be anywhere from slightly negative to meaningfully positive.

### Weekly Performance After Switch

| Weeks | Pattern |
|-------|---------|
| 15, 21-22 | Switched branches outperformed |
| 16-20 | Switched branches underperformed |

This inconsistency suggests the effect may still be stabilizing as branches adjust to the new procedure.

---

## Recommendation

**Do not halt the change, but do not rush to full rollout either.**

We propose a **phased expansion**:

1. **Continue monitoring** the 20 switched branches for 8-12 additional weeks
2. **Invite 5-7 additional branches** to adopt the procedure quarterly
3. **Reconvene this analysis** when we have 6+ months of post-switch data
4. **Make full rollout decision** once confidence intervals narrow sufficiently

This approach balances optimism about the positive signal with prudent stewardship of cooperative resources.

---

## Questions the Board May Have

**Q: If we believed this would work, why isn't the evidence clearer?**  
A: Service scores naturally fluctuate week-to-week. Detecting a modest improvement requires more time and observations than we currently have.

**Q: Is there any risk in continuing the pilot?**  
A: Minimal. The 20 switched branches show no signs of harm, and the procedure appears operationally feasible.

**Q: What would change our recommendation?**  
A: Either (a) the effect becomes statistically significant with continued positive results, or (b) the effect diminishes or turns negative with additional data.
