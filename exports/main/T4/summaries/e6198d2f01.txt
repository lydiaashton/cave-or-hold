# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced service score improvements of **2.1 points** (approximately **4%**) compared to branches that maintained the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, has persisted across all eight weeks since implementation, and represents a meaningful gain in service quality.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measured Impact**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| 425 branches that switched | 50.7 | 54.0 | +3.3 |
| 284 branches that did not switch | 50.7 | 51.9 | +1.2 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

The new procedure is associated with a **2.1-point increase** in service scores (95% confidence interval: 1.6 to 2.7 points).

### **2. Statistical Confidence**

- The effect is **highly statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- Results remain robust when accounting for:
  - Branch-to-branch variation
  - Weekly staffing levels
  - Weekly visit volumes

### **3. Timing and Sustainability**

- **Before the switch (weeks 6-14):** Service score trends were comparable between both groups, supporting a valid comparison
- **After the switch (weeks 15-22):** Improvement appeared immediately and has been sustained throughout the observation period
- No evidence of diminishing returns; some indication the effect may strengthen over time

### **4. Practical Significance**

- System-wide average service score: **51.9**
- The 2.1-point gain represents a **4% improvement** relative to baseline
- Applied across 709 branches, this improvement would elevate service quality cooperative-wide
