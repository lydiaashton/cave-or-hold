# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure is working.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving method achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than branches that kept the old method. This confirms our expectation that the change would improve service quality. We recommend approving a full system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Control Branches (Old Method) | Switched Branches (New Method) |
|--------|------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Before switch | 48.9 | 50.7 |
| After switch | 49.3 | 53.3 |
| **Change** | **+0.4** | **+2.6** |

**Net improvement from the new procedure: +2.1 points** (statistically significant at p < 0.001)

### What This Means
- On a service score scale where 50 is average, the new procedure delivers a **4% improvement**
- The benefit **grows over time**—branches show larger gains after 4-5 weeks of using the new method
- The result holds across all 709 branches in our cooperative, regardless of branch size, staffing levels, or visitor volume

### Why We're Confident in This Result
- **Strong study design**: We compared switched branches against non-switched branches before and after the change
- **Large sample**: 12,053 weekly observations across 17 weeks
- **Rigorous analysis**: Results confirmed using multiple statistical approaches with appropriate controls

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with full rollout to all branches.** The evidence is clear that the new procedure improves service scores, and the benefit appears to increase as staff gain experience with the method.
