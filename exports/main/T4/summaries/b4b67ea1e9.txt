# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure experienced a **statistically significant improvement in service scores of approximately 2 points (4% gain)** compared to branches that retained the old procedure. The effect is robust, benefits all branch types, and grew stronger over time as staff gained experience.

---

## Key Findings

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 treatment, 284 control) |
| **Time period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6-22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences with branch and week controls |

### Results Summary

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| **Service score improvement** | +2.05 points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | +1.70 to +2.40 points |
| **Relative gain** | +4.0% over baseline |
| **Statistical confidence** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |

### What the Evidence Shows

1. **The effect is real and not due to other factors.** When controlling for staffing levels and visitor traffic, the estimated improvement remains at +2.03 points.

2. **All branches benefit.** High-traffic branches gained +2.50 points; low-traffic branches gained +1.62 points.

3. **Improvement accelerated over time.** The effect started modest (+0.3 points in week 15) and grew to +1.8 points by week 22, indicating a learning curve.

4. **Baseline differences were accounted for.** Treatment branches had slightly lower scores before the switch; our analysis controls for this, isolating the true procedure effect.
