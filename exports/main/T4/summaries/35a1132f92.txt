# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure showed **higher service scores** than branches that kept the old procedure. The estimated improvement is **2.4 points** (about 5% of the average score). While this finding is encouraging and aligns with our expectations, the result does not reach conventional statistical significance with the current data. We recommend **proceeding with a system-wide rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes, as the evidence points in a positive direction with no detectable downside.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Performance**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 48.3 | 47.6 | -0.7 |
| Branches using new procedure | 47.8 | 49.5 | +1.7 |
| **Net improvement attributable to new procedure** | | | **+2.4** |

### **What This Means**

- **Direction of effect:** Positive — switched branches improved while non-switched branches declined slightly
- **Magnitude:** 2.4 points on a ~50-point index (approximately 5% improvement)
- **Confidence level:** The data suggests improvement, though we cannot rule out some uncertainty (95% confidence interval ranges from -0.55 to +5.40 points)
- **Practical significance:** Even at the conservative end of the estimate, the new procedure shows no meaningful harm to service quality

### **Supporting Evidence**

- **Pre-switch trends were parallel:** Before week 15, both groups of branches followed similar patterns, validating our comparison
- **Result is robust:** The finding holds when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Visual confirmation:** The trend chart shows switched branches pulling away from non-switched branches after the procedure change

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

### **Rationale**

1. **Positive signal:** All point estimates indicate improvement; none indicate deterioration
2. **Low risk:** The lower bound of our confidence interval suggests negligible downside
3. **Operational momentum:** Branches that have already switched can serve as trainers for others
4. **Continued learning:** System-wide implementation will generate more data to refine our understanding of the effect

## **Appendix: Analysis Details**

- **Data:** 34 branches, 17 weeks (weeks 6-22), 578 observations
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch-level clustering
- **Switch date:** Week 15
- **Statistical note:** p-value of 0.10 indicates the result would occur by chance approximately 10% of the time if there were no true effect — suggestive but not definitive evidence
