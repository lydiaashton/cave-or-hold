# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out to all branches.**

Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by **2.0 points** (approximately 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, consistent across the post-switch period, and represents a meaningful gain in service quality.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide implementation.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches analyzed** | 709 |
| **Switched to new procedure** | 425 branches |
| **Kept old procedure** | 284 branches |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |

### Results Summary

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control branches (old procedure) | 48.7 | 52.0 | +3.3 |
| Treatment branches (new procedure) | 50.3 | 55.6 | +5.3 |
| **Effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

The 2.0-point improvement is attributable to the new procedure itself, after accounting for seasonal trends and other factors that affected all branches equally.

### Statistical Confidence
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.5 to 2.6 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Relative improvement:** ~4% over baseline service scores

### What the Data Shows

Both groups followed similar trends before the switch. After Week 15, branches using the new procedure pulled ahead and maintained that advantage through the end of the study period. The effect held after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume.

## **CONSIDERATIONS**

**Strengths of this analysis:**
- Large sample (709 branches) provides reliable estimates
- Control group isolates the procedure's effect from other factors
- Results are statistically robust and practically meaningful

**Caveats:**
- Study covers 8 weeks post-switch; long-term effects remain to be confirmed
- Some branches may need additional support during transition
- Combining the new procedure with staffing optimizations may yield further gains

---

## **DECISION REQUESTED**

**Approve rolling out the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.**
