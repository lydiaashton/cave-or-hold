# LIBRARY COOPERATIVE BOARD MEMORANDUM

---

## Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)  
**Data:** 578 weekly observations from 34 branches

---

## BOTTOM LINE

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain** effect on service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by approximately **2 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. However, this result is not statistically significant at conventional levels (p=0.084), meaning we cannot confidently rule out chance as an explanation. The evidence is **suggestive but not conclusive**.

---

## KEY FINDINGS

### What We Compared

| Group | Branches | Procedure | Switch Timing |
|-------|----------|-----------|---------------|
| Treatment | 20 | New procedure | Week 15 |
| Control | 14 | Old procedure | No switch |

### Service Score Changes

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 51.95 | 50.86 | −1.09 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 47.90 | 48.53 | +0.62 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.71** |

### Statistical Results

- **Estimated effect:** +2.13 points (approximately 4% of baseline score)
- **95% Confidence Interval:** −0.29 to +4.55 points
- **P-value:** 0.084
- **Interpretation:** The confidence interval includes zero, so we cannot conclude with 95% certainty that the effect is different from zero. However, the p-value of 0.084 indicates modest evidence of a positive effect.

### Validation Checks

✓ **Parallel trends confirmed:** Treatment and control groups showed similar patterns before the switch  
✓ **No confounding detected:** Staffing levels and visitor volume do not explain the results  
✓ **No evidence of harm:** All point estimates are positive; the new procedure does not appear to reduce service quality

---

## NEXT STEPS

### Recommended Actions

1. **Extend the pilot period** — Additional weeks of data would increase statistical power and provide more definitive evidence

2. **Proceed with cautious expansion** — Given the positive direction and no evidence of harm, consider rolling out to additional branches while continuing to monitor

3. **Ensure consistent implementation** — Verify that all branches are following the new procedure correctly; inconsistent adoption could mask true effects

4. **Track complementary metrics** — Consider adding measures of operational efficiency, staff satisfaction, or patron wait times

5. **Establish decision criteria** — Define in advance what level of evidence would justify full rollout (e.g., "proceed if effect remains positive with p<0.10 after 10 more weeks")

### Recommendation

**Proceed with phased rollout.** The data provide tentative support for the new procedure. While not statistically conclusive, the evidence points in a positive direction with no signs of negative effects. A measured expansion, combined with continued monitoring, appears justified.

---

*Technical note: Analysis uses difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). Controls include staffing index and visits index.*
