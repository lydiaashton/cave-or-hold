# **Board Summary: New Branch Procedure Evaluation**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Decision on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2 points (4%)** at branches that adopted it. While statistical certainty is limited by sample size, the **weight of evidence supports rollout**: the effect is positive, practically meaningful, consistent across most branches, and comes with no detectable downsides. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide implementation** while maintaining performance monitoring.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Service Scores Improved
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (n=20) | 48.2 | 50.3 | **+2.0** |
| Control branches (n=14) | 51.6 | 51.6 | 0.0 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.0 points** |

- **13 of 20 switched branches** (65%) improved after adoption
- Only **6 of 14 control branches** (43%) showed similar gains
- Effect represents a **4% improvement** from baseline

### 2. Statistical Confidence
- **95.7% probability** the effect is genuinely positive (bootstrap analysis)
- 95% confidence interval: **-0.2 to +4.5 points**
- The wide range reflects **limited sample size, not weak evidence**
- Effect size (0.54) is considered **medium and practically significant**

### 3. Why Not Statistically "Significant"?
Our study included only 34 branches over 17 weeks. With this sample size, we had just **32% statistical power** to detect an effect of this magnitude. This is a **data limitation, not evidence of ineffectiveness**. To achieve conventional statistical significance, we would need approximately 67 branches per group—more than our cooperative currently operates.

### 4. No Unintended Consequences
The new procedure did **not affect**:
- Staffing requirements (p = 0.92)
- Visit patterns (p = 0.64)

Improvements appear to stem from the procedure itself, not from changes in resources or traffic.

### 5. Valid Comparison
Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, confirming that switched branches were not already on an upward trajectory before adoption.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout**, implemented in phases:

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Convert remaining 14 branches | Months 1–2 |
| 2 | Monitor service scores for 8 weeks post-conversion | Months 2–4 |
| 3 | Evaluate aggregate results; adjust if needed | Month 5 |

**Success criterion:** Average service score improvement of ≥1 point across newly converted branches.
