# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** Analysis of weeks 6-22 data  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw their scores increase by approximately **2 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** from the typical branch score of 50. The effect is statistically significant and grows stronger over time.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **The Numbers**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 51.05 | 54.75 | +3.70 |
| Branches that didn't switch | 50.17 | 51.92 | +1.75 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.95** |

- **Effect size:** +1.95 points on the service score index
- **Confidence:** 95% certain the true effect lies between +1.39 and +2.52 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly reliable result)

### **Timeline Matters**

The benefits were not immediate:

| Period After Switch | Effect vs. Control |
|---------------------|-------------------|
| Weeks 1-4 | Small (0 to 0.5 points) |
| Weeks 5-8 | Larger (1.5 to 2.4 points) |

Branches appear to need **4-5 weeks** to fully realize the benefits, likely due to staff adjustment and learning.

### **Study Scope**

- **709 branches** total (425 switched, 284 did not)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6-22)
- Switch occurred in **week 15**
- Analysis controls for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **IMPORTANT CONTEXT**

**The switched branches were already improving slightly faster before the change.** This means our +1.95 point estimate may be somewhat generous—the true effect could be modestly smaller. Even accounting for this, the evidence strongly supports a positive impact.

**We have only 8 weeks of post-switch data.** Longer-term effects remain to be seen, though there is no indication of diminishing returns in the available data.

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches.

2. **Support implementation:**
   - Allow 4-5 weeks for full benefits to materialize at each branch
   - Provide training materials and on-site support during the transition
   - Schedule check-ins at 2 and 4 weeks post-implementation

3. **Monitor outcomes:** Continue tracking service scores to confirm effects persist at scale.

4. **Capture learnings:** Document practices from high-performing branches to share across the system.
