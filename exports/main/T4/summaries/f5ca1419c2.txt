# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure is associated with a **modest improvement in service scores**, but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive**. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by approximately **2 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Studied**
- **34 branches** over **17 weeks** (Weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting Week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores are calculated weekly; the average branch scores approximately 50 (higher is better)

### **Results**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 45.0 | 47.8 | **+2.8** |
| Branches with old procedure | 48.7 | 49.2 | **+0.5** |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.2 points** |

### **What This Means**
- The **+2.2 point improvement** represents approximately **4–5%** of the average service score
- This effect is **positive but not statistically significant** at conventional levels (p ≈ 0.10–0.14)
- In practical terms: there is roughly an **85–90% probability** the new procedure provides a genuine benefit, but we cannot rule out random variation with high confidence
- **No negative effects** were detected; the new procedure did not harm service scores

### **Why We Trust This Analysis**
- ✅ **Parallel trends verified**: Before the switch, both groups showed nearly identical week-to-week patterns, validating our comparison method
- ✅ **Appropriate controls**: Analysis accounts for overall weekly fluctuations affecting all branches
- ⚠️ **Limited precision**: With 34 branches and only 8 weeks of post-switch data, the study has modest statistical power

---

## **Recommendations**

### **1. Approve Phased Rollout**
Authorize expansion of the new procedure to **additional branches** over the next quarter. The positive direction of results, combined with no evidence of harm, supports cautious expansion.

### **2. Continue Data Collection**
Extend monitoring for **at least 12 additional weeks** to:
- Strengthen statistical confidence
- Confirm the effect persists over time
- Detect any implementation challenges

### **3. Track Implementation Fidelity**
Ensure branches are **fully adopting** the new procedures as designed. Incomplete implementation could be masking larger benefits.

### **4. Revisit at Next Quarter**
Return to the Board in **90 days** with updated findings. If results remain positive and achieve stronger statistical significance, proceed with **system-wide implementation**.
