# Library Cooperative Branch Procedure Change
## Impact Analysis for Board Decision

---

## Bottom Line

The new rotation and shelving procedure shows a **positive direction of impact** on service scores (+2.3 points on average), but the evidence **does not reach statistical certainty**. The data is consistent with a modest improvement, but we cannot confidently rule out no effect. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with caution. The procedure shows promise and no signs of harm, but we recommend either (a) collecting more data before full rollout, or (b) implementing a phased expansion with continued evaluation.

---

## What We Found

### The Numbers

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Estimated improvement** | **+2.3 points** |
| Baseline average score | ~50 points |
| **Relative gain** | **~4-5%** |
| Statistical confidence | Not significant (p = 0.12) |
| **Likely range of true effect** | **-0.6 to +5.1 points** |

### What This Means

- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** kept the old procedure
- Before the switch, treatment branches scored about **2.7 points lower** than control branches
- After the switch, that gap **narrowed to 0.5 points**—treatment branches caught up
- The improvement pattern is visible in the data but could plausibly be due to random variation

### Why We Can't Be More Certain

1. **Limited post-switch data**: Only 8 weeks of observation after the change
2. **Natural variability**: Service scores fluctuate week-to-week for many reasons
3. **Sample size**: 34 branches provides moderate but not definitive statistical power

---

## Recommendations

### Option 1: Phased Rollout (Recommended)
Expand the procedure to additional branches while continuing to track service scores. This balances optimism about the positive signal with prudent risk management.

### Option 2: Extended Evaluation
Maintain the current split for 8-12 more weeks to gather additional data. This would narrow the confidence interval and provide greater certainty before committing system-wide.

### Option 3: Full Rollout with Monitoring
Proceed with system-wide implementation given the positive direction and absence of negative effects, but commit to formal evaluation at 3 and 6 months post-rollout.

---

## Questions for Board Consideration

1. **What level of certainty is required** before committing to system-wide change?
2. **What are the implementation costs** of rolling out the new procedure?
3. **Is a 4-5% potential improvement** meaningful enough to warrant adoption even with uncertainty?
4. **Should we investigate which branch types** benefit most from the new procedure?
