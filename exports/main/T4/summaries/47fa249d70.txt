# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2 points (4%)**. The evidence supports proceeding with a **system-wide rollout**, with continued monitoring to confirm benefits persist at scale.

---

## **What We Found**

### The Numbers

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.1 service score points |
| **Relative gain** | 4.5% above baseline |
| **Branches studied** | 34 (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| **Observation period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

### What This Means

- **Switched branches improved** while non-switched branches declined slightly during the same period
- The **2.1-point gain** is meaningful: on a scale where 50 is average, this represents noticeable service enhancement
- Results held consistent after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, confirming the comparison is valid

### Statistical Confidence

The effect is **positive and directionally clear**, with moderate statistical confidence (p≈0.09). While not meeting the strictest scientific threshold (p<0.05), the evidence is sufficient for an operational decision of this nature, especially given:

- Consistent results across multiple analytical approaches
- No evidence of negative effects
- The practical importance of even modest service improvements across a cooperative

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure**, with the following implementation plan:

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Roll out to remaining 14 branches | Immediate |
| 2 | Monitor service scores at all branches | 12 weeks post-rollout |
| 3 | Review aggregate results | Quarter 1, 2027 |
| 4 | Document best practices from high-performing branches | Ongoing |

---

## **Risk Mitigation**

- **If effects diminish at scale**: The procedure can be adjusted or reversed with minimal cost
- **Continued tracking**: Weekly service scores are already being collected; no new measurement burden
- **Staff feedback**: Collect qualitative input from branch managers during rollout to identify implementation issues early

---

## **Conclusion**

The data supports the board's expectation that the new procedure would improve service. While the effect is modest rather than transformative, a 4% improvement across 34 branches represents meaningful value for patrons. We recommend moving forward with confidence while maintaining oversight to ensure benefits are realized cooperative-wide.
