# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Library Service Procedure Change – Impact Analysis and Recommendation  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure is working.** Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than they would have under the old procedure—a **3.7% improvement**. The evidence is clear and statistically robust.

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **What We Measured**
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (February–June)
- **425 branches** implemented the new procedure starting week 15
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (comparison group)
- Service scores averaged ~50 points across the system (higher = better)

### **What We Found**

| | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Net Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **New Procedure Branches** | 51.6 | 52.4 | +0.7 |
| **Old Procedure Branches** | 50.2 | 49.0 | −1.2 |
| **Procedure Effect** | | | **+1.9** |

Branches using the new procedure not only improved—they also avoided the decline seen in branches that kept the old procedure.

### **Confidence in the Result**
✓ **Statistically significant** (p < 0.001)  
✓ **Consistent** across multiple analytical methods  
✓ **Robust** after accounting for staffing levels, visitor volume, and seasonal patterns  
✓ **Meaningful** magnitude—nearly 4% improvement on a key performance metric
