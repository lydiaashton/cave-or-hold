# **Memorandum to the Board**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **2.1 points** (approximately **4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and consistent across all weeks after implementation.

---

### **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.13 points (95% CI: 0.97 to 3.30) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Baseline Score** | 48.9 (average before switch) |
| **Percentage Improvement** | ~4% |
| **Sample Size** | 150 branches over 17 weeks |
| **Treatment Group** | 90 branches |
| **Control Group** | 60 branches |

**Additional Observations:**

- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch (weeks 6-14), both groups followed similar patterns, validating the comparison.

- **Consistent impact:** The treatment group outperformed the control group in every week after the switch (weeks 15-22), with gains ranging from 1.1 to 3.8 points.

- **Robust result:** The improvement holds even when accounting for staffing levels and visit volumes, indicating the effect is attributable to the procedure change itself.

---

### **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.** The evidence supports a positive, meaningful impact on service quality with no observed negative effects.

---

### **Next Steps**

1. **Approve rollout** to remaining 60 branches over the next 8-12 weeks.

2. **Monitor service scores** during implementation to confirm similar gains at newly switched branches.

3. **Document best practices** from the 90 early-adopter branches to support smooth implementation.

4. **Track operational metrics** (staff time, costs) to capture the full impact of the change.

---

*Analysis based on difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors. Technical documentation available upon request.*
