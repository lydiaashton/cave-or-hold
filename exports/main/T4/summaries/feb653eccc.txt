# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (a **4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and consistent across most branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Net improvement** | +2.1 points on service score index (p < 0.001) |
| **Percentage gain** | 4.2% improvement over baseline |
| **Effect size** | 0.22 standard deviations (small-to-moderate) |
| **Branch consistency** | 73% of switched branches improved vs. 48% of control branches |
| **Robustness** | Effect holds after controlling for staffing and visitor volume |

### What the Data Shows

- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure; **60 branches** kept the old procedure (control group)
- Before the switch (weeks 6–14), both groups tracked closely together
- After the switch (weeks 15–22), treatment branches pulled away with consistently higher scores
- The improvement is not explained by changes in staffing or visitor traffic

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Implement rollout** to the 60 branches still using the old procedure
2. **Monitor adoption** during the first 8–12 weeks at each branch to ensure proper implementation
3. **Track service scores** post-rollout to confirm the 2-point improvement is replicated
4. **Capture best practices** from top-performing switched branches to support training
5. **Conduct follow-up review** in 6 months to verify sustained improvement

---

## **Analysis Methodology**

- **Data:** 150 branches × 17 weeks = 2,550 observations
- **Approach:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch-clustered standard errors
- **Switch date:** Week 15
- **Controls:** Staffing index and visits index
