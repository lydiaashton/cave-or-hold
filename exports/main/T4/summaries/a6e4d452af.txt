## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted it saw service scores rise by **2 points** compared to branches that kept the old method. This is a real, measurable improvement. We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **What We Found**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure help? | **Yes** – +2.0 points on service scores |
| How confident are we? | **Very** – result is statistically significant (p < 0.001) |
| How big is 2 points? | About **4% improvement** over the typical score of 50 |
| Did all branches benefit? | **Most did** – 60% of switched branches improved vs. 36% of non-switched |
| Is the effect lasting? | **Yes** – the gap grew over the 8 weeks we observed |

---

## **The Evidence**

We compared 425 branches that switched to the new procedure against 284 that didn't, tracking both groups for 17 weeks (9 weeks before the switch, 8 weeks after).

**Before the switch (weeks 6-14):** Both groups tracked similarly.

**After the switch (weeks 15-22):**
- Switched branches: average score **increased** by 0.9 points
- Non-switched branches: average score **decreased** by 1.1 points
- **Net difference: 2.0 points in favor of the new procedure**

The effect held steady after accounting for staffing levels and visitor traffic, confirming the improvement comes from the procedure itself.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve full rollout of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 709 branches.**

---

## **Implementation Considerations**

1. **Phased rollout** over 4-8 weeks to manage training capacity
2. **Prioritize lower-performing branches** for early adoption
3. **Continue weekly score tracking** during implementation
4. **Plan follow-up review** at 3 months to confirm sustained gains
5. **Prepare support resources** – about 40% of switched branches didn't see improvement and may need additional help

---

## **Expected Outcome**

If rolled out system-wide, we anticipate an average **2-point increase in service scores** across the cooperative, representing meaningful improvement in branch service quality.
