# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores**, consistent with expectations. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by an average of **2.1 points** (approximately 4%) relative to branches that kept the old procedure. However, with the current data, this result is **not statistically definitive**. We recommend proceeding with a **phased rollout** while continuing to collect data to confirm the effect.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Service Score Improvement
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment (New Procedure) | 50.8 | 53.9 | **+3.2** |
| Control (Old Procedure) | 52.2 | 53.2 | +1.1 |
| **Difference (Net Effect)** | | | **+2.1** |

- The new procedure is associated with a **2.1-point improvement** in service scores
- This represents approximately **4% improvement** over the baseline average of 51.3
- **Statistical note**: The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.5 to +4.7 points. While the point estimate is positive, we cannot rule out no effect with high confidence using this dataset alone.

### 2. Study Validity
- **Parallel trends confirmed**: Treatment and control branches followed similar trajectories before the switch (weeks 6-14), supporting a valid comparison
- **No confounding factors**: The effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Complete data**: All 34 branches reported for all 17 weeks with no missing values

### 3. Branch-Level Patterns
- **Treatment branches** (n=20): Median improvement of +4.0 points; 75% showed gains of at least +1.4 points
- **Control branches** (n=14): Median improvement of +1.8 points; more variable results
- The distribution of effects suggests the procedure benefits most adopting branches

---

## **Recommendations**

### Proceed with Phased Rollout

Given that:
- The effect is in the **expected direction** (positive)
- The **magnitude is meaningful** (4% improvement on a key performance metric)
- The **risk of harm is low** (no branches showed substantial negative effects)
- **Implementation costs** appear manageable

We recommend expanding the new procedure to additional branches while continuing to monitor outcomes.

### Continue Data Collection

- Track service scores for **8-12 additional weeks** to increase statistical confidence
- With more data, we can narrow the confidence interval and confirm whether the 2-point effect is reliable

### Monitor Implementation Quality

- Investigate why some treatment branches saw gains of 5-7 points while others saw minimal improvement
- Use findings to refine training and support for adopting branches

### Set Review Checkpoint

- Re-evaluate in **Q2 2026** with expanded data
- If the effect remains positive and becomes statistically precise, proceed with system-wide rollout
