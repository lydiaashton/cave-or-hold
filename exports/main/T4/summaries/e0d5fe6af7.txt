# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** [Current Date]  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** (a **4% improvement**) than branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically robust and sustained across the entire post-implementation period.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. The New Procedure Works
Branches using the new procedure saw their service scores increase by **+1.95 points** compared to branches that did not switch. This effect is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001) and represents meaningful improvement in patron service.

### 2. The Comparison Is Valid
We used a rigorous Difference-in-Differences approach, comparing switched branches to non-switched branches before and after week 15. Both groups followed nearly identical trends before the switch, confirming that the improvement is attributable to the procedure change itself—not pre-existing differences between branches.

### 3. Results Are Robust
The improvement held steady even after accounting for:
- Weekly staffing variations
- Visitor volume fluctuations
- Branch-specific characteristics

### 4. Impact Is Consistent
The positive effect appeared immediately after implementation (week 15) and persisted through week 22 with no signs of diminishing. This suggests the benefits are durable, not temporary.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new shelving and rotation procedure across all 709 branches.
