# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.** The new rotation and shelving procedure increased service scores by **2.0 points** (approximately 4% improvement). The effect is statistically significant and consistent across the 20 pilot branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Tested
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** implemented the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Compared service score changes between the two groups before and after the switch

### What We Found

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.7 | 50.9 | +1.2 |
| New Procedure | 53.6 | 56.9 | +3.3 |
| **Net Impact of New Procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

- **Effect: +2.03 points** on the service score index
- **Confidence: 95% certainty** the true effect lies between 0.5 and 3.6 points
- **Statistical significance: p = 0.011** (highly unlikely to be due to chance)

### Why We Trust These Results
- Both groups followed parallel trends before the switch, confirming the comparison is valid
- The effect holds after accounting for branch differences, weekly fluctuations, staffing levels, and visitor volume
- The 20 pilot branches represent a substantial test base (59% of all branches)

---

## **Recommendation & Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure | Immediate |
| **Develop training materials** based on pilot branch experience | 2–4 weeks |
| **Implement in phases** across remaining 14 branches | 1–3 months |
| **Monitor service scores** post-rollout to confirm sustained gains | Ongoing |
| **Report back to the board** on rollout progress and outcomes | 6 months |

---

## **Expected Impact**

If the effect holds across all branches, the cooperative-wide service score should increase by approximately **2 points**, moving the average from ~52 to ~54. This represents meaningful improvement in patron service quality at no additional cost.
