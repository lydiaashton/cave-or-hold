# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Date:** Analysis of weeks 6–22 service score data
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Procedure Rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.** Contrary to initial expectations, the new procedure produced a **statistically significant improvement** in service scores. Branches that switched maintained their performance while control branches declined, resulting in a **net gain of 2.1 points** (p=0.001).

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Question
The board asked whether the new rotation and shelving procedure affected service scores. Initial expectation was **no measurable impact**. The data shows otherwise.

### The Evidence

| Metric | Control Group (Old Procedure) | Treatment Group (New Procedure) |
|--------|-------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Branches | 60 | 90 |
| Score Before Switch | 49.22 | 50.91 |
| Score After Switch | 47.36 | 51.10 |
| **Change** | **−1.86** | **+0.19** |

**Net Treatment Effect: +2.05 points** (95% CI: 0.86 to 3.38)

### Why This Matters

1. **Unexpected positive effect**: The procedure was not designed to improve service scores, yet it did
2. **Protective effect**: Treatment branches held steady while control branches declined—suggesting the new procedure may buffer against external pressures
3. **Robust finding**: Result holds after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume; statistically significant at p=0.001

### Analysis Method
- **Difference-in-differences design** comparing 90 switching branches against 60 non-switching branches
- **17 weeks of data** (9 weeks before, 8 weeks after the switch)
- **Standard errors clustered by branch** to account for repeated measurements
- Results validated against weekly trends and control variables

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new rotation and shelving procedure across all remaining 60 branches.

**Rationale:**
- Clear, statistically significant improvement in service scores
- No identified downside or negative effects
- Low-risk operational change with measurable benefit
- May provide resilience against factors causing score declines in control branches
