# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **1.9 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** on the service score index and is statistically significant. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 49.99 | 53.01 | +3.02 |
| Branches with old procedure | 49.09 | 50.19 | +1.10 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.92** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Estimated effect:** +1.9 points (95% confidence interval: 1.2 to 2.7)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Analysis method:** Difference-in-differences regression with controls for staffing, visitor volume, and weekly trends

### Study Scope
- **709 branches** across the cooperative (425 adopted new procedure; 284 retained old)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- **12,053 branch-week observations**

### Why We Trust These Results
1. **Parallel trends verified:** Before the switch, treatment and control branches moved in lockstep, validating the comparison.
2. **Effect grows over time:** Scores improved by ~0.24 points for each week under the new procedure, suggesting sustained benefits.
3. **Robust to alternative explanations:** Results hold when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.** The evidence demonstrates a clear, meaningful improvement in service quality that outweighs implementation costs.
