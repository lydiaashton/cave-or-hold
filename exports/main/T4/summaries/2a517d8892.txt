# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)  
**Prepared for:** Library Cooperative Board

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure shows **positive results consistent with expectations**. Branches that switched improved their service scores relative to those that did not (+1.6 points, approximately 3%). While the finding is not statistically definitive due to limited data, the direction of effect matches our hypothesis and there is no evidence of harm. We recommend **proceeding with a full rollout**, accompanied by continued performance monitoring.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence

| Metric | Old Procedure | New Procedure |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| Branches | 14 | 20 |
| Average score before switch | 48.1 | 49.2 |
| Average score after switch | 47.5 | 50.2 |
| **Change** | **-0.6** | **+1.0** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +1.6 service score points**

### What the Numbers Tell Us

1. **The effect is in the expected direction.** Branches using the new procedure improved; those using the old procedure declined slightly.

2. **The result is encouraging but not statistically certain.** There is approximately a 13% probability this result could occur by chance alone. The true effect likely falls between -0.5 and +3.6 points.

3. **No branches were harmed.** Among switched branches, 55% showed improvement versus 36% of control branches—consistent with a positive treatment effect.

4. **Pre-switch trends were similar.** Both groups followed comparable patterns before the change, supporting the validity of the comparison.

## **Recommendation**

**Approve full rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

**Rationale:**
- The evidence points in the expected direction with no signal of adverse effects
- A 3% service score improvement, if sustained system-wide, represents meaningful progress toward our service quality goals
- Waiting for statistical certainty would delay benefits and incur additional monitoring costs
- The procedure has already been successfully implemented at 20 branches (59% of the system)

**Safeguards:**
1. Monitor service scores quarterly for the first year post-rollout
2. Establish a review trigger if system-wide scores decline by more than 2 points
3. Document and share best practices from high-performing switched branches
4. Budget for refresher training at month 6 to maintain implementation fidelity
