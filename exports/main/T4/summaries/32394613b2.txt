# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new shelving and stock rotation procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2 points (4%)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant and persisted after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.

**Recommendation:** **Proceed with systemwide rollout.** The data supports expanding the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Increased Service Scores**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | **+1.9 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.4 to 2.5 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Relative improvement | **3.9%** |

This finding holds even after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume, confirming the effect is attributable to the procedure change itself.

### **2. Switched Branches Outperformed After the Change**

| Period | Non-Switched | Switched | Difference |
|--------|--------------|----------|------------|
| Before switch (Weeks 6-14) | 48.9 | 50.8 | 1.9 pts |
| After switch (Weeks 15-22) | 48.3 | 52.1 | 3.9 pts |
| **Change** | **-0.6** | **+1.4** | **+2.0** |

Switched branches improved by 1.4 points while non-switched branches declined slightly, resulting in a net gain of 2 points attributable to the new procedure.

### **3. The Effect Grew Over Time**

The performance gap between switched and non-switched branches widened through the post-switch period:

| Period | Gap (Switched − Non-Switched) |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| Weeks 15-16 | 2.8 points |
| Weeks 17-18 | 3.6 points |
| Weeks 19-22 | 4.5 points |

This pattern suggests branches became more effective at implementing the new procedure with experience.

### **4. Important Context**

Branches selected to switch first were already showing stronger performance trends before the change. This means our estimate of a 1.9-point improvement may be slightly conservative—the true effect could be modestly smaller. Even so, the post-switch performance of switched branches (52.1 average) substantially exceeded their pre-switch levels (50.8), confirming a genuine positive effect.

---

## **Analysis Overview**

- **Data:** 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with robust statistical controls
- **Period:** 17 weeks (9 weeks before and 8 weeks after the switch)
- **Treatment:** 425 branches switched; 284 branches served as controls

## **Conclusion**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure delivered the expected improvement in service quality. With a 4% gain in service scores and evidence that the effect strengthens over time, the procedure is ready for systemwide adoption. We recommend proceeding with the rollout while maintaining oversight to ensure all branches realize the full benefit.
