# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**
### *Recommendation for System-Wide Rollout*

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw an average improvement of **2.5 points** on the service score index—approximately **5% above baseline**. This effect is statistically significant and consistent across all analytical methods. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **Study Design**

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Branches studied** | 150 total (90 switched, 60 control) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences with branch and week fixed effects |
| **Outcome measure** | Service score index (average ~50, higher is better) |

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Service Score Improvement

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.8 | 53.4 | +2.6 |
| Control branches | 50.1 | 50.1 | 0.0 |
| **Net effect** | — | — | **+2.5** |

- **Treatment effect: +2.5 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.2 to 3.8)
- **Statistical significance: p < 0.001**
- **Relative improvement: ~5%** from baseline

### 2. Valid Comparison Confirmed

Pre-switch trends (weeks 6–14) showed **no significant difference** between treatment and control branches (p = 0.40). This confirms the groups were on parallel trajectories before the intervention, supporting a causal interpretation.

### 3. Results Hold Across All Tests

The 2.5-point effect remained stable across four model specifications:
- Simple difference-in-differences
- With staffing and visit controls
- With branch fixed effects
- With branch + week fixed effects

### 4. Effect Sustained Over Time

The improvement persisted throughout all 8 post-switch weeks, with treatment branches outperforming control branches by 2.4 to 4.2 points each week.

✅ **Approve immediate system-wide rollout** of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure to all 60 branches currently using the old method.

| Priority | Action | Timeline |

1. **Weekly tracking** of service scores during rollout to identify any implementation issues

## **Technical Summary**

**Model:** Two-way fixed effects difference-in-differences  
**Estimate:** β = 2.50 (SE = 0.68, p < 0.001)  
**Controls:** Staffing index, visits index, branch fixed effects, week fixed effects  
**Standard errors:** Clustered at branch level
