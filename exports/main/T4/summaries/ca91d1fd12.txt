# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and rotation procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2.1 points** (a 4% increase). This effect is statistically significant and represents a meaningful improvement in branch performance. **We recommend proceeding with a full rollout to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **60 branches** continued with the old procedure as a control group
- Analysis compares changes in service scores between the two groups before and after the switch

### **Results**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated Treatment Effect** | +2.13 service score points |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | [0.98, 3.28] |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Percentage Improvement** | 4.2% from baseline |

### **Interpretation**
- Branches that switched to the new procedure saw their service scores **increase by about 2 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure
- The control group's scores actually **declined slightly** after week 15 (−1.6 points), while treatment group scores **increased** (+0.5 points)
- The effect is **statistically robust**—the probability this result occurred by chance is less than 1 in 1,000

### **Validity Check**
A critical assumption is that treatment and control branches were on similar trajectories before the switch. Our analysis confirms:
- Pre-switch trends were **not significantly different** between groups (p = 0.11)
- This supports confidence that the observed effect is caused by the procedure change, not pre-existing differences

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with full rollout** of the new procedure to the remaining 60 branches. The evidence indicates this change will improve service quality across the cooperative without the negative effects some had anticipated.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Implement rollout** to remaining branches over the next quarter
2. **Monitor adoption** to ensure consistent implementation across all locations
3. **Continue tracking** service scores for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale
4. **Conduct follow-up** with branch staff to understand why the new procedure improved scores (e.g., efficiency gains, better inventory accuracy, improved patron experience)
5. **Document lessons learned** for future operational changes

---

## **Appendix: Technical Notes**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects
- **Standard errors:** Clustered at the branch level to account for within-branch correlation over time
- **Baseline service score:** 50.8 points (average across all branches pre-switch)
- **Data source:** Weekly branch-level service scores, weeks 6–22
