# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** (approximately 4%) than branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, grew stronger over time, and held after controlling for staffing levels and branch traffic.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Clear Positive Impact on Service Quality

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6-14) | After Switch (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| Treatment (425 branches) | 52.5 | 53.6 | +1.0 |
| Control (284 branches) | 50.6 | 49.5 | -1.1 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.1** |

The new procedure is associated with a **+2.1 point increase** in service scores (95% confidence interval: +1.6 to +2.7 points; p < 0.001).

### 2. Benefits Accumulated Over Time

The improvement was not immediate but grew as staff adapted:
- **Weeks 15-17:** Minimal effect (adjustment period)
- **Weeks 18-22:** Clear positive effects (+1.1 to +1.8 points)

This suggests a **2-3 week learning curve** before full benefits are realized.

### 3. Protected Against System-Wide Decline

Control branches experienced a 1.1-point decline in service scores during the study period, possibly due to seasonal factors or increased demand. The new procedure appears to have **shielded switching branches from this decline** while delivering genuine improvements.

### 4. Results Are Robust

The finding holds when:
- Controlling for staffing levels and visit volumes
- Using cluster-robust standard errors (accounting for repeated branch observations)
- Testing for pre-existing trends between groups

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining 284 branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Develop rollout timeline** allowing 2-3 weeks for staff adaptation at each branch

2. **Monitor service scores** during implementation to confirm effects replicate at scale

3. **Document implementation best practices** from high-performing switched branches

4. **Coordinate with staffing plans** since adequate resourcing amplifies the procedure's benefits

5. **Report back to the Board** within 12 weeks of rollout completion with full-system results
