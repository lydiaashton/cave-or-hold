# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on branch service scores. Switching branches maintained service levels better than non-switching branches during the evaluation period, with an estimated improvement of **1.7 points** (approximately 3%). However, this result is not statistically significant, meaning we cannot rule out the possibility that the observed difference occurred by chance.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout accompanied by continued monitoring**, rather than an immediate system-wide implementation or a complete halt.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| **Positive effect detected** (+1.7 points) | The new procedure may improve service quality |
| **Not statistically significant** (p=0.13) | We lack confidence the effect is real |
| **No evidence of harm** | Switching branches did not perform worse |
| **Both groups declined post-switch** | External factors may have affected all branches |

**Decision:** The evidence suggests **cautious optimism**. A monitored rollout balances potential benefits against uncertainty.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. What the Data Shows**

| Group | Branches | Pre-Switch Score | Post-Switch Score | Change |
|-------|----------|------------------|-------------------|--------|
| **Adopted New Procedure** | 20 | 50.5 | 49.9 | **-0.6** |
| **Kept Old Procedure** | 14 | 52.3 | 49.9 | **-2.4** |

Both groups experienced declining service scores after week 15, but branches using the new procedure declined **less sharply**. The estimated benefit of the new procedure is **1.7 points**—the difference between these two declines.

### **2. Statistical Confidence**

- **95% Confidence Interval:** -0.5 to +3.9 points
- **Interpretation:** The true effect could range from slightly negative to moderately positive
- **Statistical Significance:** Not achieved at conventional levels (p=0.13)

### **3. Context Matters**

- **Staffing levels significantly affect service scores** (+1.0 point per unit increase)
- **Week-to-week variability is substantial**, with post-switch effects ranging from -3.4 to +3.0 points
- **Eight weeks of post-switch data** may be insufficient to capture the full, stable effect

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Immediate Action: Phased Rollout with Enhanced Monitoring**

1. **Expand implementation** to 5–10 additional branches over the next quarter
2. **Extend data collection** for a minimum of 12 weeks post-implementation per branch
3. **Track implementation fidelity**—document how consistently each branch follows the new procedure

### **Secondary Actions**

4. **Conduct branch manager interviews** to identify contextual factors affecting outcomes
5. **Review staffing patterns** alongside procedure changes to isolate effects
6. **Establish a decision checkpoint** after 12 weeks of additional data to reassess

### **Success Metrics for Continued Evaluation**

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Service score improvement | ≥2.0 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.10 |
| Implementation consistency | ≥80% adherence |
| Branch manager satisfaction | ≥70% positive feedback |

---

## **Risk Assessment**

| Scenario | Risk Level | Rationale |
|----------|------------|-----------|
| **Roll out now with monitoring** | Low-Moderate | Potential benefit with safety net |
| **Roll out system-wide immediately** | Moderate | May invest in ineffective procedure |
| **Halt implementation entirely** | Low-Moderate | May abandon a beneficial practice |
| **Wait for more evidence** | Low | Preserves resources, delays potential benefit |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure shows **promise but not proof**. Given the absence of negative effects and the possibility of meaningful improvement, a **cautious, data-informed expansion** is warranted. Continued monitoring will provide the evidence needed for a confident system-wide decision within the next quarter.
