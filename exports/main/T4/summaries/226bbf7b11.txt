# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. Branches that switched improved by approximately **2 points** relative to those that did not, but this result could reasonably occur by chance. We recommend **continued monitoring with expanded data collection** before committing to a system-wide rollout.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches Studied** | 34 total (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| **Observation Period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch Date** | Week 15 |
| **Outcome Measure** | Service Score (average ~50; higher is better) |

### **2. Main Result: +1.9 Point Improvement**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Switched Branches** | 47.52 | 48.95 | +1.43 |
| **Non-Switched Branches** | 49.28 | 48.81 | −0.47 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+1.90** |

The switched branches improved while non-switched branches declined slightly, yielding a net positive effect of **1.9 points**.

### **3. Statistical Confidence: Not Definitive**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Effect Size** | +1.90 points | Modest improvement (~4% of average score) |
| **P-value** | 0.10 | 10% chance this occurred randomly |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | −0.4 to +4.2 | True effect could be zero or negative |

**What this means:** The evidence points in the expected direction, but we cannot rule out that the observed improvement is due to random variation rather than the procedure itself.

### **4. Supporting Evidence**

- **Pre-treatment trends were stable:** The gap between switched and non-switched branches remained relatively consistent before Week 15, supporting the validity of the comparison.
- **Week 16 showed strongest effect:** A +4.5 point spike in the first week after switching (statistically significant), followed by smaller positive effects.
- **No negative effects detected:** No branch showed a systematic decline attributable to the new procedure.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Do not proceed with immediate system-wide rollout.** Instead:

1. **Maintain the current pilot** with continued monitoring
2. **Gather 8–12 additional weeks of data** to improve statistical confidence
3. **Complete a cost-benefit analysis** to determine if a ~2-point improvement justifies implementation costs
4. **Revisit the decision** once more data is available

This approach balances the promising directional evidence against the statistical uncertainty, minimizing risk while preserving the opportunity to adopt a beneficial procedure.
