# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Recommendation:** Proceed with Systemwide Rollout

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **1.85 points** (3.6%) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and sustained. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches studied | 150 (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Switch date | Week 15 |
| Method | Difference-in-differences analysis |

### Results
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.74 | 53.94 | +3.20 |
| Non-switched branches | 50.04 | 51.39 | +1.35 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.85** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **95% confidence interval:** 0.97 to 2.72 points
- **p-value:** < 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Effect size:** 3.6% improvement over baseline

### Why We Trust This Result
1. **Strong study design** — The comparison between switching and non-switching branches isolates the procedure's effect from other factors (seasonal trends, systemwide changes, etc.)
2. **Parallel pre-switch trends** — Both groups had similar score patterns before week 15, validating the comparison
3. **Consistent post-switch gains** — The improvement held across all 8 weeks after the switch
4. **Robust to controls** — The result persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

## **CONCLUSION**

The data provide clear evidence that the new procedure improves service quality. The original expectation—that the change would make no difference—was incorrect. The 1.85-point improvement is both statistically significant and practically meaningful for patron experience. We recommend proceeding with confidence.
