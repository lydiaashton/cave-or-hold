## **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

### **Bottom Line**

Contrary to our initial expectation that the procedure change would have no measurable impact, the data show a **statistically significant positive effect** on service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure maintained their performance better than those that kept the old procedure during the study period. We recommend **proceeding with systemwide rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring.

---

### **Key Findings**

**1. Measurable Improvement**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.86 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 0.73 to 2.99 |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.001 |
| Relative improvement | ~4% of average score |

**2. What Actually Happened**
- All branches experienced declining service scores after week 15 (likely seasonal or external factors)
- **Control branches** (old procedure): scores fell by 2.57 points
- **Treatment branches** (new procedure): scores fell by only 0.70 points
- The new procedure appears to have **buffered branches against decline** rather than boosting scores outright

**3. Pattern Over Time**
- Strongest positive effects in the first two weeks after switching (+1.0 to +1.3 points)
- Effects varied in subsequent weeks, but the treatment group consistently outperformed the control group overall
- This suggests the procedure provides **operational resilience** during transition periods

**4. Analysis Validity**
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (p=0.86), supporting causal interpretation
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visit volumes
- Standard errors account for repeated observations at each branch

---

### **Recommendations**

| Action | Priority | Timeline |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Approve systemwide rollout | High | Immediate |
| Create implementation support materials from early adopters | High | 2 weeks |
| Establish monitoring dashboard for service scores | Medium | 4 weeks |
| Re-evaluate with 90 days of post-rollout data | Medium | Quarterly |

---

### **Caveats**

1. **Unexpected finding**: We anticipated no effect; this positive result should be confirmed as rollout continues
2. **Modest effect size**: The improvement is meaningful but not transformative
3. **Study period**: Only 8 post-switch weeks observed; longer-term effects remain unknown
4. **External factors**: Some unmeasured factor may have affected branches differently during this period

---

### **Conclusion**

The new stock rotation procedure delivered an unexpected benefit. While the effect is modest, it is statistically robust and came at no apparent cost. Rolling out the procedure systemwide is a low-risk decision with potential upside for service quality across the cooperative.

---

*Analysis based on 2,550 weekly observations from 150 branches (weeks 6-22). Full technical appendix available upon request.*
