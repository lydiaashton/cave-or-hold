# **Library Cooperative Board Memorandum**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2025  
**Data Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)  
**Branches Analyzed:** 34 (20 treatment, 14 control)

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **promising but not definitive** improvement in service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure gained approximately **2.4 points** (about 5%) relative to those that kept the old procedure. However, this result is only marginally significant (p ≈ 0.08–0.10), meaning we cannot rule out random variation with full confidence.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** while continuing to collect data. The potential benefit is operationally meaningful, and early evidence is encouraging, but additional weeks of observation will strengthen the decision basis.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences analysis comparing treatment branches (new procedure) against control branches (old procedure)
- **Switch Point:** Week 15
- **Pre-period:** Weeks 6–14 | **Post-period:** Weeks 15–22

### Results

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Old Procedure (14 branches) | 48.3 | 47.6 | −0.7 |
| New Procedure (20 branches) | 47.8 | 49.5 | +1.7 |

**Estimated Treatment Effect: +2.4 points** (95% CI: −0.5 to +5.3)

### What Supports This Conclusion

✓ **Parallel pre-trends confirmed** — Both groups moved similarly before the switch (p = 0.67), validating the comparison  
✓ **Consistent post-switch pattern** — Treatment branches outperformed control branches in 6 of 8 weeks after implementation  
✓ **Meaningful effect size** — A 2.4-point gain represents ~5% improvement on the service score index  
✓ **Robust to covariates** — Effect persists when controlling for staffing and visit levels  

### What Limits Confidence

⚠️ **Marginal statistical significance** — p-value of 0.08–0.10 falls short of the conventional 0.05 threshold  
⚠️ **Wide confidence interval** — True effect could range from slightly negative to substantially positive  
⚠️ **Limited observation window** — Only 8 weeks of post-switch data; longer-term effects unknown
