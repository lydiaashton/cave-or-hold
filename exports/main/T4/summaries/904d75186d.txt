# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. Branches that switched experienced an average gain of **2.3 points** compared to those that did not. While this improvement is not statistically definitive, there is no evidence of harm. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 46.5 | 46.6 | +0.1 |
| Branches adopting new procedure | 50.4 | 52.8 | +2.4 |
| **Attributable to new procedure** | | | **+2.3** |

### **Statistical Confidence**

- **95% Confidence Interval:** -0.6 to +5.3 points
- **Interpretation:** We estimate the true effect is most likely between negligible and moderately positive. The data do not rule out no effect, but also do not suggest any negative impact.

### **Study Scope**

- 34 branches tracked over 17 weeks (February–June)
- 20 branches adopted the new procedure; 14 maintained the old
- Analysis accounts for branch differences, staffing levels, and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased rollout to all branches, accompanied by active monitoring.**

**Rationale:**
1. The observed effect is positive with no evidence of harm
2. Service scores are a key performance metric; even modest gains matter at scale
3. Waiting for statistical certainty would delay potential benefits across the cooperative
4. Continued monitoring will provide clearer evidence within 2–3 months

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| Roll out new procedure to remaining 14 branches | Weeks 1–4 | Operations Team |
| Collect weekly service scores | Ongoing | Data Team |
| Interim evaluation report | 8 weeks post-rollout | Data Team |
| Final assessment and adjustment decision | 12 weeks post-rollout | Board |

### **Success Metrics**
- **Target:** Maintain or improve upon the +2.3 point average gain
- **Warning threshold:** If systemwide scores decline by more than 1 point, pause and reassess
