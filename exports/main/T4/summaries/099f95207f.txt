# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**Prepared for the Board of Directors**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **2.4 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and was not driven by changes in staffing levels or visitor volume. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Works
- **90 branches** switched to the new procedure in Week 15; **60 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores improved for both groups after Week 15, but the improvement was **significantly larger** for branches using the new procedure
- The new procedure added **2.4 points** to service scores (95% confidence interval: 1.1 to 3.6 points)

### 2. Results Are Statistically Reliable
- The effect is **highly statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- We verified that both groups were on similar trajectories before the switch, validating our comparison
- The analysis controlled for staffing levels and visitor volume—neither changed differently between groups

### 3. What Changed (and What Didn't)

| Metric | Effect of New Procedure |
|--------|------------------------|
| Service Score | **+2.4 points** ✓ |
| Staffing Levels | No significant change |
| Visitor Volume | No significant change |

The improvement came from the procedure itself, not from branches receiving more staff or attracting more visitors.

### 4. Context
- The average branch scores about 50 on the service index
- A 2.4-point improvement represents approximately **5% of the baseline score**
- Switched branches started with slightly lower scores but showed greater gains after the change

## **Technical Appendix**

**Data:** 2,550 observations (150 branches × 17 weeks, weeks 6-22)

**Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (by branch)

**Model:** Service Score = β₀ + β₁(Switched Branch) + β₂(After Switch) + β₃(Treatment Effect) + controls

**Key Result:** β₃ = 2.38, SE = 0.64, p < 0.001

**Parallel Trends Test:** No significant pre-treatment differences between groups (p = 0.87)
