# **Board Summary: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6-22 (17 weeks of data)  
**Sample:** 150 branches, 2,550 weekly observations

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Contrary to initial expectations, branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure. We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (n=102) | 53.31 | 52.92 | -0.39 |
| Control branches (n=48) | 52.28 | 49.91 | -2.37 |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+1.98** |

While service scores declined across the cooperative after week 15, branches using the new procedure were largely protected from this decline. The **net benefit of the new procedure is 1.98 points** on the service index.

### 2. Results Are Statistically Reliable

- **p-value = 0.003** (highly significant)
- **95% confidence interval:** 0.66 to 3.30 points
- **Pre-trends were parallel** (p = 0.53), confirming valid comparison between groups
- **Effect persists** after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### 3. Context for the Board

Given the service index averages around 50, a 2-point gain represents approximately **4% improvement** in service quality. This is a meaningful benefit for patrons and validates the operational changes made.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all remaining branches |
| **2** | Continue monitoring service scores during rollout to confirm effect persists |
| **3** | Document implementation practices from early-adopter branches for training |
| **4** | Investigate factors behind the broader post-week-15 decline affecting control branches |

---

## **Technical Notes**

Analysis used a difference-in-differences design with branch and week fixed effects. Standard errors clustered at the branch level. The switched_branch × after_switch interaction term is the primary estimator of the treatment effect.
