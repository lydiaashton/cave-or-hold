# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Evaluation of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** [Current Date]  
**To:** Board of Directors

**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** We recommend approving a system-wide rollout.

Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores **2.2 points higher** (approximately **4% improvement**) than would have been expected under the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, widespread across branches, and robust to alternative specifications.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**1. Clear Positive Impact on Service Quality**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment branches (new procedure) | 50.30 | 50.56 | +0.26 |
| Control branches (old procedure) | 48.88 | 46.88 | −2.00 |
| **Net treatment effect** | | | **+2.20** |

- **95% confidence interval:** 0.89 to 3.51 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.001
- **Practical significance:** ~4% improvement relative to baseline

**2. Rigorous Analysis Confirms the Result**

We used a difference-in-differences approach—the standard method for evaluating policy changes when random assignment isn't feasible. Key validation checks:

- ✓ **Parallel trends confirmed:** Treatment and control branches followed similar trajectories before the switch (p = 0.24)
- ✓ **Results hold with controls:** Effect persists after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- ✓ **Robust standard errors:** Analysis accounts for repeated observations at each branch

**3. Benefits Are Broadly Distributed**

- **51% of treatment branches** saw their service scores improve after adoption
- **71% of treatment branches** outperformed the average control branch
- No evidence that results are driven by a small number of outliers

**4. Important Context**

Control branches experienced a 2-point decline during the study period, possibly reflecting seasonal patterns or system-wide pressures. Treatment branches **maintained their service quality** while others declined—suggesting the new procedure may provide resilience against external challenges.

---

### **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all 60 branches currently using the old method.**
