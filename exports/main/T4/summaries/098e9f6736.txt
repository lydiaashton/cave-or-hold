# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We recommend against a system-wide rollout at this time.** The data do not provide statistically reliable evidence that the new procedure improved service scores. While branches that switched showed a modest average improvement, this effect is not statistically distinguishable from zero, and nearly half of the switched branches saw no improvement or declined.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Data covers **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring in **week 15**
- Analysis compares changes in switched branches against changes in non-switched branches

### **Results**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect of new procedure | **+1.6 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.4 to +3.5 points** |
| Statistical significance | **Not significant** (p = 0.12) |
| Effect size | **Small** (0.15 standard deviations) |

### **Branch-Level Outcomes**

| Group | Improved | Declined | % Improved |
|-------|----------|----------|------------|
| Switched branches | 11 | 9 | 55% |
| Non-switched branches | 5 | 9 | 36% |

### **Interpretation**

1. **The effect is not statistically significant.** The confidence interval includes zero, meaning we cannot rule out that the new procedure had no effect.

2. **The effect is uncertain.** The confidence interval also includes negative values, meaning we cannot rule out that the new procedure reduced service scores.

3. **Results varied widely across branches.** Among the 20 switched branches, improvements ranged from -5.2 to +7.4 points. This inconsistency suggests the procedure may work well in some contexts but not others.

4. **The estimated effect is small.** Even if the +1.6 point effect is real, it represents only about 3% of the average service score.

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Immediate Action**
**Do not proceed with system-wide rollout.** The evidence is insufficient to justify the costs and disruption of changing procedures across all 34 branches.

### **Next Steps**

1. **Continue monitoring the pilot.** Collect 8–12 additional weeks of data to increase statistical power and reduce uncertainty.

2. **Investigate variation across branches.** Understand what distinguished the 11 improving branches from the 9 declining ones. Factors may include staff training, branch size, or local conditions.

3. **Gather qualitative feedback.** Survey staff at switched branches about implementation challenges, training adequacy, and operational impacts.

4. **Re-evaluate in Q1 2026.** With additional data and qualitative insights, the board will be better positioned to make an informed rollout decision.

---

## **Appendix: Technical Notes**

- Analysis used difference-in-differences regression with standard errors clustered by branch
- Parallel trends assumption appears satisfied (pre-switch trends were similar between groups)
- Service scores average ~50 points; higher scores indicate better service
