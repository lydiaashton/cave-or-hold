# MEMORANDUM

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Shelving Procedure on Branch Service Scores

---

## Bottom Line

**The new shelving procedure improved service scores by approximately 1.8 points (3.5% above the average score of 50). This improvement is statistically significant and represents a meaningful gain in service quality.**

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+1.78 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 0.24 to 3.33 points |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.024 |
| Relative to average score | **+3.5%** |

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores rise relative to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is unlikely to be due to chance (less than 3% probability).

### 2. Analysis Is Reliable

- **Fair comparison confirmed**: Before the switch, branches that would later adopt the new procedure showed similar trends to those that did not. This validates our comparison method.
- **Robust to other factors**: The improvement holds even after accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume.
- **Large sample**: Analysis covers 2,550 weekly observations from all 150 branches over 17 weeks.

### 3. Effect Pattern Over Time

The improvement was not uniform week-to-week, which is normal for operational changes:

- **Strongest gains**: Weeks 16, 20, and 21 (+4.0, +3.4, and +4.2 points)
- **Modest gains**: Other weeks showed smaller but generally positive effects
- **No negative effects**: No week showed a statistically significant decline

This pattern suggests the procedure takes effect quickly and sustains over time.
