# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** December 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved significantly higher service scores than those that kept the old method. The improvement confirms the board's expectation that this change would benefit branch performance. We recommend approving system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Numbers

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | **+2.2 points** |
| Confidence range | 1.7 to 2.8 points |
| Relative gain | **4.5%** above baseline |
| Statistical confidence | >99.9% (p<0.001) |

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure at week 15; **284 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Analysis compares score changes between the two groups before and after the switch

### What the Data Shows

1. **Clear positive effect**: After accounting for staffing levels, visitor volume, and normal week-to-week variation, adopting branches improved their service scores by an average of 2.2 points relative to non-adopting branches.

2. **Benefits accumulate**: The improvement grew over the 8-week post-switch period, with the strongest gains appearing in weeks 20–22. This suggests staff proficiency with the new procedure increases with experience.

3. **Effect is meaningful**: A 4.5% improvement on a ~50-point service index represents a substantive gain in patron experience, comparable to the effect of noticeable increases in staffing or resources.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new rotation and shelving procedure. The data confirms the procedure delivers the expected improvement in service quality.

---

## **Implementation Plan**

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| Develop training materials for remaining branches | 2–3 weeks |
| Phase rollout by region (3 cohorts) | Weeks 1–9 |
| Monitor service scores during transition | Ongoing |
| Full evaluation after 12 weeks | Week 12 post-rollout |

### Support During Transition
- Provide hands-on training for branch staff
- Assign implementation champions from early-adopter branches
- Track scores weekly to identify branches needing additional support

---

## **Caveats**

Branches that volunteered to switch early may have been more motivated or better resourced than average. The true effect across all branches could be slightly smaller than the 2.2-point estimate, but the direction and significance of the improvement are clear.
