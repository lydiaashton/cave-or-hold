# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Title: Impact of New Shelving Procedure on Branch Service Scores**

**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving procedure improved service scores by approximately 1.8 points (3.5% improvement). This result is statistically significant and consistent across all post-implementation weeks.** We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** participated: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 continued with the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), with the change implemented at week 15
- Analysis used a **difference-in-differences** approach to isolate the procedure's effect

### Results Summary

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement from new procedure | **+1.76 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **0.66 to 2.87 points** |
| Statistical Significance | **p = 0.002** |
| Relative Improvement | **~3.5%** |

### Why We Trust These Results

1. **Comparable groups before the switch**: Service score trends were nearly identical for both groups during weeks 6–14, before any branches changed procedures.

2. **Consistent effects after implementation**: Branches using the new procedure outperformed control branches in **all 8 weeks** following the switch.

3. **No confounding factors**: Staffing levels and visitor volumes were balanced between groups, and results hold when accounting for these variables.

4. **Rigorous statistical methods**: Analysis used cluster-robust standard errors to account for repeated measurements on the same branches.

Contrary to initial expectations that the procedure change would have no effect, the data demonstrates a **clear, positive impact on service quality**. The new shelving procedure should be adopted cooperative-wide.
