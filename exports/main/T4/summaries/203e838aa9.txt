# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 2025
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but not statistically conclusive effect** on service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by approximately **2.4 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure. While we cannot confirm this effect with high statistical certainty, there is no evidence of harm, and the most likely outcome is a modest improvement. **We recommend proceeding with the rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Numbers

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated Effect | +2.4 points on service score |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.4 to +5.2 points |
| Statistical Significance | p = 0.09 (not significant at 0.05 level) |
| Branches Studied | 34 total (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| Study Period | 17 weeks (weeks 6-22) |

### What Happened

- **Switched branches** improved from 45.2 to 49.4 (+4.2 points)
- **Control branches** improved from 46.6 to 48.5 (+2.0 points)
- **Net effect attributable to the new procedure:** +2.4 points

Both groups improved after week 15, but switched branches improved roughly twice as much.

### Why We Trust This Analysis

✓ **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories
✓ **Controls included:** Results account for staffing levels and visitor volume
✓ **Stable effect:** No evidence the improvement fades or grows over time

---

## **What This Means**

The data suggests the new procedure **likely helps**, but we cannot rule out the possibility of no effect. The confidence interval tells us the true effect could range from essentially zero to a meaningful 5-point improvement.

**Importantly:** There is no signal of negative impact. Even in the worst plausible scenario, the new procedure does not harm service quality.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Proceed with system-wide rollout** — The evidence points positive with no red flags |
| **2** | **Monitor for 8-12 weeks post-rollout** — Confirm effects persist at scale |
| **3** | **Document implementation details** — Capture what works well at each branch |
| **4** | **Re-evaluate in Q2 2026** — Assess whether continued monitoring is warranted |

---

## **Questions the Board May Have**

**Q: Why not wait for more conclusive evidence?**
A: We have 17 weeks of data across 34 branches. Collecting more data would delay benefits. The risk of proceeding is low since no negative effects are detected.

**Q: What if the effect is actually zero?**
A: Then the new procedure is neutral—no harm done. The cooperative would have invested in a change that maintains service quality while potentially offering operational benefits not captured in the service score.

**Q: Should we have expected stronger results?**
A: The original hypothesis anticipated improvement. The observed direction matches expectations; the uncertainty reflects normal variation in service metrics rather than a failed intervention.
