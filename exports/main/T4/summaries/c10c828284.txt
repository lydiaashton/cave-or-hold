# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** New Shelving Procedure – Impact Analysis and Rollout Recommendation  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **works as expected**. Branches that adopted it saw service scores rise by **1.9 points (approximately 4%)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The evidence is strong and statistically significant. **We recommend immediate system-wide rollout.**

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.92 points |
| **Confidence interval** | 1.38 to 2.47 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | >99% (p < 0.001) |
| **Branches studied** | 709 |
| **Weeks of data** | 17 (weeks 6–22) |

### Performance by Group

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 49.60 | 53.51 | +3.91 |
| Branches with old procedure | 48.86 | 50.85 | +1.99 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.92** |

### The Benefit Grows Over Time

The improvement was not immediate—it built steadily:

- **Weeks 15–16:** +1.6 to +2.0 points
- **Weeks 17–18:** +2.2 to +2.5 points  
- **Weeks 19–22:** +3.1 to +3.4 points

This pattern suggests branches become more effective with the new procedure as they gain experience.

---

## **Why We Trust These Results**

1. **Clean comparison:** 284 branches kept the old procedure, providing a valid control group
2. **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups tracked together (no pre-existing differences)
3. **Large sample:** 12,053 weekly observations across 709 branches
4. **Consistent pattern:** Every post-switch week showed positive effects; all were statistically significant

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure.**

The analysis confirms what the cooperative expected: the new procedure improves service quality. The effect is meaningful, statistically robust, and appears to strengthen with time.
