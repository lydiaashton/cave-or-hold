# **To the Board of Directors**
# **Library Cooperative Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2 points.** The evidence is moderately strong but not definitive. We recommend proceeding with a systemwide rollout while continuing to monitor results.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. The New Procedure Had a Positive Effect**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.1 service score points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | -0.4 to +4.7 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.098 (marginally significant) |

Branches that adopted the new procedure improved their scores by about 2 points more than branches that kept the old procedure.

### **2. Treatment Branches Narrowed the Gap**
| Period | New Procedure Branches | Old Procedure Branches |
|--------|------------------------|------------------------|
| Before switch (Weeks 6–14) | 47.9 | 51.1 |
| After switch (Weeks 15–22) | 49.6 | 50.8 |
| **Change** | **+1.7** | **-0.3** |

Branches using the new procedure improved while control branches stayed flat.

### **3. No Warning Signs in Pre-Treatment Trends**
Before the switch, both groups of branches followed similar patterns. This validates our comparison and increases confidence that the observed improvement is due to the procedure change, not pre-existing differences.

### **4. Effects Appeared Consistent Over Time**
The improvement was visible across most post-switch weeks, with the strongest effects appearing 1 week and 8 weeks after implementation.

---

## **WHAT THIS MEANS**

- **A 2-point gain on a ~50-point scale represents about a 4% improvement** in service quality
- **The result is not definitive** by conventional statistical standards (which typically require p < 0.05)
- **The risk of harm is low**—no evidence suggests the new procedure hurt any branch's performance
- **Waiting for more certainty has a cost**—delaying rollout postpones benefits to the remaining 14 branches

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

### **Recommended: Proceed with Rollout**

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| **1. Begin phased rollout** to remaining branches | Next 4–6 weeks |
| **2. Monitor service scores** for all branches | Ongoing, weekly |
| **3. Review results** after 8 weeks | Board update |
| **4. Investigate outliers** (branches that don't improve) | As needed |

### **If the Board Prefers More Certainty**

Extend the pilot study by 8–12 weeks before making a final decision. This would narrow the confidence interval but delay potential benefits.

---

## **STUDY DETAILS**

- **34 branches** participated (20 new procedure, 14 old procedure)
- **17 weeks of data** (Weeks 6–22)
- **Analysis method**: Difference-in-Differences with branch fixed effects
- **Statistical approach**: Cluster-robust standard errors (accounts for branch-level variation)
