# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure – Impact Assessment

**Date:** [Current Date]

**From:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and stock rotation procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure—a **4% improvement** that is statistically significant. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Treatment effect | **+1.95 service score points** |
| Statistical confidence | **p = 0.032** (significant at 5% level) |
| 95% Confidence interval | **+0.17 to +3.74 points** |
| Relative improvement | **~4%** above baseline |

### **Study Overview**

- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15
- **14 branches** maintained the old procedure (control group)
- Rigorous difference-in-differences analysis controls for staffing, visitor volume, and branch-specific factors

### **What the Data Shows**

1. **Comparable starting point:** Before the switch, both groups averaged nearly identical service scores (~50 points), confirming a valid comparison.

2. **Clear improvement after adoption:** Following week 15, switched branches consistently outperformed control branches by approximately 2 points.

3. **Effect holds under scrutiny:** The result remains significant after accounting for weekly staffing levels, visitor volume, and inherent differences between individual branches.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure across all 34 branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Roll out to remaining branches** – Develop an implementation timeline for the 14 branches still using the old procedure.

2. **Monitor during transition** – Track service scores weekly during rollout to ensure expected improvements materialize at each branch.

3. **Maintain data collection** – Continue weekly service score tracking to verify sustained improvement over time.

4. **Capture lessons learned** – Document implementation best practices to inform future operational changes.

---

*Analysis based on 578 branch-week observations using difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects.*
