# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to the expectation of no difference, branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **2.1 points** (approximately 4% improvement) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and consistent across most branches. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated impact | +2.12 service score points |
| 95% Confidence interval | 0.9 to 3.3 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| Relative improvement | ~4% above baseline |

### **2. Study Overview**
- **150 branches** participated: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 retained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** collected: 9 weeks before implementation (weeks 6-14), 8 weeks after (weeks 15-22)
- ** Rigorous methodology:** Difference-in-differences analysis controlling for branch-specific characteristics

### **3. Branch-Level Performance**
- **86% of switched branches** (77 of 90) showed improvement in service scores
- Average improvement for switched branches: **+3.2 points**
- Average change for non-switched branches: **+1.1 points** (reflecting normal variation)

### **4. Validation of Results**
Before the switch, both groups followed similar trends. After implementation, the gap between groups widened substantially, confirming the procedure change drove the improvement rather than pre-existing differences.

### **5. Implementation Notes**
The improvement does not appear to require additional staffing or result from changes in visit volume. The benefit appears to stem from the procedure itself.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches.

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with branch managers to ensure smooth transition.

3. **Monitor service scores** for 8-12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale.

4. **Document best practices** from top-performing switched branches to support training.

5. **Report back to the board** within one quarter on rollout progress and outcomes.
