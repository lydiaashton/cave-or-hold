# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.2 points** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and represents a meaningful gain in service quality.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Studied**
- **709 branches** over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **284 branches** kept the old procedure
- Compared service score changes in both groups before and after the switch

### **Results**

| | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branches with old procedure | 49.9 | 49.6 | −0.2 |
| Branches with new procedure | 50.6 | 52.6 | +2.0 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.2** |

- **Effect: +2.2 points** (95% confidence: +1.7 to +2.8)
- **Confidence: >99.9%** (p < 0.001)
- **Practical impact:** ~4% improvement in service scores

### **Why We're Confident**
- The control group provides a clear counterfactual—without the new procedure, treatment branches would likely have followed the same flat trend
- Results hold even when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- A conservative estimate (using only the 3 weeks immediately before the switch) still shows **+1.4 points** improvement

### **Caveats**
- Treatment branches showed slightly stronger momentum in the weeks just before the switch, so the true effect may be closer to 1.5–2.0 points
- Weekly results vary, suggesting the benefit may depend on implementation quality or local conditions

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all 284 remaining branches.

**Expected impact:** 1.5–2.5 point service score improvement per branch

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Roll out to remaining branches** over the next quarter
2. **Track service scores weekly** during implementation to confirm benefits are realized
3. **Identify and support** branches where improvement is weaker than expected
4. **Review in 90 days** to assess full rollout impact and adjust as needed
