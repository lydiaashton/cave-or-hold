# **SUBJECT: Recommendation on Systemwide Rollout of New Branch Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new rotation and shelving procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out to all branches.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.1 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** over the average service score. The effect is statistically significant, has grown over time, and validates the cooperative's investment in this change.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| 425 branches (new procedure) | 51.19 | 52.66 | +1.47 |
| 284 branches (old procedure) | 50.48 | 49.86 | -0.62 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.09** |

### What This Means

- **Statistical confidence:** The result is highly significant (p < 0.001). We can be confident the improvement is real and not due to chance.
- **Practical impact:** A 2-point gain on a ~50-point scale is meaningful for patron experience.
- **Momentum:** The effect grew from negligible in the first two weeks to +2.5 points by week 8, suggesting staff proficiency increases with time.

### Why We Trust This Result

1. **Strong study design:** We compared switched branches to non-switched branches over the same time period, controlling for external factors that affected all branches.
2. **Robust analysis:** The result holds after accounting for staffing levels, visitor volume, branch-specific characteristics, and week-to-week variations.
3. **Clean pre-trends:** In the weeks immediately before the switch, both groups were tracking similarly, supporting a valid comparison.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate systemwide rollout of the new procedure to all 284 remaining branches.**

---

## **Implementation Plan**

| Phase | Timeline | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| Training | Weeks 1–2 | Staff orientation and hands-on practice |
| Transition | Weeks 3–4 | Full implementation at each branch |
| Stabilization | Weeks 5–8 | Monitor scores; provide targeted support where needed |

**Expected outcome:** Full benefits should materialize 4–6 weeks after each branch completes training.

---

## **Monitoring**

- Continue weekly service score tracking during rollout
- Flag any branch with scores declining more than 3 points for additional support
- Conduct a follow-up evaluation at 6 months to confirm sustained improvement

---

## **Conclusion**

The data confirms what the cooperative anticipated: the new rotation and shelving procedure delivers measurable improvements in service quality. With 425 branches already successfully using the new method and a clear path for the remaining 284, we recommend proceeding with full rollout.
