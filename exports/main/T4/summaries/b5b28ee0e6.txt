**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to the expectation that this change would make no difference, branches that adopted the new procedure saw their service scores increase by approximately **2.0 points** (about **4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically robust and consistent across multiple analytical approaches.

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. The New Procedure Works

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| **Branches with New Procedure** | 51.11 | 53.62 | +2.51 |
| **Branches with Old Procedure** | 52.27 | 52.77 | +0.51 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | — | — | **+2.00** |

- **Estimated impact:** +2.03 points on the service score index
- **Percentage improvement:** ~4% relative to baseline
- **Statistical confidence:** 95% confidence interval ranges from +0.78 to +3.27 points
- **Significance level:** p = 0.001 (less than 1 in 1,000 chance this is random)

### 2. Study Overview

- **150 branches** participated: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 maintained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** collected (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring in week 15
- **2,550 observations** total (one per branch per week)
- Analysis compares changes in treatment branches against changes in control branches

### 3. Analysis Validity

✓ **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories (p = 0.94)  
✓ **Results are robust:** Finding holds when controlling for staffing levels, visitor volume, and branch-specific factors  
✓ **Effect grows over time:** Benefits increase by approximately 0.4 points per week post-implementation, suggesting continued improvement with experience
