# **Board Memo: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**Subject:** Impact of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores—contrary to our initial expectations.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **2.4 points** (approximately **5% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The evidence is strong and consistent. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Clear Positive Effect
| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Average score improvement | **+2.37 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **+1.07 to +3.68 points** |
| Relative improvement | **~5%** (vs. average score of 48.5) |
| Statistical significance | **p < 0.001** (highly significant) |

### 2. Effect Was Consistent and Immediate
- Every single week after the switch (weeks 15-22) showed positive effects
- Effects ranged from +2.4 to +4.6 points across post-implementation weeks
- No evidence of the effect fading over time

### 3. Analysis Is Methodologically Sound
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, treatment and control branches followed the same trajectory (p = 0.35)
- **Large sample:** 150 branches over 17 weeks (2,550 observations)
- **Robust to controls:** Effect unchanged when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Clean identification:** 90 branches switched, 60 did not—natural experiment design

### 4. What We Expected vs. What We Found
| Expectation | Finding |
|-------------|---------|
| No meaningful change in service scores | **+2.4 point improvement** |
| Procedure neutral for patrons | **Measurable positive impact** |
| Uncertain whether to expand | **Clear case for rollout** |

---

## **Recommendations**

### Immediate Actions
1. **Approve system-wide rollout** to the remaining 60 branches
2. **Communicate results** to branch managers—this change benefits patrons without compromising operations

### During Rollout
3. **Implement in phases** over 4-6 weeks to allow for training and adjustment
4. **Track service scores** for each branch during their transition to confirm effects replicate at scale

### Follow-Up
5. **Conduct cost analysis** to quantify any operational cost changes alongside the service quality gains
6. **Gather qualitative feedback** from staff and patrons to understand *why* the new procedure works better
7. **Re-evaluate in 6 months** to confirm sustained impact

---

## **Appendix: Study Design Summary**

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Design** | Difference-in-differences with branch-level controls |
| **Treatment group** | 90 branches (switched to new procedure at week 15) |
| **Control group** | 60 branches (kept old procedure) |
| **Pre-period** | Weeks 6-14 (9 weeks before switch) |
| **Post-period** | Weeks 15-22 (8 weeks after switch) |
| **Outcome** | Service score index (average ~50, higher is better) |
| **Statistical method** | OLS with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch) |
