# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure is **moving service scores in the right direction**, but the evidence is not yet strong enough to justify immediate system-wide rollout. Branches using the new procedure showed an average **2-point improvement** in service scores relative to branches keeping the old procedure. While this positive effect aligns with expectations, statistical uncertainty means we cannot yet confirm the improvement is definitively due to the new procedure rather than normal variation.

**Recommendation:** Continue the pilot with extended monitoring. Gather 8–12 more weeks of data before making a final rollout decision.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. The New Procedure Shows Positive Momentum

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Estimated improvement** | **+2.0 points** on service score index |
| Direction of effect | Positive (as expected) |
| Statistical confidence | Inconclusive (p = 0.12) |
| Plausible range | −0.5 to +4.4 points |

The point estimate confirms the new procedure is associated with higher service scores, consistent with your expectations. However, with the current data, we cannot rule out that some portion of this improvement may reflect normal week-to-week variation.

### 2. What Changed After Week 15

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **New Procedure Branches** | 45.7 | 49.0 | **+3.3** |
| **Old Procedure Branches** | 47.3 | 48.5 | +1.2 |
| **Net Effect** | — | — | **+2.1** |

Branches that adopted the new procedure improved more than branches that did not. The net difference of 2 points is our best estimate of the procedure's contribution.

### 3. Why We Recommend Caution

- **The confidence interval is wide.** The true effect could reasonably be as low as −0.5 or as high as +4.4 points
- **Only 8 weeks of post-switch data.** This limits our ability to distinguish the procedure's effect from random fluctuation
- **Some pre-existing differences.** The two groups had different score patterns before the switch, which adds uncertainty

### 4. What a 2-Point Improvement Means

Given that the average branch scores approximately 50 on the service index:
- A 2-point gain represents roughly **4% improvement**
- This would be **meaningful for patron experience** if confirmed
- Across 34 branches, this could represent substantial cumulative benefit

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

### Immediate Actions (Next 4 Weeks)

1. **Extend data collection through Week 34.** This will approximately double the post-switch observation period and significantly improve our ability to detect a true effect.

2. **Document implementation quality.** Survey branch managers on how consistently the new procedure is being followed. Variation in execution may be masking the true effect.

3. **Identify early success stories.** Branches showing the largest improvements may reveal best practices worth sharing.

### Decision Timeline

| Date | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| **8 weeks from now** | Re-run analysis with extended data |
| **10 weeks from now** | Board briefing on updated findings |
| **12 weeks from now** | Final rollout decision |

### Rollout Decision Criteria

| If updated analysis shows... | Then... |
|------------------------------|---------|
| Effect ≥2 points, p < 0.05 | **Proceed with full rollout** |
| Effect ≥2 points, p < 0.10 | **Proceed with phased rollout** |
| Effect <1 point or negative | **Pause and reassess procedure design** |

---

## **BOARD DISCUSSION POINTS**

1. **Risk tolerance:** Is the cooperative comfortable proceeding with moderate evidence, or do we require high certainty before system-wide change?

2. **Implementation capacity:** Do we have the training and support infrastructure ready for a full rollout if warranted?

3. **Opportunity cost:** What other improvements might we pursue if we delay or decline this rollout?

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data provides **encouraging but not definitive** support for the new procedure. The positive direction of the effect validates the thinking behind the change. With additional weeks of data, we expect to reach a clearer conclusion. Continuing the pilot while gathering more evidence is the prudent path forward.
