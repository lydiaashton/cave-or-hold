# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors
**From:** Data Analysis Office
**Date:** [Current Date]
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new branch rotation and shelving procedure **successfully improved service scores**. Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a **2.1-point increase** in service scores relative to branches that maintained the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** on the service score index and is statistically significant (p < 0.001). The effect strengthens over time, suggesting growing operational familiarity enhances the benefit.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Analysis Overview**

| Metric | Control Branches (Old Procedure) | Treatment Branches (New Procedure) |
|--------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Before Switch (Weeks 6-14) | 48.9 | 50.7 |
| After Switch (Weeks 15-22) | 49.3 | 53.3 |
| **Change** | **+0.4** | **+2.6** |

**Net Treatment Effect: +2.1 points** (95% CI: 1.5 to 2.7)

The analysis covers **709 branches** over **17 weeks** (12,053 observations), using a difference-in-differences design that isolates the procedure's effect from other factors like staffing levels, visitor volume, and inherent branch characteristics.

---

## **Key Findings**

1. **Clear Positive Impact**: The new procedure caused a meaningful and statistically significant improvement in service scores.

2. **Benefits Accumulate Over Time**: The effect was modest in the first month after switching (weeks 15-18) but grew substantially by weeks 19-22, reaching **2.7 points**. This indicates a learning curve—branches improve with experience.

3. **Robust Results**: The finding holds across multiple statistical specifications, including models that control for staffing, visitor volume, and fixed differences between branches.

4. **No Adverse Effects**: Service scores declined in neither group; the treatment group simply improved faster.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Implementation Considerations**

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **Phase rollout over 4-6 weeks** | Allows training staff to support multiple transitions simultaneously |
| **Set 4-week expectation for full benefit** | Event study shows effects strengthen after the first month |
| **Continue monitoring service scores** | Confirms effect persists at scale and identifies any outliers |
| **Capture lessons from early adopters** | High-performing switched branches may reveal best practices |

---

## **Expected Impact**

If the effect holds at system-wide scale:
- **~700 branches** would see an average **2-point service score improvement**
- This represents the equivalent of moving a branch from the **50th to the 60th percentile** of service quality
- Improved patron satisfaction and operational efficiency
