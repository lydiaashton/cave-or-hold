# **TO: Board of Directors, Library Cooperative**

# **SUBJECT: Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive direction of effect** on service scores, consistent with expectations, but the evidence is **not yet statistically conclusive**. We estimate an improvement of approximately **2 points** on the service score index, but with enough uncertainty that we cannot confidently rule out smaller or no effects. We recommend **proceeding with caution**—either by extending the evaluation period or implementing a phased rollout with continued monitoring.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Studied**
| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Branches** | 34 total (20 switched to new procedure; 14 kept old procedure) |
| **Time Period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6-22); switch occurred in Week 15 |
| **Outcome** | Service Score Index (average ~50; higher is better) |
| **Method** | Difference-in-Differences regression with branch/week controls |

### **Results**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated Improvement** | **+1.8 to +2.0 points** |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | **-0.9 to +4.5 points** |
| **Statistical Significance** | **Not significant at conventional levels (p=0.12-0.20)** |

### **What This Means**

✓ **The effect is in the expected direction** – branches that switched showed greater score improvements than those that did not

✓ **The magnitude is meaningful if real** – a 2-point gain represents roughly a 4% improvement over the baseline

⚠️ **Uncertainty remains** – the data are consistent with effects ranging from slightly negative to moderately positive

⚠️ **Eight weeks of post-switch data may be insufficient** – service quality improvements may take time to fully materialize

## **Why the Uncertainty?**

1. **Limited post-switch observations** – Only 8 weeks of data after implementation
2. **Natural score variability** – Service scores fluctuate week-to-week due to factors beyond the procedure (staffing, visits, seasonal patterns)
3. **Modest effect size** – Even if real, the improvement is subtle and requires more data to detect confidently

---

## **Next Steps**

| Option | Action | Timeline |
|--------|--------|----------|
| **A. Extend Evaluation** | Continue collecting data for 8-12 more weeks before deciding | Re-assess in Q1 2027 |
| **B. Phased Rollout** | Expand new procedure to additional branches while monitoring | Begin expansion; evaluate quarterly |
| **C. Full Rollout** | Implement systemwide based on positive direction of evidence | Immediate |

**Our Recommendation: Option A or B**

Given the positive direction of results and operational momentum, we suggest either:
- **Extending the evaluation** to reduce uncertainty before full commitment, OR
- **Proceeding with a phased rollout** while maintaining rigorous monitoring to confirm the effect strengthens with more data

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure is **performing as hoped** but the data do not yet provide the statistical certainty typically required for major operational decisions. This is not evidence that the procedure *fails*—rather, it is evidence that we need **more time or broader implementation** to confirm the benefit with confidence.

We stand ready to support whichever path the board chooses and to continue monitoring outcomes.
