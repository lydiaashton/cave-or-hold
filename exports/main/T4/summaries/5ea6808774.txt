# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** (4% improvement) than branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant, sustained over the 8-week observation period, and not explained by staffing or visitor volume changes. **We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.05 points (p = 0.02) |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | 0.3 to 3.8 points |
| **Relative Improvement** | ~4% above baseline |
| **Branches Studied** | 34 total (20 switched, 14 control) |
| **Observation Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

### What We Measured
We compared service scores before and after week 15, when 20 branches switched to the new procedure while 14 branches continued with the old procedure. This design isolates the effect of the procedure change from other factors like seasonal variation or branch-specific characteristics.

### What We Controlled For
- **Branch fixed effects** (accounting for inherent differences between branches)
- **Staffing levels** (no differential change between groups)
- **Visitor volume** (no differential change between groups)

### Effect Over Time
The improvement appeared immediately after implementation and persisted throughout the 8-week post-switch period. Week-to-week variation is normal for service metrics and does not indicate instability in the effect.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new shelving and rotation procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Develop rollout schedule** for the 14 control branches plus any new locations
2. **Prepare training materials** documenting the new procedure
3. **Monitor first 4–8 weeks** at each branch during implementation
4. **Continue tracking service scores** to confirm effect replicates at scale
5. **Schedule 6-month follow-up** to assess long-term sustainability
