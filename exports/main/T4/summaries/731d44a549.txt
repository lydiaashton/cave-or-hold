# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure is working as intended.** Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher (4% improvement)** than comparable branches using the old procedure. The evidence supports **proceeding with a full rollout** to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Impact on Service Scores

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated treatment effect | **+1.98 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | +1.43 to +2.52 points |
| Relative improvement | **~4%** over baseline |
| Statistical significance | **p < 0.001** (highly reliable) |

### Study Overview

- **709 branches** participated: 425 switched to the new procedure, 284 continued with the old
- **17 weeks of data** collected (weeks 6–22), with the switch implemented at week 15
- Analysis controlled for weekly patterns, staffing levels, and visitor volume

### What the Numbers Show

Branches using the new procedure improved their service scores by **2.26 points** after the switch. Branches keeping the old procedure improved by only **0.28 points** over the same period. The **net attributable gain is 1.98 points**.

---

## **Why We're Confident in This Result**

1. **Strong research design:** We compared branches that switched against similar branches that didn't, before and after the change.

2. **Consistent findings:** The result holds across multiple statistical approaches and when adjusting for other factors like staffing and visits.

3. **Clear timing:** The improvement appeared after the switch, not before.

4. **Large sample:** Over 12,000 branch-week observations provide robust evidence.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Approve full rollout** to remaining 284 branches |
| **2** | **Monitor outcomes** for 6–12 months post-rollout to confirm sustained impact |
| **3** | **Document best practices** from high-performing switched branches |
| **4** | **Review operational costs** to confirm the benefit justifies the investment |

---

## **Considerations**

- The improvement is **modest but meaningful**—this procedure should be viewed as one component of service quality, not a standalone solution.
- We have **8 weeks of post-switch data**; longer-term effects should be tracked.
- Service scores naturally fluctuate week to week; the procedure provides a steady lift above this baseline variation.

---

## **Conclusion**

The new stock rotation procedure delivers the expected improvement in service scores. With statistically significant gains and no identified drawbacks, we recommend the Board approve system-wide implementation.
