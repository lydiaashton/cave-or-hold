## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted it achieved service scores **2.0 points higher** than branches using the old procedure. Given the strong evidence and your confidence in this initiative, we recommend **immediate system-wide rollout** to all 284 remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| 425 Switched Branches | 52.0 | 54.8 | +2.8 |
| 284 Non-Switched Branches | 50.6 | 51.4 | +0.8 |
| **Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

### **Why We're Confident**

1. **Statistically significant:** The probability this result occurred by chance is less than 1 in 1,000 (p < 0.001)

2. **Robust to other factors:** The improvement holds even after accounting for staffing levels and branch visit volumes

3. **Meaningful impact:** On a scale where the average branch scores ~50, a 2-point gain represents a **4% improvement** in service quality

4. **Clean comparison:** We had a natural experiment—some branches switched, others didn't—allowing us to isolate the procedure's effect from other factors

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with full rollout.** The data confirms what you expected: the new procedure improves service. Delaying implementation means leaving service quality gains unrealized at 284 branches.
