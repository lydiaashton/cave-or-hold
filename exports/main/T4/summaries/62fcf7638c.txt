# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure is performing **in the expected direction**, with adopting branches showing improved service scores relative to those maintaining the old procedure. The observed improvement of **2.4 points** aligns with our hypothesis that the new procedure would enhance service quality. However, with the current data, we cannot confirm this effect with statistical certainty. We recommend **proceeding with the rollout while continuing to monitor outcomes** to build a stronger evidence base.

---

## **Analysis Overview**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Branches studied | 34 |
| Treatment branches (new procedure) | 20 |
| Control branches (old procedure) | 14 |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| Switch point | Week 15 |

---

## **Results**

### Service Score Changes

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 48.77 | 50.98 | **+2.21** |
| Old Procedure Branches | 50.31 | 50.14 | -0.17 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.38** |

### Statistical Findings

- **Point estimate:** +2.38 points improvement
- **Confidence range:** The true effect likely falls between -0.8 and +5.5 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.14 (below conventional threshold of 0.05)

---

## **Interpretation**

The data shows the new procedure is **moving service scores in the right direction**. Branches that switched improved by over 2 points while control branches remained flat. This positive trend supports our original hypothesis.

The lack of statistical significance reflects **limited data rather than ineffectiveness**. With only 8 weeks of post-switch data across 34 branches, our ability to detect modest but meaningful improvements is constrained. A 2.4-point gain on a 50-point scale represents approximately a **5% improvement**—operationally meaningful even if not yet statistically definitive.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **1** | **Approve phased rollout** to remaining 14 branches | Immediate |
| **2** | **Extend monitoring period** by 12 weeks to strengthen evidence | Q1 2027 |
| **3** | **Document implementation practices** at high-performing branches | Ongoing |
| **4** | **Report updated findings** to the board | Q2 2027 |

---

## **Bottom Line for Decision**

**The new procedure is delivering improvements consistent with expectations.** While we would prefer stronger statistical confirmation before a full rollout, the positive direction of the effect, combined with operational benefits already reported by branch managers, supports moving forward. Continued monitoring will provide the additional evidence needed to confirm the magnitude of improvement.
