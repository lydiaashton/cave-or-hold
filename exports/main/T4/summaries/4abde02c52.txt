# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.**

Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure maintained service scores approximately **2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure—a **3.9% improvement** relative to baseline. The effect is statistically significant (p < 0.01), consistent across all post-implementation weeks, and robust to alternative analytical approaches.

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Treatment Effect
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated impact | +1.96 service score points |
| 95% Confidence interval | +0.78 to +3.13 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.01 |
| Relative improvement | 3.9% of baseline |

### 2. What the Data Shows

The new procedure **protected branches from a system-wide decline** in service scores:

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.14 | 46.92 | **−2.22** |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 49.86 | 49.56 | **−0.31** |

**Net benefit of new procedure: +1.92 points**

### 3. Consistency Across Time

The treatment effect was **positive in every single week** after implementation:

| Week | DiD Estimate |
|------|--------------|
| 15 | +1.33 |
| 16 | +1.64 |
| 17 | +1.56 |
| 18 | +1.98 |
| 19 | +2.46 |
| 20 | +1.95 |
| 21 | +2.62 |
| 22 | +1.78 |
| **Average** | **+1.92** |

### 4. Analysis Validity

✓ **Parallel trends confirmed**: Treatment and control branches followed statistically similar trajectories before the switch (p = 0.41)

✓ **Minimal pre-existing differences**: Treatment branches scored only 0.7 points higher before the switch (not statistically significant)

✓ **Robust to controls**: Effect persists when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

✓ **Robust to branch fixed effects**: Effect holds when controlling for individual branch characteristics (p = 0.002)
