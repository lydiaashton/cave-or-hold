# **Board Memorandum**

---

## **Subject: New Shelving and Rotation Procedure — Service Score Impact Analysis**

**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted it achieved service scores **2.2 points higher** than they would have under the old procedure—a **4.4% improvement**. The effect is statistically robust and consistent across branches. We recommend **full system-wide rollout**.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment effect** | +2.19 service score points |
| **Confidence range** | 1.6 to 2.8 points (95% CI) |
| **Statistical confidence** | p < 0.001 (virtually no chance this is random) |
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Observations** | 12,053 branch-week records |
| **Baseline score** | 49.8 (average before switch) |

### What We Compared

- **Treatment group:** 425 branches that switched to the new procedure at week 15
- **Control group:** 284 branches that kept the old procedure
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences analysis, which isolates the procedure's effect from other factors (seasonal trends, staffing changes, visitor volume)

### Results by Group

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.2 | 54.3 | +4.1 |
| Control branches | 49.1 | 51.0 | +1.9 |
| **Net effect** | — | — | **+2.2** |

The control group also improved slightly (likely due to seasonal factors), but the switched branches improved **more than twice as much**.

---

## **Why We're Confident**

1. **Parallel pre-trends:** Both groups followed similar trajectories before the switch, validating the comparison.
2. **Controls applied:** Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.
3. **Consistent effect:** The gain persisted across all post-switch weeks (15–22).
4. **Large sample:** 709 branches over 17 weeks provides strong statistical power.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with full rollout to all remaining branches.** The evidence is clear that the new procedure improves service quality.

---

---

## **Appendix: Technical Notes**

- **Model:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects
- **Standard errors:** Clustered at branch level
- **Effect size:** 0.21 standard deviations (moderate practical significance)
- **Data period:** Weeks 6–22 (pre-switch: weeks 6–14; post-switch: weeks 15–22)
