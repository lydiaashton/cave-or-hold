# **Board Memorandum**

## **New Branch Stock Rotation & Shelving Procedure: Impact Evaluation**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6-22 (17 weeks of data)  
**Branches Studied:** 709 (425 switched, 284 control)

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.0 points higher** than they would have under the old procedure—a **4% improvement**. The effect is statistically significant, consistent across all post-implementation weeks, and robust to controls for staffing and customer volume.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|------------------|------------------|
| Average score before Week 15 | 49.5 | 48.5 |
| Average score after Week 15 | 52.3 | 49.2 |
| **Change** | **+2.8** | **+0.8** |

**Net effect attributable to new procedure: +2.0 points**

- **Statistical confidence:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.5 to 2.6 points
- **Effect consistency:** Positive in all 8 post-implementation weeks
- **Robustness:** Effect persists after controlling for staffing levels and visit volumes

---

## **Why We Can Trust This Result**

1. **Strong study design:** The control group (branches keeping the old procedure) isolates the effect of the new procedure from other factors like seasonal trends or system-wide changes.

2. **Clean identification:** Both groups followed parallel trends before the switch, then diverged precisely when the new procedure was implemented.

3. **Adequate sample:** 12,053 branch-week observations provide strong statistical power.

4. **Consistent effect:** The improvement appeared immediately in Week 15 and has been sustained through Week 22.
