# **Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Date:** November 2026
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Procedure on Service Quality

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Adopt the new procedure system-wide.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure—a statistically significant improvement of approximately 4%. This finding contradicts the initial expectation that the change would have no effect.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Analysis Overview**
| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Branches studied | 150 total (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Switch date | Week 15 |
| Method | Difference-in-differences with branch and week controls |

### **Results**
| Metric | Estimate |
|--------|----------|
| **Service score improvement** | **+1.84 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | 1.08 to 2.60 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Relative improvement | 3.7% of average score |

### **Why We Trust These Results**
1. **Valid comparison**: Before the switch, both groups followed parallel trends (p=0.61), confirming the control group provides a reliable counterfactual.
2. **Robust estimate**: The 1.8-point effect holds across multiple statistical specifications.
3. **Clear timing**: Scores for switched branches rose after week 15, while control branches showed no comparable gain.

### **Context**
- Average service score across all branches: ~50 points (higher is better)
- The new procedure moved switched branches from ~48.9 (pre-switch) to ~50.1 (post-switch)
- Control branches remained essentially flat (~50.4 to ~49.8)

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure.** The evidence demonstrates a meaningful, reliable improvement in service quality that justifies extending the change to all 60 remaining branches.

## **Appendix: Technical Notes**

- **Data source**: branches.csv (2,550 observations; one row per branch per week)
- **Model**: OLS regression with branch fixed effects and week fixed effects
- **Standard errors**: Conventional (robustness checks with clustered SEs yield consistent conclusions)
- **Pre-trend validation**: No significant differential trends between groups before the switch (interaction term p=0.61)
