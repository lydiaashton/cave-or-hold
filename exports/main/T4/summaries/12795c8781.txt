# **Board Summary: New Shelving Procedure Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, consistent across all post-implementation weeks, and shows no signs of fading. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated effect** | +1.9 points on service score |
| **95% Confidence interval** | +1.4 to +2.5 points |
| **Relative improvement** | ~4% (vs. average score of 50) |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |

The analysis controls for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume, isolating the effect of the procedure change itself.

### 2. Effect Is Consistent and Sustained

The improvement was not a temporary spike. **Every single week after implementation** showed a positive, statistically significant effect:

- **Weeks 15-18**: Average +2.6 points
- **Weeks 19-22**: Average +3.0 points

The effect appears to be **strengthening over time**, suggesting branches are becoming more proficient with the new procedure.

### 3. Analysis Is Methodologically Sound

Our study design compares 425 branches that switched to 284 branches that didn't, tracking both groups before and after week 15:

- ✅ **Parallel pre-trends confirmed**: Before the switch, both groups moved together week-to-week, supporting the validity of our comparison.
- ✅ **Adequate sample size**: 709 branches, 12,053 observations over 17 weeks.
- ✅ **Robust statistics**: Standard errors account for repeated measurements within each branch.

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|

| **2** | **Develop implementation support materials** (training guides, checklists) to help branches achieve the full benefit. |
| **3** | **Continue monitoring** service scores for 6-12 months post-rollout to confirm sustained impact. |

| **5** | **Document best practices** from high-performing branches to share across the cooperative. |

---

## **Study Details**

- **Period**: Weeks 6-22 (17 weeks of data)
- **Branches**: 709 total (425 switched, 284 did not)
- **Switch date**: Week 15
- **Method**: Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
