# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **promising but not conclusive** evidence of improving service scores. Branches that switched experienced an average improvement of approximately **2 points** (about 4% relative to the baseline score of 50), but this effect is not statistically significant at conventional levels. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than an immediate systemwide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Overall Effect**
- **Estimated improvement:** +2.0 service score points for branches using the new procedure
- **Statistical confidence:** 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.5 to +4.8 points (includes zero)
- **Interpretation:** The data suggests a positive effect, but we cannot rule out the possibility that the observed improvement occurred by chance

### **Branch-Level Results**
- **14 of 20 switched branches (70%)** showed score improvements after adopting the new procedure
- **Average change among switchers:** +1.7 points
- **Range of effects:** Individual branch changes varied from -5.1 to +6.8 points, indicating meaningful heterogeneity

### **Comparison Groups**
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (new procedure) | 47.9 | 49.6 | +1.7 |
| Non-switched branches (old procedure) | 51.1 | 50.8 | -0.3 |

### **Data Quality Check**
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (no significant difference), supporting the validity of our comparison
- Analysis covers 34 branches over 17 weeks (weeks 6-22), with the switch occurring in week 15
- 20 branches adopted the new procedure; 14 branches maintained the old procedure

---

## **Next Steps**

### **Recommended Actions**

1. **Proceed with Cautious Expansion**
   - Roll out the new procedure to an additional 10-15 branches over the next quarter
   - Maintain the 14 control branches for continued comparison

2. **Extend the Evaluation Period**
   - Collect at least 8-12 more weeks of data to improve statistical precision
   - This would narrow the confidence interval and provide a clearer answer

3. **Investigate Variation Across Branches**
   - Six switched branches saw score declines; understand why
   - Identify characteristics of the 14 branches that benefited most
   - Consider whether the procedure works better in certain contexts (size, staffing, visit volume)

4. **Monitor Implementation Fidelity**
   - Ensure branches are following the new procedure consistently
   - Document any adaptations or deviations that may affect outcomes

5. **Reassess in Q1 2027**
   - With additional data, the board can make a more confident decision about systemwide rollout
   - Target: achieve statistical significance or narrow confidence interval to exclude zero

---

## **Appendix: Technical Notes**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects and clustered standard errors
- **Controls:** Staffing index and visits index (neither significantly affected results)
- **Limitations:** Relatively short post-switch period (8 weeks); some branches may need more time to realize full benefits
