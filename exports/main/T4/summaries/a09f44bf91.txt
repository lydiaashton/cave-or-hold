# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks of data)

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **improved service scores by 1.8 points** (3.5% above baseline). This effect is statistically significant and was unexpected given prior assumptions. **We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Control Branches (Old Procedure) | Treatment Branches (New Procedure) |
|--------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Pre-switch average (weeks 6–14) | 51.9 | 51.2 |
| Post-switch average (weeks 15–22) | 51.3 | 52.4 |
| **Change** | **–0.6** | **+1.2** |

**Net Effect (Difference-in-Differences): +1.8 points**

### Statistical Confidence
- **P-value:** 0.002 (less than 1% probability this result occurred by chance)
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 0.65 to 2.93 points
- **Interpretation:** Even under conservative estimates, the procedure produces a meaningful positive effect

### Validation Checks
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Treatment and control branches showed similar score trajectories before the switch (p = 0.92), validating the comparison
- **No confounding factors:** Staffing levels and visitor volumes remained stable across both groups, indicating the improvement reflects the procedure itself
- **Complete data:** All 150 branches contributed observations in both pre- and post-switch periods

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate rollout** of the new procedure to all 60 branches currently using the old method. The evidence indicates this change delivers measurable service improvements at no apparent cost to staffing or visitor experience.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Schedule rollout** to remaining 60 branches within the next quarter
2. **Prepare training materials** based on practices from the 90 early-adopter branches
3. **Continue monitoring** service scores post-rollout to confirm sustained benefits
4. **Report back to the board** in 6 months with full system performance data
