# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores—an unexpected benefit.** While we anticipated the rotation change would have no effect on service quality, branches using the new procedure scored approximately **2 points higher** than those using the old procedure. This represents a **3.9% improvement** relative to baseline performance. The effect is statistically significant and consistent across all post-implementation weeks.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure delivers operational changes without the service quality trade-offs we anticipated—in fact, it provides a measurable service advantage.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Treatment Effect
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated impact | +1.96 service score points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 0.78 to 3.13 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.01 |
| Relative improvement | 3.9% above baseline |

### 2. What the Data Shows
The new procedure **protected branches from a system-wide decline** in service scores:

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.14 | 46.92 | **−2.22** |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 49.86 | 49.56 | **−0.31** |

Branches using the old procedure lost 2.2 points; branches using the new procedure lost only 0.3 points.

### 3. Consistency Across Time
- **All 8 post-switch weeks** showed positive treatment effects
- Weekly estimates ranged from +1.3 to +2.6 points
- Effect remained stable after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Results hold when accounting for individual branch characteristics

### 4. Analysis Validity
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Treatment and control branches had statistically similar trends before the switch (p = 0.41)
- **Minimal pre-existing differences:** Treatment branches scored only 0.7 points higher before the switch (not statistically significant)

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** to all 150 branches

2. **Monitor during implementation** to confirm the effect replicates at scale

3. **Investigate the system-wide decline** affecting control branches—understanding what drove the 2.2-point drop will help us maximize the new procedure's protective effect

4. **Document operational learnings** from the 102 branches already using the new procedure to support training during rollout

5. **Consider phased implementation** to manage operational capacity, prioritizing branches with the lowest current service scores
