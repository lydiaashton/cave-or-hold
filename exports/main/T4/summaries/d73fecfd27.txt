# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores** (+2.2 points, approximately 4% improvement), consistent with our expectations. However, with only 8 weeks of post-implementation data, the estimate lacks the statistical precision needed for a definitive conclusion. We recommend **proceeding with the rollout while continuing to monitor outcomes** to confirm the effect and refine implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a comparison group
- Service scores average ~50 points; higher scores indicate better service

### Results Summary

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | **+2.18 points** |
| **Confidence range** | −0.3 to +4.7 points |
| **Direction of effect** | Positive (70% of switched branches improved) |
| **Practical significance** | ~4% improvement over baseline |

### What the Data Shows

1. **Switched branches improved more than control branches.** After the procedure change, switched branches gained an average of 2.7 points versus 0.5 points for branches that kept the old procedure.

2. **The effect is in the expected direction.** Every week after the switch except one showed the treatment group outperforming the control group relative to pre-switch patterns.

3. **Most branches benefited.** 14 of 20 switched branches (70%) showed score improvements after adoption, compared to 7 of 14 control branches (50%).

4. **No evidence of harm.** The lower bound of our confidence interval suggests the procedure is unlikely to reduce scores by more than 0.3 points.

### Why Results Are Not More Conclusive

- **Limited post-switch data:** Only 8 weeks of observations after implementation
- **Natural week-to-week variation:** Service scores fluctuate considerably (standard deviation ~10 points)
- **Sample size:** 34 branches provides moderate but not definitive statistical power

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout, with monitoring.**

The evidence points in the expected direction—service scores improved in branches that adopted the new procedure. While statistical certainty is limited by the short observation window, there is no signal of negative effects, and the magnitude of improvement (4%) is operationally meaningful.

## **Conclusion**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure is performing as hoped. While we would prefer greater statistical certainty before a full rollout, the positive signal, absence of negative effects, and operational benefits support moving forward. Continued monitoring will ensure we can confirm the effect and make adjustments if needed.
