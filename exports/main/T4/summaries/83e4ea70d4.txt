# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** Analysis of Weeks 6–22 Service Score Data  
**Recommendation:** Proceed with Cooperative-Wide Rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a meaningful 4% improvement on the standard service score scale.

**We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment effect** | +2.09 service score points |
| **Confidence interval** | 1.74 to 2.44 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Observation period** | 17 weeks (9 before switch, 8 after) |

### What Changed

- **Treatment branches** (new procedure): Scores rose from 50.4 to 53.2 (+2.8 points)
- **Control branches** (old procedure): Scores rose from 49.7 to 50.4 (+0.7 points)
- **Net effect of new procedure**: +2.1 points

### Consistency of Results

The improvement appeared immediately after the switch (week 15) and persisted consistently through week 22. The gap between treatment and control branches widened from an average of 0.7 points before the switch to 2.7 points after.

---

## **Why This Matters**

A 2-point improvement on a 50-point scale translates to:
- **Better patron experience** across 425+ branches
- **Scalable benefit** when applied cooperative-wide
- **Validated investment** in the procedural change

---

## **Considerations**

Our analysis noted that treatment branches showed slightly stronger improvement trends even before the switch. Accounting for this reduces the estimated effect somewhat, but the post-switch improvement remains clear and positive in all analyses.

**Bottom line:** The new procedure delivers real, measurable improvement.

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| Approve cooperative-wide rollout | Immediate |
| Implement at remaining 284 branches | Next 4–8 weeks |
| Monitor service scores post-rollout | Ongoing (minimum 8 weeks) |
| Report results to Board | Quarter 1 |

---

## **Conclusion**

The data confirm what we expected: **the new stock rotation and shelving procedure works.** Service scores improved significantly at branches that adopted it, with no evidence of negative effects. We are confident in recommending full rollout across the cooperative.
