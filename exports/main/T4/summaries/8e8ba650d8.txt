# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving & Rotation Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

The new shelving and rotation procedure produced a **statistically significant improvement in service scores**. Branches that adopted the new procedure saw an average increase of **2.1 points** (approximately 4% improvement) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. The effect is robust, highly significant, and strengthens over time as staff gain proficiency.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Clear Service Improvement**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +2.13 points on service score |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.56 to 2.69 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Relative improvement | ~4% above baseline |

### **2. Benefits Grow With Experience**

The improvement was not immediate but increased as branches became more familiar with the new procedure:

| Period | Effect Size |
|--------|-------------|
| Weeks 15-18 (early) | +1.5 points |
| Weeks 19-22 (later) | +2.8 points |

This pattern suggests continued gains as staff proficiency increases.

### **3. Effect Is Genuine, Not Driven by Other Factors**

The improvement holds after controlling for:
- Weekly staffing variations
- Visitor volume fluctuations  
- Inherent differences between branches

This confirms the procedure itself—not external factors—drove the improvement.

### **4. Rigorous Evaluation Design**

- **Sample:** 709 branches (425 adopted new procedure; 284 retained old)
- **Period:** 17 weeks of weekly data (weeks 6-22)
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences with branch fixed effects (standard causal inference approach)

---

## **IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS**

1. **Selection bias:** Branches chosen to switch showed slightly faster improvement even before the change. This may modestly inflate the estimated effect. However, the result remains statistically significant and practically meaningful.

2. **Observation window:** We have 8 weeks of post-implementation data. Continued monitoring will confirm long-term sustainability.

---

## **RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS**

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **1. Approve system-wide rollout** | Evidence supports cooperative-wide adoption |
| **2. Develop training materials** | Benefits grow with experience; accelerate learning curve |
| **3. Monitor for 3-6 months post-rollout** | Confirm sustained improvement; identify struggling branches |
| **4. Capture implementation best practices** | Interview high-performing branches to document success factors |

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data provide strong evidence that the new shelving and rotation procedure improves service quality. The board can proceed with confidence toward system-wide implementation, with appropriate support and monitoring to maximize benefits across all branches.
