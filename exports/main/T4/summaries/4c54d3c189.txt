# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.5 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically reliable and represents approximately **5% better performance** on our service quality index. The evidence strongly supports expanding the new procedure to all 150 branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Works**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (90) | 50.4 | 52.2 | **+1.8** |
| Control branches (60) | 51.6 | 50.9 | **−0.7** |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.5** |

The 2.5-point advantage for switched branches is the estimated impact of the new procedure itself, isolated from other factors like seasonal changes or overall system trends.

### **2. The Result Is Statistically Sound**

- **Confidence level:** 99%+ (p < 0.001)
- **Confidence interval:** The true effect is very likely between 1.2 and 3.8 points
- **Validation:** Both groups followed parallel trends before the switch, confirming the comparison is valid

### **3. The Improvement Is Meaningful**

- Average service score across the system: **51 points**
- A 2.5-point gain moves a typical branch from median performance to approximately the **60th percentile**
- The effect persisted consistently across all 8 weeks after implementation

### **4. Results Hold After Accounting for Other Factors**

The analysis controlled for:
- Weekly staffing levels
- Visitor volume
- Seasonal variation

The procedure effect remained significant after adjusting for all of these variables.
