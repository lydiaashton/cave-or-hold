# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Yes, the new procedure improves service scores.** Branches that switched showed a **+2 point increase** compared to those that kept the old method. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout**, with continued monitoring to confirm long-term benefits.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Effect on service score** | +2.07 points |
| **Statistical confidence** | >99% (p < 0.001) |
| **Branches studied** | 709 (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| **Baseline score** | ~50 points |

### What This Means
- A typical branch moving to the new procedure can expect its service score to rise from ~50 to ~52
- This is a **meaningful improvement**—roughly equivalent to moving from the 50th to the 55th percentile
- The effect remained stable across all 8 weeks after the switch, with no signs of fading

### Controls and Validation
The analysis accounted for:
- Weekly staffing variations
- Visitor volume changes
- Branch-specific characteristics
- Pre-existing trends between groups

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

**Rationale:**
1. **Positive effect confirmed** — The +2 point gain is both statistically significant and operationally meaningful
2. **Consistent performance** — Benefits appeared immediately and persisted throughout the study period
3. **Low risk** — 425 branches have already adopted the procedure successfully; no negative outcomes detected
4. **Equity consideration** — Delaying rollout denies remaining branches a proven improvement

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
