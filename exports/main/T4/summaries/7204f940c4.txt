# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.3 points higher** (a **4.5% improvement**) than branches that kept the old procedure. The evidence is strong and statistically significant. **We recommend immediate approval to roll out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Numbers Speak Clearly

| Metric | Control Branches (Old Procedure) | Switched Branches (New Procedure) |
|--------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Before Week 15** | 52.53 | 51.83 |
| **After Week 15** | 53.21 | 54.86 |
| **Change** | +0.68 | +3.02 |

**Net effect of the new procedure: +2.34 points (4.5% improvement)**

### Why We're Confident in This Result

1. **Strong research design:** We compared 425 branches that switched against 284 that didn't, before and after the change. This isolates the procedure's effect from other factors.

2. **Clean pre-switch trends:** Before Week 15, both groups moved in parallel—no hidden differences that would bias the result.

3. **Effect is real and stable:** The improvement appeared immediately after the switch and held steady across all 8 post-switch weeks.

4. **Statistically solid:** The result is significant at p < 0.001. We can be highly confident this isn't random noise.

5. **Holds up under scrutiny:** The effect persists even after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The data confirms what we hoped: the new rotation and shelving procedure delivers meaningful service improvements. With 425 branches already successfully using the new method, we have both the evidence and the operational experience to move forward.

## **Questions the Board May Have**

**Q: Could something else explain the improvement?**  
A: Unlikely. The control branches experienced the same external conditions (season, staffing changes, visitor patterns) but showed only modest gains. The 2.3-point gap is attributable to the procedure.

**Q: Will the effect last?**  
A: The 8-week post-switch window shows stable effects with no decay. We recommend continued monitoring, but there's no signal of diminishing returns.

**Q: What if a branch struggles with the transition?**  
A: The 425 early-adopter branches provide a base of experience. We should capture their lessons learned and make them available during rollout.
