# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**RE:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**DATE:** January 2026

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that switched to the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved **significantly higher service scores** than branches that kept the old procedure. We recommend **immediate system-wide rollout.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Estimated Impact** | +1.9 points per branch (≈4% improvement) |
| **Statistical Confidence** | 95% certainty the effect is between +1.3 and +2.4 points |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 total (425 new procedure, 284 old procedure) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Service Score Improvements

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** | 49.7 | 54.8 | **+5.0** |
| **Old Procedure** | 49.2 | 52.4 | +3.2 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.9** ✓ |

Both groups improved after week 15 (likely due to seasonal factors), but branches using the new procedure improved **an additional 1.9 points** beyond what the control group achieved.

### Why We're Confident

1. **Strong statistical evidence** (p < 0.001) — the result is highly unlikely to be due to chance
2. **Consistent across time** — the benefit persisted in all 8 weeks after the switch
3. **Robust to controls** — the effect holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
4. **Large sample** — 709 branches over 17 weeks provides substantial statistical power

---

## **WHAT THIS MEANS**

- **Service quality improves:** Patrons at branches using the new procedure experience measurably better service
- **Scalable benefit:** Rolling out to all remaining branches should yield similar gains system-wide
- **Cost-effective:** This is a procedural change, not a major capital investment

---

## **RISKS & MITIGATIONS**

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data confirms what we expected: **the new procedure improves service.** With strong statistical evidence and consistent results across 425 branches, we have the confidence to recommend full rollout. This is a clear opportunity to elevate service quality across our entire cooperative.
