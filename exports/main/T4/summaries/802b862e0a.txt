# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**Subject:** New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure – Impact Assessment  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation procedure **improves service scores**. Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores **1.7 points higher** than would have been expected under the old procedure. This represents a meaningful **3.3% improvement** and is statistically robust. We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Clear Positive Impact on Service Quality

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 51.8 | 55.6 | +3.8 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 50.4 | 52.5 | +2.0 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.7** |

- The 1.7-point improvement is **highly statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- Effect remains consistent when controlling for staffing levels, visit volumes, and branch-specific factors

### 2. Benefits Accumulate Over Time

The improvement is not immediate—it grows as branches gain experience with the new procedure:

- **Weeks 1-2 after switch:** ~1.0 point improvement
- **Weeks 7-8 after switch:** ~2.4 point improvement

This indicates branches need time to fully implement and realize the benefits of the new procedure.

### 3. Results Are Robust

Multiple analytical approaches confirm the same conclusion:

- Basic comparison of before/after scores
- Difference-in-differences regression
- Models with branch fixed effects (controlling for inherent branch differences)
- Models with week fixed effects (controlling for system-wide trends)

All methods estimate the effect between **1.7 and 1.8 points**.

---

## **Recommendations**

### Proceed with Rollout

| Action | Timeline | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **1. Begin phased rollout** to remaining branches | Q1 2027 | Operations |
| **2. Develop implementation guide** based on high-performing branches | 60 days | Operations + Training |
| **3. Provide staff training** at each cohort before switch | Ongoing | Human Resources |
| **4. Monitor service scores weekly** during rollout | Ongoing | Data Office |
| **5. Report progress to Board** quarterly | Quarterly | Director |

### Implementation Approach

- **Roll out in cohorts** of 50-75 branches per month to ensure adequate training and support
- **Prioritize branches** with capacity for smooth transition (consider staffing levels and current workload)
- **Allow 8-10 weeks** for each branch to realize full benefits before evaluating performance

---

## **Risk Mitigation**

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Implementation variability | Standardized training materials and checklists |
| Temporary disruption during transition | Schedule switches during lower-traffic periods |
| Uneven adoption across branches | Assign implementation champions at each branch |
| Benefits not realized in all contexts | Monitor closely; provide additional support where needed |

---

## **Expected Outcomes**

If rolled out system-wide, the new procedure is projected to:

- Raise **average service scores from ~52.7 to ~54.4**
- Improve **patron satisfaction** across the cooperative
- Create **consistent service quality** regardless of branch location

---

## **Conclusion**

The data confirms what the Board anticipated: the new stock rotation procedure improves service quality. The effect is statistically significant, grows over time, and holds across multiple analytical approaches. With proper implementation support, a system-wide rollout will deliver measurable benefits to patrons across all branches.

**We recommend approval to proceed with full rollout beginning Q1 2027.**

---

*Analysis based on 12,053 weekly observations from 709 branches over 17 weeks. Statistical methods: Difference-in-Differences regression with fixed effects.*
