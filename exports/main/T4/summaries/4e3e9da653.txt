# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.**

Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** on the service score index and is statistically significant (p = 0.001).

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** participated: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 continued with the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** collected (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring in week 15
- Rigorous difference-in-differences analysis compared changes between the two groups

### Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| New Procedure (90 branches) | 51.7 | 53.5 | +1.8 |
| Old Procedure (60 branches) | 50.2 | 50.1 | −0.1 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.0 points** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 0.8 to 3.1 points
- **p-value:** 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Effect Size:** Moderate (Cohen's d = 0.56)

### Validity Checks Passed
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, supporting causal interpretation
- Results are robust to controlling for staffing levels and patron visits
- Effect is consistent across branches with different baseline performance

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve immediate system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining 60 branches.

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Implement rollout** to remaining branches with clear timeline and training support

2. **Monitor outcomes** during implementation:
   - Track service scores for newly switched branches
   - Flag any branches not achieving expected improvements for additional support

3. **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** if the new procedure requires additional resources

4. **Continue data collection** to assess long-term sustainability of improvements
