# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Title: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new stock rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.95 points higher** than branches that retained the old procedure. This represents a meaningful 4% improvement and is statistically robust. We recommend **approving immediate system-wide rollout** to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence

| Metric | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| Branches in group | 425 | 284 |
| Average score before week 15 | 51.9 | 51.2 |
| Average score after week 15 | 53.3 | 50.5 |
| **Change** | **+1.3** | **-0.6** |

**Net improvement attributable to new procedure: +1.95 points**

### Why We're Confident

1. **Strong statistical evidence:** The effect is highly significant (p < 0.001), with a 95% confidence interval of 1.4 to 2.5 points. This means we can be very confident the true effect is positive and meaningful.

2. **Consistent over time:** The improvement was sustained across all 8 weeks after the switch, with no sign of fading. In fact, the gap widened slightly in later weeks (from ~2.2 to ~3.0 points), suggesting staff may become more effective with the new procedure over time.

3. **Robust to other factors:** The result holds when accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume, confirming the improvement is due to the procedure change itself.

4. **Large, representative sample:** 709 branches across 17 weeks provides substantial evidence for decision-making.

### What This Means in Practice

A 2-point gain on a ~52-point scale translates to noticeably better patron experience across the system. For context, this improvement is comparable to the difference between a below-average and average-performing branch.

### Success Metrics

We will consider the rollout successful if:
- System-wide service scores increase by at least 1.5 points within 8 weeks
- No branch experiences a sustained decline in scores
- Staff feedback on the new procedure remains positive

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve the rollout.** The data clearly supports expanding the new procedure to all branches. The improvement is real, meaningful, and achieved without additional staffing or resources. Delaying implementation means foregoing measurable service improvements for our patrons.
