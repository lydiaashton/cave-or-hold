# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new procedure saw a statistically significant improvement of approximately **1.9 points** on the service score index compared to branches that kept the old procedure. Given that the average branch scores around 50, this represents a meaningful **4% gain**. We recommend proceeding with the system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22, 2026)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure
- The switch occurred in **week 15**, providing comparable before-and-after data for both groups

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6–14) | After Switch (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 45.7 | 48.1 | +2.4 |
| Non-switched branches | 49.5 | 50.0 | +0.5 |
| **Net improvement** | | | **+1.9** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- The **1.9-point improvement** is statistically significant (p = 0.026)
- **95% confidence interval:** 0.2 to 3.5 points
- Results are robust after accounting for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week variation
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, supporting the validity of the comparison

### **Important Context**
- Switched branches started with lower baseline scores, but the analysis fully accounts for this difference
- The improvement cannot be explained by changes in staffing levels or visitor volume
- Benefits appeared within the first 8 weeks after implementation

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Implement rollout** — Extend the new procedure to the remaining 14 branches.

2. **Monitor during implementation** — Track service scores weekly during rollout to ensure consistent results.

3. **Provide clear documentation** — Ensure all branches receive standardized guidance on the new procedure.

4. **Follow-up evaluation** — Conduct a follow-up analysis after 6–12 months to confirm sustained improvement and identify any branches needing additional support.

---

**Analysis method:** Difference-in-Differences regression with branch and week fixed effects, robust standard errors.
