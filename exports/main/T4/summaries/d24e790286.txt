# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out to all branches.**

Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced service score increases of **1.8 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.3 to 2.4) beyond what would have occurred otherwise. This represents a **3.7% improvement** over baseline performance and is statistically significant (p < 0.001).

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches analyzed | 709 |
| Treatment branches (new procedure) | 425 |
| Control branches (old procedure) | 284 |
| Study period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22, 2026) |
| Switch date | Week 15 |

### Results Summary

| Metric | Treatment Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| Average score before switch | 49.7 | 49.2 |
| Average score after switch | 54.8 | 52.4 |
| **Change** | **+5.0** | **+3.2** |

Both groups improved after week 15 (likely seasonal effects), but treatment branches improved **1.8 points more**. This difference is the effect of the new procedure.

### Statistical Confidence
- **Estimated effect:** +1.84 points
- **95% confidence interval:** [1.28, 2.40]
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Methodology:** Difference-in-differences with cluster-robust standard errors

### Robustness
The finding holds under multiple checks:
- Excluding week 14 (which showed unusual pre-switch movement): effect = +2.1 points
- Controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume: effect unchanged
- Consistent positive effects across all 8 post-switch weeks

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

### Recommended Actions

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | **Approve system-wide rollout** to remaining 284 branches | Immediate |
| 2 | Develop implementation timeline and training materials | 2–4 weeks |
| 3 | Monitor service scores during rollout | Ongoing |
| 4 | Conduct cost-benefit analysis (staff time, patron satisfaction) | 60 days |

### Considerations

- **Implementation support:** Document best practices from the 425 early-adopter branches to assist training
- **Monitoring track:** Continue weekly service score tracking for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effects persist
- **Complementary metrics:** Consider adding patron satisfaction surveys to capture qualitative benefits

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data provide strong evidence that the new stock rotation and shelving procedure improves branch service performance. The 1.8-point gain is statistically robust and practically meaningful. We recommend the Board approve immediate rollout to all remaining branches.
