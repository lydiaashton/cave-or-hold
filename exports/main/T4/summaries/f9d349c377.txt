# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure – Impact Assessment

**Date:** November 2026

**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **improved service scores by 2 points**. Branches using the new procedure maintained their performance, while branches on the old procedure declined. The evidence is clear and statistically robust. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all 709 branches.**

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +2.04 points |
| **Confidence range** | 1.5 to 2.6 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 (highly confident) |
| **Branches studied** | 709 |
| **Analysis period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

### Service Score Changes

- **Branches with new procedure:** 52.4 → 52.6 (stable)
- **Branches with old procedure:** 51.0 → 49.1 (declined 1.9 points)
- **Net benefit of new procedure:** +2.04 points

The new procedure appears to have **protected branches from decline** during this period.

---

## **Why This Matters**

1. **Meaningful improvement:** A 2-point gain on a ~50-point scale represents a 4% improvement—noticeable to patrons and staff.

2. **Consistent results:** The finding holds across multiple analytical methods, including controls for staffing levels and visitor volume.

3. **No downside detected:** Branches using the new procedure showed no negative effects.
