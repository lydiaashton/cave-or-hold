# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 2025
**Subject:** Results of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Pilot

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **successfully improved service scores**. Branches that adopted the new procedure gained approximately **2 points** on the service score index—a **4% improvement**—compared to branches that retained the old procedure. The evidence supports a **system-wide rollout**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Numbers

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Branches in study | 709 (425 new procedure; 284 old procedure) |
| Study period | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22, 2025) |
| Service score improvement | **+2.07 points** (95% CI: +1.54 to +2.60) |
| Statistical confidence | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| Effect size | **4% improvement** relative to baseline |

### What the Data Shows

- **Treatment branches improved**: Average service score rose from 50.96 to 52.55 (+1.59 points)
- **Control branches declined slightly**: Average service score fell from 49.85 to 49.37 (-0.48 points)
- **The gap widened by 2 points** after the switch, attributable to the new procedure

### Effect Timeline

The improvement was not immediate. Service scores began showing significant gains approximately **3 weeks after implementation** (week 18), with effects strengthening through week 22. This pattern is consistent with staff learning and adapting to new workflows.

### What Did *Not* Drive the Result

The improvement was **not** explained by changes in staffing levels or branch visitation—both remained stable across treatment and control groups. The procedure itself produced the gain.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide implementation** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure across all remaining 284 branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Owner |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Approve rollout budget and timeline | Next board meeting | Board |
| Develop branch training materials | 4 weeks | Operations |

---

## **Risk Mitigation**

- **Allow 3–4 weeks per branch** for staff adaptation before expecting full benefits
- **Provide enhanced support** during the first month of implementation at each branch
- **Track leading indicators** (staff feedback, processing time) alongside service scores

---

## **Conclusion**

The pilot confirms what the cooperative anticipated: the new procedure delivers measurable service improvements. With 425 branches already successfully operating under the new system and a clear 2-point service score gain documented, the path forward is clear. Full rollout will raise service quality across the entire cooperative.
