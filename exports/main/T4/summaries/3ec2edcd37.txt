# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We recommend proceeding with the new stock rotation procedure, but with continued monitoring.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed service score improvements of approximately **2 points** (about 4% above average) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant. However, because branches self-selected into the new procedure, we cannot rule out that some of this difference reflects pre-existing differences between branches rather than the procedure itself.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The New Procedure Is Associated with Better Service Scores**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Estimated improvement | **+1.9 points** on service score index |
| Confidence range | +1.3 to +2.4 points |
| Relative to average score (51) | **+3.7%** |
| Statistical confidence | **High** (p < 0.001) |

### **Context Matters**

- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** kept the old procedure
- The switch occurred in **week 15** of the study period
- Service scores system-wide showed some decline over the study period; switched branches declined **more slowly** than non-switched branches

### **Important Limitation**

Branches that chose to switch were already showing different performance trends before the switch. This means our estimate may include some pre-existing differences between branches, not just the effect of the new procedure. The true effect is likely **positive but may be smaller** than our 1.9-point estimate.

---

## **Recommendations**

### **1. Approve System-Wide Rollout**
The evidence supports expanding the new procedure to all branches. Even if the true effect is at the lower end of our confidence interval (~1.3 points), this represents meaningful service improvement.

### **2. Implement Phased Rollout**
Roll out to remaining branches in waves over the next quarter. This allows for:
- Operational learning and adjustment
- Identification of any implementation challenges
- Continued monitoring of effects

### **3. Establish Ongoing Monitoring**
Track service scores for 12 weeks post-rollout to confirm:
- Effects persist at the system level
- No unintended consequences emerge
- All branches achieve similar benefits

### **4. Document Best Practices**
Survey early-adopter branches to capture:
- Successful implementation strategies
- Staff training approaches
- Any adaptations that improved outcomes

---

## **Questions for Discussion**

1. What is the timeline for full rollout?
2. What resources are needed for branch training and support?
3. Should we establish a formal evaluation checkpoint at 90 days post-rollout?
