# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2.1 points higher** (a 4% improvement) than branches that retained the old procedure. This finding is statistically robust. **We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Analysis Overview
- **150 branches** participated: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 retained the old
- **17 weeks** of data collected (weeks 6-22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- Method: Difference-in-differences analysis comparing score changes between groups

### Results Summary

| Group | Before (Weeks 6-14) | After (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 53.6 | 54.4 | +0.8 |
| Branches that did not switch | 53.3 | 51.9 | -1.3 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Effect size:** +2.11 points on the service score index
- **95% confidence interval:** 0.89 to 3.33 points (entirely positive)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.0004 (highly significant)
- **Robustness:** Effect persists after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Validity Assurance
- Pre-switch trends were statistically similar between groups, confirming the comparison is valid
- Standard errors account for repeated measurements per branch
- Effect size represents meaningful improvement relative to the baseline average of ~53

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new rotation and shelving procedure across all 150 branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Roll out** the new procedure to remaining 60 branches within the next quarter
2. **Monitor** service scores for 8-12 weeks post-implementation to confirm sustained improvement
3. **Capture** implementation learnings from the 90 early-adopter branches
4. **Report back** to the board on rollout progress and outcomes
