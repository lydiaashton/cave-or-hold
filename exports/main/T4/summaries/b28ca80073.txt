# **Memorandum to the Board**

## **Subject: Impact of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores**

---

### **Bottom Line**

Contrary to initial expectations, the new shelving and stock rotation procedure **produced a measurable improvement** in branch service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure saw scores increase by **2.0 points** (approximately 4%) relative to branches that retained the old procedure. The evidence supports proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

### **Key Findings**

**What We Studied**
- 150 branches over 17 weeks (February through June)
- 90 branches implemented the new procedure starting week 15; 60 branches continued with the existing procedure
- Service scores are calculated weekly by our office (scale average ~50; higher is better)

**What We Found**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 48.55 | 51.72 | +3.17 |
| Branches that did not switch | 47.24 | 48.35 | +1.12 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.04** |

- **Estimated improvement: +2.04 points** on the service score index
- **Confidence interval: 0.72 to 3.37 points** (95% confidence)
- **Statistical significance: p = 0.003** — the result is unlikely to be due to chance

**Why We Trust This Result**
- Branches that switched and those that did not were trending similarly before the change, which validates the comparison
- The effect appeared consistently across all post-switch weeks
- The analysis accounts for branch-specific factors and week-to-week variation

---

### **Recommendation**

**Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.** The data indicate a real, positive effect on service quality that was not anticipated. This represents an opportunity to improve patron experience across the cooperative without additional investment.

---

### **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide implementation** of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure

2. **Develop a rollout timeline** for the remaining 60 branches, with target completion within 8–10 weeks

3. **Continue monitoring service scores** post-rollout to confirm the effect holds at full scale

4. **Document the procedure** and provide training materials to ensure consistent implementation

5. **Revisit in one quarter** to assess sustained impact and any operational considerations
