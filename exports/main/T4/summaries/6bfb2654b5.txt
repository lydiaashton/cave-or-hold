# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **shows promising improvement** in branch service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by approximately **2.6 points (6%)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. While the evidence is not statistically definitive, the consistent positive direction across all post-switch weeks supports moving forward with a **phased rollout**, accompanied by continued monitoring.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Measured Impact**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches using **new procedure** | 46.88 | 49.41 | +2.54 |
| Branches using **old procedure** | 44.94 | 44.92 | -0.02 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.56** |

- The new procedure is associated with a **2.6-point improvement** in service scores
- This represents approximately **6% improvement** relative to baseline scores
- The control group's flat performance confirms the improvement is not due to systemwide factors

### **2. Confidence in the Result**

- **Statistical confidence:** The finding is *suggestive but not definitive* (90% confidence level)
- **Range of likely effects:** The true improvement could reasonably be anywhere from **no effect to 5.7 points**
- **Consistency:** Every week after the switch showed a positive gap between treatment and control branches

### **3. Analysis Validity**

- ✓ **Comparable groups:** Both groups showed similar trends before the switch
- ✓ **No confounding factors:** Control branches showed no improvement, ruling out systemwide changes
- ✓ **Robust to controls:** Results unchanged when accounting for staffing and visitor traffic

---

## **RECOMMENDATIONS**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Proceed with phased rollout** to remaining branches | Positive direction aligns with expectations; low risk of harm |
| **2** | **Continue monitoring** service scores for 8-12 additional weeks | Will increase statistical confidence and narrow uncertainty |
| **3** | **Track implementation fidelity** at each branch | Ensures the procedure is being applied as designed |
| **4** | **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** | Confirm operational costs are justified by service improvements |
| **5** | **Investigate branch-level variation** | Identify whether certain branch types benefit more than others |

## **TECHNICAL NOTES**

- **Method:** Difference-in-Differences regression with branch-clustered standard errors
- **Data:** 34 branches, 578 weekly observations (weeks 6-22)
- **Treatment group:** 20 branches switched at week 15
- **Control group:** 14 branches maintained existing procedure
- **Software:** Python with statsmodels
