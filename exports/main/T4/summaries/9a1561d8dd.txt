# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks of data)

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.25 points** (approximately **4.5%**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This finding is statistically significant and contradicts our initial expectation that the change would have no effect.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout to all branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Clear Positive Impact on Service Quality**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches (90) | 50.45 | 53.11 | +2.66 |
| Non-Switched Branches (60) | 49.65 | 50.12 | +0.47 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.25** |

- **Statistical confidence:** 95% confidence interval [1.0 to 3.5 points]
- **Significance level:** p = 0.0005 (highly significant)
- **Relative improvement:** 4.5% above baseline

### **2. Rigorous Analysis Method**

We used a **difference-in-differences** design that isolates the procedure's effect from other factors:
- Compares 90 treatment branches against 60 control branches
- Controls for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume
- Validates that both groups followed similar trends before the switch (parallel trends test: p = 0.78)

### **3. Results Are Robust**

- Effect remains significant after controlling for staffing and visits
- Improvement visible consistently across the post-switch period
- No evidence of negative side effects on other operational measures
