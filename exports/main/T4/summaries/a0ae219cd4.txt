# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.** Although the new rotation and shelving procedure was expected to have no impact on service scores, branches that adopted it experienced a **statistically significant improvement of 1.8 points** (p < 0.01) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents a **3.6% gain** in service quality at no anticipated cost to operations.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Found

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 52.21 | 53.37 | +1.16 |
| Branches with old procedure | 48.73 | 47.98 | −0.75 |
| **Net effect (Difference-in-Differences)** | | | **+1.91** |

After adjusting for staffing levels, visit volumes, and branch-specific factors, the estimated improvement is **+1.82 points** (95% confidence interval: 0.75 to 2.88).

### Why We Trust This Result

- **Comparable groups**: Before the switch, both groups followed parallel trends in service scores
- **Controls applied**: Results account for weekly fluctuations in staffing and visitor traffic
- **Statistically robust**: The finding is significant at the 0.1% level (p = 0.0008)
- **Meaningful magnitude**: The effect equals roughly 18% of one standard deviation in service scores

### What This Means

The new procedure was implemented as an operational change with **no expectation of affecting service quality**. Instead, it delivered a **measurable service improvement** as a bonus. This suggests the new rotation and shelving method may help staff locate and serve materials more efficiently, even though that was not the intended outcome.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Roll out the new procedure to all branches.** The evidence indicates:

1. **No downside**: The change was not expected to affect service scores, and we find no negative effects
2. **Clear upside**: Service scores improved by approximately 1.8 points where implemented
3. **Low risk**: The procedure is already operational in 90 branches (60% of the system)
