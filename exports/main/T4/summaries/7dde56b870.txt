# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks of data)

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure gained approximately **1.9 points** on the service score index compared to branches that maintained the old procedure.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 49.99 | 53.01 | +3.02 |
| Branches with old procedure | 49.09 | 50.19 | +1.10 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.92** |

### Statistical Confidence

- **Effect size:** +1.9 points (95% confidence interval: 1.2 to 2.7 points)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Practical significance:** ~4% improvement on a ~50-point scale

### Analysis Strength

- **709 branches** across the cooperative (425 treatment, 284 control)
- **12,053 branch-week observations**
- **Validated methodology:** Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, confirming the comparison is sound
- **Robust to controls:** Effect holds when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve full rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

The evidence is clear and actionable:
1. The effect is **real** (statistically significant)
2. The effect is **meaningful** (~4% improvement)
3. The effect is **reliable** (validated across multiple model specifications)
