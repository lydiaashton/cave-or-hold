# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** Analysis of Weeks 6–22 Service Score Data

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than would have been expected under the old procedure. This represents a **3.8% improvement** and is statistically significant (p < 0.001). We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.59 | 52.70 | +1.11 |
| Control branches | 51.24 | 50.40 | −0.84 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.94** |

- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** served as controls
- Effect is **robust** — unchanged when accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Effect size equals **0.20 standard deviations** — a meaningful operational improvement

### **Why This Matters**

While control branches saw service scores decline after week 15 (−0.84 points), switched branches improved (+1.11 points). The new procedure appears to **protect against performance decay** while delivering modest gains.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The analysis confirms the procedure change delivers the expected improvement in service quality. The 1.9-point gain, while modest in percentage terms, represents a real improvement in patron experience across the cooperative.
