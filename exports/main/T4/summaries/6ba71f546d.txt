# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation procedure experienced a **1.9-point increase** in service scores (approximately **4% improvement**) compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and consistent across analytical methods.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Analyzed**
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores average ~50 points across the system (higher is better)

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| New Procedure (425 branches) | 51.6 | 52.4 | **+0.7** |
| Old Procedure (284 branches) | 50.2 | 49.0 | **−1.2** |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.9 points** |

The control group's scores declined after week 15 while treatment group scores rose. This suggests the new procedure both **improves performance** and **protects against declines** that affected branches using the old procedure.

### **Confidence in the Result**
- **Statistical certainty:** The 1.9-point effect is highly significant (p < 0.001)
- **Precision:** We are 95% confident the true effect lies between **1.3 and 2.4 points**
- **Robustness:** The finding holds after accounting for seasonal patterns, branch differences, staffing levels, and visitor volume
