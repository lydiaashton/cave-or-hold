# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.**

Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure experienced service score increases of approximately **2.0 points** compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p < 0.01) and represents roughly a **4% improvement** on the service score scale. The evidence supports approving a full rollout to all remaining branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches analyzed** | 150 total (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| **Time period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences comparison |

### **2. Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|:-------------:|:------------:|:------:|
| Control (old procedure) | 48.55 | 50.30 | +1.75 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 49.46 | 53.24 | +3.78 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.03** |

- **Estimated effect:** +2.03 points (95% CI: 0.82 to 3.25)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.001
- **Result holds** after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### **3. Analysis Validity**

✓ **Parallel trends confirmed** — Treatment and control groups followed similar trajectories before the switch (pre-trend test p = 0.19)

✓ **Effect is consistent** — The treatment-control gap widened after week 15 and remained elevated throughout the post-period (ranging from 2.2 to 3.7 points)

✓ **Robust to controls** — Including staffing and visits indices does not materially change the estimated effect (+2.18 points, p = 0.001)

### **4. Practical Significance**

- A 2-point gain on a ~50-point scale equals approximately **4% improvement** in service quality
- Improvement was sustained across all 8 post-switch weeks observed
- Effect size is meaningful for patron experience across the cooperative
