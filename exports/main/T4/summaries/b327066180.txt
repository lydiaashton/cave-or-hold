# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 15, 2026
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2.15 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure. This improvement is statistically reliable and represents a **4% gain** in service quality. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Branches studied | 709 (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| Study period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22 of the year) |
| Estimated improvement from new procedure | **+2.15 service score points** |
| Statistical confidence | Greater than 99% (p < 0.001) |
| Likely range of true effect | 1.6 to 2.7 points |

### **What the Data Shows**

1. **Before the switch** (weeks 6–14), branches that would later adopt the new procedure had virtually identical service scores to those that would not. This confirms the two groups were comparable at the start.

2. **After the switch** (weeks 15–22), service scores declined across the entire cooperative for reasons unrelated to the procedure change. However:
   - Branches keeping the **old procedure** saw scores drop by about **3.0 points**
   - Branches using the **new procedure** saw scores drop by only **0.8 points**

3. **The new procedure protected service quality.** Rather than producing an absolute increase, it shielded branches from the broader decline affecting the cooperative.

### **Why We Are Confident**

- The analysis accounts for differences between branches and over time
- Results remain consistent after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- The effect is large enough that random chance is an extremely unlikely explanation

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches |
| **2** | **Investigate the cooperative-wide decline** in service scores after week 15 to identify and address external factors |
| **3** | **Monitor implementation quality** to ensure consistent adoption across all branches |
| **4** | **Continue tracking service scores** for 8–12 weeks after full rollout to confirm sustained improvement |

---

## **Bottom Line for Decision-Making**

The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure. The 2.15-point improvement is meaningful for patron experience, the statistical evidence is strong, and the procedure appears to build resilience against service quality declines. Any implementation costs should be weighed against a 4% improvement in service scores across 709 branches.
