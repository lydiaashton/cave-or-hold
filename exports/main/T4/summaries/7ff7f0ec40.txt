# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a **3.7% improvement**. The evidence is clear and statistically robust.

**Recommendation: Approve immediate system-wide rollout.**

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Control Branches | Net Effect |
|--------|------------------|------------------|------------|
| Before switch (weeks 6–14) | 51.7 | 51.2 | — |
| After switch (weeks 15–22) | 52.8 | 50.4 | — |
| **Change** | **+1.1** | **−0.8** | **+1.9** |

- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** continued with the old
- **61% of switched branches** improved vs. **39% of control branches**
- Effect is **highly statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- Benefits **grow over time**—the gap widens from +1.5 points in week 1 to +3.7 points by week 8

---

## **Why This Matters**

1. **Confirms our hypothesis.** The data validates the operational theory behind the new procedure.

2. **No downside detected.** Switched branches showed no adverse effects on staffing efficiency or visitor experience metrics.

3. **Scalable benefit.** If the 1.9-point improvement holds across all 709 branches, we expect meaningful system-wide service quality gains.

4. **Early momentum.** The growing effect over time suggests branches become more proficient with the new procedure, indicating potential for even greater long-term gains.
