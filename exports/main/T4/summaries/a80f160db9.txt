# **Board Summary: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure is associated with a **statistically significant improvement in service scores of approximately 2 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.4 to 2.5). This represents a modest but meaningful gain of about 4% relative to the average service score.

However, branches that adopted the new procedure were already outperforming and improving faster than other branches before the switch. This suggests the true causal effect may be smaller than our estimate.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** while implementing stronger monitoring and evaluation to confirm results.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measured Impact
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | **+1.94 points** |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| 95% Confidence interval | 1.4 to 2.5 points |
| Effect size | 0.19 standard deviations |

### 2. What This Means in Practice
- A branch at the average service score (50.3) would improve to approximately **52.2**
- This moves a typical branch from the 50th to roughly the 54th percentile of performance
- The effect is **real and measurable**, but not transformative

### 3. Performance Trends
| Period | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches | Gap Between Groups |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Before switch (weeks 6-14) | 50.75 | 48.85 | 1.91 points |
| After switch (weeks 15-22) | 52.14 | 48.27 | 3.87 points |
| **Change** | **+1.39** | **-0.58** | **+1.96 points** |

### 4. Important Limitation
Switched branches showed **stronger improvement trends even before adopting the procedure**. The gap between groups grew from 0.2 points in week 6 to 2.6 points by week 14 (before the switch). This indicates that branches selected for early adoption may have had underlying advantages—such as better management or more engaged staff—that contributed to their performance independent of the procedure itself.

**The actual benefit of the procedure alone is likely smaller than 2 points.**

---

## **Next Steps**

### For Board Approval
1. **Authorize phased rollout** to additional branches over the next 6-12 months
2. **Allocate resources** for implementation support and staff training at converting branches
3. **Set expectations** that a 1-2 point service score improvement is the realistic target

### For Management
4. **Document implementation best practices** from early-adopter branches
5. **Create a monitoring dashboard** to track service scores at newly converted branches
6. **Establish a 6-month review checkpoint** to assess whether results match expectations
7. **Consider randomized assignment** for remaining branches to enable cleaner future evaluation
8. **Gather staff feedback** on implementation challenges and success factors

---

## **Data & Methodology**

- **709 branches** analyzed over 17 weeks (weeks 6-22 of the year)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- The switch occurred in **week 15**
- Analysis used **difference-in-differences regression** with cluster-robust standard errors
- Results controlled for staffing levels and visitor volume
