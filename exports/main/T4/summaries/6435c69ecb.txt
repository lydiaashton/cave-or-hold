# Memorandum to the Board

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 2026

---

## Bottom Line

The new procedure shows a **positive direction of effect** on service scores, but the evidence is **not strong enough to confirm** that the improvement is real rather than due to random variation. We recommend **continuing the pilot while expanding data collection** before committing to a full rollout.

---

## Key Findings

### What the Data Shows

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect on service score | **+1.6 points** |
| Baseline average score | 51.1 |
| Relative improvement | ~3% |
| Statistical confidence | **Not significant** (p = 0.14) |
| 95% confidence interval | **-0.5 to +3.8 points** |

### Interpretation

1. **The effect points in the expected direction.** Branches that switched to the new procedure improved relative to those that did not, consistent with our hypothesis.

2. **We cannot rule out chance.** The confidence interval includes zero, meaning the observed improvement could plausibly be due to normal week-to-week variation in service scores.

3. **The study has limited power.** With 34 branches over 17 weeks, we can only detect fairly large effects with confidence. Smaller but meaningful improvements may go undetected.

4. **No red flags emerged.** The new procedure did not negatively affect service scores, and pre-switch trends between switching and non-switching branches were parallel.

---

## Recommendations

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | Continue the pilot for 8–12 additional weeks | More data will narrow the confidence interval and provide greater certainty |
| **2** | Expand to 5–10 additional branches | Increases sample size and statistical power |
| **3** | Conduct a cost-benefit analysis | Even if the +1.6 point effect is real, confirm it justifies operational costs |
| **4** | Plan for phased rollout contingent on results | Avoids committing system-wide before evidence is conclusive |

---

## Decision Framework

| If the true effect is... | Recommended action |
|--------------------------|-------------------|
| **Zero or negative** (lower bound of CI) | Discontinue the new procedure |
| **~1.6 points** (point estimate) | Proceed if benefits exceed costs |
| **~3.8 points** (upper bound of CI) | Strong case for full rollout |
