# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 2025
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure—a **4% improvement** that is both statistically significant and practically meaningful. We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Branches studied | 709 (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| Study period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Treatment effect | **+2.07 points** (p < 0.001) |
| 95% Confidence interval | +1.54 to +2.60 points |
| Effect size | **4% improvement** over baseline |

### What the Data Shows

- **Treatment branches improved** (+1.59 points from pre- to post-switch)
- **Control branches declined slightly** (-0.48 points over the same period)
- **The gap widened by 2 points** after the switch—this is the causal effect of the new procedure

### Implementation Pattern

The benefit was not immediate. Service scores began showing significant gains approximately **3 weeks after implementation** (week 18), suggesting staff needed time to master the new procedure. By weeks 20–22, the full effect was realized.

### What Did NOT Drive the Result

The improvement was **not** due to changes in staffing levels or visitor volume—both remained stable across groups. The procedure itself produced the gain.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining 284 branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Owner |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Approve system-wide implementation | Board meeting | Board |
| Develop rollout schedule | 2 weeks | Operations |
| Train branch staff | 4–6 weeks | Training Team |

### Implementation Notes

1. **Allow an adaptation period.** Expect 2–3 weeks before branches realize the full benefit.
2. **Track early adopters.** Identify branches that excel post-rollout and document their practices.
3. **Set success metrics.** Target: +1.5 to +2.5 point improvement in service scores within 8 weeks of implementation.

---

## **Confidence Level**

**High.** The analysis uses a rigorous quasi-experimental design (difference-in-differences) with cluster-robust standard errors. The effect is statistically significant, consistent across model specifications, and aligns with our hypothesis.
