# **BOARD SUMMARY: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new stock rotation procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. The estimated improvement is **+2.2 points** (approximately 4% of the average score), but the 95% confidence interval ranges from **−0.3 to +4.8 points**, meaning we cannot rule out no effect. The evidence is **suggestive but not definitive**.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Design**
| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Branches | 34 total (20 treatment, 14 control) |
| Time Period | 17 weeks (weeks 6-22 of the year) |
| Intervention | New procedure implemented at week 15 |
| Method | Difference-in-differences with branch fixed effects |

### **2. Service Score Results**
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (Old Procedure) | 52.45 | 51.90 | −0.55 |
| Treatment (New Procedure) | 47.83 | 49.14 | +1.31 |
| **Estimated Effect** | | | **+2.23 points** |

### **3. Statistical Assessment**
- **Point Estimate:** +2.23 points
- **95% Confidence Interval:** [−0.33, +4.78]
- **P-value:** 0.087 (marginally significant)
- **Interpretation:** The confidence interval includes zero, so the result is not statistically significant at the conventional 5% level

### **4. Effect Size Context**
- Average service score across all branches: ~50 points
- Estimated improvement: ~4.5% of average score
- Effect size: 0.25 standard deviations (small-to-moderate)

### **5. Validity Checks**
- **Parallel Trends:** Pre-switch trends show no significant differential pattern between groups, supporting the validity of the analysis
- **Weekly Variation:** Effects fluctuate week-to-week, contributing to estimation uncertainty
