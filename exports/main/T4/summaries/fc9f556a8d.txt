# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved significantly higher service scores than those that kept the old method. We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The New Procedure Improved Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches using new procedure | 50.69 | 52.45 | **+1.76** |
| Branches using old procedure | 48.96 | 48.89 | -0.07 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.86 points** |

- The **+1.86 point improvement** is statistically significant (p < 0.001)
- This represents approximately a **4% improvement** in service quality
- The 95% confidence interval is **1.30 to 2.41 points**, indicating high certainty in this estimate

### **Benefits Across All Branch Types**

The improvement was consistent regardless of a branch's starting performance:

| Baseline Performance | Improvement |
|---------------------|-------------|
| Lowest-performing branches | +1.26 points |
| Mid-performing branches | +1.66 to +2.78 points |
| Highest-performing branches | +2.42 points |

**This means the new procedure helps all branches, not just those that were struggling.**

### **Analysis Was Rigorous**

Our findings account for:
- Differences in staffing levels
- Variations in visitor volume
- Inherent differences between individual branches
- Week-to-week fluctuations in service demand

The two groups of branches showed similar trends before the switch, confirming that the improvement is attributable to the new procedure itself.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 284 branches still using the old method.**

**Expected Impact:**
- ~1.8 point average improvement in service scores across the cooperative
- Approximately 4% improvement in overall service quality
- Benefits distributed across branches of all performance levels

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Plan Rollout Timeline**
   - Develop a phased implementation schedule
   - Coordinate training for staff at remaining branches

2. **Monitor During Implementation**
   - Track service scores as each branch transitions
   - Identify and support any branches that need additional assistance

3. **Document Best Practices**
   - Capture lessons learned from the 425 branches already using the new procedure
   - Create training materials based on successful implementations

4. **Evaluate After Full Rollout**
   - Conduct a follow-up analysis 6-12 months after complete implementation
   - Assess whether improvements are sustained over time
