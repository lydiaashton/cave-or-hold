# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors
**From:** Data Analysis Office
**Date:** November 15, 2026
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.2 points higher** than branches using the old method—a **4% improvement**. The result is statistically robust and consistent across all post-switch weeks. **We recommend immediate system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Delivered Measurable Gains

| Metric | Control Branches (Old) | Switched Branches (New) |
|--------|----------------------|------------------------|
| Before switch | 50.64 | 51.57 |
| After switch | 50.84 | 53.95 |
| **Change** | +0.21 | **+2.38** |

**Net improvement attributable to new procedure: +2.17 points**

### The Result Is Statistically Sound

- **Confidence level:** 99.9% (p < 0.001)
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.5 to 2.9 points
- **Effect persists** after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### The Improvement Was Consistent

Every week after the switch showed gains:
- **Smallest weekly effect:** 2.4 points (week 19)
- **Largest weekly effect:** 3.8 points (weeks 21–22)
- **No degradation** over the 8-week observation period

### The Comparison Is Valid

Before the switch, both groups followed parallel trends (average difference: 0.9 points). After the switch, the gap widened to 3.1 points—confirming the procedure, not pre-existing differences, drove the improvement.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new rotation and shelving procedure across all 284 branches currently using the old method.
