# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure gained an average of **2.3 points** on the service score index compared to branches that retained the old procedure.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Branches studied | 709 |
| Branches using new procedure | 425 (60%) |
| Branches using old procedure | 284 (40%) |
| Study period | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| Procedure switch date | Week 15 |

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 51.83 | 54.86 | +3.02 |
| Old Procedure Branches | 52.53 | 53.21 | +0.68 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.34** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Effect size:** +2.34 points (95% confidence interval: 1.75 to 2.94)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Practical significance:** ~4.7% improvement relative to the cooperative average of 50

### **Supporting Evidence**
- Effect remained consistent after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volumes
- Improvement was sustained across all 8 post-switch weeks
- Results held when accounting for branch-specific characteristics and weekly trends

---

## **Important Context**

Branches selected for the new procedure had slightly lower service scores than control branches before the switch (average gap: 0.7 points). This suggests the new procedure may be particularly effective at helping lower-performing branches improve. The analysis accounts for this baseline difference, and the 2.3-point improvement estimate reflects the effect beyond what would be expected from natural convergence.
