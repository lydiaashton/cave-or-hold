# **Impact of New Stock Rotation Procedure on Library Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to initial expectations that the change would have no effect, branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by approximately **2.5 points (5% improvement)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically significant and robust across multiple analytical approaches.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **150 branches** total: 90 adopted the new procedure (treatment group), 60 kept the old procedure (control group)
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring in week 15
- Analysis used a **difference-in-differences** approach, which compares changes over time between the two groups while controlling for pre-existing differences

### Results Summary

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.82 | 49.64 | -0.18 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.21 | 52.55 | +2.34 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.52** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Effect size:** +2.53 service score points
- **95% confidence interval:** [1.22, 3.83] points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)
- The result holds even after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Why This Matters
- The average branch scores about 50 on the service index, so a 2.5-point gain represents a **5% improvement**
- Control branches showed no meaningful change, ruling out seasonal or system-wide factors
- The treatment and control groups followed parallel trends before the switch, validating the analysis

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 60 branches still using the old method

2. **Monitor implementation** during the transition:
   - Track service scores for newly converted branches to confirm the effect replicates
   - Watch for any operational challenges during the switchover

3. **Document best practices** from the 90 early-adopter branches to support training at remaining locations

4. **Consider additional analysis** after full rollout to assess:
   - Whether the improvement is sustained over longer time periods
   - Any cost implications or staffing impacts of the new procedure

*Analysis prepared using weekly service score data from weeks 6–22. Statistical methods account for branch-level differences and repeated observations over time.*
