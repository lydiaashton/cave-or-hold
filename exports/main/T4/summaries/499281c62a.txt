# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.3 points** (approximately 4.5% above baseline) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, consistent across the post-switch period, and robust to controls for staffing and visitor traffic.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Overview**
- **150 branches** participated: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 retained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** design, the standard method for evaluating policy changes with treatment and control groups

### **2. Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 51.81 | 49.45 | −2.36 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 51.24 | 51.19 | −0.05 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.31** |

- **Treatment effect: +2.31 points** (p < 0.001)
- Effect remains significant (+2.21 points, p = 0.001) when controlling for staffing levels and visitor traffic
- Baseline average service score across all branches: 51.0

### **3. Validation of Results**
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Event study analysis shows no significant differences between treatment and control branches in the weeks before the switch
- **Sustained effect:** The positive impact persisted consistently across all 8 post-switch weeks, with treatment branches outperforming control branches by 2–3 points weekly
- **Practical significance:** The new procedure helped switching branches maintain their performance while control branches experienced a notable decline

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve immediate system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 60 branches still using the old method.

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Implement rollout** to remaining 60 branches within the next quarter

2. **Monitor transition** over 8–12 weeks post-implementation to confirm effects persist at scale and identify branches needing additional support

3. **Investigate control group decline:** The 2.36-point drop at non-switching branches warrants attention. Prioritize rollout to branches showing the steepest declines

4. **Capture best practices** from the 90 early-adopter branches to inform training materials and support smooth implementation
