# **Board Summary: New Branch Procedure Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive effect** on service scores, with switched branches improving by approximately **1.6 points** (about 3% relative to baseline) while non-switching branches remained flat. However, with the current data, this result is **not statistically conclusive**—we cannot rule out random variation. Importantly, the benefit **grows over time**, suggesting branches need weeks to fully realize gains.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** while continuing to monitor, rather than immediate system-wide adoption or halting the program.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches studied | 34 |
| Switched to new procedure | 20 |
| Remained on old procedure (control) | 14 |
| Weeks of data | 17 (weeks 6–22) |
| Switch implemented | Week 15 |

### **Service Score Changes**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** | 50.38 | 51.96 | **+1.58** |
| **Non-switched branches** | 52.30 | 52.27 | -0.03 |

**Estimated effect of new procedure: +1.6 points**

### **Statistical Confidence**
- The 1.6-point improvement has a **95% confidence interval of approximately -0.4 to +3.7 points**
- Probability this result occurred by chance: **about 12%** (above the conventional 5% threshold)
- **Interpretation:** The evidence points positive, but more data would strengthen confidence

### **Effect Grows Over Time**
- Each week after implementation, switched branches gained an **additional 0.35 points** (statistically significant, p = 0.012)
- By week 22 (8 weeks post-switch), the cumulative benefit reached approximately **2.8 points**
- This suggests the procedure requires an **adjustment period** before full benefits are realized

### **Visual Trend**
A line chart (saved as `service_scores_trend.png`) shows:
- Both groups tracked similarly before week 15
- After week 15, switched branches trend upward while control branches remain flat
- The gap widens in the final weeks

---

## **Next Steps**

### **1. Extend Monitoring (Immediate)**
- Collect 8–12 additional weeks of data
- This will improve statistical precision and confirm whether the growing effect continues

### **2. Phased Rollout**
- Offer the new procedure to additional branches in waves
- Keep 3–5 branches on the old procedure as an ongoing control group
- Prioritize branches similar to current switchers (size, traffic patterns, staffing levels)

### **3. Capture Best Practices**
- Interview managers at high-performing switched branches
- Identify implementation factors that correlate with stronger gains
- Incorporate findings into training materials for new adopters

### **4. Decision Timeline**
| Timeline | Action |
|----------|--------|
| 3 months | Interim review with expanded data |
| 6 months | Final decision on system-wide adoption |

## **Bottom Line for Decision-Makers**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure help? | **Likely yes**, but not yet proven beyond doubt |
| Is the effect meaningful? | **Yes**—1.6 points is ~3% of average score; growing over time |
| Should we roll out everywhere now? | **Not yet**—phase the rollout and keep monitoring |
| Should we stop the new procedure? | **No**—evidence points positive, and effects strengthen over time |
