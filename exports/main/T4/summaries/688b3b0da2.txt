# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**SUBJECT:** New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure — Impact Evaluation

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that switched saw service scores rise by **1.8 points (3.4% improvement)**. The effect is statistically significant, consistent across all post-switch weeks, and holds after accounting for staffing, visitor volume, and branch differences.

**Recommendation: Roll out to all branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.81 points (p < 0.001) |
| **Confidence interval** | 1.45 to 2.18 points |
| **Relative gain** | 3.4% above baseline |
| **Branches studied** | 709 (425 switched, 284 did not) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (switch at week 15) |

### Effect Was Consistent Every Week After Switch
The improvement appeared immediately in week 15 and persisted through week 22, with no sign of fading. This suggests the benefit is durable, not a temporary novelty effect.

### Result Is Robust
The finding holds when controlling for:
- Weekly staffing variations
- Visitor volume fluctuations  
- Individual branch characteristics

---

## **WHAT THIS MEANS**

The data confirms what we expected: **the new procedure improves service quality.** At ~1.8 points per branch, system-wide rollout should lift overall cooperative service scores measurably. Given that the average branch scores around 50, this represents meaningful progress toward our service excellence goals.
