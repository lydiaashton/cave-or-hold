## **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team
**DATE:** November 15, 2026
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

### **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. Branches that switched improved by approximately **2 points** (4% of average score) relative to branches that did not, but this difference could plausibly be due to chance. We recommend **continued monitoring with a phased rollout** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

### **Key Findings**

**Study Overview**
- 34 branches tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- 20 branches adopted the new procedure starting week 15; 14 branches continued with the old procedure
- Service scores average approximately 50 points (higher is better)

**Estimated Effect**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Point estimate | +2.16 points |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.53 to +4.84 points |
| Statistical significance | Not significant at 5% level (p=0.116) |
| Effect size | ~4% of average service score |

**What This Means**
- The data suggest a **modest improvement** from the new procedure
- However, we **cannot rule out no effect** (or even a small negative effect)
- The positive direction is **consistent across all analytical methods** tested
- Later weeks show **stronger effects**, suggesting benefits may accumulate over time

**Important Context**
- The two groups of branches had different baseline service scores before the switch (treatment branches averaged ~47 vs. ~50 for control)
- Our analysis accounts for these differences, but this adds uncertainty to the estimates
- With only 34 branches and 8 weeks of post-switch data, the study has **limited power** to detect modest effects

---

### **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| Continue collecting service score data for all branches | Ongoing | Operations Team |
| Re-analyze effects with additional weeks of data | 3 months | Data Team |
| Assess operational costs of the new procedure | 6 weeks | Finance Team |
| Consider phased rollout to 5–10 additional branches | After cost review | Operations Team |
| Evaluate whether certain branch types benefit more | Next analysis cycle | Data Team |

---

### **Recommendation**

**Proceed with caution.** The evidence is promising but not definitive. We recommend:

1. **Do not mandate system-wide rollout at this time**
2. **Allow interested branches to adopt the procedure voluntarily** while continuing to track outcomes
3. **Reconvene in one quarter** with expanded data to make a more confident determination

This approach balances the potential benefits against the uncertainty in the current evidence while building a stronger foundation for a future decision.
