# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than expected—a statistically significant improvement. We recommend approving immediate rollout to all remaining branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** monitored over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **90 branches** implemented the new procedure starting week 15; **60 branches** retained the old procedure
- Service scores average ~50 points across the system (higher is better)

### Results Summary

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 51.28 | 51.53 | +0.25 |
| Branches with old procedure | 49.63 | 47.97 | −1.66 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.91** |

### What This Means
The new procedure delivered two benefits:
1. **Direct improvement:** Switched branches maintained their service scores
2. **Protective effect:** The new procedure appears to shield branches from a system-wide decline that affected non-switched branches

### Statistical Confidence
- **Estimated effect:** +1.91 points
- **95% confidence interval:** +0.74 to +3.09 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.001 (highly reliable result)
- **Assumption check passed:** Pre-intervention trends were parallel between groups, validating the analysis

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve system-wide implementation** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure across all 60 branches still using the old method.

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

| Action | Timeline | Responsible Party |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| Board approval of rollout | Immediate | Board |
| Develop implementation schedule | 2 weeks | Operations |

## **ADDITIONAL OBSERVATION**

Non-switched branches experienced a notable decline (−1.66 points) after week 15. We recommend investigating whether seasonal factors, staffing changes, or other external conditions contributed to this drop. The new procedure may offer protection against such headwinds.
