# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

The new stock rotation procedure **significantly improved service scores** at branches that adopted it. Compared to branches that kept the old procedure, switching branches saw service scores increase by **2.1 points** (approximately 4% improvement). This effect is statistically robust and grew stronger over the 8 weeks following implementation.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality

| Analysis Approach | Estimated Effect | Confidence Interval |
|-------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| Basic comparison | +2.12 points | 1.4 to 2.8 |
| With branch characteristics controlled | +2.14 points | 1.6 to 2.7 |
| With staffing and visit patterns controlled | +2.14 points | 1.6 to 2.7 |

All three approaches confirm the same conclusion: the new procedure caused a meaningful improvement.

### 2. Benefits Accumulated Over Time

The improvement was not immediate. Service scores climbed steadily as branches gained experience:

- **Weeks 1-2 after switch:** +2.0 points
- **Weeks 3-4 after switch:** +3.0 points
- **Weeks 7-8 after switch:** +4.0 points

This suggests staff needed time to master the new procedure, and full benefits emerge after several weeks.

### 3. Comparison Groups Were Well-Matched

We verified that branches assigned to switch were comparable to those that were not:

- Both groups had similar average service scores before the change (~51 points)
- Week-by-week trends before the switch were largely parallel
- Our statistical models account for any remaining differences

### 4. Scale of Impact

- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** kept the old procedure
- **12,053 weekly observations** analyzed across 17 weeks
- Average branch service score: **51 points** (higher is better)
- Expected improvement per branch: **~2 points**

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

### Immediate Actions

1. **Authorize full rollout** to remaining 284 branches currently using the old procedure

2. **Develop implementation timeline** that allows 8-10 weeks for benefits to materialize at each branch

3. **Prepare training materials** emphasizing that initial weeks may show modest gains, with full benefits emerging after one to two months

### Monitoring Plan

4. **Track service scores weekly** for all branches during rollout

5. **Flag branches** where scores do not improve within 4 weeks for additional support

6. **Document implementation practices** at high-performing branches to share as best practices

### Further Analysis

7. **Evaluate cost implications** of the new procedure (staff time, materials, etc.)

8. **Assess impact on other metrics** including staffing efficiency and patron visit satisfaction

9. **Revisit in 6 months** with full-year data to confirm long-term effects

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data provide clear evidence that the new stock rotation procedure improves service quality. The effect is statistically significant, practically meaningful, and consistent across multiple analytical approaches. We recommend proceeding with confidence toward system-wide implementation.
