# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** [Current Date]  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** (approximately **4% improvement**) than branches that retained the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and consistent across the post-switch period. The evidence supports extending the new procedure to all 150 branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Analysis Overview**
| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Branches studied | 150 total (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| Methodology | Difference-in-differences regression |
| Data points | 2,550 branch-week observations |

### **Impact on Service Scores**
| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 48.0 | 50.6 | +2.5 |
| Non-switched branches | 47.9 | 48.4 | +0.5 |
| **Net effect (DID)** | — | — | **+2.1** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Estimate:** +2.06 points
- **95% Confidence Interval:** [0.86, 3.27]
- **P-value:** 0.0008 (highly significant)
- **Interpretation:** We are 95% confident the true effect lies between 0.9 and 3.3 points

### **Validity Checks Passed**
✓ **Parallel trends confirmed:** Both groups moved together before the switch (p=0.22)  
✓ **Robust to controls:** Results unchanged when accounting for staffing and visitor volume  
✓ **No pre-existing differences:** Groups had comparable baseline scores (~48)

## **Next Steps**

### **Immediate Actions**
1. **Approve system-wide rollout** to the remaining 60 branches
2. **Develop implementation timeline** (recommend phased approach over 8–12 weeks)
3. **Prepare training materials** based on practices from the 90 early-adopter branches

### **Monitoring Plan**
| Metric | Frequency | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Service scores | Weekly | Maintain ≥50 average |
| Branch-level variance | Monthly | No branch drops >5 points |
| Staff feedback | 4 weeks post-switch | Qualitative assessment |

### **Risk Mitigation**
- Assign implementation support staff to assist branches during transition
- Flag any branch with declining scores for targeted review after 4 weeks
- Maintain helpdesk capacity for procedure-related questions

---

## **Caveats and Considerations**

- Analysis covers a 4-month window; long-term sustainability should be monitored
- Effect size is meaningful but modest; pair with complementary operational improvements
- Individual branch characteristics (size, location, staffing) may influence outcomes

---

## **Conclusion**

The new rotation and shelving procedure delivers a measurable, statistically significant improvement in service scores. With validation of the study design and robustness of results, we recommend proceeding with full implementation across the cooperative.
