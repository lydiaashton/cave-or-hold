# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by **2.2 points** (approximately **4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant and robust across multiple analytical methods.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with cooperative-wide rollout to all remaining branches.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Non-switched branches (284) | 50.6 | 50.8 | +0.2 |
| Switched branches (425) | 51.6 | 53.9 | +2.4 |
| **Net treatment effect** | | | **+2.2** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Estimated effect:** +2.17 points (95% CI: 1.59 to 2.75)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **Practical significance:** ~4.2% improvement relative to average service score of 51.9

### Study Design
- **709 total branches** across the cooperative
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6-22), with the switch implemented at week 15
- Analysis used difference-in-differences with branch and week fixed effects
- Results control for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **IMPORTANT CONTEXT**

1. **Branch selection:** The 425 branches that switched had slightly higher baseline service scores than the 284 that did not (about 1 point higher). Our statistical models account for these pre-existing differences.

2. **Trend validation:** Both groups showed similar service score trends before the switch, supporting the validity of the comparison.

3. **Observation window:** We have 8 weeks of post-switch data. The effect appears stable across this period.

---

## **RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Approve cooperative-wide rollout to remaining 284 branches | Immediate |
| 2 | Develop implementation support materials from early adopters | 2-4 weeks |
| 3 | Monitor service scores monthly post-rollout | Ongoing |
| 4 | Conduct cost-benefit analysis if procedure has different resource requirements | 4-8 weeks |
| 5 | Evaluate sustained impact at 6-month mark | 6 months |

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The evidence strongly supports expanding the new rotation and shelving procedure to all branches. The 4% improvement in service scores represents meaningful progress toward our cooperative's service quality goals, with no identified negative consequences. We recommend board approval to proceed with full implementation.
