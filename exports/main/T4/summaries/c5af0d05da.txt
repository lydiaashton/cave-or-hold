# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team  
**Data Source:** branches.csv (12,053 observations, 709 branches, 17 weeks)

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and rotation procedure improved service scores.** We recommend approving system-wide rollout to all branches.

Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved a **2.16-point increase** in service scores (approximately **4.4% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is **highly statistically significant** (p < 0.001), meaning it is very unlikely to be due to random chance.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Overview**
| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Branches Analyzed** | 709 total |
| **Treatment Group** | 425 branches (new procedure) |
| **Control Group** | 284 branches (old procedure) |
| **Study Period** | Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks) |
| **Switch Point** | Week 15 |

### **2. Impact Estimate**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Effect Size** | +2.16 service score points |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | [1.60, 2.71] |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Percentage Improvement** | 4.4% over baseline |

### **3. What the Numbers Mean**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Control (Old Procedure)** | 49.13 | 50.00 | +0.87 |
| **Treatment (New Procedure)** | 49.72 | 52.75 | +3.02 |
| **Net Effect** | — | — | **+2.16** |

The control group improved slightly (likely due to system-wide factors), but the treatment group improved **3.5 times more**.

### **4. Branch-Level Results**
- **80.5%** of treatment branches saw score improvements
- **62.0%** of control branches saw score improvements
- The new procedure benefits the **majority of branches**

### **5. Analysis Validity**
Our methodology meets rigorous standards:
- ✓ Pre-treatment trends were parallel (groups were comparable before the switch)
- ✓ Controlled for staffing levels and visitor volume
- ✓ Accounted for branch-specific characteristics
- ✓ Used clustered standard errors for accurate confidence intervals
