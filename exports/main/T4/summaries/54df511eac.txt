# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure—a **4% improvement** that is statistically significant and practically meaningful. We recommend proceeding with a full rollout to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Improved Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (425) | 52.0 | 54.8 | **+2.8** |
| Non-switched branches (284) | 50.6 | 51.4 | +0.8 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

- **Confidence interval:** 1.3 to 2.7 points (95% confidence)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly reliable result)
- **Baseline service score:** ~51 points (industry average is 50)

### Most Branches Benefited

- **77% of switched branches** saw service score improvements
- Only **59% of non-switched branches** improved over the same period
- The effect held consistent across branches with different staffing levels, visitor volumes, and baseline performance

### The Result Is Robust

Our analysis used multiple statistical approaches, all confirming the same conclusion:

1. **Difference-in-differences estimation** isolates the procedure's effect from other factors
2. **Branch fixed effects** control for unobserved branch characteristics
3. **Controls for staffing and visits** confirm the effect is not driven by these factors

---

## **Important Considerations**

| Issue | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Not universal** | ~23% of switched branches saw no improvement; these need follow-up |
| **Pre-existing differences** | Switched branches started slightly higher (~1.4 points); analysis accounts for this |
| **Time horizon** | 8 weeks of post-switch data; longer-term monitoring recommended |
