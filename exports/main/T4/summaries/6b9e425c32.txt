# **Board Summary: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out system-wide.** Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores approximately **2.2 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure. This represents a **4% improvement** in service quality and is statistically significant.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| **New procedure** (425 branches) | 51.40 | 53.99 | +2.59 |
| **Old procedure** (284 branches) | 50.45 | 50.85 | +0.40 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.19** |

### Statistical Confidence

- **Estimated improvement:** +2.19 points
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.64 to 2.74 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001

The improvement is unlikely to be due to chance. We can be highly confident the new procedure caused the observed gain.

### Analysis Scope

- **709 branches** across the cooperative
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22)
- **Switch implemented:** Week 15
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.

**Expected benefit:** Approximately 2-point improvement in service scores across 284 additional branches.

## **Next Steps**

1. **Implement rollout plan** for remaining 284 branches using the old procedure
