# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

**Date:** November 15, 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **successfully improved service scores**. Branches that adopted the new procedure gained approximately **2.1 points** on the service score index—a **4% improvement** that is statistically significant and operationally meaningful. We recommend approving system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Delivered Measurable Gains

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched Branches (425) | 50.5 | 52.3 | +1.8 |
| Control Branches (284) | 49.6 | 49.2 | −0.4 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.1** |

The 2.1-point improvement is statistically significant (p < 0.001) with a 95% confidence interval of 1.5 to 2.6 points.

### 2. The Effect Is Attributable to the Procedure Change

- The improvement persists after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- The gap between switched and control branches widened consistently after week 15, when the switch occurred
- Branch-level analysis shows switched branches improved by an average of 1.7 points, while control branches declined slightly

### 3. Implementation Context

- **425 branches** voluntarily adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **284 branches** continued with the standard procedure
- Data covers **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22) with **12,053 observations**
- Service scores are stable week-to-week; the average branch scores approximately 50

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Develop rollout timeline** for the 284 branches still using the old procedure
2. **Create implementation guide** to ensure consistent adoption across all branches
3. **Continue monitoring** service scores for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm sustained benefits
4. **Document lessons learned** from the 425 early-adopter branches to support training
