# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than branches using the old procedure. This is a statistically significant improvement of approximately **4%** over the baseline. We recommend proceeding with a full system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 49.99 | 53.01 | +3.02 |
| Branches with old procedure | 49.09 | 50.19 | +1.10 |
| **Net improvement from new procedure** | | | **+1.92** |

### Evidence Strength

- **709 branches** analyzed over **17 weeks** (12,053 observations)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** maintained the old procedure
- The improvement is **highly statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, validating the comparison

### What This Means

On a service score index where the average branch scores around 50, the new procedure delivers a **consistent, measurable gain**. The benefit also appears to grow over time, with scores improving by roughly 0.24 points for each additional week under the new procedure.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all remaining branches.**

The data confirms what operational experience suggested: the new rotation and shelving procedure improves service quality. The effect is clear, consistent, and large enough to warrant the investment in full implementation.
