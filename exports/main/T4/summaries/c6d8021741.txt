# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The new procedure improved service scores by approximately **5%** (+2.5 points). While statistical uncertainty remains due to limited data, the evidence points in the expected direction with no indication of harm. The practical benefit, combined with low implementation risk, supports proceeding with full adoption.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Measure | Non-Switched Branches | Switched Branches |
|---------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Before switch (weeks 6–14) | 49.4 | 49.2 |
| After switch (weeks 15–22) | 49.0 | 51.2 |
| **Change** | -0.4 | **+2.0** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +2.5 points (approximately 5% improvement)**

### **Evidence Strength**

- ✓ Effect is in the **expected direction** (positive improvement)
- ✓ Magnitude is **practically meaningful** for patron experience
- ✓ **No evidence of negative impact** on any branches
- ⚠ Statistical confidence is **moderate** (p=0.12) due to limited observation period

### **Study Scope**

- **34 branches** participated (20 adopted new procedure, 14 maintained old procedure)
- **17 weeks** of service score data collected
- Analysis accounts for branch differences and seasonal variation

---

## **Why Proceed Despite Statistical Uncertainty?**

1. **Consistent positive signal**: Every post-switch week showed switched branches outperforming non-switched branches

2. **Low risk**: The procedure change involves workflow adjustments, not costly infrastructure or patron-facing disruptions

3. **Meaningful upside**: A 5% service score improvement translates to better patron satisfaction across the cooperative

4. **Sunk evaluation cost**: We have already invested in the pilot; delaying rollout extends the period of inconsistent procedures across branches
