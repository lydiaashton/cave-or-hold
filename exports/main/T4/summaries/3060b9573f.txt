# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **TITLE: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows a **modest positive effect** on service scores (+2.1 points, or ~4%), but this improvement is **not statistically conclusive** (p=0.10). We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than an immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| Metric | Treatment Branches (New Procedure) | Control Branches (Old Procedure) |
|--------|-----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Number of Branches | 20 | 14 |
| Pre-Switch Average (Weeks 6-14) | 53.0 | 48.5 |
| Post-Switch Average (Weeks 15-22) | 58.0 | 51.5 |
| Change | +4.9 points | +3.1 points |

**Estimated Treatment Effect: +2.1 points** (95% CI: -0.4 to +4.6; p=0.10)

### **What This Means:**
- Branches using the new procedure improved **slightly more** than those using the old procedure
- However, we cannot rule out that this difference occurred by chance
- The true effect could range from slightly harmful to meaningfully beneficial

### **Analysis Validity:**
✓ **Parallel trends confirmed** – Treatment and control branches followed similar patterns before the switch (p=0.82)  
✓ **Results robust** – Findings hold when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume  
✓ **Appropriate methodology** – Used difference-in-differences with cluster-robust standard errors

---

## **RECOMMENDATIONS**

### **Primary Recommendation: Phased Rollout with Monitoring**

Given the suggestive but inconclusive evidence, we recommend:

1. **Expand to 5-10 additional branches** on a voluntary basis
2. **Collect 12+ weeks of additional data** to improve statistical confidence
3. **Track implementation costs** to assess cost-effectiveness
4. **Gather qualitative feedback** from branch staff and patrons

### **Alternative Options for Board Consideration:**

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Full Rollout Now** | Consistency across system; potential service gains | Costs may outweigh benefits; effect uncertain |
| **Extend Evaluation** | More definitive evidence before committing | Delays potential benefits; evaluation costs |
| **Status Quo** | No implementation costs; no risk | Misses opportunity for improvement |
