# Memorandum to the Board

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

The new procedure **improved service scores by 1.8 points** (3.7% above baseline) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p = 0.001). Importantly, while we expected the procedure change to have no impact on service scores, the data shows a meaningful positive effect. The new procedure delivers operational benefits without compromising—and in fact enhancing—service quality.

---

## Key Findings

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure; **60 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Switch occurred in **week 15**
- Method: Difference-in-differences analysis with cluster-robust standard errors

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 50.0 | 51.4 | +1.4 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.7 | 53.9 | +3.2 |
| **Net Effect** | — | — | **+1.8** |

### Statistical Results

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | **+1.84 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | [0.76, 2.93] |
| P-value | 0.001 |
| Effect size | +3.7% vs. baseline |

### Why This Matters

1. **Unexpected benefit:** We anticipated no change in service scores. The positive effect suggests the new procedure may improve staff efficiency or patron experience in ways we did not predict.

2. **No trade-off detected:** Operational changes sometimes improve efficiency at the expense of service quality. This analysis shows the opposite—both can improve together.

3. **Results are robust:** The effect holds after controlling for staffing levels and visit volumes, and accounts for branch-level variation.

4. **Pre-trends support validity:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories, strengthening confidence in the causal interpretation.
