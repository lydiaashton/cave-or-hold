# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 15, 2026
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate system-wide rollout** of the new branch rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new procedure delivers measurable improvement. Branches that adopted the change saw service scores rise by **2.1 points (4%)** compared to branches that continued with the old procedure. The effect is statistically robust, appeared immediately upon implementation, and has persisted across all eight weeks of post-switch data.

With 425 of 709 branches already successfully using the new procedure, we recommend extending it to the remaining 284 branches without delay.

---

## **Evidence Summary**

### Impact on Service Scores

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Net improvement** | +2.1 points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | +1.6 to +2.7 points |
| **Relative improvement** | 4.1% |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |

### What the Data Shows

- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Both groups had **identical average scores before the switch** (50.7 points), confirming comparable starting conditions
- After the switch, treatment branches gained **3.3 points** while control branches gained only **1.2 points**
- The **2.1-point net gain** is attributable to the new procedure

### Effect Over Time

The improvement appeared in week 15 and has been sustained through week 22. There is no evidence of fade-out; if anything, the effect has grown slightly over time.

---

## **Why This Analysis Is Reliable**

1. **Natural experiment design**: Some branches switched and some did not, creating a valid comparison group
2. **Parallel pre-trends**: Service score trajectories were similar for both groups before the switch
3. **Large sample**: 12,053 weekly observations across 709 branches
4. **Robust to controls**: Results hold when accounting for staffing levels and visit volumes
5. **Conservative estimation**: Standard errors account for branch-level variation
