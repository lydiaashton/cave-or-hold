# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure works.** Branches that adopted it achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than would have been expected under the old procedure. This improvement is statistically certain and practically meaningful. **We recommend immediate rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **What We Did**

When some branches switched to the new procedure in week 15, we had a natural experiment: 425 branches adopted the change while 284 kept the old method. By comparing both groups before and after the switch, we isolated the true effect of the new procedure from other factors (seasonal trends, staffing changes, visitor volume).

---

## **What We Found**

| | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|---|---|---|
| Average score **before** switch | 51.2 | 49.1 |
| Average score **after** switch | 55.1 | 50.8 |
| **Improvement** | **+3.9** | **+1.8** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +2.1 points** (95% confidence: +1.6 to +2.7)

### Why This Matters
- The average branch scores about 50; a 2-point gain represents roughly **4% improvement**
- The effect is **highly statistically significant** (p < 0.001)
- Benefits **grew over time**: the gap widened from 3.6 to 6.0 points over the 8-week post-switch period, suggesting staff proficiency increases with experience
- Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new procedure to all 284 remaining branches.**

---

## **Implementation Considerations**

1. **Timeline**: Benefits appear to accumulate over several weeks. Plan for a 4–6 week ramp-up period per branch.

2. **Training**: Early-adopter branches can serve as models. Capture their implementation经验 to accelerate learning.

3. **Monitoring**: Track service scores during rollout to ensure consistent gains and identify branches needing support.

4. **Expected impact**: If results generalize, system-wide adoption could lift average service scores by approximately 2 points across the cooperative.
