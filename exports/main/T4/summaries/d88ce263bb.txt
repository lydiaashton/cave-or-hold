# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new stock rotation procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores**, but the evidence is **not yet statistically conclusive**. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by approximately **2.4 points (5%)** relative to those that kept the old procedure. We recommend a **phased expansion** while continuing to collect data, rather than an immediate system-wide rollout or abandoning the initiative.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **What the Data Shows**

| Metric | Old Procedure Branches | New Procedure Branches |
|--------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Average score before switch (Weeks 6-14) | 48.3 | 47.8 |
| Average score after switch (Weeks 15-22) | 47.6 | 49.5 |
| **Change** | **-0.7** | **+1.7** |

**Net improvement attributable to new procedure: +2.4 points**

### **How Confident Are We?**

- The 2.4-point improvement is **not statistically significant** at the conventional 95% confidence level (p=0.10)
- The **95% confidence interval ranges from -0.55 to +5.40 points**
- This means the true effect could be slightly negative, zero, or meaningfully positive
- The data is **suggestive but not definitive**

### **Why This Matters**

- Service scores average ~50 points across all branches; a 2.4-point gain represents roughly **5% improvement**
- Non-switched branches saw scores decline slightly after week 15 (-0.7 points), while switched branches improved (+1.7 points)
- The result holds even after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

We recommend a **three-phase approach**:

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **1** | Continue current pilot with expanded monitoring | Immediate |
| **2** | Offer new procedure to 5-10 additional interested branches | Q1 2026 |
| **3** | Re-evaluate with 6+ months of data; decide on full rollout | Q3 2026 |

### **Rationale**

1. **The signal is positive** — No branches showed harm, and the point estimate favors the new procedure
2. **The evidence is incomplete** — Eight weeks of post-switch data provides limited statistical power
3. **The risk is low** — Even the lower bound of our confidence interval suggests minimal downside
4. **More data will clarify** — Extending the observation period will narrow the confidence interval and support a more definitive decision

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Extend data collection** through at least week 40 (end of calendar year)
2. **Survey branch staff** on operational impacts (time, effort, satisfaction)
3. **Report back to the board** in Q2 2026 with updated analysis
4. **Budget for potential full rollout** contingent on continued positive results

---

## **APPENDIX: ANALYSIS DETAILS**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch-level clustering
- **Sample:** 34 branches (20 switched, 14 did not) × 17 weeks = 578 observations
- **Switch date:** Week 15
- **Software:** Python with statsmodels package
