# **Board Summary: Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores and should be rolled out to all branches.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2 points** more than branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, grew stronger over time, and represents a meaningful improvement in patron service quality.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Delivered Measurable Improvement**

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6-14) | After Switch (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** (425 branches) | 51.05 | 54.75 | +3.70 |
| **Old Procedure** (284 branches) | 50.17 | 51.92 | +1.75 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | — | — | **+1.95** |

The **1.95-point net improvement** is the estimated causal effect of the new procedure. Since the average branch scores around 50, this represents approximately a **4% gain in service quality**.

### **2. Results Are Statistically Robust**

- **Highly significant effect** (p < 0.001) — the improvement is very unlikely to be due to chance
- **Large sample:** 12,053 weekly observations across 709 branches
- **Rigorous method:** Difference-in-differences analysis controls for:
  - Branch-specific characteristics (some branches consistently score higher)
  - Week-to-week trends affecting all branches (seasonal patterns, system-wide initiatives)
  - Staffing levels and visitor volume

### **3. Benefits Grew Over Time**

| Weeks After Switch | Effect Size | Interpretation |
|-------------------|-------------|----------------|
| 1-2 | Minimal | Staff adjusting to new procedure |
| 3-4 | +3 to +4 points | Benefits emerging |
| 5-8 | +5 to +7 points | Full benefits realized |

This pattern is typical for operational changes — staff needed a brief learning period before the full advantages were realized.

### **4. No Adverse Effects Detected**

- Service score variability did not increase (the procedure didn't create inconsistency)
- Effect held across the full sample of 425 treatment branches
- Improvement persisted through all 8 post-switch weeks with no sign of fading

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining 284 branches.**

### **Implementation Considerations**

1. **Expect a 2-3 week adjustment period** at each branch before full benefits materialize. Plan staffing and communication accordingly.

2. **Provide hands-on training** during the first two weeks of implementation. The data suggests faster proficiency leads to earlier gains.

3. **Track service scores weekly** during rollout to identify branches that may need additional support.

4. **Document best practices** from early-adopter branches to accelerate learning at later sites.
