# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6-22 (17 weeks of data)

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure **did not produce a statistically significant improvement** in service scores, but it appears to have **protected branches from a system-wide decline** that affected control branches. The estimated benefit is approximately **2 points on the service score index**, though this result could be due to chance (p = 0.15).

**Recommendation:** Do not proceed with immediate system-wide rollout. Instead, extend the pilot period and investigate why control branch scores declined after week 14.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### What We Tested
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure (treatment group)
- **14 branches** kept the old procedure (control group)
- Service scores tracked weekly before and after the switch (week 15)

### What Happened

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| New Procedure | 51.41 | 51.27 | -0.14 |
| Old Procedure | 49.26 | 47.19 | **-2.07** |

**Net Effect of New Procedure: +1.93 points**

### What This Means
1. **Branches using the new procedure held steady** while control branches declined
2. **The 2-point advantage is not statistically significant** – we cannot rule out chance
3. **Something caused system-wide scores to drop** after week 14 (seasonal? staffing? other factors?)
4. **Pre-trends were parallel** – the two groups were on similar trajectories before the switch, validating our comparison

### Statistical Confidence
- **95% Confidence Interval:** -0.63 to +4.49 points
- **P-value:** 0.15 (conventional threshold is 0.05)
- **Interpretation:** The data are consistent with both a small positive effect and no effect at all

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

| Action | Timeline | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Extend pilot by 8-12 weeks** | Q1 2026 | Increase statistical power to detect true effect |
| **Investigate control group decline** | Immediate | Identify system-wide factors affecting service scores |
| **Add 10-15 branches to second wave** | Q2 2026 | Randomize assignment to strengthen causal inference |
| **Conduct cost-benefit analysis** | Q1 2026 | Weigh ~2-point benefit against implementation costs |
| **Set clear rollout criteria** | Immediate | Define success threshold before expanding |

### Decision Criteria for Full Rollout
We recommend proceeding with system-wide implementation only if future data shows:
- **Statistically significant effect** (p < 0.05), **OR**
- **Consistent positive effect of 3+ points** across multiple periods, **OR**
- **Clear cost savings** that justify even a small service score benefit

---

## **APPENDIX: Technical Notes**

- **Method:** Difference-in-Differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
- **Controls tested:** Staffing index and visits index (neither materially changed results)
- **Parallel trends assumption:** Validated (p = 0.48 for differential pre-trends)
- **Data quality:** 578 observations, no missing values
