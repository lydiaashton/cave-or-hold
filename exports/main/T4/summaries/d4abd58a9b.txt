# **Memorandum to the Library Cooperative Board**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure produced a **positive improvement in service scores**, though the evidence falls short of statistical certainty. Branches that adopted the new procedure gained approximately **2.2 points** on the service score index relative to branches that maintained the old procedure. While this direction aligns with expectations, the result is not statistically significant at conventional levels (p=0.10), meaning we cannot definitively rule out chance variation.

**Recommendation:** Approve a **phased system-wide rollout** with continued evaluation. The positive direction of the effect, combined with no evidence of harm, supports broader adoption while we gather additional data to confirm the magnitude of benefit.

---

## **Analysis Overview**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **14 branches** maintained the old procedure (control group)
- Service scores averaged ~47 points system-wide (higher is better)

### **Key Results**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated Effect | **+2.24 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.46 to +4.94 points** |
| P-value | **0.104** |
| Effect Size | **~5% of average score** |

**Group Performance:**
- **Switched branches:** Improved from 45.0 → 47.8 (+2.8 points)
- **Control branches:** Improved from 48.7 → 49.2 (+0.5 points)
- **Net improvement attributable to new procedure:** +2.2 points

---

## **Interpretation**

### **What the Data Shows**

1. **Positive Direction:** The effect moves in the expected direction. Switched branches improved more than control branches after the procedure change.

2. **Statistical Uncertainty:** With 34 branches and 8 weeks of post-switch data, the study has limited power. We could reliably detect effects of ~4 points or larger; the observed 2.2-point effect is smaller and therefore harder to distinguish from random variation.

3. **No Negative Effects:** The lower bound of the confidence interval (-0.46) suggests the procedure is unlikely to be harming service scores.

4. **Parallel Trends Validated:** Before the switch, switched and control branches followed similar trajectories, supporting the validity of the comparison.

### **Why Results May Be Less Definitive Than Expected**

- **Sample size:** Only 20 branches adopted the new procedure
- **Limited post-switch period:** Only 8 weeks of data after implementation
- **Natural variability:** Service scores fluctuate week-to-week due to factors beyond shelving procedures (staffing, visits, seasonal patterns)
- **Implementation variation:** Not all branches may have adopted the new procedure with equal fidelity

---

## **Recommendations**

### **1. Approve Phased Rollout (Primary Recommendation)**

Proceed with expanding the new procedure to remaining branches over the next quarter, with these conditions:

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| Roll out to remaining 14 branches | Weeks 1–6 |
| Continue weekly score tracking | Ongoing |
| Interim review with expanded data | Week 12 post-rollout |
| Final evaluation and adjustment | Week 24 post-rollout |

**Rationale:** The positive direction of effects, absence of negative impacts, and operational benefits reported by staff support adoption. Continued monitoring will provide stronger evidence of the true effect size.

### **2. Strengthen Implementation Support**

- Provide standardized training materials to all branches
- Designate implementation champions at each location
- Document any deviations from the prescribed procedure

### **3. Extend Evaluation Period**

- Collect at least 16 weeks of post-rollout data system-wide
- This will approximately double statistical power
- Enables detection of effects as small as 1.5 points with confidence

### **4. Consider Complementary Measures**

- Track staff time spent on shelving (efficiency metric)
- Monitor patron satisfaction surveys
- Record any operational challenges or successes

---

## **Decision Summary**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Yes, by approximately 2.2 points** |
| Is this improvement statistically certain? | **No – there is meaningful uncertainty** |
| Could the procedure be causing harm? | **Unlikely – no evidence of negative effects** |
| Should we roll out system-wide? | **Yes, with continued monitoring** |
