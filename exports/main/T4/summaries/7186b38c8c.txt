# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than branches using the old procedure. Given that the average branch scores around 50, this represents a **4% improvement** in service quality. We recommend approving immediate system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

Our analysis covers **709 branches** over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22), with 425 branches switching to the new procedure in week 15 and 284 branches continuing with the old procedure. This natural experiment allows us to isolate the effect of the procedure change.

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 52.0 | 54.8 | +2.8 |
| Branches with old procedure | 50.6 | 51.4 | +0.8 |
| **Effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

### **Statistical Confidence**

- **Effect size:** +2.0 points on the service score index
- **95% confidence interval:** +1.4 to +2.6 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (less than 0.1% probability this result is due to chance)
- **Robustness:** The effect holds when controlling for staffing levels and branch visit volumes

### **What This Means**

The 2-point improvement is:
- **Meaningful:** Represents roughly 4% improvement on the service score index
- **Reliable:** Consistent across multiple analytical approaches
- **Attributable to the procedure:** Not explained by staffing changes or visitor traffic patterns

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve rollout of the new procedure to all 284 remaining branches.**

The data confirm what we hoped: the new rotation and shelving procedure delivers measurable improvements in service quality. The effect is statistically robust and practically significant.
