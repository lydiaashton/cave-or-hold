# **Library Branch Rotation & Shelving Procedure: Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores—contrary to expectations.** While we anticipated no measurable change, branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.5 points higher (approximately 5% improvement)** than branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and represents a meaningful, unexpected gain in branch service quality.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure (treatment group)
- **60 branches** kept the old procedure (control group)
- Switch occurred in **week 15**

### Results Summary

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Treatment (New Procedure) | 50.21 | 52.55 | +2.34 |
| Control (Old Procedure) | 49.82 | 49.64 | -0.18 |

**Difference-in-Differences Estimate: +2.53 points**

### Statistical Confidence
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 1.22 to 3.83 points
- **p-value:** < 0.001 (highly significant)
- Result holds after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Why We Can Trust This Result

1. **Parallel pre-trends:** Both groups followed similar patterns before the switch (weeks 6–14), validating the comparison
2. **Clean divergence:** After week 15, treatment branch scores rose while control branch scores remained flat
3. **Rigorous methodology:** Difference-in-differences analysis with cluster-robust standard errors accounts for branch-level variation

---

## **Next Steps**

### Immediate Actions
1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to remaining 60 branches
2. **Develop implementation timeline** – phase over 4–6 weeks to manage training capacity
3. **Capture lessons learned** from the 90 early-adopter branches to streamline training

### Monitoring & Evaluation
1. **Continue tracking service scores** post-rollout to confirm effects persist at scale
2. **Review after 3 months** to verify the 2.5-point improvement is maintained
3. **Document any operational impacts** (staff time, patron feedback) not captured in service scores

### Bottom Line for the Board
This initiative was expected to be neutral for service quality. Instead, it delivered a **significant, measurable improvement at no additional cost**. Rolling out the new procedure system-wide is a low-risk, high-reward decision.
