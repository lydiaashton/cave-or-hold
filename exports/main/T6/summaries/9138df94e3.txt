# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect** on service scores (+1.9 points, or ~4%), which aligns with expectations. However, the statistical evidence is **not strong enough to confirm the effect is real** rather than random variation. We recommend **a cautious, monitored expansion** rather than a full immediate rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches | 53.0 | 57.9 | +4.9 |
| Non-switched branches | 48.5 | 51.5 | +3.1 |
| **Attributed to new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

### **What This Means**

- **Good news:** The effect is in the expected direction (positive)
- **Uncertainty:** The 95% confidence interval ranges from **-0.9 to +4.6 points**
  - This means the true effect could be slightly negative, zero, or meaningfully positive
- **Why the uncertainty?** Only 20 branches switched, with just 8 weeks of post-switch data

### **Why We Trust the Analysis**

- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (validates our comparison method)
- Analysis controls for inherent differences between branches
- Both groups improved after week 15, suggesting favorable external conditions

---

## **Recommendation**

**Do not proceed with full system-wide rollout yet.** Instead:

1. **Extend the pilot** for another 8–12 weeks to gather more data
2. **Add 5–10 more branches** to the treatment group to improve statistical power
3. **Set a decision threshold:** If the effect remains positive and the confidence interval excludes zero, proceed with full rollout
4. **Track operational costs** alongside service scores to assess net benefit

---

## **What the Board Should Consider**

| If the true effect is... | Full rollout would... |
|--------------------------|----------------------|
| +4 points (upper bound) | Deliver meaningful service improvements |
| 0 points (possible) | Incur change costs with no benefit |
| -1 point (possible) | Actually reduce service quality |

Given this uncertainty, a **phased approach minimizes risk** while allowing the cooperative to capture potential benefits.
