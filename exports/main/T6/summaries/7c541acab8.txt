# **Board Summary: New Shelving & Rotation Procedure Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but with measured expectations and close monitoring.**

The new procedure produced a **statistically significant but modest improvement** of ~2.4 points (5%) in service scores. However, **only 65% of switching branches actually improved**—about one-third saw no benefit or declined. The effect is real but not universal, suggesting branch-level factors influence success.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Service Score Impact

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment (switched) | 49.96 | 51.40 | +1.44 |
| Control (no switch) | 51.28 | 50.26 | −1.01 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.45** |

### Critical Nuances

| Metric | Finding | Implication |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **Branches that improved** | 65.6% of treatment branches | About 1 in 3 branches saw no benefit |
| **Effect size** | 0.27 standard deviations | Small-to-moderate practical impact |
| **Range of effects** | -7.8 to +10.0 points | Significant variation across branches |
| **Statistical confidence** | p < 0.001, CI: 1.7 to 3.2 | Effect is real, not random noise |

### What This Means

✓ The procedure **does work on average**—the effect is statistically robust  
⚠️ But it **doesn't work everywhere**—branch characteristics likely matter  
⚠️ The **magnitude is modest**—don't expect transformative change  
⚠️ We have **only 8 weeks of post-data**—longer-term effects unknown  

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **1. Approve phased rollout** rather than immediate system-wide | Allows learning and adjustment; reduces risk if effects don't persist |
| **2. Investigate why 35% of branches didn't benefit** | Identify branch characteristics that predict success; target support accordingly |
| **3. Establish success metrics and monitoring** | Track scores for 6+ months post-rollout; be prepared to adjust |
| **4. Document implementation differences** | Some branches may have adopted the procedure more faithfully than others |

## **Risk Considerations**

| Risk | Assessment |
|------|------------|
| Effect doesn't persist beyond 8 weeks | **Medium** — Need longer-term data |
| One-third of branches see no benefit | **Confirmed** — Understand why before full rollout |
| Implementation costs exceed benefits | **Unknown** — Cost data not in this analysis |
| Effect attenuates at scale | **Possible** — Early adopters may be more motivated |

---

## **Bottom Line Restated**

The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure, **but**:
- Expect modest gains, not transformation
- Plan to learn which branches benefit most
- Monitor closely and be prepared to adjust
- Consider whether implementation support can help the 35% who didn't improve
