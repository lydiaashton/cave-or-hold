**Net Effect: +2.0 points** (p = 0.125, not statistically significant)

### What This Means

1. **The direction is positive.** All estimates point to improvement—there's no evidence the new procedure harms service scores.

2. **The uncertainty is substantial.** The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.6 to +4.7 points. The true effect could be zero, modestly negative, or as high as +4.7 points.

3. **More data would help.** With only 8 weeks of post-switch data and 34 branches, we have limited statistical power. Extending the evaluation period would narrow the confidence interval.

4. **No red flags.** Switched branches improved without any detectable negative consequences. Staffing and visit patterns remained stable.

### Why the Uncertainty?
- **Sample size**: 34 branches with 8 weeks of post-switch data limits precision
- **Natural variation**: Service scores fluctuate week-to-week (standard deviation ~10 points)
- **Branch differences**: Some branches consistently score higher or lower regardless of procedure
