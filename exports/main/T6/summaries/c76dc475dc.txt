## **Key Findings**

### **1. Point Estimate Is Positive**

The raw difference-in-differences is **+1.9 points**. With statistical controls, the estimate is **+2.2 points**.

### **2. Statistical Uncertainty Is Substantial**

- **p-value = 0.085**: Above the 0.05 threshold typically required for statistical significance
- **95% confidence interval: −0.3 to +4.6 points**: The true effect could be zero, slightly negative, or as high as +4.6 points
- **Sample size limitation**: Only 34 branches (20 treatment, 14 control) limits statistical power

### **3. No Evidence of Harm**

- The effect is **not negative** — the lower bound of the confidence interval is essentially zero
- Switched branches maintained their scores; non-switched branches declined slightly
- No adverse patterns detected in the data

### **4. Pre-Trends Support Validity**

Service score trends were **parallel before the switch**, indicating the comparison between groups is valid. The divergence after Week 15 is consistent with a treatment effect, even if we cannot confirm it with high statistical confidence.
