# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We cannot conclude that the new procedure improved service scores.** The estimated effect is positive but **not statistically significant** (p = 0.09–0.11), and the confidence interval includes zero. Importantly, the treatment branches showed minimal actual improvement (+0.7 points); the estimated "effect" partly reflects that control branches declined during the same period.

**Recommendation:** Do not proceed with organization-wide rollout based on current evidence. Either (a) continue the pilot and collect more data, or (b) investigate why control branches declined before making changes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What Actually Happened to Scores**
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment (new procedure) | 52.8 | 53.5 | **+0.7** |
| Control (old procedure) | 47.9 | 46.7 | **−1.2** |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+1.9** |

The +1.9 point "effect" is the *difference* between these trends, not the treatment group's actual gain.

### **Statistical Evidence**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +1.9 points |
| p-value | 0.09–0.11 (not significant at 0.05) |
| 95% Confidence interval | −0.5 to +4.3 points |
| Interpretation | Data are consistent with effects ranging from slightly negative to moderately positive |

### **What We Can Say**
✓ No evidence the new procedure **harmed** service scores  
✓ Pre-switch trends were parallel (supports study validity)  
✗ No statistically significant evidence the new procedure **helped**  
✗ Cannot rule out that the observed difference is due to chance
