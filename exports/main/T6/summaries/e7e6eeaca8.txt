# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure produced a **small but statistically significant improvement** in service scores (+2.2 points, or ~4%). While this contradicts our expectation of no difference, the effect is modest relative to normal week-to-week score fluctuations. **We recommend a phased rollout** with continued monitoring to confirm benefits justify implementation costs.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **60 branches** continued the old procedure
- Difference-in-differences analysis compares both groups before and after the switch

### **Results**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Average score before switch | 51.0 | 50.4 |
| Average score after switch | 53.3 | 50.5 |
| **Change** | **+2.3** | **+0.1** |

- **Treatment effect: +2.19 points** (95% CI: 1.0 to 3.4 points; p < 0.001)
- **73% of switched branches** improved vs. 43% of control branches
- **Context**: Weekly scores naturally fluctuate 4–8 points; the treatment effect is smaller than typical week-to-week variation

### **Important Caveats**
- **Low explanatory power**: The model explains only 1.6% of score variation—most drivers of service quality remain unidentified
- **Unexpected finding**: We anticipated no difference; this positive result warrants careful validation
- **Heterogeneous effects**: Some switched branches saw score declines despite the average gain

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Phased rollout**: Extend the procedure to additional branches in stages, not all at once

2. **Cost-benefit analysis**: Quantify implementation costs (training, labor, disruption) against the 4% service improvement

3. **Extended monitoring**: Track scores for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm the effect persists

4. **Investigate variation**: Identify why some branches benefited more than others; document implementation best practices

5. **Revisit decision**: After phased rollout data is collected, the board should reassess whether to proceed cooperative-wide
