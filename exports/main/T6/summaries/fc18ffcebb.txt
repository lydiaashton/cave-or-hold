**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Recommendation:** Approve rollout with monitoring provisions

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores on average by 2.1 points** (4% improvement), with high statistical confidence. However, **about one-third of switched branches did not benefit**, suggesting implementation quality matters. We recommend approval **with a phased rollout and support program** to maximize success across all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Effect Is Real
- **Estimated impact: +2.1 points** (95% CI: 1.5 to 2.7)
- **Statistical significance: p < 0.001** (robust to clustering by branch)
- **Effect strengthened over time**, consistent with staff learning

### But Results Varied Across Branches
| | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|---|---|---|
| Average change | +1.47 | −0.62 |
| % that improved | 66% | 44% |
| Median change | +1.7 | −0.9 |

**Important:** 34% of switched branches saw scores decline, indicating the procedure doesn't work equally well everywhere.

### Switched Branches Were Slightly Different at Baseline
- Pre-switch service scores: 51.2 (switched) vs. 50.5 (control)
- This difference is small but suggests switched branches may have had advantages (staffing, management, facilities) that helped them succeed

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve systemwide rollout with the following conditions:**

1. **Phased implementation** - Roll out to 50-75 branches first, monitor for 4-6 weeks, then expand
2. **Implementation support** - Provide training, on-site assistance, and troubleshooting during the first 2 weeks at each branch
3. **Success monitoring** - Track service scores weekly; branches showing no improvement after 4 weeks should receive additional support
4. **Exit option** - Branches that struggle despite support should be allowed to revert to the old procedure

---

## **Expected Impact**

If the average effect holds: **~2 point systemwide improvement** in service scores.

If only 66% of branches benefit (as observed): **~1.3 point systemwide improvement**.

Either scenario represents meaningful improvement worth pursuing, provided we support branches that struggle.
