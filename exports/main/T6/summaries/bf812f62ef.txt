# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** Current  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Proceed with caution—recommend phased rollout with continued evaluation.** The new procedure is associated with a **2.17-point improvement** in service scores (4.3% above baseline). However, methodological limitations prevent us from claiming full causal certainty. A phased approach allows us to confirm the effect persists while minimizing rollout risk.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### What We Found

| | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Switched Branches** | 49.72 | 52.75 | +3.02 |
| **Non-Switched Branches** | 49.13 | 50.00 | +0.87 |
| **Net Difference** | | | **+2.17** |

- **Statistical precision:** 95% confidence interval is 1.62 to 2.72 points
- **Post-switch consistency:** All 8 weeks showed improvement (gap ranged from 2.00 to 3.50 points)
- **Stability:** Post-switch performance was notably more consistent than pre-switch

### Important Limitations

| Concern | Why It Matters | Impact on Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-switch trends not parallel** | Switched and non-switched branches had different trajectories before the change (gap varied from -1.18 to +1.86 points) | Reduces confidence in causal interpretation |
| **Only 8 weeks of post-switch data** | Effects could fade, grow, or reverse over time | Warrants continued monitoring |
| **Modest effect size** | 4% improvement may not justify high implementation costs | Cost-benefit analysis needed |
| **Possible selection effects** | Branches that switched may differ in unmeasured ways (motivation, management quality) | Could overstate true effect |

### Why We're Still Optimistic

- The post-switch improvement was **sustained across all 8 weeks**—never reverting to pre-switch levels
- Performance became **more stable** after the switch (variability dropped by 60%)
- Effect persists after controlling for **staffing levels** and **visitor volume**
- 425 branches successfully implemented the change without reported operational issues
