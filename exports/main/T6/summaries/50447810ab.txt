# **Board Summary: New Branch Procedure Evaluation**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Decision on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence suggests the new procedure **likely improved service scores by about 2 points (4%)**, but we **cannot be statistically certain** at conventional confidence levels. The effect is positive, there's no evidence of harm, and the study was too small to detect effects reliably. 

**Recommendation depends on implementation cost:**
- **If low-cost/low-disruption:** Proceed with rollout (upside outweighs downside)
- **If high-cost/high-disruption:** Expand the pilot first to gather more evidence

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. What We Found
| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.0 points** | Positive direction |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.2 to +4.5** | Includes zero (uncertain) |
| P-value | **0.097** | Not significant at 0.05 threshold |
| Probability effect > 0 | **95.7%** | Likely positive |
| Branches improved | **13/20 (65%)** vs 6/14 control (43%) | Consistent pattern |

### 2. Why the Uncertainty?
- Only **34 branches** in the study (20 switched, 14 control)
- Only **17 weeks** of data
- Statistical power was **32%** — we had less than 1-in-3 chance of detecting a real effect
- **Non-significance was expected** even if the procedure works

### 3. What We Can Say With Confidence
- The effect is **not meaningfully negative** (lower bound ≈ 0)
- **No adverse effects** on staffing or visits detected
- Switched branches were **not already improving** before the switch (parallel trends confirmed)

### 4. What We Cannot Say With Confidence
- Whether the true effect is 0 points or 4 points
- Whether the effect will hold at system-wide scale
- Whether the improvement is large enough to justify significant costs

---

## **Recommendation**

We present **two options** for the Board:

| Option | Action | When Appropriate |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **A. Proceed with Rollout** | Implement at all remaining branches | If procedure is low-cost, low-disruption, and aligns with strategic goals |
| **B. Expand Pilot First** | Add 10–15 more branches to treatment, monitor 8+ weeks | If procedure requires significant investment, training, or operational changes |

**Our neutral assessment:** The evidence leans positive but is not definitive. The decision should hinge on **implementation cost and risk tolerance**, not statistical significance alone.

## **If Proceeding: Safeguards**

1. **Phase the rollout** (4–6 branches per month)
2. **Set a review point** after 50% conversion
3. **Define a stop-loss threshold** (e.g., pause if average effect < 0)
4. **Collect staff feedback** to identify implementation issues early
