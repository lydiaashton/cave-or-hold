# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2025  
**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Subject:** Evidence Review on New Procedure

---

### **Bottom Line**

The data shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive** effect. Switched branches improved by an estimated **2.4 points (5%)**, but the confidence interval ranges from **-0.5 to +5.4 points**, meaning we cannot rule out no effect or even a small negative effect. We **recommend a limited pilot expansion** with continued data collection before committing to system-wide rollout.

---

### **Key Findings**

**What the Data Shows**

| | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|---|---|---|
| **Before week 15** | 49.21 | 49.38 |
| **After week 15** | 51.24 | 48.97 |
| **Change** | **+2.03** | **-0.42** |

**Estimated treatment effect: +2.45 points**

**What the Statistics Tell Us**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Point estimate | +2.45 points | Best guess of the effect |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.49 to +5.38 | Range of plausible values |
| P-value | 0.103 | Not statistically significant |
| Significance threshold | p < 0.05 | **Not met** |

**Key Takeaway:** The data is **consistent with** a meaningful improvement, but also **consistent with** no effect. We need more evidence.

**Supporting Observations**
- Switched and non-switched branches had nearly identical scores before the change (parallel trends confirmed)
- Switched branches outperformed controls in all 8 post-switch weeks (consistent direction)
- The effect size (5%) would be operationally meaningful if real

---

### **Recommendation**

**Do not proceed with full system-wide rollout at this time.** Instead:

1. **Expand the pilot** to include additional branches willing to adopt the procedure
2. **Collect 12+ more weeks of data** to improve statistical precision
3. **Re-evaluate** when the confidence interval narrows sufficiently to exclude zero (or confirm the effect is negligible)

---

| Consideration | Assessment |

| **Cost of full rollout** | ⚠️ Training, adjustment, potential reversal |
