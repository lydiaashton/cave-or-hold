## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Unclear** — treatment branches declined less, but we cannot rule out chance |
| Is the effect statistically significant? | **No** — p = 0.14; 95% confidence interval includes zero |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Not yet** — gather more data before committing |

**Recommended approach:** Extend the pilot for 3-6 more months with additional branches, then re-evaluate with stronger evidence.

## **Key Findings**

### **1. What the Data Shows**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **20 branches (new procedure)** | 51.41 | 51.27 | **-0.14** |
| **14 branches (old procedure)** | 49.26 | 47.19 | **-2.07** |

**Observed difference: +1.9 points** — but this could be due to random variation.

### **2. Statistical Uncertainty**

- **95% confidence interval:** -0.6 to +4.5 points
- **Interpretation:** The true effect could be slightly negative, zero, or moderately positive
- **We have 93% confidence** the effect is positive — suggestive, but below the 95% threshold typically required for confident decisions

### **3. Why the Uncertainty?**

- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** — too short to separate signal from noise
- Only **34 branches total** — limited statistical power
- **System-wide score decline** after week 14 complicates interpretation

### **4. What We Can Say with Confidence**

- The new procedure **did not harm** service scores
- Treatment branches showed **more stable performance** than control branches
- **55% of treatment branches** improved vs. 36% of control branches

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Primary Recommendation: Extend the Pilot**

1. **Continue current arrangement** for 3-6 more months
2. **Add 5-10 more branches** to the treatment group to increase statistical power
3. **Re-analyze** with 6+ months of post-switch data

### **If Board Prefers Action Now**

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Full rollout** | Consistency across system | Risk of adopting ineffective procedure |
| **Phased rollout** | Balances action with caution | Still proceeds on uncertain evidence |
| **Extend pilot** (recommended) | Better evidence before commitment | Delays potential benefits |

### **Regardless of Decision**

1. **Maintain 5-7 control branches** for ongoing comparison
2. **Track implementation fidelity** — ensure the procedure is applied consistently
3. **Monitor branch-level variation** — investigate why some branches don't improve

## **What Would Change Our Conclusion?**

| Evidence Threshold | Current Status | What's Needed |
|-------------------|----------------|---------------|
| Statistical significance (p < 0.05) | ❌ p = 0.14 | ~2x more data or larger effect |

## **Bottom Line Restated**

The new procedure shows **promise but not proof**. The observed pattern is consistent with a modest beneficial effect, but the data is too limited to be confident. Given no evidence of harm and a positive direction, continuing the pilot with expanded participation is the lowest-risk path to a sound decision.
