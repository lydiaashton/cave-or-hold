## **REVISED BOARD MEMORANDUM**

### **BOTTOM LINE (REVISED)**

The evidence for the new procedure's effectiveness is **inconclusive**. While branches that switched show higher service scores, those branches were already improving before the change. The apparent ~2-point improvement may largely reflect pre-existing trends rather than the procedure itself. 

**We recommend PAUSING systemwide rollout** until we can obtain clearer evidence through a more rigorous evaluation design.

---

### **KEY CONCERNS**

1. **Selection Bias**: Branches chosen for the new procedure were already on an improvement trajectory (+0.30 points/week before the switch). This violates the key assumption needed to attribute score changes to the procedure.

2. **Uncertain True Effect**: After accounting for pre-treatment trends, the actual procedure effect could be:
   - As high as ~1.0 to 1.5 points (optimistic)
   - Near zero or even negative (possible)
   
3. **Post-Treatment Pattern**: The gap between treated and control branches in weeks 15-22 (average +1.82) is only modestly higher than the gap in the final pre-treatment weeks (average +0.63). Some of this could simply be continuation of the pre-existing convergence.

---

### **REVISED RECOMMENDATIONS**

1. **DO NOT proceed with systemwide rollout yet**

2. **Conduct a randomized rollout** for the remaining 284 control branches:
   - Randomly assign half to switch now, half to wait
   - This would give clean causal evidence

3. **Investigate selection criteria**: Why were certain branches chosen for the initial rollout? Understanding this may reveal confounding factors.

4. **Gather qualitative data**: Interview staff at switched branches about what actually changed in their workflows.

5. **Re-evaluate in 8-12 weeks** after randomized evidence is available.

---

**The board should not commit resources to systemwide rollout based on this evidence alone.** The procedure may work, but we cannot confidently say so from this data. A more rigorous evaluation is warranted before making a cooperative-wide decision.
