# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **Bottom Line**

The data shows a **positive association** between the new procedure and service scores (+2.2 points), but **we cannot rule out the possibility of no effect** at conventional confidence levels. The evidence is **suggestive but not definitive**. We recommend a **measured rollout** with explicit success metrics and a commitment to re-evaluate after 6 months.

---

### **What the Numbers Actually Say**

| Statistic | Value | Interpretation |
|-----------|-------|----------------|
| **Point estimate** | +2.2 points | Best guess of the true effect |
| **P-value** | 0.085 | 8.5% chance this result occurred by random variation |
| **95% confidence interval** | -0.3 to +4.8 points | True effect could be slightly negative or up to +5 |
| **90% confidence interval** | +0.1 to +4.4 points | At 90% confidence, effect is positive |

**Plain language:** We are roughly **90% confident** the procedure helps, but not **95% confident** (the conventional threshold for "statistical significance").

---

### **Key Findings**

**Supporting a positive effect:**
- ✓ All model specifications show positive estimates (+2.0 to +2.6 points)
- ✓ Effect strengthens over time (+0.7 early → +3.3 late)
- ✓ Pre-switch trends were parallel (validates the comparison)
- ✓ 65% of switching branches improved vs. 57% of non-switching
- ✓ No evidence of harm to any branch

**Reasons for caution:**
- ✗ Does not meet conventional statistical significance (p < 0.05)
- ✗ Effect size is modest (~4% improvement)
- ✗ Substantial variation across branches (-4.5 to +11.6 points)

---

### **Recommendations**

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **Proceed with rollout, but stage it** | Evidence leans positive; staging allows course-correction |
| **Define success criteria upfront** | E.g., "Average +2 points within 8 weeks of switch" |
| **Track implementation fidelity** | Variation in outcomes suggests execution matters |
| **Commit to 6-month re-evaluation** | Decide whether to continue, modify, or abandon based on accumulated data |
| **Investigate high-performers** | Branches with +5+ point gains may reveal best practices |

---

### **Bottom Line, Restated**

> **The procedure likely helps, but we are not as certain as we hoped to be.** The board should decide whether a ~4% improvement with 90% confidence justifies the cost and effort of systemwide implementation—or whether more evidence is needed before full rollout.
