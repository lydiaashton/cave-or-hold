The p-value of 0.11 means there's about a **10% chance** we'd see this result even if the procedure had no real effect. That's not strong enough evidence for high-confidence decision-making. However, there's approximately a **95% probability the true effect is positive**.

---

# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The data suggests the new procedure **likely improves service scores**, but we **cannot confirm this with high confidence**. The estimated improvement is **+2.1 points**, but the statistical evidence is inconclusive (p=0.11). There is approximately a **95% probability the true effect is positive**, yet we cannot rule out zero effect or even a small negative effect. We recommend **extending the pilot period** before making a systemwide rollout decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Numbers Say**
| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.1 points** | Branches using new procedure scored higher |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.4 to +4.7 points** | True effect could be slightly negative or moderately positive |
| P-value | **0.11** | Not statistically significant at conventional threshold (0.05) |
| Probability effect is positive | **~95%** | Evidence leans toward improvement |

### **Observed Changes**
| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches | 47.9 | 49.6 | **+1.7** |
| Non-switched branches | 51.1 | 50.8 | -0.3 |

### **Branch-Level Results**
- **14 of 20 switched branches (70%)** improved
- **6 of 20 switched branches (30%)** declined
- Range: -5.1 to +6.8 points

---

## **Why Isn't This Conclusive?**

1. **Limited post-switch data:** Only 8 weeks after the change
2. **Sample size:** 34 branches provides moderate but not definitive power
3. **Natural variation:** Service scores fluctuate week-to-week
4. **To achieve statistical significance** with this effect size, we would need approximately **50% more data** (either more branches or ~12 additional weeks)

---

## **Recommendations**

### **1. Extend the Pilot (Recommended)**
Continue the current arrangement for **8-12 more weeks**. This would:
- Narrow the confidence interval
- Provide clearer evidence for decision-making
- Allow struggling branches more time to adapt

### **2. Investigate the 6 Declining Branches**
Understand why 30% of switched branches saw scores drop:
- Implementation issues?
- Different branch characteristics?
- Temporary disruption during transition?

### **3. Hold Systemwide Rollout Decision**
Given the uncertainty, defer the full rollout decision until:
- Additional data is collected, OR
- The confidence interval excludes zero

### **4. If Proceeding Anyway...**
If the board wishes to move forward despite uncertainty:
- Implement in phases (not all at once)
- Build in explicit checkpoints for reevaluation
- Be prepared to reverse course if scores decline

---

## **Bottom Line Restated**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did scores improve in switched branches? | **Yes, on average** |
| Can we attribute this to the new procedure? | **Probably, but not certainly** |
| Is the evidence strong enough for systemwide rollout? | **No—more data needed** |
| Is there evidence of harm? | **No—lower bound is near zero** |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure shows **promise but not proof**. The board faces a choice:

- **Conservative path:** Extend the pilot, gather more data, decide with greater confidence
- **Aggressive path:** Proceed with rollout based on suggestive (but inconclusive) evidence

Given that the procedure involves operational changes across all branches, we recommend the **conservative path**. An additional 2-3 months of data would transform this from an uncertain decision into a confident one.
