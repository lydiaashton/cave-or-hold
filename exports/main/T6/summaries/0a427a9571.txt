# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The data **suggests** the new procedure improved service scores, but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive**. The estimated effect is +1.9 points, but we cannot rule out that the true effect is zero (or even slightly negative). The board faces a decision under uncertainty.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What the Data Shows
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +1.9 points |
| 95% confidence interval | -0.4 to +4.3 points |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.11 (not significant at 5% level) |

### What This Means
- The **point estimate is positive** and in the expected direction
- However, the **confidence interval includes zero**, meaning no effect is plausible
- With only 8 weeks of post-switch data across 34 branches, we have **limited statistical power**

### Branch-Level Pattern
- **75% of treatment branches** improved after switching (vs. 43% of control branches)
- This pattern is consistent with a real effect, but not definitive proof

---

## **Decision Framework**

| If the board prioritizes... | Recommended action |
|----------------------------|-------------------|
| **Statistical certainty** | Wait for more data (3-6 more months) |
| **Potential upside** | Proceed with rollout (up to ~4 point gain possible) |
| **Risk avoidance** | Pilot in more branches before full rollout |

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased rollout while continuing to collect data.**

Rationale:
1. The point estimate is positive and meaningful (~4% improvement)
2. No negative side effects were detected
3. The cost of rollout appears modest relative to potential benefit
4. Continued monitoring will resolve the uncertainty

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Roll out to 5-10 additional branches** in Q1 2027
2. **Track all branches for 6 more months** to build statistical power
3. **Reconvene the board in Q3 2027** with updated evidence
4. **Document implementation practices** to identify what drives success
