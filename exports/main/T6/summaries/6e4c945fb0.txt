# **Library Branch Shelving Procedure: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The data do not provide statistically conclusive evidence that the new procedure improves service scores.** While the estimated effect is positive (+1.8 points), the result is not statistically significant at conventional levels (p = 0.097), and the confidence interval includes the possibility of no effect or even a slight negative effect.

**Recommendation:** Continue the current pilot and collect more data before making a system-wide decision. If the board wishes to proceed despite the uncertainty, a limited phased expansion to a few additional branches would be more defensible than a full rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. What the Data Show
| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.83 points** | Suggests improvement |
| P-value | **0.097** | Not significant at 5% level |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.33 to +4.00** | Includes zero (no effect) |

### 2. What This Means
- **We cannot reject the possibility that the new procedure has no real effect** on service scores
- The observed improvement could plausibly be due to random variation
- At the same time, we cannot rule out a meaningful positive effect (up to +4 points)

### 3. Supporting Observations
- **Parallel trends validated:** Before the switch, treatment and control branches showed similar trajectories (p = 0.46)
- **Consistent post-switch pattern:** All 8 weeks after the switch showed positive differences between treatment and control branches
- **Study design is sound:** 34 branches over 17 weeks, with appropriate controls for staffing and visitor volume

## **Next Steps**

### Recommended Actions

3. **If proceeding despite uncertainty:**
