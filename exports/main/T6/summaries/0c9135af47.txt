# LIBRARY COOPERATIVE BOARD MEMORANDUM

## Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 15, 2026

---

## BOTTOM LINE

**We cannot conclude with statistical confidence that the new procedure improves service scores.** The estimated effect is positive (+2 points), but the evidence falls short of conventional statistical standards (p=0.084). The true effect could plausibly range from slightly negative to moderately positive (−0.3 to +4.5 points). 

**Decision context:** There is no evidence the new procedure harms service quality. The board must decide whether the *possibility* of improvement justifies rollout, or whether more evidence is needed.

---

### Statistical Evidence

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Effect estimate | +2.13 points | Treatment branches improved ~2 points more than control |
| 95% Confidence Interval | −0.29 to +4.55 | Zero effect is plausible; so is a 4.5-point gain |
| P-value | 0.084 | Does not meet 0.05 threshold; modest evidence |
| Probability effect is positive | ~96% | One-sided; suggests direction is likely correct |

### What This Means

- **Statistically:** We cannot rule out that the observed improvement occurred by chance
- **Practically:** The new procedure shows no signs of harm and may provide meaningful benefit
- **Scientifically:** More data would be needed to reach a definitive conclusion

### Validation Checks

## NEXT STEPS

### Option 1: Cautious Rollout (Recommended if benefits outweigh costs)
- Proceed with phased expansion to additional branches
- Continue monitoring service scores
- Accept the risk that the true effect may be smaller than estimated or zero

### Option 2: Extended Pilot (Recommended if rollout is costly)
- Continue the current study for 8–12 more weeks
- This would increase statistical power and narrow the confidence interval
- Make a more informed decision with stronger evidence

### Option 3: Status Quo
- Maintain current arrangement (some branches switched, others not)
- Low risk but forfeits potential benefits if the procedure does work

### Recommendation

**If rollout costs are low:** Proceed with Option 1. The evidence, while not conclusive, points in a positive direction with no detected harms.

**If rollout costs are significant:** Choose Option 2. Invest in additional data collection before committing cooperative-wide resources.
