# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure is associated with an estimated 2-point improvement in service scores (about 4%), but this result is not statistically significant at conventional levels.** The evidence is suggestive but not conclusive. We recommend a cautious, monitored rollout rather than immediate system-wide adoption.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** were studied over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **14 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 50.8 | 53.9 | +3.2 |
| Branches with old procedure | 52.2 | 53.2 | +1.1 |
| **Estimated effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

### Statistical Confidence — Important Caveat

| Analysis Method | Estimated Effect | 95% Confidence Interval | Statistically Significant? |
|-----------------|------------------|-------------------------|---------------------------|
| Recommended (cluster-robust) | +2.0 points | -0.7 to +4.7 | **No** (p = 0.16) |
| Branch-level (conservative) | +2.1 points | -0.5 to +4.7 | **No** (p = 0.12) |

**What this means:** The confidence intervals include zero, so we cannot rule out that the true effect is zero or even slightly negative. The sample size (34 branches) limits our ability to detect smaller effects with confidence.

### What We Can Say With Confidence
- All analysis methods estimate a **positive effect of approximately 2 points**
- The direction of the effect matches expectations
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (p = 0.93), supporting the validity of the comparison
- A 2-point improvement would be **meaningful for patrons** if the effect is real

---

## **Next Steps**

### Recommendation
**Proceed with a phased rollout accompanied by continued monitoring**, rather than immediate system-wide adoption or rejection of the procedure.

### Rationale
- The estimated benefit is in the expected direction and would be valuable if real
- Statistical uncertainty is due primarily to limited sample size, not contradictory evidence
- A phased approach allows the cooperative to gather more data while beginning to realize potential benefits

### Implementation Plan
1. **Expand to 5–10 additional branches** over the next 8–12 weeks
2. **Continue tracking service scores** weekly for all branches
3. **Re-analyze after 12 weeks** with the larger sample to achieve greater statistical confidence
4. **Set a decision checkpoint**: If the effect remains positive and grows more precise, proceed to full rollout

### Alternative Consideration
If the board requires statistical certainty before any expansion, **extend the observation period** for the current 34 branches. An additional 8–12 weeks of data would improve statistical power and narrow the confidence intervals.
