# **REVISED MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure likely improved service scores, but the effect is modest and took time to emerge.** The more rigorous analysis suggests an average improvement of **approximately 0.9 points** (about **1.8%**), with the effect becoming clearer 2-3 weeks after implementation. There is no evidence of harm, and the trend is positive.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but set realistic expectations and monitor closely.**

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**Study Overview**
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **284 branches** maintained the old procedure (control group)

**Results – Two Analytical Approaches**

| Analysis Method | Estimated Effect | Confidence |
|-----------------|------------------|------------|
| Simple comparison | +1.82 points | High statistical significance |
| Week-adjusted (more rigorous) | +0.88 points | Moderate statistical significance |

**Why the Difference?**
The simpler analysis doesn't account for week-to-week fluctuations that affect all branches (e.g., holidays, seasonal patterns). The week-adjusted analysis is more reliable.

**Effect Over Time**

| Weeks After Switch | Effect | Significance |
|-------------------|--------|--------------|
| Weeks 1-2 (15-16) | Minimal | Not significant |
| Weeks 3-4 (17-18) | +1.3 to +1.6 | Statistically significant |
| Weeks 5-8 (19-22) | +0.7 to +1.2 | Mixed significance |

**What This Means**
- The effect was **not immediate** – branches needed 2-3 weeks to realize benefits
- This suggests a **learning curve** or adjustment period
- The average improvement of ~0.9 points is **modest but meaningful** at scale

---

### **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve rollout with phased implementation** – Allow 3-4 weeks per branch for adjustment before evaluating success

2. **Set realistic expectations** – Communicate that improvements may take 2-3 weeks to materialize

3. **Provide training support** – The delayed effect suggests branches need time to master the new procedure; invest in onboarding

4. **Monitor for 12 weeks post-rollout** – Track whether effects persist and whether all branches achieve similar gains

5. **Identify high-performers** – Some branches may achieve larger gains; document their practices for peer learning

---

### **CAVEATS**

- Treatment and control branches had some pre-existing differences, which introduces uncertainty
- The effect is positive but smaller than initial analysis suggested
- Continued monitoring is essential to confirm benefits at full scale
