# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a modest but statistically significant improvement in service scores on average (+2.1 points, ~4%).** However, results varied considerably: while 76% of switched branches improved, **24% actually saw scores decline**. We recommend a **phased rollout** with close monitoring and support for struggling branches, rather than an immediate system-wide mandate.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Average Improvement, But Variable Results**

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6-14) | After Switch (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches (90 branches) | 50.11 | 52.35 | **+2.23** |
| Old Procedure Branches (60 branches) | 50.61 | 50.73 | +0.12 |

**Net effect: +2.1 points** (statistically significant, p < 0.01)

### **2. Not All Branches Benefited**

| Outcome | % of Switched Branches |
|---------|----------------------|
| Score decreased | **24%** |
| Score increased < 1 point | 34% |
| Score increased > 5 points | 22% |

### **3. Statistical Confidence**

- 95% confidence interval: **+0.9 to +3.4 points**
- Effect size: **0.24 standard deviations** (small-to-moderate)
- Results hold after controlling for staffing and visitor volume

### **4. Study Limitations**

- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** – long-term effects unknown
- **No cost or implementation burden data** available
- Quasi-experimental design (not randomized), though pre-trends were parallel

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve a phased rollout** rather than immediate system-wide adoption:
   - Start with 15-20 volunteer branches
   - Provide additional training and support during transition

2. **Monitor closely** for 12 weeks:
   - Track service scores weekly
   - Identify branches struggling with the transition early
   - Be prepared to provide targeted support or pause rollout if needed

3. **Collect implementation cost data**:
   - Staff time required for training
   - Any changes to operational efficiency
   - Branch-level feedback on practical challenges

4. **Re-evaluate after the pilot phase** before committing to full rollout across all 60 remaining branches.

5. **Investigate why some branches struggled** – there may be branch characteristics (size, staffing, layout) that predict success with the new procedure.
