## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure protected service quality and should be rolled out system-wide.** During the 8 weeks after implementation, all branches experienced declining service scores. However, branches using the new procedure declined by only **0.8 points**, while branches using the old procedure declined by **3.0 points**. The new procedure **prevented approximately 2.1 points of deterioration**—a meaningful protective effect that is statistically robust.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The New Procedure Mitigated Score Declines

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| 425 branches using new procedure | 53.09 | 52.29 | **-0.80** |
| 284 branches using old procedure | 53.06 | 50.10 | **-2.96** |

**Protective effect of new procedure: +2.14 points relative to control**

Both groups experienced declines (possibly due to seasonal factors or external pressures), but the new procedure substantially buffered branches against deterioration.

### 2. Protection Strengthened Over Time

The protective effect grew as staff became more familiar with the new procedure:

| Weeks After Switch | Protective Effect |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Week 1 | +0.85 points |
| Week 4 | +1.84 points |
| Week 8 | +3.18 points |

Full benefits appear to materialize after 4-6 weeks of implementation.

### 3. Effect Is Consistent Across Branches

The protective effect held regardless of staffing levels:

- Low-staffing branches: +2.12 points
- Medium-staffing branches: +2.06 points
- High-staffing branches: +2.34 points

### 4. Analysis Is Statistically Sound

- Pre-implementation trends were similar between groups (validating the comparison)
- Results hold when controlling for branch-specific characteristics and weekly variations
- Effect is highly statistically significant (p < 0.001)

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure to all 284 branches currently using the old method. The procedure demonstrably protects service quality during challenging periods.
