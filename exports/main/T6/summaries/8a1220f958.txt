# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but with monitoring and support.**

The new procedure is associated with a **2.1-point improvement** in service scores (approximately 4%). The effect is statistically significant and sustained over 8 weeks. However, this was not a randomized trial—branches self-selected into switching—which introduces uncertainty. Additionally, **19% of switching branches saw no improvement or declined**, suggesting the procedure may not work equally well everywhere.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measured Impact**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| 425 branches that switched | 50.7 | 54.0 | +3.3 |
| 284 branches that did not switch | 50.7 | 51.9 | +1.2 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.1** |

**95% Confidence Interval:** 1.6 to 2.7 points

### **2. Important Caveats**

| Concern | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Non-randomized assignment** | Branches chose whether to switch; motivated branches may have both switched AND improved for other reasons |
| **Heterogeneous effects** | 81% of switching branches improved, but 19% saw no benefit or declined |
| **Limited timeframe** | Only 8 weeks of post-data; long-term sustainability unknown |
| **Pre-trend differences** | Groups differed in weeks 6-11, though trends aligned in weeks 12-14 (immediately before switch) |
| **No cost data** | Implementation costs, training time, and disruption not measured |

### **3. Distribution of Effects Among Switching Branches**

| Percentile | Service Score Change |
|------------|---------------------|
| 10th (bottom) | -1.4 points |
| 50th (median) | +3.2 points |
| 90th (top) | +8.1 points |
