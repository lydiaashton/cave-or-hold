# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores, but the evidence is not statistically conclusive**. The best estimate is a **+2.4 point improvement** (approximately 5%), and there is roughly a **90% probability the true effect is positive**. However, because the 95% confidence interval includes zero (-0.46 to +5.31), we cannot claim statistical significance at conventional standards. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout if the board is comfortable acting on suggestive evidence with low downside risk. If the board requires statistical certainty, extend the pilot and collect more data.

## **Decision Options for the Board**

### **Option A: Proceed with Rollout**
**Choose this if:** The board is comfortable acting on suggestive evidence, the cost of rollout is low, and potential upside outweighs waiting.

**Rationale:** 
- Point estimate is positive and in the expected direction
- No signal of harm in the data
- 5% improvement would be meaningful for patrons if real
- Delaying denies potential benefits to remaining branches

### **Option B: Extend Pilot, Collect More Data**
**Choose this if:** The board requires statistical certainty before committing, or if rollout costs are significant.

**Rationale:**
- Current sample (8 post-switch weeks) may be underpowered
- 8-16 additional weeks would narrow the confidence interval
- Reduces risk of adopting an ineffective procedure

### **Option C: Hybrid Approach (Recommended)**
**Begin phased rollout while continuing to monitor.** Expand to half the remaining branches now, evaluate after 8 weeks, then decide on full rollout.

## **Our Recommendation**

**Option C: Phased Rollout with Continued Monitoring**

This balances the positive signal in the data against the statistical uncertainty:

1. **Roll out to 7 of the 14 remaining branches** in the next 4 weeks
2. **Continue tracking service scores** for all branches
3. **Re-analyze in Q2 2026** with 16+ weeks of post-switch data
4. **Complete full rollout** if the effect remains positive and confidence increases
