## **Subject: Impact of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores**

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure produced a modest but statistically significant improvement in service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **1.9 points** (95% CI: 0.7 to 3.1 points) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **4% improvement** on the service index.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but implement with monitoring. The effect is real and positive, but modest in magnitude. Approximately 38% of switching branches saw no improvement or a decline, suggesting implementation quality matters. A phased rollout with support for struggling branches is advisable.

**Interpretation:** The average effect is positive, but more than one-third of branches did not benefit. This suggests **implementation quality** or **branch characteristics** may influence outcomes.

### 3. Control Group Declined Slightly

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|

The control group experienced a slight decline over the same period. The new procedure appears to both **improve scores** and **protect against decline**.
