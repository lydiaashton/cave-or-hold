# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The data suggest the new procedure may have improved service scores by about 2 points, but the evidence is not statistically conclusive.** The study was not large enough to confirm the effect with certainty. However, there is no evidence of harm, and the weight of evidence points toward a modest benefit.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with **new procedure** | 50.4 | 52.8 | **+2.4** |
| Branches with **old procedure** | 46.5 | 46.6 | +0.1 |
| **Estimated effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.3 points** |

### **What the Statistics Tell Us**

| Finding | Interpretation |
|---------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +2.3 points (positive direction, as expected) |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.12 (not conclusive at standard thresholds) |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.6 to +5.3 points |
| Probability effect is positive | ~94% |

### **What This Means**
- The **best estimate** is a 2-point improvement, consistent with expectations
- However, we **cannot rule out** the possibility of no real effect
- We **can rule out** meaningful harm (scores did not drop)
- The study had limited power—it was designed to detect effects of 4+ points; the observed effect is smaller

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout, with continued monitoring.**

### **Rationale**
1. **No evidence of harm:** The lower bound of uncertainty is essentially zero
2. **Evidence points positive:** ~94% probability the effect is beneficial
3. **Consistent with expectations:** The direction matches what we anticipated
4. **Practical consideration:** 20 of 34 branches have already transitioned

### **Implementation Safeguards**
- Monitor service scores for **all branches** for 8–12 weeks post-rollout
- Flag any branch with a **decline of 5+ points** for immediate review
- Plan a **follow-up analysis** in Q2 2027 with a full year of data

---

## **Bottom Line Restated**

We do not have definitive proof the new procedure works. But the evidence points in the expected direction, there's no sign of negative impact, and the potential upside justifies completing the rollout with appropriate monitoring.
