# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

The new procedure shows a **positive signal** (+1.75 points) but **does not meet conventional statistical significance** (p = 0.10). There is approximately a **95% probability the true effect is positive**, but we cannot be confident about the magnitude.
