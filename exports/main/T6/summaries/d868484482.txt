# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Impact of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The evidence suggests the new procedure has a positive effect, but with important caveats.** Branches that switched showed service score gains of approximately **+1 to +1.5 points** in weeks 6-8 after implementation. However, there was no detectable benefit in the first 5 weeks, and the analysis has limitations due to pre-existing differences between branches that switched versus those that didn't.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but implement careful monitoring and allow 6+ weeks before evaluating success at each branch.

**3. Important Limitations**
- **Pre-existing differences:** Branches that chose to switch had systematically different service score patterns in the early weeks of the study, raising questions about whether they were also different in unobserved ways
- **Convergence before switch:** The groups' trends did align in the 4 weeks immediately before the switch, which supports (but doesn't prove) the validity of the comparison
- **Modest effect size:** Even the significant effects represent only ~2-3% improvement relative to the baseline score of 50

**4. Simple Difference-in-Differences Estimate**
The pooled estimate of **+1.94 points** (p < 0.001) should be interpreted cautiously—it averages together weeks with no effect and weeks with positive effects, and doesn't fully account for pre-existing differences between groups.
