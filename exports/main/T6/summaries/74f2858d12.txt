# **REVISED MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 15, 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We recommend AGAINST a full system-wide rollout at this time.**

While branches using the new procedure show higher service scores, we cannot confidently attribute this to the procedure itself. **Treatment branches were already improving faster than control branches before the switch**, which means our estimated effect (+2 points) likely overstates the true impact. More evidence is needed before committing to system-wide implementation.

---

## **What We Found**

### **The Concerning Pattern**

| Week | Control Avg | Treatment Avg | Gap (T-C) |
|------|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| 6 | 46.4 | 46.3 | -0.1 |
| 8 | 53.0 | 53.2 | +0.2 |
| 10 | 46.6 | 48.6 | +1.9 |
| 12 | 51.0 | 52.9 | +2.0 |
| 14 | 49.4 | 51.3 | +1.8 |
| **15 (switch)** | **50.3** | **52.3** | **+2.0** |
| 17 | 49.6 | 52.3 | +2.7 |
| 20 | 51.3 | 55.0 | +3.7 |
| 22 | 45.5 | 49.7 | +4.2 |

**The gap was widening BEFORE the switch** (0.34 points per week, p=0.001). This violates the key assumption needed to claim the new procedure caused the improvement.

### **What This Means**

| Scenario | Likely Explanation |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Optimistic** | New procedure adds ~1 point to an already-widening gap |
| **Realistic** | Most of the +2 point "effect" reflects pre-existing differences |
| **Pessimistic** | The procedure has little to no real effect |

We cannot distinguish between these scenarios with the current data.

---

## **Why This Matters**

The difference-in-differences method requires **parallel trends**—treatment and control groups should be moving similarly before the intervention. **This assumption is violated here.** Treatment branches may have been:
- Better managed overall
- More motivated to improve (selection bias)
- Located in areas with different member demographics

Any of these could explain the gap without the procedure itself being effective.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Pause system-wide rollout** pending additional evidence |
| **2** | **Extend the pilot** for 8-12 more weeks to gather more post-switch data |
| **3** | **Consider a randomized rollout** for remaining branches to enable cleaner causal inference |
| **4** | **Investigate branch characteristics** that correlate with improvement (location, size, staffing) |
| **5** | **Revisit the decision** after 3-6 months with additional data |

---

## **What Would Change Our Recommendation**

We would support rollout if future analysis shows:
- The treatment-control gap **stabilized before the switch** (parallel pre-trends)
- The post-switch improvement **exceeds the pre-switch trend**
- Effects are **consistent across different types of branches**
