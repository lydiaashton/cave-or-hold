# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The data suggests the new procedure **likely improved service scores**, but we cannot say this with high statistical confidence. The estimated effect is +2.2 points, but the evidence is not strong enough to rule out the possibility of no effect. Given the positive direction of the results and no detected harms, a full rollout is reasonable—but expectations should be tempered, and continued monitoring is essential.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Measured**
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores averaged around 50 points (higher is better)

### **Results**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 52.05 | 50.90 | −1.15 |
| Branches adopting new procedure | 51.78 | 52.80 | +1.02 |

**Estimated Effect: +2.22 service score points**

### **Statistical Confidence**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| P-value | 0.097 | Not significant at conventional 5% level |
| 95% Confidence Interval | −0.4 to +4.9 | Includes zero; effect could be negligible |
| Probability effect is positive | ~95% | Evidence points in favorable direction |

### **What This Means**
1. **The results are encouraging but not definitive** — the point estimate is positive, but statistical uncertainty means we cannot be confident the procedure caused the improvement
2. **Control branches declined** during the same period (−1.15 points), while treatment branches improved (+1.02 points) — this relative difference is what drives the estimated effect
3. **No negative effects detected** — there is no evidence the new procedure harmed service quality
4. **Pre-trends were parallel** — before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories, supporting the validity of the comparison

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with rollout, but with measured expectations.**

The evidence does not conclusively prove the new procedure improves service scores, but it also does not suggest any harm. The positive point estimate, combined with the absence of negative effects, supports moving forward. However, the board should anticipate that the true effect may be smaller than hoped—or possibly zero.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Complete the rollout** to remaining branches, but frame it as a cooperative-wide improvement rather than a proven intervention
2. **Continue rigorous monitoring** for at least 12 weeks to accumulate more data and narrow the confidence interval
3. **Set realistic expectations** — a 2-point gain is modest; larger improvements may require additional interventions
4. **Reassess in 6 months** — if scores do not improve as expected, consider alternative or complementary approaches

---

*This analysis uses a difference-in-differences design with branch-level clustered standard errors. The marginal statistical significance (p=0.097) means the findings should be interpreted with appropriate caution.*
