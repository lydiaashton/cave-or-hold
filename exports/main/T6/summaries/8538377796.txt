# **Impact of New Stock Rotation Procedure on Library Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2.5 points (5%).** This contradicts the initial expectation that the change would have no effect. The improvement is statistically significant and consistent across analytical approaches.

**The data supports expanding the new procedure**, though the board should weigh this benefit against implementation costs and operational considerations.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **150 branches**: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 kept the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring in week 15
- Used **difference-in-differences** analysis to isolate the procedure's effect

### Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.82 | 49.64 | -0.18 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.21 | 52.55 | +2.34 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.52** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Effect:** +2.53 service score points
- **95% confidence interval:** [1.22, 3.83] points
- **Significance:** p < 0.001
- Result holds after controlling for staffing and visitor volume

### Validation
- Pre-switch trends between groups showed no systematic divergence
- Control branches had no meaningful change, ruling out system-wide factors
