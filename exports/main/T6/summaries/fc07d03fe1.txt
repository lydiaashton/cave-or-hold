## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +2.1 points |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.0005 |
| 95% confidence interval | 0.9 to 3.2 points |
| Effect size | 4% of average score (0.22 standard deviations) |

### Why We're Confident

1. **Comparison group held steady**: Branches keeping the old procedure showed minimal change (+0.5 points), while switching branches improved (+2.6 points)
2. **Pre-trends were parallel**: Before week 15, both groups moved together (no significant difference in trends, p = 0.25)
3. **Not explained by other factors**: Result persists after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
4. **Visual confirmation**: Clear divergence between groups appears exactly at week 15

### What Remains Unknown

- **Why did this happen?** The procedure change was operational (shelving/rotation), not service-facing. Possible explanations include staff confidence, faster item location, or unmeasured factors.
- **Will it persist?** We have only 8 weeks of post-switch data.
- **Are all branches benefiting?** We haven't examined heterogeneity across branch types or sizes.
