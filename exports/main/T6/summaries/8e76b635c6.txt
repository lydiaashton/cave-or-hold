# **Analysis of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Date:** November 2026
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Procedure Impact on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain** effect on service scores. Branches that switched improved by **2.9 points relative to control branches**, but the confidence interval includes zero, meaning we **cannot rule out no effect**. The evidence is **suggestive but not conclusive**. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued evaluation** rather than immediate system-wide adoption.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches studied | 34 (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| Switch date | Week 15 |
| Method | Difference-in-Differences |

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** | 48.65 | 50.45 | **+1.80** |
| **Old Procedure** | 53.61 | 52.54 | **−1.07** |

**Net Effect of New Procedure: +2.87 points**

| Statistic | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| 95% Confidence Interval | −0.54 to +6.28 points |
| p-value | 0.10 |

### **What the Data Tells Us**

| Finding | Interpretation |
|---------|----------------|
| Point estimate is positive (+2.87) | The new procedure *likely* helps |
| Confidence interval includes zero | But we're not statistically certain |
| 11 of 20 treatment branches improved | Effects vary across branches |
| Pre-trends were parallel | The comparison is valid |
| Control group declined slightly | External factors may be affecting all branches |

---

## **Why Isn't This More Conclusive?**

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Only 8 weeks of post-switch data | Limited statistical power |
| 34 branches total | Modest sample size |
| Week-to-week score variation | Adds noise to the signal |

**With 12–15 more weeks of data, we could narrow the confidence interval substantially.**

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased rollout while continuing to collect data.**

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Full rollout now** | Maximizes potential benefits quickly | Risk if effect is smaller than estimated |
| **Wait for more data** | Greater certainty before committing | Delays potential benefits to 14 branches |
| **Phased rollout** (recommended) | Balances benefit and risk; builds evidence | Requires ongoing monitoring |

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| Roll out to 5–7 additional branches | 4 weeks |
| Continue weekly score tracking | Ongoing |
| Re-analyze with expanded data | 12 weeks |
| Make final rollout decision | After re-analysis |

---

## **Bottom Line Restated**

We understand the board expected clear evidence of improvement. The data shows a **positive direction** consistent with that expectation, but **statistical uncertainty remains**. A phased approach allows the cooperative to capture potential benefits while building the evidence base for a confident final decision.
