## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a statistically significant benefit, but the practical impact is modest.** Switched branches improved slightly (+0.8 points) while control branches declined (-1.3 points), resulting in a net difference of **2.2 points** (p<0.001). However, only **51% of switched branches saw any improvement** — suggesting the procedure helps some branches but not others.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but **identify which branches benefit most** before mandating system-wide. Consider a phased approach with ongoing evaluation.

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Statistically Real, Practically Modest
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Net treatment effect | +2.21 points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 0.99 to 3.43 points |
| Raw improvement (switched branches) | +0.79 points |
| Effect size | ~23% of one standard deviation |

### 2. Protection vs. Improvement
The data suggests the new procedure may **protect against decline** rather than drive dramatic gains:
- Switched branches: **53.6 → 54.4** (slight gain)
- Control branches: **53.3 → 51.9** (notable decline)

Something affected all branches after week 14; switched branches were more resilient.

### 3. Inconsistent Benefits Across Branches
| Outcome | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|---------|-------------------|------------------|
| Improved | 46 (51%) | 17 (28%) |
| Declined | 44 (49%) | 43 (72%) |

Nearly half of switched branches saw no benefit. A blanket rollout may not serve all branches equally.

### 4. Benefits Took Time to Materialize
- **Weeks 15-18:** No detectable effect
- **Weeks 19-22:** Clear positive effect

Expect a **4-week adjustment period** per branch.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Analyze which branch characteristics predict success** (size, location, staffing, visit volume) before full rollout.

2. **Pilot with 15-20 remaining branches** representing different profiles; monitor for 8 weeks.

3. **Develop implementation support** for the 4-week adjustment period (training, check-ins, troubleshooting).

4. **Set realistic expectations**: The procedure is unlikely to transform service scores but may protect against decline.

5. **Revisit in 6 months** to assess whether benefits persist and whether certain branch types should use alternative approaches.
