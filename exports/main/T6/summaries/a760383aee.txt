# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is inconclusive.** The new procedure is associated with a 2-point improvement in service scores, but we cannot confidently rule out that the true effect is zero. The data do not provide statistically reliable evidence that the procedure caused the improvement.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Point estimate** | +2.3 points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | -0.6 to +5.3 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.12 (not significant) |
| **Branches in study** | 34 (20 switched, 14 control) |

### **What the Confidence Interval Means**

The data are compatible with **three different realities**:
1. **No effect** (true effect ≈ 0)
2. **Modest benefit** (true effect ≈ 2–3 points)
3. **Moderate benefit** (true effect ≈ 5 points)

We cannot distinguish among these possibilities with the current data.

### **Important Caveat: Pre-Existing Differences**

Treatment and control branches had **different service score trajectories before the switch**. This violates a key assumption of our analysis method and means the 2.3-point estimate may not reflect a true causal effect. Some of the apparent improvement could be due to pre-existing differences between branches that chose (or were chosen) to switch.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Do not proceed with immediate systemwide rollout.** Instead:

### **Option 1: Extend the Pilot (Recommended)**
| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| Continue current pilot design | 8–12 additional weeks |
| Re-analyze with expanded data | Week 30 |
| Board decision on rollout | Week 32 |

**Why:** More data will narrow the confidence interval and allow a clearer decision.

### **Option 2: Proceed with Enhanced Monitoring**
If operational pressures require a decision now:
- Roll out to remaining branches
- **Commit to rigorous evaluation** with predefined success/failure thresholds
- Be prepared to **reverse course** if scores decline

| Success threshold | Systemwide increase ≥2 points within 8 weeks |
|-------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Failure threshold | Any branch declines ≥3 points sustained |

---

## **Why We Can't Be Certain**

1. **Sample size is limited**: 34 branches over 17 weeks provides modest statistical power
2. **Pre-trend differences**: Switching and non-switching branches behaved differently before the intervention
3. **External factors**: Staffing and visitation patterns vary week-to-week and may confound the results

---

## **Bottom Line for Decision-Makers**

| If you prioritize... | Recommended action |
|---------------------|-------------------|
| **Certainty** | Extend the pilot; gather more evidence |
| **Speed** | Roll out now with strict monitoring and reversal triggers |
| **Risk avoidance** | Extend the pilot; do not expand until effects are clearer |
