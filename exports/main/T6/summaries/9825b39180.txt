# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Do not proceed with system-wide rollout at this time.** The evidence does not demonstrate that the new procedure improved service scores. While switched branches showed a slightly larger gain than non-switched branches (+1.7 points), this difference is not statistically distinguishable from zero. The data cannot rule out that the new procedure has no effect—or even a slight negative effect. Continue the pilot and collect more data before making an expansion decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches**: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 continued with the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), with the switch implemented at week 15
- Analysis uses **difference-in-differences** to isolate the procedure's effect

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Pre-Switch (Weeks 6–14) | Post-Switch (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|------------------------|--------------------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 50.26 | 56.37 | +6.11 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 50.60 | 54.96 | +4.36 |
| **Difference (Treatment Effect)** | | | **+1.75** |

### **Statistical Conclusion**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated Effect | +1.75 points | Point estimate is positive |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.31 to +3.80 | **Includes zero** — effect could be null |
| P-value | 0.096 | **Not statistically significant** (p > 0.05) |
| Effect Size | 0.17 standard deviations | Small, even if real |

### **Critical Context**
- **Both groups improved substantially** after week 15 (+4 to +6 points), suggesting external factors (seasonal trends, system-wide initiatives) drove most of the gain
- The new procedure's **incremental contribution** is uncertain — the data cannot confirm it added value beyond these broader improvements
- Pre-switch trends were parallel (p = 0.87), validating the study design

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Do Not Expand Yet**: The evidence is insufficient to justify the cost and disruption of system-wide implementation.

2. **Extend the Pilot**: Continue monitoring both groups for 6–12 more months. More post-treatment data will improve statistical precision.

3. **Consider Increasing Sample**: If feasible, randomize additional branches to treatment to strengthen the analysis.

4. **Track Implementation Fidelity**: Verify that switched branches are actually implementing the new procedure as intended.

5. **Re-evaluate in 6 Months**: With more data, the effect may become clearer—either confirming a real benefit or revealing the procedure has no meaningful impact.
