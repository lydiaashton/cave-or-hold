### 1. Statistically Significant but Modest Improvement
- Branches using the new procedure improved by **2.1 points** on the service score index
- The 95% confidence interval is **0.9 to 3.3 points**—the effect is reliably positive
- However, this represents only **0.2 standard deviations**, which is considered a small-to-moderate effect in practice

### 2. Strong Statistical Evidence
- The effect is **highly statistically significant** (p = 0.0005)
- Analysis controlled for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Results are based on 150 branches over 17 weeks

### 3. Valid Comparison Between Groups
- Treatment branches had slightly higher baseline scores (50.3 vs 48.3), but this difference was not statistically significant (p=0.13)
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, supporting the validity of the comparison
