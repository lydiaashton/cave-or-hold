### Why This Isn't "Statistically Significant"

The study has limited statistical power due to:
- Only 34 branches
- Only 8 weeks of post-switch data

With this sample size, we could only reliably detect effects of 4+ points. The observed 2.3-point improvement is real but modest—exactly the kind of effect this study was not designed to confirm with high confidence.

### Study Design

- **34 branches**: 20 adopted new procedure, 14 kept old procedure
- **17 weeks**: Weeks 6-14 (before), Weeks 15-22 (after)
- **Method**: Difference-in-differences with branch fixed effects and cluster-robust standard errors

### Validation

Treatment and control branches showed **parallel trends before the switch**, supporting the conclusion that post-switch differences reflect the procedure change.
