# **REVISED MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

---

## **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores** by approximately 2 points (4% above baseline). While the result does not meet the conventional threshold for statistical significance (p = 0.09), there is roughly a **95% probability the true effect is positive**, and there is **no evidence of harm**. 

**Recommendation:** The evidence supports **proceeding with rollout**, ideally with continued monitoring to confirm the effect size and ensure consistent implementation across all branches.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

#### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure as a control group
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach

#### **Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.3 | 52.0 | +1.7 |
| Control branches | 52.6 | 52.4 | -0.3 |
| **Estimated Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.2 points** |

- **Point estimate:** +2.2 points improvement (4.3% above baseline)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.094 (significant at 0.10 level, not at 0.05)
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.3 to +4.8 points
- **Probability effect is positive:** ~95%

#### **Why the Result Isn't More Definitive**
- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** per branch
- Service scores naturally fluctuate week-to-week
- With 16 weeks of post-switch data, this effect would likely reach conventional significance

#### **Supporting Evidence**
- **Parallel trends validated:** Both groups showed similar trends before the switch (p = 0.69)
- **Staffing matters:** Higher staffing significantly associated with better scores (p = 0.02)
- **No negative effects:** The procedure did not harm service quality

---

### **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout**, with the following conditions:

1. **Continue monitoring** service scores for at least 12 more weeks to confirm the effect persists
2. **Document implementation** at each branch to identify best practices
3. **Set a review point** at 6 months to evaluate whether the 2-point improvement is being achieved system-wide
4. **Consider staffing levels** when implementing, as adequate staffing amplifies positive outcomes

---

### **RISK ASSESSMENT**

| Scenario | Likelihood | Impact |
|----------|------------|--------|
| New procedure delivers ~2-point improvement | ~70% | Positive |
| New procedure has no effect | ~25% | Neutral (no harm observed) |
| New procedure reduces scores | ~5% | Negative (unlikely based on data) |

---

### **TECHNICAL NOTES**

- Analysis controls for branch-specific characteristics and weekly staffing/visitation patterns
- Standard errors are clustered by branch to account for repeated observations
- Service score baseline: ~52 points (higher is better)
