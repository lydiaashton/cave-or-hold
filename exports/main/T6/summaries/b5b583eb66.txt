# **Memorandum to the Board**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

### **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores by approximately 1.8 points (4%)**. While the result does not meet the conventional threshold for statistical significance (p = 0.14), there is a **94% probability the true effect is positive**. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but implement monitoring to confirm the expected improvement.**

The evidence is sufficiently positive to warrant adoption, especially if implementation costs are modest. The main risk is that the true effect is smaller than estimated, not that the procedure is harmful.

---

### **Key Findings**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | +1.8 points | 4% improvement from baseline |
| p-value | 0.14 | Not significant at 0.05 threshold |
| Probability effect is positive | 94% | Strong suggestive evidence |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.5 to +4.1 | Mostly positive range |
| Branches that improved | 65% | Majority saw gains |
| Research design validity | ✓ Confirmed | Parallel trends satisfied |

**What the Numbers Mean:**
- The procedure is **more likely than not to help** (94% confidence in positive direction)
- The uncertainty is about **magnitude**, not direction
- To achieve p < 0.05 would require ~12 additional weeks of data
- Waiting for certainty has an opportunity cost: branches continue with the inferior procedure

---

### **Risk Assessment**

| Scenario | Probability | Impact |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Procedure works as estimated (+1.8 pts) | ~50% | Service improves across all branches |
| Procedure has smaller positive effect (+0.5 pts) | ~44% | Modest improvement, still beneficial |
| Procedure has no effect (0 pts) | ~5% | No gain, but no harm |
| Procedure is slightly negative (-0.5 pts) | ~1% | Minimal downside risk |

**Key Insight:** The confidence interval is asymmetric in practical terms—the upside potential (+4.1 points) exceeds the downside risk (-0.5 points).

---

### **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout, with these conditions:**

1. **Monitor service scores** for the first 12 weeks post-rollout to confirm improvement
2. **Document implementation** to ensure the procedure is applied consistently
3. **Set a review date** (e.g., 6 months) to evaluate whether expected benefits materialized
4. **Be prepared to adjust** if certain branches show no improvement or decline

**If the Board Prefers More Certainty:**
Extend the pilot by 12 weeks. This would provide definitive statistical evidence but delays potential benefits to 14 branches that are still using the old procedure.

### **Bottom Line for Decision-Making**

| Consideration | Favors Rollout | Favors Waiting |
|---------------|----------------|----------------|
| Strength of evidence | 94% probability of positive effect | p > 0.05 |
