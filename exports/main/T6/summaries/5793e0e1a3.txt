# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores on average, but not for all branches.** Across the 425 branches that adopted the new procedure, service scores increased by an average of **2.3 points** (4.7% improvement). However, **21% of branches saw no improvement or a decline**. Branches that were already performing well before the switch benefited less. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but implement monitoring and targeted support for branches that may not benefit.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Overall Impact
| Metric | Control Group (No Switch) | Treatment Group (Switched) |
|--------|---------------------------|----------------------------|
| Average score BEFORE switch | 48.7 | 50.1 |
| Average score AFTER switch | 49.4 | 53.2 |
| **Change in score** | **+0.8** | **+3.1** |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | — | **+2.3 points** |

### Effect Varies by Branch
- **79% of branches** (336 of 425) saw improvement after switching
- **21% of branches** (89 of 425) saw no improvement or a decline
- Branches with **lower baseline scores benefited more**; branches already performing well had less room to improve
- Effect size: 0.23 standard deviations (small-to-moderate)

### Statistical Confidence
- **Treatment effect:** +2.34 points
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 1.76 to 2.92 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, supporting causal interpretation

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve phased rollout** to remaining 284 branches, starting with lower-performing branches most likely to benefit

2. **Establish success metrics and monitoring** – Track service scores for 8–12 weeks post-implementation at each branch

3. **Create support protocol** – For branches where scores don't improve within 4–6 weeks, provide additional training or troubleshooting

4. **Document implementation factors** – Capture what worked well in the 336 branches that improved to identify best practices

5. **Reassess after 6 months** – Evaluate whether the 21% non-response rate persists and whether additional interventions are needed
