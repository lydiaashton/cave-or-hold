# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Extend the pilot and gather more data before system-wide rollout.** While switched branches show higher service scores (+1.9 points), we cannot confidently attribute this to the new procedure. Pre-existing differences between branches explain much or all of the observed gap. A 6–8 week extension would allow a clearer causal assessment.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Show**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| 425 switched branches | 51.6 | 52.7 | +1.1 |
| 284 control branches | 51.2 | 50.4 | −0.8 |
| **Raw difference** | | | **+1.9** |

### **What We Cannot Confirm**

| Issue | Why It Matters |
|-------|----------------|
| **Pre-trends not parallel** | Switched branches were already on a different trajectory before the change (p < 0.001) |
| **Convergence pattern** | Treatment branches went from 1.1 points BELOW control (week 6) to 1.8 points ABOVE (week 14) — all before the switch |
| **Limited post-data** | Only 8 weeks after switch; insufficient to distinguish treatment effect from continuing trends |
| **Possible explanations** | The observed gap could reflect: (1) real treatment effect, (2) mean reversion, or (3) unobserved branch characteristics |

### **Analysis Strengths**

- Large sample: 12,053 observations across 709 branches
- Control group available for comparison
- Effect is stable across post-switch weeks (no fade-out observed yet)

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Continue current pilot** | 6–8 more weeks | Gather additional post-switch data to strengthen causal inference |
| **Do NOT expand to remaining 284 branches yet** | Until analysis complete | Avoid scaling a procedure whose effect is uncertain |
| **Re-analyze with extended data** | Week 30 | More post-period data improves ability to separate trend from treatment |
| **If effect persists, approve rollout** | Q4 | Conditional on clearer evidence |

---

## **Conclusion**

The initial results are **encouraging but inconclusive**. Switched branches do show higher service scores, and there is no evidence of harm. However, the pre-existing differences between branches mean we cannot confidently claim the procedure caused the improvement. 

**Prudent course:** Extend the evaluation period before committing to system-wide implementation. If the effect is real, it will remain detectable with more data. If it fades, we avoid an ineffective rollout.
