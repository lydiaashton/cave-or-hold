### What This Means

The data suggests the new procedure probably helps, but we cannot be as certain as we would like. The confidence interval includes zero, meaning we cannot rule out no effect—but the bulk of the probability distribution favors a positive outcome.

### Important Caveats

1. **Self-selection bias**: Branches that chose to switch had higher service scores throughout the study, which may inflate the estimated benefit.

2. **Limited post-treatment data**: Only 8 weeks of data after the switch; effects may stabilize or change over time.

3. **Modest effect size**: Even if the full 2-point gain is real, it represents a ~4% improvement—meaningful but not transformative.
