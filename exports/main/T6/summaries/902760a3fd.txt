### **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests a positive effect, but with important caveats.** Branches using the new procedure showed service score gains of approximately **2.35 points**, but the result is sensitive to how we analyze the data. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide adoption.

---

### **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result | Confidence |
|--------|--------|------------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.35 points | Moderate |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.005 (with controls) | ⚠️ Model-dependent |
| **Simple comparison** | p = 0.115 (not significant) | ⚠️ |
| **Branches with gains** | 16 of 20 (80%) | Encouraging |

### **Important Limitations**

| Concern | Why It Matters |
|---------|----------------|
| **Pre-existing differences** | Switched branches started 4 points lower; some improvement may reflect natural recovery (regression to the mean) |
| **Inconsistent effects** | Weeks 18, 21, 22 showed little to no effect — the benefit may not be stable |
| **Limited post-data** | Only 8 weeks after implementation; long-term sustainability unknown |
| **Model sensitivity** | Significance depends on including branch/week controls |

---

### **Recommendation**

**Proceed with cautious expansion:**

1. **Roll out to 5–7 additional branches** (not all 14 at once)
2. **Monitor for 12 weeks** before further expansion
3. **Investigate the 4 switched branches that didn't improve** — identify implementation barriers
4. **Reconvene the board** after Q2 2027 with extended data to decide on full rollout
