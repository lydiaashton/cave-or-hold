# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests a modest, uncertain improvement.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed a statistically significant increase of approximately **2 points** in service scores overall. However, the effect appears to diminish over time and is small in practical terms. **We recommend a phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** switched to new procedure in week 15
- **14 branches** kept the old procedure (control group)

### **Results**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| **Overall treatment effect** | +1.95 points | Statistically significant (p=0.032) |
| **95% Confidence interval** | +0.17 to +3.74 | Lower bound near zero |
| **Effect size (Cohen's d)** | 0.19 | Below "small" threshold |
| **Early period (weeks 15-18)** | +3.30 points | Not significant (p=0.133) |
| **Late period (weeks 19-22)** | +0.36 points | Not significant (p=0.872) |

### **Concerns**

1. **Effect diminishes over time**: The improvement was larger in the first month after switching but nearly disappeared by weeks 19-22.

2. **Weekly volatility**: In 3 of 8 post-switch weeks, treatment branches actually scored lower than control branches.

3. **Small practical impact**: A 2-point improvement on a ~50-point scale represents about 4%—whether this is noticeable to patrons is unclear.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Phased rollout**: Extend the new procedure to 5-10 additional branches and monitor for 8-12 weeks before full implementation.

2. **Gather more data**: Continue tracking service scores to determine if the effect stabilizes, grows, or disappears.

3. **Investigate mechanism**: Understand *why* the procedure change affected scores (faster service? better organization? staff morale?) to optimize implementation.

4. **Set a success threshold**: Define what improvement level would justify system-wide adoption (e.g., sustained +2 points over 12 weeks).

5. **Consider costs**: Weigh any implementation costs against the modest benefit observed.
