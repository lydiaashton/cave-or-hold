## **Bottom Line**

There is **moderate evidence** that the new procedure improves service scores by approximately **2.4 points (~5%)**. Given your directional expectation that the change would help, the result is **marginally statistically significant** (one-tailed p = 0.056). We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes, as the evidence points in the expected direction and the potential benefit is operationally meaningful.

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Impact**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched (20 branches) | 49.2 | 51.2 | **+2.0** |
| Branches that did not switch (14 branches) | 49.4 | 49.0 | **-0.4** |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.4 points** |

### **Statistical Evidence**

| Test | Result | Interpretation |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Two-tailed p-value | 0.103 | Not significant at conventional 0.05 level |
| One-tailed p-value | 0.056 | Marginally significant (appropriate for directional hypothesis) |
| 90% Confidence Interval | -0.02 to +4.91 | Almost entirely positive |
| Effect size (Cohen's d) | 0.29 | Small-to-moderate practical effect |

### **Branch-Level Outcomes**
- **65% of switched branches** (13 of 20) improved their scores
- **36% of non-switched branches** (5 of 14) improved their scores
- Individual results varied: some branches gained 6–10 points, others declined

### **Validation**
- Treatment and control branches had nearly identical scores before the switch (49.2 vs. 49.4)
- This supports the validity of the comparison

---

## **Recommendations**

### **1. Proceed with System-Wide Rollout**
The evidence supports your original expectation. The effect is in the right direction, marginally significant given the directional hypothesis, and represents a meaningful ~5% improvement.

### **2. Continue Monitoring**
Track service scores for at least one full quarter post-rollout to confirm the effect persists and to identify any branches that struggle with implementation.

### **3. Support Struggling Branches**
Approximately 35% of switched branches did not improve. Investigate what distinguishes successful adopters (staff training, branch layout, management support) and provide targeted assistance.

### **4. Document Implementation Practices**
Capture best practices from high-performing branches to support consistent adoption across the cooperative.

---

## **Limitations**

- **Statistical uncertainty**: The two-tailed test does not meet conventional significance thresholds; there remains a ~10% chance the effect is zero or negative
- **Sample size**: 34 branches over 17 weeks provides limited statistical power
- **Short post-period**: Only 8 weeks of data after the switch
- **Variation across branches**: Not all branches benefited equally

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure shows a positive effect consistent with your expectations. While statistical certainty is not absolute, the weight of evidence supports proceeding with rollout. Continued monitoring will provide additional confidence and help optimize implementation.
