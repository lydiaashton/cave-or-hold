# **Board Summary: Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure had a significant positive effect on service scores**, though not in the way we might have expected. Branches that adopted the new procedure **maintained their service scores** (averaging ~51 before and after), while branches keeping the old procedure **declined by nearly 2 points** (from 49.2 to 47.4). 

The net effect is a **2.1 point advantage** for branches using the new procedure (p = 0.001). This suggests the new procedure helped branches **resist a system-wide decline** that affected control branches during weeks 15–22.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.** The evidence indicates it protects service quality during challenging periods.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **60 branches** continued with the old procedure

### What Happened

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.9 | 51.1 | **+0.2** |
| Control (old procedure) | 49.2 | 47.4 | **-1.9** |
| **Difference (DiD estimate)** | | | **+2.1** |

### Statistical Evidence

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.12 points** |
| Statistical significance | **p = 0.001** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **+0.86 to +3.38 points** |

### Important Context

Both groups showed similar week-to-week fluctuations (suggesting common external factors like seasonal variation), but **only control branches experienced an overall decline** in the post-switch period. The new procedure appears to have provided **resilience** against whatever factors drove this decline.

### Parallel Trends Check

Before the switch (weeks 6–14), the gap between treatment and control branches was stable at approximately 1.7 points. After the switch (weeks 15–22), the gap widened to approximately 3.7 points. This supports the conclusion that the new procedure caused the divergence.
