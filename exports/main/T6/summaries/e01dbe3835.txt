# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is suggestive but not conclusive.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed service score increases of approximately **2.2 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. However, with only 34 branches in the study, this result is **not statistically significant** (p = 0.12). The data are consistent with a meaningful improvement, but also consistent with no effect. **We recommend a cautious, phased rollout with continued monitoring.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total branches | 34 |
| Treatment branches (switched) | 20 |
| Control branches (no switch) | 14 |
| Observation period | Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks) |
| Total observations | 578 |

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Treatment (switched) | 46.7 | 48.5 | +1.7 |
| Control (no switch) | 50.3 | 49.8 | -0.4 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.2** |

**Statistical Summary:**
- Point estimate: **+2.2 points** on the service score index
- 95% confidence interval: **-0.6 to +4.9 points**
- Statistical significance: **p = 0.12** (not significant at conventional levels)
- The confidence interval includes zero, meaning we cannot rule out no effect

### Why the Uncertainty?
- **Small sample:** Only 34 branches limits statistical power
- **Natural variation:** Service scores fluctuate week-to-week due to many factors
- **Short observation window:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data

### What the Data Do Show
- The **point estimate is positive** (2.2 points, ~4.5% improvement)
- **No negative effect detected:** The lower bound of the confidence interval is only -0.6 points
- **Parallel trends held:** Both groups followed similar patterns before the switch, supporting the validity of the comparison
- **No harm observed:** Switched branches did not experience service score declines relative to controls

---

## **Recommendations & Next Steps**

1. **Proceed with a phased rollout** rather than immediate system-wide adoption
   - Convert 3–5 additional branches per month
   - Monitor service scores closely during conversion

2. **Establish clear success metrics** before full rollout
   - Target: sustained 2+ point improvement over 12 weeks
   - If achieved in phased rollout, proceed to full adoption

3. **Extend the evaluation period**
   - Continue collecting data for 6–12 months
   - More data will narrow the confidence interval and provide greater certainty

4. **Document implementation details**
   - Capture which specific procedural changes drove any improvements
   - Ensure consistent training across all branches

5. **Reconvene in 3 months** to review phased rollout results and decide on full adoption

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure shows **promise but not proof**. The 2.2-point improvement is meaningful if real, but the study lacks the statistical power to confirm it with confidence. A phased rollout balances the potential benefit against the uncertainty, allowing the cooperative to gather more evidence before committing all branches to the new procedure.
