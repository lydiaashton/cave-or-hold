# **REVISED MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is suggestive but not conclusive.** Branches using the new procedure showed higher service scores after the switch, but there's an important caveat: treatment branches were already improving relative to control branches *before* the procedure change. This makes it difficult to isolate the true effect of the new procedure. The estimated 2.1-point improvement may be overstated. We recommend a **cautious, monitored expansion** rather than immediate system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What the Data Shows

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| **Branches with new procedure** (425 branches) | 51.98 | 53.04 | **+1.06** |
| **Branches with old procedure** (284 branches) | 51.88 | 50.81 | **-1.07** |
| **Raw difference-in-differences** | | | **+2.13** |

### Important Caveat: Pre-Existing Trends

**The treatment and control groups were on different trajectories before the switch:**

| Week | Treatment-Control Gap |
|------|----------------------|
| Week 6 | -1.06 |
| Week 9 | +0.03 |
| Week 14 (last pre-switch week) | +0.99 |
| Week 22 (last post-switch week) | +3.02 |

The gap was already widening by **2 points before the intervention began**. This violates a key assumption of our analysis method and suggests the 2.13-point estimate likely **overstates the true effect**.

### What We Can Say With Confidence

- Service scores at switched branches **did increase** after adoption (+1.06 points)
- Service scores at non-switched branches **declined** during the same period (-1.07 points)
- The divergence accelerated after week 15, but was already underway
- **60%** of switched branches improved vs. **38%** of non-switched branches

### What We Cannot Confidently Claim

- The **exact magnitude** of the procedure's effect (could be smaller than 2.13 points)
- Whether the effect is **entirely due to the procedure** vs. other factors that differ between branch types
- Whether **all branches** would see similar improvements

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased expansion with rigorous monitoring, not immediate system-wide rollout.**

Given the promising but inconclusive evidence:

1. **Expand to 50-100 additional branches** over the next quarter
2. **Track weekly service scores** for these branches vs. a continuing control group
3. **Re-evaluate in 90 days** with cleaner before/after data for the new adopters
4. **Consider randomizing** the next phase if feasible, to eliminate selection bias
