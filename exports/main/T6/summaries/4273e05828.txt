### **Important Caveat**

The branches that switched to the new procedure were **already outperforming** control branches before the switch:

| Period | Treatment-Control Gap |

### **What This Means**

- The **full +2.0 point difference cannot be attributed to the procedure alone**
- Some portion reflects pre-existing differences between branches
- The **true causal effect is likely smaller than 2.0 points**, possibly substantially so
- However, there is **no evidence of harm**—scores did not decline relative to controls
