| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Estimated Impact** | +2.2 points (95% CI: -0.3 to +4.8) |

**1. Point estimate is positive, but uncertainty is high.**  
The best estimate suggests a 2-point improvement, but the confidence interval includes zero. We cannot confidently conclude the procedure caused any change.

**2. Effects may strengthen over time.**  
The estimated effect was smaller in weeks 15–18 (+0.7) and larger in weeks 19–22 (+3.3). This pattern is consistent with a real effect but could also reflect random variation.

**3. Analysis methodology is sound.**  
Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, validating the comparison design. Results are robust to controlling for staffing and visitor volume.

**4. Individual branch experiences varied widely.**  
Among adopting branches, score changes ranged from -4.5 to +11.6 points. This variation exceeds the estimated treatment effect, suggesting other factors dominate branch-level outcomes.
