# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

We **cannot conclude** that the new procedure improves service scores. The estimated effect is positive (+1.9 points) as expected, but the evidence is too weak to distinguish a real improvement from random variation. **Do not proceed with system-wide rollout** at this time. Continue the pilot and collect more data.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 53.0 | 57.9 | +4.9 |
| Branches with old procedure | 48.5 | 51.5 | +3.1 |
| **Estimated effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

### **What This Means**

- The **95% confidence interval** ranges from **-0.9 to +4.6 points**
- This range **includes zero** (no effect) and even small negative effects
- There is an **18% probability** of seeing this result even if the procedure has no real impact
- By conventional standards, this is **not statistically significant**

### **Why the Uncertainty?**

- Only **34 branches** total (20 switched, 14 did not)
- Only **8 weeks** of data after the switch
- Both groups improved after week 15, suggesting other factors (seasonal trends, system-wide initiatives) affected scores

---

## **Recommendation**

**Do not roll out system-wide.** Instead:

1. **Continue the pilot** with current 20 branches
2. **Extend data collection** for at least 6 more months
3. **Consider adding more branches** to the treatment group to improve statistical power
4. **Reconvene** when we have sufficient evidence to make a confident decision

Rolling out based on current evidence risks implementing a procedure that may have no real benefit—or could even be slightly harmful.

---

## **Bottom Line Restated**

The data shows a encouraging signal in the expected direction, but **we do not yet have reliable evidence** that the new procedure works. Prudent stewardship requires more data before committing system-wide resources.
