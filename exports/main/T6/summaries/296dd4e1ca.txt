# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**SUBJECT:** New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure – Impact Evaluation

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but monitor closely and be prepared to adjust.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure showed a **1.8-point improvement in service scores**. However, because branches *chose* whether to switch (rather than being randomly assigned), we cannot be certain the full effect is due to the procedure itself. The true impact is likely positive but may be smaller than observed. A **phased rollout with continued evaluation** is the prudent path forward.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

| What We Measured | Result |
|------------------|--------|
| **Observed service score improvement** | +1.8 points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | [+1.5, +2.2] points |
| **Relative gain** | 3.4% improvement |
| **Branches studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 did not) |

### Important Caveat: Selection Bias

Branches that opted into the new procedure were **already trending upward** before the switch. The gap between switching and non-switching branches widened from -1.1 to +1.8 points during the pre-switch period alone.

**What this means:** Some portion of the observed improvement likely reflects pre-existing differences (e.g., more motivated management, better resources) rather than the procedure itself. The true causal effect is **positive but uncertain in magnitude**.

### The Effect Was Consistent Post-Switch

All eight weeks after implementation showed gains:

| Week | Effect | Week | Effect |
|------|--------|------|--------|
| 15 | +1.6 pts | 19 | +1.2 pts |
| 16 | +1.0 pts | 20 | +2.0 pts |
| 17 | +1.9 pts | 21 | +2.6 pts |
| 18 | +1.9 pts | 22 | +2.3 pts |

The consistency suggests a real effect, even if the exact size is uncertain.

---

## **WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE BOARD**

| Scenario | Likely Outcome |
|----------|----------------|
| **Best case** | Full 1.8-point gain replicates system-wide |
| **Most likely** | 1.0–1.5 point gain (accounting for selection bias) |
| **Worst case** | Minimal gain if early adopters were uniquely positioned to benefit |

Even the conservative estimate represents a **meaningful improvement** at scale across 709 branches.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with rollout, but with safeguards:**

1. **Phase the rollout** over 6–8 weeks rather than all at once
2. **Track service scores weekly** during rollout to detect any branches that underperform
3. **Set a success threshold** (e.g., minimum +1.0 point average gain) and be prepared to pause if not met
4. **Document implementation conditions** at high-performing vs. low-performing branches to identify success factors
