# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. The estimated improvement is **+1.9 points (4%)**, but we cannot rule out that this result occurred by chance (p = 0.11). **18 of 20 treatment branches improved**, yet the evidence is not strong enough for high-confidence decision-making. We recommend **extending the pilot** before committing to system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Shows**

| Measure | Treatment Branches | Control Branches |
|---------|-------------------|------------------|
| Before switch | 50.8 | 49.9 |
| After switch | 53.2 | 50.5 |
| **Change** | **+2.4** | **+0.7** |

**Net effect: +1.9 points**

### **The Uncertainty**

| Statistic | Value | Interpretation |
|-----------|-------|----------------|
| P-value | 0.11 | 11% chance this result is random noise |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.4 to +4.2 | True effect could be zero or slightly negative |
| Effect needed for significance | 2.3 points | Observed effect falls short |

### **Branch-Level Results**
- **18 of 20 treatment branches** (90%) showed score improvements
- **2 branches** saw declines after switching
- Improvement ranged from -2.7 to +12.1 points across branches

### **Why the Uncertainty?**
- Only 8 weeks of post-switch data
- Service scores have natural week-to-week variation
- 34 branches provides limited statistical power for this effect size

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Primary Recommendation: Extend the Pilot**

Given the uncertainty, we advise **6 additional months of data collection** before making a rollout decision:

1. **Continue current pilot** with existing 20 treatment branches
2. **Hold control branches** on the old procedure for comparison
3. **Re-analyze in Q2 2026** with ~1,000 observations (sufficient for statistical confidence)

### **If Board Prefers to Proceed Now**

Should the Board wish to move forward despite uncertainty:

1. **Phase rollout** over 3–4 months (not all at once)
2. **Set clear success metrics** (e.g., +2 point sustained improvement)
3. **Build in review points** at 3 and 6 months with authority to pause

---

## **Risk Assessment**

| Scenario | Probability | Impact |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| True effect is positive (+2 or more) | ~60% | Vindicates decision to rollout |
| True effect is small (+0.5 to +1.5) | ~30% | Modest benefit, may not justify change costs |
| True effect is zero or negative | ~10% | Rollout provided no benefit or harmed service |

---

## **Conclusion**

The results are **encouraging but not conclusive**. The preponderance of evidence points to improvement, yet statistical rigor demands more data before claiming the procedure "works." Extending the pilot protects the Cooperative from committing resources to an unproven change while preserving the option to rollout if benefits are confirmed.
