# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with caution.** The new procedure shows a **statistically significant average improvement of 1.8 points** in service scores. However, the week-to-week effects are **highly inconsistent**—ranging from -4.1 to +6.3 points—with two weeks showing significant *declines* in service. Given that this change was expected to have no service impact, we recommend **expanded monitoring before system-wide rollout**.

---

The positive average effect is real, but **we cannot predict when branches will see benefits versus declines**. This volatility suggests either:
1. The effect interacts with other weekly factors (staffing, visits, seasonal patterns)
2. The observed effect may not be fully causal
3. More time is needed to stabilize the pattern

## **Recommendation**

**Do not proceed with immediate system-wide rollout.** Instead:

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **Continue the pilot** with current 90 branches | Gather more weeks of data to assess stability |
| **Investigate the negative weeks** (16 and 19) | Identify what drove service declines |
| **Re-evaluate in 8-12 weeks** | Determine if effects stabilize or remain volatile |
| **If effects stabilize positive**, then rollout | Reduce risk of system-wide disruption |
