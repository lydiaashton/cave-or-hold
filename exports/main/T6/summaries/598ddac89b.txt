# **Revised Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Executive Summary**

**The data does not provide clear evidence that the new procedure improves service scores.** While the estimated effect is positive (+1.7 points), the result is not statistically significant, and the confidence interval includes zero. We cannot confidently conclude the procedure works—but we also cannot rule out a meaningful benefit.

**Recommendation:** **Do not proceed with system-wide rollout yet.** Instead, extend the evaluation period to gather more evidence before making a final decision.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores?** | **We cannot tell from this data** |
| **Is the effect statistically significant?** | **No** (p = 0.13) |
| **Could the true effect be zero or negative?** | **Yes** (95% CI: -0.5 to +3.9) |
| **Is there evidence of harm?** | **No** |
| **Should we roll out system-wide?** | **Not yet—more evidence needed** |

**Key Point:** A p-value of 0.13 means there is a **13% probability** of observing this result even if the procedure has no real effect. This exceeds standard thresholds for confident decision-making.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The Numbers**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | +1.7 points | Suggests improvement |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.5 to +3.9 | **Includes zero**—effect could be null |
| P-value | 0.13 | **Not statistically significant** |
| Effect size | 3.3% of average score | Modest, even if real |

### **2. What Happened to Service Scores**

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------|
| Switched branches (20) | 50.5 | 49.9 | -0.6 |
| Non-switched branches (14) | 52.3 | 49.9 | -2.4 |

Both groups declined after week 15. Switching branches declined **less**, but we cannot be confident this difference reflects the procedure rather than random variation.

### **3. Why the Result Is Uncertain**

- **Only 8 weeks of post-switch data**—insufficient to establish a stable effect
- **34 branches total**—modest sample size for detecting small effects
- **High week-to-week variability** in service scores (standard deviation = 9.1 points)
- **External factors** likely affected all branches (both groups declined)
