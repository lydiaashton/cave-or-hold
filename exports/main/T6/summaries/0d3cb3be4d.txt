# **Library Branch Shelving Procedure: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Shelving Procedure Pilot

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is promising but not definitive.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed an average improvement of **2.1 points** (4%) in service scores, but this result is **not statistically significant** at conventional levels when properly accounting for the limited number of branches. We recommend a **cautious phased rollout** with continued monitoring rather than an immediate system-wide mandate.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Shows**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 50.8 | 53.9 | +3.2 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 52.2 | 53.2 | +1.1 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.1** |

- **Point estimate:** +2.1 points improvement
- **95% Confidence Interval:** -0.5 to +4.7 points (includes zero)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.13 (not significant at 5% level)
- **Direction of effect:** 80% of switched branches improved vs. 64% of control branches

### **Why the Uncertainty?**

- Only **34 branches** in the study limits statistical power
- The confidence interval includes both modest harm (-0.5) and meaningful benefit (+4.7)
- Week-to-week results after the switch were variable, not consistently positive

### **Reasons for Cautious Optimism**

✓ Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (validating the comparison)  
✓ Effect size is medium and would be operationally meaningful if real  
✓ No evidence of negative consequences (staffing, visits unchanged)  
✓ Majority of switched branches showed improvement

---

## **Recommendation & Next Steps**

### **Recommendation**
**Proceed with a phased rollout** rather than immediate system-wide adoption:

1. **Expand to 5–10 additional branches** over the next quarter
2. **Monitor for 8–12 weeks** to gather more data
3. **Re-evaluate** with the larger sample before full rollout

### **Rationale**
The current pilot provides **suggestive but inconclusive** evidence. A modest expansion will:
- Increase statistical power to detect the true effect
- Limit risk if the effect proves smaller than estimated
- Allow operational learning before full implementation

### **Decision Threshold**
With ~45 branches and an additional 8 weeks of data, we could detect a 2-point effect with 80% power at the 5% significance level.
