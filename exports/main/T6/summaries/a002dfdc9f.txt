**The new procedure improved service scores. We recommend system-wide rollout.**

### Why This Evidence Is Reliable

✓ **Parallel trends validated** – Treatment and control groups moved similarly before the switch (p = 0.25)

## **Important Caveats**

1. **Observational study** – Branches were not randomly assigned to switch; however, the parallel trends test suggests selection bias is minimal.
2. **Short-term data** – We have only 8 weeks of post-switch data; long-term effects remain unknown.
3. **Implementation variation** – Effects may vary depending on how faithfully each branch implements the procedure.
4. **Costs not analyzed** – This analysis measures service score impact only; implementation costs were not evaluated.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout**, with the following conditions:

1. Phase the rollout to allow for monitoring and adjustment
2. Document implementation practices from early adopters to support training
3. Track service scores for at least 8 weeks after each branch switches
4. Re-evaluate after 6 months to assess sustained impact
