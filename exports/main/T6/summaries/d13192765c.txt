## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The evidence is inconclusive.** Branches using the new procedure showed a 2.2-point improvement, but this result could plausibly be due to random variation (p=0.094). We cannot confidently say the procedure caused the improvement. However, there is **no evidence of harm**, and the data are consistent with a meaningful positive effect. **If implementation costs are low, a cautious rollout is reasonable—but this should not be presented as a proven improvement.**
