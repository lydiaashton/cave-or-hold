# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The evidence for the new procedure is **inconclusive**. While branches that switched showed a 2.6-point improvement relative to non-switching branches, this result **does not meet standard statistical thresholds** (p = 0.11). The true effect could reasonably be zero or even slightly negative. 

**Recommendation:** Do **not** proceed with full rollout yet. Instead, **extend data collection for 8-12 additional weeks** to reduce uncertainty before making a systemwide commitment.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. What We Observed**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches using **new procedure** | 46.88 | 49.41 | +2.54 |
| Branches using **old procedure** | 44.94 | 44.92 | -0.02 |
| **Estimated effect** | | | **+2.6 points** |

### **2. What We Can Conclude**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did scores go up in switching branches? | Yes, but they also fluctuated before the switch |
| Is this improvement statistically reliable? | **No** – p-value = 0.11 (above 0.05 threshold) |
| Could the true effect be zero? | **Yes** – the 95% confidence interval includes zero |
| What's the most likely scenario? | Small positive effect (~2-3 points), but we lack confidence |

### **3. Why the Uncertainty?**

- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** – too short to distinguish signal from noise
- **34 branches total** – limited statistical power
- Service scores naturally **fluctuate week-to-week** (standard deviation = 8.3 points)
- The observed effect (2.6 points) is smaller than typical weekly variation

---

## **RECOMMENDATIONS**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Continue current pilot** for 8-12 more weeks | Additional data will narrow confidence intervals and support a definitive decision |
| **2** | **Do not commit to full rollout** yet | Insufficient evidence to justify systemwide operational change |
| **3** | **Document implementation details** at each branch | Ensures we can identify what's working if effects strengthen |
| **4** | **Reconvene in Q1** with updated analysis | Make rollout decision with adequate statistical evidence |

## **WHAT WE NEED FOR A CONFIDENT DECISION**

To detect a 2.6-point effect with 95% confidence, we would need approximately:
- **16-20 weeks of post-treatment data** (we currently have 8), OR
- **50+ branches** in the study (we currently have 34)

Extending the pilot is the most practical path forward.
