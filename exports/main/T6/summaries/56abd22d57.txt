# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. The estimated improvement is 2 points (4.3%), but the evidence is not strong enough to rule out the possibility of no effect. We recommend **a cautious, monitored expansion** rather than immediate systemwide rollout, with predefined criteria for evaluating success.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** participated over 17 weeks
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** served as a comparison group
- The switch occurred in Week 15

### Results

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+2.0 points** on service score index |
| Relative improvement | **+4.3%** above baseline |
| Statistical significance | **Not conclusive** (p = 0.11 with appropriate methods) |
| 95% Confidence range | **-0.5 to +4.6 points** (includes zero) |

### What the Statistics Mean

The confidence interval tells us the true effect could reasonably be:
- A **modest improvement** (up to +4.6 points), OR
- **No meaningful change** (around zero), OR
- A **small negative effect** (down to -0.5 points)

**We cannot be confident the procedure causes improvement**, though the most likely scenario is a small positive effect.

### Why the Uncertainty?

1. **Limited independent units**: Though we have 578 observations, they come from only 34 branches. The effective sample size is 34, not 578.
2. **Short post-switch period**: Only 8 weeks of data after the change.
3. **Natural variation**: Service scores fluctuate week-to-week for many reasons.

### Encouraging Signs

- The point estimate is **positive**, not negative
- **No evidence of harm** to service quality
- Pre-switch trends were **similar between groups** (validating the comparison)
- Effect persists after controlling for staffing and visitor volume

---

## **Recommendations**

### Proposed Approach: **Staged Expansion with Clear Evaluation**

| Phase | Action | Success Criteria |
|-------|--------|------------------|
| **1** | Expand to 10–15 additional branches | Service scores improve by ≥1.5 points vs. matched controls |
| **2** | Evaluate after 12 weeks | Statistical evidence reaches p < 0.10 |
| **3** | If successful: full rollout | If not: pause and reassess |

### Monitoring Requirements

1. **Monthly service score reports** comparing new vs. continuing branches
2. **Quarterly board updates** on rollout progress
3. **Predefined stop criteria**: If scores decline or show no improvement after 6 months, halt expansion
4. **Cost tracking**: Ensure labor and operational costs remain acceptable

---

## **Conclusion**

The pilot results are **suggestive but not definitive**. The new procedure may improve service quality by a modest amount, and there is no evidence of harm. However, the statistical evidence does not meet conventional standards for confidence.

A **cautious, evidence-based expansion** allows the cooperative to capture potential benefits while protecting against investing in a procedure that may not deliver meaningful improvements. We recommend the board approve Phase 1 with the evaluation framework above.
