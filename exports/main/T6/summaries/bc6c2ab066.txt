# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence is **inconclusive**. While branches using the new procedure showed higher service scores (+1.9 points), this difference is **not statistically significant** and could be due to random variation. We cannot confidently say the new procedure improves service. We recommend **extending the pilot** to gather more data before making a rollout decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15 (treatment group)
- **14 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)

### **Results**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated treatment effect** | +1.89 service score points |
| **Percentage improvement** | 3.7% above baseline |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.107 (not significant) |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | -0.41 to +4.19 points |

### **What This Means**
- The observed improvement **could be real** (up to +4.2 points)
- The observed improvement **could be zero or slightly negative** (down to -0.4 points)
- We would need approximately **50% more data** (8-10 additional weeks) to reach a definitive conclusion
- **No evidence of harm**: The new procedure did not reduce service scores at any branch

### **Supporting Evidence**
- **Parallel pre-trends confirmed**: Treatment and control branches had similar trends before the switch, validating our comparison
- **Consistent direction**: 15 of 20 treatment branches showed score improvements after switching
