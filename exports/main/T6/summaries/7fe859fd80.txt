## **Recommendations**

### Revised Approach: Cautious Expansion

1. **Expand the pilot** to 5–10 additional branches rather than full rollout
   - This generates more data before system-wide commitment
   - Reduces risk if the effect proves smaller than expected

2. **Set clear success criteria** for full rollout
   - Example: Confirm positive effect with p<0.05 after 12 more weeks
   - Or: Achieve minimum 1.0 point improvement with 95% confidence

3. **Continue monitoring all branches**
   - Track whether early adopters sustain their gains
   - Watch for any negative effects in expanding pilot

4. **Document implementation details**
   - Identify which branches implement the procedure most faithfully
   - Correlate fidelity with outcomes to refine the procedure

### If Board Prefers Full Rollout

Given the ~95% probability of a positive effect and low apparent risk, a full rollout is **defensible** if:
- Implementation costs are modest
- The board is comfortable with moderate (not definitive) evidence
- Ongoing monitoring is committed

## **Caveats**

1. **Not statistically definitive**: P-value of 0.10 means 1 in 10 chance of seeing this result even if there's no true effect

2. **Short observation window**: Only 8 weeks of post-implementation data

3. **Confidence interval includes zero**: True effect could be negligible

4. **External validity**: Results from 20 branches may not generalize to all 34

---

## **Conclusion**

The evidence suggests the new procedure improves service scores, but statistical certainty is moderate rather than high. A **phased expansion with clear success criteria** balances the likely benefits against the uncertainty. This approach allows the cooperative to capture potential gains while maintaining the option to adjust or halt if further data proves less favorable.
