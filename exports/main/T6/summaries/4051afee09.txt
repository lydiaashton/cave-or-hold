# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**
## **Revised Assessment**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests a modest improvement, but with important caveats.** The new procedure likely improved service scores by **1.0–1.5 points**, not the 2.2 points initially estimated. We recommend a **phased rollout with careful monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide adoption.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Found**

| | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branches with old procedure | 49.9 | 49.6 | −0.2 |
| Branches with new procedure | 50.6 | 52.6 | +2.0 |
| **Raw difference-in-differences** | | | **+2.2** |
| **Adjusted estimate** | | | **+1.0 to +1.5** |

### **Critical Limitation**

**The treatment branches were already improving before the switch.** The gap between treatment and control branches widened by **1.8 points** in the weeks *before* the new procedure was implemented. This means:

- The +2.2 point estimate likely **overstates** the true effect
- Branches selected for the switch may have been on an upward trajectory already
- The more reliable estimate (using only recent pre-switch weeks) is **+1.35 points** (95% CI: 0.64 to 2.06)

### **What We Can Say with Confidence**

✓ The effect is **still positive** even after adjustment  
✓ The effect is **still statistically significant** (p < 0.01)  
✓ Post-switch gaps (2.0–4.0 points) exceed the pre-switch gap (1.8 points)  
✓ The benefit appears **real but modest**—roughly 2–3% improvement in service scores

### **What Remains Uncertain**

⚠️ The **exact magnitude** of the improvement (1.0 vs. 1.5 vs. 2.0 points)  
⚠️ Whether **all branches** will see similar benefits  
⚠️ Whether the effect is **sustained** beyond the 8-week observation period  
⚠️ Why some weeks show stronger effects than others (gap ranges from 2.0 to 4.0 points post-switch)

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased rollout, not immediate system-wide adoption.**

1. **Expand to 50–100 additional branches** over the next 4–6 weeks
2. **Monitor weekly** to confirm the effect replicates in new branches
3. **If results hold**, complete system-wide rollout in the following quarter
4. **If results are weaker**, pause and investigate before proceeding

This approach balances the **likely benefit** against the **uncertainty** in our estimates.
