## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Show**

| Metric | Value | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.3 points** | ~4-5% improvement on average |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.7 to +5.2** | True effect likely in this range |
| Probability effect is positive | **93%** | Strong evidence of benefit |
| Probability effect > 1 point | **80%** | Moderate evidence of meaningful gain |
| Statistical significance (p-value) | **0.14** | Below conventional 0.05 threshold |

### **Why "Not Significant" Is Misleading**

The p-value of 0.14 does **not** mean "no effect." It means we lack the precision to rule out zero with 95% confidence. The data actually favor a positive effect:

- **93% chance** the procedure helps
- **Only 7% chance** it has no effect or harms service
- The study was designed to detect large effects; smaller benefits are harder to confirm

### **Observed Outcomes**

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched branches (20) | 50.4 | 52.8 | +2.4 |
| Non-switched branches (14) | 46.5 | 46.6 | +0.1 |

## **Recommendations**

### **Option A: Proceed with Rollout** ✓ *If costs are modest*
- **Rationale:** 93% probability of positive impact; expected benefit is meaningful
- **Risk:** If true effect is near zero, resources spent on rollout yield little return
- **Mitigation:** Monitor scores post-rollout; be prepared to adjust

### **Option B: Continue Pilot, Collect More Data** ✓ *If costs are substantial*
- **Rationale:** Additional weeks of data would narrow the confidence interval
- **Timeline:** 8-12 more weeks would substantially improve precision
- **Risk:** Delaying benefits if the procedure does work

### **Our Neutral Recommendation**
Given we don't know implementation costs, we present both options. **If the board's original expectation was correct (that this would improve service), the data are consistent with that belief.** The uncertainty is about *how much* improvement, not *whether* there is improvement.
