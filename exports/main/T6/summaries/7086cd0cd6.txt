# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure is associated with a small but statistically significant improvement in service scores (+1.7 points, or 3.5% above average). However, the effect is modest in practical terms. The board should weigh this benefit against implementation costs before deciding on system-wide rollout.**
