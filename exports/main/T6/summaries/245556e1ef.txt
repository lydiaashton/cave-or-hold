Treatment branches were already on a slightly better trajectory before the switch. Accounting for this pre-existing trend, the **conservative estimate is +1.4 to +1.6 points** rather than the headline +1.95 points.

### Why We Still Recommend Rollout

1. **Effect is robust:** Even conservative estimates show a positive, meaningful improvement
2. **Sustained over time:** The gap persisted across all 8 post-switch weeks
3. **No downside observed:** Switched branches did not experience any negative effects
4. **Low implementation cost:** The procedure change requires training but no additional resources
