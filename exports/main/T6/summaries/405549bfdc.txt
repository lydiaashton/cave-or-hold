# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. The best estimate is a **+2 point improvement**, but the data cannot rule out anything from **no effect to a +4 point gain**. There is approximately a **95% probability the true effect is positive**, and minimal risk of harm. We recommend **proceeding with rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. What We Know About the Effect

| Statistic | Value | Interpretation |
|-----------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | **+1.94 points** | Best single estimate of the effect |
| 95% confidence interval | **-0.4 to +4.3 points** | Range of plausible true effects |
| P-value | **0.11** | Not significant at 5% level |
| Probability effect is positive | **~95%** | Bayesian interpretation |

**What this means:** The evidence does not meet the conventional threshold for statistical significance (p < 0.05). However, the confidence interval suggests the true effect is very unlikely to be meaningfully negative, and is likely modestly positive.

### 2. Before-and-After Comparison

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| **Treatment (New Procedure)** | 47.5 | 48.8 | **+1.3** |
| **Control (Old Procedure)** | 51.2 | 50.6 | **-0.7** |

### 3. Branch-Level Variation

- **15 of 20 treatment branches** (75%) improved after switching
- **5 treatment branches** saw declines, ranging from -1.3 to -5.7 points
- This variation suggests **implementation quality matters**

### 4. Analysis Validity

✓ **Parallel trends confirmed:** No evidence treatment and control branches were diverging before the switch  
✓ **No confounding:** Staffing and visit patterns remained comparable between groups  
✓ **Appropriate methods:** Used panel regression with cluster-robust standard errors

---

## **Risk Assessment**

| Scenario | Likelihood | Consequence |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| True effect is positive (+1 to +4 points) | **High** | Service improves across all branches |
| True effect is near zero (0 to +1 points) | **Moderate** | Minimal benefit, but no harm |
| True effect is negative (-0.4 to 0 points) | **Low** | Negligible negative impact |

The **lower bound of the confidence interval (-0.4 points)** represents the worst plausible outcome—too small to be practically meaningful.

---

## **Recommendations**

### 1. **Proceed with Rollout**
Given the high probability of a positive effect and minimal downside risk, expanding the new procedure is justified.

### 2. **Support Implementation Quality**
The variation in branch outcomes suggests training and support matter. Document and share practices from high-performing branches.

### 3. **Continue Monitoring**
Extend data collection for 6+ more months to:
- Reduce uncertainty around the effect estimate
- Confirm effects persist over time
- Identify and assist struggling branches

### 4. **Set Realistic Expectations**
Communicate that the expected improvement is modest (~2 points, or ~4% of average score), not transformative.

---

## **Methodology Note**

Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects and cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). Data: 34 branches, 17 weeks (weeks 6-22), switch at week 15.
