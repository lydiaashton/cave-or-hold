**What we don't know:**
- Whether the observed improvement reflects the procedure or random variation

## **Why the Uncertainty?**

1. **Limited post-implementation data** – Only 8 weeks after the switch
2. **Modest sample size** – 34 branches provides reasonable but not definitive power
3. **Natural variability** – Service scores fluctuate week-to-week for many reasons
4. **Control group decline** – Non-switched branches got worse, which may reflect external factors

## **Bottom Line for Decision-Makers**
