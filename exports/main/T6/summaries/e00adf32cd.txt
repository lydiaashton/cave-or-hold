**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure had a modest protective effect, but did not improve service scores in absolute terms.** Both groups experienced declines after the switch date, but branches using the new procedure declined less (-0.70 points) than those using the old procedure (-2.57 points). The net relative benefit is **+1.87 points** (p = 0.001).

**Recommendation: The evidence does not strongly support a system-wide rollout.** The effect is statistically significant but modest (0.19 standard deviations), and the procedure did not produce an absolute improvement in service quality. The board may wish to:
- Investigate what caused the overall decline after week 15 (affecting all branches)
- Consider whether the modest protective benefit justifies the cost of system-wide implementation
- Pilot the procedure in additional branches to gather more evidence

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches**: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 kept the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), with the switch at week 15
- Analysis compares changes between groups using difference-in-differences

### Results
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 50.56 | 47.98 | **-2.57** |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 49.63 | 48.93 | **-0.70** |

**Critical observation:** Service scores declined for ALL branches after week 15. The new procedure mitigated this decline but did not reverse it.

### Statistical Assessment
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Relative effect (DiD) | +1.87 points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | [0.73, 3.01] |
| p-value | 0.0013 |
| Effect size | 0.19 SD (small) |
| Pre-trend balance | Confirmed (p = 0.81) |

### Practical Significance
