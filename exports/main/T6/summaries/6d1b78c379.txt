# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**We cannot yet determine whether the new procedure improves service scores.** The best estimate suggests a 2.4-point improvement, but the statistical uncertainty is too large to draw a firm conclusion. The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.5 to +5.3 points, meaning the true effect could be slightly harmful, neutral, or meaningfully beneficial. We recommend **continuing to collect data before making a system-wide decision.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **What the Data Shows**

| Metric | Old Procedure Branches | New Procedure Branches |
|--------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Average score before switch (Weeks 6-14) | 48.3 | 47.8 |
| Average score after switch (Weeks 15-22) | 47.6 | 49.5 |
| **Change** | **-0.7** | **+1.7** |

**Estimated effect of new procedure: +2.4 points**

### **What the Statistics Tell Us**

| Measure | Value | Interpretation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +2.4 points | Best guess of the effect |
| p-value | 0.10 | Not statistically significant |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.5 to +5.3 points | Plausible range of true effects |
| Probability effect ≤ 0 | ~5% | Small but non-negligible risk of no benefit |

**Key takeaway:** The confidence interval includes zero and slightly negative values. We cannot rule out that the new procedure has no effect or even a small negative effect.

### **Context**

- Service scores average ~50 points; a 2.4-point gain would represent ~5% improvement
- 34 branches participated (20 switched, 14 did not)
- Only 8 weeks of post-switch data per branch limits statistical power
- Approximately 1.4× more data would be needed to achieve statistical significance if the true effect is 2.4 points

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Do not proceed with system-wide rollout at this time.** Instead:

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **Continue the pilot** through end of 2025 | More data will narrow the confidence interval |
| **Expand to 5-10 additional branches** (optional) | Increases sample size without full commitment |
| **Re-convene the board in Q2 2026** | Decide based on stronger evidence |

### **Why This Approach?**

1. **The evidence is inconclusive** — We genuinely do not know if the procedure works
2. **The risk of harm appears low** — Even the lower bound (-0.5 points) is minimal
3. **The potential benefit is meaningful** — If the +2.4 estimate is accurate, system-wide adoption would improve service noticeably
4. **More data is the solution** — This is a power problem, not a fundamental flaw in the procedure
