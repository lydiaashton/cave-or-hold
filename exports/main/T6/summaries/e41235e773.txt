# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests the new procedure improved service scores, but the finding is not definitive.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed an average improvement of approximately **2.3 points (4.5%)**, and this result is statistically significant at the conventional 5% level when properly accounting for the data structure. However, the evidence has important limitations, and the board should weigh the modest, uncertain benefit against implementation costs.

**Recommendation: Consider a phased rollout with continued monitoring rather than immediate system-wide adoption.**

---

### **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Point Estimate** | +2.3 service score points |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.048 (marginally significant with proper clustering) |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | +0.1 to +4.4 points (wide range) |
| **Alternative Model** | p = 0.14 without branch fixed effects (not significant) |
| **Study Scope** | 34 branches, 17 weeks (20 treatment, 14 control) |

**1. Positive but Modest Effect**
- Treatment branches improved from 50.0 to 52.3; control branches remained stable at 49.1
- The 2.3-point improvement represents about 4.5% on the service score scale
- However, the confidence interval includes effects as small as 0.1 points

**2. Statistical Evidence Is Mixed**
- The result is only significant when controlling for branch-specific characteristics
- Without these controls, the effect is not statistically distinguishable from zero (p=0.14)
- With proper clustering of standard errors (required for panel data), p=0.048—barely crossing the 5% threshold

**3. Important Caveats**
- Treatment branches already scored 0.9 points higher than control branches *before* the switch
- Week-to-week results vary considerably (Week 19: +0.5; Weeks 21-22: +5.0)
- Only 8 weeks of post-implementation data limits confidence in sustained effects

---

### **Recommendation**

**Approve a phased rollout with rigorous monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation:

1. **Expand to 5-7 additional branches** in the next quarter
2. **Collect 12+ weeks of data** from new adopters before full rollout decision
3. **Set a clear success threshold** (e.g., maintain 95% CI entirely above zero)
4. **Document implementation practices** from high-performing early adopters

### **Methodology Note**

Analysis uses difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects and cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). This approach accounts for time-invariant branch characteristics and the panel structure of the data. The marginal statistical significance (p=0.048) and wide confidence interval indicate the evidence is suggestive but not conclusive.
