# **Analysis of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure: Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new procedure showed **modest but statistically significant improvements** in service scores (+1.8 points, p=0.001). However, because branches self-selected into the new procedure, we cannot rule out that motivated or better-resourced branches both chose to switch AND would have improved anyway.

**Recommendation:** The evidence is **suggestive but not definitive**. Consider a phased rollout with continued monitoring, or a randomized trial for stronger evidence.

---

### 1. Observed Improvement
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +1.8 points |
| 95% Confidence interval | 0.8 to 2.9 points |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.001 |
| Relative improvement | 3.6% above average |

### 2. Important Limitation
Branches that switched to the new procedure had **higher service scores even before the switch** (average 3.5 points higher). While our analysis controls for this baseline difference, unobserved factors (e.g., management quality, staff motivation) could explain both the decision to switch AND the improvement.

### 3. Parallel Trends Supported
Pre-switch trends were similar between groups (p=0.358), which supports the validity of the comparison.

### 4. Effect Appears Stable
Improvements persisted across all 8 weeks post-implementation without fading.
