## **Key Findings**

### **What We Analyzed**
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** kept the old procedure
- Service scores average ~50 points (higher is better)

### **Results**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 52.05 | 50.90 | **-1.15** |
| Branches with new procedure | 51.78 | 52.80 | **+1.02** |

**Estimated Effect: +2.22 points** (95% CI: -0.4 to +4.9; p=0.10)

### **What This Means**
1. **Statistically inconclusive**: The confidence interval includes zero, so we cannot rule out no effect
2. **No evidence of harm**: The worst plausible effect is -0.4 points (negligible)
3. **Consistent positive pattern**: Switched branches outperformed non-switched branches in all 8 weeks after the change
4. **Practically modest**: If real, the effect represents ~4% of the average service score

The analysis uses a difference-in-differences approach, comparing changes in treatment branches to changes in control branches. This method controls for pre-existing differences between branches and system-wide trends.
