# **REVISED MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Subject: Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure shows a positive effect, but proceed with caution.** 

The data shows a **statistically significant improvement of ~2.1 points** in service scores. However, branches that adopted the new procedure were already outperforming control branches by ~2 points *before* the switch, suggesting they may be systematically different (better resourced, more motivated, etc.). This raises questions about whether the same gains would generalize to all branches.

**Recommendation: Approve a phased rollout with careful monitoring, rather than immediate system-wide implementation.**

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| Metric | Control Branches | Treatment Branches |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Before switch** | 48.3 | 50.3 |
| **After switch** | 48.4 | 52.4 |
| **Change** | +0.1 | +2.1 |

**Net Effect: +2.1 points** (95% CI: 1.2 to 3.0; p < 0.001)

**Critical Context:**
- Treatment branches scored **~2 points higher than control branches even BEFORE switching**
- This suggests branches were not randomly assigned to adopt the procedure
- The improvement is **statistically real** but **modest in magnitude** (~4% of average score)
- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** limits our ability to assess durability

---

### **KEY UNCERTAINTIES**

| Concern | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| **Selection bias** | Branches that chose to switch may be better managed; effects may not generalize |
| **Effect size** | 2.1 points is meaningful but modest; weigh against implementation costs |
| **Time horizon** | 8 weeks post-switch is short; effects could fade or grow |
| **Implementation costs** | Not captured in this analysis (staff time, training, disruption) |

---

### **RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| 1 | **Pilot expansion**: Roll out to 20-30 additional branches (preferably lower-performing ones) to test generalizability |
| 2 | **Track implementation costs**: Document staff time, training needs, and any operational disruption |
| 3 | **Extend monitoring**: Collect at least 6 more months of data to assess durability |
| 4 | **Decision point**: Re-evaluate full rollout after pilot results and cost analysis |

---

### **ANALYTICAL NOTES**

This evaluation used a **difference-in-differences** design. While the parallel pre-trends assumption appears satisfied (p = 0.21), the pre-existing performance gap between groups suggests non-random selection into the treatment. The estimated effect is internally valid for branches similar to those that adopted the procedure, but external validity to all branches is less certain.

**Data:** 2,550 weekly observations from 150 branches (weeks 6–22)  
**Switch date:** Week 15
