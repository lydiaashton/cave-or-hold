# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 2025
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We cannot confirm that the new procedure improves service scores.** The data shows a positive trend (+2.4 points, ~5% improvement), but this result is **not statistically significant** at the conventional confidence level. The true effect could range from essentially zero to a meaningful 5-point gain. 

Given the original expectation of improvement and the absence of any negative signal, **we recommend a cautious phased rollout** while collecting additional data to reach a more definitive conclusion.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Numbers

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated Effect | +2.4 points | Suggests improvement |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.4 to +5.2 points | **Includes zero** |
| Statistical Significance | p = 0.09 | **Not significant at 0.05 level** |
| Probability Effect > 0 | ~95% (one-sided) | Suggestive but not conclusive |
| Branches Studied | 34 total (20 switched, 14 did not) | |
| Study Period | 17 weeks (weeks 6-22) | 8 weeks pre, 8 weeks post |

### What Happened

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 45.2 | 49.4 | +4.2 |
| Control Branches | 46.6 | 48.5 | +2.0 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.4** |

Both groups improved after week 15. Switched branches improved more, but we cannot confidently attribute this difference to the new procedure rather than random variation.

### Why the Uncertainty?

- **Sample size:** 34 branches over 17 weeks provides moderate statistical power
- **Natural variation:** Service scores fluctuate week-to-week (standard deviation ~9 points)
- **More data needed:** Approximately 16% more precision would be needed to confirm this effect at conventional significance levels

### Validity Checks

✓ **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories (p=0.74)
✓ **No negative signal:** Even the lower bound of the confidence interval suggests minimal harm
✓ **Results robust:** Effect persists after controlling for staffing and visitor volume

## **Questions the Board May Have**

**Q: You originally expected improvement. Did the procedure fail?**
A: Not necessarily. The effect points in the expected direction. We simply lack sufficient evidence to confirm it isn't due to chance.

**Q: Why not wait for conclusive evidence before any rollout?**
A: The data shows no harm. A phased rollout allows branches to benefit if the effect is real while we continue gathering evidence.

**Q: What if we roll out and the effect is zero?**
A: The cooperative would have invested in a neutral change. This should be weighed against operational considerations not captured in the service score.

**Q: Should we have designed a better study?**
A: The natural experiment (some branches switched, some didn't) is a solid design. More branches or a longer study period would have increased statistical power.
