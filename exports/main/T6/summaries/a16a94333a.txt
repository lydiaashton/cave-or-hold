## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measured Impact

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated treatment effect** | **+1.81 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | [1.27, 2.36] |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Effect size (Cohen's d) | 0.17 (small-to-moderate) |

### 2. Important Context

**The treatment and control groups were not perfectly comparable before the switch:**
- Early in the study (weeks 6-10), treatment branches had lower scores than control branches
- By weeks 12-14 (just before the switch), the groups had converged
- This convergence before the intervention is **encouraging** and supports the validity of our estimates

**The effect appears to be growing:**
| Period | Treatment-Control Gap |
|--------|----------------------|
| Weeks 15-16 | ~2.0 points |
| Weeks 17-18 | ~1.9 points |
| Weeks 19-20 | ~2.5 points |
| Weeks 21-22 | ~3.0 points |

This pattern suggests branches may continue to benefit as staff gain experience with the new procedure.

### 3. What 1.8 Points Means

- A branch averaging 50 points would improve to approximately **52 points**
- This represents about **3.6% improvement** above baseline
- In practical terms: noticeable but not transformative

## **Limitations & Uncertainties**

| Issue | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| **Non-random assignment** | Branches self-selected (or were selected) for the new procedure; unobserved differences may exist |
| **Pre-trend differences** | Groups differed in early weeks, though they converged before the switch |
| **Short post-period** | Only 8 weeks of data after the switch; long-term effects unknown |
| **No cost data** | Cannot assess whether benefits outweigh implementation costs |
| **Modest effect size** | 1.8 points is meaningful but not large |

---

## **Recommendations**

### Primary Recommendation: **Phased Rollout with Evaluation**

1. **Expand to additional branches** in phases (not all at once)

2. **Track implementation costs** (staff time, training, materials)

3. **Continue monitoring service scores** to confirm effects persist

4. **Set a decision checkpoint** after 3-6 months to evaluate:
   - Are effects consistent with our estimates?
   - Do benefits outweigh costs?
   - Are there branches struggling with implementation?

### Alternative: **Targeted Rollout**

If resources are limited, consider prioritizing:
- Branches with below-average service scores (most to gain)
- Branches with adequate staffing (best positioned to implement)

### If Costs Are High: **Reconsider**

If the new procedure requires substantial additional resources, the board may wish to:
- Limit adoption to willing branches
- Modify the procedure to reduce costs
- Invest in other service improvements with higher returns
