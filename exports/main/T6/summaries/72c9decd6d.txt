# **REVISED MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We do not have statistically reliable evidence that the new procedure improves service scores.** The estimated effect is positive (+2 points) but small and imprecisely measured. The data are consistent with three possibilities:

1. The procedure has **no meaningful effect** (true effect ≈ 0)
2. The procedure provides a **modest benefit** (true effect up to +4.6 points)
3. The procedure has a **slight negative effect** (true effect as low as -0.5 points)

**Recommendation:** **Do not proceed with full rollout yet.** Either (a) collect 8–12 more weeks of data to reduce uncertainty, or (b) if operational factors favor the new procedure for other reasons, proceed with careful monitoring—but not based on service score evidence alone.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Finding | What It Means |
|---------|---------------|
| **+2.1 point estimated effect** | Suggests improvement, but... |
| **95% CI: -0.5 to +4.6 points** | ...includes zero and small negative values |
| **p-value = 0.125** | Not statistically significant; could easily occur by chance |
| **Effect size (Cohen's d = 0.23)** | Small effect—even if real, impact is modest |
| **Pre-trends balanced (p=0.999)** | Comparison between groups is valid |

### **Study Limitations**

- **Underpowered**: With 34 branches, we can only reliably detect effects larger than ~3.7 points
- **Our estimate (2.1 points) falls below this threshold**, explaining the non-significant result
- **Short observation window**: Only 8 weeks of post-switch data

---

## **What Should the Board Decide?**

### **If the only consideration is service score evidence:**
→ **Wait for more data.** The current evidence is insufficient to justify system-wide change.

### **If there are other reasons to prefer the new procedure** (e.g., staff efficiency, cost savings, easier implementation):
→ **Proceed with phased rollout**, but recognize that service score benefits are unproven. Continue monitoring.

### **If there are costs or risks to the new procedure:**
→ **Do not roll out.** The evidence does not demonstrate benefit sufficient to outweigh potential downsides.

---
