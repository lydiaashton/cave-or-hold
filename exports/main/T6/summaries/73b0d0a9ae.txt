## Bottom Line

**The evidence does not demonstrate that the new procedure improves service scores.** The estimated +1.6 point effect is not statistically significant and is likely biased upward because branches that switched were already declining relative to others before the change. **We recommend against system-wide rollout based on current evidence.** If the cooperative wishes to pursue this procedure, a more rigorous evaluation with better research design is needed.

### 1. Main Result

| P-value | 0.11 | Cannot reject "no effect" |

### 2. Critical Problem: Pre-Existing Trends

The parallel trends assumption required for valid causal inference **does not hold**:

| Period | Treatment - Control Gap |
|--------|------------------------|
| Weeks 6-10 (early) | -0.98 points |
| Weeks 11-14 (late) | -3.08 points |

The treatment group was already falling behind by an additional **2.1 points** before the switch occurred. This means the +1.61 "effect" likely captures **regression to the mean**, not the procedure's impact.

### 3. Post-Switch Pattern Is Inconsistent

| Weeks | Pattern |
|-------|---------|
| 15, 21, 22 | Treatment ahead (3 weeks) |

No clear, sustained improvement is visible.

## Next Steps

### Recommended Actions

1. **Do not proceed with system-wide rollout** based on current evidence.

2. **If continuing the pilot**, consider:
   - Extending the observation period (16+ weeks post-switch)
   - Randomizing future switches to ensure comparable groups
   - Collecting qualitative feedback from staff about implementation challenges

3. **Alternative evaluation approaches**:
   - Staggered rollout with random assignment
   - Longer pre-period to establish stable baselines
   - Additional outcome measures beyond service scores

4. **Revisit decision** after collecting more rigorous evidence.

## Conclusion

The current analysis cannot distinguish the procedure's effect from pre-existing trends and natural variation. Rolling out system-wide would be premature. The cooperative should either abandon the procedure or invest in a more rigorous evaluation before expanding.
