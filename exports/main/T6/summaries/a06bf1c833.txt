## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, with monitoring.** The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores (+2.0 points, ~4%). The effect is real but modest, and not all branches benefited equally. A phased rollout with support for struggling branches is advisable.

## **Key Findings**

### What We Tested
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Used difference-in-differences analysis to isolate the procedure's effect from other factors

### What We Found

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 49.7 | 50.9 | +1.2 |
| Branches with new procedure | 53.6 | 56.9 | +3.3 |
| **Attributable to new procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

- **Estimated effect:** +2.03 points (95% CI: +0.5 to +3.6)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.011
- **Effect size:** ~4% improvement over baseline

### Branch-Level Variation
- **17 of 20 treated branches (85%)** improved after switching
- **3 of 20 treated branches (15%)** saw scores decline
- This variation suggests some branches may need additional support during transition

### Why We Trust This Result
- Parallel pre-trends between groups (no significant difference before week 15)
- Effect persists after controlling for branch characteristics, week effects, staffing, and visits
- Direction matches our expectation

---

## **Recommendation and Next Steps**

### 1. **Approve phased rollout**
The evidence supports system-wide adoption, but a phased approach allows for learning and adjustment.

### 2. **Provide implementation support**
- Develop training materials from the 20 pilot branches
- Identify branches at risk of struggling (based on characteristics of the 3 that declined)
- Offer hands-on support during transition

### 3. **Monitor outcomes closely**
- Track service scores for 12 weeks post-rollout per branch
- Set a threshold (e.g., no improvement after 6 weeks) to trigger additional support
- Be prepared to provide extra assistance to branches that don't show expected gains

### 4. **Evaluate long-term sustainability**
- Reassess after 6 months to confirm effects persist
- Document any operational challenges or cost implications not captured in this analysis
