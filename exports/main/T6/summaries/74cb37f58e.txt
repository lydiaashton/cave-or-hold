# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure Evaluation**

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests the new procedure improved service scores, but with important caveats.** Our best estimate is a **1.5–2.0 point improvement** (approximately 3–4% of average scores). However, pre-existing differences between branches that switched and those that didn't mean we cannot rule out some bias in this estimate. We recommend proceeding with rollout **with careful monitoring** to confirm the effect materializes across all branches.

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Positive Treatment Effect Detected
- **Estimated effect: +1.96 points** (95% CI: 1.42 to 2.50)
- Statistically significant (p < 0.001)
- Represents approximately **4% improvement** over average scores

### 2. Important Limitation: Pre-Trend Concerns
The treatment and control groups were **not on parallel trends before the switch**:
- The gap between groups widened from ~0.5 points (weeks 6-7) to ~2.7 points (week 14)
- This suggests branches that chose to switch may have been on an improving trajectory regardless of the procedure change
- **The true effect is likely smaller than 1.96 points**—possibly in the 1.0–1.5 point range

### 3. Supporting Evidence for a Real Effect
Despite the pre-trend concern, several factors suggest a genuine treatment effect:
- Post-switch effects (weeks 18-22) reached 2–3 points, exceeding the pre-trend divergence
- All 8 post-switch weeks showed positive effects; none showed negative effects
- The control group declined while the treatment group improved, suggesting the new procedure protects against downward pressures

### 4. Effect Size Context
- Service score standard deviation: 10.0 points
- Treatment effect: ~0.2 standard deviations (small-to-moderate)
- Not transformative, but meaningful for a operational procedure change

---

## **Recommendations**

### Proceed with Rollout, But Cautiously

1. **Approve phased rollout** rather than system-wide immediate implementation
   - Start with 50–100 branches per month
   - Allow time to verify effects before scaling

2. **Establish clear success metrics** before each phase:
   - Target: Minimum 1.0 point service score improvement within 4 weeks of switch
   - If effects fall below 0.5 points, pause and investigate

3. **Monitor weekly during rollout**:
   - Track service scores for each branch for 8 weeks post-switch
   - Compare to similar branches that haven't switched yet

4. **Investigate heterogeneity**:
   - Do certain branch types (size, location, staffing) see larger or smaller effects?
   - Use findings to prioritize rollout order

5. **Document implementation quality**:
   - Track training completion, staff feedback, and any operational issues
   - Poor implementation could mask real benefits

---

## **What Would Strengthen Future Analysis**

- Extend observation period to 6–12 months to assess long-term effects
- Consider random assignment of procedure type for future pilot programs
- Collect qualitative data on staff and patron experiences with the new procedure
