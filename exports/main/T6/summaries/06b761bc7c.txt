# **Board Summary: New Branch Procedure Impact Assessment**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 2026
**Subject:** Findings on New Shelving & Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Branches using the new procedure maintained their service scores while branches using the old procedure declined.** The new procedure is associated with a **2-point advantage**, but we cannot definitively prove the procedure caused this difference. Branches that adopted the new procedure were already trending upward before the switch. **We recommend proceeding with caution—monitor closely during rollout rather than assuming guaranteed improvement.**

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Switched branches** | 52.4 → 52.6 (+0.1) |
| **Non-switched branches** | 51.0 → 49.1 (-1.9) |
| **Statistical association** | +2.0 points (p < 0.001) |

### The Critical Caveat

**The switched branches were already outperforming before the procedure change:**

| Week | Switched | Non-Switched | Gap |
|------|----------|--------------|-----|
| Week 6 | 49.9 | 50.1 | -0.2 |
| Week 14 | 53.9 | 51.5 | +2.3 |
| Week 22 | 53.1 | 48.7 | +4.5 |

The gap was **widening before the switch ever happened**. This means we cannot confidently attribute the post-switch difference to the procedure itself.

---

## **Two Possible Interpretations**

| Scenario | What It Means |
|----------|---------------|
| **Optimistic** | The new procedure protected branches from the decline that hit the old-procedure branches |
| **Concerning** | Branches that chose to switch were already on an upward trajectory; the procedure may have had little effect |

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with rollout, but with realistic expectations and close monitoring:**

1. **Do roll out** to remaining branches—the procedure shows no harm and may provide benefit
2. **Do NOT expect** a guaranteed 2-point improvement at every branch
3. **DO track** service scores carefully during implementation
4. **Consider a more rigorous evaluation** (e.g., randomized rollout) for future changes
