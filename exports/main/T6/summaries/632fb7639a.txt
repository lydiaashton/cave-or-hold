## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is suggestive but not definitive.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed modest service score improvements, but pre-existing differences between branches make it difficult to isolate the true effect. The estimated benefit is **1.5 to 2 points** (about **3% improvement**), but this could be partially attributable to trends that existed before the switch.

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Observed Effect (With Caveats)
- **Switched branches**: +1.07 points (51.70 → 52.77)
- **Control branches**: -0.79 points (51.22 → 50.43)
- **Raw difference-in-differences**: +1.86 points
- **Adjusted for pre-trends**: Effect may be **smaller than 1.86 points**

### 2. Concerning Pre-Trends
Before the procedure change, switched branches were already gaining ground on control branches:

| Week | Switched − Control |
|------|-------------------|
| 6    | -0.63             |
| 10   | +0.60             |
| 14   | +0.83             |

This **upward pre-trend** suggests switched branches may have been improving for other reasons (better management, more resources, etc.), not just because of the new procedure.

### 3. Effect Size Context
- The effect equals **0.19 standard deviations**—a small-to-moderate impact
- Typical week-to-week variation (3.5 points) exceeds the treatment effect
- **61% of switched branches improved** vs. 39% of controls—this is more compelling evidence

### 4. What We Can Say With Confidence
- The new procedure **did not harm** service scores
- Switched branches **absolutely improved** (+1.07 points), regardless of what controls did
- Benefits appear to **grow over time**, suggesting learning effects

## **Next Steps**

1. **Continue the pilot** with more branches before full rollout

2. **Investigate pre-trend causes**—were switched branches systematically different (size, location, management)?

3. **Extend the evaluation period**—collect 8–12 more weeks of data to see if effects persist and pre-trends stabilize

4. **Consider random assignment** for the next phase to eliminate selection bias

5. **If proceeding with rollout**, implement in phases with clear success metrics and exit criteria
