## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure is associated with improved service scores, and we recommend rollout—with monitoring.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed service score gains of approximately **1.9 points** (~4%) relative to branches that kept the old procedure. However, some of this difference may reflect pre-existing trends. The weight of evidence suggests a real positive effect, but we recommend phased implementation with careful tracking.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Service Scores Improved After the Switch

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.92 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | 1.38 to 2.47 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Effect size | ~4% of average score |

### 2. Important Caveat: Pre-Existing Trends

Treatment branches showed slightly faster improvement *before* the switch (p=0.004). This suggests:
- The true effect may be somewhat smaller than 1.9 points
- Branches selected for early adoption may have been on an upward trajectory already
- **However**, 86% of switched branches improved vs. 71% of non-switched branches, and post-switch gains exceeded pre-switch trends

### 3. Benefits Appear to Grow Over Time

| Period After Switch | Average Effect |
|---------------------|---------------|
| Week 15 (first week) | +1.6 points |
| Weeks 16-18 | +2.2 points |
| Weeks 19-22 | +3.2 points |

This pattern suggests the procedure's benefits accumulate, which is inconsistent with a simple pre-trend explanation.

### 4. What Changed

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches | 49.60 | 53.51 | +3.91 |
| Non-switched branches | 48.86 | 50.85 | +1.99 |
| **Net difference** | | | **+1.92** |

## **Recommendation**

**Approve phased rollout of the new procedure, with monitoring.**

The evidence supports a positive effect, but the pre-trend issue warrants caution. A phased approach allows the cooperative to:
- Confirm benefits persist across different branch types
- Adjust implementation based on early results
- Minimize risk if some branches do not benefit

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Begin phased rollout** starting with branches similar to the early adopters.

2. **Track service scores** for 8-12 weeks after each branch switches to verify expected gains.

3. **Set a success threshold:** If branches do not show at least +1.0 point improvement within 8 weeks, pause and investigate.

4. **Document implementation factors** that correlate with larger improvements (staffing, training, branch size, etc.).

5. **Re-evaluate after 6 months** to decide whether to accelerate, continue, or adjust the rollout.

---

## **Appendix: Analysis Details**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Data:** 709 branches, 12,053 weekly observations (weeks 6-22)
- **Treatment:** 425 branches switched in week 15; 284 branches served as control
- **Limitation:** Pre-existing trend differences suggest effect may be partially overstated
