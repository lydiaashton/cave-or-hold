# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**Prepared for the Board of Directors**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores on average, but effects vary across branches.** Treatment branches gained **2.1 points** (4% improvement) relative to control branches, with high statistical confidence. However, **27% of treatment branches saw no benefit or negative effects**. We recommend a **phased rollout with monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide adoption, allowing identification of branches most likely to benefit.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Average treatment effect** | +2.11 points (95% CI: 0.92 to 3.30) |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.0005 |
| **Branches with positive effect** | 73% of treatment branches |
| **Branches with negative/no effect** | 27% of treatment branches |
| **Effect size** | 0.37 standard deviations (small-to-moderate) |

**What this means:** The procedure works for most branches, but not all. The average benefit is real and reliable, but branch-level outcomes vary considerably.

**Why effects may vary:**
- The analysis found no clear pattern linking the effect to staffing levels or visitor volumes
- Unobserved branch characteristics (layout, staff experience, collection size) may moderate the effect
- Implementation quality may differ across branches

---

## **Study Design & Validity**

| Aspect | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Design** | Difference-in-differences (quasi-experimental) |
| **Sample** | 150 branches, 17 weeks each (2,550 observations) |
| **Parallel trends** | ✓ Confirmed—no differential pre-trends detected |
| **Confounding controls** | ✓ Adjusted for staffing and visit volumes |
| **Statistical power** | ✓ Narrow confidence interval rules out null effect |

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Pilot rollout to 20–30 branches** | Test whether effects replicate in the remaining control branches before full commitment |
| **2** | **Monitor branch-level outcomes** | Identify which branches benefit and which do not |
| **3** | **Investigate implementation factors** | Understand why 27% of branches saw no benefit—training, fidelity, or branch characteristics? |
| **4** | **Proceed to full rollout if pilot succeeds** | Conditional on pilot results matching current findings |

## **Important Context**

**This finding exceeds initial expectations.** The project team anticipated *no effect* on service scores. The data show a positive average effect, which is favorable news—but the variation across branches suggests the procedure is not a universal solution.

**Cost considerations not analyzed.** This analysis measures service score impacts only. The board should weigh the 4% average improvement against implementation costs (training, staff time, potential disruption) before approving full rollout.

## **Questions for Board Discussion**

2. Should we prioritize branches for rollout based on characteristics that may predict success?
