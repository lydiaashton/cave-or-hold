# **Board Summary: New Shelving Procedure Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores of approximately 2 points.** The evidence is robust: the effect is consistent across all post-implementation weeks and unlikely to be due to chance. 

**Recommendation**: Proceed with rollout **pending confirmation that implementation costs are justified** by the service quality gains. A 4% improvement in service scores should be weighed against training, staffing, and operational costs.

---

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated effect** | +1.9 points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | +1.4 to +2.5 points |
| **Relative improvement** | ~4% of average score |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |

### 2. Effect Is Consistent Over Time

All 8 weeks after implementation showed positive effects ranging from +2.5 to +3.3 points, with no evidence of the effect fading.

| **Long-term sustainability** | Only 8 weeks of post-implementation data available |
