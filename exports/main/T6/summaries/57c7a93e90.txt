### What This Means

- **The data is inconclusive**: The true effect could be slightly harmful (-1 point), zero, or moderately beneficial (+4-5 points)
- **The study was underpowered**: With only 34 branches, we could only detect fairly large effects. A 2-point improvement is plausible but unproven
- **No evidence of harm**: The new procedure did not negatively affect staffing or visits

---

## **Recommendation**

**Do not roll out system-wide.** Instead:

1. **Extend data collection** — 8-12 more weeks would substantially improve our ability to detect modest effects
2. **Consider a randomized rollout** — If expanding, randomly assign additional branches to switch, creating a larger evaluation sample
3. **Weigh costs against uncertain benefits** — A 2-point improvement (if real) is modest; ensure implementation costs don't exceed potential gains

---

## **Why the Uncertainty?**

With only 34 branches split between treatment and control, random week-to-week fluctuations in service scores make it difficult to isolate the procedure's effect. This is a limitation of the study design, not necessarily evidence that the procedure doesn't work.
