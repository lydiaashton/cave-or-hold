# **MEMORANDUM (FINAL)**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores, but the board should weigh the benefit against implementation costs before deciding on full rollout.** Service scores increased by **2.45 points** (p < 0.001) in branches that adopted the new procedure. Whether this ~5% improvement justifies system-wide adoption depends on the operational costs, which this analysis does not capture.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### What Changed
| Metric | Effect | Statistical Significance |
|--------|--------|-------------------------|
| Service score | **+2.45 points** | p < 0.001 ✓ |
| Staffing index | +0.10 | p = 0.24 (not significant) |
| Visits index | +0.10 | p = 0.19 (not significant) |

### What This Means
- Service scores **did improve** after adopting the new procedure (contrary to the expectation of no change)
- The improvement is **statistically reliable** (95% CI: 1.20 to 3.70 points)
- No detectable impact on staffing patterns or visitor volume
- **Unknown**: Whether a 2.45-point improvement is noticeable or meaningful to library patrons

### Study Overview
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure; **60 branches** kept the old procedure
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22); switch occurred at week 15
- **Pre-trends validated**: Both groups followed similar trajectories before the switch

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Request a cost-benefit assessment before approving full rollout.** Specifically:

1. **Quantify implementation costs** — Training, staff time, any equipment or process changes
2. **Assess practical significance** — Determine if a 2.45-point service score improvement translates to measurable patron satisfaction gains
3. **Consider phased expansion** — Monitor additional branches before committing all 150 locations

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

| Action | Owner | Timeline |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Estimate rollout costs (training, materials, staff time) | Operations | 2 weeks |
| Survey patrons at switched branches about perceived service quality | Member Services | 4 weeks |
| Review service score trends for 3 additional months | Data Team | Ongoing |
| Board decision on full rollout | Board | After above completed |

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

**Conditional recommendation to proceed with rollout.**

The statistical evidence is clear: the new procedure improved service scores with no detected negative consequences. However, because (a) the practical meaning of a 2.45-point improvement is unknown, (b) implementation costs have not been quantified, and (c) we have only 8 weeks of post-switch data, I recommend:

**Proceed with rollout IF** the cost per branch is modest AND patron feedback from the 90 switched branches is positive. **Delay rollout IF** costs are substantial OR patron surveys show no perceived improvement.

The data supports adoption, but the board should confirm the benefit justifies the expense before committing all 150 branches.

---

*Analysis based on difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors at the branch level. Limitations: 8 weeks post-switch data; cost data not included.*
