# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The data suggest the new procedure improves service scores, but the evidence is not statistically conclusive.** We estimate a **+2.1 point gain (approximately 4%)**, but there is roughly a **10% chance this result occurred by random variation** rather than a real effect. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but treat this as a **continuing pilot** with formal re-evaluation after 6 months. The potential benefit outweighs the risk, but we should confirm the effect with additional data.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches studied | 34 |
| Observation period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Branches using new procedure | 20 |
| Branches using old procedure | 14 |
| Post-switch data | 8 weeks |

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **New procedure branches** | 52.2 | 52.5 | **+0.3** |
| **Old procedure branches** | 51.9 | 50.3 | **-1.6** |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.1 points** |

### Statistical Certainty

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | +2.07 points | Modest improvement |
| P-value | 0.10 | **Not statistically significant** at conventional threshold (0.05) |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.4 to +4.5 | **Includes zero** – cannot rule out no effect |
| Probability effect is positive | ~95% | Suggestive but not definitive |

### What This Means for Decision-Making

| Scenario | Likelihood | Implication |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| Real positive effect (~90%) | Most likely | Rollout delivers ~4% service improvement |
| No real effect (~10%) | Possible | Rollout has no impact (but no harm detected) |
| Negative effect | Very unlikely | Data do not support this |

**Key observation:** Branches keeping the old procedure experienced **declining scores** (-1.6 points) while new procedure branches held steady. The benefit may be **preventing decline** rather than driving improvement.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### **Proceed with Rollout — Conditionally Approved**

| Consideration | Assessment | Weight |
|---------------|------------|--------|
| **Direction of effect** | Positive (as expected) | ✓ Favorable |
| **Magnitude of effect** | +4% improvement | ✓ Meaningful |
| **Statistical certainty** | p = 0.10 | ⚠ Below standard |
| **Risk of harm** | None detected | ✓ Low risk |
| **Cost of reversal** | Minimal | ✓ Low cost |
| **Opportunity cost of delay** | Continued decline at control branches | ⚠ Moderate |

**Decision Rationale:**

This is a **Type II decision** (reversible, moderate stakes) rather than a **Type I decision** (irreversible, high stakes). For Type II decisions, acting on suggestive evidence is appropriate when:

1. ✓ The potential upside is meaningful (4% service improvement)
2. ✓ The potential downside is minimal (no harm detected)
3. ✓ We can learn and adjust (6-month re-evaluation committed)
4. ✓ Inaction has costs (control branches are declining)

**The cost of waiting for perfect certainty exceeds the cost of acting on good-but-imperfect evidence.**

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve rollout with conditions:**

1. **Proceed** with systemwide implementation over the next 4 weeks
2. **Commit** to formal re-evaluation after 6 months (approximately 26 weeks of post-rollout data)
3. **Establish** a minimum success threshold (e.g., maintain or improve current service scores)
4. **Document** any implementation challenges for future refinement

---

## **Risks and Mitigation**

| Risk | Assessment | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Effect doesn't materialize | Moderate (10% chance) | Low cost to maintain; no reversal needed |
| Effect smaller than estimated | Likely | Still positive; adjust expectations |
| Effect not sustained | Unknown | 6-month evaluation will detect |
| Implementation varies by branch | Likely | Provide consistent training materials |

---

## **Conclusion**

This is a **low-risk, moderate-reward decision**. The evidence is not definitive, but it points in a favorable direction with no countervailing concerns. Proceeding with rollout while committing to rigorous follow-up evaluation is the prudent course.

**The board should approve the rollout with the understanding that this is a data-informed decision, not a data-proven decision. Commitment to follow-up analysis is essential.**
