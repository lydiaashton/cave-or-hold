# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks of data from 34 branches)

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Expand cautiously; do not commit to full rollout yet.**

The new procedure shows a **statistically significant average improvement of 2.3 points**, but the effect is **not consistent across branches**. Only 65% of adopting branches improved; 35% saw no benefit or decline. We recommend an extended pilot with 5-10 additional branches while investigating why results vary, then reassessing before system-wide commitment.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Average Effect (Positive)
| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| New Procedure (20 branches) | 50.45 | 52.08 | +1.63 |
| Old Procedure (14 branches) | 53.93 | 53.27 | −0.66 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.29** |

### Branch-Level Variation (Concerning)
| Metric | Treatment Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| Branches that improved | 13 of 20 (65%) | 7 of 14 (50%) |
| Median change | +1.80 points | +0.12 points |
| Range | −5.4 to +8.7 points | −3.1 to +4.2 points |

### Important Limitations
- Treatment branches started with lower scores (potential regression to the mean)
- Only 8 weeks of post-switch data
- Treatment branches still scored below control in 6 of 8 post-switch weeks
- Both groups experienced sharp declines in weeks 21-22 (external factors)

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores on average?** | Yes — by 2.3 points (statistically significant) |
| **Did every branch benefit?** | No — 7 of 20 branches (35%) saw no improvement |
| **Should we roll out to all branches now?** | **No** — evidence is insufficient for system-wide commitment |
| **Should we abandon the new procedure?** | **No** — average effect is real and meaningful |
| **What should we do?** | **Extended pilot + root-cause analysis**, then decide |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure shows promise but is not a universal solution. The average improvement is real, but substantial variation across branches suggests the procedure works better in some contexts than others. A measured expansion with careful monitoring will help identify which branches will benefit before committing cooperative-wide resources.
