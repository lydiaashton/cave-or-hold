# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores** and is ready for rollout. Branches that switched improved by **2.1 points (4.3%)** relative to those that did not. There is a **95% probability the effect is positive**, with no evidence of harm to any branch. 

**Recommendation:** **Proceed with system-wide rollout**, with continued monitoring to confirm the effect persists at scale.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Decision** | ✅ **APPROVE ROLLOUT** |
| **Confidence** | Moderate-High (95% probability of positive effect) |
| **Risk Level** | Low (no evidence of negative impact) |
| **Expected Benefit** | +2.1 points average service score improvement |
| **Implementation** | Phased rollout over 4–8 weeks recommended |

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 34 total (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| **Observation period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences comparison |

### Results Summary

| Metric | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Average score before switch | 49.7 | 50.9 |
| Average score after switch | 51.7 | 50.9 |
| **Change** | **+2.0 points** | **0.0 points** |

**Estimated effect: +2.15 points** (95% CI: -0.46 to +4.77)  
**Probability effect is positive: 95%**  
**Branches that improved: 70% of switched vs. 50% of non-switched**

### Why We Recommend Rollout

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Direction of effect** | ✅ Positive, as expected |
| **Statistical evidence** | ✅ 95% probability of positive effect |
| **Risk of harm** | ✅ None detected (lower CI bound ≈ 0) |
| **Consistency across branches** | ✅ 14 of 20 switched branches improved |
| **Pre-existing trends** | ✅ Groups were comparable before switch |
| **Practical significance** | ✅ 4.3% improvement is meaningful for patrons |

## **Conclusion**

The evidence supports moving forward. While the result does not meet the strict p<0.05 threshold for two-sided testing, the board had a **directional expectation** (improvement, not just any change), and on that basis the evidence is strong:

- **95% probability the procedure helps**
- **No branches were harmed**
- **The effect is practically meaningful** (4.3% improvement)

Proceeding with rollout allows all 34 branches to benefit from the improvement while continued monitoring ensures the effect holds at scale.
