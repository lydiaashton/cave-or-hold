# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The data do not provide statistically conclusive evidence that the new procedure improves service scores.** The estimated effect is positive (+2.1 points) and aligns with expectations, but the result is not statistically significant (p = 0.13). The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.5 to +4.6 points, meaning the true effect could be zero, slightly negative, or meaningfully positive.

**Recommendation:** Given the positive direction, absence of harm signals, and limited sample size, we recommend **continuing the pilot with expanded data collection** before deciding on full rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Measure | Result | Interpretation |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.1 points** | Positive direction, as expected |
| p-value | **0.13** | Not statistically significant |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.5 to +4.6** | Includes zero; uncertainty is substantial |
| Probability effect is positive | **94%** | Evidence leans favorable |
| Baseline service score | ~50 points | Context for effect size |

### **What We Can Conclude**

✓ The new procedure did **not harm** service scores  
✓ The effect is in the **expected direction**  
✓ Effects larger than +4.6 points are **unlikely**  
✓ Effects near zero are **compatible with the data**  

### **What We Cannot Conclude**

✗ We **cannot confirm** the procedure improves scores  
✗ We **cannot rule out** no effect or minimal effect  
✗ We **cannot quantify** the true effect with precision  

### **Why the Uncertainty?**

The primary limitation is **sample size**. With 34 branches, the analysis lacks power to detect modest effects reliably. This is a data limitation, not evidence against the procedure.

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Option 1: Extend the Pilot (Recommended)**

| Action | Timeline | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Continue current pilot | 8-12 more weeks | Increases statistical power |
| Add 5-10 more branches to pilot | Immediate | More clusters = better precision |
| Re-evaluate with expanded data | Week 30-35 | More definitive conclusion |

**This option best balances evidence-building with practical decision-making.**

### **Option 2: Proceed with Cautious Rollout**

If the board prefers action over additional data collection:

- Roll out in **phases** (e.g., 5-7 branches per month)
- Continue **weekly monitoring** of service scores
- Set a **review checkpoint** at 12 weeks post-rollout
- Define **success criteria** in advance (e.g., sustained +1.5 points)

### **Option 3: Maintain Status Quo**

If the board requires statistical significance before proceeding:

- Keep current mixed approach
- No additional resources committed
- Opportunity cost: potential benefits delayed

---

## **Decision Framework**

| Board Priority | Recommended Option |
|----------------|-------------------|
| **Evidence certainty** | Option 1: Extend pilot |
| **Potential upside** | Option 2: Cautious rollout |
| **Risk minimization** | Option 1 or 3 |
| **Quick decision needed** | Option 2 with clear review checkpoint |

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### **Do Not Proceed with Full Rollout at This Time**

**Rationale:**

1. **Insufficient evidence:** The p-value of 0.13 does not meet conventional thresholds for statistical confidence. Rolling out system-wide based on this evidence would be premature.

2. **Meaningful uncertainty remains:** The confidence interval (-0.5 to +4.6) is too wide to support a confident investment decision. The true effect could be negligible.

3. **Low cost of waiting:** Extending the pilot by 8-12 weeks requires minimal additional resources but would substantially improve decision quality.

4. **No urgency signal:** There is no evidence of harm requiring immediate reversal, nor is there conclusive evidence of benefit requiring immediate scale-up.

**Specific Next Steps:**

| Action | Owner | Deadline |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Approve pilot extension | Board | Next meeting |
| Collect 12 additional weeks of data | Operations | Week 34 |
| Re-analyze with expanded dataset | Data Team | Week 36 |
| Make final rollout decision | Board | Week 38 |

**Success Criteria for Full Rollout:**

- Statistically significant effect (p < 0.05) **OR**
- Point estimate ≥ +2.0 points with narrow confidence interval **OR**
- Consistent positive signal across complementary metrics (patron satisfaction, circulation)

---

## **Conclusion**

The analysis yields **inconclusive but encouraging** results. The new procedure shows a positive effect consistent with expectations, but the evidence does not meet conventional thresholds for statistical confidence. 

**Our verdict: Extend the pilot. Gather more evidence. Then decide.**

This approach protects the cooperative from premature investment while keeping the path to adoption open if stronger evidence emerges.
