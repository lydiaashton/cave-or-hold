# LIBRARY COOPERATIVE BOARD MEMORANDUM

## Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)  
**Data:** 578 weekly observations from 34 branches

---

## BOTTOM LINE

**We cannot conclude with statistical confidence that the new procedure improves service scores.** The estimated effect is positive (+2 points), but the evidence falls short of conventional statistical standards (p=0.084). The true effect could plausibly range from slightly negative to moderately positive (−0.3 to +4.5 points).

**Decision context:** There is no evidence the new procedure harms service quality. The board must decide whether the *possibility* of improvement justifies rollout, or whether more evidence is needed.

---

## KEY FINDINGS

### Service Score Changes

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 51.95 | 50.86 | −1.09 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 47.90 | 48.53 | +0.62 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+1.71** |

### Statistical Evidence

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Effect estimate | +2.13 points | Treatment branches improved ~2 points more than control |
| 95% Confidence Interval | −0.29 to +4.55 | Zero effect is plausible; so is a 4.5-point gain |
| P-value | 0.084 | Does not meet 0.05 threshold; modest evidence |
| Probability effect is positive | ~96% | One-sided; suggests direction is likely correct |

### What This Means

- **Statistically:** We cannot rule out that the observed improvement occurred by chance
- **Practically:** The new procedure shows no signs of harm and may provide meaningful benefit
- **Scientifically:** More data would be needed to reach a definitive conclusion

### Validation Checks

✓ Parallel trends confirmed between groups before the switch  
✓ No confounding from staffing or visitor volume  
✓ No evidence of negative effects in any week

---

## FINAL VERDICT

### **RECOMMENDATION: APPROVE PHASED ROLLOUT WITH CONTINUED MONITORING**

**Rationale:**

1. **No detected harm** — Across all measures and time periods, the new procedure shows no negative impact on service scores

2. **Evidence points positive** — While not statistically conclusive, all indicators suggest improvement rather than decline

3. **Potential benefit is meaningful** — If the effect is real, a 2-point improvement represents roughly 4% gain in service quality

4. **Reversible decision** — A phased approach allows the cooperative to halt or adjust if problems emerge

**Implementation Conditions:**

- Roll out to no more than 5 additional branches per month
- Continue weekly service score tracking for all branches
- Review results after 12 weeks; proceed only if trend remains positive
- Establish a minimum threshold (e.g., +1.0 point average improvement) for full cooperative-wide adoption

**If the board prefers greater certainty:** Extend the pilot study for 8–12 additional weeks before any rollout. This would narrow the confidence interval and support a more definitive decision.
