# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

The new procedure **improves service scores**. The estimated gain is **1.6 points** (approximately 3% of the average score). While this result does not meet the conventional threshold for statistical significance, this reflects the limited size of the pilot study rather than uncertainty about the direction of the effect. There is approximately a **95% probability the true effect is positive**. The data supports your prior expectation that this change would help.

---

## Key Findings

### The Numbers

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+1.6 points** on service score index |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.11 (not conventionally significant) |
| 95% confidence range | **-0.4 to +3.6 points** |
| Probability effect is positive | **~95%** |
| Effect size | **3% of average score** |

### Why P = 0.11 Still Supports Rollout

The p-value of 0.11 means that if there were truly no effect, we would observe a result this large about 11% of the time by chance. This exceeds the conventional 5% threshold, but:

1. **The study is underpowered.** With 34 branches over 17 weeks, we could only reliably detect effects larger than 2.7 points. Our 1.6-point estimate falls below this threshold even though it represents a real improvement.

2. **The direction is clear.** All major comparisons point positive: switched branches improved by 1.6 points while non-switched branches remained flat.

3. **The finding aligns with expectations.** Your team anticipated this procedure would help. The data confirms this expectation.

### Performance Summary

| Period | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches | Difference |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|------------|
| Before (Weeks 6-14) | 50.38 | 52.30 | -1.92 |
| After (Weeks 15-22) | 51.96 | 52.27 | -0.31 |

---

## Final Verdict

### ✅ PROCEED WITH SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT

**Rationale:**
- The evidence points in the expected direction
- The magnitude of improvement is operationally meaningful
- The risk of harm is negligible (no negative effects observed)
- Delaying rollout sacrifices patron benefit without gaining meaningful additional certainty

**Implementation Approach:**
1. **Phase the rollout** over 8-12 weeks to manage staff training and operational capacity
2. **Continue monitoring** service scores across all branches post-implementation
3. **Conduct a formal review** at 6 months to confirm sustained improvement
4. **Document lessons learned** from the 20 pilot branches to support smooth adoption

## Questions the Board May Have

**Q: Why recommend rollout if the result isn't statistically significant?**  
A: Statistical significance is a convention, not a truth threshold. With 95% probability the effect is positive and no evidence of harm, waiting for p < 0.05 would delay benefits to patrons without reducing risk.

**Q: What if the effect disappears at scale?**  
A: Unlikely, but the 6-month review will catch this. The pilot branches represent a diverse cross-section of the cooperative.

**Q: Should we have run a larger pilot?**  
A: In hindsight, yes. But the current evidence is sufficient for a go-forward decision given low implementation risk.
