# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores (+1.8 points, p = 0.002). The effect is consistent across all 8 post-implementation weeks but modest in magnitude (3.5% of average score). We recommend proceeding with rollout if implementation costs are low, with continued monitoring to confirm the effect persists.**

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure change service scores?** | **Yes** — clear statistical evidence of improvement |
| **Is the effect large?** | **Modest** — 1.8 points on a ~50-point scale |
| **Is the finding reliable?** | **Yes** — consistent across all 8 post-switch weeks; robust to controls |
| **Should we roll out system-wide?** | **Conditionally yes** — proceed if costs are minimal; monitor for persistence |

**Rationale:** The evidence strongly suggests the new procedure causes improved service scores. However, the effect size is small enough that implementation costs could outweigh benefits. We recommend a **phased rollout** with explicit cost tracking and a **12-week post-implementation review** to confirm the effect persists beyond the initial 8-week window.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.76 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **+0.66 to +2.87 points** |
| Improvement vs. average | **3.5%** |
| Effect size | **0.18 standard deviations** |

### **2. Statistically Robust Result**

- **P-value: 0.002** (highly significant)
- Result holds after controlling for staffing levels and branch visit volumes
- **All 8 post-switch weeks** show switched branches outperforming non-switched branches

### **3. Valid Comparison Between Groups**

| Group | Number of Branches | Pre-Switch Average Score |
|-------|-------------------|-------------------------|
| Switched to new procedure | 90 | 49.85 |
| Kept old procedure | 60 | 50.34 |

The treatment group actually scored slightly **lower** before the switch, making the post-switch reversal more compelling evidence of a real effect.

### **4. Important Limitations**

| Limitation | Implication |
|------------|-------------|
| Only 8 weeks of post-switch data | Unknown if effect persists long-term |
| No cost data available | Cannot calculate net benefit |
| Effect is modest (0.18 SD) | May not justify significant implementation costs |
| Model explains <1% of score variation | Many other factors influence service scores |

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with conditional rollout:**

1. **Approve phased implementation** to remaining 60 branches over 8–12 weeks
2. **Track implementation costs** (training time, labor hours, any equipment)
3. **Continue monitoring** service scores for at least 12 weeks post-rollout
4. **Reconvene in Q2 2026** to review cost-benefit analysis and decide on permanent adoption

## **TECHNICAL APPENDIX**

- **Analysis method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch-clustered standard errors
- **Data source:** Weekly service scores from all 150 branches
- **Observation period:** Weeks 6–22 (9 pre-switch, 8 post-switch)
- **Total observations:** 2,550 branch-week records
- **Control variables:** Staffing index, visits index
- **Switch implementation date:** Week 15
