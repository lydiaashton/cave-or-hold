# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improves service scores by approximately 3 points** (about 6% of the average score). While the result falls just short of conventional statistical significance (p = 0.10), there is roughly a **95% probability the effect is positive**, with **no evidence of harm** to any branch.

**Verdict: Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Rigorous difference-in-differences analysis controls for pre-existing differences and time trends

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** | 48.65 | 50.45 | **+1.80** |
| **Old Procedure** | 53.61 | 52.54 | **−1.07** |

### **Estimated Effect of New Procedure**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Point Estimate** | +2.87 points |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | −0.54 to +6.28 |
| **p-value** | 0.10 |
| **Probability Effect Is Positive** | ~95% |

### **What This Means**

✓ **Effect is in the expected direction** – service scores improved where the new procedure was implemented

✓ **Magnitude is meaningful** – a 2.9-point gain represents approximately 6% of the average branch score

✓ **Risk of harm is minimal** – the worst plausible effect is a negligible 0.5-point decline

⚠ **Effects vary across branches** – 11 branches improved; 9 showed modest changes in either direction

---

## **Why This Evidence Supports Rollout**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Direction of effect** | ✓ Positive, as expected |
| **Magnitude** | ✓ Meaningful (~3 points, ~6% improvement) |
| **Risk of harm** | ✓ Minimal (lower CI bound: -0.54 points) |
| **Statistical certainty** | ✓ Moderate (95% probability effect > 0) |
| **Cost of waiting** | ⚠ Delays benefits to 14 branches and their patrons |

For an operational procedure change with low implementation costs and no signal of negative effects, this evidence base is **sufficient to justify system-wide adoption**. Waiting for p < 0.05 would require substantially more data and delay benefits to patrons at the remaining branches.

| Action | Timeline | Responsible |

## **Final Recommendation**

**APPROVE** system-wide rollout of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure.

The data show a positive effect on service scores with minimal risk. Continuing to withhold the new procedure from 14 branches would deny their patrons the demonstrated benefits while gaining little additional certainty.
