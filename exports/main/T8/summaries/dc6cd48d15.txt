# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 1.8 points (about 3.5%).** The evidence is statistically strong, and the effect appears to grow as staff gain experience. **We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recommendation** | ✅ **APPROVE system-wide rollout** |
| **Confidence Level** | **High** |
| **Expected Impact** | +1.8 points on service score across all branches |
| **Risk Assessment** | **Low** — No evidence of negative effects; benefits accumulate over time |

**Rationale:** The data provides robust evidence that the new procedure causes meaningful improvements in service quality. The statistical analysis meets standard criteria for causal inference, and the growing effect over time indicates sustainable benefits rather than temporary gains.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Estimated impact** | +1.8 points on service score |
| **Statistical confidence** | 99%+ (p < 0.001) |
| **Effect size** | ~0.2 standard deviations |
| **Branches studied** | 709 (425 switched, 284 control) |

**What we observed:**
- Service scores at switched branches rose relative to control branches after week 15
- The benefit increased over time: +0.75 points in week 15, growing to +1.89 by week 22
- 7 of 8 post-switch weeks showed statistically significant improvements

**Why we're confident:**
- In the weeks immediately before the switch (10-13), switched and non-switched branches followed parallel trends
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- The growing effect over time is consistent with a genuine procedural improvement (not a one-time anomaly)

**Important context:**
- Branches selected for the new procedure were underperforming earlier in the year (weeks 6-9)
- This suggests the switch may have been targeted at struggling branches
- By weeks 10-13, these branches had converged with controls, validating our comparison
