# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **demonstrably affected service scores** (p=0.001), contradicting the initial expectation of no difference. However, the effect was not an absolute improvement — rather, branches using the new procedure experienced a **smaller decline** than those keeping the old procedure. The net benefit is approximately 2 points on a 50-point scale (~4%).

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **60 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)

### Service Score Results

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| New Procedure (90 branches) | 55.2 | 54.1 | −1.1 |
| Old Procedure (60 branches) | 53.1 | 50.0 | −3.1 |

**Net Effect of New Procedure: +1.99 points** (95% CI: 0.8 to 3.2; p=0.001)

### What This Means
- The new procedure provided a **buffer against decline**, not an absolute gain
- Pre-switch trends were statistically parallel between groups, validating the comparison
- The effect is statistically reliable but modest in practical terms

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but investigate the system-wide decline.**

**Rationale:**
| Consideration | Assessment |
|--------------|------------|
| Statistical evidence | **Strong** — p=0.001, confidence interval excludes zero |
| Practical significance | **Modest** — ~2 points on a 50-point scale |
| Direction of effect | **Positive** — buffers against decline |
| Initial hypothesis | **Rejected** — there IS a difference |
| Unexplained factors | **Concerning** — all branches declined after week 14 |

**The new procedure is worth adopting** because it helps maintain service quality during periods when scores otherwise fall. Across 150 branches, even a 2-point buffer could meaningfully affect patron experience. However, this alone will not address whatever caused the cooperative-wide decline after week 14 — that warrants separate investigation.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure
2. **Continue monitoring** service scores during implementation to confirm the effect persists
3. **Investigate the post-week-14 decline** affecting all branches (seasonal patterns, staffing changes, or other external factors)
4. **Prioritize lower-performing branches** if a phased rollout is necessary
