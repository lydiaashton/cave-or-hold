# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** We estimate a **2.0-point increase** (95% confidence interval: 0.5 to 3.6 points). The effect is statistically significant and consistent across the 20 pilot branches.

**VERDICT: Recommend system-wide rollout.** The procedure delivers a real, measurable improvement with no identified downsides. While the effect is modest per branch, the cumulative benefit across all 34 branches is meaningful for patron service quality.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What the Data Shows

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated effect** | +2.03 points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | +0.47 to +3.59 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.011 |
| **Effect size** | ~4% of baseline average |

### What Changed

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.7 | 50.9 | +1.2 |
| New Procedure | 53.6 | 56.9 | +3.3 |
| **Net Impact** | | | **+2.0** |

Both groups improved after week 15, but branches using the new procedure improved significantly more.

### Why We Trust These Results

- **Parallel pre-trends:** Both groups followed similar trajectories before the switch (p = 0.21 for differential trends)
- **Robust to controls:** Effect holds after accounting for branch characteristics, weekly variation, staffing, and visitor volume
- **Substantial pilot:** 20 branches (59% of system) tested over 8 post-switch weeks
