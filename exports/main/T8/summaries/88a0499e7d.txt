# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Subject: Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **Bottom Line**

**The data show a statistically significant improvement, but we recommend cautious interpretation.** Branches using the new procedure had service scores **2.1 points higher** than expected (p=0.001). However, given that we anticipated no effect, and the observed effect weakened in later weeks, we recommend **additional validation before full rollout**.

---

### **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Estimated effect | +2.1 points (95% CI: 0.9 to 3.4) |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.001 |
| Effect size | ~4% of average score |
| Pattern over time | Stronger early (+3.0 pts weeks 15-18), weaker later (+1.5 pts weeks 19-22) |

---

### **Reasons for Caution**

1. **Unexpected direction**: We hypothesized no effect on service scores. Unexpected findings require extra validation.

2. **Attenuation**: The effect diminished after the first month. This could indicate:
   - Initial enthusiasm/training effect that faded
   - Normalization of the procedure
   - Random variation

3. **Non-random assignment**: Branches self-selected into early adoption. Unobserved factors (motivated staff, better management) could explain part of the effect.

4. **Magnitude**: A 2-point change may not translate to meaningful patron experience improvements.

---

### **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Statistical Evidence** | ✓ Significant positive effect detected |
| **Practical Significance** | ? Modest effect; patron impact unclear |
| **Study Design** | ⚠ Observational (not randomized) |
| **Effect Stability** | ⚠ Some attenuation over time |
| **Alignment with Theory** | ✗ Unexpected finding |

**VERDICT: Proceed with Caution – Validate Before Full Rollout**

The evidence is **suggestive but not conclusive**. We cannot confidently attribute the entire 2.1-point gain to the procedure change itself. Before committing system-wide resources:

- **Validate** the effect persists beyond the initial implementation period
- **Randomize** the next phase of rollout to eliminate selection bias
- **Clarify** whether the statistical improvement translates to meaningful patron benefits

**Recommendation: Pause full rollout. Complete 8-12 weeks of additional monitoring and/or a randomized pilot before making a system-wide decision.**

---

### **Next Steps**

1. **Extend observation period** – Monitor current branches through week 30+

2. **Consider randomized rollout** – Randomly assign remaining branches to switch now vs. later

3. **Gather qualitative data** – Interview staff and patrons at switched branches

4. **Define success thresholds** – Agree on what improvement magnitude justifies the operational change

5. **Revisit decision in 8-12 weeks** – With more data, provide a more confident recommendation
