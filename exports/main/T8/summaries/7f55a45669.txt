# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The data provide **suggestive but not definitive evidence** that the new procedure improves service scores. The estimated effect is **+2.1 points** in the expected direction, with a **94% probability the true effect is positive**. However, the result does not meet conventional statistical significance thresholds (p=0.11), meaning we cannot rule out no effect with high confidence. Given the positive direction, meaningful effect size, and low implementation risk, we recommend **proceeding with rollout while continuing to monitor**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Service Score Impact
| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Treatment (New Procedure) | 50.8 | 53.9 | **+3.2** |
| Control (Old Procedure) | 52.2 | 53.2 | +1.1 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.1** |

### 2. Statistical Evidence
| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point Estimate | +2.10 points | 4% improvement over baseline |
| P-value (two-tailed) | 0.113 | Not significant at 0.05 level |
| P-value (one-tailed) | 0.057 | Marginally significant |
| 95% Confidence Interval | [-0.5, +4.7] | Includes zero |
| Probability Effect > 0 | 94% | Substantial but not definitive |

### 3. Study Strengths
- **Parallel trends confirmed**: Treatment and control branches tracked together before the switch
- **No confounding**: Effect persists after controlling for staffing and visitor volume
- **Complete data**: All 34 branches, all 17 weeks, no missing values

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **APPROVE PHASED ROLLOUT**

**Rationale:**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Direction of effect** | ✅ Positive (as expected) |
| **Magnitude** | ✅ Meaningful (+4% if real) |
| **Statistical confidence** | ⚠️ Suggestive, not definitive |
| **Risk of harm** | ✅ Low (no branches substantially harmed) |
| **Implementation cost** | ✅ Already absorbed by pilot branches |
| **Opportunity cost of delay** | ⚠️ 20 branches already benefiting; 14 waiting |

**Decision:** The balance of evidence favors adoption. Waiting for definitive statistical proof would delay benefits to 14 control branches and thousands of patrons. The prudent course is to **approve rollout to remaining branches** while committing to continued evaluation.

---

## **Recommendations**

### 1. Approve System-Wide Rollout
- Implement new procedure at remaining 14 branches within 60 days
- Provide training materials based on pilot branch experience

### 2. Continue Monitoring
- Collect 8-12 additional weeks of post-rollout data
- Track service scores, staffing efficiency, and staff feedback

### 3. Establish Success Metrics
- **Primary**: Service score improvement of ≥1.5 points sustained over 6 months
- **Secondary**: Staff satisfaction, processing time, patron feedback

### 4. Review Checkpoint
- **Q2 2026**: Full evaluation with expanded dataset
- If effect diminishes or reverses, pause and investigate
