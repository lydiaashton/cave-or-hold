## **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

### **Subject: New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure – Impact Evaluation**

**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**Verdict: APPROVE ROLLOUT WITH TEMPERED EXPECTATIONS**

The new procedure is **associated with higher service scores** and shows **no evidence of harm**. However, due to pre-existing differences between switched and control branches, we cannot definitively attribute the full 2.0-point gap to the procedure itself. The true effect is likely **positive but modest** (approximately 0.5–1.5 points).

**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout, but:
- Set realistic expectations (benefits may be smaller than pilot suggests)
- Continue monitoring to confirm effects persist
- Consider randomized phasing for remaining branches to generate cleaner evidence

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| Average score before week 15 | 49.5 | 48.5 |
| Average score after week 14 | 52.3 | 49.2 |
| **Observed change** | **+2.8** | **+0.8** |

**Raw difference-in-differences: +2.0 points**  
**Adjusted estimate (accounting for pre-trends): +0.5 to +1.5 points**

1. **Positive Association Confirmed**
   - Switched branches outperform controls after implementation
   - Effect is consistent across all 8 post-implementation weeks
   - No negative impacts detected

2. **Causal Uncertainty**
   - Switched branches were already improving relative to controls *before* the procedure changed
   - Gap widened from 0.6 points (weeks 6-9) to 1.7 points (weeks 12-14) prior to intervention
   - This suggests better-performing branches may have been selected for early adoption

3. **What This Means for the Board**
   - The procedure is **safe** (no harm observed)
   - The procedure is **promising** (positive association confirmed)
   - The procedure's **true causal effect is uncertain** but likely positive and modest

---

### **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did service scores improve in switched branches? | **Yes** |
| Can we attribute all improvement to the new procedure? | **No** |
| Is there evidence of harm? | **No** |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Yes, with monitoring** |

**Rationale:** The organization expected improvement, and the data shows improvement with no downside. Even if the true effect is at the lower end of our adjusted estimate (~0.5 points), the procedure still provides value. The risk of inaction (foregoing potential gains) outweighs the risk of rollout (minimal, given no negative effects observed).
