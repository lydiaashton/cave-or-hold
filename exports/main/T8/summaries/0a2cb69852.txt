## **Executive Summary**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure shows a **positive directional effect** on service scores, consistent with expectations. However, the evidence is **not strong enough to confirm the procedure works**. Branches using the new procedure scored approximately **2 points higher** than expected, but this difference could plausibly be due to random variation.

**Recommendation:** Extend the pilot for 8-12 more weeks before making a rollout decision.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Uncertain. The data suggests a ~2 point improvement, but we cannot rule out no effect.** |
| Should we roll out to all branches now? | **No—evidence is insufficient for system-wide implementation.** |
| Is the analysis reliable? | **Yes—the methodology is sound, but the study is too small for a definitive conclusion.** |

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The Effect Is Positive But Inconclusive**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+2.05 points** (on ~50-point scale) |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.49 to +4.59 points** |
| Statistical Significance | **Not significant (p = 0.11)** |

The confidence interval tells us the true effect could be:
- A meaningful improvement (up to +4.6 points)
- A modest improvement (~+2 points)
- No effect (0 points)
- A small negative effect (down to -0.5 points)

### **2. What Happened to Each Group**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| **Control** (old procedure) | 47.09 | 45.96 | **▼ -1.13** |
| **Treated** (new procedure) | 49.36 | 50.25 | **▲ +0.89** |

Branches using the new procedure **maintained their scores** while control branches declined. The 2-point difference represents this divergence.

### **3. Why We Can't Be Certain**

| Limitation | Impact |
|------------|--------|
| Only 8 weeks of post-switch data | Not enough observations to detect modest effects |
| 34 branches total | Limited statistical power |
| Week-to-week score variability | Adds noise to the estimates |

**The methodology is sound** (parallel trends confirmed, p = 0.85), but the study is underpowered.
