# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to our expectation that the change would make no difference, branches that switched to the new procedure saw their service scores increase by an average of **2.4 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and robust across multiple analytical approaches. **We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** participated (90 switched to new procedure, 60 kept old procedure)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22); switch occurred at week 15
- Analysis used **difference-in-differences** methodology to isolate the procedure's effect

### Results

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated effect of new procedure** | **+2.37 service score points** |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.01 (highly significant) |
| **95% confidence interval** | +0.76 to +3.99 points |
| **Relative improvement** | ~5% increase from baseline |

### Score Changes by Group

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Kept old procedure | 47.6 | 49.4 | +1.8 |
| Switched to new procedure | 46.6 | 50.8 | +4.2 |
| **Attributable to new procedure** | — | — | **+2.4** |

### Analysis Confidence
The finding is robust across multiple model specifications:
- ✓ Controls for week-to-week variations
- ✓ Controls for individual branch characteristics  
- ✓ Controls for both branch and week effects simultaneously
- ✓ Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (validating the methodology)

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

**APPROVE THE ROLLOUT.**

The evidence is clear and statistically sound: the new rotation and shelving procedure improves service scores. With 90 branches already demonstrating success and no evidence of negative effects, extending this procedure to all 150 branches is a low-risk, high-reward decision.

**Confidence Level: High**
- Statistically significant result (p = 0.004)
- Consistent across analytical methods
- Effect size is meaningful (~5% improvement)
- No red flags in the data

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all branches

2. **Monitor implementation** over the next 8–12 weeks to confirm effects persist at scale

3. **Document best practices** from the 90 early-adopter branches to support training

4. **Assess resource implications**—verify whether the new procedure requires additional staff time or costs

5. **Schedule follow-up review** in Q1 2027 to evaluate sustained impact and address any implementation challenges
