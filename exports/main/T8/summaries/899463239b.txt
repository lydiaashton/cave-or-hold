# **Evaluation of New Shelving Procedure on Library Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain** effect on service scores. The best estimate is a **2-point improvement**, but the data cannot rule out no effect. There is approximately a **94% probability the true effect is positive**, but this falls short of the conventional 95% confidence threshold. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout **if** the board is comfortable acting on suggestive (but not definitive) evidence. If the board requires statistical certainty, **extend the evaluation period** before deciding.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Show**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | +2.03 points | Switching branches improved 2 points more than non-switching |
| p-value | 0.11 | Not significant at 5% level |
| 95% confidence interval | -0.48 to +4.53 | True effect could be zero or modestly positive |
| Probability effect > 0 | ~94% | Likely positive, but not certain |
| Effect size | 0.21 SD | Small-to-moderate practical significance |

### **Service Score Trajectory**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Kept Old Procedure | 47.2 | 48.9 | +1.7 |
| Switched to New | 51.4 | 55.1 | +3.8 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.0** |

### **Why the Uncertainty?**

1. **Limited sample:** 34 branches over 17 weeks provides modest statistical power
2. **Pre-existing differences:** Switching branches had higher scores even before the change (+4.2 points), suggesting systematic differences between groups
3. **Short post-switch window:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data limits precision

---

## **Decision Framework for the Board**

| If the board prioritizes... | Then... |
|----------------------------|---------|
| **Acting on best available evidence** | Proceed with rollout. The point estimate is positive, there's no signal of harm, and 94% probability of benefit is reasonably high. |
| **Statistical certainty** | Delay rollout. Collect 8-12 more weeks of data to narrow the confidence interval. |
| **Risk mitigation** | Proceed with **phased rollout** (e.g., 5-10 branches per month) with continued monitoring. |
| **Cost-benefit balance** | Consider implementation costs. If rollout is low-cost, proceed. If costly, wait for stronger evidence. |

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with phased rollout.**

**Rationale:**

1. **The evidence points in the expected direction.** The 2-point improvement is consistent with the board's expectation that the new procedure would help.

2. **No harm detected.** There is no evidence the new procedure reduced service scores at any branch or in any week.

3. **The cost of waiting exceeds the cost of proceeding.** Delaying rollout means forgoing potential benefits for weeks or months. If the procedure is indeed beneficial (94% probability), every week of delay denies patrons better service.

4. **Reversibility.** If future data show the effect was illusory or negative, the procedure can be adjusted or reversed. The risk is manageable.

5. **Practical significance.** A 2-point gain on a 50-point scale (~4% improvement) is operationally meaningful for a cooperative serving the public.

**Conditions:**

- Roll out over 8–12 weeks, not all at once
- Continue weekly service score tracking
- Report back to the board after week 30 with updated analysis
- Be prepared to adjust implementation if certain branches underperform

---

## **Recommended Path Forward**

**Phased Rollout (Recommended)**
- Roll out to remaining branches over 2-3 months
- Continue tracking service scores weekly
- Re-evaluate after week 30 with expanded dataset
- If effect remains positive but uncertain, assess whether operational benefits justify continuation

---

## **Bottom Line (Restated)**

The data are **encouraging but not conclusive**. The new procedure likely helps, but we cannot be statistically certain. **On balance, the evidence supports proceeding with a cautious, monitored rollout.**
