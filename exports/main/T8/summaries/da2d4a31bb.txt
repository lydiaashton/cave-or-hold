# **MEMORANDUM**

**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores** by approximately **2–3 points** (about 5%). While statistical uncertainty remains, there is a **94% probability the effect is positive**, and the data shows no evidence of harm. **We recommend proceeding with a phased rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure change service scores? | **Yes, likely positive** |
| Is the evidence conclusive? | **No—moderate confidence** |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Yes, but phased** |
| What is the risk of inaction? | **Delayed patron benefits** |
| What is the risk of rollout? | **Low—no harm detected** |

**VERDICT: APPROVE PHASED ROLLOUT**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Rigorous **difference-in-differences** analysis with cluster-robust standard errors

### Results

| Metric | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|------------------|------------------|
| Before switch (weeks 6–14) | 48.8 | 50.3 |
| After switch (weeks 15–22) | 51.0 | 50.1 |
| **Change** | **+2.2** | **-0.2** |

**Estimated Treatment Effect: +2.4 points** (95% CI: -0.7 to +5.4)

### Interpretation
- **94% probability** the true effect is positive
- Effect size represents approximately **5% improvement** on the service score scale
- **No evidence of negative impact**: the lower bound of uncertainty is only -0.7 points
- **Effect appears to strengthen over time**: post-switch weeks 20–22 show growing gains (+1.3 to +2.8 points)

### Validity Checks
- ✅ **Parallel trends confirmed**: Pre-switch trajectories were statistically indistinguishable (p = 0.58)
- ✅ **No pre-existing differences**: Switched and control branches had similar baseline characteristics

---

## **Next Steps**

### Recommended Actions

1. **Proceed with phased rollout**
   - Begin expanding the new procedure to additional branches
   - Prioritize branches with capacity for careful implementation

2. **Continue systematic monitoring**
   - Track service scores for all branches during rollout
   - Target 12+ weeks of post-implementation data per branch

3. **Document implementation quality**
   - Capture staff training completion, timeline adherence, and any deviations
   - This will help explain variation in outcomes across branches

4. **Re-evaluate at 6 months**
   - With expanded data, confidence intervals will narrow significantly
   - Decision point: full system-wide adoption vs. targeted adjustments

---

## **Conclusion**

The evidence supports moving forward. The new procedure shows a **meaningful positive effect** on service scores with **no detectable downside**. While statistical certainty is not absolute, waiting for perfect evidence would delay benefits to patrons. A phased rollout balances opportunity with prudence.
