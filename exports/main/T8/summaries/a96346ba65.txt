# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new procedure showed a **2.2-point improvement** in service scores (approximately 4%). While this result is **not statistically conclusive** (p = 0.13), the evidence points in a positive direction with **no detected negative effects**. Given the low cost of this process change, we recommend a **phased rollout** rather than waiting for definitive proof.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Performance**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Average score before switch (Weeks 6-14) | 47.4 | 50.7 |
| Average score after switch (Weeks 15-22) | 49.6 | 50.8 |
| **Change** | **+2.2** | **+0.0** |

### **Statistical Confidence**

- **P-value:** 0.13 (above the 0.05 threshold for statistical significance)
- **95% Confidence Interval:** -0.6 to +5.0 points
- **Interpretation:** The true effect is most likely positive, but we cannot rule out a small or zero effect with current data

### **Important Context**

1. **Baseline differences:** Branches selected for the switch had lower service scores before the change. The new procedure may help lower-performing branches catch up.

2. **Possible learning curve:** The performance gap narrowed progressively after the switch. Week 22 was the first week switched branches outperformed non-switched branches.

3. **Limited data:** With only 8 weeks of post-switch data across 34 branches, the analysis has limited power to detect modest effects.

## **Final Verdict**

**APPROVE PHASED ROLLOUT**

The new procedure shows **promising positive effects** without evidence of harm. While the data is not yet definitive, waiting for statistical certainty would delay potential benefits for a low-cost operational change. A phased approach allows the cooperative to:

- Capture benefits sooner for additional branches
- Continue gathering evidence to confirm the effect
- Retain flexibility to adjust or halt if results disappoint

**Decision Required:** Authorize expansion of the new procedure to 50% of remaining branches in Q1 2027, with full board review in Q2 2027.
