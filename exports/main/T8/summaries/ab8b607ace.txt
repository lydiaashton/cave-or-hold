# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving & Rotation Procedure  

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

# **DO NOT PROCEED WITH SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**

**Confidence Level: High**

The evidence does not support causal claims that the new procedure improves service scores. The observed improvement is confounded by pre-existing differences between branches that switched and those that did not. Approving rollout based on this analysis would risk investing in a procedure that may have little to no actual benefit.

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

While branches that switched to the new procedure show higher service scores, **we cannot confidently attribute this improvement to the procedure itself**. The branches selected to switch were already improving faster than other branches before the change occurred. After accounting for this pre-existing difference, the true effect of the new procedure is uncertain and likely much smaller than initial estimates suggested.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. The Core Problem: Pre-Existing Differences**

| Issue | Evidence |
|-------|----------|
| Treatment branches were improving faster BEFORE the switch | +0.21 points/week (p = 0.032) |
| Gap between groups doubled during pre-period alone | From 1.3 to 3.2 points (weeks 6-14) |
| No clear "jump" when the switch occurred | Only 0.4 point discontinuity at week 15 |

**This violates the key assumption** needed to claim the procedure caused the improvement.

### **2. What the Data Actually Show**

| Metric | Initial Estimate | Adjusted Estimate |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Raw difference-in-differences | +2.13 points | — |
| After accounting for pre-trends | — | **+0.5 to +1.3 points** |
| Statistical certainty | High | **Low/Uncertain** |

### **3. The Gap Was Widening Before the Switch**

```
Week:     6     8     10    12    14    15    17    19    21
Gap:    1.3   1.5   1.5   2.3   3.2   3.6   3.5   3.9   4.9
        |--------- Pre-period ---------|---- Post-period ----|
```

The treatment-control gap grew steadily throughout the observation period, with no obvious break when the procedure changed.

### **4. Selection Bias Is the Likely Explanation**

The 425 branches that switched may differ systematically from the 284 that didn't:
- More motivated management?
- Better baseline resources?
- Already on an improvement trajectory?

Without random assignment, we cannot separate these factors from the procedure's effect.

---

## **WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE DECISION**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did switched branches improve more than non-switched? | **Yes** |
| Can we credit the new procedure? | **No — not confidently** |
| Is system-wide rollout justified by this evidence? | **No** |
| Is the procedure potentially harmful? | **No evidence of harm** |

---

## **RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Pause system-wide rollout** | Current evidence is insufficient to justify cooperative-wide investment |
| **2** | **Investigate selection process** | Understand why certain branches switched and whether they differed systematically |
| **3** | **Consider a randomized trial** | Randomly assign remaining branches to switch or not; this would provide causal evidence |
| **4** | **Continue monitoring switched branches** | Track whether improvements sustain or regress over 6-12 months |
| **5** | **Gather qualitative feedback** | Interview staff at switched branches about implementation challenges and perceived benefits |

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The data show a **positive association** between the new procedure and service scores, but **not a causal effect**. Branches that adopted the new procedure were already on a stronger improvement trajectory before the switch. 

**The board should not approve system-wide rollout based on this evidence alone.** A randomized evaluation or more rigorous quasi-experimental design is needed before making a cooperative-wide investment decision.
