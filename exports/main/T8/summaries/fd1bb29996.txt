# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure produced a **modest, statistically significant improvement** in service scores (+2.1 points, p=0.002). However, the effect **appears to diminish over time**—stronger in the first month post-switch, weaker thereafter. Given the board's expectation of no effect, this positive result is noteworthy, but the **practical impact is limited** and may not persist. We recommend a **cautious, monitored rollout** rather than immediate system-wide adoption.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Positive but Modest Effect
- Switching branches improved by **2.1 points** relative to controls (95% CI: 0.8 to 3.5)
- This is approximately **4% of the average score** and **0.22 standard deviations**
- The effect is **smaller than typical week-to-week variation** in scores (11+ points)

### 2. Effect May Not Be Durable

| Post-Switch Period | Treated − Control Difference |
|-------------------|------------------------------|
| Weeks 15-18 (early) | +1.7 points average |
| Weeks 19-22 (later) | +0.4 points average |

The gap between switching and control branches narrowed substantially after the first month. This could indicate:
- A temporary novelty or enthusiasm effect
- Control branches adapting informally
- Implementation quality declining over time

### 3. Analysis Is Methodologically Sound
- **Difference-in-differences design** with 150 branches over 17 weeks
- **Parallel pre-trends confirmed** (p=0.51)—groups were tracking together before the switch
- Results hold when controlling for staffing and visitor volume

### 4. Contradicts Prior Expectation
The board expected **no difference**. We found a **positive effect**, albeit modest. This warrants attention but not necessarily enthusiasm.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Recommendation: APPROVE LIMITED ROLLOUT**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Statistical evidence | ✓ Significant (p=0.002) |
| Effect size | ⚠ Modest (2.1 points) |
| Durability | ⚠ Uncertain (fading observed) |
| Cost-benefit | ⚠ Unknown (costs not quantified) |
| Risk | ✓ Low (no negative effects detected) |

**Rationale**: The procedure does improve service scores, contradicting the expectation of no effect. There is no evidence of harm. However, the modest effect size and signs of fading warrant a **phased approach** rather than immediate system-wide adoption. Expand to 50% of remaining branches, monitor for 12 weeks, then reassess.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Pilot expansion** – Extend the procedure to ~75 additional branches (not all 150) to confirm the effect and study the fading pattern.

2. **Investigate the fade** – Interview branch staff about implementation challenges. Document whether procedures are being followed consistently over time.

3. **Quantify costs** – Calculate training time, materials, and ongoing labor required. Compare against the 2-point service score benefit.

4. **Extend monitoring** – Collect at least 12 additional weeks of data to determine if scores stabilize or continue declining toward baseline.

5. **Set a decision checkpoint** – Reconvene the board after the pilot expansion to decide on full rollout based on updated evidence.

---

## **Analytical Notes**

- **Method**: Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects and cluster-robust standard errors
- **Data**: 2,550 observations (150 branches × 17 weeks; 8 weeks post-switch)
- **Limitation**: Short post-period makes it difficult to assess long-term impact
- **Key uncertainty**: Whether the observed fading is temporary or indicates the effect will disappear
