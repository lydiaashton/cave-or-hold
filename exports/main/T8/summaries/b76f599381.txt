# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure shows a modest positive effect and is worth expanding, with continued monitoring.**

Branches using the new procedure maintained service scores better than those keeping the old procedure—approximately **2 points higher** after the switch (about 4% relative difference). However, scores declined system-wide, and the new procedure protected against decline rather than driving absolute improvement. We recommend proceeding with rollout while monitoring for sustained effects.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure change service scores?** | Yes—modestly but significantly |
| **Should we roll it out to all branches?** | **Yes, with conditions** |
| **Confidence level** | Moderate (statistically solid, modest effect size) |
| **Expected impact** | Protection against service quality decline, not dramatic improvement |
| **Risk of rollout** | Low—procedure appears beneficial with no detected harms |

**Decision:** Approve system-wide rollout with phased implementation and 12-month evaluation checkpoint.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **What the Data Shows**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| New Procedure (102 branches) | 53.07 | 52.57 | −0.50 |
| Old Procedure (48 branches) | 51.58 | 49.08 | −2.50 |

**Estimated Effect: +2.0 points (statistically significant, p < 0.01)**

### **Important Context**

- **Both groups declined** after week 15, likely due to seasonal or system-wide factors
- The new procedure **mitigated the decline** rather than producing absolute gains
- Treatment branches had **higher baseline scores** before the switch, though pre-switch trends were parallel
- Effect size is **modest** (~4%) relative to week-to-week variability

### **Analytical Strengths & Limitations**

| Strengths | Limitations |
|-----------|-------------|
| ✓ Control group for comparison | ⚠ Only 8 weeks of post-switch data |
| ✓ Parallel trends verified before switch | ⚠ Treatment branches started with higher scores |
| ✓ Effect robust to staffing/visits controls | ⚠ Modest effect size relative to variability |
| ✓ Statistically significant with proper standard errors | ⚠ Long-term sustainability unknown |

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout, with the following conditions:**

1. **Phase the rollout** to allow for monitoring and adjustment
2. **Set realistic expectations**—the procedure protects service quality rather than dramatically improving it
3. **Track outcomes for 6–12 months** to confirm sustained benefits
4. **Identify and support branches** that don't see expected gains
