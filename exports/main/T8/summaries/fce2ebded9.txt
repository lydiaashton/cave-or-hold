# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**Recommendation: Proceed with a phased rollout and continued evaluation.**

The new procedure is associated with a **1.9 point improvement** in service scores. However, important analytical limitations reduce confidence that this effect is entirely caused by the procedure itself.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did service scores improve for branches that switched?** | **Yes** — treated branches improved 3.9 points vs. 2.0 points for controls |
| **Can we attribute this entirely to the new procedure?** | **No** — pre-existing trends and system-wide changes confound the estimate |
| **Is the effect large enough to matter?** | **Modest** — 1.9 points (~4% of baseline, 0.19 SD) is a small-to-medium effect |
| **Should we roll out system-wide immediately?** | **No** — evidence is promising but not definitive |
| **Should we abandon the new procedure?** | **No** — all post-switch indicators point in a positive direction |

**Verdict: Cautiously optimistic. The weight of evidence suggests a real but modest benefit. A phased rollout with embedded evaluation is the prudent path forward.**

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The new rotation and shelving procedure shows **promising but not conclusive** evidence of improving service scores. The pattern of results is consistent with a genuine benefit, but pre-existing differences between branches and a system-wide change at week 15 mean we cannot rule out alternative explanations.

A **phased rollout** allows the cooperative to:
- Begin realizing potential benefits at additional branches
- Maintain scientific rigor to confirm the effect
- Avoid the cost and disruption of a full reversal if the effect proves spurious
- Build organizational learning before full implementation

**We recommend the Board approve a phased expansion with continued evaluation, with a final system-wide decision after 8-12 weeks of additional data.**
