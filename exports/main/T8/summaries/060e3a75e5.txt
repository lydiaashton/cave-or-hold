# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is suggestive but not conclusive.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed service scores approximately **2 points higher** than control branches, but this difference is **not statistically significant** when accounting for the proper uncertainty in the data. The true effect could reasonably range from slightly negative (-0.5 points) to moderately positive (+4.6 points). **We recommend a cautious, phased expansion with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated Effect** | +2.05 points |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | -0.5 to +4.6 points |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.11 (not significant at 5% level) |
| **Interpretation** | Data is consistent with no effect, small positive effect, or moderate positive effect |

### What This Means
- The **point estimate** (2 points) is positive and aligns with expectations
- However, the **uncertainty is large** – we cannot confidently distinguish the effect from random variation
- With only 34 branches and 8 post-switch weeks, the study may be **underpowered** to detect the true effect

---

## **Final Verdict**

**PROCEED WITH CAUTION — EXPAND THE PILOT, DO NOT YET ROLL OUT SYSTEM-WIDE**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Direction of Effect** | ✅ Positive (as expected) |
| **Magnitude of Effect** | ✅ Meaningful (~4% improvement) |
| **Statistical Certainty** | ⚠️ Insufficient (p = 0.11) |
| **Risk of Full Rollout** | ⚠️ Moderate (costly if effect is zero) |
| **Risk of Waiting** | ✅ Low (pilot branches already benefiting if effect is real) |

**Rationale:** The data shows a promising signal in the expected direction, but the study lacks the statistical power to confirm the effect with confidence. A phased expansion allows the cooperative to capture potential benefits while avoiding the cost and disruption of a full rollout that may not deliver meaningful returns. Additional data from an expanded pilot will provide the evidence needed for a definitive decision.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with phased expansion, not full rollout.** Specifically:

1. **Expand to 5–10 additional branches** as a second-phase pilot
2. **Extend the observation period** to 12–16 weeks post-switch for better precision
3. **Re-evaluate** after collecting more data before committing to system-wide implementation

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Continue monitoring** the current 20 treatment branches
2. **Select 5–10 branches** for phase-2 pilot expansion
3. **Collect 3–6 months of additional data** to improve statistical precision
4. **Re-analyze** with larger sample before making final rollout decision
5. **Document implementation fidelity** to ensure the procedure is being applied consistently

---

## **Technical Notes**

- Initial analysis showed p = 0.02, but this used incorrect (non-clustered) standard errors
- Proper cluster-robust standard errors (accounting for within-branch correlation) yield p = 0.11
- The study has limited statistical power due to the number of branches (34) and post-switch weeks (8)
- A larger sample or longer observation period would provide more definitive evidence
