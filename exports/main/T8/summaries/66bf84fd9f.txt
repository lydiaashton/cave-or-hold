# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores.** Branches that adopted it gained approximately **1.9 points** (about 4%) above what would have been expected based on trends at non-switched branches. The effect is reliable (p=0.001) and persisted across the 8-week post-implementation period.

**However, the improvement is modest** (about 0.2 standard deviations). The board should weigh this benefit against implementation costs before approving full rollout. If costs are low, the evidence supports proceeding. If costs are substantial, the board may wish to consider whether a ~4% service improvement justifies the investment.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with rollout, conditional on cost review.**

The data provide clear evidence that the new procedure improves service scores. The effect is statistically robust and shows no signs of fading over the observation period. However, the magnitude of improvement is modest. 

**Our recommendation:**
- **If rollout costs are minimal** (existing staff can implement with brief training, no new equipment needed): **Approve full rollout immediately.**
- **If rollout requires significant investment**: **Request a formal cost-benefit analysis** before proceeding. A 4% service improvement may or may not justify substantial expenses, depending on budget priorities.

**We do NOT recommend:** Abandoning the procedure or extending the pilot indefinitely. The evidence is sufficient to conclude the procedure works; the only remaining question is whether the benefit justifies the cost.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measurable Improvement, Modest in Size**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 50.59 | 51.02 | +0.43 |
| Branches using new procedure | 50.26 | 52.62 | +2.36 |

**Net effect: +1.93 points** (95% confidence interval: 0.78 to 3.08; p=0.001)

### **2. The Result Is Statistically Sound**

- **Significant at 99% confidence level** (p=0.001)
- **Parallel trends verified**: Both groups tracked together before the switch (p=0.76)
- **Robust to controls**: Effect persists after accounting for staffing and visitor volume
- **No fade-out observed**: Effect was consistent in weeks 15-18 and weeks 19-22

### **3. Important Context**

- Service scores have a standard deviation of ~10 points; the 1.9-point gain equals about **0.2 standard deviations**
- The control group's natural variation over the same period was only +0.4 points
- Analysis covers 8 weeks post-switch; longer-term effects are unknown
- Implementation costs were not included in this analysis
