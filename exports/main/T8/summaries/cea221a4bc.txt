# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is mixed.** While initial analysis suggested a ~2-point improvement, deeper examination reveals the switched branches were already on a different trajectory *before* the change. When we account for this, the estimated effect shrinks to **~1.5 points**, and the effect is **not statistically significant** in the weeks immediately surrounding the switch. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with caution.** Consider a phased rollout with continued monitoring rather than a full immediate rollout.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer | Confidence |
|----------|--------|------------|
| Did service scores improve after the switch? | **Yes** | High |
| Was the new procedure the cause? | **Uncertain** | Medium |
| Did any branches benefit? | **Yes, most did** | High |
| Should we roll out to all branches now? | **Not yet** | High |
| Should we continue evaluating? | **Yes** | High |

**Overall Assessment:** 🟡 **PROCEED WITH CAUTION**

The new procedure shows promise and likely provides some benefit, but the evidence is not strong enough to justify an immediate system-wide rollout. A phased expansion with rigorous monitoring will provide clearer evidence while minimizing risk.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Initial Analysis vs. Adjusted Analysis

| Analysis Method | Estimated Effect | Statistical Significance |
|-----------------|------------------|-------------------------|
| Simple Difference-in-Differences | +2.0 points | p < 0.001 ✓ |
| Matched Comparison (controls for baseline) | +1.5 points | p < 0.001 ✓ |
| Narrow Window (weeks 13-16 only) | +0.55 points | p = 0.47 ✗ |

### Critical Concern: Pre-Existing Differences

**The parallel trends assumption is violated.** Switched branches were already improving at a different rate than non-switched branches *before* the procedure change:

- Pre-trend interaction term: **p = 0.002** (statistically significant)
- This means we cannot fully attribute the post-switch difference to the new procedure
- Some of the observed improvement may reflect pre-existing momentum

### What We Can Say With Confidence

1. **Switched branches did improve more** than non-switched branches overall (+2.8 vs +0.8 points)
2. **77% of switched branches** saw improvements vs. 59% of non-switched
3. **The procedure did not harm** branches on average
4. **Staffing and visitor volume** both positively affect service scores (independent of the procedure)

### What Remains Uncertain

1. **How much of the improvement is due to the procedure** vs. pre-existing trends
2. **Whether the effect will persist** beyond the 8-week observation period
3. **Why ~23% of switched branches saw no benefit** (or declines)
