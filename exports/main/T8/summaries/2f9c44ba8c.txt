# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** [Current Date]  
**Subject:** Results of Branch Procedure Change Pilot – Recommendation for Phased Rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a modest improvement in service scores, but the effect is inconsistent.** We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

The pilot branches achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** than control branches on average. However, the benefit varied significantly week-to-week and appeared to fade over time. This suggests the procedure helps, but may require ongoing reinforcement to sustain the effect.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Metric | Result | Interpretation |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Average treatment effect | **+1.95 points** | Modest improvement |
| Statistical significance | **p = 0.032** | Statistically significant |
| 95% Confidence interval | **+0.17 to +3.74** | Lower bound barely above zero |
| Relative improvement | **~4%** | Small but meaningful |

### **Week-by-Week Results After Switch**

| Weeks | Average Effect | Pattern |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| 15–18 (early) | +3.19 points | Strong initial effect |
| 19–22 (later) | +1.18 points | Effect faded |
| **3 of 8 weeks** | Negative effect | Treatment group scored worse |

### **Study Overview**

- **34 branches** participated over 17 weeks
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure (starting week 15)
- **14 branches** maintained the old procedure (control group)
- Analysis compared score changes between groups before and after the switch

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with caution.** The data supports rolling out the new procedure, but with important caveats:

| ✅ Strengths | ⚠️ Concerns |
|--------------|-------------|
| Statistically significant positive effect | Effect size is modest (~2 points) |
| No pre-existing differences between groups | Benefit faded after initial weeks |
| Effect holds after controlling for staffing/visits | 3 of 8 post-switch weeks showed no benefit |
| Low risk, low cost intervention | Effect is small relative to normal score variation |

**Bottom line:** The procedure likely provides a real but modest benefit. It is worth implementing, but should not be expected to transform service quality on its own.

---

## **Recommendation**

### **Approve a phased rollout with the following conditions:**

1. **Implement in phases** over 8–12 weeks, not all at once
2. **Set success thresholds:** Require sustained improvement of 1.5+ points to consider the rollout successful
3. **Monitor closely:** Track weekly service scores for all branches during rollout
4. **Investigate the fade:** Understand why the effect diminished after week 18 (implementation drift? seasonal factors?)
5. **Document best practices:** Capture what high-performing branches did differently
