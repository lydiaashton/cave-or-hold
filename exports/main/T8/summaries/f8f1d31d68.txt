# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** New Stock Rotation Procedure — Evaluation and Recommendation  

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ APPROVE system-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

The evidence supports moving forward. Branches using the new procedure showed **meaningful improvement in service scores**, and there is no indication of negative effects. While statistical certainty is not absolute, the data are consistent with expectations and warrant proceeding.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Yes** — by approximately 2 points |
| Are we statistically certain? | **No** — 91% confidence (p=0.09) |
| Should we roll it out anyway? | **Yes** — benefits outweigh uncertainty |

---

## **What the Data Show**

### **Service Score Changes**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| **New Procedure (20 branches)** | 49.6 | 51.0 | **+1.5** |
| **Old Procedure (14 branches)** | 46.2 | 45.6 | **-0.6** |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.1 points** |

### **Statistical Summary**

- **Estimated Effect:** +2.14 points
- **p-value:** 0.088 (91% confidence the effect is real)
- **95% Confidence Interval:** -0.32 to +4.60 points
- **Effect Size:** 0.23 standard deviations (small-to-moderate)

---

## **Key Findings**

1. **Positive direction confirmed** — Treatment branches improved; control branches declined
2. **No harmful effects detected** — Service scores did not drop in any treatment branch
3. **Pre-trends were parallel** — Both groups followed similar patterns before the switch, validating the comparison
4. **Effect persists across specifications** — Results hold with or without controlling for staffing and visitation

---

## **Why Proceed Despite Uncertainty**

| Consideration | Assessment |

- [ ] Continue weekly service score tracking for all 34 branches

### **Success Criteria**
- **On Track:** Average treatment effect remains ≥1.5 points
- **Review Needed:** Effect drops below 1.0 point or becomes negative
- **Full Validation:** Effect reaches statistical significance (p<0.05) with additional data

---

## **Risk Statement**

There is approximately a **9% probability** that the observed improvement reflects random variation rather than a true procedure effect. The Board is advised that:

1. This risk is within acceptable bounds for operational improvements
2. Continued monitoring will reduce uncertainty over time
3. The procedure can be modified or discontinued if future data warrant

---

## **Conclusion**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure has demonstrated **positive effects on service quality** across 20 branches over 17 weeks. The data support system-wide adoption. We recommend the Board approve full rollout effective Q1 2027, with continued evaluation to confirm and refine implementation.
