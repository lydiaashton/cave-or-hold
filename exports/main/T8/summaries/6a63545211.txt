# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The best estimate is that the new procedure improves service scores by about **2.3 points (~5%)**, but this result is **not statistically significant at conventional levels** (p=0.10). The data are consistent with both a meaningful positive effect and no effect. There is **no evidence of harm**. Given the positive direction of evidence and relatively low risk, a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** is recommended.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Analysis uses a rigorous difference-in-differences approach with branch and week fixed effects

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect of new procedure | **+2.31 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.43 to +5.05** |
| P-value | **0.099** |
| Relative improvement | **~4.6%** |

### **What This Means**
- Branches that switched improved by **2.38 points** on average; control branches improved by only **0.07 points**
- The procedure likely has a **positive effect**, but we cannot rule out the possibility of no effect
- There is **no evidence of harm** from the new procedure
- Pre-switch trends were nearly identical between groups, supporting the validity of the comparison

## **Final Verdict**

### **Recommendation: Proceed with Phased Rollout**

**The board should approve expanding the new procedure to additional branches while continuing to collect and analyze data.**

**Rationale:**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|--------------|------------|
| **Likely effect** | Positive (+2.3 points, ~5% improvement) |
| **Statistical certainty** | Moderate (p=0.10; not definitive but suggestive) |
| **Risk of harm** | Low (confidence interval lower bound is near zero) |
| **Implementation cost** | Should be weighed against potential benefit |
| **Value of more data** | High (additional weeks will increase confidence) |

**This verdict balances three factors:**

1. **The point estimate is meaningfully positive** — Our best guess is that the procedure helps, and a 5% improvement in service scores is worth pursuing.

2. **We cannot be certain** — The result does not meet the conventional threshold for statistical significance, so full commitment would be premature.

3. **The risk is asymmetric** — If we're wrong and the effect is zero, we've invested in a neutral change. If we're right, branches and patrons benefit. There's no signal of negative effects.

---

## **Supporting Actions**

1. **Extend the evaluation period** — Collect 8–12 additional weeks of data to increase statistical confidence

2. **Monitor implementation quality** — Ensure consistent adoption across branches; variation in implementation may be diluting the measured effect

3. **Track costs alongside benefits** — If the new procedure requires additional staff time or resources, compare those costs against the ~5% service improvement

4. **Set a review date** — Reconvene in 3 months with updated data to make a final go/no-go decision on full rollout

---

## **Decision Framework**

| Board Priority | Recommended Action |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Maximize service quality | Roll out broadly; effect is likely positive |
| Minimize resource risk | Continue pilot; gather more data first |
| **Balanced approach** | **Phased rollout with monitoring** ← **Recommended** |
