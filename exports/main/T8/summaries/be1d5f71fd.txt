# Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

## Bottom Line

**Verdict: DO NOT APPROVE system-wide rollout at this time.**

The evidence is **inconclusive**. While the point estimate suggests a 2.3-point improvement, proper statistical testing shows this effect is not distinguishable from zero (p = 0.13). The 95% confidence interval [-0.6, 5.2] includes both modest harm and modest benefit. Additionally, 35% of switched branches saw scores decline, indicating the procedure does not work uniformly.

**Recommendation:** Pause full rollout. Investigate why outcomes vary across branches, then pursue a targeted expansion to branches most likely to benefit.

---

## Key Findings

### The Result Is Less Certain Than It Appeared
Initial analysis used standard errors that were too optimistic. When accounting for the fact that we have multiple weekly observations from the same branches (the correct statistical approach), the evidence weakens considerably.

| Metric | Initial Analysis | Corrected Analysis |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Treatment effect | +2.29 points | +2.29 points |
| p-value | 0.005 (significant) | 0.13 (not significant) |
| 95% CI | [0.68, 3.89] | [-0.60, 5.17] |

### Substantial Variation Across Branches
The procedure did not work uniformly:

| Outcome | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|---------|------------------|------------------|
| Scores improved | 13 of 20 (65%) | 7 of 14 (50%) |
| Scores declined | 7 of 20 (35%) | 7 of 14 (50%) |

Some switched branches gained 8+ points; others lost 5+ points. This suggests **local factors matter**—the procedure may work well in some contexts but not others.

### What We Still Don't Know
- **Why** did some branches improve while others declined?
- **How long** do effects persist? (We only have 8 weeks post-switch)
- **What are the costs** of implementing the new procedure?
- **Which branch characteristics** predict success or failure?

## Final Verdict

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores on average? | **Uncertain** — point estimate is positive but not statistically significant |
| Should we roll out to all branches now? | **No** — evidence is too weak to justify system-wide change |
| Should we abandon the procedure entirely? | **No** — many branches did improve; there is potential value |

**Bottom line for decision-makers:** The procedure shows promise but is not a universal solution. A cautious, data-driven expansion is warranted—not a blanket rollout.
