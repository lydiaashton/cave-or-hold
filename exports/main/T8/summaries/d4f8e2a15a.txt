# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** November 15, 2026
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Does the new procedure work?** | **Yes** — it protects service scores from decline |
| **Should we roll it out system-wide?** | **Yes** — with investigation into broader score trends |
| **Will scores go up?** | **No** — the procedure maintains, not improves, scores |
| **Confidence in findings?** | **High** — statistically significant (p = 0.004) |

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure protected service scores from decline, but did not improve them in absolute terms.** After the switch, all branches experienced lower service scores. However, branches using the new procedure declined by only **0.6 points** compared to **2.9 points** for branches using the old procedure. The new procedure provided a **2.3-point buffer** against this system-wide decline.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but investigate what caused the system-wide score drop after week 15.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Pre-switch average | 51.82 | 51.93 |
| Post-switch average | 51.25 | 49.08 |
| **Observed change** | **-0.57** | **-2.85** |

### What the Numbers Mean

1. **Service scores declined across the system after week 15.** This affected all branches, regardless of procedure. Something changed at that time (seasonal factor? staffing changes? budget cuts?) that warrants investigation.

2. **The new procedure mitigated the decline.** Switched branches lost less than 1 point; non-switched branches lost nearly 3 points. The 2.3-point difference is statistically significant (p = 0.004).

3. **The counterfactual benefit is real.** A switched branch would have scored ~2.3 points lower had it not adopted the new procedure. This is meaningful on a ~50-point scale.

4. **Pre-switch trends were parallel.** Both groups tracked together before week 15, supporting the validity of the comparison.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve rollout with conditions:**

1. **Proceed with system-wide adoption** — the procedure demonstrably protects service quality
2. **Investigate the week-15 inflection point** — understand what drove the system-wide decline
3. **Set realistic expectations** — the new procedure maintains scores; it does not elevate them

## **Methodology Note**

This analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** design comparing 90 switched branches against 60 non-switched branches over 17 weeks. The approach isolates the procedure's effect from system-wide factors affecting all branches. Pre-trend analysis confirms parallel trajectories before the switch (p = 0.90), supporting causal interpretation.
