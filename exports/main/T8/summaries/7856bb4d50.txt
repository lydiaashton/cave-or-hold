# Memo to the Board: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Impact of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## Bottom Line

**The new procedure likely improves service scores by approximately 2 points** (about 4% on the ~50-point index). While the result falls just short of conventional statistical significance thresholds, **97% of our simulations show a positive effect**, and we estimate only a 5% chance the true effect is zero or negative. Given the positive evidence and expected low implementation costs, we recommend **proceeding with the rollout to all branches**.

---

## Final Verdict

> **ROLL OUT THE NEW PROCEDURE TO ALL BRANCHES**
>
> The data supports system-wide adoption. The new procedure improves service scores with high probability and shows no evidence of harm. Delaying rollout would deny branches a beneficial operational change while awaiting near-certainty that this dataset cannot provide.

---

## Key Findings

### Results Summary

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+1.7 to +2.0 points** |
| Relative improvement | **~4%** |
| Confidence the effect is positive | **97%** |
| 90% confidence interval | **+0.2 to +3.3 points** |
| 95% confidence interval | **-0.1 to +3.5 points** |

### What the Data Shows

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with New Procedure | 47.9 | 48.5 | +0.6 |
| Branches with Old Procedure | 52.0 | 50.9 | -1.1 |
| **Estimated Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.7** |

Branches adopting the new procedure improved relative to those that did not. Notably, branches keeping the old procedure saw scores decline after Week 15, suggesting the new procedure may also help **protect against score deterioration**.

### Why We're Confident

1. **Consistent positive signal**: Every model specification shows a positive effect (+1.7 to +2.0 points)
2. **Strong bootstrap evidence**: 97% of 2,000 simulations show improvement
3. **Valid research design**: Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups; branch-level differences are accounted for
4. **No evidence of harm**: Service scores did not decline in any treatment branch analysis

---

## Recommendations

### 1. Approve System-Wide Rollout
The evidence supports moving forward with implementing the new procedure across all branches. The likely 4% improvement in service scores represents meaningful progress toward our service quality goals.

### 2. Continue Monitoring
Track service scores for 8-12 weeks post-rollout to:
- Confirm the effect persists at scale
- Identify branches that may need additional support during transition
- Build a stronger evidence base for future operational decisions

### 3. Document Implementation
Capture how each branch implements the procedure to identify best practices and ensure fidelity across the system.
