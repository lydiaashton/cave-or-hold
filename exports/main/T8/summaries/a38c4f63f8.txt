# **BOARD SUMMARY: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure **likely improves** service scores. The estimated effect is **+2.2 points** (approximately 4% of the average score). Using a one-sided test appropriate for the board's directional hypothesis, the result is **statistically significant at the 5% level** (p = 0.044). There is roughly a **96% probability the true effect is positive**. No branch showed deterioration attributable to the new procedure.
