## **Key Findings**

### Measured Impact

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 52.53 | 53.21 | +0.68 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 51.83 | 54.86 | +3.02 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.34** |

- **Statistical confidence:** 95% CI [1.75, 2.94]; p < 0.001
- **Effect size:** 0.23 standard deviations (small-to-moderate)
- **Durability:** Effect appeared immediately and remained stable across all 8 post-switch weeks

### Why the Evidence Is Reliable

1. **Strong identification:** 425 switched branches vs. 284 control branches, tracked over 17 weeks
2. **Parallel pre-trends:** Both groups followed similar trajectories before the switch
3. **Robust to controls:** Effect holds after accounting for staffing and visitor volume
4. **Clean break:** Sharp change at Week 15, not a gradual drift
