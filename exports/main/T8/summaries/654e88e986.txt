# **Library Branch Shelving Procedure: Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and rotation procedure is associated with a **modest improvement in service scores** (approximately +2.3 points, or about 5% above baseline). The statistical evidence is suggestive but not definitive (p ≈ 0.10). Most branches (70%) benefited, though 30% saw declines.

**Bottom line: The evidence supports proceeding with cooperative-wide rollout, accompanied by monitoring and targeted support.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. **Estimated Impact**
| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Service score improvement | +2.3 points |
| 95% confidence interval | -0.4 to +5.0 points |
| Statistical significance | Marginal (p ≈ 0.10) |
| Improvement relative to baseline | ~5% |

The confidence interval is mostly positive—the data are more consistent with a genuine improvement than with no effect or harm.

### 2. **What Happened to Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (20 branches) | 50.03 | 52.27 | **+2.25** |
| Non-switched branches (14 branches) | 49.11 | 49.06 | **-0.05** |

Branches that adopted the new procedure improved; those that kept the old procedure remained flat.

### 3. **Not All Branches Benefited Equally**

Among the 20 branches that switched:
- **14 branches (70%)** saw improvements
- **6 branches (30%)** saw declines
- **Range:** From -7.0 to +11.2 points

This variation suggests branch-specific factors influence how well the new procedure works.

### 4. **Trends Before the Switch Were Similar**

Analysis confirms that switched and non-switched branches had comparable service score trends before week 15, supporting the validity of the comparison.

### 5. **Results Hold After Accounting for Other Factors**

The estimated effect remains stable when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume, and when excluding the transition week (week 15).

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with cooperative-wide rollout.**

**Rationale:**

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Direction of effect** | Positive (+2.3 points) |
| **Practical significance** | Meaningful (~5% service improvement affects patron experience) |
| **Statistical evidence** | Suggestive (p ≈ 0.10); CI mostly positive |
| **Consistency** | 70% of branches improved; control group flat |
| **Risk of inaction** | Forgoing genuine service improvement for all patrons |
| **Risk of action** | Procedure may have zero effect; some branches may need support |

**Conditions for rollout:**

1. **Implement branch-level monitoring** for the first quarter to verify effects persist
2. **Provide enhanced training and support** to branches that initially struggle
3. **Document implementation practices** at high-performing branches for knowledge sharing
4. **Establish a review point** at 12 weeks to reassess if effects are weaker than expected
