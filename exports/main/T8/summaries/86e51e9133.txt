# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores (+1.9 points, p=0.002), but the effect is modest and not universal.** 

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but with conditions.** Implement with careful documentation and training, monitor outcomes closely, and conduct a cost-benefit analysis before full commitment.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Quality
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +1.9 points on service score index |
| 95% Confidence interval | 0.7 to 3.1 points |
| Relative improvement | ~4% (on base of ~47) |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.002 |

### 2. Strong Evidence of Causation
The analysis used a rigorous difference-in-differences design:

| Period | Control Branches | Treatment Branches | Gap |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|-----|
| **Before switch** (weeks 6-14) | 47.05 | 47.13 | 0.08 |
| **After switch** (weeks 15-22) | 48.17 | 50.04 | 1.87 |
| **Change** | +1.11 | +2.91 | **+1.80** |

Both groups started on parallel trajectories, then diverged after the procedure change—supporting a causal interpretation.

### 3. Effect Holds Across Specifications
The 1.9-point improvement persists after controlling for:
- Staffing levels (staffing index)
- Visitor volume (visits index)
- Branch-specific characteristics

### 4. Not All Branches Benefited Equally
- **79% of treatment branches** improved after switching
- **60% of control branches** improved during the same period
- **21% of treatment branches** saw no improvement or decline

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Statistical evidence** | ✅ Strong (p = 0.002; CI excludes zero) |
| **Effect size** | ⚠️ Modest (~4% improvement; lower bound 1.5%) |
| **Consistency** | ⚠️ Variable (21% of branches did not benefit) |
| **External validity** | ⚠️ Limited (only 8 weeks post-switch data) |
| **Cost-benefit** | ❓ Unknown (no cost data available) |

**Verdict: Cautious Approval**

The evidence supports adoption, but this is a **conditional yes** rather than an enthusiastic endorsement. The board should approve rollout while acknowledging:
- The average improvement is real but modest
- Roughly 1 in 5 branches may not benefit
- Implementation quality likely matters
- Costs have not been evaluated
