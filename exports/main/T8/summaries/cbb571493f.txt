# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a modest positive effect, but with important caveats.** Branches that adopted it saw service scores improve by **1.9 points** on average compared to branches that kept the old procedure. However, pre-existing differences between groups and the fact that 39% of switched branches saw no improvement suggest the effect is real but not uniform.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but phase it carefully and monitor closely.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Control Branches | Net Effect |
|--------|------------------|------------------|------------|
| Average score change | +1.07 points | −0.79 points | **+1.86 points** |
| Branches that improved | 61% | 39% | — |
| Statistical significance | — | — | p < 0.001 |
| Effect size | — | — | 0.19 standard deviations |

**What this means:** On average, the new procedure helps. But it's not a guarantee—about 4 in 10 switched branches saw no benefit.

**The benefit grows over time:** The gap between switched and control branches widened from +1.5 points in week 1 after the switch to +3.7 points by week 8, suggesting branches realize more value as they gain experience.

---

## **Important Caveats**

1. **Groups weren't perfectly comparable at baseline.** Switched branches were already trending differently before the intervention, which may inflate the estimated effect.

2. **Results vary by branch.** The middle 50% of switched branches saw changes ranging from -1.6 to +3.7 points. Some branches clearly benefit more than others.

3. **Effect is modest.** At 1.9 points (about 4% of the average score), this is a meaningful but not transformative improvement.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the procedure improve scores on average? | **Yes** |
| Is the effect statistically reliable? | **Yes** |
| Will every branch benefit? | **No** |
| Is system-wide rollout justified? | **Yes, with conditions** |

**Conditions for rollout:**
- Phase implementation over 60–90 days
- Track scores for each branch for 8+ weeks after switching
- Provide extra support to branches that don't show improvement within 4 weeks
- Document what high-performing switchers do differently
