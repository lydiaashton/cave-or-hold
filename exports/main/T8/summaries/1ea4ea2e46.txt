# **Memorandum to the Board**

## **Title: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The data show the new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2 points**—an unexpected but statistically significant finding. While we anticipated no service impact, branches using the new procedure outperformed control branches by 2.0 points (95% CI: 0.8 to 3.3; p=0.001) after the switch. This represents roughly a 4% improvement on the service score scale.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but implement in phases with continued monitoring to confirm the effect persists.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure change service scores? | **Yes** |
| Direction of effect? | **Positive** (scores increased) |
| Statistically significant? | **Yes** (p=0.001) |
| Practically meaningful? | **Yes** (~4% improvement) |
| Confident in the finding? | **Yes** (multiple validity checks passed) |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes** |

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. **Study Design**
- **150 branches**: 90 adopted the new procedure, 60 retained the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data**: 9 weeks before switch (weeks 6–14), 8 weeks after (weeks 15–22)
- **Method**: Difference-in-differences with branch-clustered standard errors

### 2. **Main Result**
| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 48.55 | 50.30 | +1.75 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 49.46 | 53.24 | +3.78 |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+2.03** |

- **Statistically significant** at the 1% level (p=0.001)
- **95% confidence interval**: 0.82 to 3.25 points (does not include zero)

### 3. **Validity Checks Passed**
- **Parallel pre-trends**: Treatment and control gaps were stable before the switch (average 0.9 points, no trend)
- **Placebo test**: No effect when testing a fake switch date during the pre-period (p=0.21)
- **Consistent post-effect**: All 8 post-switch weeks show elevated gaps (range: 2.2 to 3.7 points)
- **Robust to controls**: Effect remains significant when adjusting for staffing and visitor volume

### 4. **Why This Matters**
- We expected **no service impact** from this operational change
- The data show a **clear positive effect** instead
- Possible explanations: improved staff capacity for patron services, better item findability, or indirect workflow benefits

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve phased rollout** to remaining 60 branches (e.g., 15–20 branches per month)

2. **Continue monitoring** service scores during rollout to confirm effect persists at scale

3. **Investigate the mechanism** through staff surveys or operational audits to understand *why* scores improved

4. **Document learnings** for future operational changes—assumptions about "neutral" impacts should be tested

5. **Reassess after 12 weeks** of full implementation to confirm sustained benefit before declaring success

---

## **Caveats**

- Observational study (not randomized); unmeasured confounders cannot be fully ruled out
- Only 8 weeks of post-period data; longer-term effects remain unknown
- Effect size, while significant, is modest (~4% improvement)
