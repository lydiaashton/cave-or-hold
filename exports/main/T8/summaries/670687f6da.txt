# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a statistically significant but modest improvement in service scores.** Branches that switched saw scores increase by approximately **1.8 points** (3.6%) relative to branches that didn't switch. However, branches that adopted the new procedure already had slightly higher baseline scores, which introduces some uncertainty about whether the full effect would replicate across all branches. **We recommend a phased rollout with careful monitoring**, rather than an immediate cooperative-wide mandate.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.03 | 51.47 | +1.44 |
| Non-switched branches | 48.77 | 48.43 | −0.34 |
| **Estimated effect** | | | **+1.78** |

- **Statistical significance**: p = 0.003 (the effect is unlikely to be due to chance)
- **95% confidence interval**: 0.60 to 2.97 points
- **Effect size**: Small (Cohen's d = 0.17); smaller than typical week-to-week variation

### **Important Caveats**

| Concern | What the data shows | Implication |
|---------|---------------------|-------------|
| **Selection bias** | Switched branches had 1.27 points higher scores at baseline | Effect may be overstated if better branches self-selected into the new procedure |
| **Modest magnitude** | 1.78 points on a ~50 point scale; CI lower bound is only 0.60 | Benefits are real but not transformative |
| **External validity** | 60 branches chose not to switch; we don't know why | Effect may differ for branches that declined to participate |

### **Why We Have Confidence (Despite Caveats)**
- Parallel trends held before the switch (both groups moved similarly week-to-week)
- Results are robust after controlling for staffing and visitor volume
- The control group slightly declined while treatment group improved, suggesting the effect isn't due to system-wide factors

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with caution, not full commitment.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recommendation** | ✅ **Approve limited expansion** (10–15 additional branches) |
| **Confidence** | ⚠️ **Moderate** — effect is real but may be smaller for non-volunteer branches |
| **Expected benefit** | 📈 **0.6 to 1.8 points** per branch (conservative estimate) |
| **Risk** | 🟡 **Low** — procedure change appears harmless; main cost is staff time |
| **Full rollout decision** | ⏸️ **Defer** until pilot results confirm effect replicates |

**Rationale:** The data supports a genuine positive effect, but the modest size and selection bias concerns argue against an immediate cooperative-wide mandate. A pilot expansion tests whether the benefit extends to branches that didn't initially volunteer, providing stronger evidence before committing all 150 branches.
