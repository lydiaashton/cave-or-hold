# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The data suggest the new procedure may improve service scores by approximately **2 points (4%)**, but this estimate is **not statistically significant** (p=0.10). The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.4 to +4.6 points, meaning we cannot confidently rule out no effect.

There is **suggestive evidence** that benefits grow over time (2.8 points in weeks 19-22, p=0.035).

**No evidence of harm** was detected—service scores did not decline in any switched branch.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Estimated Effect — Uncertain

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Point estimate | +2.1 points |
| Standard error | ±1.28 points |
| P-value | 0.102 |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.4 to +4.6 points |

**Interpretation:** The best estimate is a 2-point improvement, but statistical uncertainty means the true effect could be zero or modestly positive.

### 2. Effect May Grow Over Time

| Period | Effect | P-value |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Weeks 15-18 | +1.2 points | 0.43 |
| Weeks 19-22 | +2.8 points | 0.035 |

This pattern suggests a learning curve—branches may need 4-6 weeks to realize full benefits.

### 3. Analysis Is Valid

- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups
- Results hold after controlling for staffing and visitor volume
- 34 branches, 17 weeks of data (578 observations)

### 4. Risk Assessment

| Scenario | Implication |
|----------|-------------|
| True effect is positive (~2-3 points) | Rollout delivers meaningful service improvement |
| True effect is near zero | Minimal impact; some staff time invested |
| True effect is negative | Unlikely; no negative signals in the data |

## **Final Verdict**

**PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT — WITH CONDITIONS**

The board should **approve cooperative-wide implementation** of the new procedure, subject to the following conditions:

| Condition | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Allow 6 weeks per branch** before evaluating performance | Benefits appear to accumulate with experience |
| **Monitor service scores weekly** for first 12 weeks of rollout | Early detection if effects differ from pilot |
| **Set success threshold at +1.0 point** after 6 weeks | Conservative benchmark; still represents 2% improvement |
| **Document implementation practices** from high-performing branches | Accelerates learning curve across cooperative |

**Why proceed despite uncertainty?**

1. **Upside outweighs downside** — Potential 4% service gain vs. modest implementation costs
2. **No harm detected** — Zero evidence of negative effects on service scores
3. **Later-period results are significant** — Suggests real effect that strengthens over time
4. **Reversible decision** — Procedure can be adjusted or reverted if monitoring shows no benefit
