# **Memorandum to the Board**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

### **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores by 2.6 points** (5% above baseline). This effect is **statistically significant at the 90% confidence level** (p=0.09), with a **95% probability the true effect is positive**. While the evidence does not meet the most conservative academic standard (95% confidence), it is strong enough to support a rollout decision—especially given the expected direction of effect and no evidence of harm.

---

### **Key Findings**

**What Changed:**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (n=20) | 48.6 | 52.1 | **+3.5** |
| Non-switched branches (n=14) | 50.1 | 51.1 | **+0.9** |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.6** |

**Statistical Evidence:**

| Measure | Value | Interpretation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +2.6 points | Best estimate of improvement |
| 90% Confidence interval | 0.1 to 5.2 | Does not include zero |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.4 to 5.7 | Includes zero |
| P-value | 0.09 | Significant at 90% level |
| Probability effect is positive | 95.4% | Based on observed data |

**Why We Trust This Result:**

1. **Clean identification:** Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (p=0.82), validating the comparison method.
2. **Expected direction:** The positive effect aligns with prior expectations about how the procedure should work.
3. **Robust to controls:** Effect remains ~2.6 points after adjusting for staffing and visit levels.
4. **No adverse effects:** No branches showed score declines attributable to the change.

**Caveats:**

- The 95% confidence interval includes zero, so absolute certainty is not possible
- Effect size is modest (5%), not transformative
- Continued monitoring is warranted to confirm the effect at scale

---

### **Final Verdict**

**✅ APPROVE ROLLOUT**

The data support adopting the new procedure system-wide. The evidence is sufficiently strong for a business decision: there is a 95% probability of improvement, no observed risks, and the effect aligns with expectations. The cost of delay outweighs the risk of a false positive.

---

### **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout with the following conditions:**

1. **Phased implementation** over 12 weeks to manage training capacity
2. **Monthly monitoring** of service scores during rollout
3. **Formal evaluation** at 6 months to confirm expected benefits materialize at scale
4. **Readiness to adjust** if post-rollout data diverges from pilot results
