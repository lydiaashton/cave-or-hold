# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a modest but statistically significant improvement in service scores on average (+2.1 points, ~4%).** However, results varied considerably: while 76% of switched branches improved, **24% actually saw scores decline**. We recommend a **phased rollout** with close monitoring and support for struggling branches, rather than an immediate system-wide mandate.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Average Improvement, But Variable Results**

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6-14) | After Switch (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches (90 branches) | 50.11 | 52.35 | **+2.23** |
| Old Procedure Branches (60 branches) | 50.61 | 50.73 | +0.12 |

**Net effect: +2.1 points** (statistically significant, p < 0.01)

### **2. Not All Branches Benefited**

| Outcome | % of Switched Branches |
|---------|----------------------|
| Score decreased | **24%** |
| Score increased < 1 point | 34% |
| Score increased > 5 points | 22% |

### **3. Statistical Confidence**

- 95% confidence interval: **+0.9 to +3.4 points**
- Effect size: **0.24 standard deviations** (small-to-moderate)
- Results hold after controlling for staffing and visitor volume

### **4. Study Limitations**

- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** – long-term effects unknown
- **No cost or implementation burden data** available
- Quasi-experimental design (not randomized), though pre-trends were parallel

---

## **Final Verdict**

**PROCEED WITH CAUTION**

The evidence supports expanding the new procedure, but not as an unconditional mandate. The average benefit is real and meaningful for most branches, yet the one-in-four branches that saw declines signals that **context matters**. A phased rollout with monitoring protects the cooperative from scaling a procedure that may not suit all branches equally, while still capturing value for the majority.

**Decision:** Approve expansion to additional branches, contingent on:
- Pilot phase with 15-20 volunteer branches
- 12-week monitoring period with predefined success metrics
- Commitment to revisit the decision if >20% of pilot branches show score declines

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Launch a pilot phase** with 15-20 volunteer branches within the next quarter.

2. **Establish monitoring protocols**:
   - Weekly service score tracking
   - Branch feedback surveys at weeks 4, 8, and 12
   - Staff time/cost tracking during transition

3. **Define success criteria** before pilot begins:
   - Target: ≥70% of pilot branches show improvement
   - Stop-loss: ≤30% of pilot branches show declines

4. **Reconvene the board** after the 12-week pilot to decide on full system-wide rollout.

5. **Investigate branch characteristics** that predict success (size, staffing, layout, urban/rural) to inform targeted implementation.
