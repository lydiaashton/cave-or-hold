# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure produces a real, measurable improvement in service scores.** We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout**, accompanied by monitoring and support for branches that may struggle with the transition.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Yes** — by approximately 2 points (4%) |
| Is the evidence reliable? | **Yes** — statistically significant (p < 0.001) with validated methodology |
| Did all branches benefit? | **No** — 73% improved, 27% saw little or no benefit |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Yes** — the aggregate benefit outweighs the risk of uneven effects |

**Rationale:** The improvement is statistically robust, the majority of branches benefited, and there is no evidence of harm to the cooperative overall. The fact that some branches did not improve suggests we should invest in understanding what differentiates successful adopters—but this argues for supported rollout, not abandonment.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Design**
- **709 branches** participated (425 switched to new procedure; 284 kept old procedure)
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22); switch occurred at week 15
- Analysis controlled for branch-specific characteristics and weekly trends

### **Service Score Impact**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| Before switch (avg) | 51.5 | 51.0 |
| After switch (avg) | 53.7 | 51.2 |
| **Change** | **+2.3** | **+0.3** |

- **Net improvement: +2.0 points** (95% CI: 1.6 to 2.3)
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001

### **Branch-Level Results**
- **73%** of switched branches showed improvement vs. **53%** of control branches
- Effect was consistent across the study period
- No systematic relationship between baseline performance and improvement

### **Validation**
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (supports causal interpretation)
- Staffing and visit patterns did not differ between groups

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** with a 12-week implementation timeline

2. **Provide implementation support:**
   - Training materials based on high-performing switched branches
   - Dedicated support contact for transitioning branches

3. **Monitor outcomes:**
   - Track service scores for 12 weeks post-implementation at each branch
   - Flag branches showing no improvement after 6 weeks for targeted assistance

4. **Evaluate at 6 months:**
   - Assess whether improvements persist
   - Document lessons learned for future procedural changes

---

## **TECHNICAL NOTES**

- Analysis used difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects
- Baseline service score: ~51 points (cooperative average)
- Improvement represents ~4% gain in service quality
