# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests the new procedure improved service scores, though the effect is modest.** Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores approximately **2 points higher** (about 4% improvement) than comparable branches using the old procedure. The result is statistically significant but comes with uncertainty—the true effect could reasonably be smaller. We recommend **proceeding with rollout while monitoring outcomes closely.**

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Does the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Yes** — evidence supports a positive effect |
| **Is the effect statistically significant?** | **Yes** — p = 0.02 |
| **Is the effect large?** | **Modest** — approximately 4% improvement |
| **Should we roll out system-wide?** | **Yes, with monitoring** |

**Rationale:** The procedure change shows a positive, statistically significant effect without requiring additional resources or causing negative side effects. While the effect size is modest and uncertain, the low risk and potential for meaningful service improvement justify system-wide adoption.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated Effect** | +2.05 points on service score |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | 0.3 to 3.8 points |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.02 (significant at 0.05 level) |
| **Effect Size** | ~4% improvement (~0.2 standard deviations) |
| **Branches Studied** | 34 total (20 switched, 14 control) |
| **Post-Switch Data** | 8 weeks |

### What We Compared
- **Treatment group:** 20 branches that adopted the new procedure in Week 15
- **Control group:** 14 branches that continued with the old procedure
- **Method:** Difference-in-differences analysis with branch fixed effects, controlling for staffing and visitor volume

### Strengths of the Evidence
✓ Statistically significant at conventional levels  
✓ Pre-switch trends were similar between groups  
✓ Effect not explained by staffing or visit changes  
✓ Improvement visible immediately after switch  

### Limitations to Consider
⚠️ Confidence interval includes modest effects (as low as 0.3 points)  
⚠️ Only 8 weeks of post-switch data  
⚠️ Week-to-week effects varied (6 of 8 weeks showed improvement)  
⚠️ Effect size is modest relative to typical score variation
