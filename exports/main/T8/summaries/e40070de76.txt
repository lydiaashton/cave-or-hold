## **Why This Matters**

The analysis compared 20 branches that adopted the new procedure against 14 branches that continued the old approach, controlling for branch-specific characteristics, week-to-week variation, staffing levels, and visit volumes. The treatment branches showed systematically greater improvement after implementation.

2. **Continue monitoring** service scores for 8-12 weeks post-rollout to confirm the effect stabilizes

4. **Report back to the board** in Q1 2027 with updated results
