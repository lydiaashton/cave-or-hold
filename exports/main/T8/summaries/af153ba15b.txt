# **Board Memorandum (Final)**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new procedure showed **higher service scores** than those that kept the old procedure—an estimated **2.4 point improvement** (about 5%). However, with the current data, **we cannot conclude with statistical confidence that the new procedure caused this improvement.** The result is promising but inconclusive. We recommend **continuing the pilot and collecting more data** before committing to a system-wide rollout.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

> **Do not approve system-wide rollout at this time.**
>
> **Reason:** The evidence is suggestive but not statistically conclusive. The observed improvement could reasonably be due to chance.
>
> **Action:** Extend the pilot for 8-12 additional weeks, then re-evaluate.

---

## **Why the Uncertainty?**

| What the data shows | What it means |
|---------------------|---------------|
| Point estimate: +2.4 points | Switched branches improved relative to non-switched |
| 95% Confidence interval: -0.55 to +5.40 | The true effect could be slightly negative, zero, or meaningfully positive |
| p-value: 0.10 | There's a 10% chance of seeing this result even if the new procedure has no real effect |
| **Conventional threshold for confidence** | **p < 0.05** (our result does not meet this) |

In plain terms: **The data is consistent with improvement, but also consistent with no effect.** We need more observations to distinguish between these possibilities.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Performance**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 48.3 | 47.6 | -0.7 |
| Branches using new procedure | 47.8 | 49.5 | +1.7 |
| **Net difference** | | | **+2.4** |

### **What Supports a Positive Effect**

✓ Switched branches improved; non-switched branches declined slightly  
✓ Pre-switch trends were similar between groups (validates comparison)  
✓ Result holds when controlling for staffing and visitor volume  
✓ No evidence of harm to service quality

### **What Limits Our Confidence**

✗ Only 8 weeks of post-switch data  
✗ 34 branches total (20 switched, 14 did not) — modest sample size  
✗ Confidence interval includes zero  
✗ p-value (0.10) exceeds conventional threshold (0.05)

---

## **Recommendation**

**Do not proceed with system-wide rollout at this time.** Instead:

1. **Extend the pilot** — Continue collecting data for at least 8-12 more weeks
2. **Consider expanding the treatment group** — If operationally feasible, have some additional branches switch to increase statistical power
3. **Re-evaluate with more data** — Schedule a follow-up analysis in Q2 2026
4. **Document operational feedback** — Gather qualitative input from staff on the new procedure's practical impacts

---

## **Decision Framework**

| If the board prioritizes... | Recommended action |
|----------------------------|-------------------|
| **Statistical certainty** | Wait for more data before any expansion |
| **Early adoption / innovation** | Allow voluntary opt-in for additional branches while monitoring |
| **Risk minimization** | Maintain current pilot; no expansion until results are clearer |

Given that the confidence interval includes the possibility of no effect (or slight negative effect), **caution is warranted** before committing cooperative-wide resources to the new procedure.
