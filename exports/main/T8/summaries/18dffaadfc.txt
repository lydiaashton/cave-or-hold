# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores** by approximately 2 points (4%). Statistical evidence supports a positive effect (95.5% probability), though uncertainty remains about the exact magnitude. We recommend **proceeding with rollout**, paired with continued monitoring to confirm benefits and track implementation costs.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **ROLL OUT THE NEW PROCEDURE TO ALL BRANCHES**

**Confidence Level:** Moderate-to-High

**Rationale:**
- Evidence indicates a positive effect with 95.5% probability
- 90% confidence interval excludes zero (0.07 to 4.30 points)
- Potential benefit (improved service across 34 branches) outweighs implementation risk
- No evidence of negative effects or harmful pre-trends

**Conditions:**
1. Monitor service scores for 6–12 months post-rollout
2. Track implementation costs for cost-benefit verification
3. Schedule formal review in Q3 2026 to confirm expected benefits materialize

---

## **Key Findings**

### Main Results

| Measure | Estimate |
|---------|----------|
| Effect on service score | **+2.18 points** |
| 90% Confidence Interval | **0.07 to 4.30 points** ✓ |
| Probability effect is positive | **95.5%** |
| Relative improvement | **~4.4%** |

### What This Means

- At 90% confidence, the effect is **positive** (interval excludes zero)
- The true effect is most likely between **0 and 4 points**
- There is a 95.5% probability the procedure improves scores

### Supporting Evidence

| Indicator | Finding |
|-----------|---------|
| **Branch outcomes** | 70% of switched branches improved vs. 50% of controls |
| **Pre-switch trends** | No differential trends detected (p = 0.98) ✓ |
| **Post-switch performance** | Treatment group ahead in 6 of 8 weeks |
