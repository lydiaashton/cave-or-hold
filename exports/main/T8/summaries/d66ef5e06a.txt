# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure produced a measurable improvement in service scores.** Despite initial expectations of no effect, branches that adopted the new procedure scored **1.96 points higher** (4.1% improvement) than comparable branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically significant (p = 0.003) and the 95% confidence interval [0.67, 3.25] excludes zero.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout.

### Why We Trust This Result
1. **Parallel trends validated** – Treatment and control branches moved together before the switch (pre-trend test p = 0.076)
2. **Consistent post-period effect** – Positive effect in 7 of 8 post-switch weeks
3. **Robust to controls** – Effect persists after accounting for staffing and visit volumes
4. **Proper inference** – Standard errors clustered at branch level to account for within-branch correlation
