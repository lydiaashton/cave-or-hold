# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure appears to have improved service scores modestly.** Branches using the new procedure scored approximately **2 points higher** than expected—a **4% improvement** on the service index. This was unexpected, as the procedure change was not designed to affect service quality.

The statistical evidence is solid, but the effect is modest. We recommend **proceeding with the rollout** while monitoring closely to confirm the effect persists.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure change service scores? | **Yes** |
| Is the effect statistically reliable? | **Yes** (p = 0.002) |
| Is the effect practically meaningful? | **Modestly** (~4% improvement) |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Yes** |

**Verdict: APPROVE system-wide rollout.**

The evidence supports a real, positive effect on service scores. While modest in magnitude, the improvement comes at no apparent cost to service quality and may help buffer branches against system-wide performance declines. The effect strengthened over time, suggesting branches adapt and improve with experience.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Treatment Effect Estimate
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated impact | +1.96 service score points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 0.78 to 3.13 points |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.002 |
| Relative improvement | ~4% above baseline |

### 2. What the Data Shows
The new procedure **protected branches from a system-wide decline**:

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.14 | 46.92 | **−2.22** |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 49.86 | 49.56 | **−0.31** |

### 3. Effect Built Over Time
| Period | Estimated Effect | Significance |
|--------|------------------|--------------|
| Weeks 15-17 (early) | +1.5 points | Marginal (p≈0.08) |
| Weeks 18-22 (later) | +2.3 points | Significant (p<0.05) |

This pattern suggests the benefit may have taken a few weeks to materialize as staff adapted to the new procedure.

### 4. Analysis Validity Checks
- ✓ **Parallel trends confirmed**: No pre-existing difference in trends (p=0.41)
- ✓ **Placebo test passed**: No effect when testing a fake switch date
- ✓ **Robust to controls**: Effect holds with staffing and visits controls
- ✓ **Consistent direction**: All 8 post-switch weeks showed positive effects

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Proceed with rollout** — the evidence supports a real, positive effect

2. **Monitor during implementation** — track whether the effect replicates as more branches adopt the procedure

3. **Investigate the system-wide decline** — control branches dropped 2.2 points after week 15; understanding this could reveal additional opportunities

4. **Allow adaptation time** — the effect strengthened after 3-4 weeks; new branches may need similar time to realize full benefits

5. **Revisit in 6 months** — confirm the effect persists and assess whether it justifies any additional operational costs
