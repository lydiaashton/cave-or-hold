# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect of approximately 2.6 points** on service scores, but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive** at conventional confidence levels. The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.4 to +5.7 points, meaning we cannot rule out no effect. However, there is roughly a **95% probability the true effect is positive**, and the result would be significant if we had pre-specified a directional hypothesis (which aligns with the board's expectation of improvement).

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **DO NOT APPROVE FULL ROLLOUT AT THIS TIME.**
>
> **Approve a phased expansion instead.** Extend the new procedure to 5-10 additional branches and collect 8-12 more weeks of data. Return to the board in Q2 with updated analysis. If the 95% confidence interval then excludes zero, proceed with cooperative-wide adoption.

**Rationale:** The potential benefit (~5% service improvement) is meaningful, but the current evidence does not meet the standard of statistical certainty appropriate for a cooperative-wide commitment. A phased approach balances opportunity with prudence.
