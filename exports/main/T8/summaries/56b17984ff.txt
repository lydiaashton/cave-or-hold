# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is inconclusive.** While branches that switched showed higher service scores after the change, they were already improving relative to control branches *before* the switch occurred. This pre-existing trend makes it impossible to determine whether the procedure itself caused the improvement. **We cannot recommend system-wide rollout based on this evidence alone.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Observed difference** | +2.07 points (switched vs. control) |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Pre-switch trend test** | p = 0.010 (significant divergence) |
| **Gap before switch (Week 14)** | +1.92 points |
| **Conservative effect estimate** | +0.1 to +2.1 points |

### The Core Problem

The gap between switched and control branches was **already widening before the procedure changed**:

| Week | Gap (Switched − Control) |
|------|--------------------------|
| 6 | −0.15 |
| 10 | −0.01 |
| 14 | +1.92 |
| 15 (switch) | +1.61 |
| 22 | +3.06 |

This pattern suggests that **branches predisposed to improve were the ones that switched**, not that the procedure caused improvement.

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **DO NOT APPROVE SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**

**Reason:** The study design does not support a causal conclusion. The observed +2 point advantage likely reflects **selection bias** (which branches chose to switch) rather than **treatment effect** (the procedure itself).

**Risk of rollout:** If the procedure has no true effect, the cooperative would incur implementation costs and staff disruption for no benefit.

---

## **Recommended Path Forward**

| Step | Action | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Pause rollout** | Avoid committing resources on uncertain evidence |
| 2 | **Randomized trial** | Assign 50–100 branches to switch by random selection |
| 3 | **Monitor 12+ weeks** | Distinguish true effects from ongoing trends |
| 4 | **Return to Board** | Decide on rollout with valid causal evidence |

**Timeline:** Complete randomized trial and report back by Q2 2027.

---

## **What Would Change Our Verdict?**

We would recommend rollout if a properly randomized study shows:
- Effect size remains +1.5 points or higher
- Pre-trend test shows no significant divergence (p > 0.10)
- Effect persists 12+ weeks post-switch

---

**Bottom line:** The new procedure *may* help, but this study cannot prove it. The Board should not approve cooperative-wide adoption without stronger evidence.
