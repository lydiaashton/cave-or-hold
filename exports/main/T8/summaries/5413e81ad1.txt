# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved scores approximately **2.1 points higher** than comparable branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant and consistent across analytical approaches. **We recommend approving the system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Delivered Measurable Gains

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 49.7 | 50.4 | +0.7 |
| Branches with old procedure | 49.4 | 48.0 | −1.5 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.1** |

### The Results Are Statistically Solid

- **Confidence level:** 99.9% (p < 0.001)
- **Precision:** We estimate the true effect lies between **1.6 and 2.7 points**
- **Robustness:** Findings hold when accounting for staffing levels, visit volumes, and branch-specific factors

### Important Context

During the study period, branches using the old procedure experienced declining service scores. Branches using the new procedure not only avoided this decline but improved—suggesting the new procedure provides both improvement and protection against system-wide pressures affecting service quality.

### Limitations to Note

- Treatment branches showed a slight upward trend relative to control branches before the switch, suggesting the true effect may be toward the lower end of our confidence interval
- The effect size is modest (approximately 4% of baseline score)
- Continued monitoring is warranted to confirm effects persist at full scale
