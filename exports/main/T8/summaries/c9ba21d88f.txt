# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by 2 points (4%). The evidence is statistically robust. We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Evidence |
|---------|----------|
| **Statistically significant improvement** | +2.0 points, p < 0.001, 95% CI [1.3 to 2.8] |
| **Effect is modest but meaningful** | 4% of baseline; 0.2 standard deviations |
| **Effect is stable** | Consistent across all 8 post-switch weeks |
| **Causal interpretation is credible** | Pre-switch trends were parallel; reversal occurred at switch |

### The Numbers

| Metric | Treatment Branches (Switched) | Control Branches (Not Switched) |
|--------|-------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Pre-switch average | 50.01 | 51.02 |
| Post-switch average | 52.29 | 51.25 |
| **Change** | **+2.28** | **+0.23** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +2.0 points**

---

## **What We Don't Know**

| Question | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Does the new procedure require more staff time? | Not measured |
| Are there training or implementation costs? | Not measured |
| Will the effect persist beyond 8 weeks? | Unknown |

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Roll out the new procedure to all branches.**

**Reasons:**

1. **The effect is real.** The 2-point improvement is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and the research design supports a causal interpretation. The board expected no difference; the data shows otherwise.

2. **The effect is stable.** Benefits appeared immediately and persisted across all 8 post-switch weeks with no sign of fade-out.

3. **Procedural changes typically have low ongoing costs.** Stock rotation and shelving procedures are workflow changes, not resource-intensive initiatives. Any one-time training cost is likely outweighed by sustained service improvement.

4. **A 4% service improvement matters.** For a public library system, consistent gains in service quality across 150 branches represents meaningful value to patrons.

5. **Risk is low.** If the rollout reveals unexpected costs or implementation challenges, the procedure can be adjusted. The 90 early-adopter branches provide a base of experience to draw from.
