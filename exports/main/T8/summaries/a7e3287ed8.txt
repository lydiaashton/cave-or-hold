# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with the rollout.** The new rotation and shelving procedure delivers a clear, measurable improvement in service quality. Branches using the new procedure achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than those using the old procedure—a statistically significant gain that grows over time.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.81 points |
| **Confidence range** | 1.26 to 2.36 points |
| **Relative improvement** | 3.5% above baseline |
| **Statistical confidence** | Greater than 99.9% |

### **What the Data Shows**

- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure in week 15; **284 branches** continued with the old procedure
- The improvement is **real and growing**: effects started at +0.75 points in the first week and reached +1.89 points by week 22
- Branches using the old procedure showed no comparable improvement, confirming the gain comes from the new procedure itself

### **Why We Trust These Results**

The analysis compared trends before and after the switch for both groups. In the weeks leading up to the change, both groups followed similar patterns—ruling out pre-existing differences as an explanation. This gives us strong confidence that the new procedure caused the improvement.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**The new procedure works. Roll it out.**

The data provides strong, statistically rigorous evidence that the new rotation and shelving procedure improves service scores. The effect is:

- **Real** — not due to chance (p < 0.001)
- **Meaningful** — a 3.5% improvement on a key performance metric
- **Durable** — effects grow over time rather than fading
- **Trustworthy** — validated against pre-existing trends

There is no analytical reason to delay. The cooperative should move forward with confidence.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve immediate system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches.
