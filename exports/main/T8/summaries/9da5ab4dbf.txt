# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a modest but statistically significant improvement in service scores.** Branches that switched saw scores increase by an average of **2.1 points** (approximately 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. However, the effect is not universal—14% of switched branches saw no improvement or declines. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, but use a phased approach with continued monitoring.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Treatment Effect**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated impact | +2.12 service score points |
| 95% Confidence interval | 0.9 to 3.3 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |
| Effect size | 0.21 standard deviations (modest) |

### **2. Not All Branches Benefited Equally**
- **86% of switched branches** improved (77 of 90)
- **14% saw no improvement or decline** (13 of 90)
- This suggests branch-specific factors may influence success

### **3. Study Limitations**
- Only 8 weeks of post-implementation data
- Some pre-existing volatility in group differences
- Unknown whether effects persist long-term

---

## **Final Verdict**

**The evidence supports adopting the new procedure, with caveats.**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the procedure change service scores? | **Yes** — the null hypothesis of "no difference" is rejected (p < 0.001) |
| Is the improvement meaningful? | **Modestly** — 4% average gain, but not transformative |
| Will every branch benefit? | **No** — roughly 1 in 7 branches saw no improvement |
| Should we roll out system-wide? | **Yes, but phased** — proceed with monitoring and support |

**Bottom line verdict:** The new procedure delivers real, measurable improvement at the cooperative level. The board should approve rollout, but implement it in phases with ongoing evaluation to identify and support branches that don't benefit.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve phased rollout** starting with 20-30 branches over the next quarter.

2. **Establish monitoring dashboard** to track service scores weekly during rollout.

3. **Identify support needs** for branches that don't show improvement within 4-6 weeks.

4. **Investigate success factors** by comparing high-performing vs. low-performing switched branches.

5. **Reconvene in 90 days** to review rollout data before deciding on full system-wide implementation.

---

*This recommendation balances the positive evidence with appropriate caution given the modest effect size and limited evaluation window.*
