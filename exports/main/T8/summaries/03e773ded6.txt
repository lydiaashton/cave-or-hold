# **Board Summary: New Shelving & Rotation Procedure Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with caution.** The data shows an unexpected **2.4-point improvement** in service scores at branches that adopted the new procedure. However, since this outcome was not anticipated, we recommend a **measured rollout with close monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. An Unexpected Positive Effect

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 49.96 | 51.40 | +1.44 |
| Branches that did not switch | 51.28 | 50.26 | −1.01 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.45** |

This result is **statistically significant** (p < 0.001), but it contradicts the expectation that the procedural change would have no impact on service scores.

### 2. Effect Size Context

- **2.45 points** on a ~50-point scale (approximately 5%)
- Represents about **0.27 standard deviations** — a modest but meaningful effect
- Effect was **consistent across most post-switch weeks**, though Week 22 showed no advantage

### 3. Why Caution Is Warranted

| Consideration | Implication |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Unexpected finding** | May indicate unmeasured factors; warrants verification |
| **Modest effect size** | Benefits are real but not transformative |
| **Only 8 weeks of post-data** | Unclear if effect persists long-term |
| **No identified mechanism** | We don't yet understand *why* scores improved |

### 4. Study Strengths

- **150 branches** over 17 weeks provides solid statistical power
- **Parallel pre-trends** confirm valid comparison between groups
- **No negative effects** detected on staffing or visitor patterns

---

## **Recommendation**

**Phase 1 (Next 8–12 weeks):** Expand to 20–30 additional branches with enhanced monitoring

**Phase 2:** Evaluate results; if effect persists, proceed with full rollout

**Track specifically:**
- Whether the 2.4-point gain holds at scale
- Any operational costs or disruptions not captured in this analysis
- Staff feedback on the new procedure

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Discuss implementation costs** with operations team before committing to full rollout

2. **Interview early-adopter branches** to understand *why* service scores may have improved

3. **Plan phased expansion** with predefined success metrics

4. **Report back to the board** in one quarter with Phase 1 results

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Approve a phased rollout, not immediate system-wide implementation.**

The statistical evidence is strong enough to justify expanding the procedure, but the unexpected nature of the finding and limited post-switch data warrant verification before full commitment. This approach captures potential benefits while protecting against the risk that the observed effect proves temporary or context-specific.

---

*Analysis based on 2,550 weekly observations from 150 branches. The unexpected nature of this finding suggests verification before full commitment.*
