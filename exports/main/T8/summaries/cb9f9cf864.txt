# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is suggestive but not conclusive.** The raw analysis shows a 1.9-point improvement, but branches that adopted the new procedure were already improving faster than other branches *before* the switch occurred. After accounting for this pre-existing trend, the true effect is uncertain—likely between **0.5 and 1.0 points**.

**Recommendation: Proceed with a phased rollout and continued monitoring.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Shows**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Raw estimated effect | **+1.89 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | [1.34, 2.43] |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 |

### **Critical Limitation: Pre-Existing Trends**

Branches that switched to the new procedure were **already improving faster** than control branches before the switch occurred:

| | |
|---|---|
| Pre-treatment trend difference | **+0.24 points/week** |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.0045 |
| Gap at week 6 | -0.63 (treatment behind) |
| Gap at week 14 | +0.83 (treatment ahead) |

This violates a key assumption of the analysis method. The pre-existing trend alone could explain approximately **1.8 points** of the observed 1.89-point effect.

### **Adjusted Estimate**

When comparing actual post-switch performance to what the pre-trend would predict:

| | |
|---|---|
| Average actual vs. expected | **+0.44 points** |
| Early post-switch effect (weeks 15-18) | ~0.75 points |
| Late post-switch effect (weeks 19-22) | ~2.7 points |

The effect appears to grow over time, which could indicate benefits accumulating with experience—or continued pre-existing trends.

---

## **Why This Matters**

Branches were **not randomly assigned** to switch procedures. Those that chose to adopt the new method may differ systematically:
- More motivated management
- Better staffing or resources
- Greater capacity for operational change

These factors could drive both the decision to switch *and* the improving scores—independent of the procedure itself.

---

## **Next Steps**

### **Recommended Approach: Phased Rollout with Evaluation**

1. **Expand to additional branches** (not all at once)
2. **Randomly assign** which branches switch next (if feasible)
3. **Track service scores for 8-12 weeks** after each phase
4. **Compare early vs. late adopters** to isolate true treatment effect

### **Alternative: Proceed with Monitoring**

If the board prefers to move forward:

1. **Approve system-wide rollout**
2. **Commit to 6-month evaluation** post-implementation
3. **Set success threshold** (e.g., maintain 1+ point improvement)
4. **Plan to revisit** if effects don't materialize

### **Additional Analysis**

5. **Survey branch managers** on why they did/didn't switch
6. **Analyze branch characteristics** (size, traffic, staffing) for heterogeneity
7. **Track operational costs** of the new procedure

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve scores?** | Probably yes, but less than it appears |
| **How large is the true effect?** | Likely **0.5 to 1.0 points** (not 1.9) |
| **Is rollout justified?** | **Yes, with caveats** |
| **What should the board expect?** | Modest improvement, not transformation |

### **Rationale**

1. **The effect is likely real, just smaller.** Even after accounting for pre-trends, the treatment branches outperformed expectations by ~0.4-0.7 points in early post-switch weeks.

2. **The procedure is low-risk.** Changing shelving and stock rotation procedures is operationally simple and reversible if needed.

3. **Late-post effects are encouraging.** Weeks 19-22 show larger gaps, which could indicate genuine learning effects.

4. **But expectations must be realistic.** The board should not budget or plan around a 1.9-point system-wide improvement. A more conservative 0.5-1.0 point estimate is appropriate.

### **Decision**

**✓ Approve phased rollout** beginning with 100-150 additional branches
**✓ Require quarterly evaluation reports** for the first year
**✓ Set continuation threshold** at 0.5+ points sustained improvement
**✓ Reserve right to pause or modify** if effects don't materialize
