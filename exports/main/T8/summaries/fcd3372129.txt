# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **promising evidence of improvement** in service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by approximately **2 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure—a **4% gain** on the service score scale. While this result does not meet conventional statistical significance thresholds (p = 0.12), there is a **94% probability the true effect is positive**. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** to all branches, with continued monitoring. The evidence supports adoption, and the cost of delaying a beneficial change likely outweighs the risk of a modest ineffective intervention.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **The Board should approve rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

**Rationale:**
1. **Evidence points to benefit** — 94% probability of positive effect
2. **Effect is meaningful** — 4% improvement in service quality
3. **No evidence of harm** — No branch showed deterioration
4. **Risk is manageable** — Phased implementation allows course correction
5. **Cost of delay is real** — Patrons at remaining branches forfeit improved service

**Vote Required:** Simple majority to authorize Operations Team to begin phased implementation in Q1.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Results Summary

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6–14) | After Switch (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| **New Procedure Branches** | 51.4 | 55.1 | +3.8 |
| **Old Procedure Branches** | 47.2 | 48.9 | +1.7 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.0 points** |

### Interpreting the Statistics

| Metric | Value | What It Means |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| **Point Estimate** | +2.0 points | Best estimate of the improvement |
| **P-value** | 0.12 | 12% chance of this result if effect were zero |
| **Probability Effect Is Positive** | 94% | Likelihood the new procedure helps |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | -0.5 to +4.6 | Range of plausible true effects |
| **Effect Size** | 4% of baseline | Meaningful for service quality |

### Why Isn't It "Statistically Significant"?
The study included **34 branches over 17 weeks** (578 observations). To confirm this effect size at conventional significance levels, we would need approximately **900+ observations**. The lack of significance reflects **limited data**, not lack of effect.

### Validity Checks
- ✓ **Parallel trends confirmed**: Both groups followed similar patterns before the switch
- ✓ **No confounding factors**: Staffing and visitor volume changes don't explain results
- ✓ **No detected harms**: No branch showed deterioration attributable to the new procedure

### Risk Mitigation
- Implement in **batches of 5–7 branches** per month
- Pause rollout if any branch shows **sustained score declines** (>4 weeks)
- Maintain documentation for **continuous improvement**

---

## **Decision Framework**

The Board faces a choice between two types of error:

| Error Type | Cost |
|------------|------|
| **Adopt if ineffective** | Minor operational disruption; can revert |
| **Reject if effective** | 4% service improvement delayed indefinitely; patron impact |

Given the **94% probability of benefit** and **low reversal cost**, the evidence favors adoption.
