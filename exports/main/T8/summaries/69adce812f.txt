# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with rollout, but monitor outcomes closely.**

The evidence indicates the new procedure **likely improves service scores**. While we cannot pinpoint the exact benefit with high precision, the data favor a positive effect over no effect. For a procedural change of this nature, the evidence is sufficient to move forward.

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **Recommendation: ROLL OUT THE NEW PROCEDURE** ✓

**Confidence Level:** Moderate-to-High

**Rationale:**
1. **93% probability the effect is positive** — the odds strongly favor benefit
2. **Expected gain of +2.3 points** — meaningful improvement on a ~50-point scale
3. **Low risk of harm** — only 7% chance the effect is zero or negative
4. **Consistent with original hypothesis** — the board's expectation that this would help appears correct
5. **Reversible decision** — if outcomes disappoint, the procedure can be adjusted

**Condition:** Implement a **monitoring plan** to track service scores for 12 weeks post-rollout. If scores do not improve as expected, reassess.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Show**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.3 points** | ~4-5% improvement |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.7 to +5.2** | Range of plausible values |
| Probability effect is positive | **93%** | Strong evidence of benefit |
| Probability effect > 1 point | **80%** | Likely meaningful gain |
| Statistical significance (p-value) | **0.14** | Below 0.05 threshold, but not evidence of "no effect" |

### **Why This Supports Rollout**

The conventional "not statistically significant" label is often misinterpreted. A p-value of 0.14 does **not** mean the procedure doesn't work. It means:

- We lack precision to rule out zero with 95% confidence
- But we also lack precision to rule out +5 points
- The **most likely value is +2.3 points** — a real benefit

### **Observed Outcomes**

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched branches (20) | 50.4 | 52.8 | **+2.4** |
| Non-switched branches (14) | 46.5 | 46.6 | **+0.1** |

The switched branches improved; the non-switched branches did not.

### **Success Metrics**
- Service scores increase by **1-3 points** system-wide
- No decline in any branch's scores
- Staff feedback remains neutral or positive

### **Risk Mitigation**
- If scores do not improve within 8 weeks, convene review committee
- Document implementation fidelity at each branch
- Maintain flexibility to refine the procedure

---

## **Why Not Wait for More Data?**

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| Cost of delay | Continued variation in member experience across branches |
| Cost of being wrong | Modest — procedure can be adjusted or reversed |
| Value of additional certainty | Limited — 93% confidence is adequate for operational decisions |
| Opportunity cost | Staff time spent maintaining two systems instead of one |

**Waiting would only be warranted if:**
- Rollout costs were exceptionally high
- The procedure was irreversible
- We had reason to suspect hidden harms

None of these conditions apply here.

---

## **Technical Notes**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch-clustered standard errors
- **Sample:** 34 branches, 17 weeks, 578 observations
- **Controls:** Staffing index and visits index
- **Limitation:** Pre-switch trends differed slightly between groups (p=0.09), adding modest uncertainty
- **Strength:** Real-world implementation data; controlled comparison group
