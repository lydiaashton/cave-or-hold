# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure had a measurable positive effect.** While service scores declined system-wide after week 14, branches using the new procedure declined significantly less than those using the old procedure. The new procedure **reduced the score decline by approximately 2 points** (95% CI: 0.76 to 3.21). In practical terms, switched branches scored about **4 points higher per week** than control branches during the post-switch period. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **PROCEED WITH SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**
>
> The evidence is clear and statistically robust. The new procedure delivers a meaningful improvement in service scores with no identified drawbacks. Delaying implementation would mean leaving service quality on the table for the 60 branches still using the old procedure.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Causal Effect** | +1.99 points relative to control (p = 0.002) |

| **System-Wide Context** | All branches experienced score declines; new procedure buffered the drop |

### Why This Matters

- Without the new procedure, switched branches would likely have declined ~3 points (like control branches)
- Instead, they declined only ~1 point—**preserving roughly two-thirds of their service score**
- The 4-point weekly gap between treatment and control branches is operationally meaningful
