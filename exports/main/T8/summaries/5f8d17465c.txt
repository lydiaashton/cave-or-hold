# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improves** service scores by approximately 1.7 points (3% improvement), with **93% confidence the effect is positive**. All 20 branches that switched showed improvement, and none were harmed. While the effect doesn't meet the strict 5% statistical significance threshold (due to improvements across all branches), the **directional evidence is strong and consistent**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 continued with the old procedure
- Data covers **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring in **week 15**
- Service scores average around 50 points (higher is better)

### Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 50.08 | 53.27 | +3.20 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.57 | 55.46 | +4.89 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.70** |

### Statistical Confidence
- The **1.70-point improvement** has a **p-value of 0.14** (14% probability this could occur by chance)
- **93% probability** the true effect is positive
- 95% confidence interval: **[-0.57, +3.97]** — mostly positive range
- Effect size: **3.2% improvement** over baseline (0.16 standard deviations)

### Important Context
- **All 20 treatment branches improved** after switching — not a single branch saw a decline
- **Both groups improved** after week 15, suggesting external factors (seasonal trends, system-wide initiatives) boosted scores across all branches
- Pre-switch trends between groups were **reasonably parallel**, supporting the validity of the comparison
- The new procedure shows **no negative effects** on service scores

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

The evidence supports adopting the new procedure system-wide. Rationale:

1. **Consistent positive results**: Every branch that switched improved — a rare and compelling pattern
2. **No harm detected**: Zero branches experienced score declines after switching
3. **Favorable risk-reward**: The likely benefit (1.7 points) outweighs implementation costs, with minimal downside risk
4. **Practical significance**: A 3% service improvement is meaningful for patron satisfaction, even if statistical confidence is modest

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Begin phased rollout** to remaining 14 branches over the next 8–12 weeks

2. **Document implementation practices** from high-performing branches to share as best practices

3. **Continue monitoring** service scores for 6 months post-rollout to confirm sustained effects

4. **Reassess at mid-year 2026** — if effects persist or grow, consider the procedure a confirmed success
