# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect**. The estimated 2.1-point improvement in service scores could reflect a real benefit, but we cannot rule out that the true effect is zero. We recommend **expanding the pilot to gather more evidence** before committing to a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** kept the old procedure
- Switch occurred at **week 15**; analysis compares before/after changes between groups

### Results Summary

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 53.83 | 55.09 | +1.26 |
| Non-switched branches | 46.52 | 45.68 | −0.84 |
| **Estimated effect** | | | **+2.10** |

### Statistical Evidence
- **Parallel trends confirmed**: Before the switch, both groups followed similar trends (p=0.89), validating our comparison approach
- **Effect size**: +2.1 points on the service score index (4.5% improvement)
- **Statistical significance**: p≈0.10; 95% confidence interval ranges from −0.3 to +4.7 points
- **Robustness**: Effect remains consistent when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Important Context
- Non-switched branches experienced a slight **decline** in scores after week 15 (−0.84 points)
- Switched branches **improved** (+1.26 points), resulting in the 2.1-point differential
- The new procedure may help **maintain or improve** service quality during periods when scores might otherwise drift downward

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Direction of effect** | ✅ Positive |
| **Statistical confidence** | ⚠️ Insufficient (p≈0.10) |
| **Practical significance** | ⚠️ Modest (~4% improvement) |
| **Risk of harm** | ✅ No evidence of negative effects |
| **Recommendation** | **Continue pilot; do not yet roll out system-wide** |

**Rationale:** The data shows a promising signal but falls short of the evidence needed to justify the cost and disruption of a full rollout. Expanding the pilot is low-risk and will provide clearer guidance within 4–6 months.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Expand the pilot**: Add 10–15 branches to the new procedure as a formal extended trial

2. **Extend observation period**: Collect at least 12 more weeks of data to improve statistical power

3. **Pre-commit to a decision rule**: For example, "Proceed with full rollout if effect remains positive and p < 0.05 after expanded pilot"

4. **Monitor for heterogeneity**: Check whether the procedure works better for certain branch types (size, location, baseline performance)

5. **Reconvene in 4–6 months**: Make a final rollout decision with stronger evidence

---

*Analysis based on difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects and clustered standard errors. Data covers weeks 6–22, with procedure switch at week 15.*
