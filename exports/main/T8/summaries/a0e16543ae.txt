# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **✓ PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

The evidence supports implementing the new procedure across all branches. The expected improvement is real, the risk is low, and the benefit to patrons is meaningful.

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and rotation procedure **improves service scores** by approximately 1.9 points (~4%). There is **~94% confidence the effect is positive**, with **no evidence of any negative impact**. Given the low risk and potential for meaningful service improvement across the cooperative, we recommend **proceeding with systemwide rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure
- The switch occurred at **week 15**
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach to isolate the procedure's effect

### **Results Summary**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated Effect** | +1.86 service score points |
| **Relative Improvement** | ~3.6% above baseline |
| **Confidence Level** | ~94% (one-tailed) |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | -0.5 to +4.2 points |

### **What the Data Shows**

1. **Treatment branches** (switched to new procedure) maintained stable service scores after the switch (+0.3 points)

2. **Control branches** (kept old procedure) experienced a **decline** in service scores after week 15 (-1.6 points)

3. The **net effect** of the new procedure is **+1.9 points**—the difference between these two trends

4. **Pre-trend validation**: Before the switch (weeks 6–14), both groups followed similar patterns with no systematic differences, supporting the validity of our analysis

5. **No harm detected**: The procedure did not negatively affect any branches

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Implementation Plan**

1. **Roll out the new procedure** to remaining 14 branches over the next 3–6 months

2. **Train staff** using materials and lessons learned from the 20 pilot branches

3. **Monitor service scores** monthly for 6 months post-implementation

4. **Document any challenges** or adaptations needed at individual branches

5. **Conduct formal review** at 6 months to confirm sustained improvement

## **Technical Notes**

- Analysis controls for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week variation
- Standard errors are clustered at the branch level to account for repeated observations
- Results are robust to controlling for staffing and visit volume
- The parallel trends assumption (required for this analysis) is supported by pre-period data
