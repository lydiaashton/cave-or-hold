# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure had a positive effect and should be rolled out to all branches.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure performed significantly better than those that did not. While switching branches saw a small decline in service scores (−0.3 points), non-switching branches declined much more sharply (−2.2 points). The net benefit of switching is **+1.9 points** (p < 0.01), approximately 4% of the average score.

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide implementation.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Switching Branches Fared Better**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Did **not switch** | 49.1 | 46.9 | ▼ -2.2 |
| **Switched** | 49.9 | 49.6 | ▼ -0.3 |

**Net benefit of switching: +1.9 points** (95% CI: 0.7 to 3.1)

The procedure did not raise scores in absolute terms, but it **protected branches from a larger decline** that affected non-switching branches during the same period.

### **2. Results Are Statistically Reliable**

- **p-value: 0.002** (less than 1% chance this is random)
- **Confidence interval: 0.7 to 3.1 points** (effect is positive even at the lower bound)
- **Pre-trends were parallel**: Before week 15, both groups moved similarly, supporting causal interpretation

### **3. Important Context**

Something caused service scores to drop system-wide after week 14 (possibly seasonal factors or external conditions). The new procedure helped branches **weather this decline better** than the old procedure.
