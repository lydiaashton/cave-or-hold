# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **✓ APPROVE ROLLOUT**

The new procedure should be implemented systemwide. The evidence demonstrates a clear, statistically significant improvement in service scores that is consistent, robust, and unlikely to be due to chance or confounding factors.

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.1 points higher** than comparable branches that kept the old procedure. This finding is statistically robust and consistent across all 8 weeks following the switch.

**Confidence level: High** — The analysis meets standard criteria for causal inference (parallel trends confirmed, p < 0.001, effect persists across specifications).

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Treatment (New Procedure) | Control (Old Procedure) |
|--------|--------------------------|-------------------------|
| Branches | 90 | 60 |
| Score before switch | 49.36 | 49.55 |
| Score after switch | 50.78 | 48.87 |
| **Change** | **+1.42** | **-0.68** |

**Net effect: +2.10 points** (approximately 4% improvement; p < 0.001)

### Why This Conclusion Is Reliable

| Check | Result | Interpretation |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| Parallel trends (pre-switch) | p = 0.71 | ✓ Treatment and control groups were on similar trajectories |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 | ✓ Effect unlikely due to random chance |
| Consistency across weeks | +1.5 to +2.8 every week | ✓ No fading or irregular pattern |
| Robustness to covariates | Effect unchanged | ✓ Not explained by staffing or visits |

### Limitations to Acknowledge

- **8-week follow-up**: We cannot yet confirm long-term sustainability
- **Observational design**: While methods are rigorous, this is not a randomized trial
- **Modest effect size**: 2.1 points is meaningful but not transformative

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout**, with the following conditions:

1. Implement over the next quarter with proper training
2. Continue monitoring service scores for 6+ months post-rollout
3. Be prepared to adjust implementation based on ongoing results
