# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 1.9 points** (about 4% on a ~50-point scale). The effect is statistically significant and robust across analytical approaches. While treatment branches had slightly stronger momentum before the switch, the magnitude and consistency of the results support proceeding with a full rollout.

---

## **Final Verdict**

### ✅ **APPROVE THE ROLLOUT**

The data supports system-wide adoption of the new procedure. The improvement is real, meaningful, and worth the investment.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| New procedure branches | 49.99 | 53.01 | +3.02 |
| Old procedure branches | 49.09 | 50.19 | +1.10 |
| **Net improvement** | | | **+1.92** |

### Evidence Strength

- **709 branches**, **17 weeks**, **12,053 observations**
- **Highly significant**: p < 0.001; 95% confidence interval: 1.2 to 2.7 points
- Effect persists after controlling for staffing and visitor volume

### Important Context

- Treatment branches started 0.9 points higher on average (accounted for in the analysis)
- Treatment branches had slightly stronger pre-switch momentum (a limitation to note)
- Even accounting for this, the effect size and statistical significance support the conclusion

**Approve system-wide rollout.** The evidence indicates a real, meaningful improvement in service quality from the new procedure.
