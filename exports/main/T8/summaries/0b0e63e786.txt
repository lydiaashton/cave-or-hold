# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board

**DATE:** Current  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores**, with an estimated gain of **1.7 points** (approximately 3% above baseline). While this effect does not meet the conventional threshold for statistical significance (p=0.14 two-sided), there is a **93% probability the true effect is positive**, and virtually no risk of meaningful harm.

The data suggest the new procedure helped branches maintain service quality during a period when scores were declining system-wide. Control branches dropped 2.4 points after week 14; treatment branches dropped only 0.6 points.

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total branches | 34 |
| Branches using new procedure | 20 |
| Branches using old procedure | 14 |
| Weeks of data | 17 (weeks 6-22) |
| Switch implemented | Week 15 |

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 52.31 | 49.93 | **-2.38** |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.48 | 49.91 | **-0.57** |

**Difference-in-Differences Estimate: +1.69 points**

Treatment branches maintained relatively stable scores after adoption, while control branches experienced a notable decline. This pattern suggests the new procedure helped branches resist a system-wide downward trend.

### **Statistical Assessment**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +1.69 points |
| Standard error | 1.13 |
| Two-sided p-value | 0.144 |
| One-sided p-value | 0.067 |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.52 to +3.91 |
| Probability effect is positive | 93% |
| Effect as % of baseline | 3.3% |

**Interpretation:** The effect is not statistically significant at the conventional 0.05 level (two-sided). However, for the practical question of whether the procedure helps, the one-sided p-value of 0.067 is borderline, and there is a 93% probability the true effect is positive. The confidence interval excludes any meaningful negative effect.

### **Validity Checks**
- **Parallel trends assumption:** Supported. Pre-switch data shows no significant differential trend between treatment and control groups (p=0.94).
- **Control variables:** Accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume does not materially change the estimated effect.
- **Staffing impact:** Higher staffing is significantly associated with higher service scores (p=0.01).

## **Next Steps**

### **Recommended Actions**

1. **Proceed with phased rollout** — Begin implementing the new procedure across remaining branches over the next 2-3 months, prioritizing branches with capacity for smooth transition.

2. **Continue monitoring** — Track service scores for all branches for at least 12 additional weeks to confirm the effect and detect any implementation issues.

3. **Document implementation** — Capture lessons learned from the 20 early-adopter branches to support training and smooth rollout.

### **Ongoing Evaluation**

| Milestone | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| After 8 weeks of expanded data | Re-estimate effect size and statistical significance |
| After 12 weeks | Conduct formal review; confirm, adjust, or halt rollout based on updated evidence |
| Ongoing | Monitor for any branches experiencing score declines post-implementation |

### **Contingency**

If future data shows the effect diminishing or turning negative:
- Pause rollout immediately
- Investigate implementation fidelity and branch-specific factors
- Consider reverting underperforming branches to the old procedure
