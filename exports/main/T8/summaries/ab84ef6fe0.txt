# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure is associated with a **2-point improvement in service scores**, and there is approximately a **95% probability the true effect is positive**. However, the pilot was too small to rule out chance with conventional statistical certainty (p = 0.10). The evidence supports proceeding, but expectations should be calibrated accordingly.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Direction and Magnitude of Effect**
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched (n=20) | 48.7 | 50.5 | **+1.8** |
| Branches that did not switch (n=14) | 47.6 | 47.5 | **-0.1** |

**Net effect: +1.9 points** (95% confidence interval: -0.4 to +4.3)

### **2. Statistical Certainty**
- **p-value = 0.10** — above the 0.05 threshold for statistical significance
- **~95% probability** the true effect is positive (not zero or negative)
- The pilot lacked power to detect effects smaller than ~3 points

### **3. Branch-Level Variation**
- **14 of 20 branches (70%)** that switched saw improvements
- **6 of 20 branches (30%)** saw declines (-0.6 to -3.6 points)
- Top performers gained **6 to 8 points**
- Control branches split evenly: 7 improved, 7 declined

### **4. Practical Significance**
- Effect size (Cohen's d = 0.57) is **medium** by conventional standards
- A 2-point gain is comparable to moving from average to top-quartile performance

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Recommendation: Proceed with phased rollout, accompanied by monitoring and support.**

**Rationale:**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Likelihood of benefit** | High (~95% probability of positive effect) |
| **Magnitude of benefit** | Modest but meaningful (~2 points on service index) |
| **Risk of harm** | Limited (30% of branches declined; similar pattern in controls) |
| **Cost of waiting** | Delayed benefits system-wide; would require 2-3x more data for certainty |
| **Cost of proceeding** | Training and implementation resources; manageable with safeguards |

**This is a favorable risk-reward proposition.** The procedure is unlikely to cause widespread harm, and the probability of system-wide improvement is high.

---

## **Next Steps**

### **1. Approve Phased Rollout (6-month timeline)**
- Phase 1: 10 branches (Months 1-2)
- Phase 2: 12 branches (Months 3-4)
- Phase 3: Remaining branches (Months 5-6)

### **2. Implement Safeguards**
- Enhanced training for all adopting branches
- 30-day check-ins to identify struggling branches early
- Peer mentoring from top-performing pilot branches

### **3. Continue Monitoring**
- Weekly service score tracking for all branches
- 6-month evaluation against success criteria (sustained +1.5 point average improvement)
- Document implementation practices at high-performing branches

### **4. Decision Gates**
- If aggregate scores decline after Phase 1: Pause and investigate
- If 6-month target is not met: Revert to optional adoption model

---

## **Conclusion**

The pilot did not deliver statistical proof, but it did deliver **actionable evidence**. The new procedure likely helps, probably by a modest amount, and the risk of system-wide harm is low. Proceeding with appropriate safeguards is the prudent course.

---

*Analysis based on 578 weekly observations from 34 branches, weeks 6–22. The new procedure was implemented at week 15 in 20 branches; 14 branches served as controls.*
