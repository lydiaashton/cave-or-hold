# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is inconclusive.** We cannot determine whether the new procedure improved, harmed, or had no effect on service scores. The estimated effect (+1.9 points) is not statistically distinguishable from zero (p=0.14). Switched branches maintained their scores while non-switched branches declined significantly, suggesting the new procedure may have protected against a system-wide decline—but this cannot be confirmed with current data.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **DO NOT APPROVE SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT AT THIS TIME**

**Reason:** The analysis does not provide sufficient evidence to justify expanding the procedure to all 34 branches. The observed effect could plausibly be zero, slightly negative, or moderately positive. Making a system-wide change on this evidence would be premature.

**What would change our verdict:**
- A statistically significant result (p < 0.05), OR
- A larger effect size with narrower confidence intervals, OR
- Additional data showing consistent positive trends over a longer period

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Performance**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6-14) | After (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|--------------------|--------------------|--------|
| **Switched Branches** (20 branches) | 51.4 | 51.3 | **-0.1** |
| **Non-Switched Branches** (14 branches) | 49.3 | 47.2 | **-2.1**\* |

\* Control group decline is statistically significant (p=0.015)

### **Statistical Results**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated Treatment Effect | +1.93 points | Point estimate only |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.6 to +4.5 points | **Includes zero** |
| P-value | 0.14 | **Not statistically significant** |
| Minimum Detectable Effect | ~3.7 points | Study is underpowered |

### **What We Can and Cannot Conclude**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve scores? | **Cannot determine** — effect not statistically significant |
| Did the new procedure harm scores? | **Cannot rule out** — CI includes small negative values |
| Did switched branches perform differently than controls? | **Yes** — they held steady while controls declined |
| Is the observed difference likely real? | **Uncertain** — could be random variation |

## **Appendix: Methodology**

- **Approach**: Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Sample**: 34 branches, 578 observations, 17 weeks (weeks 6-22)
- **Treatment**: 20 branches switched to new procedure at week 15
- **Control**: 14 branches maintained old procedure
- **Validation**: Parallel trends assumption confirmed (no pre-treatment differences)
