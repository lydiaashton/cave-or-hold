# Memorandum to the Board

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 2026

---

## Bottom Line

The new procedure shows a **positive effect in the expected direction** (+1.6 points on service scores), but the evidence is **not yet statistically conclusive**. The data does not show harm, and the main limitation is insufficient post-switch observation time rather than negative results.

---

## Key Findings

### Effect on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 48.9 | 49.3 | +0.4 |
| Branches with old procedure | 54.3 | 53.1 | −1.2 |
| **Net effect (Difference-in-Differences)** | | | **+1.6** |

- The new procedure is associated with a **1.6-point improvement** in service scores (approximately 3% above baseline)
- The 95% confidence interval (−0.5 to +3.8 points) means we cannot rule out no effect, but the **entire range of plausible effects is modest**
- **No evidence of negative impact** on service scores

### Important Context

1. **Baseline differences**: Branches selected for the new procedure had lower average service scores before the switch (48.9 vs. 54.3), suggesting targeted deployment to branches needing improvement.

2. **Parallel pre-trends**: Before week 15, both groups showed similar week-to-week patterns, supporting the validity of our comparison.

3. **Limited statistical power**: With only 8 weeks of post-switch data and natural week-to-week variation of 8–9 points, this study can only reliably detect effects larger than 3 points.

---

## Final Verdict

**Proceed with cautious expansion, not full rollout.**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the procedure improve scores? | **Likely yes, but we cannot be certain** |
| Did the procedure harm scores? | **No evidence of harm** |
| Is the effect large enough to matter? | **Modest (~3% improvement)** |
| Should we roll out everywhere now? | **No—insufficient evidence** |
| Should we abandon the procedure? | **No—no evidence it fails** |

**Recommended path:** Expand to 10–15 additional branches and collect 12+ more weeks of data. This would provide sufficient statistical power to confirm whether the observed 1.6-point improvement is real. If the effect persists with more data, proceed with full rollout.

## Technical Appendix

- **Analysis method**: Difference-in-Differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Sample**: 34 branches (20 treatment, 14 control), 17 weeks, 578 observations
- **DiD coefficient**: +1.61 (SE = 1.10, p = 0.14)
- **Minimum detectable effect** (80% power): ~3.0 points
- **Software**: Python with statsmodels
