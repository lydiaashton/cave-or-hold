# **Board Summary: Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure helped branches maintain service scores during a period of system-wide decline.** After week 14, all branches experienced lower scores, but branches with the new procedure declined much less than those without it. The procedure provided a **relative benefit of approximately 2.3 points**.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout—the new procedure builds resilience against score declines, though we should also investigate what caused the system-wide drop after week 14.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What Happened to Service Scores**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.9 | 51.8 | **-0.2** |
| Non-switched branches | 52.1 | 49.7 | **-2.4** |

**Critical observation:** Something caused service scores to drop across the entire system after week 14. The new procedure helped branches resist this decline.

### **The Week 14→15 Transition**

| Group | Week 14 | Week 15 | One-Week Change |
|-------|---------|---------|-----------------|
| Switched branches | 57.8 | 48.6 | **-9.2** |
| Non-switched branches | 57.0 | 47.3 | **-9.7** |

Both groups dropped sharply at the switch point. The new procedure reduced the decline by about 0.5 points in the immediate transition, and continued providing protection in subsequent weeks.

### **Statistical Confidence**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect (DiD) | +2.25 points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.83 to 2.68 |
| Statistical Significance | p < 0.001 |
| Sample | 709 branches, 12,053 observations |

### **No Unintended Consequences**

- Staffing requirements: **No significant change**
- Visit volumes: **No significant change**

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure affect service scores?** | **Yes** — statistically significant positive effect |
| **Did scores improve in absolute terms?** | **No** — scores declined system-wide after week 14 |
| **Did the procedure provide relative benefit?** | **Yes** — switched branches declined 2.3 points less |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes** — the procedure protects against score declines |
| **Are there any concerns?** | **Yes** — investigate the system-wide week 15 drop |

**Verdict: APPROVE ROLLOUT**

The evidence supports adopting the new procedure system-wide. While it did not produce absolute score gains, it demonstrably protected branches from a larger decline. In an environment where external factors may pressure service scores, this protective effect has clear operational value.
