# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive direction of effect** (+1.6 points), and there is approximately a **94% probability the true effect is beneficial**. However, the evidence is **not statistically conclusive** by conventional standards (p=0.13), and we estimate only a **33% probability the improvement will reach 2+ points**.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout **if implementation costs are modest**, but set realistic expectations and maintain close monitoring. If the rollout is costly or disruptive, consider extending the pilot to gather more definitive evidence.

## **What This Means for the Board**

### The Good News
- The data **does not contradict** your expectation that the procedure would improve scores
- There is a **94% likelihood** the effect is genuinely positive
- No branches showed dramatic declines attributable to the new procedure
- The direction of travel is correct

### The Caveats
- We **cannot guarantee** the improvement will materialize at the expected scale
- There is a **6% chance** the procedure has no benefit or slight negative effect
- The estimated effect (+1.6 points) may be **smaller than anticipated**
- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** limits our confidence

---

## **Recommendation**

### **Conditional Rollout with Monitoring**

We recommend proceeding, but with explicit acknowledgment of the uncertainty:

| If... | Then... |
|-------|---------|
| Implementation costs are **low** | Proceed with full rollout |
| Implementation costs are **moderate** | Proceed with phased rollout (10-15 branches first) |
| Implementation costs are **high** | Extend pilot 8-12 weeks before deciding |

### **Regardless of Decision, Implement:**

1. **Weekly score tracking** for all branches during rollout
2. **Pre-defined success threshold** (e.g., +1.0 point sustained over 12 weeks)
3. **Exit criteria** if scores decline system-wide (>5% of branches drop 3+ points)
4. **Re-evaluation at Week 20** post-rollout with recommendation to continue, adjust, or revert

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **APPROVE THE ROLLOUT**

After weighing the evidence, the Data Analysis Team's final verdict is to **proceed with system-wide implementation** of the new stock rotation and shelving procedure, subject to the monitoring safeguards outlined above.

**Rationale:**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| Direction of effect | ✓ Positive |
| Probability of benefit | ✓ 94% |
| Risk of harm | ✓ Low (6% chance of negative effect) |
| Magnitude of effect | ⚠ Modest (+1.6 points, may be less than expected) |
| Quality of evidence | ⚠ Suggestive but not definitive |

**This verdict reflects a practical business decision, not statistical certainty.** In operational contexts, waiting for p<0.05 often means missing opportunities. A 94% probability of positive impact, combined with minimal downside risk, justifies moving forward—provided the board accepts that the actual benefit may be smaller than hoped and commits to monitoring outcomes.

**We will not recommend this course if:**
- Implementation costs exceed $50,000 (or equivalent resource commitment)
- Branch staff capacity is already stretched thin
- The board requires guaranteed ROI before proceeding

**Otherwise: Proceed with confidence, monitor closely, and adjust as needed.**
