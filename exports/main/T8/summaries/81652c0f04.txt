# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched saw their scores increase by **2.1 points** (95% CI: 1.4 to 2.9) relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This is a **4% improvement** over baseline and is statistically significant (p < 0.001). We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

The evidence is clear and actionable: the new stock rotation and shelving procedure delivers measurable improvements in service quality. The effect is statistically robust, grows over time, and holds across different analytical approaches. There is no evidence of harm, and the pre-switch data confirms treatment and control branches were comparable before the intervention.
