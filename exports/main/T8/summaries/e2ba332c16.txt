# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improves service scores by approximately 2-3 points** (about 4-5%). While statistical uncertainty means we cannot rule out a smaller effect, the evidence strongly favors a positive impact. The data supports the board's expectation that the change would help.

**Verdict: Proceed with rollout, with monitoring.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Estimated Impact**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Treatment effect | **+2.29 service score points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.48 to +5.05 points** |
| Probability effect is positive | **~95%** |
| Effect size | **~4% improvement** over baseline |

### **2. Branch-Level Outcomes**

| Outcome | Switched Branches (n=20) | Non-Switched Branches (n=14) |
|---------|--------------------------|------------------------------|
| Branches that improved | 13 (65%) | 7 (50%) |
| Branches that declined | 7 (35%) | 7 (50%) |
| Average score change | +1.63 | -0.66 |

### **3. Analysis Quality**

✓ **Valid comparison:** Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (p = 0.70)  
✓ **Complete data:** All 34 branches reported for all 17 weeks  
✓ **Controls applied:** Results account for staffing levels and visitor volume  

### **4. Important Context**

- Switched branches began with **lower baseline scores** (~3.5 points below non-switched branches)
- The new procedure appears to have **narrowed this gap** rather than creating a new advantage
- **7 of 20 switched branches** did not show improvement—implementation quality may vary

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure change service scores? | **Yes, likely positive** |
| Is the effect statistically conclusive? | **No—magnitude uncertain** |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Yes, with monitoring** |
| What's the risk of being wrong? | **Low—the effect is unlikely to be negative** |

**Rationale:** The statistical uncertainty reflects imprecision about the *size* of the benefit, not the *direction*. With a 95% probability the effect is positive and low implementation costs, proceeding with rollout is the sound decision. Continued data collection will refine our estimate of the true effect size.

## **Technical Notes**

- **Method:** Difference-in-Differences regression with branch-level clustered standard errors
- **Data period:** Weeks 6-22 (procedure change implemented at week 15)
- **Sample:** 34 branches, 578 branch-week observations
- **Controls:** Staffing index and visits index
