# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores by 2.1 points** (4% above baseline). The effect is statistically significant and consistent across all post-switch weeks. **We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.13 points (95% CI: 1.4 to 2.9) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Branches Studied** | 709 total (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Study Period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |

**What the data shows:**

1. **Treatment and control branches performed identically before the switch** (difference: 0.1 points, not significant), confirming the groups are comparable.

2. **After the switch, treatment branches pulled ahead by 2+ points** and maintained this advantage through week 22.

3. **The improvement grew over time**—from +1.6 points in weeks 15–18 to +2.6 points in weeks 19–22—suggesting branches benefit more as they gain experience.

4. **The result holds after accounting for staffing and visit volumes**, confirming the procedure itself drove the improvement.
