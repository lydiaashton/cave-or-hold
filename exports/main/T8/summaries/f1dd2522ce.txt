# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests the new procedure improved service scores, but with important caveats.** Our best estimate is a **1–2 point increase** in service scores, though pre-existing differences between branches make this uncertain. The result is statistically significant but modest in magnitude. We recommend **proceeding with rollout while monitoring outcomes closely**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Studied**
- **709 branches**: 425 adopted the new procedure; 284 kept the old procedure
- **17 weeks** of data (weeks 6–22), with the switch in week 15

### **The Result**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| New procedure branches | 52.0 | 54.8 | +2.8 |
| Old procedure branches | 50.6 | 51.4 | +0.8 |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+2.0** |

**Statistical confidence:** The 2.0-point effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001), with a 95% confidence interval of 1.4 to 2.6 points.

### **Important Caveats**

1. **Pre-existing differences**: Switched branches had lower scores than control branches in the early weeks of the study. Some of the observed "improvement" may reflect recovery to normal levels rather than the procedure itself.

2. **Modest post-switch effects**: After the switch, only 2 of 8 weeks showed statistically significant improvements individually. The effect is real but not large.

3. **Parallel trends near the switch**: In the two weeks immediately before the switch, the groups performed similarly—this supports the validity of our comparison.

### **Bottom Line on the Evidence**

| Strength | Assessment |
|----------|------------|
| Statistical significance | ✓ Strong (p < 0.001) |
| Effect size | △ Modest (~2 points on a ~50-point scale) |
| Pre-trend validity | △ Acceptable near switch, concerning earlier |
| Consistency over time | △ Positive but variable |

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with rollout, but temper expectations.**

The data provide **moderate-confidence evidence** that the new procedure improves service scores. The statistical signal is clear, but the magnitude is uncertain and likely smaller than the headline 2-point estimate suggests.

**Why I recommend proceeding:**
- All post-switch effects are positive (none negative)
- Pre-trends were parallel in the critical weeks immediately before the switch
- Even a 1-point system-wide improvement benefits patrons
- The procedure appears to do no harm

**Why I recommend caution:**
- The true effect may be closer to 1 point than 2 points
- Early pre-trends suggest switched branches were different in unobserved ways
- Implementation quality may vary across branches

**Bottom line:** This is a **modest win, not a transformation**. Roll out the procedure, but invest in good implementation and track results. Be prepared to adjust if outcomes don't match expectations.
