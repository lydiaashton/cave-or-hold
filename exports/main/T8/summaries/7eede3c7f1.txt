# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

## **Bottom Line**

**The data indicates the new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2 points (4%).** This finding was unexpected given prior expectations. The effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and the analysis meets standard validity checks. **We recommend a system-wide rollout with close monitoring** to confirm the effect holds at scale.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.1 points** on service score |
| Statistical significance | **p = 0.0006** (highly significant) |
| 95% confidence interval | **+0.9 to +3.4 points** |
| Branches showing improvement | **76%** of switched vs. 53% of control |
| Parallel trends test | **Passed** (p = 0.79) |

---

## **Final Verdict**

**PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

The evidence supports adopting the new procedure across all branches. While this result contradicts prior expectations, the analysis is methodologically sound:

- The treatment and control groups followed identical trends before the switch
- The improvement is unlikely to be due to chance (less than 1 in 1,000 probability)
- The effect is consistent across most switched branches
- No credible alternative explanation emerges from the data

**Caveat:** Given the unexpected nature of this finding, implementation should include active monitoring to confirm the effect replicates when extended to all 150 branches.
