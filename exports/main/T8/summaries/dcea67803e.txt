# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests the new procedure improved service scores, but with moderate confidence.** Branches that switched showed an estimated **1.9-point improvement** (approximately 4% gain) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The result is statistically significant but marginal (p ≈ 0.03), and the confidence interval [0.2 to 3.5 points] indicates uncertainty about the true effect size. **We recommend a cautious rollout with close monitoring**, rather than an immediate system-wide mandate.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure
- Switch occurred in **week 15**

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 45.7 | 48.1 | +2.4 |
| Non-switched branches | 49.5 | 50.0 | +0.5 |
| **Net improvement** | | | **+1.9** |

### **Statistical Assessment**
| Model Specification | Effect Estimate | P-value | 95% CI |
|--------------------|-----------------|---------|--------|
| Basic (no fixed effects) | 1.87 | 0.21 | [-1.1, 4.8] |
| Branch fixed effects | 2.36 | 0.001 | [1.0, 3.7] |
| Branch + Week fixed effects | 1.87 | 0.026 | [0.2, 3.5] |
| + Staffing/visits controls | 1.92 | 0.022 | [0.3, 3.6] |

**Interpretation:** The effect is statistically significant only when accounting for branch-specific characteristics, which is appropriate for this design. The marginal significance and narrow confidence interval suggest **moderate but not definitive evidence**.

### **Validation Checks**
- ✓ Parallel trends assumption satisfied (pre-treatment trends did not differ significantly)
- ✓ Effect persists after controlling for staffing and visitor volume
- ⚠️ Only 8 weeks of post-treatment data (limited window for sustained impact)

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with rollout, but phase it and monitor closely.**

The data provide **moderate evidence of benefit** with no detected harms. While the statistical evidence is not overwhelming, the consistent positive effect across specifications, combined with the low risk of adverse outcomes, supports moving forward. However, the uncertainty warrants a measured approach rather than immediate full deployment.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Phased rollout with evaluation checkpoints:**

1. **Expand to 5–10 additional branches** over the next quarter
2. **Monitor weekly service scores** during expansion
3. **Re-evaluate after 12 weeks** with additional data before full system-wide rollout
4. **Document implementation details** at each branch to identify best practices

---

**Analysis method:** Difference-in-Differences regression with robustness checks across multiple model specifications.
