# **Memorandum to the Library Cooperative Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. The best estimate is an improvement of about 2 points, but the evidence is not strong enough to confirm the effect with confidence.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **DO NOT PROCEED WITH FULL ROLLOUT AT THIS TIME.**
>
> **Extend the pilot and collect more data before making a system-wide commitment.**

**Rationale:**
- The p-value (0.096) exceeds the standard threshold for statistical significance (0.05)
- The 95% confidence interval (-0.4 to +4.4 points) includes zero
- We cannot confidently distinguish a real improvement from random variation
- A full rollout based on inconclusive evidence risks wasting resources on a change that may not deliver benefits

---

## **Key Statistics**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Estimated Effect | +2.0 points |
| P-value | 0.096 |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.4 to +4.4 points |
| Statistical Conclusion | **Not significant at 5% level** |

---

## **What the Data Show**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 51.1 | 52.8 | +1.7 |
| Old Procedure Branches | 52.6 | 52.4 | -0.2 |

The pattern is consistent with improvement, but the sample size is not large enough to confirm the effect with high confidence.

---

## **Recommended Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Purpose |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Continue pilot at 20 treatment branches | 8–12 more weeks | Gather additional data |
| Keep 14 control branches unchanged | Through pilot period | Maintain comparison group |
| Re-analyze with full dataset | End of Q1 2027 | Reach statistical conclusion |
| Decide on rollout | Q2 2027 | Evidence-based decision |

---

## **What Would Change Our Verdict?**

| Scenario | Recommended Action |
|----------|-------------------|
| P-value drops below 0.05 with more data | Proceed with rollout |
| Effect size grows to 3+ points | Strong case for rollout |
| Effect shrinks toward zero | Abandon the new procedure |
| Costs prove minimal | Lower bar for rollout |
| Costs prove substantial | Higher bar for rollout |

---

## **Summary for Decision-Makers**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did service scores improve? | Point estimate says yes (+2 points) |
| Are we sure it wasn't random chance? | **No** (p = 0.096) |
| Should we roll out system-wide? | **Not yet** — more data needed |
| When should we revisit? | After 8–12 additional weeks |

---

*This analysis used difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects and clustered standard errors, the standard method for evaluating policy changes with treatment and control groups.*
