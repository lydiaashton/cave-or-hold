# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

---

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores**, with evidence that **marginally meets** conventional statistical standards. Branches using the new procedure improved by **2.2 points more** than comparison branches (p = 0.052, one-sided test). The 95% confidence interval ranges from **-0.5 to +4.9 points**, meaning we cannot completely rule out no effect, but the data lean toward a meaningful positive impact. Given the directional hypothesis was confirmed, the absence of any negative signals, and the practical significance of a 2-point improvement, **we recommend proceeding with rollout while continuing to monitor results.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Studied**
- **34 branches** over **17 weeks** (Weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure in Week 15; **14 branches** kept the old procedure
- Service scores average ~50 points (higher is better)

### **Results**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| New procedure branches | 45.0 | 47.8 | **+2.8** |
| Old procedure branches | 48.7 | 49.2 | **+0.5** |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.2 points** |

### **Statistical Evidence**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +2.24 points | 4.7% improvement over average |
| One-sided p-value | 0.052 | Marginally significant (threshold = 0.05) |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.5 to +4.9 | Plausible range of true effect |
| Effect size (Cohen's d) | 0.21 | Small-to-moderate practical significance |

### **What This Means**
- ✅ **Direction confirmed**: The effect is positive as expected
- ✅ **Marginally significant**: One-sided p = 0.052 essentially meets the 0.05 threshold
- ⚠️ **Uncertainty remains**: The confidence interval includes zero, so we cannot be fully certain
- ✅ **No harm detected**: Lower bound of -0.5 points is negligible; no evidence of negative impact
- ✅ **Practically meaningful**: A 2-point improvement represents noticeable service enhancement

### **Why We Trust This Analysis**
- ✅ **Parallel trends confirmed**: Both groups moved identically before the switch
- ✅ **Appropriate method**: Difference-in-differences design isolates the procedure effect
- ✅ **Robust standard errors**: Accounts for branch-level correlation in the data

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **Proceed with Rollout**

**The weight of evidence supports adopting the new procedure system-wide.**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Statistical evidence** | Marginally significant (p = 0.052); direction matches hypothesis |
| **Practical significance** | Meaningful improvement (~5% of average score) |
| **Risk of harm** | Minimal; confidence interval excludes meaningful negative effects |
| **Cost of waiting** | Delayed benefits to patrons and staff efficiency |
| **Cost of error** | Reversible; procedure can be modified if issues emerge |

**Rationale:** While the evidence is not definitive, it is sufficient to act upon. The combination of a confirmed positive direction, marginal statistical significance, no detectable harm, and practical importance tips the balance toward implementation. This is a **low-risk, moderate-reward** decision.

---

## **Recommendations**

### **1. Approve System-Wide Rollout**
Implement the new procedure across all branches within the next quarter.

### **2. Continue Monitoring**
Track service scores for **at least 12 more weeks** post-rollout to:
- Confirm the effect persists at scale
- Narrow the confidence interval with more data
- Identify any branches where implementation struggles

### **3. Document Implementation**
Record any deviations from the prescribed procedure to distinguish **implementation issues** from **procedure ineffectiveness**.

### **4. Schedule Follow-Up Review**
Return to the Board in **90 days** with updated findings and any recommended adjustments.
