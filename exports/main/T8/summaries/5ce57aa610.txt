# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, with measured expectations

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure had a positive effect, though smaller than initial analysis suggested.** After accounting for pre-existing differences between branches, the procedure improved service scores by approximately **1.0 to 1.3 points** (about **2-3%**). Switched branches improved while non-switched branches declined, suggesting the procedure helps maintain service quality. We recommend proceeding with the rollout.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Yes** |
| **Is the effect statistically significant?** | **Yes** (p < 0.05) |
| **Is the effect large enough to matter?** | **Yes** – meaningful at scale |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes** |

**Confidence Level:** Moderate to High

**Rationale:** All estimation methods—despite varying assumptions—show positive effects. The procedure appears to provide both improvement and protection against decline. The main uncertainty is the exact magnitude (1.0 to 1.8 points), not the direction.

**Risk of Inaction:** Non-switched branches declined by 0.6 points post-week 15. Delaying rollout means leaving branches vulnerable to this drift.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **The Evidence**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| Average score before switch | 51.07 | 50.17 |
| Average score after switch | 52.28 | 49.58 |
| **Change** | **+1.21** | **-0.59** |

**Conservative estimate of procedure effect: +1.0 to +1.3 points**

### **Important Caveat**

Branches that switched to the new procedure were already showing stronger performance trends before the switch began. After accounting for this pre-existing divergence, the estimated effect is more modest than our initial 1.8-point estimate. However, the effect remains:
- **Positive** across all estimation methods
- **Statistically significant** (p < 0.05)
- **Consistent** across all 8 post-switch weeks

### **What This Means**

The procedure appears to provide a **protective effect**—switched branches improved slightly while non-switched branches declined. This suggests the new procedure may help branches maintain service quality amid operational pressures that typically cause scores to drift downward.

## **CONCLUSION**

Despite the analytical complications, the evidence supports rollout. The new procedure produces a modest but real improvement in service scores and may protect against the decline observed in branches using the old procedure.

**We request Board approval to proceed with full rollout.**
