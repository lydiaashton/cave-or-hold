# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### ✅ **ROLL OUT THE NEW PROCEDURE SYSTEM-WIDE**

**The evidence is conclusive:** The new rotation and shelving procedure **improves service scores by 2.2 points** (p < 0.001). This is a real, measurable gain that justifies immediate expansion to all 284 remaining branches.

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **increased service scores by 2.2 points** (4.2% improvement). This is a meaningful, statistically significant gain that validates the cooperative's investment in this change.

---

## **Evidence Summary**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Branches studied** | 709 (425 switched, 284 control) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Service score improvement** | **+2.17 points** |
| **Statistical confidence** | 95% CI: 1.59 to 2.75 (p < 0.001) |
| **Effect size** | 4.2% above baseline; 0.22 standard deviations |

### **What Happened**

- **Switched branches** improved from 51.59 to 52.83 (+1.24 points)
- **Control branches** declined from 51.31 to 50.37 (-0.95 points)
- **Net attributable effect:** +2.19 points

The control group's slight decline makes the treatment effect even more compelling—without the new procedure, switched branches would likely have followed the same downward trend.

### **Why We Trust This Result**

✓ **Valid comparison:** Switched and non-switched branches followed identical trends before the switch (pre-trend test: p = 0.99)  
✓ **Robust analysis:** Result holds with branch-level controls and fixed effects  
✓ **Large sample:** 12,053 weekly observations across 709 branches  
✓ **Consistent effect:** Improvement observed across branches with varying staffing levels
