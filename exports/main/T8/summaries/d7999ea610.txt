# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure – Impact Assessment

**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **protected branches from decline** rather than actively improving scores. Branches that switched maintained their performance, while branches on the old procedure dropped nearly 2 points. The 2-point difference is statistically significant and meaningful.

**Verdict: Proceed with rollout, but investigate why the old procedure is failing.**

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Net difference** | +2.04 points |
| **Confidence range** | 1.5 to 2.6 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |
| **Branches studied** | 709 |
| **Analysis period** | 17 weeks |

### Service Score Changes

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| **New procedure** | 52.4 | 52.6 | +0.1 |
| **Old procedure** | 51.0 | 49.1 | −1.9 |

**Key insight:** The benefit comes from *preventing loss*, not creating gain.

---

## **Why This Matters**

1. **Something is causing scores to drop** system-wide. The old procedure no longer sustains prior performance levels.

2. **The new procedure buffers against this decline.** Whether by design or coincidence, switched branches held steady.

3. **We don't yet know the root cause** of the decline affecting non-switched branches. Possibilities include seasonal patterns, staffing pressures, or procedural obsolescence.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Does the new procedure help?** | Yes—it prevents decline |
| **Should we roll it out?** | Yes—but not as a complete solution |
| **Is further investigation needed?** | Yes—urgent |

**Recommendation:**
- **Approve rollout** to remaining branches (they need the protection)
- **Launch parallel investigation** into why scores are falling under the old procedure
- **Set a 6-month review** to confirm the effect persists and identify any unintended consequences
