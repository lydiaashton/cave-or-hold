# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

**The new procedure likely improves service scores, and we recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout.** There is a **94% probability** the effect is positive, with an estimated improvement of **2-3 points (4-5%)**. No evidence of harm was detected.

---

## Final Verdict

### ✅ **APPROVE ROLLOUT**

The evidence supports expanding the new procedure to all branches. While not statistically definitive by academic standards, this level of confidence is appropriate for an operational decision where:

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| Direction of effect | ✓ Positive (as expected) |
| Probability of improvement | ✓ 94% |
| Risk of negative impact | ✓ None detected |
| Practical significance | ✓ Meaningful (4-5% gain) |

---

## What We Found

### The Numbers

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+2.3 points** |
| Typical branch service score | ~50 points |
| Percentage improvement | **~4-5%** |
| Confidence level | **94% probability of positive effect** |
| Statistical significance | Moderate (p = 0.12) |

### Study Design

- **34 branches**: 20 adopted new procedure, 14 kept old procedure
- **17 weeks of data**: 9 weeks before switch, 8 weeks after
- **Method**: Difference-in-differences with branch-level controls
- **Validation**: Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups

### Key Observation

Treatment branches started with lower baseline scores (~2.7 points below control). After the switch, **the gap narrowed to less than 0.5 points**, indicating the procedure helped lower-performing branches catch up.

---

## Implementation Recommendations

### Rollout Plan

1. **Full expansion** to remaining 14 branches over next 8-12 weeks
2. **Continue weekly monitoring** of service scores system-wide
3. **Document implementation** to capture best practices from early adopters
4. **Re-evaluate in Q2** with full year of data

### Success Metrics

| Timeline | Target |
|----------|--------|
| 4 weeks post-rollout | No decline in any branch scores |
| 8 weeks post-rollout | System-wide average +1.5 points |
| 12 weeks post-rollout | System-wide average +2.0 points |

---

## Caveats

- **8 weeks of post-switch data** limits long-term effect assessment
- **Some branches may respond differently** based on local conditions
- **Continued monitoring essential** to confirm effects persist

---

## Bottom Line for Decision-Makers

If the board requires **95%+ statistical certainty**, defer the decision and collect more data.

If the board is comfortable with **94% confidence** and sees value in a 4-5% service improvement, **proceed with rollout now**.
