# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We cannot conclude that the new procedure improved service scores.** The estimated effect is positive but **not statistically significant** (p = 0.11), and the confidence interval includes zero. Importantly, the treatment branches showed minimal actual improvement (+0.7 points); the estimated "effect" partly reflects that control branches declined during the same period.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **DO NOT ROLL OUT TO ALL BRANCHES AT THIS TIME.**
>
> The evidence does not support organization-wide adoption. The observed difference is statistically indistinguishable from zero, and the treatment group's actual improvement was negligible. Before proceeding, the cooperative should either (a) extend the pilot to gather more evidence, or (b) investigate and address the factors causing control branches' scores to decline.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What Actually Happened to Scores**
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment (new procedure) | 52.8 | 53.5 | **+0.7** |
| Control (old procedure) | 47.9 | 46.7 | **−1.2** |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+1.9** |

The +1.9 point "effect" is the *difference* between these trends, not the treatment group's actual gain.

### **Statistical Evidence**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +1.9 points |
| p-value | 0.11 (not significant) |
| 95% Confidence interval | −0.5 to +4.3 points |
| Interpretation | Data are consistent with effects ranging from slightly negative to moderately positive |

### **What We Can Say**
✓ No evidence the new procedure **harmed** service scores  
✓ Pre-switch trends were parallel (supports study validity)  
✗ No statistically significant evidence the new procedure **helped**  
✗ Cannot rule out that the observed difference is due to chance

## **Technical Notes**

- Analysis uses difference-in-differences with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
- 34 branches, 17 weeks, 578 branch-week observations
- Results robust to controlling for staffing index and visits index
- Parallel trends assumption satisfied in pre-period (weeks 6–14)
