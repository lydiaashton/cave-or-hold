# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** Analysis of Weeks 6–22 Service Score Data

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

Branches that adopted the new procedure showed **significantly higher service scores** than those that did not (+1.9 points, p < 0.001). However, because the switched branches were already on a different performance trajectory before the change, **we cannot definitively attribute the full 1.9-point gain to the procedure itself.** The true effect is likely positive but smaller than the headline estimate.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Observed Performance**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Switched branches (425) | 51.59 | 52.70 | +1.11 |
| Control branches (284) | 51.24 | 50.40 | −0.84 |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+1.94** |

- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 1.40 to 2.48 points
- **Effect size:** 0.20 standard deviations (modest but meaningful)
- **Robustness:** Unchanged when controlling for staffing and visitor volume

### **Critical Limitation**

⚠️ **Pre-existing trends differ between groups.** Switched branches were already converging toward control branches before the procedure change. This means:

- The 1.94-point estimate likely **overstates** the true causal effect
- We cannot isolate how much is due to the procedure vs. ongoing convergence
- The direction of evidence still supports a **positive but uncertain** effect

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did switched branches outperform after the change? | **Yes** — clearly and significantly |
| Can we claim the procedure caused the full 1.9-point gain? | **No** — pre-trends confound the estimate |
| Is there evidence the procedure helps? | **Yes** — the weight of evidence points positive |
| Should we roll out system-wide? | **Yes, with monitored implementation** |

**Bottom line:** The procedure change is associated with improved performance and shows no negative effects. While the exact magnitude is uncertain, the risk of rollout is low and the likely benefit is real.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve system-wide rollout with the following conditions:**

1. **Set conservative expectations** — Anticipate ~1.0–1.5 point improvement, not the full 1.9
2. **Monitor for 8–12 weeks post-rollout** — Track whether gains persist across all branches
3. **Document implementation quality** — Identify which practices drive the best results
4. **Report back to the Board** — Provide updated analysis after full rollout
