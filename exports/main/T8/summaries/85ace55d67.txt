# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ APPROVE ROLLOUT — with monitoring and support**

The new procedure produces a **real, positive effect** on service scores. However, the benefit is **modest and not universal**. We recommend proceeding with system-wide adoption while implementing safeguards for branches that may not benefit.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +1.5 to +2.1 points |
| **Relative gain** | 3–4% |
| **Branches that benefit** | ~81% |
| **Branches that don't** | ~19% |
| **Statistical confidence** | High (p < 0.001) |

---

## **Evidence Summary**

### What Changed After Week 15

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches (425) | 50.7 | 54.0 | +3.3 |
| Non-switched branches (284) | 50.7 | 51.9 | +1.2 |
| **Net effect** | — | — | **+2.1** |

After adjusting for pre-existing differences between groups, the **conservative estimate is +1.5 points**.

### Key Findings

1. **Positive average effect** — Service scores improved more in switched branches than in control branches
2. **Effect is sustained** — Improvement persisted across all 8 post-switch weeks
3. **Not universal** — 19% of switched branches saw scores decline
4. **Modest magnitude** — Expect gradual improvement, not transformation

---

## **Why the Effect Varies**

The data do not yet reveal which branches will benefit most. Possible factors:

- Branch size and layout
- Staff experience and training
- Patron demographics and visit patterns
- Local implementation quality

**Recommendation:** Collect implementation data during rollout to identify success factors.

|------|------------|------------|
| Effect smaller at scale | Medium | Monitor closely; adjust training |
| Some branches see decline | Confirmed (19%) | Provide targeted support |
| Effect fades over time | Unknown | 90-day and 6-month follow-ups |
| Implementation inconsistency | Medium | Standardize training materials |

---

## **Conclusion**

The new stock rotation procedure **works on average** and should be adopted system-wide. However, it is **not a universal solution** — roughly 1 in 5 branches may not see improvement. The Board should approve rollout with the understanding that:

1. Expected gains are **modest** (~1.5–2 points)
2. **Monitoring is essential** to identify struggling branches
3. **Support resources** should be allocated for branches that need help

**This is a measured improvement, not a transformation. Proceed with realistic expectations.**
