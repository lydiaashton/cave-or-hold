### Rationale

The data show branches using the new procedure achieved higher service scores. However, because branches self-selected into switching (rather than random assignment), and because treatment branches were already improving faster before the switch, we cannot confidently attribute the full 1.8-point difference to the procedure alone.

A **phased rollout to 50-100 branches** strikes the right balance: it allows the cooperative to capture potential benefits while generating cleaner evidence before committing all 284 remaining branches.
