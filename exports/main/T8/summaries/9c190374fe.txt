# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores that is marginally statistically significant** (one-tailed p = 0.07). The estimated 1.8-point improvement represents about 4% of average scores, with no evidence of harm to any branches. The evidence is encouraging but not definitive.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Studied**
- 34 branches tracked over 17 weeks (February through June)
- 20 branches adopted the new procedure in mid-April (week 15); 14 branches kept the old procedure
- Service scores average around 50 points; higher scores indicate better service

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before Mid-April | After Mid-April | Change |
|-------|------------------|-----------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 49.3 | 51.4 | +2.1 |
| Branches with old procedure | 50.9 | 51.1 | +0.2 |
| **Estimated effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.8** |

- The new procedure is associated with a **1.8-point improvement** in service scores
- **Statistical confidence:** One-tailed p = 0.07 (two-tailed p = 0.14)
- **90% confidence interval:** -0.1 to +3.8 points
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.5 to +4.1 points

### **Why We Trust This Analysis**
- Switched and non-switched branches were on similar trajectories before the change (pre-trends test p = 0.45)
- The analysis accounts for inherent differences between branches and week-to-week variation
- Results are unchanged when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with a phased rollout, with continued monitoring.**

**Rationale:**
1. The effect is in the expected direction and close to conventional statistical significance
2. There is no evidence of negative impacts on any branches
3. A 4% improvement in service scores, if real, represents meaningful value to patrons
4. The risk of proceeding is low; the risk of delaying a beneficial change is continued suboptimal service at the 20 pilot branches

**Conditions:**
- Monitor service scores quarterly; if effects diminish or reverse, pause and reassess
- Ensure all branches receive consistent training on the new procedure
- Re-evaluate at 6 months with full system data

---

## **Alternative Path**

If the board prefers higher statistical certainty before any expansion:

**Extend the pilot 8–12 weeks** and reconvene for a definitive recommendation. This would provide sufficient data to detect the observed effect size with conventional statistical confidence.

---

*Analysis based on weekly service scores from weeks 6–22. Uses difference-in-differences methodology with branch and week fixed effects and cluster-robust standard errors.*
