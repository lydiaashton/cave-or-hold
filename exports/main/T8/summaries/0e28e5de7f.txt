# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Analysis Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks of data)  
**Branches Studied:** 150 total (90 switched to new procedure, 60 retained old procedure)

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure protected service scores from decline and should be rolled out system-wide.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure maintained their service scores much better than branches that kept the old procedure. While scores declined slightly across the system after week 14, switched branches declined far less. The new procedure delivered a **2.3-point advantage** relative to the old procedure (p < 0.001).

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. The New Procedure Mitigated Score Declines**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (new procedure) | 51.82 | 51.25 | **−0.57** |
| Non-switched branches (old procedure) | 51.93 | 49.08 | **−2.85** |
| **Benefit of new procedure** | | | **+2.28 points** |

Both groups experienced declines after week 14, suggesting system-wide factors affected all branches. **The new procedure shielded branches from approximately 80% of this decline.**

### **2. The Effect Is Statistically Reliable**

- **p < 0.001**: Less than 0.1% probability this result is due to chance
- **Effect size (Cohen's d = 0.60)**: Medium-to-large practical significance
- **Validated comparison**: Groups had nearly identical scores before the switch (51.8 vs. 51.9)

### **3. Original Expectation Was Wrong**

The team expected **no difference** in service scores. The data show a **clear, significant difference** favoring the new procedure.

### **4. Branch-Level Evidence**

- **41% of switched branches** improved or held steady vs. **27% of non-switched branches**
- Switched branches were **1.5 times more likely** to avoid decline

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### **✅ ADOPT THE NEW PROCEDURE SYSTEM-WIDE**

**Confidence Level: High**

The evidence is clear and statistically robust. The new procedure provides meaningful protection against service score declines. While it does not elevate scores above baseline, it prevents the sharper declines experienced by branches using the old procedure.

**Why this verdict:**
1. **Statistically significant benefit** (p < 0.001) — not a fluke
2. **Practically meaningful** — 2.3 points on a ~50-point scale is noticeable to patrons
3. **No identified risks** — no evidence of negative side effects
4. **Original hypothesis rejected** — we expected no difference; we found one

**What the board should expect:**
- Service scores will not dramatically improve
- But scores will be **protected from declines** that would otherwise occur
- Best viewed as **risk mitigation**, not performance enhancement

**Approve rollout to all 60 branches currently using the old procedure.**
