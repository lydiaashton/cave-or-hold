# Library Cooperative Board Summary
## New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation

---

## Bottom Line

**The new procedure shows a positive association with service scores (+2.2 points, or ~4%), but the evidence is not statistically conclusive.** We cannot rule out that the observed improvement is due to chance. The data are **encouraging but not definitive**. We recommend **proceeding cautiously**—either with a phased rollout accompanied by continued monitoring, or with additional data collection before a full commitment.

---

## Key Findings

### What the Numbers Show

| Group | Branches | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|----------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| New Procedure | 20 | 51.72 | 52.86 | +1.14 |
| Old Procedure | 14 | 53.35 | 52.40 | −0.95 |

**Estimated Effect: +2.2 points** (95% confidence interval: −0.4 to +4.8)

### What This Means

- **Best estimate:** The new procedure improved scores by about 2 points (~4% of average)
- **Uncertainty:** The true effect could plausibly be zero, slightly negative, or as high as +5 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.09 (not meeting the conventional 0.05 threshold, but suggestive)
- **Pre-trends:** Treatment and control branches had different score *levels* but similar *trends* before the switch—this supports (but doesn't prove) a causal interpretation

### Practical Significance

Even at the lower end of the confidence interval, the effect is modest. The board should weigh whether a potential 2-point improvement justifies the costs of system-wide implementation (training, disruption, ongoing operational changes).

---

## Final Verdict

**Do not approve system-wide rollout at this time.**

**Rationale:**
1. **The evidence is inconclusive.** A p-value of 0.09 means there's roughly a 1 in 10 chance the observed improvement is due to random variation—not acceptable confidence for a major operational change affecting all 34 branches.

2. **The effect size is modest.** Even if real, a 2-point improvement (~4%) may not justify full implementation costs without stronger evidence.

3. **Better data is achievable.** With 8-12 more weeks of observation or a more controlled expansion design, we could reach a definitive conclusion.

**Approved Path Forward:**
- **Authorize a phased expansion** to 5-10 additional branches
- **Require continued monitoring** with monthly reports to the board
- **Set a decision point** in 6 months with pre-specified success criteria (e.g., effect size ≥2 points, p < 0.05)
- **Budget for evaluation** — treat this as a pilot, not a final decision
