# **BOARD SUMMARY: New Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The data shows a **positive direction** (+2.2 points) but **does not confirm** the expected improvement with statistical certainty. At the standard 95% confidence level, we cannot rule out that the procedure has no effect. However, there is approximately a **95% probability the true effect is positive**, and the effect is significant at the 90% confidence level.

**Recommendation:** The evidence is suggestive but not conclusive. We advise a **measured rollout** with continued monitoring rather than full implementation or rejection.

## **Why Didn't We Get Clear Confirmation?**

1. **Only 8 weeks of post-switch data** — Effects may need more time to stabilize
2. **High week-to-week variability** — Service scores naturally fluctuate ±10 points
3. **34 branches is modest** — Limits statistical power to detect small effects

**Importantly:** Nothing in the data contradicts the hypothesis. The question is precision, not direction.

## **Final Verdict**

### **PROCEED WITH PHASED ROLLOUT**

**Rationale:**
- ✓ The effect points in the expected direction
- ✓ No evidence of negative impact on any branch
- ✓ ~95% probability the true effect is positive
- ✓ Service score improvements, even modest, compound across the cooperative

**Conditions:**
1. Roll out to **5–10 branches per quarter** (not all at once)
2. **Track weekly scores** for minimum 6 months post-implementation per branch
3. **Reconvene at Q3** to review accumulated data and confirm effect size

**If after 12 months the effect remains below +1.5 points or becomes statistically indistinguishable from zero, revisit the decision.**
