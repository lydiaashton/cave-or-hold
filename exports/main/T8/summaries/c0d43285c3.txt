# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows **statistically significant but modest improvement** (+1.6 points, ~3%). However, switching branches still score slightly below non-switching branches, and week-to-week results have been inconsistent. We recommend **continuing the pilot with extended monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide rollout. Reassess after 6 months of additional data.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. The New Procedure Had a Positive Effect

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+1.59 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | 0.23 to 2.96 points |
| Statistical significance | **Yes (p = 0.029)** |
| Relative improvement | ~3% above baseline |

### 2. Performance by Group

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** (20 branches) | 50.38 | 51.96 | **+1.57** |
| **Non-switched branches** (14 branches) | 52.30 | 52.27 | −0.03 |

### 3. Important Context

- **Switching branches started behind:** Before the change, switching branches averaged 1.9 points below non-switching branches
- **The gap narrowed but persists:** After the switch, the gap closed to only 0.3 points—switching branches still underperform
- **Inconsistent weekly results:** In 5 of 8 post-switch weeks, switching branches scored *lower* than control branches that week

### 4. What Did Not Drive the Results

- Staffing levels and visitor volume did not significantly affect service scores
- The improvement is attributable to the procedure change itself, not external factors

---

## **RECOMMENDATIONS**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Extend the pilot period** by 6 months | Current 8-week post-switch window is too short for confident rollout decisions |
| **2** | **Investigate week-to-week variation** | Inconsistent results suggest implementation quality varies across branches |
| **3** | **Document implementation practices** at top-performing switching branches | Identify what successful adoption looks like |
| **4** | **Reassess rollout decision** after Q2 2026 | Use extended data to confirm whether gains are sustained |
| **5** | **No system-wide rollout at this time** | Evidence is promising but not yet conclusive |

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Yes, modestly** — statistically significant +1.6 point gain |
| **Did switching branches catch up to non-switching branches?** | **No** — they narrowed the gap but still trail by 0.3 points |
| **Is the evidence strong enough for system-wide rollout?** | **No** — effect is real but small, inconsistent week-to-week, and based on limited post-switch data |
| **Should we abandon the new procedure?** | **No** — the improvement is real and directionally correct; worth continuing to monitor |
| **What should the Board decide today?** | **Continue the pilot, delay rollout decision** — gather 6 more months of data before committing |

---

## **ANALYTICAL NOTES**

This evaluation used a **difference-in-differences design**:

- **578 observations** across 34 branches over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 treatment branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 control branches** did not
- **Branch fixed effects** control for permanent differences between branches
- **Cluster-robust standard errors** account for correlation within branches over time
- The switch occurred in **week 15**, providing 9 weeks of pre-data and 8 weeks of post-data

**Key limitation:** The post-switch period is short (8 weeks). Longer-term effects may differ from initial results.

---

## **CONCLUSION**

The new procedure delivers a **real but modest improvement** in service quality. The evidence does not support immediate system-wide rollout, but neither does it warrant abandoning the change. The prudent course is to **continue the pilot, strengthen implementation support, and reassess with more data** before making a final rollout decision.
