# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows a positive signal but with meaningful uncertainty.** Service scores improved by approximately 2.2 points in branches that adopted the change, but the statistical evidence is borderline (p=0.037) and the confidence interval includes the possibility of minimal benefit. **We recommend a cautious, phased rollout with continued monitoring rather than immediate system-wide adoption.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Positive but Uncertain Effect**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (treatment) | 49.15 | 51.81 | **+2.66** |
| Non-switched branches (control) | 49.71 | 50.19 | **+0.48** |

**Estimated net effect: +2.18 points** (95% CI: 0.1 to 4.2; p=0.037)

### **2. Important Limitations**

- **Statistical uncertainty**: The confidence interval ranges from minimal benefit (+0.1) to moderate benefit (+4.2)
- **Standard errors not clustered**: Proper clustering by branch would likely widen the confidence interval further
- **Inconsistent weekly results**: Treatment branches outperformed control in 6 of 8 post-switch weeks, but underperformed in weeks 20-21

### **3. Valid Study Design**

- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (p=0.97), supporting the comparison
- 20 branches adopted the procedure; 14 served as controls
- Analysis controlled for branch-specific characteristics

### **4. Practical Context**

- Effect represents approximately **4% improvement** on a ~50-point scale
- Whether this justifies rollout depends on implementation costs and operational burden

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with conditional approval.**

The evidence suggests the new procedure likely provides a modest benefit, but the uncertainty is too great to justify an unconditional system-wide mandate. We recommend:

| Recommendation | Rationale |
|----------------|-----------|
| ✅ **Approve expanded pilot** | Add 5-10 branches to gather more data |
| ⚠️ **Defer full rollout** | Wait for stronger evidence before committing all branches |
| 📊 **Require follow-up analysis** | Re-evaluate after 8 weeks of expanded pilot data |
| 💰 **Track implementation costs** | Ensure benefits outweigh operational burden |

**Decision threshold**: If the expanded pilot shows a consistent effect of 1.5+ points with p<0.05, proceed to system-wide rollout. If the effect diminishes or remains statistically uncertain, reconsider the procedure or investigate implementation variations.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Pilot expansion**: Roll out to 5-10 additional branches and monitor for 8 weeks
2. **Track implementation costs**: Document staff time, training, and any operational disruptions
3. **Calculate cost-benefit**: Compare improvement value against rollout expenses
4. **Gather qualitative feedback**: Survey branch staff about the new procedure's practicality
5. **Reconvene in 60 days**: Review expanded pilot results before deciding on system-wide adoption

---

*Analysis based on weekly service scores from 34 branches over 17 weeks (weeks 6-22). The switch was implemented at week 15. Statistical analysis used difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects. Standard errors are not clustered at the branch level, which may understate uncertainty.*
