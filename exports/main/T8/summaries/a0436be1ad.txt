# **Memorandum to the Board**

## **Title: Evaluation of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted it achieved service scores **2.2 points higher** than they would have under the old procedure—a **4.4% improvement**. The evidence is strong and consistent. We recommend approving system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence
- **709 branches** participated in the pilot: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 kept the old one
- Over 17 weeks, the new procedure produced a **statistically significant improvement** in service scores
- The effect is **precisely estimated**: we are 95% confident the true effect lies between **1.6 and 2.8 points**

### What Changed
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 50.2 | 54.3 | +4.1 |
| Branches with old procedure | 49.1 | 51.0 | +1.9 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.2** |

Both groups improved over time, but branches using the new procedure improved **twice as much**.

### Why We're Confident
- The analysis accounts for branch-specific factors (location, size, typical traffic) and week-to-week fluctuations
- Branches that switched were performing similarly to those that didn't before the change
- The improvement held steady across all post-switch weeks

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **APPROVE SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Does it work?** | ✅ Yes — clear positive effect |
| **Are we sure?** | ✅ Yes — statistically significant (p < 0.001) |
| **Is the effect meaningful?** | ✅ Yes — 4.4% improvement in service quality |
| **Is there risk of harm?** | ✅ No — no negative effects detected |
| **Does it meet expectations?** | ✅ Yes — confirms the board's hypothesis |

**Rationale:** The new shelving and rotation procedure delivers a reliable, measurable improvement in service scores. With 284 branches still using the old procedure, extending the new method system-wide will raise service quality across the cooperative without identified downside risk.
