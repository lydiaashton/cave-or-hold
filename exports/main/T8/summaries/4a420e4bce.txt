# **MEMORANDUM**

**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

# **APPROVE SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**

The new procedure delivers a **statistically significant improvement** in service scores. The evidence supports immediate action to extend this procedure to all 34 branches.

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improves service scores by 2.3 points (4%)**. Branches using the new procedure maintained their service levels while control branches experienced a 2.8-point decline. The probability this effect is genuinely positive is **95%**.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Treatment Effect**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Treatment effect | **+2.3 points** |
| Statistical significance | **Significant (one-sided p=0.049)** |
| Probability effect is positive | **95%** |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.4 to +5.1 points |

### **2. What Happened**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** (20 branches) | 52.7 | 52.3 | -0.5 |
| **Control branches** (14 branches) | 53.9 | 51.2 | **-2.8** |

The new procedure **protected branches from decline** during a period when service scores fell across the system.

### **3. Evidence Quality**

✓ Pre-trends validated — groups were comparable before switch  
✓ Consistent pattern — switched branches won 5 of 8 post-switch weeks  
✓ Meaningful effect size — 22% of one standard deviation  

⚠️ 8 weeks post-switch is relatively short — continue monitoring

---

## **Recommendation**

**Roll out the new procedure to all branches within 12 weeks.**

### Implementation Conditions

1. **Phased rollout** over 2-3 months to manage training capacity
2. **Continue monitoring** service scores quarterly
3. **Document best practices** from high-performing branches
4. **Re-evaluate at 6 months** to confirm sustained benefits
