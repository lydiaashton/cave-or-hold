# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence is **inconclusive**. While branches using the new procedure showed higher service scores (+1.9 points), this difference is **not statistically significant** and could be due to random variation. We cannot confidently say the new procedure improves service. We recommend **extending the pilot** to gather more data before making a rollout decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15 (treatment group)
- **14 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)

### **Results**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated treatment effect** | +1.89 service score points |
| **Percentage improvement** | 3.7% above baseline |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.107 (not significant) |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | -0.41 to +4.19 points |

### **What This Means**
- The observed improvement **could be real** (up to +4.2 points)
- The observed improvement **could be zero or slightly negative** (down to -0.4 points)
- We would need approximately **50% more data** (8-10 additional weeks) to reach a definitive conclusion
- **No evidence of harm**: The new procedure did not reduce service scores at any branch

### **Supporting Evidence**
- **Parallel pre-trends confirmed**: Treatment and control branches had similar trends before the switch, validating our comparison
- **Consistent direction**: 15 of 20 treatment branches showed score improvements after switching

---

## **Final Verdict**

**DO NOT ROLLOUT SYSTEM-WIDE AT THIS TIME**

**Recommended Action: Extend Pilot with Decision Deadline**

| Option | Recommendation | Rationale |
|--------|---------------|-----------|
| **Immediate system-wide rollout** | ❌ Not recommended | Evidence too weak; risk of implementing an ineffective procedure across all branches |
| **Abandon the new procedure** | ❌ Not recommended | No evidence of harm; potential benefits remain plausible |
| **Extend pilot 8-12 weeks** | ✅ **Recommended** | Balances opportunity for improvement against uncertainty; achieves statistical clarity |

**Decision Timeline:**
- **Week 8-10 of extension**: Interim analysis check
- **Week 12 of extension**: Final analysis and board decision
- **Go-forward criteria**: Statistically significant positive effect (p < 0.05) OR strong evidence of cost-effectiveness despite marginal significance

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Extend the pilot** by 8-12 weeks to achieve statistical clarity

2. **Track implementation costs** alongside service scores to assess cost-effectiveness

3. **Analyze branch heterogeneity**: Identify whether certain branch types (size, volume, staffing) benefit more

4. **Set decision deadline**: Board to vote on system-wide rollout by end of Q2

- Standard errors clustered at branch level
- Controls for staffing index and visits index included
- Baseline service score: 50.4 points (higher is better)
