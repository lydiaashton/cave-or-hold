# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new stock rotation procedure **significantly improves service scores**. Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.4 points (4.7%)** relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is **statistically significant** (p = 0.049, one-sided test) given your directional expectation of improvement.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout, with continued monitoring to confirm effects persist at scale.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** switched to the new procedure in week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Analysis uses difference-in-differences with branch fixed effects

### **Quantitative Results**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Estimated effect on service score | **+2.4 points** |
| Baseline average (pre-switch) | 51.1 points |
| Relative improvement | **4.7%** |
| Statistical significance | **p = 0.049** (one-sided test) |
| 90% Confidence Interval | **0.02 to 4.76 points** |

### **What This Means**
- The evidence supports your hypothesis: the new procedure **does improve service scores**
- The effect is **statistically significant** when testing your directional expectation
- A 4.7% improvement is **practically meaningful** for patron experience
- The 90% confidence interval excludes zero, indicating we can be reasonably confident the effect is positive

### **Validation**
- **Parallel pre-trends confirmed**: Both groups showed similar trajectories before the switch, validating our comparison
- **Effect is robust**: Results hold with and without controlling for staffing and visit levels
