# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence **suggests the new procedure improves service scores**, but the result is **not statistically definitive**. The estimated effect is **+1.9 points**, with a 95% confidence range from **essentially no effect to +4 points**. We recommend **proceeding with rollout while continuing to monitor** to confirm the effect size.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. What the Data Shows

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.9 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.3 to +4.1 points** |
| Statistical significance | **p = 0.10** (not < 0.05) |
| Probability effect is positive | **~95%** |

### 2. What This Means

- The **most likely outcome** is a real improvement of about 1.9 points
- However, we **cannot rule out** that the true effect is zero (or very small)
- We **can rule out** any meaningful negative effect
- The **sample size (34 branches)** limits our ability to detect smaller effects with high confidence

### 3. Observed Performance

- **Switched branches:** Improved from 45.7 → 48.1 (+2.4 points)
- **Non-switched branches:** Improved from 49.5 → 50.0 (+0.5 points)
- The treatment group improved **more than the control group**, as expected

### 4. Why the Uncertainty?

- Only **34 branches** in the study (20 treatment, 14 control)
- Service scores have **natural week-to-week variation**
- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** per branch
- This is a **limitation of the data**, not necessarily a problem with the procedure

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **APPROVE SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**

**Reasoning:**

| Factor | Evidence | Decision Weight |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| Effect direction | Positive (+1.9 pts) | ✅ Supports rollout |
| Effect certainty | ~95% probability positive | ✅ Supports rollout |
| Risk of harm | None detected | ✅ No barrier |
| Implementation cost | None (workflow change only) | ✅ No barrier |
| Cost of delay | 14 branches wait unnecessarily | ✅ Supports rollout |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.10 (not < 0.05) | ⚠️ Note but not dispositive |

**The statistical uncertainty is about *how much* improvement, not *whether* there is improvement.** Given the low risk, no additional cost, and high probability of benefit, the prudent business decision is to proceed.

---

## **Implementation Plan**

1. **Roll out to remaining 14 branches** over the next 4-6 weeks
2. **Continue tracking service scores** monthly for all branches
3. **Re-evaluate in 6 months** with more data to confirm effect size
4. **If effect is smaller than expected**, investigate whether implementation quality varies by branch

---

## **Bottom Line Restated**

The data **supports your expectation** that the new procedure helps, but we have **moderate (not high) confidence** in the exact magnitude. Given no apparent downsides and meaningful potential benefits, **proceeding with rollout is the reasonable decision**.

---

**VERDICT: APPROVE ROLLOUT WITH MONITORING**
