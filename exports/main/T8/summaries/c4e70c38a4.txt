# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests the new procedure improved service scores, but we cannot confirm this with high statistical confidence.** The best estimate is a **2-point improvement**, and there is approximately a **96% probability the true effect is positive**. However, at conventional scientific standards (95% confidence), we cannot definitively rule out that the observed improvement could be due to chance. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but continue monitoring to confirm the effect with additional data.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Likely yes, but not confirmed** |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes** |
| **Confidence level** | **Moderate (90%, not 95%)** |
| **Risk of rollout** | **Low** (no evidence of harm) |
| **Risk of delay** | **Moderate** (foregoing potential benefits) |

**Decision:** The balance of evidence favors rollout. The procedure shows a positive effect with no detected downsides. While additional data would increase confidence, waiting is not necessary given the low risk and potential benefit to patrons.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Estimated Effect of the New Procedure**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Best estimate of improvement | **+2.1 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.3 to +4.6 points** |
| 90% Confidence Interval | **+0.1 to +4.2 points** |
| Probability effect is positive | **~96%** |

The confidence interval tells us the true effect could reasonably be as low as essentially zero (-0.3) or as high as meaningfully positive (+4.6).

### **What the Data Shows**

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Branches with **old** procedure | 46.2 | 45.6 | -0.6 |
| Branches with **new** procedure | 49.6 | 51.0 | +1.5 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.1** |

### **Why We're Not Fully Certain**

1. **Limited data**: Only 8 weeks of post-switch observations across 20 branches
2. **Natural variation**: Service scores fluctuate week-to-week for many reasons
3. **p-value of 0.088**: Above the 0.05 threshold commonly used in research

### **Reasons for Confidence**

1. **Effect is in the expected direction**: We hypothesized improvement, and the data shows improvement
2. **Pre-trends were parallel**: Before the switch, both groups moved together, validating the comparison
3. **High probability of positive effect**: ~96% chance the true effect is not zero or negative

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with rollout to all branches, with continued monitoring.**

**Rationale:**
- The potential benefit (~2 points) is meaningful for our service quality goals
- There is no evidence of harm to any branches
- The cost of delaying rollout while gathering more data likely exceeds the risk
- If the effect is real, earlier rollout delivers value sooner; if not, we can adjust after collecting more data
