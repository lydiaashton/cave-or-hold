# Memorandum to the Board

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 2026

---

## Bottom Line

The new procedure **likely improves service scores** by approximately **1.6 points (~3%)**, with **93% confidence the effect is positive**. While not statistically definitive by conventional standards, the evidence supports proceeding with a **phased rollout** while continuing to monitor results.

---

## Key Findings

### What the Data Shows

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect on service score | **+1.6 points** |
| Direction of effect | **Positive** (as expected) |
| Confidence effect is positive | **93%** |
| 95% confidence interval | **-0.5 to +3.8 points** |
| Baseline average score | 51.1 |

### Interpretation

1. **The effect is in the expected direction.** All evidence points toward improvement, consistent with our hypothesis.

2. **Statistical certainty is moderate, not definitive.** A conventional two-sided test yields p = 0.14, but our one-sided hypothesis (will it improve?) yields p = 0.07. This is **suggestive but not conclusive** evidence.

3. **The practical impact is meaningful.** A 3% improvement in service scores, if real, represents tangible value across the cooperative.

4. **The main risk is overestimation, not harm.** The lower bound of the confidence interval suggests the effect could be near zero, but there is no evidence of negative impact.

## Recommendations

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|

## Bottom Line for Decision-Making

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the procedure improve scores? | **Probably yes** (93% confidence) |
| Are we certain? | **No**—some uncertainty remains |

## Final Verdict

**APPROVE PHASED ROLLOUT**

The evidence, while not definitive, is sufficiently supportive to warrant implementation. The procedure shows a positive effect in the expected direction, with no signals of harm. Given the low risk and plausible benefit to member service, we recommend proceeding with rollout to remaining branches while maintaining data collection to confirm the effect at scale.
