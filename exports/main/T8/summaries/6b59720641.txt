The new procedure is associated with a **2-point improvement** in service scores (4% relative to baseline). The evidence is **borderline statistically significant** (one-sided p = 0.056) and consistent across all analytical approaches. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with system-wide rollout, with continued monitoring.

## **Key Findings**

### **Results Summary**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (n=20) | 45.69 | 48.99 | +3.30 |
| Non-switched branches (n=14) | 47.31 | 48.54 | +1.23 |
| **Estimated Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.08** |

### **Statistical Assessment**
- **Point estimate:** +2.08 points (4.4% improvement)
- **95% Confidence Interval:** -0.55 to +4.65 points
- **One-sided p-value:** 0.056 (testing for improvement only)
- **Two-sided p-value:** 0.11

### **Why We Recommend Rollout**
1. **Direction is clear:** All analytical approaches showed improvement in the expected direction
2. **Pre-trends validate the design:** Both groups tracked similarly before the switch
3. **Study was short:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data — effects may strengthen with time
4. **Low downside risk:** The procedure involves shelving/rotation changes, not fundamental service reductions
