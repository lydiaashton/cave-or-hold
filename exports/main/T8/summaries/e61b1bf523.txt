# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **improved service scores by approximately 2 points** on a scale where the average branch scores about 50. This improvement is **statistically significant** and contradicts our prior expectation that the change would have no effect. We recommend **proceeding with a full rollout** to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **150 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **60 branches** maintained the old procedure as a control group
- Analysis compares service score changes before and after the switch between the two groups

### **Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches with new procedure | 49.89 | 51.45 | +1.56 |
| Branches with old procedure | 50.83 | 50.34 | −0.49 |
| **Estimated effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.05** |

- **Effect size:** +2.05 points (95% confidence interval: +0.78 to +3.31)
- **Statistical confidence:** p = 0.002 (less than 1% probability this result occurred by chance)
- **Practical significance:** Represents approximately a 4% improvement relative to the baseline average score of 50

### **Validation Checks**
- **Pre-trend patterns:** Before week 15, both groups followed similar weekly patterns, supporting the validity of the comparison
- **Alternative factors:** The improvement is not explained by changes in staffing levels or branch visit volumes

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Does the new procedure affect service scores?** | **Yes** — statistically significant positive effect |
| **Magnitude of effect** | +2.05 points (95% CI: +0.78 to +3.31) |
| **Confidence in finding** | High (p = 0.002) |
| **Consistency with expectations** | No — we expected no difference |
| **Recommendation** | **PROCEED WITH FULL ROLLOUT** |

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Approve full rollout** of the new procedure to all 150 branches | Clear evidence of positive impact on service quality |
| **2** | **Document implementation standards** to ensure consistency | Maintains effect size across all locations |
| **3** | **Monitor scores during rollout** (weeks 1–8 post-implementation) | Confirms effect holds at scale; identifies branches needing support |
| **4** | **Conduct follow-up with branch managers** to understand why scores improved | Unexpected positive effect may reveal additional improvement opportunities |

---

## **Caveats**

- Analysis covers an 8-week post-implementation period; longer-term effects remain to be observed
- Service scores are one metric among many; continue monitoring operational efficiency and staff feedback
- Effect size may vary by branch characteristics (size, location, staffing levels)
