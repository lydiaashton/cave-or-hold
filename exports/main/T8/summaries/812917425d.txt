# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We cannot confidently conclude the new procedure improved service scores.** The data show a positive trend (+1.9 points), but the evidence is **not statistically significant** at conventional standards. There is approximately a **10% chance** this result could occur even if the procedure had no real effect. We recommend **collecting more data before deciding on a full rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. What the Numbers Show

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.9 points** | Direction matches expectation |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.3 to +4.1** | Includes zero (no effect) |
| P-value | **0.098** | Not statistically significant |
| Statistical threshold | 0.05 | Our result does not meet this |

### 2. What This Means

- **We observed** switching branches improving relative to non-switching branches
- **However**, we cannot rule out that this was due to random variation
- **The data are consistent with**:
  - A modest positive effect (up to +4 points)
  - No effect at all
  - A very small negative effect (down to -0.3 points)

### 3. Study Limitations

| Limitation | Impact |
|------------|--------|
| Only 8 weeks post-switch | May be too short to detect stable effects |
| 34 branches total | Moderate statistical power |
| Week-to-week score variability | Makes detecting effects harder |

### 4. What We Can Say With Confidence

✓ The procedure did **not harm** service scores (lower bound of CI is near zero)  
✓ The effect direction matches our hypothesis  
✓ The magnitude, if real, would be modest (~4% improvement)  

✗ We **cannot confirm** the procedure caused the observed improvement  
✗ We **cannot rule out** that the result was due to chance  

---

## **Recommendations**

### Option 1: Gather More Evidence (Recommended)

**Rationale:** The current data are suggestive but inconclusive. More data would provide a clearer answer.

**Actions:**
- Continue the current study design for 12-16 additional weeks
- This would approximately double the post-switch observations
- Re-analyze with the extended dataset before making a rollout decision

**Timeline:** Decision deferred by ~4 months

### Option 2: Proceed with Cautious Rollout

**Rationale:** The effect is in the expected direction, no harm was detected, and 20 branches have already implemented successfully.

**Actions:**
- Roll out to remaining branches in phases
- Continue rigorous monitoring
- Be prepared to adjust or reverse if later data contradict initial findings

**Risk:** May invest resources in a procedure whose effectiveness is unproven

### Option 3: Do Not Roll Out

**Rationale:** The evidence does not meet conventional standards for concluding the procedure works.

**Actions:**
- Maintain current mixed approach
- Consider redesigning the procedure or testing alternative improvements

**Risk:** May forgo a genuinely beneficial improvement

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **DO NOT PROCEED WITH FULL ROLLOUT AT THIS TIME**

**Reasoning:**

1. **The evidence is inconclusive.** A p-value of 0.098 means we cannot reject the possibility that the observed improvement was due to random chance. Making a system-wide investment on this basis would be premature.

2. **The cost of waiting is low.** Extending data collection by 12-16 weeks requires minimal additional resources but would substantially improve our ability to detect a real effect.

3. **The risk of a false positive is meaningful.** If we roll out based on this evidence and the procedure has no real effect, we would incur implementation costs across all branches without achieving the intended benefit.

4. **No urgency requires immediate action.** Twenty branches are already operating under the new procedure without apparent problems. There is no operational crisis demanding an immediate decision.

**What We Recommend Instead:**

| Action | Timeline | Responsible Party |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| Continue monitoring all 34 branches | Weeks 23-38 | Operations Team |
| Interim analysis at Week 30 | Week 31 | Data Analysis Team |
| Final analysis and recommendation | Week 39 | Data Analysis Team |
| Board decision on rollout | Week 40+ | Board of Directors |

**Success Criteria for Rollout:**
- Effect size remains positive (+1.5 points or higher)
- Statistical significance achieved (p < 0.05)
- No adverse effects detected at any branch

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure **may** improve service scores, but the current data do not provide sufficient evidence to confirm this. The board faces a choice between acting on suggestive evidence or waiting for more definitive results. Our professional judgment is that **additional data collection is the prudent course.** We will return with a final recommendation after 12-16 additional weeks of observation.
