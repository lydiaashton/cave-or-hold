# **Library Cooperative Board Memorandum (Final)**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2025  
**Data Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks)  
**Branches Analyzed:** 34 (20 treatment, 14 control)

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence for the new procedure's effectiveness is **inconclusive at standard confidence levels**. The estimated improvement of 2.4 points has a p-value of 0.10, which does not meet the conventional threshold for statistical significance (p < 0.05). The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.5 to +5.3 points, meaning the true effect could be zero, slightly negative, or moderately positive.

**Recommendation:** **Do not proceed with full rollout yet.** Instead, collect additional data (8–12 more weeks) to reduce uncertainty. The potential benefit is meaningful, but the current evidence is insufficient to justify a system-wide change with confidence.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **NOT YET APPROVED FOR SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**
>
> The analysis shows a **promising signal but insufficient proof**. The new procedure may improve service scores by approximately 5%, but the data cannot confirm this with acceptable certainty. Approving a full rollout now would be premature.
>
> **The Board should:**
> 1. **Delay system-wide implementation** until statistical evidence strengthens
> 2. **Authorize continued data collection** for all 34 branches (minimum 8–12 additional weeks)
> 3. **Schedule a follow-up decision** for Q1 2026 with updated analysis
> 4. **Optionally permit limited expansion** (5–7 additional branches) only if operational needs require, with explicit commitment to evaluate results
>
> **Bottom line:** The new procedure is **neither proven effective nor ruled out**. More evidence is needed before committing cooperative-wide resources.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Results Summary

| Group | Before (Weeks 6–14) | After (Weeks 15–22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Old Procedure (14 branches) | 48.3 | 47.6 | −0.7 |
| New Procedure (20 branches) | 47.8 | 49.5 | +1.7 |

**Estimated Treatment Effect: +2.4 points**

### Statistical Assessment

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +2.4 points | Suggests improvement |
| p-value | 0.100 | Not significant at 0.05 level |
| 95% CI | -0.5 to +5.3 | Includes zero; effect uncertain |
| 90% CI | 0.0 to +4.9 | Barely excludes zero |
| Effect size | ~5% of baseline | Operationally meaningful if real |

### What the Data Shows

✓ **Direction aligns with expectations** — Treatment branches improved relative to control  
✓ **Pre-trends are parallel** — Validates the research design (p = 0.67)  
✓ **No red flags in methodology** — Analysis is sound  

⚠️ **Statistical uncertainty is high** — Cannot reject the possibility of no effect  
⚠️ **Limited post-switch data** — Only 8 weeks after implementation  
⚠️ **Confidence interval is wide** — True effect could range from negative to substantially positive

## **Why More Data Matters**

With approximately 12 additional weeks of observations:
- Standard errors would decrease by roughly 30–40%
- The 95% confidence interval would narrow from ~6 points to ~4 points
- A true effect of 2.4 points would likely achieve statistical significance if it persists
