# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure protected service quality.** While service scores declined across the cooperative after week 15, branches using the new procedure held steady while those using the old procedure dropped significantly. The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure to prevent future deterioration.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ ROLL OUT THE NEW PROCEDURE TO ALL BRANCHES**

The data provide clear, statistically significant evidence that the new rotation and shelving procedure helps branches maintain service quality during challenging periods. Keeping the old procedure system-wide would have resulted in approximately 2 points of service score decline across all 150 branches. The new procedure is the safer choice for protecting patron experience.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| **New Procedure (90 branches)** | 50.54 | 50.37 | -0.17 |
| **Old Procedure (60 branches)** | 49.85 | 47.79 | -2.06 |

**The new procedure prevented approximately 2 points of decline** (statistically significant, p < 0.01)

### **What This Means**

Something affected the entire cooperative after week 15—possibly seasonal factors, staffing challenges, or increased demand. The critical finding:

- **New procedure branches:** Essentially maintained their scores (minimal change)
- **Old procedure branches:** Lost meaningful ground (-2.06 points)

The new procedure appears to provide **operational resilience** during challenging periods.

### **Why This Matters**

If we had rolled out the new procedure to all 150 branches, the analysis suggests we would have avoided the service quality decline that affected the 60 control branches. Going forward, the new procedure should help all branches maintain stability during similar pressures.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure helps branches maintain service quality when external pressures arise.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve rollout** to remaining 60 branches

2. **Develop implementation plan** with training and transition support

3. **Monitor outcomes** for one quarter to confirm effects persist

4. **Investigate the week 15+ decline** to understand what affected the cooperative

5. **Schedule follow-up review** in 6 months
