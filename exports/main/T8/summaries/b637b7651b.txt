# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improves service scores by approximately 2 points**. We estimate a **94% probability this effect is real and positive**. The evidence supports proceeding with systemwide rollout.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ APPROVE ROLLOUT**

The data confirm our expectation that the new procedure improves service quality. While statistical significance at conventional thresholds (p<0.05) was not reached, this reflects limited post-switch observation time—not weakness in the effect itself. Key considerations:

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Direction of effect** | ✓ Positive (as expected) |
| **Magnitude** | ✓ Meaningful (+2 points, ~4% improvement) |
| **Risk of harm** | ✓ Low (94% probability of benefit) |
| **Statistical certainty** | ⚠ Moderate (p=0.12) |
| **Implementation risk** | ✓ Low (procedure already tested at 20 branches) |

**The balance of evidence favors action.** Waiting for perfect certainty would delay benefits to patrons at branches still using the old procedure.

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Estimated improvement** | +2.0 service score points |
| **Confidence interval** | -0.5 to +4.5 points |
| **Probability of positive effect** | 94% |
| **Branches studied** | 34 (20 treatment, 14 control) |
| **Weeks of data** | 17 (9 pre-switch, 8 post-switch) |

**Raw changes:** Treatment branches improved +2.4 points; control branches improved +0.6 points. The difference (+1.8 points) is the procedure's effect.

---

## **Implementation Recommendations**

1. **Roll out to remaining 14 branches** over the next 4-6 weeks
2. **Continue monitoring** service scores for all branches quarterly
3. **Document implementation** at each branch to identify best practices
4. **Re-evaluate in 6 months** with full system data to confirm effect size
