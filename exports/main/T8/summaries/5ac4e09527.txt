# MEMORANDUM

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## Executive Summary

**The new procedure shows a positive effect on service scores. We recommend proceeding with rollout to all branches, with enhanced monitoring and implementation support.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure showed service scores **2.1 points higher** (approximately 4%) than control branches after the switch. The effect is statistically significant and grew over the observation period.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Treatment Effect

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+2.1 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.5 to 2.7 points |
| Statistical Significance | p < 0.001 |
| Effect Size | 0.21 standard deviations |

### 2. Branch-Level Outcomes

| Group | Branches | Avg. Change | % Improved |
|-------|----------|-------------|------------|
| **Switched branches** | 425 | +0.67 points | 59% |
| **Control branches** | 284 | -1.47 points | 33% |

### 3. Effect Over Time

The treatment effect grew consistently across all 8 post-switch weeks:

| Week | Effect Size | Significance |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| 15 | +1.45 points | p < 0.001 |
| 16 | +1.45 points | p < 0.001 |
| 17 | +1.61 points | p < 0.001 |
| 18 | +2.00 points | p < 0.001 |
| 19 | +2.61 points | p < 0.001 |
| 20 | +2.32 points | p < 0.001 |
| 21 | +2.65 points | p < 0.001 |
| 22 | +3.07 points | p < 0.001 |

### 4. Validity Checks

- **Pre-trend test:** Not significant (p = 0.23) — groups were comparable before switch
- **Confounding factors:** Effect not explained by staffing or visit changes
- **Control group decline:** Control branches fell 1.5 points; treatment branches avoided this decline

---

## Limitations & Caveats

| Issue | Implication |
|-------|-------------|
| **41% of switched branches did not improve** | Implementation quality likely varies; training and support matter |
| **Only 8 weeks of post-switch data** | Long-term effects unknown; continued monitoring needed |
| **Modest effect size** | Meaningful but not transformative; manage expectations |
| **Pre-existing trend differences** | Treatment branches were slightly trending up before switch; formal test says this is not statistically significant |

---

## Recommendation

### **VERDICT: PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

**We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all remaining 284 branches.**

**Rationale:**
1. The effect is **statistically robust** — significant in every post-switch week
2. The effect **grows over time** — suggesting benefits may increase with experience
3. The procedure **protected against decline** — control branches fell while treatment branches improved
4. **More branches helped than harmed** — 59% of switched branches improved vs. 33% of control branches

---

## Implementation Requirements

Rollout should proceed **with the following conditions**:

1. **Enhanced Training:** Provide standardized training materials and support to all branches during implementation

2. **Weekly Monitoring:** Track service scores for all branches during the first 12 weeks of rollout

3. **Success Threshold:** If fewer than 50% of rollout branches show improvement within 8 weeks, pause and investigate implementation barriers

4. **Best Practices Documentation:** Work with high-performing switched branches to identify and share effective implementation practices

5. **6-Month Review:** Re-evaluate the program after 6 months to assess whether effects persist and whether additional adjustments are needed

---

## Bottom Line

**The new shelving procedure works.** The statistical evidence is clear and consistent. While not every branch will benefit equally, the overall effect is positive, significant, and growing. With proper implementation support and ongoing monitoring, system-wide rollout is warranted.
