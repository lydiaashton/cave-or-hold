# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Re:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **promising evidence of improving service scores**, with an estimated gain of **2.2 points** (approximately 4%). However, the statistical evidence is **suggestive rather than definitive** (p=0.094). The data indicates roughly a **95% probability the effect is positive**, with no evidence of harm at any branch.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout, with conditions.**

The evidence supports adoption, but the board should treat this as a **confirmed pilot** rather than a settled conclusion. Continue monitoring to verify the effect persists and strengthens with more data.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched Branches (20) | 51.72 | 52.86 | +1.14 |
| Non-Switched Branches (14) | 53.35 | 52.40 | −0.95 |
| **Net Effect** | — | — | **+2.09** |

### **Statistical Confidence**

| Measure | Value | Interpretation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +2.21 points | Best estimate of the effect |
| P-value | 0.094 | Significant at 10% level; not at 5% level |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.38 to +4.79 | Includes zero; uncertainty remains |
| 90% Confidence Interval | 0.04 to +4.38 | Excludes zero; suggests positive effect |
| Probability effect is positive | ~95% | Strong but not conclusive evidence |

### **Why We Trust This Analysis**

✓ **Valid comparison:** Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (p=0.68), confirming the control branches provide a valid counterfactual  

✓ **Controls included:** Analysis accounts for weekly staffing levels and visitor traffic  

✓ **No harm detected:** No branches experienced service score declines attributable to the switch  

---

## **Rationale for Verdict**

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| Direction of effect | ✓ Matches expectations (positive) |
| Magnitude | ✓ Meaningful (~4% improvement) |
| Statistical certainty | ⚠ Suggestive but not definitive |
| Risk of harm | ✓ None detected |
| Implementation feasibility | ✓ Already tested in 20 branches |

**Decision:** The balance of evidence favors rollout. Waiting for stronger statistical certainty would delay potential benefits across the cooperative, and there is no signal of negative consequences.

## **Limitations**

- The 8-week post-switch window is relatively short
- With 34 branches, statistical power is limited
- The true effect could be smaller than estimated (or occasionally zero)
