# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Title: Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2026  
**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.3 points** (approximately **4.5% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant, methodologically robust, and showed no signs of diminishing over the 8-week observation period.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Study Overview
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total branches studied | 709 |
| Branches using new procedure | 425 |
| Branches keeping old procedure | 284 |
| Weeks of data | 17 (Weeks 6–22) |
| Switch date | Week 15 |

### 2. Impact on Service Scores

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 52.53 | 53.21 | +0.68 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 51.83 | 54.86 | +3.02 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.34** |

- **Statistical confidence:** 95% confidence interval of 1.75 to 2.93 points
- **Significance level:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Percentage improvement:** 4.5% relative to baseline
- **Effect size:** 23% of one standard deviation (moderate and meaningful)

### 3. Analysis Validity

| Test | Result | Interpretation |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Parallel pre-trends | ✓ Passed (p = 0.99) | Groups were trending similarly before switch |
| Statistical significance | ✓ p < 0.001 | Effect is highly unlikely to be due to chance |
| Robustness to controls | ✓ Confirmed | Effect holds after adjusting for staffing and visits |
| Effect stability | ✓ Confirmed | Improvement persisted across all 8 post-switch weeks |

### 4. Important Context

- Branches that switched were scoring slightly **lower** than control branches before the change (−0.7 points), making the post-switch improvement more notable
- The new procedure appears to have helped switched branches not only catch up but surpass the control group
- No negative effects or unintended consequences were detected in the data

---

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|

**VERDICT: The evidence supports proceeding with system-wide rollout, contingent on reasonable implementation costs.**

The analysis demonstrates a real, measurable improvement in service quality with no identified drawbacks. Unless implementation costs are prohibitive, the cooperative should move forward with adopting the new procedure across all branches.

This evaluation provides strong evidence that the new stock rotation and shelving procedure improves branch service quality. The 2.3-point gain is statistically robust, practically meaningful, and sustained over time. Barring unexpectedly high implementation costs, we recommend the board approve system-wide adoption.
