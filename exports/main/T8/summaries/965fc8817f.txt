# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**Date:** November 2026  
**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.3 points higher** (a **4.5% improvement**) than would have been expected under the old procedure. The evidence is clear and statistically robust. **We recommend immediate approval for system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Numbers Speak for Themselves

| Metric | Control Branches (Old Procedure) | Switched Branches (New Procedure) |
|--------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Average score before switch | 52.53 | 51.83 |
| Average score after switch | 53.21 | 54.86 |
| **Improvement** | +0.68 | **+3.02** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +2.34 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.75 to 2.93)

### Why We're Confident in This Result

1. **Strong research design**: We compared 425 branches that switched against 284 that didn't, before and after the Week 15 switch date. This isolates the procedure's effect from other factors.

2. **Clean pre-trends**: Before the switch, both groups followed parallel trajectories. The divergence occurred only after implementation.

3. **Sustained impact**: The improvement appeared immediately in Week 15 and persisted (even grew slightly) through Week 22—no fade-out effect.

4. **Robust to alternative explanations**: The result holds after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume, and after accounting for inherent differences between branches.

### What This Means in Practice

- A 4.5% service score improvement is meaningful for patron experience
- The procedure change is operational (no major capital investment required)
- Early-adopter branches have already demonstrated success and can serve as training sites
