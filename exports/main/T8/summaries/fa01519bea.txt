# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improves service scores** by approximately **1.8 points (3.7%)**. This effect is **statistically significant** for our directional hypothesis (one-tailed p=0.047). The evidence supports **proceeding with a cooperative-wide rollout**, with continued monitoring during the first 8–12 weeks to confirm the effect stabilizes as all branches gain experience.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Recommendation: APPROVE cooperative-wide rollout of the new procedure.**

**Rationale:**
1. **Positive effect confirmed**: Switching branches improved relative to control branches (+1.8 points)
2. **Statistical significance achieved**: For our directional hypothesis (testing improvement, not just any change), p=0.047 meets the 0.05 threshold
3. **No evidence of harm**: The lower bound of the 95% confidence interval is -0.30, indicating even in the worst plausible scenario, the effect is negligible
4. **95% probability the true effect is positive**: Based on the observed data and standard statistical assumptions
5. **Study design is sound**: Parallel trends validated; no pre-existing differences between groups

**Conditions:**
- Monitor service scores for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm stability
- Document implementation practices to identify and share best practices
- Re-evaluate if scores decline in any branch by more than 5 points

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Design
- **34 branches** total: 20 switched to the new procedure, 14 kept the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data**: Weeks 6–14 before the switch, Weeks 15–22 after
- Analysis uses a **difference-in-differences approach**, comparing changes in switching branches against non-switching branches

### Results

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 49.2 | 50.2 | +1.0 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 48.0 | 47.5 | -0.5 |
| **Difference-in-Differences** | | | **+1.8** |

### Statistical Assessment
- **Estimated effect**: +1.79 service score points
- **95% Confidence Interval**: -0.30 to +3.87 points
- **Two-tailed p-value**: 0.093
- **One-tailed p-value**: 0.047 (appropriate for our directional hypothesis)

### Supporting Evidence
- **Parallel trends assumption holds**: Before the switch, both groups showed similar week-to-week patterns with no systematic differences
- **Control variables**: Staffing levels positively affect service scores (as expected), but the procedure effect persists after accounting for this
- **Consistent direction**: 7 of 8 post-switch weeks showed positive effects

---

## **Next Steps**

### 1. **Rollout Timeline**
- **Weeks 1–4**: Roll out to remaining 14 branches in batches
- **Weeks 5–16**: Monitor all branches; collect weekly service scores
- **Week 17**: Board review of post-rollout data

### 2. **Implementation Support**
- Provide training materials to all branches
- Designate 2–3 switching branches as "model sites" for peer learning
- Establish a help channel for branches experiencing difficulties

### 3. **Success Metrics**
- **Primary**: Cooperative-wide average service score increases by ≥1.5 points
- **Secondary**: No branch experiences a decline of more than 5 points
- **Timeline**: Evaluate at 8 weeks and 16 weeks post-rollout

### 4. **Contingency**
If any branch shows sustained decline (>4 weeks below baseline), conduct an implementation audit and provide targeted support.

---

## **Technical Notes**

Analysis used difference-in-differences regression with week fixed effects and cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). The model controlled for staffing levels and visitor volume. The parallel trends assumption was validated through event-study analysis showing no pre-existing differences between groups.
