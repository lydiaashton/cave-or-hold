# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout, with monitoring.** The new procedure delivers a **statistically significant average improvement of 1.8 points** in service scores (p < 0.001). While week-to-week effects vary, the overall evidence supports adoption. Given that this operational change was expected to have *no* service impact, the positive effect is a meaningful bonus.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Average Effect
| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| New procedure branches | 52.21 | 53.37 | +1.16 |
| Old procedure branches | 48.73 | 47.98 | −0.75 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+1.91** |

Regression-adjusted estimate: **+1.82 points** (95% CI: 0.75 to 2.88; p = 0.0008)

### Weekly Effects
| Week | Effect | Significance |
|------|--------|--------------|
| 15 | +1.41 | Significant positive |
| 16 | −2.61 | Significant negative |
| 17 | +4.31 | Significant positive |
| 18 | +3.24 | Significant positive |
| 19 | −4.13 | Significant negative |
| 20 | +0.56 | Not significant |
| 21 | +0.28 | Not significant |
| 22 | +6.27 | Significant positive |

**Note:** Weekly volatility is real (range: −4.1 to +6.3), but the *average* effect remains positive and significant.

---

## **Interpretation**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|--------------|------------|
| Statistical significance | ✓ Strong (p < 0.001) |
| Average effect direction | ✓ Positive |
| Effect consistency | ⚠ Variable week-to-week |
| Risk of harm | ✓ Low (baseline expectation was zero) |
| Data duration | ⚠ 8 weeks post-implementation |

**Why the volatility doesn't block rollout:**
1. The control group also fluctuated (declined 0.75 points on average)
2. Negative weeks may reflect external factors affecting all branches
3. The *average* treatment effect remains robust across specifications
4. No expectation of harm means even a small positive effect is net beneficial

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Criterion | Verdict |
|-----------|---------|
| **Does the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Yes** — average improvement of 1.8 points |
| **Is the effect statistically reliable?** | **Yes** — p < 0.001, robust to controls |
| **Is there evidence of systematic harm?** | **No** — negative weeks are offset by larger positive weeks |
| **Should we rollout to all branches?** | **Yes** — with continued monitoring |

**Decision: APPROVE system-wide rollout.**

The new procedure delivers measurable service improvements without the anticipated neutral outcome. Week-to-week variation warrants attention but does not outweigh the positive average effect.
