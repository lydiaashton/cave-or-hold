# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Office  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure provides meaningful protection against service score declines.** While scores dropped system-wide after week 14, branches using the new procedure declined only **0.8 points** compared to **3.0 points** for branches using the old procedure. The net benefit is **+2.1 points** relative to what would have occurred otherwise. We recommend rollout, with realistic expectations that the procedure mitigates declines rather than driving absolute gains.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure change service scores?** | **Yes** — statistically significant effect (p < 0.001) |
| **Did scores improve in absolute terms?** | **No** — scores declined system-wide after week 14 |
| **Did the new procedure help?** | **Yes** — switched branches declined 2.1 points less than control branches |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes** — the procedure provides measurable protective benefit |
| **Confidence in this recommendation?** | **High** — results robust across specifications and validity checks |

**Verdict: PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

The new procedure delivers real value. It will not single-handedly raise service scores above current levels, but it will help branches maintain performance better than the old procedure during challenging periods. For a cooperative serving patrons across hundreds of branches, a 2-point buffer against decline is operationally meaningful.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Relative Benefit, Not Absolute Improvement

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches (425) | 53.09 | 52.29 | −0.80 |
| Control branches (284) | 53.06 | 50.10 | −2.96 |
| **Difference (Treatment Effect)** | | | **+2.16** |

The +2.1 point treatment effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001), but it represents **avoided decline**, not absolute improvement.

### System-Wide Decline After Week 14

Service scores dropped across all branches after the switch date, suggesting external factors (possibly seasonal patterns, as weeks 16, 19, 21, and 22 all show lower scores). The new procedure appears to have **buffered** branches against this decline.

### Benefits Accumulate Over Time

| Weeks After Switch | Relative Benefit |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Week 1 | +0.85 |
| Week 4 | +1.84 |
| Week 8 | +3.18 |

The protective effect grows as staff become more proficient with the new procedure.

### Consistent Across Conditions

The benefit held regardless of staffing levels (+2.0 to +2.3 points across all terciles).
