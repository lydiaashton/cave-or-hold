# **Evaluation of New Shelving Procedure on Library Service Scores**

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically inconclusive effect** on service scores. The estimated improvement is **2 points** (about 4% of the average score), which is in the expected direction. However, with the current data, we cannot confidently distinguish this effect from zero. **We recommend collecting more data before committing to a full system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Kept Old Procedure | 47.2 | 48.9 | +1.7 |
| Switched to New | 51.4 | 55.1 | +3.8 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

### **Statistical Assessment**
- **Estimated effect**: +2.09 points
- **p-value**: 0.105 (not significant at the 5% level)
- **95% confidence interval**: -0.44 to +4.62 points

**What this means**: The data are compatible with effects ranging from a slight negative impact (-0.4 points) to a meaningful positive impact (+4.6 points). We cannot rule out no effect.

### **Why the Uncertainty?**
- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** (weeks 15–22)
- **34 branches** total, with natural week-to-week variation in scores
- To achieve statistical significance with this effect size, we would need approximately **270 more observations** (about 8 additional weeks of data)

---

## **Final Verdict**

**NOT RECOMMENDED FOR SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT AT THIS TIME**

**Rationale:**
- The evidence is **suggestive but not conclusive**
- A system-wide rollout represents a significant operational change that should be based on stronger evidence
- The cost of waiting for more data is low; the cost of rolling out an ineffective procedure is higher
- With one additional quarter of data collection, the board will be in a much stronger position to make a confident decision

---

## **Recommendation**

**Continue the pilot and collect more data:**

1. **Maintain current arrangement** through week 30+ (one additional quarter)
2. **Continue weekly service score tracking** for all branches
3. **Reconvene the board in Q2** with updated analysis

---

## **Decision Framework for Q2 Review**

| If the updated analysis shows: | Then: |
|-------------------------------|-------|
| Effect ≥2 points, p < 0.05 | Approve full rollout |
| Effect ≥2 points, p = 0.05–0.10 | Consider phased rollout with continued monitoring |
| Effect <1 point or p > 0.10 | Reconsider the procedure; investigate implementation issues |
