# **Analysis of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**
## Impact on Service Scores

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2 points.** This finding was unexpected—the procedure was designed for operational efficiency, not service quality. However, the evidence is robust: we have a control group, parallel pre-switch trends, and a statistically significant result (p = 0.001).

**Verdict: The service score improvement is real, but modest.** It should be treated as a secondary benefit, not the primary justification for rollout.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**1. Study Design**
- **150 branches** total: 90 switched to the new procedure, 60 kept the old procedure (control group)
- **17 weeks of data**: Weeks 6–14 (before switch) and Weeks 15–22 (after switch)
- Difference-in-differences analysis with cluster-robust standard errors

**3. Statistical Confidence**
- **p-value: 0.001** (highly statistically significant)
- **95% Confidence Interval: 0.8 to 3.1 points**
- Parallel trends verified before the switch

**4. Practical Significance**
- Effect size (Cohen's d = 0.56): moderate
