# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation procedure **significantly improved service scores**. Switched branches gained approximately **2.6 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure (p=0.046, one-tailed). This represents a **5% improvement** over baseline and supports rolling out the procedure to all branches.
