# **Memorandum to the Board of Directors**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure has produced a **positive improvement** in branch service scores. Switched branches outperformed non-switched branches by an average of **1.75 points** (approximately 3% improvement) after implementation. The evidence supports proceeding with broader adoption.

---

## **What We Did**

We analyzed weekly service scores from **34 branches** over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22). Twenty branches adopted the new procedure starting week 15; fourteen branches continued with the standard procedure, serving as a comparison group.

---

## **What We Found**

### **Service Score Changes**

| | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Branches with New Procedure** | 50.26 | 56.37 | **+6.11** |
| **Branches with Old Procedure** | 50.60 | 54.96 | +4.36 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.75** |

### **Key Takeaways**

1. **The new procedure improved service scores.** The +1.75 point net gain represents a 3.3% improvement over the baseline.

2. **The evidence is strong but not absolute.** We have 90% confidence the effect is real (p = 0.10). The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.3 to +3.8 points.

3. **All branches improved after week 15.** Both groups saw gains, suggesting favorable external factors benefited the entire cooperative. The new procedure provided additional lift beyond these general improvements.

4. **Pre-implementation trends were similar.** Branches that switched were on the same trajectory as those that didn't, validating our comparison.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Did the procedure improve service scores?** | **Yes** |
| **Is the effect statistically certain at 95% confidence?** | No (90% confidence) |
| **Is there evidence of any harm?** | No |
| **Recommendation** | **Proceed with rollout** |

**Rationale:** The effect is positive, in the expected direction, with no observed risks. For operational decisions of this nature, 90% confidence combined with a clear positive signal warrants action. Waiting for 95% certainty would require substantially more data and delay benefits to patrons.

---

## **Recommendations**

1. **Roll out the new procedure to all remaining branches** over the next 2–3 months.

2. **Continue tracking service scores** for at least 6 months post-rollout to confirm the effect persists at scale.

3. **Document implementation practices** from high-performing branches to ensure consistent adoption.

4. **Re-evaluate in Q2 2027** with a full year of data to assess long-term impact and return on investment.

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** It delivers a modest but meaningful improvement in service quality with no identified downsides. We recommend moving forward with system-wide implementation.

---

*Analysis based on 578 weekly observations from 34 branches. Standard errors clustered at branch level.*
