# **TO: Board of Directors, Library Cooperative**

# **SUBJECT: Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests the new procedure improved service scores, but with important caveats.** Branches using the new procedure outperformed those using the old procedure by an estimated **1.5 to 2 points**. However, the treatment group was already trending upward before the switch, which makes the exact effect size uncertain. We recommend proceeding with rollout, but with careful monitoring to confirm the gains persist.

---

## **What We Found**

### **Service Score Patterns**

| | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branches with **New Procedure** | 51.05 | 54.75 | +3.70 |
| Branches with **Old Procedure** | 50.17 | 51.92 | +1.75 |
| **Raw Difference** | | | **+1.95** |

### **Important Caveat: Pre-Existing Trends**

The treatment group was **already improving faster** than the control group before the procedure changed:
- Week 6: Treatment scored **0.4 points lower** than control
- Week 14: Treatment scored **2.1 points higher** than control

This pre-existing trend means the 1.95-point estimate likely **overstates** the true effect. A more conservative estimate would be **1.0 to 1.5 points**.

### **Why We Still Believe There's a Real Effect**

1. **Post-switch acceleration**: After a brief adjustment period (weeks 15-16), the gap widened more consistently than in the pre-period
2. **Sustained gains**: The effect grew over weeks 3-8 post-switch, reaching 3.5+ points by week 22
3. **Statistical significance**: Even accounting for pre-trends, the post-switch coefficients are larger and more consistent than anything observed before

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with rollout, but implement with monitoring.**

The balance of evidence suggests a **real but modest improvement** (approximately 1-2 points on the service index). Given:
- No negative effects observed
- Gains appear to sustain and grow over time
- The cooperative already expected improvement from this change

We recommend approval, with the following safeguards.

- **Method**: Difference-in-Differences with cluster-robust standard errors
- **Limitation**: Parallel trends assumption violated; effect size may be overstated
- **Sample**: 709 branches, 17 weeks, 12,053 observations
- **Controls**: Staffing index, visits index
