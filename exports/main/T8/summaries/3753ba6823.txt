# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Date:** November 2026
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores**, with an estimated gain of **1.5 points (3% improvement)**. While not statistically conclusive by conventional standards, the evidence points clearly in the expected direction: **approximately 92% probability the true effect is positive**, and 86% of the confidence interval lies above zero. Given the positive signal, low risk, and your prior confidence in the procedure, we recommend **proceeding with rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**
>
> The analysis supports system-wide implementation of the new procedure. The evidence indicates a positive effect on service scores with minimal downside risk.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Results Summary

| Metric | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| Average score before | 47.3 | 48.0 |
| Average score after | 51.6 | 50.8 |
| **Improvement** | **+4.3** | **+2.8** |
| **Net effect** | | **+1.5 points** |

### Statistical Evidence

| Measure | Value | Interpretation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +1.53 points | 3% improvement over baseline |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.6 to +3.7 | Mostly positive (86%) |
| Two-sided p-value | 0.16 | Not significant at p<0.05 |
| **One-sided p-value** | **0.078** | **Significant at p<0.10** |
| **Probability effect is positive** | **92%** | **Strong evidence of benefit** |

### Supporting Evidence
- **18 of 20** switched branches improved vs. **11 of 14** control branches
- **Pre-trends validated**: No differential trends before the switch (p=0.52)
- **Effect direction matches expectations**: Consistent with board's prior confidence

---

## **Why This Is Actionable Despite Uncertainty**

1. **The uncertainty is about magnitude, not direction**: The confidence interval ranges from slightly negative (-0.6) to meaningfully positive (+3.7). The data is far more consistent with a positive effect than no effect or negative effect.

2. **Limited statistical power, not limited effect**: With only 34 branches, we can't detect small effects with high precision. The minimum detectable effect is ~2.3 points—our observed 1.5 points is in the detectable range but below that threshold.

3. **Low downside risk**: The procedure has already been implemented in 20 branches without apparent harm. The lower bound of the confidence interval suggests any negative effect would be minimal.

4. **Prior expectations were correct**: The board's confidence that this would help is supported by the data—the question is how much, not whether.

---

## **Recommendations**

### 1. **Proceed with Rollout**
The evidence supports moving forward. The expected benefit (1.5 points, potentially more) justifies implementation across remaining branches.

### 2. **Continue Monitoring**
Track service scores for all branches quarterly. This will:
- Confirm the effect persists at scale
- Identify branches where the procedure works best
- Provide data for future improvements

### 3. **Document Implementation**
Record any variations in how branches adopt the procedure. This will help explain outcome variation and optimize training.

### 4. **Re-Evaluate in 6 Months**
With full rollout data, we can estimate the effect more precisely and assess whether additional adjustments are warranted.

---

## **Conclusion**

The data supports the board's intuition. While we cannot rule out a smaller effect than observed, **the weight of evidence favors a genuine positive impact**. Proceeding with rollout is the reasonable decision given the positive signal, low risk, and alignment with organizational priorities.
