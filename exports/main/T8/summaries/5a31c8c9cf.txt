# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Final Verdict**

**PROCEED WITH CAUTIOUS EXPANSION**

The evidence supports moving forward with the new procedure, but with continued monitoring. The data show a positive effect in the expected direction (+1.9 points), and while statistical confidence is limited, there is no evidence of harm. Given the low cost of implementation and the practical significance of even a modest improvement, a phased rollout is justified.

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **suggestive evidence of benefit** but falls short of statistical proof. Branches that switched experienced a smaller decline in service scores than those that kept the old procedure. With only 8 weeks of post-switch data, we cannot confirm the effect with high confidence—but the direction and magnitude align with expectations. **A measured rollout with ongoing evaluation is the appropriate next step.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Results Summary**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** (20 branches) | 54.27 | 52.69 | **-1.58** |
| **Control branches** (14 branches) | 50.35 | 46.84 | **-3.52** |

- **Estimated effect: +1.94 points** (approximately 4% of average score)
- **95% confidence interval: -0.6 to +4.5 points**
- **Statistical significance: p = 0.14** (inconclusive)

### **What the Data Tell Us**

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| Effect is positive (+1.94) | Consistent with expectations |
| Not statistically significant (p = 0.14) | Cannot rule out chance |
| Confidence interval includes zero | True effect could be null |
| Confidence interval upper bound is +4.5 | True effect could be meaningful |
| Both groups declined after week 15 | External factors affected all branches |
| Pre-switch trends were parallel | Comparison is valid |
