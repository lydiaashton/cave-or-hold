# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. The best estimate is a **1-point improvement** (2% of baseline), but the data is consistent with effects ranging from **−0.8 to +2.9 points**. We **cannot conclude with confidence** that the new procedure improves service. However, there is **no evidence of harm**. We recommend **continuing the pilot** and gathering more data before deciding on system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Analysis uses panel regression with branch fixed effects and clustered standard errors

### **Results**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated effect** | +1.0 points |
| **Standard error** | 0.94 |
| **P-value** | 0.27 |
| **95% Confidence interval** | −0.8 to +2.9 |
| **Effect size** | 2% of baseline; 10% of one standard deviation |

### **What This Means**
1. **The effect could be zero:** The confidence interval includes no effect
2. **The effect could be meaningful:** The interval also includes up to +3 points
3. **Harm is unlikely:** The lower bound is only −0.8 points
4. **More data needed:** With only 34 branches, statistical power is limited

### **Why the Uncertainty?**
- Small sample (34 branches)
- Service scores have natural week-to-week variation (SD ≈ 10.5 points)
- Only 8 weeks of post-switch data per branch

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **Do not approve system-wide rollout at this time.**

**Rationale:**
- The evidence does not meet the standard of statistical confidence required for a major operational change affecting all 34 branches
- Implementation costs and staff training burden should not be incurred without clearer evidence of benefit
- The pilot has demonstrated the procedure is **safe** (no negative effects detected) but not yet **proven effective**

**What we recommend instead:**

| Action | Timeline | Purpose |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **Continue current pilot** | Ongoing | Maintain data collection on existing 34 branches |
| **Extend observation period** | +12 weeks | Double post-switch data to improve precision |
| **Expand treatment group** | Q1 2027 | Add 10–15 branches to increase statistical power |
| **Re-evaluate** | Q2 2027 | Make rollout decision with stronger evidence base |

**Decision threshold for future rollout:**
- Effect size ≥ 1.0 point **AND**
- P-value < 0.10 **AND**
- Positive staff feedback on operational feasibility
