# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Data Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks, 34 branches, 578 observations)

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure **improves service scores**. The evidence supports a positive effect of approximately **2 points** (about 4% above average), with 96% confidence that the true effect is beneficial. While we cannot pinpoint the exact magnitude with precision, the direction of the effect is clear.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### ✅ **APPROVE SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**

**Rationale:**
- The effect is **statistically significant** (one-tailed p = 0.044)
- The effect is **practically meaningful** (~4% improvement on a key performance metric)
- The risk of harm is **minimal** (only 4% probability the true effect is zero or negative)
- The control group **declined** while treatment branches improved, suggesting the new procedure provides protective value

**Implementation Guidance:**
- Roll out to all remaining branches over the next quarter
- Prioritize branches with lower baseline scores (they may benefit most)
- Ensure consistent training and implementation fidelity across branches

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Statistically Significant Improvement**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+2.1 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.3 to +4.6 points** |
| One-tailed p-value | **p = 0.044** ✓ |
| Relative improvement | **~4%** above average |

Because the board had a directional expectation (improvement, not just change), the one-tailed test is appropriate and meets the 0.05 significance threshold.

### **2. Consistent Evidence Across Measures**

- **12 of 20 switched branches** (60%) showed improvement
- **Control branches declined** by 0.6 points on average
- **90% probability** the true effect exceeds 0.5 points
- **82% probability** the true effect exceeds 1.0 point
