# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is inconclusive.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed a 2.3-point average improvement in service scores, but this result is **not statistically significant** (p = 0.12). The data are consistent with three possibilities:

| Scenario | Effect | Likelihood |
|----------|--------|------------|
| Modest benefit | +2 to +5 points | Plausible |
| No effect | 0 points | Plausible |
| Slight harm | -0.5 to 0 points | Less likely, but possible |

**We cannot confidently conclude the new procedure improves service.**

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **DO NOT PROCEED with systemwide rollout at this time.**

**Reasoning:**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Evidence quality** | ❌ Insufficient – result is not statistically significant |
| **Risk of rollout** | ⚠️ Moderate – could waste resources on ineffective change |
| **Risk of waiting** | ✅ Low – pilot branches can continue; no urgent harm |
| **Value of more data** | ✅ High – would substantially reduce uncertainty |

**This is not a rejection of the new procedure.** It is a recommendation to **defer the decision** until we have evidence strong enough to justify a cooperative-wide investment.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Show**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Old procedure (14 branches) | 46.5 | 46.6 | +0.1 |
| New procedure (20 branches) | 50.4 | 52.8 | +2.4 |
| **Estimated effect** | | | **+2.3** |

### **Why We Cannot Be Confident**

1. **Statistical uncertainty:** The 95% confidence interval (-0.6 to +5.3) includes zero
2. **Baseline differences:** Treatment branches started 3.8 points higher than control branches
3. **Limited data:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch observations across 34 branches

### **What This Means Practically**

- If the effect is at the **upper bound** (+5.3): Meaningful improvement worth spreading
- If the effect is **zero**: No benefit, but no harm either
- If the effect is at the **lower bound** (-0.6): Slight degradation in service

---

## **Recommendation**

**Extend the pilot and gather more evidence before systemwide rollout.**

**Rationale:**
1. The current evidence does not meet the threshold for confident decision-making
2. An additional 8–12 weeks of data would substantially reduce uncertainty
3. The cost of waiting is low compared to the risk of implementing an ineffective procedure cooperative-wide
4. If the effect is real (+2 to +5 points), it will remain detectable with more data
