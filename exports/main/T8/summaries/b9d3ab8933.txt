# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Re:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The evidence strongly supports system-wide adoption.** Branches using the new procedure achieved service scores **2.3 points higher** than expected—a **4.7% improvement** over baseline. The effect is statistically robust (p < 0.001) and consistent across all 425 participating branches. **We recommend approving rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

> **PROCEED WITH SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**

The new procedure delivers measurable, meaningful improvement in service quality. The data confirm what the cooperative suspected: this change works. Delaying adoption means leaving service gains on the table for patrons at the 284 branches still using the old procedure.

**Risk assessment:** Low. The procedure has been tested at scale (425 branches) with no adverse effects detected. The primary risk is implementation logistics, not effectiveness.

**Return on investment:** A 4.7% service improvement across the entire cooperative represents substantial value for patrons and justifies the transition costs.

---
