# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect** on service scores (+1.9 points, or ~4%), which aligns with expectations. However, the statistical evidence is **not strong enough to confirm the effect is real** rather than random variation. We recommend **a cautious, monitored expansion** rather than a full immediate rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches | 53.0 | 57.9 | +4.9 |
| Non-switched branches | 48.5 | 51.5 | +3.1 |
| **Attributed to new procedure** | | | **+1.9** |

### **What This Means**

- **Good news:** The effect is in the expected direction (positive)
- **Uncertainty:** The 95% confidence interval ranges from **-0.9 to +4.6 points**
  - This means the true effect could be slightly negative, zero, or meaningfully positive
- **Why the uncertainty?** Only 20 branches switched, with just 8 weeks of post-switch data

### **Why We Trust the Analysis**

- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (validates our comparison method)
- Analysis controls for inherent differences between branches
- Both groups improved after week 15, suggesting favorable external conditions

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **Do Not Proceed with Full System-Wide Rollout at This Time**

**Rationale:**

1. **The evidence is inconclusive.** While the effect is positive (+1.9 points), we cannot statistically distinguish it from zero. A full rollout based on this data alone carries meaningful risk.

2. **The potential upside is real.** The upper bound of our confidence interval suggests the true effect could be as high as +4.6 points—a meaningful improvement worth pursuing.

3. **More data will clarify the picture.** Extending the pilot by 8–12 weeks and/or adding more branches would substantially improve our ability to detect a true effect.

4. **A phased approach protects the cooperative.** If the procedure truly helps, we can scale up after confirmation. If it doesn't, we avoid system-wide disruption and cost.

---

## **Recommended Action Plan**

| Step | Timeline | Decision Point |
|------|----------|----------------|
| 1. Extend current pilot | 8–12 weeks | Re-analyze with 16–20 weeks post-switch data |
| 2. Add 5–10 branches to treatment group | Next quarter | Improve statistical power |
| 3. Re-evaluate | 6 months | If effect remains positive AND statistically significant, proceed with full rollout |
| 4. Track costs throughout | Ongoing | Ensure benefits outweigh operational changes |

---

## **What the Board Should Consider**

| If the true effect is... | Full rollout would... |
|--------------------------|----------------------|
| +4 points (upper bound) | Deliver meaningful service improvements |
| 0 points (possible) | Incur change costs with no benefit |
| -1 point (possible) | Actually reduce service quality |

Given this uncertainty, a **phased approach minimizes risk** while allowing the cooperative to capture potential benefits.
