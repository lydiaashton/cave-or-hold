# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **suggestive but not definitive evidence** of improvement. The estimated effect is +2.35 points, but statistical significance is **marginal** (p = 0.08–0.12 depending on specification). The effect appears strongest in the first 3 weeks after implementation, then weakens. **We recommend a phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate cooperative-wide adoption.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Specification | Effect Size | P-value | Interpretation |
|---------------|-------------|---------|----------------|
| Simple DiD | +2.35 | 0.115 | Not significant at p < 0.05 |
| With Branch FE | +2.38 | 0.079 | Marginal significance |
| With Week FE | +2.35 | 0.079 | Marginal significance |

### Critical Observations

1. **Treatment branches started lower**: Switched branches averaged 4 points **below** control branches before the switch. They remained below control branches after the switch—the gap narrowed but did not close.

2. **Effect fades over time**: 
   - Weeks 15–17: Strong effects (+3.25 to +3.59 points)
   - Weeks 18–22: Weaker effects (+0.31 to +2.74 points)

3. **80% of switched branches improved**, but so did 57% of control branches—suggesting some improvement may reflect broader trends, not just the procedure change.

4. **Model limitations**: Fixed-effects models show rank deficiency warnings, indicating the data may not support the most rigorous specifications.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with caution.** The point estimate is positive and operationally meaningful, but statistical confidence is limited. We recommend:

1. **Phased rollout**: Convert 3–5 additional branches and monitor for 8–12 weeks
2. **Document implementation fidelity**: Ensure branches are actually following the new procedure as designed
3. **Re-evaluate after phased rollout**: If effects are consistent and stronger with more data, proceed with full adoption
4. **Investigate non-responders**: 4 of 20 switched branches did not improve—understand why

---

## **Why the Uncertainty?**

- Only 8 weeks of post-implementation data
- Treatment and control branches differed at baseline
- Effect appears to decay over time
- Sample size limits statistical power

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

### **Conditionally Recommended — Proceed with Phased Rollout**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the procedure improve scores? | **Likely yes** — positive effect in all specifications |
| Are we statistically certain? | **No** — p-values above conventional 0.05 threshold |
| Is the effect operationally meaningful? | **Yes** — ~5% improvement on baseline |
| Should we roll out to all branches now? | **No** — gather more evidence first |
| Should we abandon the procedure? | **No** — signal is positive, worth pursuing |

**Bottom line:** The data supports continuing with the new procedure, but not yet expanding it cooperative-wide. A measured, evidence-based approach protects the cooperative from adopting a change that may not deliver sustained benefits, while still allowing branches to capture potential gains.
