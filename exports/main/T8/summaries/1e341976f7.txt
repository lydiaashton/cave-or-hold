# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure is associated with a **2-point improvement in service scores** (approximately 4%). While not statistically significant by conventional standards, there is roughly a **94% probability the true effect is positive**. The main limitation is study size—with 34 branches, we cannot detect small effects with high confidence.

**Verdict: Proceed with phased rollout.** The evidence favors a beneficial effect, the risk of harm appears minimal, and continued monitoring will provide clearer answers.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Estimated Impact on Service Scores**

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Treatment effect | +2.0 points |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.5 to +4.5 points |
| P-value | 0.11 |
| Probability effect is positive | 94% |
| Baseline average score | 51.2 points |

### **2. Study Design**

- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure (treatment group)
- **14 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- **17 weeks** of data per branch (weeks 6–22)
- Procedure switch occurred in **week 15**
- **Parallel trends verified:** Treatment and control branches had similar score trends before the switch

### **3. Branch-Level Outcomes**

| Metric | Treatment Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| Branches with improved scores | 65% | 57% |
| Average score change | +1.72 | -0.29 |

### **4. Effect Over Time**

- **Weeks 15–18 (early post-period):** +2.5 points (statistically significant)
- **Weeks 19–22 (late post-period):** +1.5 points (not significant)

This pattern suggests the effect may diminish over time, warranting continued observation.

### **5. Other Factors**

- Higher staffing levels are associated with higher service scores
- The treatment effect remains after accounting for staffing and visit levels

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Direction of effect** | ✅ Favors new procedure |
| **Statistical confidence** | ⚠️ Inconclusive (p=0.11) |
| **Probability of benefit** | ✅ High (~94%) |
| **Risk of harm** | ✅ Low (no negative trend observed) |
| **Effect magnitude** | ⚠️ Modest (~4% improvement) |
| **Durability** | ⚠️ Uncertain (may fade over time) |

**Decision: Proceed with phased rollout to remaining branches over the next 2–3 months, with formal re-evaluation after 12 additional weeks of data.**

**Rationale:** The balance of evidence favors adoption. The cost of delaying a beneficial procedure likely exceeds the cost of implementing one with modest effects. A phased approach allows course correction if later data contradicts early findings.

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure shows promise without clear evidence of harm. A cautious but forward-moving approach best serves the cooperative's interests. We recommend revisiting this analysis after 12 additional weeks to confirm the effect persists and quantify the benefit with greater precision.
