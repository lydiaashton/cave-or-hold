# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure shows promising but inconclusive evidence of improvement.** Service scores at switched branches increased by an estimated 1.6 points relative to non-switched branches, with a 94% probability the effect is positive. However, this falls just short of conventional statistical certainty. We recommend **collecting 8–12 more weeks of data** before deciding on a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**DO NOT ROLL OUT SYSTEM-WIDE AT THIS TIME.**

**Reasoning:**

| Consideration | Finding | Implication |
|---------------|---------|-------------|
| **Statistical evidence** | p = 0.062 (one-sided) | Suggestive but not conclusive |
| **Confidence** | 94% probability of positive effect | 6% chance of no effect or harm |
| **Consistency** | Only 55% of switched branches improved | Procedure may not work everywhere |
| **Effect size** | +1.6 points (0.15 SD) | Modest even if real |
| **Cost of error** | System-wide change affects all 34 branches | High stakes warrant higher certainty |

**The responsible decision is to wait.** The cost of delaying a rollout by 8–12 weeks is minimal compared to the cost of implementing a system-wide change that may not deliver benefits—or could even reduce service quality at some branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Data covers **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring in **week 15**

### **Results**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.6 points** | In expected direction |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.4 to +3.5** | Includes zero (inconclusive) |
| 90% Confidence interval | **-0.1 to +3.2** | Barely includes zero |
| Probability effect is positive | **94%** | Suggestive evidence |
| One-sided p-value | **0.062** | Close to 0.05 threshold |
| Effect size | **Small** (0.15 SD) | Modest practical impact |

### **Branch-Level Outcomes**

| Group | Improved | Declined | % Improved |
|-------|----------|----------|------------|
| Switched branches | 11 | 9 | 55% |
| Non-switched branches | 5 | 9 | 36% |

---

## **Recommendations**

### **Immediate Action**
1. **Continue the pilot** with current 20 switched branches
2. **Collect 8–12 additional weeks of data** (through Q1 2026)
3. **Do not expand** to remaining 14 branches yet

### **Follow-Up Actions**
1. **Investigate branch-level variation.** Understand why 11 branches improved while 9 did not. Branch size, staffing, or local factors may matter.

2. **Gather staff feedback.** Survey branch managers about implementation challenges, training adequacy, and operational impacts.

3. **Re-evaluate in Q2 2026.** With additional data and qualitative insights, make a final rollout decision.

---

## **What Would Change This Verdict?**

| Scenario | New Recommendation |
|----------|-------------------|
| Effect persists + 8 more weeks of data | Proceed with rollout |
| Effect disappears or reverses | Abandon procedure |
| Clear pattern emerges (e.g., works only in large branches) | Targeted rollout to specific branch types |

---

## **Appendix: Technical Notes**

- Analysis used difference-in-differences regression with standard errors clustered by branch
- One-sided p-value (0.062) is appropriate given the directional hypothesis
- Parallel trends assumption appears satisfied in pre-switch period
