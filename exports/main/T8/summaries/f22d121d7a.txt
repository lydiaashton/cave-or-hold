## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation procedure shows a **modest positive association** with service scores (+2 points, or ~4%), but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive** (p=0.12). There is approximately a 94% probability the true effect is positive, with the likely range being 0 to +4 points. There is no evidence of harm.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**PROCEED WITH CAUTIOUS PHASED EXPANSION**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|

| **Risk of harm** | ✓ Low (CI lower bound near zero) |

**Rationale:** The evidence is insufficient for immediate system-wide rollout, but strong enough to warrant limited expansion. The combination of a positive point estimate, low risk of harm, and moderate confidence (94%) supports moving forward cautiously while building a stronger evidence base.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** participated: 20 adopted the new procedure, 14 kept the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), with the switch occurring at week 15
- Analysis compares service score changes before and after the switch between the two groups

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 47.3 | 48.5 | +1.2 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 45.7 | 49.0 | +3.3 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.0** |

### **Statistical Summary**
- **Estimated effect:** +1.95 points (adjusted for staffing and visitor levels)
- **95% Confidence Interval:** -0.49 to +4.39 points
- **P-value:** 0.117
- **Relative improvement:** ~4% above treatment group baseline

### **Validity Assessment**
✓ **Parallel trends confirmed:** Both groups showed similar trajectories before the switch  
✓ **No evidence of harm:** Lower bound of confidence interval is near zero  
✓ **Results robust:** Effect size remains stable after controlling for staffing and visitor volume
