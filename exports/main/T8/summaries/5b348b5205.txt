# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, with tempered expectations.**

The new procedure produced a **statistically significant but modest improvement** in service scores (+2 points, ~4%). While we expected no effect, the data shows switched branches maintained their scores better than control branches during a period when scores declined system-wide. The benefit is real but not transformative.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Effect size** | +1.99 points (95% CI: +0.76 to +3.21) |
| **Statistical confidence** | p = 0.002 |
| **Practical significance** | ~4% improvement; 0.19 standard deviations |
| **Durability** | Effect stable across all 8 post-switch weeks |
| **Context** | Both groups declined; switched branches declined less |

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ APPROVE ROLLOUT**

The evidence supports extending the new procedure to all branches. The effect is:
- **Real** (statistically significant at p < 0.01)
- **Consistent** (stable across the observation period)
- **Cost-effective** (procedure changes appear operational, not resource-intensive)

However, this is an **incremental gain, not a breakthrough**. The board should approve the rollout while recognizing that:
1. The ~4% improvement is meaningful but modest
2. The procedure protected against decline rather than driving dramatic gains
3. Other initiatives may be needed for larger service score improvements
