## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted it achieved service scores **2 points higher** than those that did not — a meaningful 4% improvement. The evidence is clear and statistically robust. We recommend immediate approval to roll out the new procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Numbers Speak for Themselves

| Metric | Switched Branches | Non-Switched Branches |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| Average score before week 15 | 51.9 | 51.2 |
| Average score after week 15 | 53.3 | 50.5 |
| **Change** | **+1.3** | **-0.6** |
| **Net Improvement** | | **+1.95 points** |

### What This Means

- **709 branches** were studied over 17 weeks (425 switched; 284 did not)
- The **+1.95 point improvement** is statistically significant (p < 0.001)
- The benefit **grew over time** — from ~2.2 points in the first weeks to ~3.0 points by week 22, suggesting staff proficiency increases the payoff
- Results hold even after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Why We're Confident

This analysis compares branches that switched against those that did not, before and after the change. The switched branches improved while non-switched branches declined slightly during the same period — a clear signal that the procedure itself drove the gain, not external factors.
