# **Library Branch Shelving Procedure: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The data do not provide statistically conclusive evidence that the new procedure improves service scores.** While the estimated effect is positive (+1.8 points), the result is not statistically significant at conventional levels (p = 0.097), and the confidence interval includes the possibility of no effect.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Do not proceed with system-wide rollout at this time.**

The evidence is insufficient to justify the costs and disruption of implementing the new procedure across all remaining branches. The board should either:

1. **Continue the pilot** with the current 20 treatment branches and collect 8-12 more weeks of data, OR
2. **Approve a limited expansion** to no more than 5 additional branches with a mandatory re-evaluation before any further rollout

A full system-wide rollout is not supported by the current evidence.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. What the Data Show
| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.83 points** | Suggests improvement |
| P-value | **0.097** | Not significant at 5% level |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.33 to +4.00** | Includes zero (no effect) |

### 2. What This Means
- **We cannot reject the possibility that the new procedure has no real effect** on service scores
- The observed improvement could plausibly be due to random variation
- At the same time, we cannot rule out a meaningful positive effect (up to +4 points)

### 3. Supporting Observations
- **Parallel trends validated:** Before the switch, treatment and control branches showed similar trajectories (p = 0.46)
- **Consistent post-switch pattern:** All 8 weeks after the switch showed positive differences between treatment and control branches
- **Study design is sound:** 34 branches over 17 weeks, with appropriate controls for staffing and visitor volume
