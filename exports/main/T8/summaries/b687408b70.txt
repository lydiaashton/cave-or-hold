# MEMORANDUM

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

The new procedure shows a **positive but not definitively proven effect** on service scores. The estimated improvement is 2.4 points (5%), with the true effect likely ranging from negligible to moderately beneficial. There is no evidence of harm.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes.

---

## Key Findings

### Study Design
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure (control group)
- Procedure switch occurred at **week 15**

### Results Summary

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 45.2 | 49.4 | +4.2 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 46.6 | 48.6 | +2.0 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.4** |

### Statistical Assessment

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated Effect | +2.40 service score points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.49 to +5.29 points |
| P-value | 0.103 |

### What This Means

- The point estimate is **positive and in the expected direction**
- The confidence interval is **80% positive** (from 0 to +5.3)
- There is **no evidence of harm** — the lower bound is essentially zero
- The result is **suggestive but not conclusive** at conventional statistical thresholds

---

## Final Verdict

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the procedure improve service scores? | **Probably, but not certain** |
| Is there evidence of harm? | **No** |
| Should we roll out to all branches now? | **No — proceed in phases** |
| Should we abandon the procedure? | **No — the signal is positive** |

**Verdict: CONDITIONAL APPROVAL FOR PHASED ROLLOUT**

The evidence is strong enough to justify expanding the procedure, but not strong enough to commit the entire cooperative at once. A phased approach allows the library to capture potential benefits while maintaining scientific rigor to confirm the effect.
