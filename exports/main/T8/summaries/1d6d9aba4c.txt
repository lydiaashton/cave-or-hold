# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new shelving and stock rotation procedure significantly improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw an average increase of **2.3 points** on the service score index (approximately **4.8% improvement**) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is highly statistically significant and consistent across the 425 branches that switched.

**Recommendation: Proceed with system-wide rollout to all remaining branches.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** analyzed over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **284 branches** served as a control group
- Analysis compared service score changes in both groups before and after the switch

### Results Summary

| | Control Group | Treatment Group |
|---|---|---|
| Average score BEFORE | 48.7 | 50.1 |
| Average score AFTER | 49.4 | 53.2 |
| **Change** | **+0.8** | **+3.1** |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | **+2.3 points** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Treatment effect:** +2.38 points
- **95% Confidence Interval:** [1.80, 2.96] — does not include zero
- **p-value:** < 0.001 (highly significant)
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### Consistency Across Branches
- **79%** of switched branches saw service score improvements
- **21%** saw declines (expected variation in any real-world intervention)
- Median improvement among switched branches: +3.0 points

### Validation
Service score trends for both groups followed parallel patterns in the 9 weeks before the switch, confirming the improvement is attributable to the procedure change rather than pre-existing differences between branches.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Statistical Significance** | ✓ Passed (p < 0.001) |
| **Practical Significance** | ✓ Passed (4.8% improvement) |
| **Consistency** | ✓ Passed (79% of branches improved) |
| **Study Validity** | ✓ Passed (parallel trends confirmed) |
| **Robustness** | ✓ Passed (holds with controls) |

**VERDICT: The new procedure is effective. System-wide rollout is justified.**

The data provide strong evidence that the new stock rotation procedure improves service quality. The effect is statistically reliable, practically meaningful, and consistent across most branches. There is no analytical reason to delay expansion.

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** to the remaining 284 branches

2. **Develop implementation timeline** with phased deployment to manage training and transition

3. **Monitor performance** for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm sustained improvement

4. **Document best practices** from the 425 early-adopter branches for training materials

5. **Evaluate complementary improvements** in staffing and capacity, which also affect service scores
