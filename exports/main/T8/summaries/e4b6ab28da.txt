# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure is associated with a **modest improvement in service scores** — approximately **2 points higher** (a 4% increase) for branches that adopted it. While not statistically significant at conventional levels (p=0.12), the evidence consistently points in a positive direction with no indication of negative effects.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** to additional branches while continuing to monitor. Full system-wide adoption should await more conclusive evidence.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Does the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Likely yes, but not conclusively proven** |
| **Is the improvement large enough to matter?** | **Modest — approximately 4% gain** |
| **Is there any evidence of harm?** | **No** |
| **Should we roll out to all branches now?** | **No — proceed with phased expansion** |
| **Should we abandon the new procedure?** | **No — evidence favors continuing** |

**Rationale:** The point estimate is stable and positive across all analytical approaches. All 8 post-treatment weeks showed gains for treated branches. The pre-trends are parallel, validating our comparison. However, with only 17 weeks of data and 34 branches, statistical power is limited. More data will sharpen the estimate.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** kept the old procedure
- Analysis compares changes in service scores between groups before and after the switch

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.2 | 51.1 | +1.9 |
| Treated (new procedure) | 47.0 | 51.0 | +3.9 |

- **Estimated effect of new procedure: +2.0 points**
- **95% confidence interval: -0.5 to +4.6 points**
- **Statistical significance: p = 0.12**

### **What This Means**
- Both groups improved after week 15, but branches using the new procedure improved slightly more
- The true effect could reasonably range from slightly negative to meaningfully positive
- The data **suggests a benefit** but does not **confirm it with high confidence**

### **Validity Checks**
- ✓ **Parallel trends confirmed**: Both groups followed similar trajectories before the switch (p=0.59)
- ✓ **Complete data**: All 34 branches reported for all 17 weeks
- ✓ **Robust methods**: Analysis accounts for branch-specific characteristics and statistical uncertainty
