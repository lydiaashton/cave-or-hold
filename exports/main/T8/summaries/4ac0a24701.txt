# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores—an unexpected but welcome finding.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.1 points** (approximately 4%) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant, appeared immediately, and has persisted for eight weeks. Despite initial expectations that this operational change would not affect service quality, the data are clear.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Main Effect** | +2.1 points on service score (95% CI: 0.9 to 3.4) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Effect Size** | ~4% improvement relative to average score of 50 |
| **Robustness** | Effect unchanged after controlling for staffing and visits |
| **Timing** | Immediate improvement in Week 15, sustained through Week 22 |
| **Validation** | Pre-switch trends were parallel—supports causal interpretation |

---

## **Analysis Approach**

We used a **difference-in-differences** design:

- **Treatment group:** 90 branches that switched to the new procedure at Week 15
- **Control group:** 60 branches that continued with the old procedure
- **Data:** 2,550 branch-week observations (17 weeks, weeks 6–22)
- **Method:** Regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
- **Validation:** Pre-trend test confirms parallel trends before the switch (p=0.51)

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **✓ PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

**Rationale:**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Statistical evidence** | Strong—effect is highly significant (p=0.001) |
| **Practical significance** | Modest but meaningful—4% improvement in service quality |
| **Cost** | Minimal—90 branches already use this procedure; extending to 60 more requires no new investment |
| **Risk** | Low—no negative effects detected; procedure already proven operationally feasible |
| **Durability** | Reasonable—effect persisted 8 weeks; some attenuation is common as novelty fades |

**The unexpected nature of this finding does not invalidate it.** Operational changes can have downstream effects that are not anticipated during design. What matters is that the data show a real, positive impact on service quality at no additional cost.

## **Conclusion**

This analysis demonstrates that an operational change implemented for logistical reasons has produced an unexpected benefit: measurable improvement in service quality. The statistical evidence is robust, the practical impact is positive, and the cost of expansion is minimal. 

**We recommend the Board approve system-wide adoption of the new procedure.**
