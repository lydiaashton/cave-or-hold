# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **suggestive evidence of improvement** (+2.4 points, ~5% gain), with a **94% probability the effect is positive**. However, the result is not statistically significant at conventional thresholds, and the pilot was too short to detect modest effects with high confidence. 

**We recommend a conditional rollout:** Proceed with system-wide implementation **if the cost is low**, but commit to continued monitoring and be prepared to adjust if later data contradicts these findings.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | +2.4 points |
| Probability effect is positive | 94% |
| 95% confidence interval | −0.6 to +5.4 points |
| Branches showing improvement | 65% (treated) vs 57% (control) |

### What This Means

- **The signal is positive**: 65% of switched branches improved, compared to 57% of non-switched branches
- **The uncertainty is real**: We cannot rule out that the true effect is zero or slightly negative
- **The study was underpowered**: With only 8 weeks of post-switch data, we could only reliably detect effects larger than ~10 points. A 2–3 point improvement is plausible but hard to confirm with this data alone

### Risk Assessment

| Decision | If Procedure Works | If Procedure Doesn't Work |
|----------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| **Roll out now** | Gain ~5% service improvement across all branches | Incur implementation costs with no benefit |
| **Wait for more data** | Delay benefits to patrons | Avoid wasted implementation costs |

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT — WITH CONDITIONS**

**We recommend implementing the new procedure across all branches**, based on the following reasoning:

1. **The balance of evidence favors improvement**: A 94% probability of positive effect is meaningful, even if not "statistically significant" by conventional standards
2. **The risk of harm appears low**: Control branches actually declined slightly (−1.3 points) while treated branches improved (+1.1 points)
3. **The potential benefit is meaningful**: A 5% improvement in service scores affects patron experience across the entire cooperative
4. **The cost of waiting is real**: Delaying rollout postpones potential benefits to patrons at all 34 branches

### Conditions for Approval

| Condition | Timeline | Owner |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Monthly service score monitoring | Ongoing | Operations Team |
| Formal re-evaluation with 6 months of system-wide data | May 2027 | Data Team |
| Documentation of implementation fidelity | Complete by rollout | Training Team |
| Trigger for review if scores decline >2 points | Immediate | Board |

---

## **Recommendation**

**Vote to approve system-wide rollout effective January 2027**, with the monitoring framework above. If 6-month data shows no improvement or decline, reconvene to consider reverting to the previous procedure.
