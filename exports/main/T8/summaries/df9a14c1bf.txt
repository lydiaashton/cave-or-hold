# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure shows a **positive effect** on service scores (+2 points, 4% improvement), though the result is **not statistically conclusive** (p = 0.14). The data is consistent with your expectation that the procedure would improve scores, and there is no evidence of harm.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with rollout, but monitor outcomes.**

The evidence supports adoption for the following reasons:

1. **The effect is in the expected direction** — Scores improved, not declined
2. **The likely benefit is meaningful** — A 4% improvement in service scores matters to patrons
3. **There is no signal of harm** — The lower bound of uncertainty is near zero (-0.6 points), not substantially negative
4. **Waiting has opportunity cost** — Delaying denies branches the potential benefit

**However, proceed with safeguards:**

- Implement in **phases** rather than all at once
- **Continue tracking** service scores for all branches
- **Re-evaluate in 8-12 weeks** — if scores do not continue to trend upward, pause and reassess

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Estimated Impact on Service Scores**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | **+2.0 points** |
| Relative improvement | **+4.1%** of average score |
| Statistical significance | **Not conclusive** (p = 0.14) |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.6 to +4.7 points** |

### **2. What the Data Shows**

| Group | Before Switch (Weeks 6-14) | After Switch (Weeks 15-22) | Change |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| **Switched Branches** (20 branches) | 47.9 | 49.6 | +1.7 |
| **Non-Switched Branches** (14 branches) | 51.1 | 50.8 | -0.3 |

### **3. Why the Result is Not Conclusive**

With 34 branches and week-to-week score variation (~10 points standard deviation), the study cannot detect effects smaller than 3-4 points with high confidence. The observed improvement may be real but modest, or partially due to random variation.

### **4. Validity Checks**

- ✓ **Parallel trends**: Both groups showed similar patterns before the switch
- ✓ **Controls included**: Branch characteristics and seasonal effects
- ✓ **Appropriate methods**: Difference-in-differences with robust standard errors
