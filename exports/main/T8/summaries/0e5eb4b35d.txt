# **Board Summary: New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a **3.6% improvement**. The effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and grows over time.

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout.**

---

## **What We Found**

| Metric | Switched Branches | Control Branches | Net Effect |
|--------|------------------|------------------|------------|
| Before switch (weeks 6–14) | 51.7 | 51.2 | — |
| After switch (weeks 15–22) | 52.8 | 50.4 | — |
| **Change** | **+1.1** | **−0.8** | **+1.9** |

- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** continued with the old
- **61% of switched branches** improved vs. **39% of control branches**
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.2 to 2.6 points
- Benefits **grow over time**—from +1.5 points in week 1 to +3.7 points by week 8

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **Yes** |
| Is the effect statistically reliable? | **Yes** (p < 0.001) |
| Is the effect practically meaningful? | **Yes** (3.6% improvement) |
| Are there warning signs or negative side effects? | **No** |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Yes** |

**Confidence Level: High**

The analysis uses a rigorous difference-in-differences design with 12,053 observations across 709 branches. The treatment effect is robust across multiple statistical specifications, including branch fixed effects and controls for staffing and visitor volume.

---

## **Why This Matters**

1. **Validates operational investment.** The data confirms the theory behind the procedure change.

2. **No downside detected.** Switched branches showed no adverse effects on staffing or visitor metrics.

3. **Compounding benefit.** The growing effect suggests branches master the procedure over time.

4. **System-wide impact.** A 1.9-point gain across all 709 branches represents meaningful service quality improvement.
