# **Board Summary: Evaluation of New Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new shelving procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (a 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and persisted across all 8 weeks of post-switch data. Although we expected no difference, the evidence indicates a real, modest benefit.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.11 points on service score (95% CI: 0.87 to 3.35) |
| **Statistical Significance** | p < 0.001 (highly significant) |
| **Effect Size** | 0.23 standard deviations (4% of average score) |
| **Treatment Branches** | 90 branches; 75.6% showed improvement |
| **Control Branches** | 60 branches; 53.3% showed improvement |
| **Pre-Trend Validation** | No significant differences between groups before the switch |
| **Persistence** | Effect remained stable across all 8 post-switch weeks |
| **Spillover Effects** | None detected on staffing or visit metrics |

---

## **Considerations**

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Statistical confidence** | ✓ Strong — effect is real, not random noise |
| **Practical significance** | △ Modest — meaningful but not transformative |
| **Consistency** | ✓ Effect present every week post-switch |
| **Mechanism** | ✗ Unknown — we don't understand *why* it worked |
| **Evidence duration** | △ Limited — only 8 weeks of post-switch data |
| **Unintended consequences** | ✓ None detected on other metrics |

## **Final Verdict**

**APPROVE PHASED ROLLOUT**

The evidence supports a genuine, positive effect on service quality. While the improvement is modest and the mechanism remains unclear, there is no evidence of harm and the statistical case is strong. A phased rollout balances the opportunity to extend benefits to more branches with prudent monitoring to confirm the effect persists at scale.

**Conditions for full system-wide adoption:**
- Effect size remains ≥1.5 points in expanded rollout
- No negative impacts emerge on staffing costs or operations
- Investigation yields plausible explanation for the improvement

---

*Analysis prepared from 17 weeks of data (weeks 6–22) covering 150 branches. Methodology: difference-in-differences with cluster-robust standard errors.*
