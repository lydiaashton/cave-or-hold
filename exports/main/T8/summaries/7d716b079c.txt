# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** [Current Date]  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2 points relative to the old procedure.** While scores declined systemwide after week 15, branches with the new procedure declined less. The evidence is statistically significant (p < 0.001). We recommend proceeding with rollout to capture this benefit.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Studied**
- **709 branches** tracked weekly from week 6 through week 22
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **284 branches** continued with the old procedure (comparison group)
- Service scores average around 50; higher scores indicate better service

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches with old procedure | 52.0 | 48.9 | −3.1 |
| Branches with new procedure | 53.0 | 51.9 | −1.1 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

While service scores declined across the cooperative after week 15 (likely due to external factors), branches using the new procedure declined **2 points less** than those using the old procedure.

### **How Confident Are We?**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated improvement | +2.0 points |
| 95% confidence range | +1.5 to +2.5 points |
| Statistical significance | p < 0.001 (highly reliable) |

The effect is **robust**—it holds when we control for staffing levels, visitor volumes, and inherent differences between branches.

### **Implementation Pattern**
Benefits grew over time as staff gained familiarity:
- **Weeks 15–17:** Modest improvement (+0.5 to −1.7 points)
- **Weeks 18–22:** Stronger, consistent improvement (+2.7 to +4.4 points)

This suggests a **3–4 week learning curve** before full benefits are realized.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **1. Approve full rollout** to all 284 remaining branches | Clear evidence of positive impact |
| **2. Plan for 4-week transition period** per branch | Benefits build as staff gain experience |
| **3. Provide training materials** from early-adopter branches | Accelerate the learning curve |
| **4. Monitor scores for 12 weeks post-rollout** | Confirm effect holds at full scale |
| **5. Investigate systemwide score decline** after week 15 | External factors may need attention |

---

## **Final Verdict**

**ROLL OUT THE NEW PROCEDURE.**

The data supports adoption. The new procedure delivers a **real, measurable benefit** of approximately 2 service score points per branch. Across 284 branches, this represents meaningful improvement in cooperative-wide service quality. The statistical evidence is strong enough to act on with confidence.

The systemwide decline in scores after week 15 warrants separate investigation, but it does not change the conclusion: **branches using the new procedure outperformed those that did not.**

---

*Analysis based on 12,053 weekly observations using difference-in-differences methodology with branch-level fixed effects and clustered standard errors.*
