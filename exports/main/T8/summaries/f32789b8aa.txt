# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores by approximately 2 points (4% improvement).** The effect is statistically significant and the study design supports a causal interpretation. However, the effect is modest in magnitude and there are early signs it may diminish over time.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but monitor closely to confirm effects persist and assess whether the improvement justifies any additional costs.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Did the new procedure change service scores?** | **Yes** |
| **Direction of change** | Positive (improvement) |
| **Confidence in finding** | High (p = 0.0003) |
| **Practical significance** | Modest (~2 points, 4%) |
| **Rollout recommendation** | **Proceed with monitoring** |

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches studied** | 150 total (90 switched, 60 control) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences analysis |

### **Results**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Treatment effect** | **+2.20 points** |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | 1.03 to 3.38 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.0003 |
| **Relative improvement** | ~4.4% above baseline |
| **Effect size** | 0.24 standard deviations |

### **Why We Trust These Results**

1. **Valid comparison:** Before the switch, both groups followed parallel trends (p = 0.88), meaning any post-switch difference can be attributed to the procedure change.

2. **Persistent effect:** The improvement was observed in all 8 post-switch weeks.

3. **Robust to other factors:** The result holds after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume.

4. **Clean data:** All 150 branches have complete data for all 17 weeks with no inconsistencies.

### **Caveats**

1. **Modest effect:** At 2 points, the improvement is meaningful but not transformative.

2. **Possible fade:** Effects were stronger in weeks 15–19 (~3 points) and smaller in weeks 20–22 (~1 point). More data is needed to confirm whether this is a real pattern.

3. **Limited follow-up:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data; long-term sustainability is unknown.
