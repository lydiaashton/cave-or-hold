# Library Cooperative Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis

**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared for:** Board of Directors

---

## Bottom Line

**The evidence is inconclusive but encouraging.** Service scores rose 2 points more in switching branches than in non-switching branches, aligning with expectations. However, the result is **not statistically significant** (p=0.105), meaning we cannot confidently distinguish a real effect from random variation. The 95% confidence interval (-0.4 to +4.5 points) includes both no effect and a meaningful improvement.

**Recommendation:** Given the positive direction, low implementation risk, and potential 4% service improvement, we suggest **continuing the rollout with enhanced monitoring** rather than halting or accelerating.

---

## Key Findings

### The Numbers

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches that switched (20) | 49.69 | 51.72 | **+2.03** |
| Branches that didn't switch (14) | 50.89 | 50.90 | +0.01 |

**Estimated Impact: +2.02 points** (Difference-in-Differences)

### Statistical Confidence

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +2.02 | Best guess of the effect |
| p-value | 0.105 | Not statistically significant |
| 95% confidence interval | -0.4 to +4.5 | True effect could be zero or negative |
| Branches improving | 70% vs. 50% | Treatment branches more likely to improve |

### What We Can and Cannot Say

| Statement | Supported? |
|-----------|------------|
| "Scores went up in switching branches" | ✓ Yes |
| "The increase was larger than in control branches" | ✓ Yes |
| "The procedure caused the improvement" | ⚠️ Suggestive, not proven |
| "We are confident the effect is real" | ✗ No |
| "The effect is definitely positive" | ✗ No |

## Final Verdict

**PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT — CONDITIONAL APPROVAL**

The board should **authorize continued rollout** of the new procedure under the following conditions:

| Condition | Timeline | Owner |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Expand to all remaining branches | Next 8 weeks | Operations |
| Collect weekly service scores | Ongoing | Analytics |
| Interim review with expanded data | 12 weeks post-decision | Board |
| Final go/no-go on permanent adoption | 6 months post-decision | Board |

### Why This Verdict?

| Consideration | Weight | Assessment |
|---------------|--------|------------|
| Effect direction | High | ✓ Positive, as expected |
| Effect magnitude | Medium | ✓ Potentially meaningful (4%) |
| Statistical certainty | High | ⚠️ Inconclusive (needs more data) |
| Risk of harm | High | ✓ Low (no systematic declines) |
| Cost of reversal | Low | ✓ Procedure can be modified |
| Opportunity cost of delay | Medium | ⚠️ Branches wait for potential benefits |

**Bottom line:** The data does not prove the procedure works, but it does not warn against it either. The balance of evidence, risk, and opportunity favors moving forward while gathering more information.
