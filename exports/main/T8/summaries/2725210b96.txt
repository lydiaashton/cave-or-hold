# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Service Score Impact Analysis**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Data Period:** Weeks 6–22 (17 weeks, 34 branches)

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive association** with service scores (+1.7 points, ~3.4% improvement), and there is a **92% probability the true effect is beneficial**. However, with only 34 branches in the study, we lack the statistical power to confirm this with conventional certainty. 

**Recommendation:** The evidence supports **continuing and expanding the pilot** rather than halting or mandating. Gather more data before making a final system-wide decision.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **DO NOT ROLL OUT SYSTEM-WIDE YET — BUT DO NOT STOP THE PILOT EITHER.**
>
> **Verdict:** *Proceed with cautious expansion.* The evidence suggests the new procedure likely provides modest benefit, but the study is too small to be certain. Expand the pilot to additional volunteer branches and collect 3–4 more months of data before making a final rollout decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. What the Data Shows**
| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.7 points** | Scores increased relative to control branches |
| Direction of effect | **92% probability positive** | Evidence leans toward improvement |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.15 | Not conclusive at conventional thresholds |
| 95% confidence interval | **-0.6 to +4.0 points** | True effect could be slightly negative or moderately positive |

### **2. Why Results Are Inconclusive**
The study has **limited statistical power**:
- Only **34 branches** total (20 switched, 14 control)
- Only **8 weeks** of post-switch data
- With this design, we could only reliably detect effects of **~4.5 points or larger**

A real effect of 1.7 points would frequently appear "non-significant" in a study this size. **Non-significance does not mean no effect.**

### **3. Branch-Level Pattern**
- **10 of 20 switched branches** showed improved scores
- **10 of 20 switched branches** showed declined scores
- This variation is consistent with a modest average effect combined with normal week-to-week fluctuation

### **4. No Evidence of Harm**
The lower bound of the confidence interval (-0.6 points) suggests the procedure is **unlikely to meaningfully worsen** service scores.
