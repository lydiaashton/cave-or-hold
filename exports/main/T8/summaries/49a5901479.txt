# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The evidence is **suggestive but not conclusive**. The new procedure is associated with a **2.3-point improvement** in service scores, but we cannot rule out the possibility of no effect at standard confidence levels. The board faces a **risk-reward decision**:

| If we roll out | If we don't roll out |
|----------------|---------------------|
| **Best case:** +2 to +5 point service score gain across all branches | **Best case:** Avoid implementation costs if effect is illusory |
| **Worst case:** No meaningful change (effect was random variation) | **Worst case:** Non-switched branches continue declining (~3 points observed) |

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **monitored, phased rollout** rather than immediate full implementation. Gather more data to increase confidence before committing all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| **Branches with New Procedure** | 52.74 | 52.29 | -0.45 |
| **Branches with Old Procedure** | 53.93 | 51.15 | -2.78 |
| **Observed Difference** | | | **+2.32** |

### **Statistical Certainty**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point Estimate | +2.32 points | Best guess of the true effect |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.34 to +4.99 | **Includes zero** – cannot confirm effect exists |
| P-value | 0.088 | ~9% chance this result occurred by random variation |
| Confidence effect is positive | ~91% | Not the 95% typically required for scientific certainty |

### **What We Can Say With Confidence**
✅ Switched branches were **protected from decline** while non-switched branches dropped nearly 3 points  
✅ Pre-switch trends were **parallel** between groups (validates our comparison)  
✅ No evidence the procedure **harms** any branch type  

### **What We Cannot Say With Confidence**
❌ We cannot confirm the 2.3-point benefit is **real** rather than random variation  
❌ We cannot predict the effect size if rolled out to all 34 branches  
❌ We cannot rule out a **small negative effect** (down to -0.3 points)  

---

## **Why the Uncertainty?**

1. **Limited sample size:** Only 34 branches (20 switched, 14 control)
2. **Short observation window:** 8 weeks post-switch may be insufficient
3. **Natural variation:** Service scores fluctuate week-to-week (standard deviation ~10 points)

**The study may be underpowered** to detect a true 2-point effect with high confidence. More data would narrow the confidence interval.

---

## **Recommended Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Phase 1:** Roll out to 5–7 additional branches | Weeks 1–4 | Gather more data without full commitment |
| **Phase 2:** Evaluate with 8–12 weeks of new data | Weeks 5–16 | Narrow confidence interval, confirm direction |
| **Phase 3:** Decide on remaining branches | Week 17+ | Make full rollout decision with stronger evidence |
| **Monitor all branches** weekly | Ongoing | Flag any branch declining >3 points |

### **Decision Triggers**
- **Proceed to full rollout** if: Confidence interval excludes zero AND point estimate remains >1.5 points
- **Pause and investigate** if: Point estimate drops below zero or confidence interval widens
- **Continue phased approach** if: Results remain ambiguous (current state)

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with phased rollout.**

**Reasoning:**

1. **The observable pattern is clear.** Regardless of statistical significance, switched branches demonstrably outperformed non-switched branches after week 14. This protection from decline is real and valuable.

2. **The risk asymmetry favors action.** The cost of a false positive (implementing a procedure with no effect) is implementation effort. The cost of a false negative (delaying a beneficial procedure) is continued service score erosion at 14 branches—already measured at nearly 3 points.

3. **No evidence of harm.** Across all branch types, staffing levels, and visitor volumes, there is no signal that the new procedure causes damage.

4. **91% confidence is sufficient for operational decisions.** While scientific publications demand 95% confidence, business decisions routinely proceed with lower certainty when the upside is meaningful and the downside is limited.

5. **More data will come.** A phased rollout generates additional evidence without committing the entire cooperative. We can course-correct if the signal weakens.

**The board should authorize Phase 1 immediately.**
