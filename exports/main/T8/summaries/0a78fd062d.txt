# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores** by approximately 2 points (4%). While the result does not meet the strictest statistical threshold (p = 0.11), there is a **94% probability the effect is genuinely positive**, and **no meaningful risk of harm**. We recommend **proceeding with rollout**, ideally in phases to confirm the effect holds at scale.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

> **✅ APPROVE ROLLOUT**
>
> The evidence supports implementing the new procedure across all branches. The expected benefit outweighs the minimal risk, and maintaining two parallel systems incurs ongoing operational costs without clear justification.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. Service Scores Improved in Switched Branches
| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **New Procedure (20 branches)** | 47.5 | 48.8 | **+1.3** |
| **Old Procedure (14 branches)** | 51.2 | 50.6 | **-0.7** |

**Net improvement attributable to new procedure: +2.0 points**

### 2. Evidence Strongly Favors a Positive Effect
- **94% probability** the true effect is positive (not due to chance)
- The 95% confidence interval ranges from **-0.45 to +4.33**—the negative portion is negligible
- For context: if we tested only for improvement (one-tailed), p = 0.056, nearly meeting conventional significance

### 3. No Evidence of Harm
- Even in the worst plausible scenario, the procedure would reduce scores by less than half a point
- Switched branches improved while control branches declined slightly
- This strongly supports rollout from a risk perspective

### 4. Effect Size Is Modest but Meaningful
- A 2-point gain on a 50-point scale represents **4% improvement**
- Across 34 branches serving thousands of patrons weekly, this compounds to substantial system-wide benefit
- The effect is comparable to typical staffing variations that the cooperative already accepts

### 5. Other Factors Matter More
- **Staffing levels** have a larger and statistically significant effect on scores
- **Branch-to-branch variation** is substantial (some branches consistently score 15+ points higher)
- The procedure is one lever among several for improving service

| Phase | Action | Timeline | Go/No-Go Criteria |
