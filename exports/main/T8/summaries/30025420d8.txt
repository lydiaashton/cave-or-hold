# **Library Cooperative Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and stock rotation procedure **produced a statistically significant improvement** in service scores, contrary to initial expectations that it would make no difference. Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **1.85 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure (p = 0.001).

However, the **practical significance is modest**: a 1.85-point gain on a ~50-point scale represents approximately a **3.7% improvement**. Whether this justifies system-wide rollout depends on implementation costs.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **60 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Analysis accounts for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week variations

### Service Score Changes

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 50.04 | 51.39 | +1.35 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.74 | 53.94 | +3.20 |
| **Net Effect of New Procedure** | | | **+1.85** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **95% confidence interval:** 0.73 to 2.97 points
- **P-value:** 0.001 (highly statistically significant)
- **Pre-trend validation:** No significant differences between groups before the switch

### Effect Timeline
The improvement appeared immediately and was sustained over the 8-week post-switch period, with effects ranging from +1.5 to +3.4 points across individual weeks.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**The new procedure works—but the board must decide if it's worth it.**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Statistical evidence** | ✓ Clear positive effect (p = 0.001) |
| **Effect size** | △ Modest (+3.7% relative to baseline) |
| **Durability** | ✓ Sustained over 8 weeks of observation |
| **Implementation cost** | ? Unknown—requires staff input |

**Recommended decision framework:**

| If implementation costs are... | Then... |
|-------------------------------|---------|
| **Low** (minimal training, no equipment) | **Roll out system-wide** |
| **Moderate** (some staff time required) | **Proceed with phased rollout**; monitor ROI |
| **High** (significant retraining or resources) | **Pause**; conduct formal cost-benefit analysis |
