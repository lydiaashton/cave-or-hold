# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Do NOT proceed with full rollout yet. Gather more evidence first.**

The analysis does **not** provide statistically significant evidence that the new procedure improves service scores. While the estimated effect is positive (+1.9 points), the p-value of 0.11 and confidence interval ranging from -0.5 to +4.3 points means we **cannot rule out that the true effect is zero**. Rolling out organization-wide based on this evidence would be premature.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **The evidence is insufficient to support organization-wide rollout at this time.**
>
> | Question | Answer |
> |----------|--------|
> | Did the new procedure improve service scores? | **We cannot say with confidence** |
> | Is the effect statistically significant? | **No** (p = 0.11) |
> | Could the true effect be zero? | **Yes** (confidence interval includes 0) |
> | Should we rollout to all branches now? | **No** |
> | Should we abandon the new procedure? | **No** — continue evaluating |
>
> **Bottom line:** The data are promising but not conclusive. Collect more evidence before committing to full rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **14 branches** maintained the old procedure

### **Results Summary**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +1.9 points |
| Standard error | 1.20 |
| **P-value** | **0.11** (not significant) |
| **95% Confidence interval** | **-0.5 to +4.3 points** |

### **What This Means**

❌ **Not statistically significant:** At p=0.11, we fail to meet the conventional threshold (p<0.05) for concluding the effect is real

❌ **Confidence interval includes zero:** The true effect could be zero, slightly negative, or up to +4 points—we simply don't know

⚠️ **Inconsistent weekly results:** Only 1 of 8 post-switch weeks showed a statistically significant difference (week 16); the other 7 did not

✓ **No evidence of harm:** The lower bound of the confidence interval is -0.5, suggesting any negative effect would be minimal

---

## **Why We Don't Have Clear Answers**

| Limitation | Impact |
|------------|--------|
| Only 34 branches | Limited statistical power |
| Only 8 post-switch weeks | Not enough time to observe stable effects |
| Week-to-week score variability | Makes detecting effects harder |
| Possible selection bias | Branches that switched may differ systematically |

### **Recommended Actions**

### **Decision Framework**

| If... | Then... |
|-------|---------|
| Implementation costs are **low** | Consider gradual rollout while continuing to evaluate |
| Implementation costs are **high** | Wait for stronger evidence before committing |
| Board wants **certainty** | Collect more data; do not rollout yet |
| Board accepts **some risk** | Phased rollout with close monitoring |

---

## **What Would Change Our Conclusion?**

We would recommend rollout if future analysis shows:
- P-value < 0.05
- Confidence interval entirely above zero
- Consistent positive effects across multiple weeks
- Similar results in additional branches

---

## **Conclusion**

The data are **inconclusive**, not supportive. The new procedure may improve service scores, but this study does not demonstrate it with sufficient confidence to justify organization-wide adoption. The responsible course is to gather more evidence before making a major operational change.
