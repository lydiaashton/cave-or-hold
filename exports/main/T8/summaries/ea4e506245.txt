# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure shows a **consistent positive effect** on service scores of approximately **2 points** (about 4% improvement). While this effect is **not statistically significant at conventional levels** (p = 0.11), the evidence is suggestive: all nine post-switch weekly effects were positive, the confidence interval is mostly above zero, and the analysis passes validity checks.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased rollout** to additional branches while continuing to monitor outcomes. The potential benefit likely outweighs implementation risks, but full system-wide adoption should await confirmation from expanded data.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Treatment Effect**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+2.05 points** on service score |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **-0.49 to +4.59 points** |
| p-value | **0.114** |
| Statistically significant | **No** |

### **2. What Happened to Each Group**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Control branches (old procedure) | 47.09 | 45.96 | **-1.13** ↓ |
| Switched branches (new procedure) | 49.36 | 50.25 | **+0.89** ↑ |

Control branches experienced a decline in service scores during the study period, while switched branches maintained and slightly improved their scores.

### **3. Analysis Validity**

The key assumption underlying this analysis—that treatment and control branches were on similar trajectories before the switch—**is supported by the data**:
- Pre-switch trends were statistically indistinguishable between groups (p = 0.85)
- This validates the comparison and suggests the estimate is unbiased

### **4. Effect Over Time**

Weekly effects after the switch ranged from -0.3 to +3.0 points, averaging **+1.5 points**. While no individual week reached statistical significance, **all nine post-switch estimates were positive**, indicating a consistent directional effect.

### **5. Study Scope**

- **34 branches total:** 20 switched to the new procedure, 14 kept the old procedure
- **17 weeks of data:** Weeks 6-22 (switch occurred at week 15)
- **578 observations:** One row per branch per week

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Probably yes** — consistent positive effects across all post-switch weeks |
| **Are we certain?** | **No** — statistical significance not reached (p = 0.11) |
| **Should we roll it out?** | **Yes, but phased** — expand to 5-10 additional branches while monitoring |
| **Should we go system-wide now?** | **No** — await 8-12 more weeks of data from expanded pilot |
| **What's the risk of waiting?** | Opportunity cost of delayed improvement (~2 points per branch) |
| **What's the risk of proceeding?** | Minimal — no evidence of harm; implementation costs only |

**Verdict:** The evidence supports **cautious expansion**. The new procedure shows promise without evident downsides. A phased rollout balances the opportunity for improvement against the need for greater certainty.
