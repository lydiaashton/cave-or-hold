# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Impact of New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure produced a small but statistically significant improvement in service scores (+2 points, p=0.001).** We expected no effect, but the data show a real, modest gain. The improvement is reliable but not large—roughly 4% on the service index. Whether this justifies system-wide rollout depends on implementation costs and board priorities.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. The Effect is Real but Modest

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | +2.05 points | Statistically significant |
| 95% Confidence interval | 0.8 to 3.3 points | True effect likely in this range |
| p-value | 0.001 | Very unlikely to be chance |
| Effect size | 0.21 standard deviations | Small-to-moderate |
| Typical weekly fluctuation | 6.1 points | Effect is ~1/3 of normal noise |

### 2. What Changed

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches (90) | 51.42 | 52.55 | +1.14 |
| Control branches (60) | 52.12 | 51.20 | -0.91 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.05** |

### 3. Why We Trust the Finding

- Switched and control branches had parallel trends before the switch
- 61% of switched branches improved vs. 40% of control branches
- Result holds after controlling for staffing and visitor volume
- Standard errors account for multiple observations per branch

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Recommend rollout to all branches.**

**Reasoning:**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Statistical evidence | ✓ Clear (p=0.001) |
| Direction of effect | ✓ Positive (no harm detected) |
| Consistency | ✓ 61% of switched branches improved |
| Practical significance | △ Modest but meaningful |
| Risk | ✓ Low |

A 4% improvement in service scores is not transformative, but it is **real, repeatable, and cost-free in terms of service quality**. For a public service organization, consistent small gains across 150 branches compound into meaningful system-wide improvement. The procedure should be adopted unless implementation costs are prohibitive.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Quantify implementation costs** for remaining 60 branches
2. **Proceed with rollout** if costs are reasonable
3. **Monitor for 8-12 weeks** post-rollout to confirm effect persists
4. **Document implementation lessons** from the 90 branches already using the new procedure

---

*Analysis based on 2,550 weekly observations from 150 branches. Method: Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors.*
