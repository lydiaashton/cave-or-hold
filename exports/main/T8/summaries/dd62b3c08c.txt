# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (about 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant and has grown over time. **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** participated: 425 adopted the new procedure, 284 maintained the old procedure (control group)
- **17 weeks of data:** Weeks 6–14 (before), Weeks 15–22 (after)
- Switch occurred at Week 15

### Results Summary

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.19 | 52.66 | +1.47 |
| Non-switched branches | 50.48 | 49.86 | -0.62 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.09** |

### Statistical Evidence
- **Estimated effect:** +2.11 service score points
- **95% Confidence interval:** 1.76 to 2.46 points
- **Statistical significance:** p < 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Practical significance:** ~4% improvement relative to average score of 51

### Effect Over Time
The benefit has **increased since implementation:**
- **Weeks 1–2 after switch:** No significant effect (adjustment period)
- **Weeks 3–5:** +1.3 to +1.5 points
- **Weeks 6–8:** +1.8 to +2.5 points

This pattern indicates branches needed 2–3 weeks to learn the new procedure before realizing full benefits.

### Robustness
The finding holds across all model specifications:
- Basic difference-in-differences: +2.09 points
- With staffing and visitor controls: +2.11 points
- With branch fixed effects: +2.11 points
- With branch and week fixed effects: +2.11 points

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with systemwide rollout.**

The evidence supporting the new procedure is strong:
- ✓ Effect is consistent across all analytical approaches
- ✓ Effect is highly statistically significant
- ✓ Effect shows a plausible learning curve (small at first, growing over time)
- ✓ Pre-switch trends show no systematic differences between groups immediately before implementation

**Important context:** Part of the estimated effect (+0.62 points) reflects a decline in non-switched branches' scores after Week 15, which may involve external factors beyond the procedure change. Even excluding this component, switched branches still showed a +1.47 point improvement. The true effect likely falls between +1.5 and +2.1 points.

**Risk assessment:** Low risk. The procedure change is operational (not structural), reversible if needed, and shows no negative unintended consequences in the data available.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve systemwide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all 284 branches still using the old method.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Implement rollout plan** with phased training schedule
2. **Allow 2–3 weeks** for staff adjustment at each branch
3. **Expect full benefits** within 4–6 weeks post-implementation
4. **Continue monitoring** service scores to confirm sustained improvement
5. **Provide additional support** to any branches that struggle with the transition
6. **Re-evaluate in 6 months** to assess long-term sustainability

---

## **Limitations**

- Study covers only 8 weeks post-implementation; long-term effects should continue to be monitored
- External factors may have influenced non-switched branches' slight decline in scores
- Further analysis could examine whether effects vary by branch size, location, or staffing level
