# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

---

**TO:** Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation & Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **2.5 points higher** than comparable branches using the old procedure—a **5% improvement** that is statistically significant (p < 0.001).

**Recommendation: Approve system-wide rollout, with continued monitoring.**

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**
>
> The evidence supports adopting the new procedure across all 150 branches. The estimated benefit (+2.5 points) is statistically robust, practically meaningful, and shows no signs of adverse effects. While long-term durability cannot yet be confirmed, the magnitude of improvement warrants system-wide implementation with appropriate monitoring safeguards.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Evidence

| Metric | Treatment (New Procedure) | Control (Old Procedure) |
|--------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| Number of branches | 90 | 60 |
| Pre-switch score (weeks 6–14) | 50.21 | 49.82 |
| Post-switch score (weeks 15–22) | 52.55 | 49.64 |
| **Change** | **+2.34** | **-0.18** |

**Net effect of new procedure: +2.53 points**

### Statistical Confidence

| Measure | Value |
|---------|-------|
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.22 to 3.83 points |
| p-value | < 0.001 |
| Effect size | ~5% of baseline score |

The entire confidence interval is positive—even the conservative lower bound (1.22 points) represents a meaningful improvement.

### Why We're Confident

1. **Parallel pre-trends:** Treatment and control branches performed similarly before the switch (average difference: 0.39 points, with no systematic divergence)
2. **Robust to controls:** Result holds after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
3. **Precise estimate:** Narrow confidence interval rules out trivial effects

---

## **Important Caveats**

| Limitation | Implication |
|------------|-------------|
| **Non-random assignment** | Branches self-selected into treatment; unobserved differences could affect results |
| **8-week post period** | We cannot yet confirm whether effects persist long-term |
| **Single outcome measure** | Analysis covers service score only; costs and staff feedback not yet assessed |

Despite these limitations, the magnitude and statistical significance of the effect support proceeding with rollout.

---

## **Recommendations**

### Decision Required
☐ **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all remaining branches

### Implementation Plan

| Phase | Timeline | Action |

## **Appendix: Analysis Details**

- **Method:** Difference-in-Differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch)
- **Sample:** 2,550 observations across 150 branches
- **Software:** Python (statsmodels)
