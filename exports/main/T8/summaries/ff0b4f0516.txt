# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Subject: Impact of New Branch Procedure on Service Scores**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The evidence suggests the new procedure improved service scores, but with important caveats.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed service score gains of approximately **2 points** relative to non-switching branches. However, some divergence between groups began before the official switch date, which introduces uncertainty about the exact causal effect. **We recommend proceeding with rollout, but with enhanced monitoring to confirm results.**

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Main Result**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.9 | 53.3 | **+1.3** |
| Non-switched branches | 51.2 | 50.5 | **−0.6** |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.95 points** |

- **Estimated effect: +1.95 points** (95% CI: 1.4 to 2.5)
- **Statistical significance: p < 0.001**

### **2. Important Caveat: Pre-Treatment Differences**

The treatment and control groups showed some divergence **before** the switch:
- Week 13 (2 weeks pre-switch): Treatment group already +0.92 points higher (statistically significant)
- This violates the standard "parallel trends" assumption for causal inference

**What this means:** The true effect could be somewhat smaller than estimated (perhaps +1.0 to +1.5 points rather than +2.0), but a positive effect is still likely because:
- Post-treatment effects are larger and more consistent than pre-treatment differences
- Weeks 13-14 may reflect preparation/anticipation of the switch
- Alternative specifications (excluding weeks 13-14) show effects of +2.18 points

### **3. Effect Persists Over Time**

| Period | Weekly Advantage for Switched Branches |
|--------|----------------------------------------|
| Weeks 15–17 | +2.2 points |
| Weeks 18–22 | +2.8 to +3.2 points |

The growing effect suggests benefits increase with familiarity.

### **4. Robustness**
- Results hold when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume
- Effect is consistent across all 8 post-switch weeks
- 709 branches total (425 treatment, 284 control)

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout, but monitor closely.** Despite the pre-treatment divergence, the weight of evidence supports a genuine positive effect:
- Consistent post-treatment gains across 8 weeks
- Effects larger than any pre-treatment differences
- No plausible alternative explanation for the pattern

However, given the analytical uncertainty, we should verify that the ~2-point improvement replicates when the remaining 284 branches adopt the procedure.
