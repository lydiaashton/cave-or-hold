# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 2025  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive association with service scores** (estimated +1.7 points, ~3% improvement), but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive**. The 95% confidence interval ranges from no effect to a 3.7-point gain. Given the positive direction, low implementation risk, and operational benefits reported by early-adopter branches, we recommend a **pilot expansion with explicit evaluation criteria** rather than immediate full rollout.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **PROCEED WITH CAUTIOUS EXPANSION**
>
> The data supports continuing the new procedure, but not yet committing to cooperative-wide adoption. Expand to 10–15 additional branches as a structured pilot, with a formal review after 12 weeks. If the effect persists and strengthens with additional data, proceed to full rollout. If the effect diminishes or remains inconclusive, reconsider the investment.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What the Data Shows

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 50.89 | 50.92 | +0.03 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 52.01 | 53.79 | +1.78 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.75** |

### What the Statistics Tell Us

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +1.75 points | Best estimate of the effect |
| P-value | 0.098 | Not significant at p < 0.05 threshold |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.31 to +3.69 | True effect could be zero or modestly positive |
| Bootstrap probability (effect > 0) | 97.8% | Strong indication the effect is positive |

### Why the Uncertainty?
- **34 branches** provides limited statistical power for this type of analysis
- We would need approximately **48 branches** to detect this effect size with conventional confidence
- Service scores naturally vary week-to-week, adding noise to the measurement

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
