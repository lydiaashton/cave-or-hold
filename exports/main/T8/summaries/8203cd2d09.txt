## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores—contrary to initial expectations.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **2.1 points** (approximately 4%) relative to branches that kept the old procedure. This result is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and methodologically robust. **We recommend proceeding with a system-wide rollout, with continued monitoring to confirm the effect persists at scale.**

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**1. Statistically Significant Improvement**
- Treatment branches gained **2.1 points** relative to control branches
- 95% confidence interval: **1.0 to 3.3 points** (does not include zero)
- P-value: **0.0003** (highly significant)

**2. Valid Comparison**
- 90 branches switched; 60 branches did not
- **Pre-trends were parallel** (p = 0.36), confirming groups were comparable before the switch
- Results hold with branch and week fixed effects, controlling for unobserved factors

**3. No Confounding Factors**
- Staffing and visit patterns did not differ between groups after the switch
- The improvement is attributable to the procedure change itself

**4. Unexpected but Favorable Result**
- Initial expectation was no effect on service scores
- The positive effect suggests the new procedure has unanticipated benefits for patron experience

3. **Continue monitoring** service scores as additional branches adopt the procedure (the 60 control branches provide an ongoing comparison group)
