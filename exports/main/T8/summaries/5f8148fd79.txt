# **Board Summary: New Shelving Procedure Impact Analysis**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores of approximately 2 points.** The evidence is robust: the effect is consistent across all post-implementation weeks and unlikely to be due to chance. 

**Recommendation**: Proceed with rollout **pending confirmation that implementation costs are justified** by the service quality gains. A 4% improvement in service scores should be weighed against training, staffing, and operational costs.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Yes** — the effect is real and statistically significant. |
| **Is the evidence reliable?** | **Yes** — strong study design with 709 branches and robust statistical methods. |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes, conditionally** — proceed if implementation costs are reasonable relative to the value of a 4% service improvement. |
| **What's the risk?** | **Low** — the procedure demonstrably works; the main uncertainty is whether benefits exceed costs at scale. |

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Clear Positive Effect

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated effect** | +1.9 points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | +1.4 to +2.5 points |
| **Relative improvement** | ~4% of average score |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |

### 2. Effect Is Consistent Over Time

All 8 weeks after implementation showed positive effects ranging from +2.5 to +3.3 points, with no evidence of the effect fading.

### 3. Study Limitations

| Issue | Assessment |
|-------|------------|
| **Pre-trend validity** | 7 of 8 pre-treatment weeks show parallel trends; week 13 shows slight divergence (minor concern) |
| **Effect size** | Modest (+1.9 points on a ~50-point scale) |
| **Cost data** | Not included in this analysis |
| **Long-term sustainability** | Only 8 weeks of post-implementation data available |

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | **Quantify implementation costs** (training, labor, equipment) before final rollout decision. |
| **2** | **If costs are reasonable**, proceed with phased rollout to all branches. |
| **3** | **Monitor outcomes** for 6-12 months to confirm effects persist at scale. |
| **4** | **Document implementation practices** from high-performing branches. |

---

## **Study Details**

- **Design**: Difference-in-differences (425 treatment branches vs. 284 control branches)
- **Period**: 17 weeks (weeks 6-22); switch occurred week 15
- **Observations**: 12,053 branch-week records
- **Controls**: Staffing levels, visitor volume
