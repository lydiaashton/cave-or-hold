# **Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with caution toward rollout.** The new procedure is associated with a **2.2-point improvement** in service scores—a statistically significant effect we did not expect. However, before committing to system-wide adoption, we recommend a **piloted expansion** with cost tracking to ensure benefits outweigh implementation expenses.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What the Data Show
| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Service score improvement | **+2.2 points** (4.6% relative to baseline) |
| Statistical significance | **p < 0.001** |
| 95% confidence interval | 1.5 to 3.0 points |
| Pre-switch group equivalence | **Confirmed** (difference: -0.02, p=0.97) |
| Effect persistence | **Yes**—consistent across all 8 post-switch weeks |

### What We Don't Know
- **Why it works**: The mechanism is unclear. Understanding this would improve implementation.
- **Implementation costs**: Staff time, training, and operational disruption haven't been measured.
- **System-wide effect**: With all branches switched, we lose the ability to verify the effect holds at scale.
- **Operational meaning**: We lack context on whether 2.2 points translates to meaningful patron experience improvements.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve a phased rollout** rather than immediate system-wide adoption:

1. **Expand to 30 additional branches** (half of remaining control group)
2. **Track implementation costs** (staff hours, training time, any disruption)
3. **Gather qualitative feedback** from branch staff on what's working
4. **Re-evaluate in 8 weeks** with full cost-benefit analysis before final decision

---

## **Next Steps**

| Action | Timeline | Owner |
|--------|----------|-------|

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **The new procedure improves service scores, but we cannot yet recommend full rollout.**
>
> The evidence for a real effect is strong. The evidence for a *worthwhile* effect is incomplete. Approve the phased expansion with cost tracking, then return to the board with a full cost-benefit analysis before committing to system-wide adoption.

---

*Analysis based on weekly service scores from weeks 6–22 across 150 branches. Method: difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects. The 90 switched branches and 60 control branches showed equivalent pre-trends, supporting causal interpretation of the post-switch difference.*
