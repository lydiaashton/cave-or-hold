# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure likely improved service scores, but confidence is moderate.** Branches that adopted the new procedure maintained or improved their scores while control branches declined. The estimated effect is **+1.9 points** (approximately 4%), statistically significant. However, because treatment branches had slightly different pre-existing trends, the true effect could be somewhat smaller.

**Recommendation: Approve rollout with monitoring safeguards.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Analyzed**
- **709 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **284 branches** kept the old procedure
- Service scores average ~50 points (higher is better)

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| New Procedure | 51.6 | 52.4 | **+0.7** |
| Old Procedure | 50.2 | 49.0 | **−1.2** |
| **Estimated Effect** | | | **+1.9 points** |

### **Confidence Assessment**

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Statistical significance** | ✓ Strong (p < 0.001) |
| **Effect consistency** | ✓ Consistent across performance quartiles (1.9–2.2 points) |
| **Parallel trends assumption** | ⚠️ Violated (groups had different pre-trends, p=0.003) |
| **Baseline gap** | ⚠️ Treatment branches already scored ~1.5 points higher before switch |
| **Post-switch pattern** | ✓ Gap widened beyond normal pre-period variation |

### **Interpretation**

The parallel trends violation means the 1.9-point estimate **may be inflated**. However, three factors support a genuine treatment effect:

1. **Control group declined** while treatment group held steady—suggesting the new procedure provides resilience
2. **Effect is consistent** across low, medium, and high-performing branches
3. **Gap widening (1.9 points)** exceeds typical pre-period fluctuation (standard deviation: 0.74 points)

**Most plausible estimate:** True effect is **positive but possibly 1.0–1.9 points** rather than the full 1.9 points.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure help? | **Probably yes** |
| How confident are we? | **Moderate** (not definitive) |
| Is the effect meaningful? | **Yes**—even 1 point represents ~2% improvement at system scale |
| Should we roll out? | **Yes, with safeguards** |

**Rationale:** The weight of evidence favors the new procedure. Even if the true effect is at the lower end of our range (~1 point), system-wide adoption would yield meaningful service improvements. The risk of proceeding is low (the procedure has not caused harm), while the risk of delay is continued underperformance at 284 branches.
