# **Board Summary: New Branch Procedure Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**We cannot confirm with statistical confidence that the new procedure improved service scores**, but all evidence points in a positive direction. The estimated improvement is **+1.6 points**, with a 95% confidence interval ranging from **-0.4 to +3.7 points**. This means:

- The data is **compatible with no effect** (the interval includes zero)
- The data is **also compatible with a meaningful improvement** (up to +3.7 points)
- The **most likely value** is a modest gain of ~1.6 points (about 3%)

**Critical finding:** The benefit **grows significantly over time** (+0.35 points per week, p=0.012). Branches that adopted the procedure later showed larger gains, suggesting an adjustment period is needed.

**Recommendation:** Continue the pilot with expanded monitoring. A full rollout is **not yet justified by the evidence**, but neither is abandoning the procedure. Gather 8–12 more weeks of data before making a system-wide decision.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Should we roll out to all branches now?** | **No** — Evidence is insufficient |
| **Should we abandon the new procedure?** | **No** — Evidence points positive |
| **Should we continue the pilot?** | **Yes** — Low cost, potential upside |
| **When should we revisit this decision?** | **3 months** — With additional data |

**Rationale:** The statistically significant finding that effects grow over time suggests the procedure has genuine merit but requires an adjustment period. With only 8 weeks of post-switch data, we are measuring the procedure before it reaches full effectiveness. Extending the pilot is the lowest-risk path to a confident decision.

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| Branches studied | 34 |
| Switched to new procedure | 20 |
| Remained on old procedure (control) | 14 |
| Weeks of data | 17 (weeks 6–22) |
| Switch implemented | Week 15 |

### **Service Score Changes**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** | 50.38 | 51.96 | **+1.58** |
| **Non-switched branches** | 52.30 | 52.27 | -0.03 |

**Estimated effect of new procedure: +1.6 points**

### **Statistical Confidence**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +1.68 | Best guess of the true effect |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.39 to +3.74 | Range of plausible values |
| P-value | 0.12 | 12% probability of this result if there's no true effect |
| **Statistically significant?** | **No** | Above the 5% threshold |

**What this means:** We cannot rule out that the observed improvement occurred by chance. However, roughly **88% of plausible values are positive**, and the upper bound (+3.7) would represent a meaningful improvement.

### **Effect Grows Over Time (Statistically Significant)**
- Each week after implementation, switched branches gained an **additional 0.35 points** (p = 0.012)
- By week 22 (8 weeks post-switch), the cumulative benefit reached approximately **2.8 points**
- **This finding IS statistically significant** and suggests the procedure requires time to show full benefits

### **Why the Uncertainty?**
- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** per branch
- **34 branches** total (modest sample for this type of analysis)
- Service scores naturally vary week-to-week (standard deviation ~10-12 points)
- The true effect may be real but too small to detect with current data

---

## **Next Steps**

### **1. Extend the Pilot (Recommended)**
- Continue collecting data for **8–12 additional weeks**
- This will narrow the confidence interval and provide a clearer answer
- Cost: Minimal (procedure is already implemented at 20 branches)

### **2. Maintain Control Group**
- Keep the 14 non-switched branches on the old procedure during the extension
- This preserves our ability to measure the true effect

### **3. Document Implementation Quality**
- Survey branch managers about fidelity to the new procedure
- Identify whether branches implementing more fully show larger gains
- Use findings to improve training materials

## **Appendix: Technical Notes**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch and week fixed effects
- **Standard errors:** Clustered by branch (accounts for repeated observations)
- **Controls:** Staffing index and visits index
- **Parallel trends:** Pre-switch differences between groups were stable (treatment group averaged 1.9 points lower throughout the pre-period)
- **Limitation:** Small sample size limits statistical power to detect modest effects
