# **SUBJECT: Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Branch Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure protected service scores from a system-wide decline and should be rolled out to all branches.**

After week 15, service scores dropped across the system. Branches using the new procedure held steady while others declined by 2.4 points. The net benefit is **preventing a 2.3-point drop**—a meaningful protection of service quality.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What the Data Shows

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| **New Procedure** | 51.92 | 51.75 | -0.17 |
| **Old Procedure** | 52.11 | 49.67 | **-2.44** |

### The Effect

- **Treatment benefit:** +2.27 points relative to non-adopters (95% CI: 1.8 to 2.7; p < 0.001)
- **In practical terms:** The new procedure prevented decline, it did not create improvement
- **System-wide context:** Something caused scores to drop after week 15 (seasonal? operational?); the new procedure shielded branches from this

### Why We're Confident

1. **Parallel pre-trends:** Both groups moved together before week 15
2. **Robust to controls:** Effect holds accounting for staffing, visits, and branch characteristics
3. **Clean identification:** 425 branches switched, 284 did not—natural experiment design

---

## **Final Verdict**

### ✅ **PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

**Confidence Level: High**

The evidence is clear and statistically robust. While the new procedure does not elevate scores above baseline, it provides critical resilience against decline. With 284 branches still on the old procedure, we recommend:

1. **Immediate action:** Begin rollout planning within 2 weeks
2. **Priority implementation:** Complete within 12 weeks
3. **Parallel investigation:** Determine what caused the system-wide decline after week 15

**Risk of inaction:** Keeping branches on the old procedure leaves them vulnerable to the same 2+ point decline observed in non-adopting branches.

## **Important Caveat**

The analysis reveals an unanswered question: **Why did service scores decline system-wide after week 15?** This affected non-switched branches and would have affected switched branches without the new procedure. We recommend investigating this underlying cause in parallel with the rollout.
