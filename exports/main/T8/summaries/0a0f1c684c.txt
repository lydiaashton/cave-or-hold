# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Approve rollout with monitoring.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure achieved service scores **2.5 points higher** than branches that kept the old procedure. This effect is statistically reliable (p < 0.001) and represents approximately **5% better performance**. However, about 28% of switched branches did not see improvement. We recommend proceeding with system-wide rollout **conditional on continued monitoring** to confirm effects persist and to support branches that may need additional implementation help.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Works On Average**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (90) | 50.4 | 52.2 | **+1.8** |
| Control branches (60) | 51.6 | 50.9 | **−0.7** |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.5** |

The 2.5-point advantage is the estimated impact of the new procedure itself, isolated from seasonal changes and system-wide trends.

### **2. The Result Is Statistically Sound**

- **p-value:** 0.0002 (less than 0.1% probability this is due to chance)
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.2 to 3.8 points
- **Validation:** Both groups followed parallel trends before the switch
- **Consistency:** 7 of 8 post-switch weeks showed positive effects

### **3. Not All Branches Benefited Equally**

Among the 90 switched branches:
- **72%** saw their service scores improve
- **28%** saw no improvement or declined
- Individual branch changes ranged from **−7.4 to +9.8 points**

This variation suggests implementation quality or branch-specific factors may influence outcomes.

### **4. Results Hold After Accounting for Other Factors**

The analysis controlled for:
- Weekly staffing levels
- Visitor volume
- Seasonal variation

The procedure effect remained significant after adjusting for all of these variables.

**Verdict: PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

The evidence meets the threshold for action. The new procedure delivers a real, measurable improvement in service quality for most branches. The risks of proceeding are manageable with proper monitoring and support. The greater risk is delaying a proven improvement while maintaining the status quo.

**Conditions for approval:**
1. Commit to 6-month post-rollout evaluation
2. Allocate resources for branch-level support during implementation
3. Establish clear metrics to identify and assist underperforming branches

---

## **Conclusion**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure delivers a clear, measurable improvement in branch service quality. With 90 branches already successfully operating under the new system and a validated 2.5-point service score gain, the cooperative is well-positioned to expand this practice system-wide.

**We recommend the Board approve full rollout with the monitoring conditions outlined above.**
