**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

Contrary to expectations, the new procedure **improved service scores by 1.8 points** (3.5% above baseline). The result is statistically robust and validated by multiple tests. **We recommend system-wide rollout.**

---

## **Final Verdict**

**PROCEED WITH SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**

The evidence is clear and conclusive: the new stock rotation and shelving procedure produces a meaningful, statistically significant improvement in service scores. This finding surprised our team as much as it may surprise you—we entered this analysis expecting no detectable effect. The data says otherwise.

With 150 branches tracked over 17 weeks, rigorous statistical testing, and multiple validation checks all confirming the same result, we have high confidence in this recommendation. Delaying rollout would mean withholding a proven service improvement from patrons at 60 branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Treatment Effect
| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 51.9 | 51.3 | –0.6 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 51.2 | 52.4 | +1.2 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.8** |

### Statistical Evidence
- **Effect size:** +1.79 points (95% CI: 0.65 to 2.93)
- **P-value:** 0.002 (less than 1% probability of chance result)
- **Permutation test:** Actual effect exceeds 99% of random assignments

### Validation Tests Passed
✓ **Parallel trends:** Treatment and control branches tracked together before the switch (p=0.92)  
✓ **Placebo test:** No effect when testing fake switch dates in pre-period (p=0.85)  
✓ **No confounding:** Staffing and visitor volumes unchanged between groups
