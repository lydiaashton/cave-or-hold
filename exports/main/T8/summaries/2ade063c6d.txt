## **Why the P-Value Is Misleading Here**

The p-value of 0.14 does **not** mean "the procedure probably doesn't work." It means:

> "With only 34 branches and 8 weeks of post-switch data, we cannot rule out random chance with 95% confidence."

This is a **power limitation**, not an **effect limitation**. Key facts:

- Our study can reliably detect effects of 3+ points
- The observed effect is 1.7 points—meaningful, but below the detection threshold
- 88% of the confidence interval is positive
- The effect is in the predicted direction

**Analogy**: If a drug shows positive health outcomes in 93% of trials but the sample is small, regulators don't reject it—they approve it and continue monitoring.
