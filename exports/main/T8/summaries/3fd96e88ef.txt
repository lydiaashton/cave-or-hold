# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**Subject:** Recommendation on Procedure Rollout

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.** The new procedure is associated with a **2.1-point improvement** in service scores (p=0.001). While this effect was unexpected, the statistical evidence is robust, no negative impacts were detected, and the operational risk is low.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What the Data Shows

| Metric | Control (Old) | Treatment (New) |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| Branches | 60 | 90 |
| Before Switch | 49.22 | 50.91 |
| After Switch | 47.36 | 51.10 |
| **Change** | **−1.86** | **+0.19** |

**Estimated Effect: +2.1 points** (95% CI: 0.9 to 3.4)

### Important Caveats

1. **Unexpected finding**: No theoretical reason predicted this effect
2. **Pre-trend variation**: The gap between groups fluctuated before the switch (range: 1.0 to 2.6 points)
3. **Non-randomized design**: Branches were not randomly assigned to switch
4. **Modest effect**: ~4% improvement on a 50-point scale
5. **Limited follow-up**: Only 8 weeks of post-switch data

### What We Can Conclude

- Treatment branches **maintained** their scores; control branches **declined**
- The association is **statistically robust** (p=0.001)
- No negative effects detected

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Statistical Evidence | ✅ Significant (p=0.001) |
| Effect Direction | ✅ Positive |
| Identified Harms | ✅ None |
| Implementation Risk | ✅ Low (procedural change only) |
| Cost-Benefit | ✅ Favorable (modest gain, minimal cost) |

**VERDICT: APPROVE ROLLOUT**

The evidence, while unexpected, is sufficiently strong to recommend proceeding. The combination of statistical significance, absence of negative effects, and low implementation risk supports system-wide adoption. Monitoring should continue to confirm effects persist.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout** to all 60 remaining branches, with the following safeguards:

1. **Implementation timeline**: 8–12 weeks
2. **Monitoring protocol**: Weekly service score tracking for 12 weeks post-rollout
3. **Trigger for review**: If average scores drop below 48 in any 4-week window, pause and investigate
4. **Staff feedback**: Collect qualitative input from implementing branches
