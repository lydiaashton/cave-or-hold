# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Unexpected positive signal, but warrants careful consideration.** The new procedure is associated with a **2.1-point increase** in service scores (p = 0.0003). However, this effect was not anticipated, the practical magnitude is modest, and the underlying mechanism is unclear. **Recommendation: proceed with a phased rollout while investigating why this effect occurred.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Statistical Results
| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| **Estimated impact** | +2.13 points | Statistically significant |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | +0.98 to +3.28 | Effect unlikely to be zero |
| **Effect size** | 0.21 SD | Small-to-medium practical impact |
| **Context** | ~57% of typical week-to-week variation | Modest but meaningful |

### 2. What We Observed
| Group | Branches | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|----------|------------|-------------|--------|
| **Switched** | 90 | 50.5 | 51.0 | +0.5 |
| **Not switched** | 60 | 51.3 | 49.7 | -1.6 |

### 3. Important Caveats

⚠️ **Unexpected finding:** This procedure change was not designed to affect service scores. The positive effect may be:
- A genuine benefit we didn't anticipate
- Indirect (e.g., staff morale, workflow efficiency)
- Spurious correlation requiring further validation

⚠️ **Modest magnitude:** A 2-point gain is statistically real but represents less than one typical week-to-week fluctuation.

⚠️ **Methodological limitations:** Autocorrelation in weekly data may affect standard error estimates.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the procedure change affect service scores?** | **Yes** — the evidence supports a real positive effect |
| **Is the effect large enough to matter?** | **Modestly** — meaningful but not transformative |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes, with monitoring** — benefits likely outweigh risks |
| **Do we need to understand the mechanism?** | **Yes** — investigate why this occurred |

**Verdict: PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

The statistical evidence is strong enough to act on. Even if the true effect is at the lower bound of our confidence interval (~1 point), there is no evidence of harm, and the operational change has already been implemented successfully at 90 branches. However, the unexpected nature of this finding means we should:

1. Monitor closely during rollout
2. Investigate the underlying mechanism
3. Be prepared to adjust if the effect doesn't replicate
