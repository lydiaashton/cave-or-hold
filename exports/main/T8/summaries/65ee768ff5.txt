# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure achieved service scores **1.9 points higher** than branches using the old procedure—a **4% improvement** that is statistically significant.

**VERDICT: Roll out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Service Score Improvement | +1.91 points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.38 to 2.45 points |
| Statistical Confidence | >99.9% (p < 0.001) |
| Relative Improvement | 3.8% above baseline |
| Effect Durability | Sustained over 8 weeks |

### **Study Overview**

- **709 branches** participated (425 switched, 284 maintained prior procedure)
- **17 weeks** of observation (weeks 6–22 of the year)
- New procedure implemented starting **week 15**
- Analysis controlled for staffing levels, visitor volume, and branch-specific factors

### **Evidence Quality**

The study design isolates the procedure's effect by comparing switched branches against non-switched branches before and after implementation.

**Strengths:**
- Large sample size (12,000+ observations)
- Clear timing of intervention
- Statistically robust result (p < 0.001)
- Effect grew over time, suggesting genuine adoption benefit

**Caveat:**
- Switched branches showed slight improvement in the 2–3 weeks before the switch, possibly due to early preparation or selection effects. This means the true causal effect may be modestly smaller than 1.9 points, but the post-switch acceleration confirms the procedure itself added value.

### **Implementation Considerations**

1. **Phased approach recommended:** The effect grew from ~1.4 points to ~3.2 points over the 8-week observation period, suggesting branches improve with experience.

2. **Set realistic expectations:** Plan for 1–2 point improvement per branch.

3. **Continue monitoring:** Track service scores during rollout to confirm the effect replicates at scale.
