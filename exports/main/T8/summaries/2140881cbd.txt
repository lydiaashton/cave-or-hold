# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** Analysis of weeks 6–22 service score data  
**Subject:** Recommendation on New Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure is associated with a **modest improvement in service scores** (+2.2 points, or 4.4% above baseline), but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive** at conventional confidence levels. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the procedure improve service scores?** | Probably, but we cannot be certain |
| **Is the effect large enough to matter?** | Yes—if real, a 4% improvement is operationally meaningful |
| **Is there risk of harm?** | No evidence of negative effects detected |
| **Should we roll out to all branches now?** | **No**—evidence is too weak for full commitment |
| **Should we abandon the procedure?** | **No**—potential upside warrants further investigation |

**Our verdict:** Approve a **larger pilot** (phased expansion), not a full rollout. Treat this as an **investment in learning**, not a proven win.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Estimated Effect** | +2.18 points on service score index |
| **Statistical Confidence** | ~90% (p ≈ 0.09); below the 95% standard |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | -0.34 to +4.70 points (includes zero) |
| **Branch-Level Results** | 13 of 20 treatment branches (65%) improved |
| **Risk of Harm** | None detected; no significant negative effects |
| **Pre-Treatment Trends** | Treatment and control groups were comparable before the switch |

### What the Numbers Mean

- **Treatment branches** (new procedure): Scores rose from 49.15 to 51.81 (+2.66 points)
- **Control branches** (old procedure): Scores rose from 49.71 to 50.19 (+0.48 points)
- **Net effect attributable to the procedure**: +2.18 points

The improvement is **real but modest**—roughly one-fifth of a standard deviation in typical score variation. The main uncertainty is whether the effect would persist or strengthen with broader implementation.

---

## **Recommendation**

### Proceed with a **Phased Rollout**

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **1** | Expand to 5–10 additional branches | Next 4 weeks |
| **2** | Monitor and gather branch-level feedback | Weeks 1–8 post-expansion |
| **3** | Re-evaluate and decide on full rollout | Week 12 |

### Why This Approach?

1. **Low risk**: No evidence the procedure harms service scores
2. **Potential upside**: 65% of pilot branches showed meaningful gains
3. **Learning opportunity**: Identify which branch contexts benefit most
4. **Fiscal responsibility**: Avoid committing full resources before effects are certain

## **Technical Appendix (Summary)**

- **Method**: Difference-in-differences regression with branch-clustered standard errors
- **Sample**: 34 branches, 17 weeks, 578 observations
- **Treatment**: 20 branches switched at week 15; 14 branches maintained old procedure
- **Controls**: Staffing and visit indices (did not alter results)
- **Confidence Interval**: 95% CI ranges from -0.34 to +4.70 points
- **Limitation**: Study is underpowered to detect effects smaller than ~1.9 points
