# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Cannot recommend system-wide rollout at this time.**

While branches using the new procedure achieved higher service scores than those that did not, the analysis cannot confidently attribute this difference to the procedure itself. Switched branches were already improving relative to non-switched branches *before* the change occurred, which undermines our ability to isolate the procedure's true effect.

**The procedure may provide modest benefits, but the current evidence is insufficient to justify the cost and disruption of system-wide implementation.**

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new procedure maintained their service scores while non-switched branches declined. However, because switched branches were already on an upward trajectory before the switch, the estimated benefit of **+2.0 points is likely inflated**. A more realistic estimate is **+1.0 to +1.5 points**, with considerable uncertainty.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. What the Data Shows

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| **Branches with new procedure** | 52.4 | 52.6 | +0.2 |
| **Branches with old procedure** | 51.0 | 49.1 | −1.9 |

Switched branches held steady; non-switched branches declined.

### 2. Critical Limitation: Pre-Existing Trends

The gap between switched and non-switched branches was **already growing before the switch**:

| Week | Gap (Switched − Non-Switched) |
|------|-------------------------------|
| 6 | −0.2 |
| 9 | +1.5 |
| 12 | +2.5 |
| 14 | +2.3 |

The gap grew by **0.35 points per week** during the pre-switch period (p<0.001). If this trend had continued without any intervention, the gap would have reached approximately 5.6 points by week 22. The actual gap was 4.5 points.

### 3. What This Means

| Estimate | Value | Interpretation |
|----------|-------|----------------|
| Raw DiD estimate | +2.0 points | Likely overstated |
| Adjusted for pre-trends | +1.0 to +1.5 points | More realistic, but uncertain |
| Statistical confidence | Low | Parallel trends assumption violated |

### 4. No Evidence of Harm

Despite the uncertainty, switched branches performed well across all subgroups (staffing levels, visit volumes). The procedure does not appear harmful.

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | **Pause system-wide rollout** | Immediate |
| 2 | **Randomize future expansion** | Next quarter |
| 3 | **Collect 8-12 more weeks of data** | Ongoing |
| 4 | **Investigate decline in non-switched branches** | 4 weeks |
| 5 | **Re-evaluate after randomized data is available** | 3-6 months |

---

## **Why Randomization Matters**

If the cooperative wishes to make an evidence-based decision, future rollout should be **randomized**. Random assignment of branches to the new procedure would ensure treatment and control groups are comparable, enabling a clean estimate of the procedure's true effect.

---

*Analysis based on weekly service score data from 709 branches, weeks 6–22. The key limitation is that treatment and control groups were not on parallel trends before the intervention, which undermines causal inference.*
