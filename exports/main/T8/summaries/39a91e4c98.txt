# Memorandum to the Board

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

**Date:** November 15, 2026

---

## Bottom Line

The evidence **suggests the new procedure improves service scores by approximately 1.6 points** (~3%), but we lack sufficient data to rule out no effect. There is an estimated **93% probability the true effect is positive**. Given the modest upside and low risk of harm, we recommend **expanding the procedure to additional branches while continuing to collect data** for a more definitive evaluation.

---

## Final Verdict

**Proceed with a phased expansion.**

The data does not prove the new procedure works, but it points in a favorable direction. With nearly 3:1 odds of a positive effect and no evidence of harm, the prudent course is to expand cautiously while gathering more evidence. We recommend:

1. **Roll out to 10-15 additional branches** using randomized assignment
2. **Continue monitoring for 12 weeks** to improve statistical confidence
3. **Reconvene in Q2 2027** to make a final system-wide rollout decision

If the procedure's implementation costs are low, the expected benefit likely justifies this approach. If costs are substantial, defer expansion until more conclusive data is available.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Estimated Effect on Service Scores

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Point Estimate | +1.6 points |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.5 to +3.8 points |
| 90% Confidence Interval | -0.2 to +3.4 points |
| P-value | 0.14 |
| Probability effect > 0 | 93% |
| Probability effect > 1 point | 71% |

The most likely scenario is a modest positive effect, but the data cannot exclude the possibility of no effect.

### 2. Why the Uncertainty?

- **Limited sample size:** 34 branches over 17 weeks provides moderate statistical power
- **High weekly variation:** Service scores naturally fluctuate (~8-9 point standard deviation)
- **Effect would need to exceed 2.2 points** to achieve conventional statistical significance with this sample

### 3. Study Validity

- **Parallel pre-trends confirmed:** Treatment and control branches followed similar trajectories before the switch (p=0.88 for trend difference)
- **Selection bias noted:** Switching branches had lower baseline scores, but this is accounted for in the analysis
