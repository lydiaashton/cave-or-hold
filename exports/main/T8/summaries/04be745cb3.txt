## **Final Verdict**

**DO NOT APPROVE SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT AT THIS TIME.**

**Rationale:** The analysis does not provide sufficient evidence that the new procedure *caused* the observed improvement. The 1.8-point difference is confounded by pre-existing differences between branches that chose to switch and those that did not.

**Required Before Reconsideration:**
1. Randomized controlled testing with remaining branches
2. Demonstration that the effect persists when selection bias is eliminated
3. Cost-benefit analysis showing expected returns exceed implementation costs

**Interim Action:** Continue monitoring current switched branches and prepare a randomized pilot protocol for board review within 60 days.
