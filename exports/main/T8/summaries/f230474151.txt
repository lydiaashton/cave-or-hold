# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure protected service scores from declining.** While scores dropped across the system after the switch date, branches using the new procedure maintained their performance while those on the old procedure fell by about 2 points. We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What We Studied
- **150 branches** tracked over 17 weeks
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure in mid-April (week 15)
- **60 branches** continued with the old procedure (comparison group)
- Service scores average ~50 points (higher is better)

### What We Found

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| Old Procedure | 49.85 | 47.79 | ▼ 2.06 |
| New Procedure | 50.54 | 50.37 | ▼ 0.17 |

**The new procedure prevented approximately 1.9 points of decline.**

Branches using the new procedure held steady while those on the old procedure experienced a noticeable drop. This suggests the new procedure provides resilience against factors that otherwise reduce service quality.

### How Confident Are We?

**Very confident.** The effect is statistically significant (p = 0.002), with a 95% confidence interval of 0.7 to 3.1 points. The analysis:
- Compared changes in treatment vs. control branches (difference-in-differences)
- Accounted for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week fluctuations
- Confirmed both groups had similar trends before the switch

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure change service scores? | **Yes** |
| Was the initial expectation (no difference) correct? | **No** |
| Is the effect statistically reliable? | **Yes** (p = 0.002) |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Yes** |

**The evidence is clear: adopt the new procedure system-wide.**

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout.**

The initial expectation was that the procedure change would make no difference. The data shows otherwise: the new procedure meaningfully protects service quality. While it does not boost scores in absolute terms, it prevents the declines seen under the old procedure.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve full rollout** to the remaining 60 branches

2. **Investigate what caused the system-wide decline** after week 15 (seasonal factors? staffing? external pressures?)

3. **Monitor newly converted branches** to confirm they maintain scores as expected

4. **Assess implementation costs** against the benefit of preventing score declines

---

*Technical note: Difference-in-differences estimate +1.88 points (SE 0.59, p=0.002, 95% CI: 0.72 to 3.05). Model includes branch and week fixed effects with cluster-robust standard errors.*
