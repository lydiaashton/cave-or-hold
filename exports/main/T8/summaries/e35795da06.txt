# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Impact of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure on Service Scores

**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Despite the initial expectation that the change would make no difference, branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a statistically significant increase in service scores of approximately **2 points** (about 4% improvement). The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Made a Positive Difference

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated effect** | **+1.97 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | [0.76, 3.18] |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.0015 |
| Baseline average score | ~51 points |
| **Percentage improvement** | **~3.9%** |

### How We Know This

The analysis compared 102 branches that switched to the new procedure against 48 branches that kept the old procedure, tracking both groups before and after the switch (weeks 6–22). This "difference-in-differences" approach isolates the effect of the procedure change from other factors.

**Service Score Changes:**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.65 | 53.48 | +1.83 |
| Non-switched branches | 50.23 | 50.09 | -0.14 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+1.97** |

### The Evidence Is Reliable

- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups followed similar trajectories (p = 0.96), validating the comparison.
- **Placebo test passed:** A fake treatment date showed no effect (p = 0.97), confirming the result is not spurious.
- **Effect persisted:** Improvement was observed consistently across all post-switch weeks.
- **Robust finding:** Results hold across multiple statistical specifications.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**The new procedure should be adopted system-wide.**

The data provide clear, statistically rigorous evidence that the procedure change improved service quality. While the effect is modest (~4%), it is:

- **Real** — The result is statistically significant and survives multiple validity checks.
- **Consistent** — The improvement held across all post-switch weeks.
- **Cost-effective** — The gain comes from a procedural change without apparent additional resource requirements.
- **Actionable** — The procedure has already been tested in 102 branches with success.

Given that the organization expected no difference and instead found a positive effect, there is no analytical basis for delaying or limiting the rollout.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve full rollout** to remaining 48 branches.

2. **Monitor during implementation** to ensure consistent results across all locations.

3. **Document implementation practices** from high-performing branches to support training.

4. **Continue tracking service scores** post-rollout to confirm sustained impact.

---

*Analysis based on 2,550 branch-week observations from 150 branches over 17 weeks. Statistical models control for branch-specific and week-specific factors. Standard errors clustered at branch level.*
