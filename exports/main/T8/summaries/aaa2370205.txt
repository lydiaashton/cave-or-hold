# Library Cooperative Stock Rotation Procedure: Impact Analysis

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## Bottom Line

**The evidence is suggestive but not conclusive.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed an average improvement of **2.3 points** compared to control branches, and 85% of switched branches improved. However, the result is **not statistically significant** (p = 0.13), meaning we cannot confidently rule out chance as an explanation. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout **if implementation costs are low**, but continue monitoring to confirm the effect.

---

## Key Findings

### What the Data Shows

| Metric | Switched Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| Average improvement | +4.24 points | +1.96 points |
| Branches that improved | 17 of 20 (85%) | 9 of 14 (64%) |
| Branches that declined | 3 of 20 (15%) | 5 of 14 (36%) |

**Net effect: +2.28 points** (95% confidence interval: −0.6 to +5.2)

### What This Means

- The **point estimate is positive** and aligns with expectations
- The **confidence interval includes zero**, so a null effect cannot be ruled out
- **3 switched branches got worse**, suggesting the procedure may not work uniformly
- Switched branches outperformed controls in **6 of 8 post-switch weeks**

### Why the Uncertainty?

- Only 34 branches in the study (20 switched, 14 control)
- 8 weeks of post-switch data limits statistical power
- Natural week-to-week variation in service scores is substantial

---

## Final Verdict

**APPROVE ROLLOUT — WITH CONDITIONS**

The data does not prove the procedure works, but it provides **sufficient evidence to proceed** given the following:

1. **Directionally correct:** The effect is positive (+2.3 points), not negative
2. **Broadly consistent:** 85% of switched branches improved; only 15% declined
3. **Low risk:** If the procedure fails to deliver, branches can revert to the old method
4. **Opportunity cost:** Delaying rollout means forfeiting potential service gains for patrons

**Conditions:**
- Implement a **90-day review** to reassess with more data
- **Track the 3 underperforming branches** to identify mitigating factors
- Set a **minimum success threshold** (e.g., +1.5 points) for continued adoption
