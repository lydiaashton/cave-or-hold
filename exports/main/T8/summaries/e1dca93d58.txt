# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **2.0 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This represents approximately a **4% improvement** and is statistically significant (p < 0.001).

This finding contradicts our initial expectation that the change would make no difference to service quality.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure change service scores? | **Yes** |
| Was the change positive or negative? | **Positive** |
| Is the result statistically reliable? | **Yes** (p < 0.001) |
| Should we roll out to all branches? | **Yes** |

**Confidence level:** High. The estimated effect is positive, statistically significant, and the confidence interval [1.28, 2.79] excludes zero. While the effect is modest rather than transformative, it represents a meaningful improvement with no identified negative consequences.

**Risk assessment:** Low. The procedure change is operational rather than structural, and the data show improved service alongside slightly reduced staffing needs—suggesting efficiency gains rather than tradeoffs.

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### 1. Clear Improvement in Service Quality

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched branches (90) | 49.5 | 53.2 | +3.8 |
| Control branches (60) | 48.6 | 50.3 | +1.7 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.0** |

### 2. Strong Statistical Evidence

- **Effect size:** +2.03 service score points
- **95% confidence interval:** 1.28 to 2.79 points
- **P-value:** < 0.001 (highly significant)
- **Data:** 2,550 observations across 150 branches over 17 weeks

### 3. Valid Comparison Between Groups

- Pre-period averages were statistically similar (p = 0.49)
- Pre-trends were not significantly different (p = 0.12)
- Sharp discontinuity at week 15 supports causal interpretation

### 4. Efficiency Gain Observed

Service scores improved despite a small decrease in staffing index at switched branches, suggesting the new procedure delivers better service with slightly less staffing input.
