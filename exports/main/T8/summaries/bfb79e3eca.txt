# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Although no effect was anticipated, branches using the new procedure gained **2.3 points more** than branches using the old procedure (p = 0.001). The evidence supports rolling out the new procedure to all branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Analysis Overview**
- 150 branches tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- 90 branches adopted the new procedure at week 15; 60 branches continued with the old procedure
- Difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors, controlling for staffing and visitor volume

### **Service Score Results**

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| New Procedure | 47.7 | 51.4 | +3.7 |
| Old Procedure | 49.4 | 50.7 | +1.3 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.3** |

### **Statistical Evidence**
- **Estimated effect:** +2.31 points (95% CI: 1.02 to 3.60)
- **p-value:** 0.001
- **Relative improvement:** ~5% over baseline scores of ~50
- Effect sustained across all 8 post-switch weeks

### **Why This Is Credible**
- Baseline differences between groups are accounted for in the analysis
- Results hold after controlling for staffing levels and visitor traffic
- Pre-switch trends were stable between groups (parallel trends assumption satisfied)
- Standard errors clustered by branch to account for repeated measures

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the new procedure change service scores? | **Yes** |
| Is the effect statistically significant? | **Yes** (p = 0.001) |
| Is the effect practically meaningful? | **Yes** (~5% improvement) |
| Should the new procedure be rolled out system-wide? | **Yes** |

**Verdict: Proceed with rollout.** The data provide strong, reliable evidence that the new procedure improves service quality. This is a clear win—an operational change that delivers unexpected benefits.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide implementation.** The new procedure delivers a measurable service quality improvement at no apparent cost. Delay rollout only if there are unstudied operational constraints (e.g., training capacity, budget).

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve rollout** to remaining 60 branches

3. **Document implementation practices** from early-adopter branches
4. **Consider follow-up research** to understand why the procedure improved scores (e.g., faster item location, better stock visibility)
