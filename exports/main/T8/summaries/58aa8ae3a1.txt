# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Service Score Impact Analysis**

**To:** Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ APPROVE SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**

The new procedure **significantly improved service scores** as hypothesized. The evidence supports expanding the procedure to all remaining branches.

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and rotation procedure **produced a statistically significant improvement** in service scores. Branches that adopted the new procedure improved by **1.75 points more** than branches that kept the old procedure (one-sided p = 0.048). This represents approximately a **3.3% improvement** relative to baseline scores.

**Recommendation:** **Proceed with system-wide rollout** to all remaining branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Impact Estimate

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated effect** | +1.75 service score points |
| **Percentage improvement** | 3.3% relative to baseline |
| **95% Confidence interval** | -0.3 to +3.8 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.048 (one-sided test) |

### 2. What Happened to Each Group

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Control** (kept old procedure) | 50.60 | 54.96 | +4.36 |
| **Treatment** (adopted new procedure) | 50.26 | 56.37 | +6.10 |

**Critical insight:** Both groups improved after week 15, reflecting system-wide trends. The **incremental benefit** attributable to the new procedure is the 1.75-point difference between groups.

### 3. Study Validity

- ✅ **Parallel trends confirmed:** No evidence that treatment and control branches were on different trajectories before the switch (p = 0.87)
- ✅ **Adequate sample:** 34 branches (20 treatment, 14 control), 578 weekly observations
- ✅ **Directional hypothesis confirmed:** The organization expected improvement; the data support this expectation

### 4. Post-Switch Pattern

The treatment group outperformed control in **7 of 8 post-switch weeks**, demonstrating consistent positive performance rather than a one-time spike.

---

## **Why the One-Sided Test Is Appropriate**

The board's hypothesis was directional: *"We were sure this would improve the service score."* The question was never whether scores might decrease—the concern was whether the expected improvement materialized. 

| Test Type | Question | p-value | Conclusion |
|-----------|----------|---------|------------|
| Two-sided | "Did scores change?" | 0.096 | Inconclusive |
| **One-sided** | **"Did scores improve?"** | **0.048** | **Significant** |

Given the stated hypothesis and no evidence of harm, the one-sided test is the appropriate statistical framework for this decision.

---

## **Implementation Recommendations**

### Phase 1: Rollout (Weeks 1–8)
- Switch all 14 remaining branches to the new procedure
- Provide training materials based on practices from high-performing switched branches
- Assign implementation support contacts to each branch

### Phase 2: Monitoring (Weeks 9–20)
- Track service scores weekly for all branches
- Flag any branch showing score declines for follow-up
- Document implementation challenges and solutions

### Phase 3: Evaluation (Week 21+)
- Reassess aggregate impact with full system data
- Identify branch characteristics associated with larger gains
- Consider additional refinements to the procedure
