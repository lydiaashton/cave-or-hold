# **Memorandum to the Library Cooperative Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores, but with meaningful statistical uncertainty**. Branches that adopted the new procedure scored approximately **1.9 points higher** (3.8% improvement) than would be expected based on control branch trends. However, the 95% confidence interval (-0.6 to +4.4 points) includes zero, meaning we cannot rule out that the true effect is negligible.

**Recommendation:** The evidence is **suggestive but not definitive**. Proceed with a phased rollout or collect additional data before committing to system-wide implementation.

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **Proceed with Phased Rollout**

After weighing the evidence, costs, and risks, **we recommend implementing the new procedure in additional branches while continuing to measure outcomes.**

**Rationale:**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Direction of effect** | ✓ Positive (+1.9 points) |
| **Magnitude** | ✓ Meaningful (~4% improvement) |
| **Statistical certainty** | ⚠ Inconclusive (p=0.13) |
| **Risk of harm** | ✓ Low (no branches showed severe decline) |
| **Consistency** | ✓ 70% of treatment branches improved |
| **Pre-trends** | ✓ Valid (groups were comparable before switch) |

**Why this verdict?**

1. **The signal is positive** — Every major metric points in a favorable direction
2. **The risk is bounded** — Even if the effect is zero, the downside is limited to implementation costs
3. **The opportunity is real** — If the effect is genuine, delaying rollout denies branches a meaningful improvement
4. **Learning continues** — A phased approach generates more data to confirm or revise this assessment

**What we are NOT recommending:**
- ❌ Immediate system-wide rollout (evidence too uncertain)
- ❌ Abandoning the new procedure (evidence too promising)
- ❌ Indefinite waiting for more data (opportunity cost of delayed benefits)

- Add 10–15 branches to the new procedure
- Continue measuring all branches weekly
- Monitor for any unexpected issues or negative effects

### **Phase 2: Evaluation (Week 12)**
- Re-run analysis with expanded dataset
- Assess whether effect size holds or diminishes
- Make final go/no-go decision on full rollout

### **Phase 3: Decision Point**
| Outcome | Action |
|---------|--------|
