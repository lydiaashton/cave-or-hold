# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Shelving and Rotation Procedure  

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Roll out the new procedure to all branches.** The evidence shows a real, positive effect on service scores with no detected harms. While the improvement is modest rather than transformative, it represents meaningful progress for patrons and justifies systemwide adoption.

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new shelving and rotation procedure improved service scores by **2.5 points** (approximately 5% above baseline). This effect is **statistically significant** (p < 0.001) and consistent across the 8-week post-switch observation period.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores

Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced service score gains relative to branches that retained the old procedure:

| Metric | Control Group (Old) | Treatment Group (New) |
|--------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Pre-switch average | 51.6 | 50.4 |
| Post-switch average | 50.9 | 52.2 |
| Change | -0.7 | +1.8 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | — | **+2.5** |

The 95% confidence interval ranges from **1.2 to 3.8 points**, confirming the effect is unlikely to be due to chance.

### 2. Valid Comparison Between Groups

- **60 branches** retained the old procedure; **90 branches** adopted the new procedure
- Pre-switch trends were nearly identical between groups, validating the comparison
- The treatment group started slightly lower but surpassed the control group after implementation

### 3. Effect Is Robust

The improvement persists after accounting for weekly staffing levels and visitor volume. The benefit shows no signs of diminishing over the observation period.

### 4. Addressing the Board's Expectation

The board anticipated no difference in service scores. The evidence indicates a **modest but clear improvement**—not a revolution, but a meaningful step forward for patron experience.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Full rollout** to the 60 branches currently using the old procedure
2. **Ongoing monitoring** of service scores during implementation
3. **Documentation** of the new procedure to ensure consistent execution
4. **Process review** to identify which aspects drive improvement (e.g., faster retrieval, better organization)
