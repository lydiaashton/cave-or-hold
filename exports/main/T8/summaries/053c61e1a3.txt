# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The data show a **positive association** between the new procedure and service scores (+1.9 points, ~4%), but this result is **not statistically significant** (p=0.15). There is approximately a **93% probability the effect is real and positive**, but we cannot rule out the possibility of no effect. Given the **low risk and modest cost** of the procedure, we recommend **continuing the pilot while gathering more data** before deciding on system-wide rollout.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **1. Measured Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Switched Branches (n=20) | 49.9 | 52.0 | +2.1 |
| Control Branches (n=14) | 49.2 | 49.3 | +0.2 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.9** |

- **Point estimate:** +1.9 points (~4% improvement)
- **95% confidence interval:** -0.7 to +4.5 points
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.15 (above the conventional 0.05 threshold)
- **Probability effect is positive:** ~93%

### **2. What This Means**

The switched branches improved more than the control branches after the procedure change. However, with only 34 branches and 8 weeks of post-switch data, we lack the statistical power to confidently distinguish this from random variation.

**Interpretation:**
- **Best guess:** The procedure provides a modest benefit (+1.9 points)
- **Uncertainty:** The true effect could range from slightly harmful (-0.7) to meaningfully beneficial (+4.5)
- **Study limitation:** More data would narrow this range

### **3. Study Design & Validity**

- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Pre-switch trajectories were similar between groups
- **Controls applied:** Results hold after accounting for staffing and visitor volume
- **No data quality issues:** Complete records with no missing values

---

## **Final Verdict**

**DO NOT APPROVE SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT AT THIS TIME.**

**Rationale:**
1. **Insufficient statistical evidence:** The p-value of 0.15 means we cannot confidently conclude the procedure causes improvement. Approving a full rollout based on this evidence carries meaningful risk.

2. **Promising but unproven:** The 93% probability of a positive effect and consistent point estimates across model specifications suggest the procedure likely helps—but "likely" is not sufficient for a cooperative-wide investment.

3. **Low-cost path to certainty:** Extending the pilot by 8–12 weeks would substantially improve statistical power and allow a more confident decision.

4. **Fiduciary responsibility:** The Board should not commit resources to a system-wide change without stronger evidence of benefit.

---

## **Next Steps**

### **Recommended Actions**

1. **Extend the pilot by 8–12 weeks** to improve statistical precision before making a final rollout decision.

2. **Consider cost-benefit:** If the procedure has minimal ongoing costs, the 93% probability of a positive effect may justify gradual expansion. If costs are significant, wait for stronger evidence.

3. **Monitor implementation** to ensure branches are following the new procedures consistently.

4. **Track additional metrics** including staff efficiency, patron satisfaction, and item retrieval times.

5. **Reconvene in Q1 2027** with updated analysis.

### **Decision Framework**

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|----------|----------------|
| Low implementation cost | Proceed with gradual expansion; upside likely exceeds risk |
| High implementation cost | Wait for more data; require stronger statistical evidence |
| Effect confirmed at +4 points (upper bound) | Strong case for full rollout |
| Effect confirmed at zero or negative | Discontinue the new procedure |
