# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team
**DATE:** November 15, 2026
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Executive Summary**

The new stock rotation and shelving procedure **successfully improved service scores** across participating branches. Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced a **2.0-point increase** in service scores relative to branches that maintained the old procedure. This confirms our hypothesis and supports proceeding with a system-wide rollout.

---

## **Bottom Line**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Effect of New Procedure** | +1.95 service score points |
| **Statistical Confidence** | 95% CI: 1.4 to 2.5 points (p < 0.001) |
| **Relative Improvement** | ~4% increase from baseline |
| **Recommendation** | **Approve system-wide rollout** |

---

## **Final Verdict**

**The new procedure works. Roll it out.**

The data are unambiguous: branches using the new rotation and shelving procedure deliver measurably better service. The effect is statistically significant, practically meaningful, and consistent across analytical approaches. There is no evidence of harm or unintended consequences. Delaying broader implementation would deny patrons at remaining branches the benefit of improved service.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Analysis Overview
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** switched to the new procedure; **284 branches** served as controls
- Switch implemented in **week 15**
- Rigorous difference-in-differences analysis with branch-level controls

### Service Score Changes

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Control (old procedure) | 49.5 | 49.0 | −0.5 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 50.7 | 52.2 | +1.5 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.95** |

### Why We're Confident in These Results
1. **Parallel pre-trends:** Both groups followed similar trajectories before the switch
2. **Controls included:** Results hold after accounting for staffing levels and visitor volume
3. **Branch-level factors:** Analysis accounts for inherent differences between individual branches
4. **Statistically robust:** Effect is highly significant (p < 0.001)

**Approve immediate rollout of the new procedure to all branches.**

The evidence is clear and actionable. The new procedure delivers measurable improvement in service quality with no identified downsides.
