# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure is associated with a **2.3-point improvement** in service scores, but this result **does not reach conventional statistical significance** (p=0.09). The 95% confidence interval ranges from -0.4 to +5.0 points, meaning we cannot rule out no effect. However, the direction of the effect is positive, no negative impacts were observed, and the change is operationally low-cost. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than an immediate full deployment.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **34 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6-22)
- **20 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15; **14 branches** served as controls
- Analysis compares changes in treatment branches relative to control branches

### **Service Score Results**

| Period | Control (Old Procedure) | Treatment (New Procedure) |
|--------|-------------------------|---------------------------|
| Before Switch (weeks 6-14) | 48.0 | 50.2 |
| After Switch (weeks 15-22) | 48.1 | 52.6 |
| **Change** | **+0.1** | **+2.4** |

**Estimated Effect: +2.3 points (95% CI: -0.4 to +5.0)**

### **Important Caveats**
1. **Statistical uncertainty**: The result is not statistically significant at the 0.05 level. The true effect could be zero or modestly negative.
2. **Pre-existing differences**: Treatment branches scored higher than control branches even before the switch (+2.2 points), though the analysis accounts for this.
3. **Limited observation window**: Only 8 weeks of post-switch data; long-term effects are unknown.
4. **No harm detected**: Control branches showed no decline, and treatment branches showed no negative trends.

### **Supporting Evidence**
- **Consistent direction**: All 8 post-switch weeks showed positive gaps between treatment and control
- **Parallel trends**: Pre-switch trends were similar between groups (statistical test p=0.99)
- **Robustness**: Excluding the anomalous Week 6 data strengthens the result (p=0.06)

---

## **Recommendations**

1. **Proceed with cautious expansion**: Roll out to an additional 5-10 branches over the next 4-6 weeks

2. **Extend monitoring period**: Track service scores for at least 12-16 weeks post-rollout to assess sustainability

3. **Set decision thresholds**: Define in advance what level of improvement justifies full rollout (e.g., +1.5 points with p<0.10)

4. **Document implementation**: Capture procedural details to ensure consistency and enable troubleshooting

5. **Reconvene in 3 months**: Review accumulated evidence before deciding on system-wide adoption

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with conditional approval.** The evidence is promising but not definitive. Given the positive direction of effects, absence of detected harms, and likely low implementation costs, a measured expansion is warranted. However, full system-wide rollout should await additional data confirming the benefit. This approach balances opportunity against uncertainty.
