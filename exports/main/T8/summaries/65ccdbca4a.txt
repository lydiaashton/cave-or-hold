# **Memorandum to the Board**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. The best estimate is a **2-point improvement**, but the data is also compatible with no effect at all. We recommend a **phased rollout with rigorous monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **What the Data Shows**

### Key Results

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Estimated effect** | +2.1 service score points |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | −0.3 to +4.6 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.09 (not significant at p < 0.05) |
| **Interpretation** | Data is consistent with effects ranging from no impact to meaningful improvement |

### What This Means

- The **point estimate is positive** (+2.1 points, ~4% improvement)
- However, **statistical uncertainty is substantial** — we cannot confidently distinguish this from random variation
- The study may be **underpowered**: only 8 weeks of post-switch data across 34 branches
- **No negative effects detected**: the lower bound of the confidence interval is essentially zero

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with caution. The evidence is suggestive but not conclusive.**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did the procedure improve service scores? | **Probably, but we cannot be certain** |
| Is there evidence of harm? | **No** |
| Should we abandon the new procedure? | **No** |
| Should we roll out to all branches immediately? | **No — more evidence needed** |
| Recommended action? | **Phased expansion with continued evaluation** |

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with a phased rollout**, treating this as a **continuing evaluation** rather than a final decision:

| Phase | Action | Decision Point |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Expand to 5–7 additional branches | Immediate |
| 2 | Collect 8–12 weeks of post-implementation data | Ongoing |
| 3 | Re-analyze with expanded sample | After Phase 2 |
| 4 | Decide on full rollout based on strengthened evidence | Quarter 1, 2027 |

---

## **Why This Approach**

1. **The signal is positive** — no evidence of harm, and the best estimate shows improvement
2. **The evidence is not definitive** — more data will reduce uncertainty
3. **Low risk** — the procedure can be adjusted or reversed if larger-scale data shows no benefit
4. **Prudent stewardship** — respects both the investment already made and the need for evidence-based decisions

---

## **Conclusion**

The initial results are **encouraging but not conclusive**. A phased expansion allows the cooperative to capture potential benefits while gathering the evidence needed for a confident system-wide decision. We recommend reconvening in 3 months with additional data to make a final determination.
