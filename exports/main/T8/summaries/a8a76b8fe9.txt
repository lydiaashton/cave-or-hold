# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **improved service scores** by an average of **2.1 points** (95% CI: 0.9 to 3.4 points), representing approximately a **4% improvement** over baseline. This result is statistically significant (p < 0.001). **We recommend rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ APPROVE system-wide rollout.**

The data provides strong evidence that the new procedure improves service scores. The board should authorize implementation across all 150 branches.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Improvement in Service Scores
- Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by **3.7 points** after the switch
- Branches that kept the old procedure improved by only **1.5 points** over the same period
- The **net effect attributable to the new procedure is +2.1 points**

### 2. Strong Statistical Evidence
- The improvement is **highly unlikely to be due to chance** (p = 0.0006)
- We are 95% confident the true effect lies between **0.9 and 3.4 points**
- The effect held after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### 3. Consistent Benefits Across Branches
- **82% of switched branches** saw their service scores improve
- Only **70% of non-switched branches** improved over the same period
- No branches experienced severe negative effects

### 4. Valid Comparison
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups (p = 0.77), confirming the comparison is valid
- Analysis accounts for branch-specific characteristics and weekly fluctuations

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new procedure to all 150 branches
2. **Develop training materials** to ensure consistent implementation
3. **Monitor service scores** during the first 8–12 weeks of rollout
4. **Schedule a follow-up review** in 6 months to assess long-term effects
