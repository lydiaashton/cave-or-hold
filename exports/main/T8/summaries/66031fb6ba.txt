# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure **likely improves service scores**, with an estimated gain of **~2 points (4%)**. There is a **95% probability the true effect is positive** and only a 5% chance it is negative. However, the evidence does not meet conventional statistical significance thresholds (p=0.10), and the magnitude of benefit is modest.

**Recommendation depends on implementation cost:**
- **If low-cost to implement:** Proceed with rollout—the probable benefit outweighs the risk.
- **If high-cost or disruptive:** Extend evaluation another 8-12 weeks before deciding.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Results Summary

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated Effect | +2.1 points (~4% improvement) |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.4 to +4.6 points |
| Probability Effect is Positive | 95% |
| Probability Effect is Negative | 5% |
| Statistical Significance | p = 0.10 (not conventionally significant) |

### What This Means

The data suggests the new procedure probably helps, but we cannot be as certain as we would like. The confidence interval includes zero, meaning we cannot rule out no effect—but the bulk of the probability distribution favors a positive outcome.

### Important Caveats

1. **Self-selection bias**: Branches that chose to switch had higher service scores throughout the study, which may inflate the estimated benefit.

2. **Limited post-treatment data**: Only 8 weeks of data after the switch; effects may stabilize or change over time.

3. **Modest effect size**: Even if the full 2-point gain is real, it represents a ~4% improvement—meaningful but not transformative.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

**PROCEED WITH PHASED ROLLOUT, WITH CONDITIONS**

Given the high probability of benefit (95%) and low risk of harm (5%), we recommend moving forward with the new procedure under the following conditions:

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Phased implementation** | Roll out to 5-10 branches per month, not all at once |
| **Continued monitoring** | Track service scores for all branches for 6+ months |
| **Stop-loss trigger** | If service scores decline by more than 2 points at any branch, pause and investigate |
| **Cost tracking** | Document all implementation costs to verify benefit justifies expense |
| **Re-evaluation point** | Board review at 6 months with full data before completing rollout |

**Rationale:** The risk of proceeding is low (5% chance of negative effect), while the risk of delaying is continued suboptimal service at branches that could benefit. A phased approach balances these concerns by allowing course correction if problems emerge.
