# **Memorandum to the Board**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive but statistically uncertain effect** on service scores. The estimated improvement is **2.1 points** (4–5%), but the 95% confidence interval ranges from **−0.3 to +4.6 points**. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued evaluation** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Results

| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched to new procedure | 53.8 | 55.1 | +1.3 |
| Kept old procedure | 46.5 | 45.7 | −0.8 |
| **Estimated effect** | | | **+2.1** |

### Statistical Evidence

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Point estimate | +2.1 points | Positive direction |
| P-value | 0.086 | Not significant at 0.05 level |
| 95% Confidence Interval | −0.3 to +4.6 | Includes zero (no effect) |
| 90% Confidence Interval | +0.1 to +4.2 | Barely excludes zero |
| Effect size | 0.19 SD | Small but meaningful |

### What This Means
- The data **leans positive** but does not provide definitive proof
- There is roughly an **8–10% chance** the observed effect is due to random variation
- The true effect could be zero, slightly negative, or as high as +4–5 points

---

## **Final Verdict**

**PROCEED WITH CAUTIONARY ROLLOUT**

The evidence is **inconclusive but promising**. We cannot confirm the new procedure definitively improves service scores, but we also cannot rule out a meaningful benefit. Given:

- ✓ No evidence of negative impact
- ✓ Positive point estimate in the expected direction
- ✓ Operational feasibility appears sound
- ✗ Statistical evidence falls short of conventional thresholds

**The prudent course is phased implementation with built-in evaluation checkpoints.**

---

## **Recommendation**

### Implementation Plan
1. **Phase 1 (Next 8 weeks)**: Roll out to 5–7 additional branches
2. **Phase 2 (Evaluation)**: Assess whether the effect replicates in new branches
3. **Phase 3 (Decision)**: If effect is confirmed (p < 0.05), complete system-wide rollout; if not, reconsider or refine the procedure

### Success Criteria
- Replicate the **+1.5 point or greater** improvement in Phase 1 branches
- Achieve statistical significance (p < 0.05) with combined data
- No branch experiences a score decline greater than 2 points post-implementation

### Go/No-Go Decision Point
**Reconvene in 10 weeks** with expanded data to make a final system-wide rollout decision.
