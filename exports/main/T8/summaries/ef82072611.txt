# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** New Branch Shelving and Stock Rotation Procedure – Impact Assessment

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence suggests a modest improvement, but with important caveats.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed service score gains of approximately **2 points** in the weeks immediately following implementation. However, this effect appears to diminish after 4-5 weeks, and the statistical evidence, while positive, is not definitive. We recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Evidence**

| Metric | Result | Interpretation |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Estimated effect | +1.95 points | Modest improvement |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.032 | Significant at 5% level, but borderline |
| 95% Confidence interval | +0.17 to +3.74 | True effect could be small |
| Effect persistence | 5 of 8 weeks positive | Effect fades in weeks 20-22 |

### **What the Data Shows**

1. **Initial improvement:** Weeks 16-19 showed clear gains (1.7 to 7.8 points higher than control branches).

2. **Effect diminishes:** Weeks 20-22 showed little to no advantage over control branches.

3. **Possible explanations:** The fade could reflect adjustment periods, seasonal factors, or that the initial boost was partly novelty-driven.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Yes** – statistically significant improvement detected |
| **Is the effect large enough to matter?** | **Modestly** – ~4% improvement, meaningful but not transformative |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Not yet** – evidence is positive but not definitive |
| **Recommended path forward** | **Phased expansion with monitoring** |

**Bottom line on the bottom line:** The procedure change *does* improve service scores, but the effect is smaller and less durable than hoped. Proceed with expansion, but treat this as an ongoing improvement initiative rather than a completed success.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with cautious, phased expansion** rather than immediate full rollout:

1. **Expand to 5-7 additional branches** over the next quarter
2. **Monitor weekly** to determine if effects persist beyond the initial weeks
3. **Investigate why the effect faded** – was it implementation drift, adaptation, or something else?
4. **Reassess after 8-12 weeks** of additional data before committing to system-wide adoption
