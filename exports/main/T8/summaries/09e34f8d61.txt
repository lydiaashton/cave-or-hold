# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** January 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on Systemwide Rollout of New Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure significantly improved service scores.** Contrary to initial expectations that the change would have no effect, branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by an average of **1.9 points** (approximately 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This finding is statistically significant and robust.

**Recommendation: Proceed with systemwide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure starting week 15
- **60 branches** continued with the old procedure (control group)
- Rigorous statistical analysis controls for branch-specific factors and weekly variations

### **Impact on Service Scores**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.88 points** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | **[0.66 to 3.10] points** |
| Statistical significance | **p = 0.0025** (highly significant) |
| Relative improvement | **~3.9%** vs. ~50-point average |

### **Why We Trust These Results**

✓ **Valid comparison:** Treatment and control branches had similar trends before the switch  
✓ **Controls applied:** Analysis accounts for staffing levels and visitor volume  
✓ **Consistent effect:** Improvement observed in all 8 post-switch weeks  
✓ **Statistically robust:** Result unlikely to occur by chance (less than 1% probability)

### **What This Means in Practice**

- A typical branch scoring 50 would be expected to score approximately **52** after adopting the new procedure
- The improvement is **meaningful for patrons** and represents genuine service enhancement
- **No negative effects** were detected in the data

## **Final Verdict**

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Statistical Evidence** | ✅ **Strong** — Effect is statistically significant (p = 0.0025) |
| **Practical Significance** | ✅ **Moderate** — ~4% improvement is meaningful but not transformational |
| **Consistency** | ✅ **Strong** — Positive effect in all 8 post-switch weeks |
| **Validity** | ✅ **Strong** — No pre-trend differences; robust to controls |
| **Risk** | ✅ **Low** — No adverse effects detected |

### **Decision: APPROVE ROLLOUT**

**Rationale:** The evidence clearly demonstrates that the new procedure improves service scores. While the magnitude of improvement is modest, the effect is reliable, consistent, and achieved without negative consequences. Given that the original hypothesis expected no difference, this positive finding represents a genuine gain for the cooperative.

**Caveat:** The board should confirm that implementation costs do not outweigh the ~4% service improvement benefit.

---

## **Technical Notes**

- **Method:** Difference-in-differences regression with branch fixed effects
- **Controls:** Staffing index, visits index, branch-specific characteristics
- **Standard errors:** Clustered at branch level to account for repeated observations
- **Data quality:** 2,550 observations with no missing values
- **Assumption check:** Parallel trends assumption verified (p = 0.44)
