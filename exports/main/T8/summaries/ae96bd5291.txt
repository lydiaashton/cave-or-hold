# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is inconclusive.** While branches that adopted the new procedure showed higher service score improvements than those that did not (+1.7 points), this result is **not statistically significant at the conventional 95% confidence level**. We cannot confidently attribute the improvement to the new procedure rather than random variation.

**Recommendation:** Do **not** proceed with system-wide rollout at this time. Instead, extend the evaluation period to gather more data before making a final decision.

---

## **Final Verdict**

> **DO NOT APPROVE system-wide rollout at this time.**
>
> **Reason:** The evidence does not meet the standard of proof required for a cooperative-wide policy change. The observed improvement could plausibly be due to random variation.
>
> **Action Required:** Extend the evaluation period by 6 months, then return to the board with updated analysis for a final decision.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What We Studied**
- **34 branches** over 17 weeks (February through June)
- **20 branches** switched to the new procedure in mid-April (week 15)
- **14 branches** continued with the old procedure as a comparison group
- Service scores averaged around 50 points; higher scores indicate better service

### **What We Found**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Branches keeping old procedure | 50.89 | 50.92 | +0.03 |
| Branches adopting new procedure | 52.01 | 53.79 | +1.78 |
| **Net improvement from new procedure** | | | **+1.69** |

### **Why We Cannot Conclude the Procedure Works**

| Measure | Result | Interpretation |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| Estimated improvement | +1.69 points | Point estimate suggests improvement |
| 95% Confidence interval | -0.31 to +3.69 | **Includes zero** – effect could be null |
| P-value | 0.098 | **Not significant at 5% level** |
| Statistical significance | No | Cannot reject "no effect" hypothesis |

**What this means:** There is approximately a 10% probability of observing this result even if the new procedure has no real effect. By conventional scientific and policy standards, this does not constitute sufficient evidence to justify a system-wide change.

### **Additional Context**
- The 90% confidence interval (0.01 to 3.37) is entirely positive, suggesting the effect *may* be real but we need more data to be confident
- The effect size (0.19 standard deviations) would be meaningful if real, but the uncertainty is too large to act on
- No branches showed declining performance after adoption, which is reassuring
- Staffing levels and visit volumes significantly affect service scores and should be managed regardless of procedure

---

## **Recommendations**

### **1. Do Not Approve System-Wide Rollout Yet**
The current evidence does not meet the standard of proof needed for a cooperative-wide policy change affecting all 34 branches.

### **2. Continue the Evaluation for 6 More Months**
- Maintain the current arrangement (20 branches on new procedure, 14 on old)
- Collect weekly service scores through the end of the year
- This will roughly double the post-implementation data and narrow the confidence interval

### **3. Re-Analyze at 6 Months**
With additional data, we can determine whether:
- The effect becomes statistically significant (p < 0.05)
- The confidence interval excludes zero
- The effect persists, grows, or diminishes over time

### **4. Document Implementation Practices**
Interview staff at high-performing switched branches to identify best practices that could improve outcomes if the procedure is eventually adopted system-wide.

### **5. Consider Additional Outcome Measures**
Service scores are one metric. Consider adding:
- Patron satisfaction surveys
- Staff time/efficiency measures
- Circulation statistics
