# **To the Board of Directors**
# **Library Cooperative Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure likely improved service scores by about 2 points, but the evidence is not definitive.** The result falls just short of conventional statistical confidence (90% confidence rather than 95%). Given the low risk and probable benefit, we recommend proceeding with a cautious rollout while monitoring closely.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the procedure improve scores?** | Probably yes (~95% probability) |
| **Are we 95% confident?** | No—the confidence interval includes zero |
| **Is there evidence of harm?** | No |
| **Is the effect meaningful?** | Yes—if real, ~2 points is a 4% improvement |
| **Recommendation** | **Proceed with rollout, with monitoring** |

**Why this verdict?** The data points in a positive direction, but we cannot rule out the possibility that the procedure has no effect. However, waiting for perfect certainty has a real cost—14 branches continue using an inferior procedure if the effect is real. The balanced approach is to move forward while building in safeguards.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Estimated Effect: +2.1 Points**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Point estimate** | +2.1 service score points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | -0.4 to +4.7 points |
| **90% Confidence interval** | +0.01 to +4.3 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p = 0.098 (significant at 10%, not at 5%) |

### **2. Treatment Branches Narrowed the Gap**
| Period | New Procedure | Old Procedure |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| Before switch (Weeks 6–14) | 47.9 | 51.1 |
| After switch (Weeks 15–22) | 49.6 | 50.8 |
| **Change** | **+1.7** | **-0.3** |

### **3. Pre-Treatment Trends Validate the Comparison**
Before the switch, both groups followed similar patterns. This increases confidence that observed differences after Week 15 reflect the procedure change, not pre-existing trends.

### **4. Effects Were Consistent Over Time**
Improvement appeared across most post-switch weeks, with statistically significant positive effects in weeks 1 and 8 after implementation.

---

## **WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE BOARD**

- **Best estimate**: The procedure adds ~2 points to service scores
- **Uncertainty**: The true effect could be anywhere from slightly negative (-0.4) to moderately positive (+4.7)
- **Risk assessment**: Low risk of harm; moderate chance of meaningful benefit
- **Decision framework**: This is a **probabilistic recommendation**, not a definitive finding

---

## **NEXT STEPS**

### **Recommended: Proceed with Phased Rollout**

| Action | Timeline | Success Metric |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **1. Roll out to 5–7 branches** | Weeks 1–4 | No score declines |
| **2. Expand to remaining branches** | Weeks 5–8 | Systemwide improvement |
| **3. Monitor all branches weekly** | Ongoing | Track service scores |
| **4. Board review** | Week 16 | Confirm ~2-point gain |

### **If Results Disappoint**
- If scores do not improve after 8 weeks, pause and investigate
- Identify branches that don't benefit and examine why
- Consider reverting branches that show no improvement

### **Alternative: Extend the Pilot**
If the board requires higher certainty before acting:
- Continue the current study design for 8–12 more weeks
- This would narrow the confidence interval
- **Trade-off**: Delays potential benefits to 14 branches

---

## **STUDY DETAILS**

- **34 branches** participated (20 new procedure, 14 old procedure)
- **17 weeks of data** (Weeks 6–22)
- **Analysis method**: Difference-in-Differences with branch fixed effects
- **Standard errors**: Clustered by branch (accounts for within-branch correlation)
- **Controls**: Staffing index and visits index
