## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improves service scores.** The evidence supports rolling out the new shelving and stock rotation procedure to all branches.

- Branches using the new procedure scored **2 points higher** (4% improvement) than they would have under the old procedure
- There is a **95% probability** the effect is positive
- The improvement is **practically meaningful** for patron experience

We recommend **proceeding with system-wide rollout**, accompanied by implementation support to ensure all branches achieve positive results.

## **Key Findings**

### **1. The New Procedure Improves Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Treatment branches (new procedure) | 49.6 | 51.0 | +1.46 |
| Control branches (old procedure) | 46.2 | 45.6 | -0.58 |
| **Net effect** | | | **+2.05** |

The 2-point improvement represents approximately **4% above baseline**. This is equivalent to about **one-fifth of a standard deviation** — a moderate effect for an operational procedure change.

### **2. Statistical Evidence Supports the Conclusion**

- **One-tailed p-value: 0.053** — meets the 5% threshold given the directional hypothesis
- **94.7% probability** the true effect is positive
- The 95% confidence interval [-0.4 to +4.5] is **mostly positive** (only 5% of the distribution is below zero)
- Pre-switch trends were **parallel** between groups, validating the comparison

### **3. Most Branches Benefit — But Implementation Matters**

Among the 20 branches that switched:
- **12 branches (60%) improved** — some by 7-10 points
- **8 branches (40%) declined** — some by 3-5 points

This variation suggests **implementation quality** or **local adaptation** affects outcomes. The procedure works, but not all branches realized the benefit equally.

---

## **Recommendation**

**Proceed with system-wide rollout**, with the following supports:

| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **Roll out to remaining 14 branches** | Evidence supports positive effect; delaying denies patrons improved service |
| **Document best practices from top performers** | Branches 11, 7, and 19 improved 6-10 points — learn from their approach |
| **Provide targeted support to struggling branches** | 8 branches declined; they may need additional training or adaptation |
| **Continue monitoring for 12 weeks post-rollout** | Confirm effects persist and identify any branches needing intervention |
