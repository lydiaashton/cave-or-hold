# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

---

## **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores and warrants system-wide rollout, with continued monitoring.**

Branches that adopted the new procedure experienced service score increases of approximately **2.0 points** compared to branches that retained the old procedure. This effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and represents roughly a **4% improvement**. The evidence supports proceeding with rollout, though the Board should be aware that our data covers only 8 weeks post-implementation.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **1. Study Overview**
| | |
|---|---|
| **Branches analyzed** | 150 total (90 switched, 60 did not) |
| **Time period** | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences comparison |

### **2. Impact on Service Scores**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|:-------------:|:------------:|:------:|
| Control (old procedure) | 48.55 | 50.30 | +1.75 |
| Treatment (new procedure) | 49.46 | 53.24 | +3.78 |
| **Net Effect (DiD)** | | | **+2.03** |

- **Estimated effect:** +2.03 points (95% CI: 0.82 to 3.25)
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.001
- **Result holds** after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### **3. Analysis Validity**

✓ **Parallel trends confirmed** — Treatment and control groups followed similar trajectories before the switch (pre-trend test p = 0.19)

✓ **Effect is consistent** — The treatment-control gap was positive in every post-switch week (range: 2.2 to 3.7 points)

✓ **Robust to controls** — Including staffing and visits indices does not materially change the estimated effect (+2.18 points, p = 0.001)

### **4. Practical Significance**

- A 2-point gain on a ~50-point scale equals approximately **4% improvement** in service quality
- Improvement was sustained across all 8 post-switch weeks observed
- Effect size is meaningful for patron experience across the cooperative

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Statistical Evidence** | ✅ **Strong** — Effect is significant at p < 0.01 with valid methodology |
| **Practical Impact** | ⚠️ **Moderate** — 4% improvement is meaningful but not transformative |
| **Long-Term Certainty** | ⚠️ **Limited** — Only 8 weeks of post-implementation data available |
| **Cost Considerations** | ❓ **Unknown** — No data on additional staffing, training, or equipment costs |

### **Overall Recommendation: PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT**

The statistical evidence is robust and the direction of effect is unambiguous. However, the Board should:

1. **Approve rollout** — The data support expanding the new procedure
2. **Plan for evaluation** — Commit to reassessing after 6–12 months of system-wide data
3. **Track implementation costs** — Ensure any additional expenses don't outweigh the 4% service gain
4. **Remain open to adjustment** — If long-term data show effect decay or operational burdens, be prepared to modify the approach
