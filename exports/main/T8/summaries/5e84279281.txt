# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2026

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure produced a modest but statistically significant improvement in service scores** — approximately 2 points on a 50-point scale (about 4%). The effect is real and consistent across post-switch weeks, but not transformative. **We recommend a phased rollout** to confirm benefits at scale before committing all branches, allowing the board to weigh the modest gains against implementation costs.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

**The new procedure works, but the question is whether it's worth it.**

The data show a genuine improvement — not a statistical fluke. However, a 4% gain in service scores is meaningful but not dramatic. The board's decision should hinge on two questions:

1. **What are the implementation costs?** (staff training, temporary disruption, ongoing operational changes)
2. **What is the value of a 4% service improvement?** (member satisfaction, circulation, community impact)

If costs are low and the board values incremental improvements, **proceed with rollout**. If costs are substantial or the board seeks transformative change, **consider alternative investments**.

Our recommendation: **Phase 2 expansion with cost tracking**, then a final go/no-go decision with full cost-benefit information.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **Study Overview**
- **150 branches** tracked over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **90 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **60 branches** continued with the old procedure
- Service scores range from ~18 to ~82, averaging approximately 50 points

### **Results**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Branches that switched | 49.46 | 53.24 | +3.78 |
| Branches that did not switch | 48.55 | 50.30 | +1.75 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.0 to +2.2** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **P-value:** 0.001 (less than 0.2% probability this result occurred by chance)
- **95% Confidence Interval:** 0.93 to 3.44 points
- **Effect is consistent:** Positive in all 8 post-switch weeks, ranging from +2.2 to +3.7 points

### **Important Context**
- **Pre-existing difference:** Switched branches scored slightly higher even before the change (~0.9 points). The analysis accounts for this, but it suggests the groups weren't perfectly matched.
- **Modest magnitude:** A 2-point improvement is meaningful but not dramatic — roughly equivalent to moving from the 50th to 55th percentile.
- **Both groups improved:** Service scores rose after week 15 for all branches; switched branches simply improved more.

---

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Proceed with a phased rollout** rather than immediate systemwide implementation:

1. **Expand to 30–50 additional branches** over 4–6 weeks
2. **Monitor outcomes** to confirm the effect replicates
3. **Document implementation costs** (staff time, training, temporary disruption)
4. **Return to the board** with full cost-benefit analysis before final systemwide decision

This approach balances the evidence of benefit with appropriate caution given the modest effect size and observational study design.

## **TECHNICAL NOTES**

Analysis used difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch), controlling for staffing and visitor volume. The parallel trends assumption appears reasonable (pre-trend p=0.194), though the treatment group had slightly higher baseline scores. This is observational data, not a randomized trial.

**Data source:** branches.csv (2,550 observations, 150 branches)
