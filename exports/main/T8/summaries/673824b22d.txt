# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**Recommendation: Expand the pilot to more branches before deciding on system-wide rollout.**

The data show a **positive trend** (+2.5 points), but the evidence is **not statistically conclusive**. We cannot confidently say the new procedure caused the improvement. A phased expansion will provide clearer evidence before committing cooperative-wide resources.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**The new procedure shows promise but the evidence is insufficient to justify system-wide rollout at this time.**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did service scores improve in switched branches? | **Yes** – by about 2.5 points on average |
| Can we attribute this to the new procedure? | **Uncertain** – other factors may explain the difference |
| Is there evidence of harm? | **No** – no branch showed sustained declines |
| Should we roll out to all branches now? | **No** – evidence is not strong enough |
| Should we abandon the procedure? | **No** – the positive signal warrants further investigation |
| **What should the board do?** | **Approve expanded pilot with 6-month re-evaluation** |

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Show**

| Measure | Non-Switched Branches | Switched Branches |
|---------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Before switch (weeks 6–14) | 49.4 | 49.2 |
| After switch (weeks 15–22) | 49.0 | 51.2 |
| **Change** | -0.4 | **+2.0** |

**Estimated effect: +2.5 points (approximately 5% improvement)**

### **What the Statistics Tell Us**

| Statistic | Value | Interpretation |
|-----------|-------|----------------|
| Effect estimate | +2.5 points | Best guess based on data |
| P-value | 0.12 | **Not statistically significant** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | -0.6 to +5.5 | True effect could be slightly negative, zero, or moderately positive |

**Bottom line on evidence:** The data are *suggestive* of improvement but not *conclusive*. There is approximately a 12% chance we would see results this favorable even if the procedure had no real effect.

---

## **Why Not Recommend Full Rollout Yet?**

1. **Statistical uncertainty**: The confidence interval includes zero, meaning no effect is a plausible explanation for what we observed

2. **Limited data**: Only 8 weeks of post-switch data across 20 branches provides limited statistical power

3. **Resource commitment**: System-wide retraining requires significant staff time and coordination—worth doing only if we're confident of benefit

4. **Better evidence is achievable**: Expanding the pilot to more branches would clarify the true effect before full commitment

---

## **Why Not Abandon the Procedure?**

1. **No evidence of harm**: No branch showed sustained score declines after switching

2. **Positive signal**: The point estimate is in the expected direction and would be meaningful if real (~5% improvement)

3. **Implementation already underway**: 20 branches have already adopted the procedure with no reported problems

---

| Phase | Action | Timeline | Success Criteria |
