## **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that adopted the new rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by an average of **2.1 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and represents approximately a **4% gain** in service quality.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rolling out the new procedure to all branches.**

---

### **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer | Confidence |
|----------|--------|------------|
| Did the new procedure change service scores? | **Yes** | High |
| Was the change positive? | **Yes** | High |
| Is the effect statistically reliable? | **Yes** | High (p < 0.001) |
| Should we roll out system-wide? | **Yes, with monitoring** | Moderate-High |

**Rationale:** The difference-in-differences analysis provides credible causal evidence that the procedure caused the improvement. The effect is consistent across model specifications and sustained over the 8-week post-switch observation period. While the magnitude is modest (~4%), the direction is unambiguously positive with no detected negative consequences in the data.

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**1. Clear, Measurable Improvement**
- Service scores increased by **+2.15 points** after adoption (95% confidence interval: +0.96 to +3.33)
- The result is highly statistically significant (p = 0.0004)
- Against a baseline average of ~50 points, this represents a **4.2% improvement**

**2. Effect Was Immediate and Sustained**
- Weeks 15-18 (early post-switch): +2.32 points
- Weeks 19-22 (later post-switch): +1.97 points
- No evidence of fading effects over the observation period

**3. Rigorous Analysis Approach**
- Used **difference-in-differences** methodology comparing 90 switched branches against 60 control branches
- Controlled for branch-specific characteristics, time trends, staffing levels, and visitor volume
- Pre-switch trends between groups showed no statistically significant differential patterns (joint test p = 0.107)

**4. Study Scope**
- 150 branches total across the cooperative
- 17 weeks of data per branch (weeks 6-22)
- Switch occurred at week 15

---

### **RISKS AND CONSIDERATIONS**

- **Low statistical risk:** The procedure has already been tested on 90 branches (60% of the cooperative) with positive results
- **Modest effect size:** A 4% improvement is meaningful but not transformative; manage expectations accordingly
- **Implementation costs:** This analysis does not evaluate training time, labor costs, or operational disruption—these should be weighed against the benefit
- **Staffing matters:** Analysis confirms that adequate staffing amplifies service score improvements; ensure resourcing supports the rollout
