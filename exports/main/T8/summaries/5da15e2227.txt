# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure  

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure is associated with improved service scores, but confidence is moderate.** Switched branches achieved service scores **1.8 points higher** than non-switched branches after the change. However, switched branches were already improving faster before the switch, which makes it difficult to isolate the procedure's true effect.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with **phased rollout** while continuing to monitor outcomes. Do not mandate system-wide adoption until additional evidence confirms the effect.

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did service scores improve after the switch?** | **Yes** — clearly and consistently |
| **Was the procedure the sole cause?** | **Uncertain** — pre-existing trends confound the result |
| **Is the new procedure harmful?** | **No** — no evidence of negative effects |
| **Should we abandon the new procedure?** | **No** — benefits likely outweigh risks |
| **Should we roll out to all branches immediately?** | **No** — gather more evidence first |

**Verdict: CONDITIONAL APPROVAL**

> *"Proceed with the rollout, but treat it as a learning opportunity rather than a foregone conclusion. Randomize the order of remaining branches, monitor outcomes rigorously, and be prepared to adjust or halt if results don't replicate."*

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Main Result

| Metric | Value | Confidence |
|--------|-------|------------|
| **Observed Effect** | +1.81 points | High (p < 0.001) |
| **Estimated Causal Effect** | ~1.0 to 1.8 points | **Moderate** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | 1.27 to 2.36 points | — |
| Effect Size | 2–4% improvement | — |

### Why Moderate Confidence?

**Concern:** Branches selected for the switch were already on an upward trajectory before the procedure change.

| Period | Average Gap (Switched − Non-switched) |
|--------|--------------------------------------|
| Week 6 | -0.75 points |
| Week 10 | +0.22 points |
| Week 14 | +1.44 points |
| **Week 15–22 (post-switch)** | **+2.0 to +3.1 points** |

The gap was widening *before* the switch. This suggests either:
1. The procedure change added to an existing positive trend, OR
2. Branches that were already improving were selected to switch first

**Reassuring signs:**
- Post-switch gaps exceed all pre-switch observations
- Effect is consistent across all 8 post-switch weeks
- Effect appears to grow over time (week 22: +3.1 points)

## **RISKS OF EACH OPTION**

| Option | Risk |
|--------|------|
| **Full immediate rollout** | May lock in a procedure whose true effect is smaller than observed; difficult to reverse |
| **Phased rollout (recommended)** | Slower benefits to remaining branches; requires ongoing monitoring |
| **Halt rollout** | May abandon a genuinely beneficial procedure; wastes implementation effort to date |
