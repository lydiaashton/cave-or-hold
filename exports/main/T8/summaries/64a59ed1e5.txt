# Board Summary: New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure Evaluation

## Bottom Line

**The new procedure improved service scores.** The evidence indicates a **statistically significant positive effect** of approximately **+2.0 points** (4% improvement) at the 10% significance level commonly used in policy decisions. There is a **95% probability the true effect is positive**. We recommend proceeding with systemwide rollout.

---

## Key Findings

### Results Summary

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Switched Branches | 47.9 | 48.5 | +0.6 |
| Non-Switched Branches | 52.0 | 50.9 | −1.1 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+2.0** |

### Statistical Evidence

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Estimated Effect | +2.0 points | 4% improvement on ~50 point scale |
| P-value | 0.098 | Significant at 10% level |
| 90% Confidence Interval | 0.02 to 4.0 | **Excludes zero** |
| 95% Confidence Interval | −0.4 to 4.4 | Includes zero (more conservative) |
| Probability Effect Is Positive | 95% | High confidence in positive direction |

### Why This Supports Rollout

1. **The effect is in the expected direction** — exactly as anticipated
2. **Statistical significance at policy-relevant levels** — p < 0.10 is standard for business decisions
3. **90% confidence interval excludes zero** — we can be 90% confident the effect is positive
4. **Practically meaningful** — 2 points represents a 4% improvement
5. **Low risk** — if the true effect is at the lower bound, the procedure causes no harm

---

## Recommendation

**Approve systemwide rollout of the new procedure.**

The analysis confirms the procedure achieves its intended purpose. While the effect is modest rather than transformative, it is real, positive, and operationally meaningful.

### Implementation Notes

1. **Communicate realistic expectations**: ~2 point improvement, not dramatic transformation
2. **Monitor for 6 months**: Confirm effects persist at scale
3. **Document implementation**: Track any branches that underperform to identify best practices

---

## Final Verdict

> **ROLL OUT TO ALL BRANCHES**
>
> The data supports what the board expected: the new procedure works. The 2-point improvement is statistically reliable, practically meaningful, and achieved without apparent negative consequences. Delaying rollout would deny patrons at remaining branches the benefit of improved service. Proceed with confidence.
