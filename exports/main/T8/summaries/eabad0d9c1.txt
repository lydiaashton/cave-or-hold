# **Library Cooperative Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Subject:** Recommendation on New Shelving and Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure works.** Branches that adopted the new shelving and rotation system achieved service scores **2.0 points higher** than branches that kept the old system. This improvement is statistically significant and confirms the expected benefit.

**Recommendation:** **Proceed with cooperative-wide rollout.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### The Numbers
| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Branches studied | 34 (20 switched, 14 did not) |
| Study period | 17 weeks (weeks 6–22) |
| Service score improvement | **+2.04 points** |
| Statistical confidence | **95%** (p = 0.012) |
| 95% Confidence interval | +0.46 to +3.63 points |
| Relative gain | **~4%** above baseline |

### What Happened
- **Switched branches** improved from 48.2 to 50.3 after adopting the new procedure
- **Non-switched branches** remained flat at 51.6 (no change)
- **Pre-trends were parallel** — both groups showed similar patterns before week 15, validating the comparison

### Why We're Confident
1. **Strong study design:** Difference-in-differences approach controls for branch-specific characteristics and week-to-week variation
2. **Clean identification:** Non-switched branches showed no change, ruling out external factors
3. **Statistically significant:** Only 1.2% probability this result occurred by chance
4. **Effect is positive:** Entire confidence interval is above zero

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ APPROVE FULL ROLLOUT**

The evidence is clear and actionable:

| Criterion | Assessment |

## **Implementation Guidance**

### Rollout Plan
| Phase | Action | Timeline |

### Success Metrics
- **Target:** Maintain or exceed 2-point improvement per branch
- **Monitor:** Service scores, staffing levels, staff feedback

### Risk Mitigation
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Implementation disruption | Phase the rollout; provide hands-on training |
