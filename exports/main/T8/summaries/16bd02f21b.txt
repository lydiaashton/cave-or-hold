# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows **promising but inconclusive evidence** of improving service scores. The estimated effect is +1.75 points (3.7% improvement), with approximately **93% probability the true effect is positive**. However, with only 34 branches, this study had just **29% statistical power** to detect an effect of this size—meaning we had a 71% chance of missing a real effect even if one exists.

**Recommendation:** The evidence is **suggestive but not definitive**. Given the low implementation risk and high potential benefit, a **phased expansion** is warranted—but the board should not interpret these results as proof the procedure works. Continue rigorous monitoring to reach a firmer conclusion.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Show**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | +1.75 service score points |
| Percentage improvement | 3.7% |
| Statistical significance | p = 0.14 (not at 0.05 level) |
| 95% confidence interval | -0.58 to +4.07 points |
| Probability effect is positive | ~93% |

### **Why Results Are Inconclusive**

| Issue | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Low statistical power** | Only 29%—the study was too small to reliably detect this effect size |
| **Minimum detectable effect** | With 34 branches, we could only reliably detect effects of 3.5+ points |
| **Wide confidence interval** | True effect could be slightly negative or as high as +4 points |
| **Two branches declined** | 2 of 20 switched branches saw scores drop after adoption |

### **Branch-Level Patterns**

| Group | Improved | Declined | Mean Change |
|-------|----------|----------|-------------|
| Switched branches | 18 (90%) | 2 (10%) | +2.41 |
| Non-switched branches | 7 (50%) | 7 (50%) | +0.67 |

The 90% improvement rate among switched branches is encouraging, though two branches did experience declines (-2.74 and -1.14 points).

### **Analysis Validity**

✓ **Parallel trends confirmed** (p = 0.51)—groups were comparable before the switch  
✓ **Results robust** to controls for staffing and visitor volume  
✓ **No obvious confounders** identified

## **What This Means for the Decision**

| If the board... | Then... |
|-----------------|---------|
| **Requires high confidence** (p < 0.05) | **Do not rollout**—evidence is insufficient |
| **Accepts moderate evidence** with low risk | **Proceed with phased expansion**—potential upside justifies limited risk |
| **Wants definitive answers** | **Collect more data**—extend monitoring 6–12 months before deciding |

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **APPROVE PHASED EXPANSION**

**Rationale:**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Direction of evidence** | ✅ Positive (93% probability of benefit) |
| **Magnitude of effect** | ✅ Modest but meaningful (+3.7%) |
| **Statistical certainty** | ⚠️ Below conventional thresholds |
| **Risk of harm** | ✅ Low (2 of 20 branches declined; reversible) |
| **Cost of waiting** | ⚠️ Delayed benefits to 18 of 20 branches |
| **Cost of expansion** | ✅ Minimal if phased and monitored |

**Decision:** Proceed with expansion to **10–15 additional branches** over the next quarter, with mandatory continued data collection. Re-evaluate in 6 months with the larger sample. If the effect persists and strengthens, proceed to full cooperative-wide rollout.

**Conditions:**
1. Investigate the 2 branches that declined before expanding further
2. Establish clear success criteria for the phased expansion (e.g., p < 0.10 with 50+ branches)
3. Maintain the control group (non-switched branches) for ongoing comparison
4. Set a decision date for full rollout (recommend: 6–9 months from expansion start)

## **Key Uncertainties to Resolve**

1. **Why did 2 branches decline?** Understanding this could prevent problems during expansion.
2. **Is the effect sustained?** We only have 8 weeks of post-switch data.
3. **Are there implementation differences?** Some branches may execute the procedure better than others.
4. **What is the true effect size?** Could be anywhere from -0.6 to +4.1 points based on current data.
