# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Office  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores increase by approximately **2.1 points** (about 4% improvement) compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This contradicts our prior expectation of no effect, but the evidence is clear and robust. **We recommend approving a full rollout to all branches.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Statistically Significant Improvement
- Branches using the new procedure improved by **2.1 points** on the service score index
- The 95% confidence interval is **0.9 to 3.3 points**—the effect is reliably positive
- The result is **highly statistically significant** (p = 0.0005)

### 2. Modest but Meaningful Effect Size
- The improvement represents approximately **4%** relative to baseline scores
- This equals about **0.2 standard deviations**, which is a small-to-moderate effect in practice
- While not transformative, the improvement is real and comes with no detected downside

### 3. Valid Comparison Between Groups
- Treatment branches had slightly higher baseline scores (50.3 vs 48.3), but this difference was not statistically significant (p=0.13)
- Pre-switch trends were parallel between groups, supporting the validity of the comparison
- Analysis controlled for staffing levels and visitor volume

### 4. Summary of Results

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| New Procedure (90 branches) | 50.3 | 52.4 | +2.1 |
| Old Procedure (60 branches) | 48.3 | 48.4 | +0.1 |

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Roll out the new procedure to all branches.**

The evidence supports adoption:
- ✓ The improvement is **real and statistically robust**
- ✓ There is **no evidence of harm** to service scores
- ✓ The effect is **consistent across 90 branches** over multiple weeks
- ✓ Operational procedure changes typically have **low marginal costs** once developed

The 4% improvement in service scores represents a meaningful gain for patrons across the cooperative. Given that the procedure has already been developed and implemented at 90 branches, the marginal cost of extending it to the remaining 60 branches is likely low relative to the benefit.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve full rollout** to all remaining 60 branches
2. **Set an implementation timeline** (e.g., complete within 6 months)
3. **Document the procedure** to ensure consistent implementation
4. **Monitor service scores quarterly** post-rollout to confirm the effect persists
5. **Investigate why the procedure works** to identify opportunities for further optimization

---

## **Methodology Note**

This analysis used a **difference-in-differences** approach with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered at branch level). The parallel pre-trends assumption was tested and confirmed. Data covers 150 branches over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22).
