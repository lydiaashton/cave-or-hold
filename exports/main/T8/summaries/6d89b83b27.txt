# **MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure produced a statistically significant but modest improvement in service scores.** Branches using the new procedure scored **2.1 points higher** (on a ~50-point scale) than comparable branches using the old procedure. This finding **contradicts the expectation** that the procedure change would have no effect.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with a phased rollout while gathering additional information on costs and long-term effects.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Value | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Treatment effect | +2.13 points | Statistically significant (p = 0.0006) |
| 95% Confidence interval | 0.91 to 3.35 | Effect is real, but magnitude uncertain |
| Effect size | 0.21 standard deviations | Small-to-moderate practical impact |
| Consistency | Positive in all 8 post-switch weeks | Effect appears stable |
| Pre-trend test | No significant difference (p = 0.69) | Groups were comparable before the switch |

---

## **Final Verdict**

**The new procedure should be adopted, but with measured implementation.**

| Question | Answer | Confidence |
|----------|--------|------------|
| Did the procedure change service scores? | **Yes** – scores increased | High |
| Is the effect statistically reliable? | **Yes** – p < 0.001 | High |
| Is the effect large enough to matter? | **Modestly** – ~4% improvement | Medium |
| Will the effect persist long-term? | **Unknown** – only 8 weeks of data | Low |
| Do benefits exceed costs? | **Unknown** – no cost data provided | N/A |

**Rationale:** The statistical evidence is strong that the new procedure improved service scores. However, the effect is modest, the observation period is short, and we lack cost information. A full immediate rollout would be premature, but there is sufficient evidence to justify continued adoption while monitoring outcomes and costs.

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve phased expansion** to 25–50 additional branches over the next quarter

2. **Request cost analysis** from operations (training time, equipment, staffing changes)

3. **Extend data collection** to 6–12 months to assess whether effects persist

4. **Investigate mechanisms** – interview staff at switched branches to understand *why* scores improved

5. **Return to board in Q2 2027** with updated cost-benefit analysis for full rollout decision

---

## **Technical Note**

Analysis used difference-in-differences regression with cluster-robust standard errors (clustered by branch). The parallel trends assumption was validated by testing for differential pre-trends during weeks 6–14 (p = 0.69). Sample: 150 branches, 2,550 branch-week observations.
