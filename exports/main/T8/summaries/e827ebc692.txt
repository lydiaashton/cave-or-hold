# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure produced a small but statistically reliable improvement in service scores (~2 points, or 4%).** The effect is consistent across all post-switch weeks but modest in magnitude. We recommend rollout, with the understanding that benefits are real but incremental.

---

## **Final Verdict**

### **ROLL OUT THE NEW PROCEDURE**

**Confidence Level:** High  
**Expected Benefit:** Small but meaningful (+2 points / +4%)  
**Risk:** Low (procedure already tested on 90 branches)  

**Rationale:** The data provides credible evidence that the new procedure improves service scores. The effect is statistically significant (p = 0.007), appears in every post-switch week without exception, and is robust to alternative analytical approaches. While the improvement is modest rather than transformative, it represents a genuine gain in service quality at presumably minimal cost.

**The board's initial expectation—that the procedure change would make no difference—was incorrect.** The new procedure does make a difference, and that difference is positive.

---

## **Key Findings**

| Metric | Estimate |
|--------|----------|
| **Treatment Effect** | +2.1 service score points |
| **95% Confidence Interval** | 0.6 to 3.7 points |
| **Statistical Significance** | p = 0.007 |
| **Relative Improvement** | 4.1% over baseline |
| **Effect Size** | Small (Cohen's d = 0.22) |

### What Changed

| Period | Control Branches | Switched Branches | Gap |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|-----|
| **Before switch** (weeks 6-14) | 49.8 | 50.7 | 0.9 |
| **After switch** (weeks 15-22) | 50.0 | 53.1 | 3.1 |
| **Change in gap** | +0.2 | +2.4 | **+2.2** |

### Why We Trust This Result

1. **Clean break at week 15** – Every post-switch week shows a larger gap (2.4–4.4 points) than any pre-switch week (−0.1 to 1.5 points)
2. **Parallel pre-trends** – The treatment-control gap was stable before the switch (p = 0.67 for trend difference)
3. **Robust to controls** – Effect persists after accounting for staffing and visitor volume

---

## **What This Means**

The board expected no difference. The data shows a **small positive effect**:

- **Not transformative:** A 2-point gain on a ~50-point scale is noticeable but not dramatic
- **Consistent:** The effect appears in every post-switch week without exception
- **Actionable:** If the new procedure requires minimal additional resources, the benefit likely justifies rollout

---

## **Recommendations**

| Priority | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **1** | Approve rollout to remaining 60 branches |
| **2** | Set realistic expectations – improvements are incremental, not dramatic |
| **3** | Monitor for 8–12 weeks post-rollout to confirm effect persists |
| **4** | Document which specific practices drive the improvement |

---

## **Caveats**

- **Short observation window:** Only 8 weeks of post-switch data
- **Modest effect size:** Not all branches may see meaningful improvement
- **Implementation matters:** Benefits depend on faithful adoption of the new procedure

---

## **Conclusion**

The new procedure delivers a **small, reliable improvement** in service quality. While not the transformative change some might hope for, the effect is real, consistent, and statistically robust. For a procedure change that presumably requires minimal investment, a 4% service improvement represents a positive return.

**Recommendation: Proceed with rollout, with measured expectations.**

---

*Analysis based on 2,550 branch-week observations. Method: Difference-in-differences regression with robust standard errors.*
