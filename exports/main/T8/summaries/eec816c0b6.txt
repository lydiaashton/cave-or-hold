# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Team

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**The new procedure likely improved service scores, but the evidence is less certain than initially apparent.** 

After accounting for pre-existing differences between branches, the estimated effect is approximately **1.0 point** (about 2% improvement) rather than the 2.0 points from our initial analysis. The effect remains statistically significant, but we recommend a **phased rollout with continued monitoring** rather than immediate system-wide implementation.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### Analysis Overview
- **709 branches** analyzed over **17 weeks** (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure at week 15; **284 branches** retained the old procedure

### Critical Discovery: Pre-Existing Trends
**Treatment branches were already improving faster than control branches BEFORE the switch.**

| Week | Treatment − Control Difference |
|------|-------------------------------|
| 6    | −0.18 points |
| 10   | +0.31 points |
| 14   | +1.66 points |

This pre-trend (0.21 points/week, p=0.004) means our initial analysis likely **overstated the effect by about 50%**.

### Revised Service Score Impact

| Estimate | Effect Size | 95% Confidence Interval |
|----------|-------------|------------------------|
| Initial (unadjusted) | +1.98 points | [1.43, 2.52] |
| **Conservative (pre-trend adjusted)** | **+1.02 points** | **[0.23, 1.82]** |

### Statistical Confidence
- **Statistical significance:** p = 0.011 (still significant, but less certain)
- **Practical significance:** ~2% improvement from baseline score of 52
- **Uncertainty:** True effect could be as small as 0.2 points or as large as 1.8 points

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure improve service scores?** | **Yes, probably** |
| **Is the effect large enough to justify full rollout?** | **Uncertain — more evidence needed** |
| **Should we abandon the new procedure?** | **No — evidence points to genuine improvement** |
| **What should the Board decide today?** | **Approve phased expansion, not full rollout** |

**Bottom-line recommendation:** The procedure shows promise, but pre-existing trends among treatment branches make it difficult to isolate the true effect. A cautious, evidence-based approach protects the cooperative from committing resources to a potentially overstated benefit while still allowing patrons to gain from likely improvements.

| Priority | Action | Timeline |

## **RECOMMENDATION**

**Approve phased expansion to 100-150 additional branches with randomized selection.** This approach:
- Tests whether the effect replicates in new branches
- Better isolates the procedure's impact from other factors
- Enables a more informed full-rollout decision with reduced risk
- Still delivers benefits to more patrons while we gather evidence

**Return to Board in 12 weeks** with updated analysis for full rollout decision.
