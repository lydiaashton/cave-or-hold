# **Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores, but the effect is modest.** Branches that adopted the new procedure showed absolute improvements (+1.07 points), and significantly more of them improved compared to control branches (61% vs. 39%). However, pre-existing trends between groups mean the true effect may be smaller than initial estimates suggest.

**Estimated benefit: 1.0 to 1.5 points** (approximately **2–3% improvement**)

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Recommendation** | ✅ **Proceed with rollout, but phased** |
| **Confidence** | Moderate (evidence is positive but not definitive) |
| **Risk** | Low (no evidence of harm; switched branches improved absolutely) |
| **Expected impact** | Modest but meaningful improvement in service scores |

**Rationale:** The new procedure shows positive effects with no detectable downsides. While pre-existing differences between branches make the exact effect size uncertain, the weight of evidence favors adoption. A phased rollout allows the cooperative to capture benefits while continuing to monitor outcomes and adjust implementation as needed.

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. What Changed
| Group | Before | After | Change |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.70 | 52.77 | **+1.07** |
| Control branches | 51.22 | 50.43 | **-0.79** |

### 2. Statistical Evidence
- **61% of switched branches** improved vs. **39% of control branches**
- Treatment effect is statistically significant (p < 0.001)
- Effect size: ~0.19 standard deviations (modest but meaningful)
- Benefits appear to **grow over time** (from +1.5 to +3.7 points over 8 weeks)

### 3. Important Caveats
- Switched branches were already trending upward relative to controls before the switch
- This pre-trend suggests the true effect may be **smaller than the raw 1.86-point estimate**
- Control branches declined after week 15, which may reflect unrelated factors

### 4. Study Parameters
- **709 branches** total: 425 switched, 284 controls
- **17 weeks of data**: Weeks 6–14 (before), Weeks 15–22 (after)
- Analysis controls for branch characteristics, staffing, and visitor volume

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve phased rollout** to remaining branches over 3–6 months

2. **Set success metrics** — target 1+ point improvement in service scores within 8 weeks of switching

3. **Monitor each cohort** — track scores for 12 weeks post-switch; pause rollout if effects don't materialize

4. **Document implementation factors** — identify which branches achieve the best results and why

5. **Re-evaluate at 6 months** — decide whether to continue, expand, or modify the procedure based on accumulated data

---

## **Methodology Notes**

This analysis uses a **difference-in-differences** approach comparing service score changes between branches that adopted the new procedure and those that did not. Results were validated using branch fixed effects regression and branch-level change comparisons. The main limitation is that switched branches showed improving trends relative to controls even before the procedure change, which may partially confound the estimated effect.
