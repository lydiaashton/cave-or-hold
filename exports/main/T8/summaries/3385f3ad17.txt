# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**
## Analysis for Board Decision on System-Wide Rollout

---

### **BOTTOM LINE**

The new procedure is associated with a **2.3-point improvement in service scores**, but this result is **not statistically significant** (p=0.09). The data cannot confirm the procedure caused the improvement—it could be random variation. However, there is **no evidence of harm**. We recommend **continuing the current pilot** and collecting more data before committing to system-wide rollout.

---

### **FINAL VERDICT**

> **DELAY full system-wide rollout. CONTINUE the pilot for 8–12 more weeks. RECONSIDER rollout decision in Q2 2026.**

**Reasoning:**
- The evidence points in the right direction but falls short of statistical confidence
- Rolling out now risks committing resources to a procedure that may have no real effect
- Waiting 8–12 weeks provides sufficient data to reach a confident conclusion
- No urgency exists—the 20 pilot branches are operating successfully with the new procedure

---

### **KEY FINDINGS**

**1. What the Data Shows**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| New Procedure Branches | 50.0 | 52.3 | **+2.3** |
| Old Procedure Branches | 49.1 | 49.1 | **0.0** |
| **Estimated Effect** | | | **+2.3 points** |

**2. What the Statistics Tell Us**

- **p-value: 0.09** — above the 0.05 threshold typically required for confident conclusions
- **95% confidence interval: -0.4 to +5.0 points**
  - The true effect could be zero (no impact)
  - The true effect could be as high as +5 points (meaningful improvement)
  - The true effect is very unlikely to be negative (no evidence of harm)

**3. Why We Can't Be Certain**

- Only **8 weeks of post-switch data** limits our ability to detect real effects
- With ~40% more data, we could confirm whether this effect is real
- The point estimate is positive and aligns with expectations, but evidence is inconclusive

**4. What We Can Say With Confidence**

- ✅ Pre-switch trends were parallel (validates our comparison)
- ✅ No branches experienced score declines attributable to the change
- ✅ The effect, if real, represents approximately 5% improvement
- ❌ We cannot confirm the new procedure *caused* the improvement

---

### **DECISION FRAMEWORK**

| Scenario | Probability | Recommended Action |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Effect is real (+2 to +5 points) | Moderate | Roll out after confirmation |
| Effect is zero (no impact) | Possible | Consider other benefits (e.g., staff efficiency) before deciding |
| Effect is negative | Very unlikely | Abandon new procedure |
