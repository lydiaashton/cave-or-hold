# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure shows a **positive effect on service scores that is suggestive but not statistically definitive**. The estimated improvement of **~2 points (4%)** has a **94% probability of being genuinely positive**, though it does not meet conventional statistical significance thresholds (p = 0.11). Weekly performance has been volatile, consistent with an adjustment period.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Overview**
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total branches | 34 |
| Branches using new procedure | 20 |
| Branches using old procedure | 14 |
| Observation period | Weeks 6–22 |
| Procedure switch date | Week 15 |
| Service score scale | ~50 average (higher is better) |

### **Main Result**

| Group | Before Week 15 | After Week 15 | Change |
|-------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| New procedure branches | 47.51 | 48.77 | +1.26 |
| Old procedure branches | 51.23 | 50.55 | −0.68 |
| **Net Effect** | | | **+1.94 points** |

- **95% Confidence Interval:** −0.45 to +4.33 points
- **P-value:** 0.11 (not significant at 5% level)
- **Probability effect is positive:** 94%

### **Weekly Performance After Switch**

Effects have fluctuated considerably:
- **Significant gains:** Weeks 17 (+2.65), 19 (+6.21)
- **Significant declines:** Weeks 15 (−4.03), 18 (−5.03)
- **Recent weeks (20–22):** Small positive effects, not significant

This volatility is consistent with branches still learning the new procedures.

### **Validity Check**

✓ Pre-trend analysis confirms switched and non-switched branches followed similar trajectories before the change.

✓ Results are robust when controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with rollout to the remaining 14 branches, with conditions.**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Evidence direction** | ✓ Favors adoption (94% probability of positive effect) |
| **Effect size** | ✓ Meaningful (~4% improvement in service scores) |
| **Statistical certainty** | ⚠ Below conventional thresholds (p = 0.11) |
| **Implementation status** | ✓ 20 of 34 branches already switched; reversal would be disruptive |
| **Risk profile** | ✓ No evidence of systematic harm; volatility appears to be adjustment-related |

**Rationale:** While we lack statistical certainty, the evidence points clearly in a favorable direction. The cost of delaying rollout (14 branches missing out on potential 4% service gains) outweighs the risk that the true effect is negligible. However, we must remain accountable for this decision.

---

| Condition | Timeline | Success Metric |
