# **Board Memorandum**

**To:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors
**From:** Data Analysis Office
**Date:** November 15, 2026
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure likely improved service scores, but the effect is smaller than initial analysis suggested.** After accounting for pre-existing trends, the procedure probably added **0.5 to 1.5 points** (1–3% improvement) to service scores. While statistically significant, switched branches were already outperforming control branches before the switch. **We recommend proceeding with rollout, but with tempered expectations and close monitoring.**

---

## **Key Findings**

### Raw Comparison Shows Large Gain
| Metric | Control (Old) | Switched (New) |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| Before switch | 50.64 | 51.57 |
| After switch | 50.84 | 53.95 |
| **Change** | +0.21 | **+2.38** |

### But Pre-Existing Trends Complicate Interpretation
The switched branches were already improving relative to control branches *before* the procedure changed:

- **Week 6 difference:** 0.19 points
- **Week 14 difference:** 2.13 points (week before switch)
- **Pre-switch trend:** +0.30 points/week

This suggests switched branches may have had other advantages (better management, more resources, etc.) independent of the procedure.

### Conservative Effect Estimate: ~1 Point
Using week 14 as the baseline (most comparable pre-switch period):
- Week 14 gap: 2.13 points
- Post-switch average gap: 3.11 points
- **Attributable effect: ~1.0 point** (95% CI: 0.3 to 1.7)

### Effect Is Consistent Post-Switch
All 8 weeks after the switch showed positive differences ranging from 2.4 to 3.8 points, with no sign of decay.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**✅ APPROVE ROLLOUT — with conditions**

| Consideration | Assessment |
|---------------|------------|
| **Did the procedure help?** | Yes, likely ~1 point improvement |
| **Is the effect meaningful?** | Modest but positive (2% gain) |
| **Is there risk of harm?** | No evidence of negative effects |
| **Is the evidence conclusive?** | No—pre-trends weaken causal claim |
| **Should we roll out?** | **Yes**, but monitor closely |

**Rationale:** The procedure shows consistent positive association with service scores and no detected harms. While we cannot fully isolate the procedure's effect from other branch characteristics, the post-switch acceleration in the gap suggests genuine benefit. Given the cooperative's goal of improving service and the absence of reported implementation costs or negative consequences, proceeding with rollout is justified—but expectations should be calibrated to the conservative estimate.

## **Methodology Note**

Standard difference-in-differences assumes parallel pre-trends, which was not fully satisfied here. The 2.17-point estimate likely overstates the true effect. The conservative 1.0-point estimate uses the week immediately preceding the switch as baseline. Further analysis with branch fixed effects and longer pre-period data would strengthen causal inference.

*Analysis: 12,053 observations, 709 branches, weeks 6–22*
