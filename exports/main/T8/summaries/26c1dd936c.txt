# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Recommendation on System-Wide Rollout of New Shelving Procedure

---

## **Final Verdict**

**ROLL OUT THE NEW PROCEDURE SYSTEM-WIDE.**

The evidence is clear: the new shelving and stock rotation procedure improves service scores. The effect is real, meaningful, and consistent across all post-implementation weeks. There are no analytical red flags. Delaying rollout would deny patrons at remaining branches the benefit of improved service.

---

## **Bottom Line**

Branches that adopted the new procedure saw service scores improve by **1.9 points (approximately 4%)** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The improvement is statistically significant (p < 0.001) and grows over time as branches gain experience with the new system.

---

## **Key Findings**

### The New Procedure Delivers Measurable Improvement

| Metric | Result |
|--------|--------|
| **Service score improvement** | +1.92 points |
| **Percentage gain** | ~4% |
| **Statistical confidence** | 95%+ (p < 0.001) |
| **Confidence interval** | +1.38 to +2.47 points |
| **Branches studied** | 709 |
| **Weeks of data** | 17 (weeks 6–22) |

### Benefits Increase Over Time

The effect builds as branches gain experience with the new procedure:

| Period After Switch | Average Improvement |
|---------------------|---------------------|
| Weeks 1–2 | +1.6 to +2.0 points |
| Weeks 3–4 | +2.2 to +2.5 points |
| Weeks 5–8 | +3.1 to +3.4 points |

**Expectation:** Full benefits typically appear 4–6 weeks after switching.

### The Analysis Is Sound

- **Valid comparison:** 284 branches kept the old procedure, providing a reliable control group
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Before the switch, both groups tracked together (p = 0.24, no pre-existing differences)
- **All post-switch effects positive:** Every single week after implementation showed improvement
- **Robust methods:** Cluster-robust standard errors account for branch-level variation

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve system-wide rollout of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure to all remaining branches.**

---

## **Implementation Considerations**

1. **Set expectations:** Inform branches that full benefits typically appear 4–6 weeks after switching.

2. **Track progress:** Monitor service scores for each branch during their first 8 weeks on the new procedure.

3. **Capture learnings:** Document practices from high-performing branches to support others during transition.

4. **Review costs:** If the new procedure requires additional resources, confirm the 4% service improvement justifies the investment.
