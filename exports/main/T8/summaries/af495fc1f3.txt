# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact Analysis**
### *Recommendation for System-Wide Rollout*

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure produced a statistically significant improvement in service scores (+2.5 points, ~5%), but benefits were not uniform across branches.** Seventy percent of switched branches improved, compared to 55% of control branches. The evidence supports a **phased rollout with performance monitoring**, rather than an immediate system-wide mandate.

**Recommendation: Approve phased rollout with close monitoring. Branches that don't show improvement after 8–12 weeks should receive additional support.**

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Criterion | Assessment | Rating |
|-----------|------------|--------|
| **Statistical evidence** | Effect is real (p < 0.001) | ✅ Strong |
| **Practical significance** | 5% improvement is modest but meaningful | ⚠️ Moderate |
| **Consistency across branches** | 70% improved; 30% did not | ⚠️ Variable |
| **Duration of effect** | Only 8 weeks observed | ⚠️ Unknown long-term |
| **Cost-benefit** | Costs not analyzed | ❓ Pending |
| **Implementation risk** | Low—procedure already tested at 90 branches | ✅ Low |

**Overall Verdict: PROCEED WITH CAUTION**

The new procedure should be rolled out, but with safeguards:
- Phased implementation (not all at once)
- Performance thresholds for each branch
- Ongoing monitoring and support for struggling branches
- Formal review at 6 months

---

## **Study Design**

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Branches studied** | 150 total (90 switched, 60 control) |
| **Study period** | 17 weeks (Weeks 6–22) |
| **Switch date** | Week 15 |
| **Method** | Difference-in-differences with branch and week fixed effects |
| **Outcome measure** | Service score index (average ~50, higher is better) |

---

## **Key Findings**

### 1. Measurable Service Score Improvement

| Group | Pre-Switch | Post-Switch | Change |
|-------|------------|-------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 50.8 | 53.4 | +2.6 |
| Control branches | 50.1 | 50.1 | 0.0 |
| **Net effect** | — | — | **+2.5** |

- **Treatment effect: +2.5 points** (95% CI: 1.2 to 3.8)
- **Statistical significance: p < 0.001**
- **Relative improvement: ~5%** from baseline

### 2. Valid Comparison Confirmed

Pre-switch trends (weeks 6–14) showed **no significant difference** between treatment and control branches (p = 0.40). This confirms the groups were on parallel trajectories before the intervention.

### 3. Branch-Level Variation

| Metric | Treatment Branches | Control Branches |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| % with improvement | 70% | 55% |
| Average change | +2.6 points | 0.0 points |
| Standard deviation | 4.35 | 3.90 |

**Implication:** The new procedure helps most branches, but not all. Implementation support matters.

### 4. Effect Sustained Over 8 Weeks

The improvement persisted throughout all post-switch weeks, with treatment branches outperforming control branches by 2.4 to 4.2 points each week.

## **Recommendations**

### **Primary Recommendation**
✅ **Approve phased rollout** of the new shelving and stock rotation procedure to all 60 branches currently using the old method.

### **Implementation Plan**

| Phase | Action | Timeline | Success Metric |
|-------|--------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | Roll out to 10–15 branches | Months 1–2 | All branches show ≥1 point improvement |
| 2 | Evaluate and adjust | Month 3 | Identify implementation barriers |
| 3 | Continue rollout | Months 4–6 | 70%+ of branches show improvement |
| 4 | Full review | Month 6 | Decide on continuation/modification |

### **Performance Thresholds**

| Metric | Target | Action if Not Met |
|--------|--------|-------------------|
| Service score change (12 weeks) | ≥ +1 point | Implementation review |
| Service score change (24 weeks) | ≥ +2 points | Additional support or procedure adjustment |
| Branch satisfaction | ≥ 80% positive | Staff feedback session |

### **Monitoring Plan**

1. **Weekly tracking** of service scores during rollout
2. **Monthly reports** to the board on progress and aggregate changes
3. **Branch-level dashboards** to identify struggling locations early
4. **6-month formal review** to assess long-term sustainability
