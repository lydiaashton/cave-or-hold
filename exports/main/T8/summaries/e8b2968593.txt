# **MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD**

**Subject:** Library Service Procedure Change – Impact Analysis and Recommendation  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Prepared by:** Data Analysis Office

---

## **BOTTOM LINE**

**We cannot conclusively attribute the score improvement to the new procedure.** While branches that switched show higher scores after week 15 (+1.9 points vs. control), those same branches were already outperforming the control group—and the gap was widening—before the switch occurred.

**VERDICT: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE FOR SYSTEM-WIDE ROLLOUT**

The observed improvement may reflect pre-existing differences between branches rather than a true causal effect of the procedure change.

---

## **KEY FINDINGS**

### **What the Data Shows**

| Period | Treatment-Control Gap |
|--------|----------------------|
| Week 6 (baseline) | +1.34 points |
| Week 14 (pre-switch) | +2.53 points |
| Week 15–22 (post-switch) | +3.36 points |

The gap was **already widening by 0.20 points per week** before the procedure changed (p = 0.003).

### **Why This Matters**

The standard analysis method requires that treatment and control groups follow **parallel trends** before the intervention. **This assumption is violated.** The observed "effect" could reflect:

1. A real procedure benefit (what we hoped)
2. Pre-existing differences between branches that switched vs. didn't
3. Unmeasured factors affecting the groups differently over time

### **What We Can and Cannot Say**

| Claim | Supported? |
|-------|------------|
| Switched branches have higher scores post-switch | ✓ Yes |
| The new procedure caused the improvement | ✗ No |
| System-wide rollout will improve scores | ✗ Unknown |

---

## **FINAL VERDICT**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did service scores improve in switched branches? | **Yes** |
| Can we credit the new procedure? | **No** |
| Should we roll out system-wide now? | **No** |

**Rationale:** Investing in a system-wide rollout based on this evidence risks spending resources on a procedure that may not deliver the expected benefits. The branches that switched appear to have been on a stronger trajectory regardless of the procedure change.

---

## **RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS**

| Action | Timeline | Owner |
|--------|----------|-------|
| 1. **Pause system-wide rollout** | Immediate | Board |
| 2. Investigate why certain branches switched – were they systematically different? | 30 days | Operations |
| 3. **Design a randomized controlled trial** with 50–100 branches randomly assigned to new vs. old procedure | 60 days | Analytics + Operations |
| 4. Re-evaluate with causal evidence | 6 months | Board |
