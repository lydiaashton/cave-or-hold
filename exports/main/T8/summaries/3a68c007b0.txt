# Memorandum

**To:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**From:** Data Analysis Team  
**Date:** November 2025  
**Subject:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## Bottom Line

**The new procedure is associated with improved service scores, but we cannot definitively attribute all of the gain to the intervention.** The estimated effect is +2.1 points, though pre-existing differences between branches mean the true causal effect may be somewhat smaller. We recommend a **monitored rollout** with continued evaluation.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Estimated Improvement

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Difference-in-differences estimate** | +2.12 points |
| **95% Confidence interval** | 1.58 to 2.66 points |
| **Statistical significance** | p < 0.001 |

**Important caveat:** Treatment branches were already outperforming control branches by an increasing margin before the switch. This means the +2.12 estimate likely includes some pre-existing trend, not just the treatment effect.

### 2. Encouraging Patterns

Despite the caveat above, several patterns support a genuine treatment effect:

- **Pre-trends converged** just before the switch (weeks 12-14 showed no significant divergence)
- **Effects grew over time** post-switch: negligible in weeks 15-17, but reaching +2.2 points by week 22
- **Effect persists** after controlling for staffing levels and visitor volume

### 3. What Happened to Each Group

| Group | Pre-Switch Average | Post-Switch Average | Change |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Switched branches | 51.85 | 52.52 | +0.67 |
| Non-switched branches | 51.09 | 49.64 | -1.45 |

Control branches declined during this period. The new procedure may have helped switched branches maintain their performance amid broader challenges.

---

## Final Verdict

**PROCEED WITH ROLLOUT — WITH MODERATE CONFIDENCE**

| Question | Verdict |
|----------|---------|
| Did service scores improve after the switch? | **Yes** — clearly |
| Can we prove the procedure caused all of it? | **No** — pre-trends complicate attribution |
| Is the evidence strong enough to act? | **Yes** — the pattern is consistent with real benefit |
| Should we expect +2.1 points at every branch? | **Uncertain** — effects may vary |

**Reasoning:** The pre-existing trend between groups means this is not a clean natural experiment. However, the convergence of trends immediately before the switch, combined with the growing post-switch effect, suggests the procedure is contributing meaningfully to service quality. The risk of rolling out appears low compared to the potential benefit.

**Confidence Level:** Moderate (60-70%). We would assign higher confidence with a randomized rollout design.

---

## Recommendation

**Proceed with phased rollout, with safeguards:**

1. **Phase the implementation** rather than switching all branches at once
2. **Continue weekly monitoring** to confirm effects persist
3. **Set a review point** at 90 days to reassess based on new data
4. **Be prepared to adjust** if results differ from this pilot
