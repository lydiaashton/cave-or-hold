# **MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Board of Directors, Library Cooperative  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The evidence is mixed.** The new procedure shows a positive association with service scores, but we cannot confidently attribute the full improvement to the procedure itself. Branches that adopted the new procedure were already improving relative to other branches before the switch. A conservative estimate suggests a **0.5 to 1.0 point improvement**, though this is less certain than our initial analysis indicated.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with caution. Consider a phased rollout with continued monitoring rather than immediate system-wide adoption.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **What the Data Show**

| Analysis | Estimated Effect | Confidence |
|----------|-----------------|------------|
| Simple comparison (DiD) | +2.0 points | High statistical significance |
| Week-by-week (event study) | +0.9 points average | Weak—only 2 of 8 post-weeks significant |
| **Adjusted for pre-trends** | **+0.5 to +1.0 points** | **Moderate uncertainty** |

### **Why the Uncertainty?**

The branches that switched to the new procedure were **already improving** before the switch:
- In weeks 6-11, switched branches scored 1-2 points **lower** than expected
- By weeks 13-14 (just before switch), this gap had closed
- This pre-existing trend makes it difficult to isolate the procedure's true effect

### **What We Can Say with Confidence**

- Service scores at switched branches **did increase** after the procedure change
- The increase is **at least partially attributable** to the new procedure
- The effect is **likely positive** but **smaller than the initial 2-point estimate**
- There is **no evidence of harm**—scores did not decline

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the procedure improve service scores?** | Probably yes, but the effect is modest |
| **Is the evidence strong enough for full rollout?** | No—pre-existing trends create uncertainty |
| **Is there risk in expanding the procedure?** | Low—no evidence of negative effects |
| **What should the Board do?** | **Approve phased expansion with monitoring** |

**The procedure likely helps, but we cannot yet quantify the benefit with confidence. A cautious expansion protects the cooperative from committing resources to an unproven intervention while allowing us to gather better evidence.**

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve a phased rollout with rigorous monitoring:**

1. **Expand to 50-100 additional branches** over the next 8 weeks
2. **Collect 8-12 weeks of post-implementation data** before full rollout
3. **Re-analyze** with the expanded dataset to confirm the effect
4. **Proceed to full rollout** only if the effect is confirmed at ~1 point or higher

This approach balances the likely benefit against the uncertainty in our current evidence.
