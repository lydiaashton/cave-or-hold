# **BOARD MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Library Cooperative Board of Directors  
**FROM:** Data Analysis Team  
**DATE:** November 15, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure  

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure helped branches maintain service scores during a period of system-wide decline.**

Branches using the new procedure held steady (−0.2 points) while branches using the old procedure dropped significantly (−2.4 points). The new procedure appears to **protect against performance erosion** rather than drive improvement. 

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but frame expectations appropriately—the procedure helps branches maintain performance during challenging periods, not necessarily boost scores above baseline.

---

## **Key Findings**

### What Actually Happened

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** | 51.92 | 51.75 | **−0.17** |
| **Non-switched branches** | 52.11 | 49.67 | **−2.44** |

**Net relative benefit: +2.27 points** (95% CI: 1.83 to 2.68)

### Important Context

1. **System-wide seasonal pattern:** Service scores naturally declined across ALL branches after week 14 (the post-switch period coincides with weeks 15-22, which historically show lower scores)

2. **The new procedure provided resilience:** Switched branches experienced only 7% of the decline that non-switched branches experienced

3. **No absolute improvement:** The procedure did not raise scores above pre-switch levels—it prevented a larger drop

### Statistical Confidence
- The protective effect is **highly significant** (p < 0.001)
- Results hold after controlling for branch characteristics, staffing, and visit volumes
- Both groups followed parallel trends before the switch

### No Operational Trade-offs
- Staffing requirements: unchanged (p = 0.14)
- Visit volumes: unchanged (p = 0.70)

---

## **Final Verdict**

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Did the new procedure change service scores?** | **Yes** — it prevented a significant decline |
| **Did scores improve in absolute terms?** | **No** — switched branches held steady, they did not gain |
| **Is the effect real or random noise?** | **Real** — statistically significant at p < 0.001 |
| **Should we roll out to all branches?** | **Yes** — the protective benefit is meaningful and comes without added costs |
| **What should the board expect?** | **Stability, not growth** — the procedure shields branches from performance drops during challenging periods |

---

## **Recommendation**

**Approve rollout with calibrated expectations.**

The new procedure delivers real value—branches using it avoided a 2+ point decline that affected branches using the old procedure. However, the board should understand this as **risk mitigation** rather than performance enhancement.

**Expected outcome:** System-wide service scores will likely remain stable (not increase) during normal conditions, but branches will be better protected during periods that typically see score declines.
