# **Board Summary: Evaluation of New Branch Rotation and Shelving Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure improved service scores.** Branches that switched to the new rotation and shelving procedure saw their service scores increase by **2.2 points** compared to branches that kept the old procedure. This improvement is statistically significant and represents a meaningful 4% gain in service quality.

---

## **Final Verdict**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Did the new procedure change service scores?** | **Yes** |
| **Direction of change** | **Positive** (scores increased) |
| **Statistical confidence** | **High** (p < 0.001) |
| **Practical significance** | **Moderate** (4% improvement) |
| **Recommendation** | **Proceed with system-wide rollout** |

---

## **Key Findings**

### **Study Design**
- **709 branches** analyzed over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** (60%) adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** (40%) kept the old procedure
- The switch occurred in **week 15**, creating a natural experiment
- Analysis compares changes in switched branches against changes in non-switched branches (difference-in-differences)

### **Results**

| Group | Before Switch | After Switch | Change |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------|
| **Switched branches** (new procedure) | 51.59 | 52.83 | +1.24 |
| **Non-switched branches** (old procedure) | 51.31 | 50.37 | −0.95 |
| **Net effect of new procedure** | | | **+2.19** |

### **Statistical Confidence**
- **Treatment effect: +2.17 points** (95% confidence interval: 1.59 to 2.75)
- **p-value < 0.001** — the result is highly unlikely to be due to chance
- **Pre-trend validation:** Branches showed parallel trends before the switch (p = 0.99), confirming the comparison is valid

### **Effect Size Context**
- The 2.2-point improvement equals **4.2% of the average service score**
- This represents about **0.22 standard deviations** — a moderate but meaningful improvement in service quality
- Control branches actually declined slightly during this period, making the treatment effect even more notable

### **Consistency**
- The positive effect held across branches with different staffing levels
- Results are robust whether or not we control for staffing and visit volumes

---

## **Next Steps**

1. **Approve system-wide rollout** of the new rotation and shelving procedure to all remaining branches

2. **Monitor implementation** during the transition:
   - Track service scores weekly for the first 8 weeks after each branch switches
   - Ensure staff receive adequate training on the new procedure

3. **Document best practices** from the 425 branches that already switched to support smooth adoption elsewhere

4. **Plan follow-up evaluation** in 3–6 months to assess whether the improvement is sustained over time
