# **Evaluation of New Branch Stock Rotation Procedure**

---

## **Bottom Line**

**The new procedure produced a modest but real improvement in service scores.** Branches that adopted the new procedure gained approximately **2 points** on the service score index compared to branches that kept the old procedure. The effect is statistically significant and consistent across all post-switch weeks, but it represents a small-to-moderate improvement (about 0.2 standard deviations).

**Recommendation:** Proceed with rollout, but set realistic expectations—the improvement is meaningful but not transformative.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**ADOPT THE NEW PROCEDURE SYSTEM-WIDE.**

The evidence supports rolling out the new rotation and shelving procedure to all branches. The 2-point improvement in service scores is:

- **Real** — statistically significant with a tight confidence interval
- **Consistent** — observed across all 8 post-switch weeks
- **Meaningful** — represents roughly 4% improvement on the service index
- **Cost-effective** — assuming implementation costs are modest, the gains justify the change

However, the board should communicate that this is an incremental improvement, not a transformation. The new procedure is a solid step forward, not a breakthrough.

---

## **Key Findings**

### Study Overview
- **709 branches** tracked over 17 weeks (weeks 6–22)
- **425 branches** adopted the new procedure; **284 branches** kept the old procedure
- Switch occurred in **week 15**

### Important Context
Treatment branches were already scoring slightly higher than control branches before the switch (50.7 vs. 49.5). The analysis accounts for this pre-existing difference by measuring the *change* in the gap, not the absolute scores.

### Results

| Period | Control Branches | Treatment Branches | Gap Between Them |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| Before switch (weeks 6–14) | 49.5 | 50.7 | 1.2 points |
| After switch (weeks 15–22) | 49.0 | 52.2 | 3.1 points |
| **Change** | **−0.5** | **+1.5** | **+1.9 points** |

**Estimated effect of new procedure: +1.9 points** (95% CI: 1.4 to 2.5; p < 0.001)

### What This Means in Practice
- A branch with a typical score of 50 would be expected to reach approximately **52** after adopting the new procedure
- This is a **small-to-moderate effect** (0.2 standard deviations)—noticeable but not dramatic
- The improvement was **consistent across all 8 post-switch weeks**, suggesting the effect is stable
- Control branches declined slightly during this period; treatment branches improved

### Why We Trust This Result
- **Pre-existing differences accounted for:** The analysis isolates the change attributable to the procedure, not baseline branch quality
- **Parallel trends confirmed:** Both groups followed similar patterns before the switch
- **Controls included:** Effect holds after adjusting for staffing levels and visitor volume
- **Large sample:** Over 12,000 weekly observations provide strong statistical power
