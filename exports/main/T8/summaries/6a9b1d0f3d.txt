# **Library Branch Procedure Change: Impact on Service Scores**

---

## **Bottom Line**

The new procedure **likely improved service scores**, but we cannot be highly confident. There is approximately a **93% probability the effect is positive**, with an estimated improvement of **about 2 points (4% of average)**. However, the result does not meet the conventional threshold for statistical significance (p=0.14), meaning we cannot rule out no effect.

**Recommendation:** If rollout costs are low, proceed with expansion. If costs are substantial, consider gathering more data before committing.

---

## **Final Verdict**

**Proceed with a phased rollout.**

The evidence leans meaningfully in favor of the new procedure:
- The point estimate is positive and practically meaningful (+2 points)
- The confidence interval is mostly positive (75% of plausible values show improvement)
- There is no evidence of harm—the lower bound of uncertainty is only slightly negative

Given that procedure changes typically have modest implementation costs compared to the value of improved service, the **expected benefit outweighs the risk**. However, we recommend a **phased approach** rather than immediate system-wide adoption, allowing continued monitoring to confirm the effect before full commitment.

---

## **Key Findings**

### **The Numbers**

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated effect | **+1.94 points** |
| 95% Confidence interval | **-0.65 to +4.53 points** |
| P-value | **0.14** |
| Probability effect is positive | **~93%** |

### **What This Means**

- **The likely scenario**: The new procedure improved scores by 2-4 points
- **The uncertain scenario**: There's about a 7% chance the procedure had no effect or slightly worsened scores
- **Both groups declined** after week 15, but switched branches declined less
- **Pre-switch trends were parallel**, supporting the validity of our comparison

### **Study Design**
- **34 branches**: 20 switched to new procedure, 14 kept old procedure
- **17 weeks of data** (weeks 6–22), switch occurred at week 15
- **Difference-in-differences analysis** comparing changes between groups

## **Decision Framework for the Board**

| If rollout costs are... | Recommended action |
|------------------------|-------------------|
| **Low** (minimal training, no equipment) | Proceed with rollout—the likely benefit outweighs modest costs |
| **Moderate** | Phase the rollout while continuing to monitor outcomes |
| **High** (significant training, system changes) | Collect more data before committing; 93% confidence may be insufficient |
